# C58 — "Auffällig"

**Fusion of:** C14 (Was hat sich geändert) · C36 (Ein Objekt) · C11's slider
**Centre of gravity:** the anomaly is a stored object with a lifetime, and the ranked list of those
objects is the app.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Eine Auffälligkeit ist ein Ding — mit Namen, Anfang, Ende, Körper und benannten Beziehungen. Der
Startbildschirm ist die nach Wucht sortierte Liste dieser Dinge. Alles Normale ist ein einziges Ding
namens Ruhe, und Ruhe hat Zahlen.**

C14 made the *difference* the primary object but left it a snapshot — a pair of frames and a sentence that
exists only while you look. C36 made every object the same shape but had nothing to say about importance:
`◼ Heizung aus` and `▲ Feuchte 40 Min über 65 %` were the same 48 pixels, and C36 admitted it.

C58 fuses them by promoting C14's *Befund* into C36's *Ding*. The moment a difference has a `thing_id`, a
`t` and a `t_end`, four things become true at once, none of them true in either source alone:

1. It can be **ranked**, because it has a measurable Wucht (Abweichung × Dauer × Steuerbarkeit). Ranking is
   the filter the brief asked for — nothing is hidden, the boring is simply last.
2. It can be **scrubbed**, because it has a duration. That is what C11's slider moves through here: not
   wall-clock, not a film — **the lifetime of one Auffälligkeit**, run-up to recovery.
3. It can be **walked to**, because it has relations: `betrifft` a plant or socket, `belegt` by frames and
   samples, `fiel_zusammen_mit` a Gabe or a Zieländerung, `endete_durch` a person or the controller.
4. Its **absence** can be an object too. `Ruhe` is a stored Ding with `t`/`t_end` and a body of real
   numbers, so a perfect week is not an empty screen — it is the largest object on it.

The discipline is C14's, verbatim: **the product describes and prescribes, it never diagnoses.** It says
*„Die Heizung lief 2 Std 10 in einer zu warmen Nacht"* and offers *„Nachtziel prüfen"*. It never says
*„Wärmestress"*. Where no deterministic remedy exists — every image finding — the Tafel prints
`Kein Mittel bekannt.` and stops. That refusal is the feature.

---

## 2. What comes from where

**From C14:** the difference as primary object; the two-register row (prose line + value line, one DOM node,
both always present); the picture pair as the readout for change; ranking by z-score against the tent's own
14-day baseline at the same time of day; the ban on diagnosis; `light_mismatch` as proof that picture and
data together see what neither sees alone.

**From C36:** one `things` collection; one screen shape (Zeile / Tafel); one navigation gesture (tap a
Zeile, it becomes the Subjekt); named German relations instead of a type picker; `Werte {…}` as the last row
of every Tafel for everybody from install; reveal by capability (per-role sockets, failing closed) and by
data (no plants ⇒ the word „Pflanze" appears once in the whole product).

**From C11's slider:** one playhead, two projections, drag either and both move; **pause is the disclosure
gesture**, self-cancelling on play, persisting nothing; a pinned scrub header instead of a tooltip, because
there is no hover on touch; correlation in both directions.

**THE FUSION — what exists only because all three were combined:**

> **Der Verlaufsregler auf einer Auffälligkeit.** C14's difference has no duration, so there is nothing to
> scrub. C36's Ding has a time but no reason to be examined frame by frame. C11's slider scrubs the clock,
> which is exactly the thing an anxious grower cannot aim. Together: the anomaly *is* the object, the object
> *has a body in time*, and the slider's entire range is that body — `t − 2 Std` (der Anlauf) to
> `t_end + 2 Std` (die Erholung), never more. **You never choose a time window. The anomaly is the window.**
>
> Its mirror: **Ruhe ist auch ein Ding, und Ruhe hat einen Regler.** Because the uniform model forces the
> calm stretch into the same shape, a perfect week is a scrubbable object whose pause-panel says, minute by
> minute, *why* that minute was normal. C14 alone shows an empty Befund list. C36 alone shows 41 identical
> grey Zeilen. C11 alone shows a film of a plant not moving.
>
> Third: because an anomaly has `t_end`, it draws on the chart as a **markArea, not a marker**, and in the
> film as an **amber span on the scrub bar**. The same object is a row, a region and a film chapter.
>
> Remove any one source and this collapses: without C14 there is no difference to rank; without C36 there is
> no uniform body to rank *across* (socket, plant, image, person); without C11 there is no way to enter the
> object's duration without a mode.

## 3. Information architecture

| Route | Guard | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/zelte` | AuthGuard | Your Zelte. Exactly one ⇒ straight through, as today. |
| `/z/:zelt_id/:ding_id?` | AuthGuard (+ share bypass) | **The browser.** One component. `ding_id` absent ⇒ the Zelt is Subjekt (§4). |
| `/z/:zelt_id/verlauf?…` | AuthGuard (+ share) | The chart (§6). The only non-Ding screen. |

Overlays, not routes: Gabe/Notiz/Foto/Zettel sheets, the `sortiert ⓘ` sheet, the actor picker.

**Back-compat — persisted user data, so not optional.** `/list` → `/zelte`; `/device/:id` → its Zelt;
`/device/:id/diary` → the device Ding (`mergeDiaryQueryParams` honoured); `/device/:id/charts?<q>` →
`/z/:zelt/verlauf?<q>` (query untouched, `applyViewParams` back-compat branch intact); `/device/:id/settings`
→ `/z/:zelt/ziele` for controller/fridge, **kept verbatim** for fan/light/plug/dryer; everything else
unchanged. `ShareLink.page` stays `'charts' | 'diary'` — no enum change, no migration; a share token still
resolves a **device** and renders a single-device Zelt.

### The tent above the device

```ts
interface Zelt {                    // NEW collection `zelte`. Owner-scoped.
  zelt_id: string; owner_id: string;
  name: string;                     // "Zelt Keller"
  device_ids: string[];             // 1..n
  tz: string;                       // "Europe/Berlin" — every day boundary uses this
  created_at: number;
}
```

**No auth rewrite.** `auth.middleware.ts:172` stays device-scoped: a request resolves `zelt_id →
device_ids[]` from a Zelt the caller owns, then runs the *existing* per-device check on each. A device that
fails is dropped with one Zeile: `Gerät nicht mehr freigegeben.`

**Migration, idempotent, at boot:** every claimed device without a Zelt gets a single-device Zelt named after
the device (or `Zelt` + serial suffix — never `devices.fridge.title`; the live „Terp Control FRIDGE GROW" bug
dies here). Nothing breaks, nobody is asked anything.

**What it fixes:** `durchgang` and its day counter hang off the **Zelt**, not the device. One grow across four
devices is one diary, one day count, one ranked list. Anomalies are computed per Zelt, so „nachts zu warm"
from the controller and „Licht 25 Min zu lang" from an old light plug are two rows in one list.

### The one object

```ts
interface Ding {                             // collection `things` — C36's model, three new arts
  thing_id: string;                          // uuid v4, MINTED BY THE CLIENT (§8 idempotency)
  zelt_id: string; device_id?: string;
  art: DingArt; name: string;
  t: number; t_end?: number | null;          // explicit null = läuft noch
  rel?: Record<string, string[]>;            // { betrifft, belegt, fiel_zusammen_mit, endete_durch }
  d?: Record<string, unknown>;
  bilder?: string[]; akteur?: string;        // akteur → thing_id of a `mensch`
  storniert_von?: string;                    // corrections, never hard edits
}
type DingArt = 'zelt'|'pflanze'|'dose'|'kamera'|'bild'|'film'|'gabe'|'notiz'|'zustand'|'phase'
             | 'ziel'|'ziel_aenderung'|'mensch'|'ereignis'|'durchgang'
             | 'auffaelligkeit'|'ruhe';      // ← the two new ones, and the concept
```

Stored: `pflanze, gabe, notiz, zustand, phase, mensch, durchgang, ziel_aenderung, auffaelligkeit, ruhe`.
Projected read-time (C36's adapters, unchanged): `zelt` (Device + last Influx point), `dose`
(`parseSocketRoles(hardwareInfo.sockets)`), `kamera` (`hardwareInfo.webcam_did` + newest Image),
`bild`/`film` (`Image`), `ziel` (`device.configuration` + `Alarm[]`), `ereignis` (`DeviceLog`).
One read API, cursor-paginated by `t`, and it is the one the webapp itself consumes:
`GET /dinge?zelt_id=&art=&von=&bis=&cursor=&format=json|csv`.

```ts
interface Auffaelligkeit extends Ding {        // art: 'auffaelligkeit'
  d: { regel: RegelId;                                  // §5.2
       messgroesse?: string;                            // temperature|humidity|co2|vpd|out_light|bild
       ist: number; soll?: number; abw: number;         // abw = |Δ| / max(σ14, σ_floor)
       dauer_min: number; wucht: number;                // the printed sort key, §4.3
       schwelle_quelle: 'alarm'|'sollwert'|'phasenband'|'eigenbasis';
       mittel?: Mittel;                                 // ABSENT when no deterministic remedy exists
       quittiert_von?: string; quittiert_am?: number; };
}
interface Mittel {           // rule-table-derived, never generated prose
  text: string;              // "Die Heizung lief 2 Std 10 in dieser Nacht."
  handlung: string;          // "Nachtziel prüfen"
  ziel_ding?: string;        // thing_id to walk to — a `ziel`, a `dose`, a sheet
  begruendung: string;       // "Regel: Nachtmittel 3 Nächte > Ziel + 1,5 °C, Heizung lief ≥ 30 Min mit."
}
interface Ruhe extends Ding {                  // t = last anomaly ended, t_end = null while it lasts
  d: { im_band: Record<string, number>;                 // % of RAW samples in band
       laengste_abweichung_min: Record<string, number>;
       bilder_behalten: number; bilder_verworfen: number;
       schaltvorgaenge: number; davon_von_hand: number;
       ruhige_dinge: string[];                          // the „41 Dinge ruhig" list
       basis: 'eigenbasis' | 'ziele'; };                // §12: day one says which
}
```

`ziel_aenderung` fixes „setpoint history is not recorded". Every cloud→device configuration push writes
`{art:'ziel_aenderung', d:{schluessel:'night.temperature', von:24.0, nach:20.0}, akteur}`. Cloud-side, no
firmware change, no new device-config key. The chart's Sollwert steps from this history (§6), and an anomaly
starting six hours after a target change carries it as `fiel_zusammen_mit` — usually the whole explanation.

### 4.1 Why the pair sits above the list

C14's pair is the only thing that outranks the ranking, for a structural reason: *„etwas wächst"* is never
auffällig, because growth is the expected case and a ranking by deviation-from-baseline always sinks it. The
pair carries the change that is normal; the list carries the change that is not. **On a perfect week the list
is one row and the pair is the screen** — the quiet-day answer, at no cost in modes, because the ranking
simply has nothing above the pair.

- **Right frame** = the newest *kept* frame. A rejected frame is never „jetzt"; if the last five were
  rejected: `Bild verworfen (Hand im Bild) — gezeigt: 14:04`.
- **Left frame** = the comparison endpoint, default *„seit du zuletzt geschaut hast"*
  (`localStorage['tc-lastseen-<zelt_id>']`, written on blur), falling back to `gestern gleiche Zeit` under 4 h.
- **No camera** ⇒ the pair becomes the **Spur**: a full-width 24 h min/max band per reported measure with the
  same two endpoint captions, one line *„Keine Kamera gekoppelt — der Vergleich zeigt nur Zahlen."* Same
  slider, same list, no film rows. Reveal by capability; it changes the instant a camera pairs.

### 4.2 THE SLIDER — der Vergleichsregler

**One cursor, two projections:** the cursor is the *left endpoint of the comparison*, and both the picture
pair and the ranked list render from it.

- **Drag** → the left frame walks backwards through kept frames; the pinned header reads
  `Sa 06.09., 14:02 · Tag 22 · 21,8 °C · 61 % · Licht 100 %`. While dragging, the list dims to one count
  line: `seit Sa 14:02 · 3 auffällig · 41 ruhig`. **On release** the list re-ranks with a 200 ms cross-fade.
  Dragging never reflows the list under the thumb — unusable at 2 a.m., and the reason the count line exists.
- **Snap ladder** (C14's mechanism): 44 px magnetic anchors `Beginn · Phase · 1 Woche · gestern · seit
  zuletzt · jetzt`. A fat thumb cannot miss `gestern`; a deliberate finger lands on any minute, printed live.
  One control, coarse and precise in the same gesture, no setting.
- **Play** (`▶`, 44 px) runs the span from the left endpoint to now inside the right frame at 12 fps.
- **Pause is the disclosure gesture.** Tapping the frame or stopping a drag unfolds the minute, in place:

```
   ╭──────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
   │ Sa 06.09.2026, 14:02 · Tag 22 · Blüte            │
   │ Temperatur    21,8 °C   Ziel 25,0    −3,2        │ ← tap a row → /verlauf, this window
   │ Luftfeuchte   61 %      Ziel 60 %    +1          │
   │ VPD           1,07 kPa  Band 1,2–1,5   ▲         │
   │ Lief: Licht 100 % · Heizung an (seit 12 Min)     │ ← only sockets the device reports
   │ Gaben ±2 Std: Gießen 2,0 L (Anna, 12:05)         │
   │ Bild: belichtet ×1,04 · Schärfe 0,71 · behalten  │
   │ ▲ Teil von: „Zu kalt am Nachmittag"          ›   │ ← ← THE FUSION, in one row
   │ [ Rohdaten ]         [ Nächste Auffälligkeit → ] │
   ╰──────────────────────────────────────────────────╯
```

The row `▲ Teil von: …` is what neither source has: **the scrubbed minute names the object it belongs to,
and one tap walks into that object's own Tafel and its own slider.** Correlation both ways —
`[ Nächste Auffälligkeit → ]` jumps the cursor to the next one's start. Nothing persisted, self-cancels on
play, hides no feature class. Not a mode.

### 4.3 The ranked list and the printed sort key

One row = a `<button>` with a **prose line** (17 px/600) and a **value line** (15 px/400, tabular-nums) —
C14's two registers in one DOM node, both always present, neither a summary of the other. A third muted line
appears only when a `Mittel` exists. `sortiert ⓘ` opens a half-sheet printing the formula **with this
screen's actual numbers**:

```
  Sortiert nach Wucht          Wucht = Abweichung × Dauer × Steuerbarkeit
  Abweichung     |Ist − Soll| ÷ eigene Streuung der letzten 14 Tage zur selben
                 Tageszeit (±1 Std), mindestens 0,4 °C · 2 %-Punkte · 40 ppm · 0,05 kPa
  Dauer          Minuten außerhalb ÷ 60, höchstens 6
  Steuerbarkeit  1,0 wenn dein Zelt Technik dafür hat, sonst 0,6

  Nachts zu warm       2,7 × 6,0 × 1,0 = 16,2
  A3 zweimal gedüngt   von Hand         = 12,0   (Menschen zählen fest)
  Kamera 3 Std still   1,0 × 3,0 × 1,0 =  3,0
```

Human-authored anomalies carry a fixed Wucht of 12,0 — a person's action outranks a wobble, C14's rule made
numeric. `Steuerbarkeit 0,6` honestly demotes an anomaly you own no kit to fix. **The order is computed once
per screen entry and does not move while you look at it** — no adaptive re-ordering by behaviour, because an
impaired user needs the screen to be in the same place at 2 a.m. as at noon.

### 4.5 How it looks — the part nobody designed

**Type scale** (16 px base): 28/600 Zelt name · 20/600 Tafel-Kopf · 17/600 prose · 15/400 tabular-nums value
· 13/500 muted meta · 13/600 uppercase section header, 0.04em tracking.
**Spacing:** 4 px grid; row 56 px, two-line row 76 px, section gap 24 px, card padding 16 px, touch target
never under 44 px.
**Marks — shape carries the meaning, colour only reinforces** (never colour alone):
`▲` filled/danger = läuft noch + Alarm ausgelöst · `▲` open/warning = läuft noch · `●` text = beendet, stays
in the list until seen · `○` success = ruhig · `◻` muted = veraltet (the value is there, it is just old).

**Die Spur — the empty-state illustration, and it is not decorative.** One `<tc-spur>` SVG: a 1 px
`--tc-line` rule across the card at 40 % height with a 6 px filled dot at its right end. On day one that is
all there is; as samples arrive the same component grows the min/max band around the same line. **The empty
state becomes the real chart**, so nobody ever sees a picture of a thing they will never have again.

## 5. NO-MODES MECHANISM

> **Falsification test:** if you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of feature,
> it is a mode.

### 5.1 Ranking is the filter, and the filter has no control

No filter chips, no severity selector, no „nur Probleme anzeigen", no notification-centre badge. Every Ding
in the Zelt is in the list at all times; Wucht decides the order; the tail is folded into **one Ding you can
walk into**. `○ 41 Dinge ruhig` is not a „show more" — it is a Zeile like every other, and tapping it makes
the Ruhe Ding the Subjekt, which is C36's single navigation gesture. The distinction is testable: a
disclosure toggle returns you to the same screen with more rows; this pushes a new Subjekt and Back returns.
Depth is subject-distance, never a preference.

### 5.2 Reveal by capability and by data — the rules prove it

A rule only *exists* for a Zelt if the data to evaluate it exists. Fourteen ship; a monitor-only tent
evaluates four and is never told about the other ten.

| `regel` | Fires when | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ausser_band` | outside alarm threshold / Soll ± Toleranz > t_min (Temp 20 · rF 45 · CO₂ 30 · VPD 60 Min) | a measure |
| `nacht_abweichung` | night mean off night target by > Toleranz on ≥ 2 consecutive nights | `daynight` |
| `drift` | 7-day rolling mean moves > 2σ₁₄ over 5 days, no `ziel_aenderung` in the window | 14 d history |
| `ungewoehnlich` | value at this time of day deviates > 3σ₁₄ | 14 d history |
| `takt` | a socket switches > 4× its own 14-day median rate for 2 h (short-cycling) | a paired socket |
| `dauerlauf` | a role-socket runs > 90 % of the hour, 3 h running (undersized kit) | a paired socket |
| `licht_stimmt_nicht` | `out_light > 50 %` for 15 Min while frame brightness is in the lights-off band, or inverse | light + camera |
| `kamera_still` | no kept frame > 3 h | camera |
| `kamera_verrutscht` | sustained shift beyond the stabiliser's 8 px inset > 6 h | camera |
| `bild_veraendert` | a tile's change score > 3σ₁₄ against its own baseline | camera + 14 d |
| `doppelte_gabe` | two `gabe` on the same plants inside the medium's window, by different `mensch` | ≥ 2 `mensch` |
| `gabe_faellig` | regime step due, no `gabe` for N days | a regime chosen |
| `offline` | device silent > 10 Min | — |
| `ziel_widerspruch` | night target above day target, or a target outside the phase's VPD band | a `ziel` |

`licht_stimmt_nicht` fuses C14's correlation thesis with C36's uniform model: neither picture nor data
detects a failed contactor alone; as one Ding they detect it in fifteen minutes.

### 5.3 Mittel — the deterministic remedy, and where it refuses

A `Mittel` is emitted only when **(a)** its precondition holds on data already owned and **(b)** the action
names a target changeable here, a socket the device reports, or a physical object with one unambiguous
referent. Otherwise the field is absent and the Tafel prints `Kein Mittel bekannt.` No filler, no
„Belüftung verbessern", no AI.

| `regel` + condition | text → **handlung** | walks to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nacht_abweichung`, heater ran ≥ 30 Min | *„Die Heizung lief 2 Std 10 in einer zu warmen Nacht. Nachtziel 20,0 °C wird nicht gehalten."* → **Nachtziel prüfen** | `ziel` night.temperature |
| `nacht_abweichung`, **no heater paired** | *„Kein Heizgerät gemeldet — die Nacht ist nicht steuerbar. Das Ziel beschreibt dein Zelt nicht."* → **Nachtziel auf 22,0 °C setzen** | `ziel` night.temperature |
| `dauerlauf` on `dehumidifier` | *„Der Entfeuchter lief 91 % der letzten 3 Std. In den Betriebsarten „temp" und „breed" kühlt diese Dose, sie entfeuchtet nicht."* → **Betriebsart prüfen** | `ziel` workmode |
| `takt` on any socket | *„Die Heizung schaltete 14× pro Stunde, sonst 3×."* → **Toleranz erhöhen oder den Fühler weg vom Heizgerät hängen** | `ziel` tolerance |
| `licht_stimmt_nicht` | *„Der Ausgang meldet Licht 100 %, das Bild ist dunkel."* → **Schütz oder Steckdose prüfen** | `dose` light |
| `kamera_still` | *„Letztes Bild Mo 11:02."* → **Kamera stromlos oder WLAN weg** | `[ Testbild anfordern ]` |
| `doppelte_gabe` | *„A3 hat 2× volle Dosis in 90 Min bekommen."* → **Nächste Gabe nur Wasser** | `[ Zettel schreiben ]`, prefilled |
| `ziel_widerspruch` | *„Nachtziel 26,0 °C liegt über dem Tagziel 24,0 °C."* → **Tag- und Nachtziel tauschen** | both `ziel` |
| `bild_veraendert`, `drift`, `ungewoehnlich` | — | **`Kein Mittel bekannt.`** Das Bild beschreibt nur, was anders aussieht. |

Every `Mittel` prints its `begruendung` under a hairline, so it can be argued with.

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line directive (owner decision); Chart.js / ng2-charts /
chartjs-adapter-luxon deleted in the same commit. **Always entered with a measure and span already set.**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ‹  Temperatur             06.09. 12:00 – 07.09. 06:00│
│ 21,8 °C  Ziel 25,0  ⌀ 23,6  min 18,1   Sa 14:02      │ ← pinned scrub header, no tooltip

│ ┄┄┄┄┄╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌  Sollwert, gestuft aus ziel_aenderung  │
│ ─────╲___╱────  Ist, minmax-Band                     │
```

- **Sollwert** is a stepped dashed `markLine` built from `ziel_aenderung` history, **not from today's
  configuration** — the fix for „every chart draws today's target over last month's data". Where that history
  does not exist yet, the line is labelled `Ziel (heutiger Wert — vor dem 01.10.2026 nicht aufgezeichnet)`.
- **Zielband** = `markArea`, priority **Alarmschwellen > Sollwert ± Toleranz > Phasen-VPD-Band**, one at a
  time, labelled with which.
- **Auffälligkeiten draw as markAreas, because they have `t_end`.** Tapping one walks to its Tafel. The only
  chart in the bake-off where the anomaly is a region rather than a pin — it falls out of the model for free.
- **Tag/Nacht** from measured `out_light > 0`, `daynight` as fallback, device timezone named in the axis
  label. A failed contactor renders as a *missing band* — a diagnostic, not decoration.
- **Ausgänge** as a state-timeline lane; region length = duration, null = gap. Never 0/1 value lines.
- **Annotation rail**, two rows (Menschen / Gerät), 10 px clustering with count badges, full-height dashed
  lines for `phase` starts. **Tappable, not hover** — there is no hover on the phone this ships for.
- **Zeit im Band** computed server-side on **raw Influx samples**, never `aggregateWindow(mean)`, split
  day/night, longest excursion as a duration, greyed below 80 % coverage (printed). `sampling:'minmax'` on
  the primary series; LTTB for the navigator only. Two fingers pan/zoom, one finger scrolls, tap = cursor.
- **Prerequisite, ships first:** parameterise the Flux interpolation of `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval`
  (`data.service.ts:80-89`), fix the `limit(n:50000)` piped after `yield()`, constrain interval by timespan.

## 7. Diary and multi-plant

**There is no diary feature.** The Zelt's `Verlauf` is the tent's diary; a `pflanze`'s `Verlauf` is that
plant's. Same Zeilen, same renderer. „Es macht das Tagebuchführen für dich" means `ereignis`, `bild`, `film`,
`phase`, `ziel_aenderung`, `auffaelligkeit` and `ruhe` are written by the machine into the same list the
human writes into — so a user who logs nothing still opens a full diary. **Every entry gets its picture
automatically** (C14): on write the server sets `bilder[0]` to the nearest *kept* frame within ±5 min.

```ts
pflanze   d: { sorte?, medium?, topf_l?, quelle?: 'samen'|'steckling'|'gekauft',
               keimung_t, ernte_t?, ernte_g?, entfernt_t? }
          name: "A3 · Wedding Cake"     // renaming NEVER changes thing_id
          rel: { in: [zelt_ding] }
durchgang d: { nummer, medium_key?, regime_key?, tag_null }   t / t_end   // the day counter lives HERE
phase     d: { stufe: DiaryLifecycleStage }  rel: { betrifft: [pflanze…] }   // [] = whole tent
gabe      d: { wasser_l, kannen?, kannen_l?, ec?, ph?, ablauf_ph?, ablauf_ec?,
               ec_basis?: 'absolut'|'plus_leitungswasser', regime_key?, schritt?,
               produkte: [{ name, ml_pro_l, aus_regime: boolean }] }
          rel: { betrifft: [pflanze…] }, akteur, bilder
notiz     d: { text }        zustand  d: { text, geschlossen_von? }   t / t_end(null = offen)
mensch    name only, Zelt-scoped, no account
```

`DiaryLifecycleStage` gains a seventh value `ernte` — harvest is not a stage today, so „wie lief dieser
Durchgang" is unanswerable. Optional field, read-time normaliser, no migration tool.

**The plant-ignorer is untaxed.** `rel.betrifft: []` means the whole tent and is the default forever. Zero
`pflanze` Dinge ⇒ no plant chooser in any sheet, no per-plant section, no plant column, and the word
„Pflanze" appears **once** in the entire product: behind the `+` on `Im Zelt`. Reveal by data, not reversible
by a setting, therefore not a mode. A monitor-only user can run three years and never learn plants exist.

`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` (`grow-report.component.ts:691-747`) becomes a one-time idempotent boot
backfill emitting `durchgang` + `pflanze` + `phase`, keyed by trimmed `lifecycleName`, then is deleted from
the browser — the string-comparison inference that forks history on a rename dies with it. Legacy `DeviceLog`
rows keep projecting as `ereignis`/`notiz`, including the seven legacy English-prose regexes. **Server-side
cursor pagination for `things` and `DeviceLog` is a v1 prerequisite.**

Litres are **derived and shown, never typed** (`kannen × kannen_l`); `kannen_l` is remembered per Zelt.

| Path | Sequence | **Taps** |
| --- | --- | --- |

Same sheet, same field order, every time. The 2-tap and 14-tap paths differ only in how far down the user
chooses to go. Scrolling is not a disclosure control.

**Medium + Regime, chosen once**, on the `durchgang` Tafel, the way a grower says it out loud:
`Biobizz Light-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom — Düngen ab Woche 2` · `Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom —
Vorgedüngt, Düngen ab Woche 3–4` · `Floragard Light + Green House Powder Feeding` · `BioTabs — kaum
Flüssigdünger, neue Tabs alle 4–6 Wochen` · `Kein Schema`. Footer: `Zuletzt geprüft: 12.08.2026 · Quelle:
Herstellerplan`.

Mongo content collection `regime`, **never in the webapp bundle**, each entry carrying `quelle_url` +
`geprueft_am` (surfaced as `zuletzt geprüft` — legal posture and a trust feature in one string). Brand names
as plain text only, never inside a Terp Control feature name.

- **Advances by feed events, not wall clock:** `Schritt 7 von 14`, with a soft calendar mapping (`etwa
  Woche 5`). Growers feed Tue/Fri and slip.
- **A regime that cannot refuse is not modelling the domain.** Before `start_ab_tag`, All-Mix prefills *no*
  products and prints `All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen.` with `[ Trotzdem düngen ]`.
- Every prefilled value stores `aus_regime: true`, flipping to `false` when edited, so the club report can
  say `wie im Plan` vs `abweichend` months later. EC canonical in mS/cm; `plus_leitungswasser` renders as
  `EC-Ziel 1,4 + dein Leitungswasser`, never a bare ppm. Autoflower: `×0,25–0,5`, no 12/12 flip.
- **Overdue** becomes `auffaelligkeit{gabe_faellig}` with `[ Gabe öffnen ]` prefilled. No task list, no
  badge, no notification centre.

**Double-feed guard.** At sheet open, query `gabe` within the medium's window (Erde: Wasser 6 h / Dünger
18 h; Coco: 3 h / 12 h — several daily feeds are correct there) intersecting the selection. A *different*
`mensch` → soft block, the primary button **relabels** so muscle memory cannot fire it. Same `mensch` →
informational only (`Du hast vor 40 Min gegossen.`), because self-repeats are usually mis-taps.
`[ Bild ansehen ]` opens the other person's auto-attached frame — **you can see whether the soil is dark.**

**And when the guard is tapped through, the miss does not vanish** — it becomes
`auffaelligkeit{doppelte_gabe}` with Wucht 12,0, ranked near the top, carrying *„Nächste Gabe nur Wasser"*.
**This is the fusion doing safety work:** C11's guard warns once and is gone; C58's guard warns once and then
leaves an object behind that survives until someone acknowledges it.

**Double *logging*** is prevented by construction: the client mints `thing_id` before the request, the server
upserts on `{zelt_id, thing_id}`, so a replayed queue can never double-log. v1 has no service worker — a
failed save is held in `localStorage`, retried on app focus, rendered `⟳ nicht gesendet`. That is not
capture-in-a-cellar; real offline is v1.1, one dependency plus one build change.

## 9. Camera, timelapse, image correlation

**What is real** (verified in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`): ffmpeg is installed and invoked via
`execFile`; `sharp` 0.34.5 is a dependency; a frame lands every 30 s; rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses are
built hourly. **Three shipping defects, all cheap:** the encode is `libx265` (does not play in Firefox
anywhere, nor Chrome on Linux/most Windows — today's timelapse is invisible to most customers who have one),
there is no `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, and no `-movflags +faststart`. **Fixing those three flags is half a day and
is the highest-value change in this document.**

**FrameMetrik**, ≤ 60 ms/frame, two `sharp` decodes plus plain JS at ingest, in a sibling collection so
scoring queries never drag Buffers: `{ image_id, zelt_id, timestamp, ok, verwerfgrund?:
'licht_aus'|'kurzzeitig'|'unscharf'|'doppelt'|'kamera_sprung', dhash, helligkeit, schaerfe, gruen_anteil?,
kacheln[48], dx, dy, licht_pct }`. `licht_pct` is read off the MQTT status we already receive at capture.
**We own the lamp**, so „was the light on" is a database join, not a computer-vision problem — and that one
field powers dark-culling, the deflicker regimes and `licht_stimmt_nicht`. **Refused as computer-vision
fantasy:** naming a drooping leaf, diagnosing a deficiency from colour, measuring height in cm, counting
plants, segmenting canopy, detecting pests. `gruen_anteil` is labelled `Blattfläche (Bildanteil)`, never
„Wachstum" as a claim.

### 9.1 What ships in v1, and HOW THE FILM IS STORED

1. **The rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses**, unchanged in structure, re-encoded to
   `libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 21 -movflags +faststart`. ≤ 2 MB each, they stay
   in `Image{format:'mp4'}` as Buffers, well under BSON. The zero-configuration film for anyone who never
   opens the film sheet.
2. **`Der ganze Durchgang`** — one `film` Ding per `durchgang`, built from the kept set with per-regime white
   balance, a ±3 % temporal deflicker clamp, translation-only stabilisation (rotation is *detected and cut*
   with a title card `Kamera neu ausgerichtet · Tag 12`, never smeared), and burn-in captions
   `Tag 24 · Blüte · 12.09. 14:02 · 24,1 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21` composited as SVG with sharp — the technique
   already proven in `imageService.addOfflineOverlay`.

**Storage — the answer the brief demanded.** `Image.data: Buffer` caps at 16 MB BSON and there is no GridFS
in the repo. An 84-day 720p film at ~17 frames/day is 1 440 frames ≈ **12–20 MB** — over the cap, and loading
it whole into Node is a latent memory problem the current mp4 path already has.

> **v1 adds GridFS.** One bucket, `filme`, via mongoose's `GridFSBucket`, 255 KB chunks, streamed with real
> `Accept-Ranges: bytes` handling. ~2 days. Chosen over a `RENDER_DIR` docker volume because ops already
> backs up Mongo and a second volume means a second backup story. Frames stay in `Image` exactly as today
> (9–40 KB each); only rendered films move. No migration: existing mp4 Buffers keep their read path behind a
> one-function adapter; new renders go to the bucket.

**Leaving the product** — three real routes, and the UI says which one you get:
`navigator.share({files:[File]})` where `navigator.canShare?.({files})` (iOS 15+, Chrome Android) puts the
file straight into WhatsApp/Telegram/Fotos; otherwise `window.location.href` to
`GET /film/:thing_id.mp4?token=<imageToken>` with `Content-Disposition: attachment;
filename="terpcontrol_zelt-keller_tag1-tag84_720p.mp4"` (ASCII-folded, `^[a-z0-9_-]{1,100}\.mp4$`); on iOS
without Web Share, one line: `Lange drücken → „Video sichern".` A pasteable `?token=` link plays for 30 days
with no login. **A share that produces a login wall is not a share.**

### 9.2 THE SLIDER on the film

The film's scrub bar carries **amber regions where an Auffälligkeit was open**, drawn from the same
`t`/`t_end` that draw the chart's markAreas.

```
  ▶ ──────▓▓▓▓▓────────────●──────▓▓▓──────────────
    Tag 1        ▲ zu warm          ▲ Kamera still      Tag 84
```

- **One playhead, two projections:** film and Bildspur/chart render from one `ReglerService`
  (`BehaviorSubject<number>`). Drag either, both move. Pressing play and switching to `/verlauf` shows the
  crosshair sweeping.
- **Pause unfolds the minute** — the §4.2 panel, identical component: measured vs Soll vs Delta, sockets that
  ran, Gaben within ±2 h, the frame's own exposure and sharpness verdict, `▲ Teil von: „Nachts zu warm" ›`.
- **Both directions:** `[ Nächste Auffälligkeit → ]` jumps the playhead to the next amber region; from an

**Camera alarms**, created by default the moment a camera pairs, cloud-side, no firmware: `kamera_still`

### 9.3 Reaching a closed app

Today the server has `sendMail` and webhooks only. v1 adds **Web Push**: VAPID keys, a
`@angular/service-worker` push handler, a `PushAbo {user_id, endpoint, keys, zelt_ids[]}` collection. **The
only thing that ever pushes is an Auffälligkeit** with `wucht ≥ 8`, inside an owner-set quiet window, at most
once per anomaly. The concept's central object is exactly the thing worth waking someone for, which is why
this is four days and not a notification framework. Stated once in the UI: `Auf dem iPhone nur, wenn die App
auf dem Home-Bildschirm liegt.` (Safari 16.4+ needs an installed PWA.) **E-mail remains the guaranteed floor.**

## 10. Clubs — attribution without an auth rewrite

- A `mensch` Ding is Zelt-scoped, has a name the owner types once, and **has no account**. Every capture
  writes `akteur`. ~2 days, no `auth.middleware.ts` surgery. The `Wer?` row renders only at ≥ 2 `mensch`
  Dinge — **the club feature *is* the second name.**
- Said once, under the name list: `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann jeden
  Namen wählen.` Anything else is a lie about a record people rely on.
- **Corrections, not edits.** Editing a `gabe` writes a new one with `storniert_von` at the old; the old
  Zeile renders struck through. Reconstructable history without cryptographic immutability, which would
  victimise the unregulated grower.
- **Zettel** (`zustand`) sit above everything on the home screen, because a human wrote them for another
  human. `[ ✓ ]` stamps `geschlossen_von` + `t_end`; closed Zettel stay in `Verlauf` as spans.
- **The cumulative question**, on the `pflanze` Tafel where it is asked: `Bisher in dieser Blüte · Wasser
  18,5 L über 11 Gaben · Bio-Bloom 31 ml · von Anna 7 · Ben 3 · ich 1`.
- **An anomaly can be about a person**, because the model is uniform: `doppelte_gabe` and `gabe_faellig` are
  the same shape as `takt` on a socket, in the same list, with the same slider. No other concept can do this
  without a second screen.
- **Upgrade path designed in:** `akteur` already points at a `mensch`; a later real `Membership` sets
  `mensch.d.user_id` and every historic row keeps resolving.
- **Privacy:** harvest weight and plant count never prompted (optional fields only); no location, no
  analytics SDK, no community feed; actor names free text („T" is fine); films and frames token-guarded;
  public diary URL opt-in per Durchgang, default pseudonymised. **`Alles löschen` actually deletes** —
  `things`, `DeviceLog`, `Image`, GridFS films, `FrameMetrik` and the Influx series — fixing the live lie
  where `deleted` is a visibility flag (`device.service.ts:739`). Refused outright: per-member dispensing,
  grams handed over, member identity, DOB. No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.
- **Export and API, both missing today:** `GET /dinge?…&format=csv` and a per-user read-only `x-api-key`
  generated in `/account`, scoped to that user's Zelte. ~1 day, and it makes `Werte {…}` an actual promise.

## 12. Day one and the first 48 hours

„Auffällig" is the concept most at risk on day one, because on day one **everything is unusual and there is
no baseline.** σ₁₄ does not exist. The answer is explicit and printed, never silent: for the first 14 days
the sort explainer's first line reads `Basis: deine Zielwerte (noch keine 14 Tage Verlauf)` instead of
`Basis: deine eigene Streuung`, and rules that *require* a baseline — `drift`, `ungewoehnlich`,
`bild_veraendert`, `takt` — are **not evaluated**, with the Ruhe Ding saying which and until when. **No delta
is ever fabricated against a synthetic zero.**

**Hour 0–6: the screen is the Ruhe Ding, and it is an inventory, not a task list.**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Zelt Keller           ● Online · Werte von vor 12 Sek│
│   [ tc-spur ]  ────────────────────────────────●     │ ← the empty state that becomes the chart
│   Der Verlauf fängt hier an.                         │
```

## 14. Honest weaknesses

1. **Ranking is a claim about importance, and a wrong ranking is worse than no ranking.** A tent with
   historically noisy temperature has a large σ₁₄, so a genuine excursion scores low and sinks. The σ_floor
   clamp is a fudge, visible the moment two tents are compared. C14 had this problem; making the score the
   *organising principle of the whole product* makes it worse, not better.
2. **`Steuerbarkeit 0,6` demotes exactly the people who can least act.** A monitor-only owner's real problems
   are systematically pushed down because he owns no kit to fix them. The rule is defensible and printed, and
   it is still a design that is kindest to the customer who already spent the most.
3. **The Ruhe Ding is the boldest bet here and may read as filler.** „1 341 Schaltvorgänge, keiner von dir"
   is a line I believe in; a user who wanted a dashboard may see a page telling him nothing happened, at
   length. There is no way to test this before October.
4. **An anomaly with a `t_end` needs a close rule, and close rules are guesses.** When does „nachts zu warm"
   end — the first normal night, or three? I close on two consecutive normal evaluation periods. Too eager
   and the list churns; too lazy and stale objects pile up. This is the parameter most likely to be wrong on
   real tents, and there is no data to tune it with.
5. **Ten stored arts is exactly how C36 becomes a database browser**, and I added two of the most abstract.
   `auffaelligkeit` and `ruhe` are not nouns a grower says out loud, which is the guard rule C36 set and I
   have bent. The German words are good; the concepts are ours, not theirs.
6. **The slider on a *short* anomaly is thin.** Scrubbing a 40-minute humidity excursion yields 80
   near-identical frames of a plant not moving. It earns its place on long anomalies and on Ruhe; on short
   ones it is decoration.
7. **`Mittel` will sometimes be wrong, and it now carries authority.** „Nachtziel prüfen" is safe. „Den
   Fühler weg vom Heizgerät hängen" is a physical instruction from an app that cannot see where the sensor
   is. All ten remedies are rules I wrote, not rules anyone validated on fifty tents.
8. **GridFS, Web Push and the ECharts migration in one release is a lot of new infrastructure** for a team
   whose server is one pm2 process with `pLimit(10)`. `FrameMetrik` alone is ~1,8 CPU-hours/day at 150
   devices before any render runs. This needs a real job queue before the first series sells out, and that is
   not in the 77 days.
9. **The tent above the device solves the diary and breaks nothing, but it does not solve control.** Four
   devices in one Zelt still have four `configuration` objects and four possible contradictions. I *detect*
   the contradiction (`ziel_widerspruch` runs per Zelt); I do not resolve it. That is firmware work and it is
   not happening before October.
10. **Most weeks, honestly, nothing is auffällig.** A product whose home screen's usual answer is one calm
    row competes against dashboards that always look busy while telling you less. The picture pair carries
    the quiet day, and it only works for the buyers who bought the camera. For the rest a perfect week is a
    paragraph of numbers, and I cannot make that exciting without lying.
