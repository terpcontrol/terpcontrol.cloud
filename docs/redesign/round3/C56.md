# C56 — "Das Blatt"

**Family:** fusion (C14 × C36 × C11-Slider) · **Centre of gravity:** every thing renders as the same
four-part sheet, and the fourth part is the remedy.

Written against C14, C36, C11, NEW-BRIEF.md, 00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md and direct reads of
`/home/user/terpcontrol.cloud` (`server/src/models/images.model.ts:22`, `share.model.ts:22`,
`device.model.ts:75-88`, `services/alarm.service.ts:144,250`, `webapp/src/app/util/socket-info.ts:9`,
`webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts`, `app-routing.module.ts`).

---

## 1. Thesis

**Everything Terp Control knows renders as one sheet — *ein Blatt* — with the same four parts in the same
order, always: *Was es ist · Was sich geändert hat · Woher wir das wissen · Was du tun kannst*.**

A tent, a plant, a socket, a photograph, a temperature series, a feed, a person, a target value, even a
rule of the app itself: same sheet, same four headings, same order. Learn one Blatt and you have learned
the product, because there is no second shape to learn and no mode to be in.

The fourth part is the point. Fifty concepts measured, charted, compared and photographed, and not one of
them ever said what to do — mean 4.93/10, zero of fifty reached 8. The reason is structural: *saying what
to do was always an optional extra, so it was always what got cut.* Here it is a heading. A Blatt with an
empty fourth part is a broken Blatt, and the way to fix one is to write the rule that fills it. Making the
remedy part of the universal anatomy is what forces it to exist.

And it can exist without an advisor, because the other three parts constrain it into buildability. Part 2
is C14's discipline: a **described difference over a named span, with numbers, never a diagnosis**. Part 3
is the evidence for that difference, scrubbable. So a remedy is not an opinion — it is a deterministic
rule that reads a described difference, checks what hardware is actually paired, and names **one other
Blatt you can walk to and one value to change there**. A rule that cannot name a destination Blatt does
not fire. That constraint is what keeps the fourth part honest, and it exists only because C36 already
made every destination a Blatt.

`Blatt` is both the leaf and the sheet of paper. The pun is the product: the record and the plant have
the same name.

## 2. What comes from where — and the fusion

**From C14:** part 2 whole — a pair (Vorher / Jetzt), the difference named in one German sentence, numbers
on the second line, ranked by `|Δ| / max(σ₁₄, σ_floor)` with the sort key printed, **describing, never
diagnosing**. Prose true without the numbers, numbers complete without the prose. The snap ladder of named
anchors. The frame-cull pipeline and its refusal list (§9.2).

**From C36:** every thing is a Blatt — one route, one component, one JSON union, `Werte {…}` at the bottom
of every sheet. Depth is a **walk**, not a switch: fully rendered as the Subjekt, a 48 px Zeile otherwise,
expansion stored nowhere. Named German relations as the only navigation. Reveal by capability (per-role
`parseSocketRoles`, failing closed) and by data. Corrections not edits; `Zettel` above history.

**From C11's slider:** a scrub band with `● jetzt` as the playhead. One cursor, two projections — drag
either, both move. **Pause is the disclosure gesture**: stop the sweep and the minute under the playhead
unfolds into measured/target/delta, sockets that ran, entries within ±2 h, and the frame's own exposure
and sharpness verdict — nothing persisted, self-cancelling on play, hiding no feature class. A pinned
scrub header, never a hover tooltip, because there is no hover on a phone.

### THE FUSION — three things that exist only because all three were combined

**F1 · The remedy is a relation, not a text field.** C36 says depth is reached by walking a named
relation; C14 says every finding is a described difference with numbers over a span. Together the fourth
part writes itself as a fourth relation — **`Abhilfe →`**, an edge from this Blatt to another Blatt plus
one value to change there. A rule that produces prose but no destination is rejected by the engine at
author time. That is why this concept has a remedy engine and fifty others did not: not because it is
cleverer, but because the shape of the app refuses advice that cannot point at something.

**F2 · Die Spur has two handles, and all four parts are a projection of them.** In C14 the Zeitleiste set
the *left endpoint of a comparison*; in C11 the playhead set a *cursor*. Fused, they are one control with
two markers: `◀ Vorher` and `● Jetzt`. Part 2 renders from the **pair**, part 3 from the **playhead
between them**, part 4 re-evaluates over the **span they enclose**. Drag Vorher from `gestern` to
`1 Woche` and the sentence, the evidence and the remedy all re-derive from one gesture. No other concept
has a control that changes what the app *advises* by dragging it.

**F3 · Every remedy carries a scrubbable proof, because the proof is a span.** A card ends
`Regel N-3 · geprüft an 5 Nächten · Beleg ansehen`. Tapping it sets Vorher and Jetzt to the rule's
evidence window and marks the five qualifying nights on the Spur; press play and you watch them, with the
heater lane empty underneath. Deterministic rules are checkable in a way an advisor never is, and the
slider makes checking a gesture rather than a support ticket. **The rule itself is a Blatt**
(`art: 'regel'`) with the same four parts, so "why is the app telling me this" and "turn this off for my
tent" are a walk, not a settings page.

Remove any one source and it collapses: without C14 the remedy has no described difference to key off and
becomes a guess; without C36 it has nowhere to point and becomes prose; without C11's slider it has no
proof and becomes an oracle.

## 3. Information architecture

Two routes. That is the app.

| Route | Guard | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/list` | AuthGuard | Zelte (tents, not devices). Exactly one → straight through, as today. |
| `/b/:tent_id/:blatt_id?` | AuthGuard (+ share bypass) | **The Blatt.** One component. `blatt_id` absent ⇒ the Zelt is the Subjekt. |

Untouched and still routed: `/login`, `/account`, `/shares`, `/demo`, `/link-expired`,
`/connection-error`, `/classes`, `/diagnostics`, `/device/:id/testmode`, and the fan/light/plug settings
pages (§11).

**There is no chart route.** The chart is section 3 of a `messreihe` Blatt: `/b/:tent/temperatur` *is* the
temperature chart, and it is the chart because you tapped the temperature. C14 said the chart is evidence
behind something you tapped; C36 said everything is one screen; the consequence is that the chart is
`Woher wir das wissen` and has no independent existence.

**Back-compat is persisted user data, not optional:**

```
/device/:id/charts?<q>  → 301 /b/:tent/<measure-from-q>?<q>   (applyViewParams back-compat branch kept)
/device/:id/diary       → 301 /b/:tent
/device/:id/settings    → 301 /b/:tent/ziele   (controller & fridge only; fan/light/plug keep the page)
```

`ShareLink.page` stays the `'charts' | 'diary'` enum — the redirect resolves it, no schema change, no
migration, every link in circulation lives.

### The Zelt above the device — the gap nobody filled

```ts
// NEW collection `tents`. One grow, one diary, one day counter, n devices.
interface Zelt {
  tent_id: string; owner_id: string;
  name: string;                       // "Zelt Keller"
  device_ids: string[];               // 1..n
  timezone: string;                   // IANA. The day counter's clock, named on every axis.
  created_at: number;
}
```

**Migration is silent and total.** On first boot after deploy, every claimed device not already in a tent
gets a `Zelt` containing only itself, named from `device.name`. Nobody is asked anything and nothing
changes for a one-device owner. Grow-side Blätter (`pflanze`, `phase`, `gabe`, `notiz`, `zettel`) key on
`tent_id`; hardware-side ones (`dose`, `kamera`, `ziel`, `ereignis`, `messreihe`) key on `device_id` and
list under their device. **Two devices reporting temperature are never averaged** — the Zelt Blatt lists
`Temperatur · Controller Keller` and `Temperatur · Steckdose Ecke` as two Zeilen. Averaging two sensors is
a lie about a physical gradient, and the gradient is often the interesting part.

### The one object

```ts
// NEW collection `blaetter`. blatt_id minted client-side (uuid v4) → server upserts → idempotent retry.
interface Blatt {
  blatt_id: string;
  tent_id: string;                  // scope. Resolves to owner_id exactly as auth.middleware.ts:172 does.
  device_id?: string;               // hardware-side arts only
  art: BlattArt;
  name: string;                     // "A3 · Wedding Cake", "Heizung (Dose 1)", "Nacht-Temperatur"
  t: number; t_end?: number | null; // every Blatt has a time; explicit null = still open
  rel?: Record<string, string[]>;   // { an, in, betrifft, gilt_fuer, abhilfe }
  d?: Record<string, unknown>;      // art-specific body
  bilder?: string[];
  akteur?: string;                  // blatt_id of a `mensch`
  storniert_von?: string;           // corrections, never hard edits
}

type BlattArt =
  | 'zelt' | 'geraet' | 'dose' | 'kamera'        // the physical world
  | 'pflanze' | 'phase'                          // the grow
  | 'gabe' | 'notiz' | 'zettel' | 'mensch'       // what humans do
  | 'bild' | 'film'                              // pictures
  | 'messreihe' | 'ziel' | 'ereignis' | 'regel'; // numbers, intentions, machine events, rules
```

**Seven arts are stored** (`zelt`, `pflanze`, `phase`, `gabe`, `notiz`, `zettel`, `mensch`). **Nine are
projected read-time:** `geraet` ← `Device` + last Influx point · `dose` ← `hardwareInfo.sockets` via the
existing `parseSocketRoles()` · `kamera` ← `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` + newest `Image` · `bild`/`film` ←
`Image{format:'jpeg'|'user/jpeg'}` / `{format:'mp4'}` (`timestampEnd` gives `t_end`) · `messreihe` ← one
per measure in `VALID_SENSORS ∩ has-data-in-7d` · `ziel` ← `device.configuration` + `Alarm[]` + the new
`setpoint_log` (§6) · `ereignis` ← `DeviceLog` including legacy diary rows · `regel` ← the rule table
(§5.3), one Blatt per rule per tent.

One API: `GET /api/blaetter?tent_id=&art=&von=&bis=&cursor=`, cursor-paginated by `t`. The webapp uses it.
The public API is it. **They cannot drift, because there is only one.**

Load-bearing:

- **The four headings are literal and identical on every Blatt.** They are the app's only chrome. A drunk
  thumb learns four positions, not four hundred labels.
- The four numbers are the existing `valuedisplay` component in a compact variant — it already renders `—`
  for missing values and already draws a target line. Reuse, do not rebuild.
- The title is `Terp Control Controller`, never `devices.fridge.title`. "FRIDGE GROW" dies in this commit;
  a `devices.controller` i18n block ships with it.
- `Zettel` sit **above** part 1, because a human wrote them for another human. Empty ⇒ not rendered.
- `Sonst nichts zu tun.` is not filler. A fourth part that speaks only when something is wrong teaches
  people to fear it; one that says "nothing" out loud is the one they trust.

## 5. NO-MODES MECHANISM

The falsification test: **if you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of
feature, it is a mode.** `webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts` and the `app-settings-expert` key are deleted,
not renamed.

**M1 · Four headings, one order, no density control.** No compact view, no "Erweitert", no remembered
expansion. Every finding is one typographic block carrying prose **and** the number in the same DOM node —
the impaired reader stops at line one, the techie's eye lands on the tabular-nums line. Density is
typography, which every reader filters for free and nobody configures.

**M2 · Depth is subject-distance; the Spur is the only continuous control.** You do not set depth, you
walk, and walking is stateless — nothing the techie does changes what the stoner sees tomorrow on the same
login. The Spur is a physical continuum with detented positions, like the rotary knob the hardware
actually has. Not "presets for beginners, custom range for experts": one gesture whose useful positions
click.

**M3 · Pause is disclosure, and it is section 3, not an overlay.** Playing, part 3 is three lines; tap and
it unfolds in place into the whole minute (§9.4). Nothing persisted, self-cancels on play, reversible by
the identical gesture, hides no feature class. Because it is one of the four permanent headings rather
than a modal, there is no state the app can be "in" — the section is always there, just shorter while
things move.

**M4 · Capability and data decide what exists — never a preference.** Per-role `parseSocketRoles()`, never
the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` collapse. No `dehumidifier` role ⇒ **no humidity `ziel` Blatt
is created**, and the humidity remedy changes shape (H-2 vs H-2b) rather than offering a control that
cannot exist. Missing `hardwareInfo.sockets` fails **closed** with one Zeile: `Gerät meldet keine
Steckdosen — Firmware zu alt.` No `pflanze` ⇒ the word "Pflanze" appears in exactly one place: the `+` on
`Im Zelt`. Neither is reversible by a setting, which is what makes them not modes.

### 5.3 The remedy engine — deterministic rules, and the rule is a Blatt

Every rule is a row in a Mongo `regeln` collection (content, not code — tunable without a deploy). The
engine refuses to register a rule whose `ziel_art` is empty:

```ts
interface Regel {
  regel_id: string;                  // "N-3"
  gilt_fuer: BlattArt[];             // where the card can appear
  bedingung: string;                 // declarative predicate, evaluated server-side. No expressions.
  fenster_tage: number;              // evidence window
  mindest_belege: number;            // qualifying intervals required before it fires
  benoetigt_rolle?: string;          // 'heater' | 'dehumidifier' | … — capability precondition
  ziel_art: BlattArt;                // MANDATORY. No destination, no rule.
  ziel_feld?: string;                // 'night.temperature'
  vorschlag: 'senken'|'heben'|'pruefen'|'nachtragen'|'anschliessen'|'zettel';
  schritt?: number;                  // proposed delta, computed and clamped
  text_de: string; text_en: string;  // one sentence, %-interpolated with the measured numbers
}
```

Fourteen rules ship. Every one reads only data the app already has.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| G-1 | no MQTT status > 10 min | `Gerät offline seit 3 Std 12` / `Zuletzt gesehen 24.08. um 14:20.` | `geraet` |

Plus five **setup rules** that exist only in the first 48 hours (§12). A rule requiring kit that is not
paired does not fire — it is not greyed out, it does not exist.

**Three buttons, and what each writes:**
- `[ Blatt öffnen ]` walks to the destination with the proposed value **pre-typed and unsaved** in its
  `value-edit-row`. The human presses save. **The app never changes a setpoint.**
- `[ Anheften ]` writes a `zettel` carrying the rule's sentence, `opened_by` = the current `mensch`. This
  is how a remedy becomes the club's shared to-do without a task system.
- `[ Passt so ]` writes `regel.stumm_bis = now + fenster_tage × 3` for this tent, with the reason visible
  on the `regel` Blatt. A deterministic, auditable snooze, not a dismissal.

**The `regel` Blatt.** `Regel N-3` is a link on every card, and its Blatt has the same four parts:
*Was es ist* — the rule in a sentence with its thresholds; *Was sich geändert hat* — `Feuert seit 21.08.`;
*Woher wir das wissen* — the Spur set to the evidence window with the five qualifying nights marked,
playable; *Was du tun kannst* — `Für dieses Zelt abschalten` / `Schwelle ändern`. Turning advice off is a
walk to a Blatt, not a preference screen, so it is not a mode either.

**What the engine refuses.** No rule may cite watering volume as a cause (no irrigation hardware, and the
app cannot know what the human did between logs). No rule may name a deficiency, a pest or a disease. No
rule fires below `mindest_belege`, and none fires on a single excursion — a spike is part 2's job.

## 7. Diary and multi-plant

There is no diary feature. **The tent's `Verlauf` is the diary**; a plant's `Verlauf` is that plant's
diary; same Zeilen, same renderer. What "es macht das Tagebuchführen für dich" means operationally:
`ereignis`, `bild`, `film`, `phase` and `ziel`-changes are written by the machine into the same list the
human writes into, so a user who logs nothing still opens a full diary.

**Every entry gets its picture automatically** (C14's idea, kept whole). On write the server sets
`gabe.d.auto_bild` = the nearest *kept* frame within ±5 min. The user uploads nothing, it costs one Mongo
lookup, and it is what makes the double-feed guard evidential rather than nagging (§8).

```ts
pflanze  d: { sorte?, medium?, topf_l?, quelle?:'samen'|'steckling'|'gekauft',
              keimung_t, ernte_t?, ernte_g?, entfernt_t? }
         name: "A3 · Wedding Cake"     // renaming NEVER changes blatt_id
         rel: { in: [tent_id] }
phase    d: { stufe: DiaryLifecycleStage }   t / t_end
         rel: { an: [pflanze…] }       // empty = the whole tent
         // belongs to the TENT. One grow across four devices = ONE day counter.
gabe     d: { wasser_l, kannen?, kannen_l?, ec?, ph?, ec_basis:'absolut'|'plus_leitungswasser',
              ablauf_ph?, ablauf_ec?, produkte:[{name, ml_pro_l, aus_schema:boolean}],
              schema_id?, schritt?, auto_bild? }
         rel: { an: [pflanze…] }, akteur, bilder
notiz    d: { text }                   rel: { an: [...] }, bilder
zettel   d: { text, geschlossen_von? } t / t_end(null = offen), akteur
mensch   d: {}                         name only, tent-scoped, no account
```

`DiaryLifecycleStage` gains `ernte` as a seventh stage — harvest is not a stage today, so "how did this
grow go" is unanswerable. Optional field, read-time normaliser, no migration tool.
`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a **one-time idempotent boot backfill** emitting `pflanze` + `phase`
Blätter from historic `diary-plant-lifecycle` rows; nothing is deleted, and the string-comparison cycle
inference that forks history on a rename dies with it.

**The plant-ignorer is untaxed.** `rel.an` absent = the whole tent, and absent is the default forever. The
chooser is not rendered until a `pflanze` exists; the only door is `+` on `Im Zelt`. A monitor-only user
can run three years and never learn the product has plants. **Server-side pagination for `blaetter` and
`DeviceLog` is a v1 prerequisite** — the product is lists, and there is no log pagination today.

**Tap counts, counted honestly** (`Gießen` on the home Blatt is tap 1):

| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine water, same as last time | `Gießen` → `Eintragen` | **2** |
| Routine water, guard fires | `Gießen` → `Trotzdem eintragen` | **2** |

```
Substrat & Dünger
  ( ) Biobizz Light-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom    Düngen ab Woche 2
  (•) Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom      Vorgedüngt — Düngen ab Woche 3–4
  ( ) Floragard Light + Green House Powder Feeding
  ( ) BioTabs                                   Kaum Flüssigdünger — neue Tabs alle 4–6 Wochen
  ( ) Kein Schema — ich trage selbst ein
  Zuletzt geprüft: 12.08.2026 · Quelle: Herstellerplan
```

Mongo content collection, never in the bundle; brand names as plain text only, no logos, each entry with
`quelle_url` + `geprueft_am` (surfaced as `Zuletzt geprüft` — legal posture and trust feature in one
string). **A scheme that cannot say "don't feed yet" is not modelling the domain:** All-Mix at day 12
prefills *no products* and one line — `All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen.` with
`[ Trotzdem düngen ]`. The step advances **by feed events** (`Schritt 5 von 12`), never by wall clock,
with a soft calendar mapping (`etwa Woche 5`), because growers feed Tue/Fri and slip. Prefilled values are
stored `aus_schema: true` and flip to `false` on edit, so the club report can say `wie im Plan` vs
`abweichend` months later. EC is canonical mS/cm; `plus_leitungswasser` renders as
`EC-Ziel 1,4 + dein Leitungswasser`, never a bare ppm. Autoflower gets a `×0,25–0,5` multiplier and no
12/12 flip event — no manufacturer chart has an auto column.

**Double-feed guard.** At sheet open, query `gabe` in the guard window whose `rel.an` intersects the
selection (whole-tent intersects everything). Windows per medium: water 6 h / feed 18 h; coco 3 h / 12 h;
owner-editable, stored in Mongo. A *different* `mensch` → soft block, the button relabels to
`Trotzdem eintragen` so muscle memory cannot fire it. The *same* `mensch` → informational only
(`Du hast vor 40 Min gegossen.`), because self-repeats are usually mis-taps. `[ Bild ansehen ]` opens the
other person's `auto_bild`: **you can see whether the soil is dark.** That is the camera doing club work
no other concept asks of it.

**Double-*logging*** is prevented by construction: `blatt_id` is a client-minted uuid v4 and the server
upserts on `{tent_id, blatt_id}`, so a retry is idempotent. Without a service worker (v1) a failed save is
held in `localStorage`, retried on app focus, and renders `⏳ noch nicht gesendet`. That is not
capture-in-a-cellar and the concept says so.

**The cumulative question** is answered on the `pflanze` Blatt's part 1, where it is asked:
`Bisher in dieser Blüte · Wasser 18,5 l über 11 Gaben · Bio-Bloom 31 ml · von Anna 7 · Tom 3 · ich 1`.

## 9. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

### 9.1 What exists, and the three flags

A frame every 30 s over MQTT, stored as `Image {…, data: Buffer, format, duration}`
(`server/src/models/images.model.ts:22`). `sharp` 0.34.5, an ImageMagick wrapper and `ffmpeg` are already
dependencies; rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses are already built hourly. There is **no GridFS anywhere**
and BSON caps a document at 16 MB.

The rolling films encode **libx265** (does not play in Firefox, Chrome on Linux, or half of Android), have
**no `-pix_fmt yuv420p`** and **no `+faststart`**. Fixing those three flags is half a day and is the
highest-value change in this document.

### 9.2 The cull, at ingest — ~60 ms/frame, no ML runtime

Two `sharp` decodes (`resize(128,96).greyscale().raw()`, `resize(64,48).raw()`) plus plain JS, written to
a `FrameMetrics {image_id, device_id, timestamp, ok, reject_reason, dhash, brightness, sharpness,
green_frac, tiles[48], shift_dx, shift_dy, light_state, light_pct}` sibling document (~200 B), kept out of
`Image` so scoring queries never drag Buffers.

`light_state` comes from `out_light` in Influx at ts ± 60 s. **We own the lamp**; every competitor infers
it from pixels. Reject reasons: `lights_off` (two conditions — `light_pct < 5` **and** `brightness < 25`,
so a grower whose lamp is not on our socket does not lose the film), `transient` (a bounded burst: frames
n…n+k each differ from n−1 by > 4× the rolling median while n+k+1 returns below 1,5× — a hand, a door, a
torch), `blur`, `duplicate` (dHash Hamming < 3), `camera_jump`. **Plus, free: any frame within ±3 min of a
human-authored `gabe`/`notiz`/`bild` is dropped, because a human told us they were standing there.**

**Explicitly refused as computer-vision fantasy:** naming a drooping leaf, diagnosing a deficiency from
colour, height in cm, counting plants, segmenting canopy, detecting pests. `green_frac` is labelled
`Blattfläche (Bildanteil)`, never `Wachstum`.

### 9.3 The film — and HOW IT IS STORED

**GridFS.** A new bucket `films` via `mongoose.mongo.GridFSBucket` — no new dependency, no new docker
volume, no second backup story (Mongo is already backed up; a `RENDER_DIR` volume would not be). Renders
stream out with real Range support; metadata lives in a small `Film` document, the bytes do not. `Image`
is untouched, so stills and the existing rolling timelapses are unaffected.

That is the honest answer to "a whole-grow film cannot be stored": **a 12-week 720p film at 24 fps, 60 s,
crf 21 is 12–20 MB** — over the BSON limit and therefore genuinely unstorable today. GridFS chunks it at
255 KB and the cap disappears. ~1 day, and it removes the "v1 caps films at 12 MB / 90 s" apology every
camera concept had to write.

Pacing: one canonical frame per day at the photoperiod midpoint ±30 min, so leaves are always in the same
praying/drooping phase and the film stops twitching; length allocated by stage (seedling 10 / veg 30 /
flower 50 / dry+cure 10 %); a user's own photo is never culled. **Deflicker:** target luminance = the
run's median, `.linear(a,b)` with `a` clamped to [0,75; 1,35] **and its change per frame clamped to ±3 %**
— three lines that remove the strobing which makes every DIY tent timelapse unwatchable. **White balance
per lighting regime, not per frame**, gains clamped to [0,6; 1,8] so a purple grow still looks purple.
Stabilisation is translation only; a sustained shift re-baselines and cuts with a title card
`Kamera neu ausgerichtet · Tag 12`. Burn-in via SVG composite (the already-unit-tested `addOfflineOverlay`
technique): `Tag 24 · Blüte · 21.08. 14:02 · 24,8 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,29`.

```sh
ffmpeg -y -framerate 24 -f image2 -i "$DIR/%05d.jpg" \
  -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -preset medium -crf 21 \
  -g 48 -movflags +faststart -metadata title="Terp Control – Zelt Keller · Tag 1–84" out.mp4
```

**Off the phone**, three ranked paths: (1) `navigator.share({files:[File]})` guarded by
`navigator.canShare`, capped at 50 MB — on iOS 15+ and Chrome Android the share sheet's *Video sichern*
puts it straight in Photos and offers WhatsApp/Telegram directly; (2) `GET /film/:film_id.mp4?token=` with
`Content-Disposition: attachment`, `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, and the existing 30-day URL-embeddable token,
so no session cookie is needed; (3) a pasteable link that plays for 30 days with no login. Filename
ASCII-folded: `terpcontrol_zelt-keller_tag1-tag84_2026-10-14_720p.mp4`. **A share that produces a login
wall is not a share.**

**The cull report is shown, not hidden**, because it is the only thing that explains why this film is
watchable when a competitor's is not:
`2 148 Bilder · −611 Licht aus · −244 doppelt · −98 Besuch · −15 unscharf = 1 180 → 49 Sek`.

### 9.4 THE SLIDER — die Spur

**What it scrubs: the Blatt's own Spanne, whatever the Blatt is about.** It is not a film player; it is
the span control and the cursor in one, and its content is art-specific while its behaviour never is:

| Blatt | The band is made of | Vorher/Jetzt compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zelt`, `pflanze`, `kamera`, `bild` | camera thumbnails, positioned by timestamp | two frames |
| `messreihe` | min/max value bars per bucket | two readings + time-in-range |
| `dose` | on/off regions | runtime, duty cycle |
| `ziel` | step marks at each recorded change | old value → new value |
| `mensch` | ticks at that person's entries | what they did then vs now |
| `regel` | the evidence window, qualifying intervals marked | when it started firing |
| `phase`, `film` | thumbnails over the span | first frame vs last frame |

**Two markers, one gesture.** `◀ Vorher` drags with C14's snap ladder (`seit zuletzt`, `gestern`,
`1 Woche`, `Blütebeginn`, `letzte Gabe`, `Start`) at 44 px magnetic zones; a deliberate finger between
snaps lands on any minute and the exact timestamp prints under the band as you drag. `● Jetzt` is C11's
playhead, defaulting to the right edge.

**What moves with it:**
- Part 2 renders from **the pair**. Drag Vorher from `gestern` to `1 Woche` and every sentence and number
  re-derives.
- Part 3 renders from **the playhead**: the pinned scrub header, the frame, the chart cursor, the output
  lanes. `▶` sweeps from Vorher to Jetzt; part 2 stays still while part 3 moves, so something on screen is
  always a stable anchor.
- Part 4 re-evaluates over **the span**, subject to each rule's `mindest_belege`.
- Correlation runs both directions: tap a thumbnail → the playhead jumps there; in the unfolded panel
  `Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` jumps to the next excursion; `Beleg ansehen` on a remedy card sets both
  markers to the rule's evidence window.

**Pause is the disclosure gesture.** Tap the frame or the band; the sweep stops and part 3 unfolds in
place:

```
WOHER WIR DAS WISSEN                    ⏸
  [ frame at 21.08. 03:12 ]
  21.08.2026 03:12 · Tag 23 · Blüte · Nacht
  Temperatur   20,5 °C   Ziel 20,0   +0,5
  Luftfeuchte  62 %      Ziel 60     +2      → tap a row = that messreihe Blatt, this span
  VPD          1,08 kPa  Band 1,2–1,5   ✗

  Einträge ±2 Std: keine
  Bild: belichtet ×1,04 · WB-Regime „Licht 0 %" · Schärfe 0,71 · behalten
  [ Nächster Moment über 30 °C → ]    [ Rohdaten ]
```

Nothing persisted, no `localStorage` key, self-cancels on `▶`, reversible by the identical gesture, and it
hides no feature class — it is the data about the minute you are looking at. The techie reads eleven rows,
the stoner reads three, on the same DOM.

**Delivery.** v1 fetches thumbnails through the existing `?width=160` resize with `max-age=3600` over
HTTP/2 — survivable. A sprite+index endpoint (`GET /film/strip/:device_id?from&to&cols=10` → one JPEG of
100 tiles at 160×90 plus `[{t,x,y}]`, 24 h TTL) is v1.1 and is what makes scrubbing feel instant over tent
wifi.

### 9.5 Camera alarms

`webcam_silence` (K-1) and `light_mismatch` (L-1) are created by default the moment a camera is paired.
They reuse `Alarm` storage and `handleAlarmAction` (`alarm.service.ts:144`) but need their own ~80-line
evaluator ticker, because `alarm.service` only evaluates on inbound MQTT `StatusMessage` and neither is a
sensor threshold. **`light_mismatch` is the whole correlation thesis in one alarm: neither the picture nor
the data can detect a failed contactor alone, and together they detect it in fifteen minutes.**

## 10. Clubs, and reaching a closed app

**Attribution is a label; authorisation is a gate. v1 ships the label and says so, once**, under the name
list: `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann jeden Namen wählen.`

- A `mensch` Blatt is tent-scoped, named once by the owner, no account, **no `auth.middleware.ts`
  surgery** — ~2 days. The `Wer?` chip row renders only at ≥ 2 `mensch` Blätter: **the club feature IS the
  second name**, which is how a club exists without a club mode. Last-used actor sticky in `localStorage`,
  so the 2-tap path survives.
- The **double-feed guard with the frame as evidence** (§8) and **`Zettel`** above everything (§4).
- **`[ Anheften ]` on a remedy card** is this concept's specific club contribution: the rule engine's
  output becomes the note on the tent door, owned by whoever pinned it, closed by whoever fixes it. The
  club's shared to-do list is generated, not maintained.
- **Corrections, not edits.** Editing a `gabe` writes a new one with `storniert_von` at the old, rendered
  struck through — reconstructable history without cryptographic immutability, which would victimise the
  unregulated grower.
- **Upgrade path designed in:** `Blatt.akteur` already points at a `mensch`; a later real account sets
  `mensch.d.user_id` and every historic row keeps resolving. Real `Membership` + rewriting
  `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` + loosening ~10 owner-scoped queries is v1.1, stated plainly.

**Nothing reaches a closed app — the honest fix that fits in v1.** There is no push and no service worker,
and building both is not six weeks' work we have. But `Alarm.actionType: 'webhook'` already exists with
configurable method, headers and payload templates (`device.model.ts:85-88`, `alarm.service.ts:250`), and
**a Telegram bot `sendMessage` URL is a GET webhook.** So v1 ships a `Benachrichtigung` Blatt with a
three-field builder (bot token, chat id, test button) that writes a webhook alarm, plus a `Zusammenfassung`
job: **the tent's Blatt rendered as text — the four parts in order, remedy cards included** — pushed on
rule fire and every Monday 08:00, and mailed via the existing `sendMail`. A lock-screen notification,
today, with zero new infrastructure and one docs page. **Real Web Push and a service worker are v1.1**,
and the same service worker is what turns the `localStorage` retry queue into genuine cellar capture.

**Export and an API credential**, both missing today, both cheap: `ApiKey {key_hash, user_id, tent_ids[],
created_at, last_used}` plus one `x-api-key` branch in `auth.middleware`; `GET /api/blaetter?…` (the
identical union the webapp consumes, so it cannot rot) plus `GET /api/export/:tent_id.json` and `.csv` for
the whole grow. ~2 days, and it converts the most sceptical segment into the one that writes the Home
Assistant card for us.

**Privacy posture — privacy-maximal by default.** Harvest weight and plant count are never prompted (a
per-tent switch, off by default, adds the field; off means the field does not exist). No location, no
analytics SDK, no community feed. Free-text names, `Vorname oder Spitzname reicht`. **`Alles löschen`
actually deletes** `blaetter`, `Image`, `Film`, `FrameMetrics`, `DeviceLog` and the Influx series — fixing
the standing lie where `deleted` is a visibility flag. Retention stated: raw frames 90 days, metrics with
them, films until deleted. **Refused outright:** per-member dispensing, grams handed over, THC content,
member identity, date of birth. No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.

## 11. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

A `fan`/`light`/`plug` device is a Zelt with one member and a thin body. **Same four headings, minus what
the hardware does not have. Nothing is stubbed, disabled or greyed.**

```

   22,1 °C     51 %     Schalter: an

 ▲ Länger an — 4 Std 20 statt 3 Std 55 (+25 Min)
```

- **Non-breakage:** `/charts` and `/diary` redirect with the query string intact; `ShareLink.page`

### How it looks — the visual spec nobody wrote

- **Grid** 4 px; vertical rhythm 8 px; section gap 24 px; page gutter 16 px; card radius 12 px; a
  `1px --tc-line` hairline between Zeilen, never a box per row.
- **Type scale** (system stack, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on every value):
  `28/500` primary number · `20/600` Blatt name · `17/600` finding prose · `15/500` a destination line ·
  `14/400` value lines and Zeilen · `13/600 --tc-text-muted` the four headings · `12/400` provenance.
  Six sizes, no seventh.
- **Colour**: status is carried by a 12 px square, never by the text — `--ion-color-success` / `warning` /
  `danger`, plus **hollow = veraltet**, so colour-blind and dark-tent readers both get shape. Findings use
  `▲ / ● / ○ / ✎`, which survive greyscale, screenshots and a 2 am screen.
- **Touch**: 44 px minimum on every target; the Spur's snap zones are 44 px; the four action buttons are
  56 px tall and equal width.
- **The one illustration.** A single line-drawn leaf whose five veins are the four part rules plus the
  Spur: 96 px, monochrome `--tc-text-muted`, one inline SVG. It appears in exactly one place — a tent with
  no data at all — over `Noch nichts passiert. Was hier geschieht, steht ab jetzt auf diesem Blatt.` The
  same silhouette is the app's loading skeleton, so it reads as a shape people recognise, not a mascot.

## 14. Honest weaknesses

1. **Fourteen rules is a small number and the fifteenth is where the trouble starts.** Every rule I wrote
   is defensible because it reads a setpoint, a socket runtime or a photoperiod — things we own. The
   requests will be for what we do not own: "why are the leaves yellow", "should I flush". The engine's
   honest answer is silence, and silence under the fourth heading looks like the feature is broken even
   when `Sonst nichts zu tun.` is printed. **The fourth part sets an expectation the data cannot always
   meet, and that is the price of making it a heading.**

2. **A wrong remedy is worse than no remedy, and N-3 can be wrong.** A tent running 2,4 K over its night
   target for five nights might have a heater on a socket we do not own, an open door, or a hot summer.
   N-3 will confidently propose lowering the target, which is exactly wrong if the grower wanted 20 °C and
   cannot reach it. I mitigate by never writing the value, by printing the evidence count, and by making
   the proof scrubbable — but the card still *reads* as advice, and people will follow it without ever
   opening the Beleg.

3. **Sixteen arts is three more than C36 had, and the ratchet turns one way.** `messreihe` and `regel` are
   genuinely new shapes, added because the chart and the rule engine needed a home. Every future feature
   will arrive as "add an art". The guard — a concrete German noun a grower says out loud, a bespoke
   Körper, and a non-empty fourth part — is a rule, and rules erode under deadline.

4. **The Spur is the hardest thing here to get right on a phone, and it is on every screen.** Two
   draggable markers, a snap ladder, a sweep, tap-to-pause and a shared cursor into a chart, inside 56 px
   on a 390 px viewport, in a dark tent, one-handed. Five days is optimistic. If it is mediocre it is
   mediocre *everywhere*, because unlike C11 it is not confined to a film tab.

5. **Uniformity flattens importance.** `◼ Heizung aus` and `▲ Feuchte 40 Min über 65 %` are the same 48
   pixels in the same list. Ranking rests on one status square, part 2's ordering and the `Zettel` strip,
   and that is thin. A ranked-density concept is better at this by construction.

6. **`green_frac` is a weak proxy for growth and saying so does not make it stronger.** It confounds
   growth with leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift, and under magenta LED it is near noise.
   It is the most visible number in part 2 and the softest.

7. **Setpoint history starts on deploy day.** Every chart over data older than October 2026 still cannot
   show what the target was; the dotted grey line is an admission, not a fix. For a user with a year of
   history the chart half-lies for another year.

8. **Nothing reaches a closed app unless the user sets up a Telegram bot.** The webhook path is real and
   free, but "create a bot with @BotFather and paste the token" is a five-step technical errand that user
   types 1, 2 and 6 will not do. The people who most need to hear that the camera died are the least
   likely to finish it. Real push is v1.1 and needs the service worker we cut.

9. **The tent abstraction is right and almost nobody will use it.** Multi-device grows are rare; the
   migration and the `tent_id` refactor cost three days to buy one honest day counter for a minority. I
   would still do it, because retrofitting a room above `device_id` later is far worse — but nobody asked.

10. **CPU is a real cost.** `FrameMetrics` at ~60 ms × 2 880 frames/day × 150 devices ≈ 7 CPU-hours/day on
    top of the existing ffmpeg poll, and a render is another 90 s of a core. The server is one pm2 process
    with `pLimit(10)`. This needs a real job queue and probably a second box before the first series sells
    out.

11. **The double-feed guard's windows are guesses people will learn to tap through.** Per-medium defaults
    help; they do not fix it. A guard dismissed reflexively is worse than none, because it manufactures
    the belief that the app checked.

12. **Part 4 changes the shape of a screen whose whole pitch is calm.** Four parts plus a Zettel strip
    plus relations plus history is a long scroll, and part 4 sits above the relations — so the day
    something fires, the home screen moves under a user who was promised it never would. I put it there on
    purpose. It is still the one place a critic will push hardest.
