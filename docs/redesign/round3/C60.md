# C60 — "Der Zeuge"

**Fusion of C14 (Was hat sich geändert) · C36 (Ein Objekt) · C11's shared-cursor slider**
**Centre of gravity:** every claim the app makes carries its evidence as a walkable relation, and the
strongest evidence is a photograph taken at that minute.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Nothing in Terp Control is asserted without its witness attached. Every sentence, every number, every
proposed remedy carries a `Beleg` — a named relation you can walk into — and standing on that Beleg you
find a pair of pictures, a raw sample window, or the output that actually ran.**

Two customers have the same complaint in two dialects. The techie says *"averaged data presented as
truth"*. The stoner says *"why should I believe you"*. Both are asking **woher weißt du das?** — and
nobody in this market answers it. C60 answers it once, with one mechanism, on every screen.

The discipline, stated as a rule the codebase enforces:

> **Eine Aussage ohne Beleg wird nicht angezeigt.**
> If a claim cannot name its evidence, it does not render. Not greyed out — absent.

That rule turns three good ideas into one product:

- Because everything is a **Ding** with one shape (C36), the witness has somewhere to live: another
  relation, rendered as a Zeile, walked with the one gesture the app has.
- Because the strongest witness is a **picture pair with the difference named in one sentence** (C14),
  the evidence is legible to somebody who cannot read a chart. A photograph is the only proof that works
  at 2 a.m.
- Because a single **shared cursor** governs every projection (C11), the claim is *testable*: drag the
  cursor and watch the claim's own numbers move. A claim you cannot move the cursor on is a slogan.

And it pays for the thing sixty concepts failed. A remedy — *"Nachtziel auf 22,0 °C setzen"* — is only
trustworthy if you can see why. C60 can afford to propose remedies precisely because it never proposes
one without its witnesses, and the user can tear each one apart before accepting it.

**This is not an advisor.** Remedies are a fixed, published set of deterministic rules over data the app
already holds (§6). No model, no inference, no agronomy. The app describes differences (C14's
discipline, kept verbatim) and proposes only changes **it can write itself**.

## 2. What comes from where — and the fusion

**From C14:** the picture pair as the primary object (`Vorher`/`Jetzt`, tap for the Wischvergleich) · the
**Befund** — one difference named in one German sentence with its numbers on the line beneath ·
**describe, never diagnose** (`Blattfläche im Bild 34 → 37 %`, never `Stickstoffmangel`) · deterministic
**printed** ranking, σ₁₄ with a per-measure floor · `auto_image_id` on every diary entry.

**From C36:** **ein Ding** — name, one time, a body, named German relations · one screen shape, Zeile when
related and Tafel when Subjekt, no third size, no expand control · **depth is walking**: tap a Zeile, it
becomes the Subjekt — the whole navigation grammar · **`Werte {…}`** as the last row of every Tafel,
showing the literal `GET` · reveal by **capability** (per-role `parseSocketRoles`, failing closed) and by
**data** · corrections, not edits (`storniert_von`).

**From C11 — the slider (§9.4):** a **scrub bar** with the playhead marked `jetzt` · **one cursor, every
projection** — drag any and all move, correlation in both directions · **pause is the disclosure
gesture**, unfolding everything known about that minute, persisted nowhere, self-cancelling on play,
hiding no feature class · a **pinned scrub header**, never a tooltip, because there is no hover on touch.

### THE FUSION — the idea that exists only because all three were combined

**Der Beleg ist ein Ding, und der Zeiger ist eine Spanne.**

**(a) The witness relation.** C36 gives every object a uniform frame with named relations; C14 gives the
most persuasive evidence a grow controller can produce. Together, evidence stops being a footnote:
**`Beleg` becomes a first-class relation with the same rendering, the same tap target and the same depth
as `an`, `in` and `betrifft`.** Tap `Beleg` on a Befund and you stand on the frame pair. Tap it on a
`ziel` change and you stand on the Maßnahme that caused it. Tap it on the Maßnahme and you stand on the
41 raw samples and the output lane showing the heater ran zero minutes. **The evidence chain is the
navigation graph.**

**(b) The cursor is C14's pair.** C11's slider has one playhead; C14's primary object is a *pair*. Fused,
the cursor becomes **two handles on one track** — `◖ Vorher` and `◗ Jetzt` — and every projection renders
from the span between them: both frames, both number columns, the difference sentence, the chart window,
the Beleg strip, the diary rail. **Press ▶ and the right handle advances while the left trails at a fixed
window width: the pair becomes a film of differences, and the German sentence re-writes itself on every
frame.** C14's static object turned into C11's film, with neither bolted onto the other.

**(c) Because every Ding has a time, every Ding can be scrubbed.** C36's uniformity means the slider is
not a camera widget — it is part of the Tafel frame, like the Kopf. One rule, thirteen behaviours (§9.4),
no mode, **no camera required**: a plug owner scrubs values.

**Remove any one source and it collapses:** without C36 the witness has nowhere to live and degenerates
into a tooltip; without C14 the witness is a data row and the impaired user is excluded again; without
C11's cursor the claim is frozen at one instant and cannot be cross-examined.

## 3. Information architecture

Four routes. Nothing else is a screen.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | Deine Zelte. Exactly one ⇒ straight through, as today. |
| `/z/:tent_id/:thing_id?` | **The browser.** One component. `thing_id` absent ⇒ the Zelt is the Subjekt. |
| `/z/:tent_id/verlauf?von=&bis=&mass=` | The chart. The only non-Tafel screen (§7). |
| `/z/:tent_id/film/:film_id` | One rendered film, playable + downloadable. |

Untouched: `/login`, `/account`, `/shares`, `/demo`, `/classes`, `/diagnostics`, `/device/:id/testmode`,
and — deliberately — `/device/:id/settings` for `fan`/`light`/`plug`/`dryer` (§12).

**Back-compat is not optional** (share links and chart presets are persisted user data):

```
/device/:id/diary        → 301 /z/<tent_of(id)>
/device/:id/charts?<q>   → 301 /z/<tent_of(id)>/verlauf?<q>   (applyViewParams keeps its
                                                                measures/date/timespan branch)
/device/:id/settings     → 301 /z/<tent_of(id)>/ziele          (controller & fridge only)
```

`ShareLink.page` keeps `'charts' | 'diary'` — no enum change, no migration. `resolveDeviceAccessInfo`
(`locked`, `canEdit`, `webcamAllowed`, `chartsAllowed`) is evaluated per device and intersected across
the tent's devices; `webcam:false` collapses the pair to the Wertepaar (§12).

### Das Zelt über dem Gerät — the missing object

```ts
// NEW collection. Fixes: four devices = four diaries = four disagreeing day counters.
interface Tent {
  tent_id: string; owner_id: string;
  name: string;                    // "Zelt Keller"
  device_ids: string[];            // 1..n. A plug measuring the same tent belongs here.
  primary_device_id: string;       // whose daynight/timezone drives the day counter
  created_at: number;
}
```

**Migration is automatic and lossless.** On first login after deploy, one `Tent` per claimed device,
`name = device.name || 'Zelt'` — nothing to answer, no wizard. Merging is one action, `+ Gerät
hinzufügen`, appending to `device_ids`; a device can be moved out again and nothing is destroyed either
way, because `things` are keyed by `tent_id` and derived arts keep their own `device_id`. **No auth
rewrite:** `Tent.owner_id` is the `owner_id` `auth.middleware.ts:172` already checks, and every
`device_id` is verified against it on resolve. ~2 days.

### The one object

```ts
// NEW collection `things`. Extends C36's model with exactly one field: `beleg`.
interface Ding {
  thing_id: string;                 // uuid v4, MINTED BY THE CLIENT (idempotent offline replay)
  tent_id: string;                  // the scope
  device_id?: string;               // only on device-bound arts (dose, kamera, bild, ereignis)
  art: DingArt;
  name: string;
  t: number; t_end?: number | null; // every Ding has a time. explicit null = still open
  rel?: Record<string, string[]>;   // { an: [], in: [], betrifft: [] }
  beleg?: Beleg[];                  // ← THE FUSION FIELD
  d?: Record<string, unknown>;      // art-specific body
  akteur?: string;                  // thing_id of a `mensch`
  storniert_von?: string;
}

type Beleg =
  | { art: 'bild';    image_id: string; t: number }
  | { art: 'messung'; mass: string; von: number; bis: number; n: number; abdeckung: number }
  | { art: 'ausgang'; rolle: string; von: number; bis: number; laufzeit_s: number }
  | { art: 'ding';    thing_id: string };

type DingArt =
  | 'zelt' | 'geraet' | 'pflanze' | 'dose' | 'kamera'   // the physical world
  | 'bild' | 'film'                                      // pictures
  | 'gabe' | 'notiz' | 'zettel' | 'phase'                // what humans do
  | 'ziel' | 'mensch' | 'ereignis'                       // intentions, people, machine events
  | 'befund' | 'massnahme';                              // differences and remedies
```

**Belegstufe** — every claim prints its own confidence, never as a percentage:
`gemessen` (raw samples, ≥ 80 % coverage) · `gesehen` (a frame pair backs it) · `gerechnet` (VPD,
time-in-band) · `angenommen` (schedule-derived where measurement is missing) · `unbelegt` — **and an
unbelegte Aussage never renders.**

**Seven arts are stored** — `pflanze`, `gabe`, `notiz`, `zettel`, `phase`, `mensch`, and `ziel` (which is
how setpoint history starts existing, §7). The rest are **projected read-time**: `zelt`/`geraet` from
`Device` + Influx, `dose` from `hardwareInfo.sockets` via `parseSocketRoles()`, `kamera` from
`hardwareInfo.webcam_did`, `bild`/`film` from `Image`, `ereignis` from `DeviceLog`.

**`befund` and `massnahme` are computed and never stored** — the guard against C36's named weakness (art
proliferation): the two arts this concept adds cost zero collections. What *is* stored when a Maßnahme
is accepted is an ordinary `ziel` Ding whose `beleg` points at the Maßnahme's witnesses.

One API: `GET /things?tent_id=&art=&von=&bis=&cursor=`, cursor-paginated by `t`. The webapp uses it, the
public API is it, they cannot drift.

## 5. NO-MODES MECHANISM

**The falsification test:** *if you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of
feature, it is a mode.*

**N1 — Depth is Belegtiefe, not Nutzertiefe.** No expand control, no density setting, no remembered
expansion state. A Ding is one line when related, a full Tafel when it is the Subjekt. **You do not set
depth; you walk into evidence.** The stoner walks zero steps: two pictures, four sentences, four buttons.
The techie walks four: Befund → Beleg → the raw sample window → `Werte {…}` → the `GET` URL. Identical
operation, identical objects, nobody profiled or given a switch. And **walking is stateless** — nothing
the techie does today changes what the stoner sees tomorrow on the same login. That is what a mode cannot
promise, and it is why this concept also refuses adaptive ordering: an impaired user needs the home
screen to be in the same place at 2 a.m. that it was at noon.

**N2 — Three registers, one DOM node.** Prose, number and provenance occupy the same block. Density is
achieved by typography, which every reader filters for free and no reader configures. No "Werte
anzeigen" preference, no compact/detailed toggle, no `Erweitert` accordion — and
`localStorage['app-settings-expert']` (`webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts`) is **deleted**, not renamed, in
the commit that ships this screen.

**N3 — Der Zeiger ersetzt jede Voreinstellung.** No saved time range, no default-span setting, no "last
used view". The cursor is the app's only view state, it is **transient** and **shared**, and because it
is a physical continuum with detented positions (§9.4) the coarse user and the precise user use the
identical control — the way a rotary knob serves both. (The hardware has a rotary knob.)
**Pause-to-unfold is not a mode** for C11's four reasons, intact: nothing is persisted; the identical
gesture reverses it in under 200 ms; it self-cancels on `▶`; and it hides no feature class.

**N4 — Capability and data decide what exists.** Per-role capability from `hardwareInfo.sockets`,
**failing closed**: a heater-only tent never gets a humidity `ziel`, Befund or dehumidifier alarm preset
— killing the live bug where `deviceControlCapability()` (`grow-presets.ts:143-173`) returns `'full'` for
it. Zero `pflanze` Dinge ⇒ no plant chooser and the word "Pflanze" appears once. One `mensch` ⇒ no `Wer?`
row. No camera ⇒ no pair, no film. Neither is reversible by a setting — which is what makes them not
modes.

### Honest audit of my own design

| Suspect | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| The **`Beleg` chip** | **The closest call, and I put it on every row.** Defence: it is a *link to a different Ding*, not a reveal — the row's numbers are complete without it, and the chip prints what is on the other end (`📷+41`), so nothing is concealed. A hostile critic will still call it a "more" button, and I would rather defend this than hide provenance behind a preference. |
| `… 5 weitere Werte` | Not a mode. Expands the same list in place under a printed sort key; the hidden rows are the low-ranked ones by construction. A long list, not a disclosure control. |
| `Werte {…}` | Not a mode — same row, same position, every Tafel, from install, for everybody. A page footer. |
| `Δ` chip | Not a mode. Changes how one image is drawn, like pinch-zoom. |
| `▶` play | Not a mode. It moves the cursor; the cursor is transient and shared and everything keeps rendering. |
| Film render toggles (§9.3) | Parameters of an artefact being produced, like an export resolution — not the app's interface. `Ruhig stellen` is an expert affordance a stoner will never touch; mitigated only by defaults right for everyone. |

Nothing here keys off who you are, a stored preference, or an onboarding answer.

## 6. Was du tun kannst — deterministic remedies

The named failure: *nothing says what to do* (mean 4.93/10). C60's answer is narrow on purpose.

Two rules keep this from becoming an advisor:

1. **A Maßnahme may only propose a change the app itself can write** — a `ziel`, an `Alarm` threshold, a
   socket role, a schedule, or opening a capture sheet. If the app cannot perform it, it is a **Befund**
   (a description), not a Maßnahme. *"Das Licht lief 25 Min länger als geplant"* stays a Befund forever,
   because we cannot fix a contactor.
2. **Every Maßnahme renders its trigger, its arithmetic and its Belege.** The `weil:` line is not a
   summary — it is the rule's own inputs.

### The v1 rule set — complete and published

| # | Trigger (all from data we already hold) | Vorschlag | Beleg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Night actual > night `ziel` on ≥ 10 of 14 nights **and** the `heater` socket ran 0 s in those windows | `Nachtziel auf <p05(gemessenes Nachtminimum)+0,5> °C setzen` | 14 sample windows + the heater lane + one frame pair |
| 2 | An `Alarm` triggered ≥ 15× / 7 d, mean excursion < 10 min | `Grenze von 28,0 auf <p95(gemessen)+0,2> °C setzen` | the 15 excursions, each walkable |
| 3 | A `dehumidifier`/`heater` socket ran > 80 % of 24 h | `Feuchte-Ziel von 50 auf 55 % setzen — das Gerät kommt nicht hinterher` | the output lane, 24 h |
| 4 | VPD outside the phase band > 30 % of lights-on, humidity the dominant term | `Feuchte-Ziel auf <RH solving VPD into band at measured T> % setzen` | the VPD series + the arithmetic, printed |
| 5 | `night.temperature` > `day.temperature` | `Tag- und Nachtziel tauschen` | the two `ziel` Dinge |
| 6 | An `Alarm` on a measure no paired socket can affect | `Alarm „Feuchte zu hoch" löschen oder einer Dose die Rolle Entfeuchter geben` | the socket list |
| 7 | Regime step due, no `gabe` since | `Schritt 5 fällig seit 2 Tagen → Gabe eintragen` (opens the sheet prefilled) | the last `gabe` + the Schema step |
| 8 | No socket has a role | `Keine Dose hat eine Rolle — der Controller kann nichts schalten → Dosen zuordnen` | `hardwareInfo.sockets` |
| 9 | No Schema chosen and ≥ 1 `gabe` logged | `Substrat & Dünger wählen — dann füllen wir Gaben vor` | the logged Gaben |
| 10 | Measured photoperiod differs from `daynight` by > 15 min on ≥ 3 days | *(no writable remedy)* → renders as a **Befund** + an auto-`zettel` | `out_light`, 3 days |

- **At most two Maßnahmen render at once**, ranked by (cost of ignoring × confidence). More than two and
  it is a task list, which is a mode by another name.
- `[ Nicht jetzt ]` suppresses that rule for 14 days on this tent; it is not a setting the user can find.
  It returns with the suffix `wieder fällig`.
- `[ Übernehmen ]` writes a `ziel` Ding — `{art:'ziel', t: now, d:{schluessel:'night.temperature',
  von:20.0, auf:22.0}, akteur, beleg:[…]}` — and pushes it through the existing settings path. **This is
  the first time this product writes config on the user's behalf**, so: never silently, never batched,
  never on an offline device, always one tap on a named number, always reversible from the `ziel` Tafel.

**The side effect that fixes a separate failure.** Because an accepted Maßnahme *and* every manual edit
write a `ziel` Ding with a `t`, **setpoint history exists from day one.** The chart stops drawing today's
target over last month's data. Nobody built a "config history" feature; it fell out of modelling the
remedy as a witnessed Ding.

```
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 84 % im Ziel · Tag 91 % · Nacht 71 %                 │
│ längste Abweichung 2 Std 10 (Nacht, zu warm)         │
│ Beleg: 41 208 Rohwerte · Abdeckung 97 %           ›  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Sollwert is real history now.** A stepped dashed `markLine` from the tent's `ziel` Dinge ordered by
  `t`, stepping additionally at `daynight.day`/`night` in the device timezone (named in the axis label).
  Before the first stored `ziel` it is drawn `~` with the current config, labelled `ab hier angenommen` —
  pretending we know last month's target is the exact lie this concept exists to stop.
- **Zielband** `markArea`, priority **Alarm thresholds > Sollwert ± Toleranz > phase `vpdRange`**, one at
  a time, labelled with which it is. **Tag/Nacht** from measured `out_light > 0`, schedule as fallback —
  a failed contactor renders as a *missing band*, a diagnostic rather than decoration.
- **Ausgänge** as a state-timeline lane; region length = duration, null = gap; never a 0/1 value line.
  The `Entfeuchter` region carries a footnote on tap: `In den Betriebsarten „temp" und „breed" kühlt
  diese Dose, sie entfeuchtet nicht.` — the first time the product tells the truth about
  `out_dehumidifier`'s double meaning.
- **Annotation rail**, two rows (humans / machine), 10 px clustering with count badges, full-height
  dashed lines for `phase` starts. **Tappable, never hover** — the rail is literally Zeilen, positioned.
- **Verdict strip** computed server-side on **raw Influx samples**, never `aggregateWindow(mean)`, split
  day/night, longest excursion as a duration, greyed below 80 % coverage — and carrying its own Beleg,
  because a verdict is a claim like any other.
- `sampling:'minmax'` on the primary series (LTTB smooths away exactly the bang-bang oscillation the
  grower is looking for). Two fingers pan/zoom, one finger drags a handle, tap sets the nearer handle.
- **Prerequisite, not a feature:** parameterise the raw Flux interpolation of
  `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval` (`data.service.ts:80-89`), fix the `limit(n:50000)` piped after
  `yield()`, constrain interval by timespan so `3y × 5s` is unreachable. Nothing above ships first.

## 8. Diary and multi-plant

**There is no diary feature.** The Zelt's `Verlauf` is the tent's diary; a `pflanze`'s `Verlauf` is that
plant's diary. Same Zeilen, same renderer. What "es macht das Tagebuchführen für dich" means
operationally: `ereignis`, `bild`, `film`, `phase` and `ziel`-changes are written by the machine into the
same list the human writes into, so a user who logs nothing still opens a full diary.

**The fusion contribution:** every human entry gets its photograph automatically. On write the server
attaches `beleg: [{art:'bild', image_id: <nearest kept frame within ±5 min>, t}]`. The user uploads
nothing. Three months later, *"who watered A3 on the 14th"* is answered **with a picture of A3 on the
14th.**

```ts
pflanze  d: { sorte?, medium_key?, regime_key?, topf_l?, quelle?: 'samen'|'steckling'|'gekauft',
              keimung_t, ernte_t?, ernte_g?, entfernt_t? }
         name: "A3 · Wedding Cake"     // renaming NEVER changes thing_id
         rel: { in: [zelt] }
phase    d: { stufe: DiaryLifecycleStage }   t / t_end   rel: { an: [pflanze…] }  // [] = whole tent
gabe     d: { kannen?, kanne_l?, wasser_l, bis_ablauf?, ec?, ph?, ablauf_ec?, ablauf_ph?,
              ec_basis: 'absolut'|'plus_leitungswasser',
              produkte: [{ name, ml_pro_l, gesamt_ml, aus_schema: boolean }], schema_key?, schritt? }
         rel: { an: [pflanze…] }, akteur, beleg: [{art:'bild', …}]
notiz    d: { text }                          rel, bilder, beleg
zettel   d: { text, geschlossen_von? }        t / t_end(null = offen), akteur
mensch   d: {}                                name only, tent-scoped, no account
ziel     d: { schluessel, von, auf }          t, akteur, beleg
```

`DiaryLifecycleStage` gains `ernte` as a seventh stage (optional, read-time normaliser, no migration
tool). `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` (`grow-report.component.ts:691-747`) becomes a **one-time idempotent
boot backfill** emitting `pflanze` + `phase` Dinge; the string-comparison cycle inference that forks
history on a rename dies with it. Legacy `DeviceLog` rows keep projecting as `ereignis`/`notiz` through a
read-time adapter, including the seven legacy English-prose regexes.

**The plant-ignorer is untaxed:** `rel.an: []` means the whole tent and is the default forever. A
monitor-only user can run three years and never learn the product has plants. **Server-side cursor
pagination for `things` and `DeviceLog` is a v1 prerequisite** — the unbounded `getDeviceLogs` cannot
survive a per-entry diary, and there is only one screen to be slow.

### 9.2 The cull and the frame metrics — what is real

Verified in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`: ffmpeg is invoked via `execFile`, `sharp` and an
ImageMagick wrapper are already dependencies (**no new binary, no build change, no ML runtime**),
`addOfflineOverlay` (line 127) already composites an SVG with sharp and is unit-tested. **`libx265` at
line 583 is a shipping defect** — HEVC in mp4 does not play in Firefox anywhere nor Chrome on most
Linux/Windows, so half the customers who own a camera today see nothing. The x264 switch is half a day
and is the highest-value change in this document.

One sibling document per stored frame, kept out of `Image` so scoring queries never drag Buffers:

```ts
interface FrameMeta {
  image_id, device_id, timestamp;
  lum, lumP05, lumP95;   // 64-bin histogram over a 320 px grey raw buffer
  rMean, gMean, bMean;   // sharp .stats()
  sharpness;             // stdev after a 3×3 Laplacian .convolve()
  dhash: Buffer;         // 8 bytes, 9×8 grey difference hash
  motion;                // Hamming(dhash, previous), 0..64
  tiles: number[];       // 48 = 8×6 mean-absolute-difference grid → the change map
  gruenanteil?; lightPct?;   // out_light AT CAPTURE — we own the lamp
  urteil: 'behalten'|'dunkel'|'doppelt'|'besuch'|'unscharf'|'blitz'|'wartung';
}                        // ~200 B/frame; index { device_id: 1, timestamp: 1 }
```

`lightPct` is the trick nobody can copy: every competitor must *infer* whether the light was on; we read
it off the MQTT status at capture. Verdicts in order: `wartung` → `dunkel` (`lightPct < 5 && lum < 25` —
two conditions, so a grower whose lamp is not on our socket does not lose the film) → `blitz` (lum
deviates > 3.5× rolling MAD) → **`besuch`** (a *bounded burst*: frames n…n+k each differ from n−1 by
`motion > 18` and n+k+1 returns below 6 — the hand, the door, the watering can, without a person
detector; **plus, free: any frame within ±3 min of a human entry, because somebody told us they were
standing there**) → `unscharf` → `doppelt` (Hamming < 4) → `behalten`.

**Refused as computer-vision fantasy at every version:** naming a drooping leaf, diagnosing a deficiency
from colour, measuring height in cm, counting plants, segmenting canopy, detecting pests. The product
points at the tile and shows the pair; **the human does the semantics.**

### 9.3 Der Film — and HOW IT IS STORED

**The mp4 does not go in Mongo.** `Image{data:Buffer}` plus the 16 MB BSON cap is exactly why no concept
could promise a twelve-week film. C60's answer, decided rather than left open:

> Renders are written to a **named Docker volume at `RENDER_DIR=/data/films`** and streamed from disk
> with real Range support. One line in `docker-compose.yml`, one env var, ~40 lines of route. Mongo
> holds only metadata.

```ts
interface Film {   // metadata only. NO Buffer.
  film_id, tent_id, von, bis;
  spec: { fps, laenge_s, kante_px, untertitel, ruhig, angleichen };
  status: 'queued'|'rendering'|'ready'|'failed'; fortschritt;
  frames_gesamt, frames_behalten, aussortiert:{dunkel,doppelt,besuch,unscharf,blitz};
  kapitel; bytes; pfad; laeuft_ab; erstellt_von;
}
```

GridFS is the alternative if ops refuses a volume; the volume wins because Range-streaming a file is ten
lines and needs no new driver surface. **Backup is the honest cost** (§15). The rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m`
timelapses stay in Mongo untouched — each is small — re-encoded to x264 so they finally play.

Per selected frame, one `sharp` pass (~25 ms): **Deflicker** — `.linear(a,b)` with
`a = clamp(L*/lum, 0.75, 1.35)` toward the run's median lum, and the *change* in `a` versus the previous
rendered frame clamped to **±3 %**; that temporal clamp is three lines and it removes the strobing that
makes every DIY tent timelapse unwatchable. **White balance per lighting regime, not per frame** — a
regime is a contiguous run sharing a `lightPct` bucket (0 / 1–49 / 50–99 / 100), gains clamped to
[0.6, 1.8] so a purple grow still looks purple. **Stabilisation, translation only** — ±6 px integer
search on a 64×48 grey, median-filtered over 5 frames. Rotation is **not** corrected: a sustained shift
re-baselines and inserts a hard cut with a title card `Kamera neu ausgerichtet · Tag 12`, because a film
problem gets a film-language answer. **Burn-in caption** via `addOfflineOverlay`'s SVG technique:
`Tag 24 · Blüte · 24.08. 14:04 · 20,5 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21`, plus `💧 Anna · 2,0 l` when an entry falls
within 10 min.

**Pacing:** one canonical frame per day at the photoperiod midpoint ±30 min — leaves pray at midday and
droop at lights-off, and fixing the phase removes the twitch; only we can do this, because `daynight` and
`out_light` are ours. Then budget by stage (veg 30 % / flower 50 %); a user's own photo is never culled.
Default **60 s at 24 fps**.

```sh
ffmpeg -y -loglevel error -threads 2 -framerate 24 -f image2 -i "$DIR/%05d.jpg" \
  -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -preset medium -crf 21 \
  -g 48 -movflags +faststart -metadata title="Terp Control – Zelt Keller · Tag 1–68" "$DIR/out.mp4"
```

**Three real routes off the phone, and the UI says which one you get:** (1)
`navigator.share({files:[new File([blob], name, {type:'video/mp4'})]})` guarded by
`navigator.canShare?.({files})`, capped at 50 MB — on iOS 15+ the share sheet's *„Video sichern"* puts it
straight in Photos; (2) `GET /film/:film_id.mp4?token=<imageToken>` with `Content-Disposition:
attachment`, `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, filename `terpcontrol_zelt-keller_2026-06-14_2026-08-20_720p.mp4`
(ASCII-folded, `^[a-z0-9_-]{1,100}\.mp4$` — WhatsApp and Android download managers mangle umlauts, and in
a camera roll the filename is the only label); (3) **Filmlink** — `ShareLink.page` gains `'film'`
(additive), `/f/:share_id` unguarded, `<video controls playsinline>` plus `og:video` so it unfurls inline
in Telegram and Discord. **A share that produces a login wall is not a share.**

**The cull report is not a debug panel** — it is the honesty made visible, and the only thing that
explains why this film is watchable and the competitor's is not:

```
1 787 von 2 148 Bildern
  − 289 Licht aus   − 41 Besuch im Zelt   − 22 unscharf   − 9 Kamera verrutscht
= 71 Sek bei 24 B/s · 2 Kapitel (Kamera verrutscht am 12.09.)
```

### 9.4 THE SLIDER — die Zeitleiste

**One shared service.** `ZeigerService: BehaviorSubject<{vorher: number; jetzt: number; laeuft: boolean}>`.
Every projection subscribes: the picture pair, the scrub header, the Befund sentences, the chart's
x-window, the Beleg strip, the annotation rail, the output lanes, the diary rail. **There is one cursor
in this product and it is a span.**

**What it scrubs: the Subjekt's own time.** One rule, thirteen behaviours, no mode, no camera required.

| Subjekt | The slider moves through |
| --- | --- |
| `zelt` | the tent's life, Tag 1 → jetzt — frames and samples on one track |
| `pflanze` | that plant's life since `keimung_t` |
| `bild` | the camera roll |
| `gabe` / `notiz` | ±48 h around the entry — *what happened after* |
| `ziel` | that target's change history and the measure it governs |
| `befund` | **the claim's own span — dragging tests the claim** |
| `massnahme` | the window the rule fired over |
| `dose` | that socket's on/off history |
| `film` / `phase` / `zettel` | the film · the phase · opened → closed (or → jetzt) |
| `mensch` | that person's entries |
| `ereignis` / `kamera` | ±6 h around it |

**The track is itself a data display** (C11's Streifen): kept frames `▪`, culled `░`, capture gaps blank,
`ereignis` marks as ticks below. With no camera the track shows value density instead.

**Two handles, detented.** `◖ Vorher` and `◗ Jetzt`, 44 px hit areas over 12 px visuals. Dragging the
segment *between* them moves the window without resizing it. The left handle has a **snap ladder** with
44 px magnetic zones — `seit zuletzt · gestern · vor 1 Woche · Phasenanfang · letzte Gabe · Anfang` — so
a fat thumb at 2 a.m. cannot miss `gestern`, while a deliberate finger between two snaps lands on any
minute and the exact timestamp prints under the strip as you drag. One control, coarse and precise in the
same gesture, no setting anywhere.

**`▶` — play.** The right handle advances at 1 Tag/s while the left trails at the fixed window width. The
pair becomes a **film of differences** and the German sentence re-writes itself on every frame: *„Die
Pflanzen sind gewachsen" → „Nachts war es wärmer" → „Anna hat gegossen"*. That is C14's static object
turned into C11's film, and it is the most demoable interaction in the concept.

**Pause is the disclosure gesture.** Releasing a handle, or tapping either frame, stops playback and
unfolds the **Zeugenstand** under the pair. **Every row is a Zeile that walks** — that is what C36 adds
to C11's panel, and it is the difference between a readout and a door:

```
╭ Zeugenstand · 24.08.2026 14:04 · Tag 24 ────────────────╮
│ Temperatur   20,5 °C   Ziel 20,0   +0,5    gemessen  ›  │
│ Luftfeuchte  58 %      Ziel 55     +3      gemessen  ›  │
│ VPD          1,21 kPa  Band 1,2–1,5   ✓    gerechnet ›  │
│ CO₂          940 ppm   Ziel 1000   −60     gemessen  ›  │
│ Licht 100 % · Heizung aus (0 Min in 2 Std) gemessen  ›  │
│ Nächster Moment über 30 °C  →                           │
│ ±2 Std: Gabe 2,0 l · Anna 12:05                      ›  │
│ Bild: behalten · belichtet ×1,04 · Schärfe 0,71         │
│       · WB-Regime „Licht 100 %"                      ›  │
╰─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯
```

Nothing is persisted, it self-cancels on `▶`, and it hides no feature class.

**Correlation runs in both directions, concretely:** tap a thumbnail in the Beleg strip → both handles
move to it and the chart window follows · `Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` jumps the right handle to the
next excursion · tap a Befund → the span becomes the Befund's own span, on every screen at once · tap a
`gabe` in the rail → the pair becomes the frame before and the frame after it · open `/verlauf` from
anywhere → it opens on the cursor you were already holding.

**Pinned scrub header, never a tooltip** — one line per handle under the pair (day, clock, and the
measures the device actually reports). There is no hover on touch and this concept never pretends there
is.

### 9.5 Camera alarms as standing Zettel

Two checks in the existing hourly `compressRtspStreams` loop, reusing `Alarm` storage,
`handleAlarmAction` and the existing webhook path, surfacing as an auto-opened `zettel` at the top of the
home screen — where a human would have taped it:

| Fires when | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| No `format:'jpeg'` frame for > 3 h | `Die Kamera hat seit 3 Std kein Bild geliefert.` |
| `out_light > 50 %` for 15 min while frame brightness sits in the lights-off band, or the inverse | `Das Licht sollte an sein, das Bild ist dunkel.` |
| Kept-frame sharpness < 50 % of the 7-day median for 6 h | `Kamera unscharf – vermutlich beschlagen.` (amber) |

The second is the strongest demonstration of the thesis: **neither the picture nor the data can detect a
failed contactor alone, and together they detect it in fifteen minutes.** It closes automatically when
frames resume.

## 10. Clubs

- **Attribution without an auth rewrite.** A `mensch` Ding is tent-scoped, has a name the owner types
  once, and has **no account**. Every capture stamps `akteur`; the last one used in this browser is
  prefilled from `localStorage`, so the 2-tap path survives. ~2 days, no `auth.middleware.ts` surgery.
  Said once, under the actor list: `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann
  jeden Namen wählen.` Anything else is a lie about a record people rely on.
- **The club feature IS the second name.** One `mensch` ⇒ no chips, no attribution column, no double-feed
  warning. Two ⇒ all of it. That is how a club exists without a club mode.
- **The double-feed guard shows the picture** (§9.1) — this concept's specific contribution: the camera
  turns a warning into evidence in one tap.
- **Corrections, not edits.** Editing a `gabe` writes a new one with `storniert_von`; the old Zeile
  renders struck through — reconstructable history without cryptographic immutability, which would
  victimise the unregulated grower. **Upgrade path designed in:** a later real `Membership` sets
  `mensch.d.user_id` and every historic row keeps resolving. Real multi-user auth is **not in v1** and
  the release notes say so.

**Privacy posture — privacy-maximal, no jurisdiction question at install.** Harvest weight and plant
count **never prompted**; no location; no analytics SDK; no community feed; the Filmlink strips actor
names, entry text and plant labels unless ticked; renders expire in 30 days unless `behalten`;
**`Alles löschen` actually deletes** — `things`, `Image`, film files, `FrameMeta`, `DeviceLog` and the
Influx series — fixing the live lie where `DELETE /device/logs/:device_id` removes nothing because
`deleted` is a visibility flag (`device.service.ts:739`). **Refused outright:** per-member dispensing,
grams handed over, THC content, member identity, DOB. § 26 KCanG is the club's system of record, not
ours. No `KCanG-konform` badge.

## 11. Reaching a closed app, export, and an API credential

Three named failures, three cheap answers. **Push:** v1 ships **Telegram as an alarm destination** — a
bot token and chat id on the Zelt Tafel, ~60 lines through the existing webhook machinery, reaching a
phone with the app closed today, with no service worker. Web Push (VAPID + `web-push` + a service worker,

`x-api-key` anywhere in `server/src`, and what turns `Werte {…}` from a demo into an API.

## 12. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

A `fan`, `light` or `plug` owner sees **the same screen, minus what their hardware does not have.**
Nothing is stubbed, disabled or greyed.

- **The pair becomes the Wertepaar.** No camera ⇒ the two picture slots become two stacked value blocks
  carrying the same two timestamps, the same day labels and **the same slider**. The slider does not
  require a camera; it scrubs values. Drag `◖` to `vor 1 Woche`, press `▶`, and a week of that plug's
  readings plays.
- **Befunde from numbers alone, and they are real:** `light`: `▲ Das Licht lief 25 Min länger — 12 Std 25
  statt 12 Std 00 (Plan)` · `● Dimmung geändert — 80 → 100 %, gestern 09:12`. `fan`: `● Drehzahl höher —
  ⌀ 1 840 → 2 210 U/min`. `plug`: `▲ Länger an — 4 Std 20 statt 3 Std 55 (+25 min)`. All types:
  `▲ Gerät war 2 Std 10 offline — von 03:12 bis 05:22`. Each carries `Beleg 📊+n` and walks into the raw
  window.
- **Settings are byte-identical.** `/device/:id/settings` is **kept**, not redirected, for
  `fan`/`light`/`plug`/`dryer`, rendering the existing `fan-settings` / `light-settings` /
  `plug-settings` components through the existing `ngSwitch`. Zero regression; `dryer` stays dead per

```
   ▶  ◂ ▪ ▸     Tag 1
```

The Befund list is populated from the first minute with `Erste Messung` rows — `● Temperatur 24,1 °C ·
Ziel 24,0 · gemessen seit 12 Min · Beleg 📊+144` — and **no delta is fabricated against a synthetic

**Hour 0–6 — the left handle unlocks** the moment two kept frames exist ≥ 30 min apart. The snap ladder
starts with only `Anfang` and grows an anchor as each becomes real (`gestern` at hour 24, `Phasenanfang`

**Hour 6–48 — `Was du tun kannst` does the setup work**, with the *same mechanism* as every later remedy.
There is no wizard and no checklist screen; rules 8, 9, 5 and 6 from §6 simply fire on a fresh tent and

```
→ Keine Dose hat eine Rolle. Der Controller kann nichts schalten.  [ Dosen zuordnen ]
```

**Hour 48 — the product turns on.** The first real pair exists, the first Befund with a photographic

### How it looks — the part nobody specified

- **Type scale, four steps, no others.** `28/1.2/600` Ding name · `17/1.35/600` Aussage (prose) ·

- **Colour discipline.** The 12 px status square is the only saturated colour on a Zeile (grün / gelb /

  frame, or the tent has a number, or the clock says when the first one arrives. That rule is the

## 15. Honest weaknesses

1. **The `Beleg` chip is on every row, and a hostile critic will call it a mode.** My defence — it links
   to a different Ding, and the row is complete without it — is the one I believe, and it is also where
   the critic will push hardest. There is no version of "show provenance everywhere" that does not add a
   glyph everywhere.
2. **Two handles on one track is harder than one.** A fat thumb at 2 a.m. grabs the wrong handle. 44 px
   zones, a left handle defaulting left of centre and long-press-to-grab are mitigations, not a fix.
   C11's single playhead is straightforwardly easier; I traded ease for the pair.
3. **Ten remedy rules will be silent on most real problems** — pests, pH lockout, light burn, a fan that
   stopped. The 4.93/10 failure is *reduced*, not solved, and I refuse to close the gap with an advisor.
   Worse: **the first wrong remedy poisons all ten**, and rule 1 fires on tents where the real answer is
   "buy a cooler".
4. **This is the first time the product writes config on the user's behalf.** One bug in `Übernehmen` is
   a burned crop; every mitigation is procedural, and procedure is not a type system. Relatedly,
   **Belegstufe is honest and almost nobody will read it** — `gemessen`/`gerechnet`/`angenommen` is the
   most intellectually satisfying thing here and probably the least used.
6. **The Tent object is a migration, and merges are where data gets lost.** Auto-creation is safe;
   `+ Gerät hinzufügen` re-parents a device's history into a shared timeline with no undo tool in v1 —
   only the fact that nothing is deleted.
7. **Films on a Docker volume have no backup.** A container rebuilt without the volume loses every
   rendered film. Renders are reproducible from frames — right up until the frames are thinned past the
   point of reproducing them.
8. **Most days, nothing changed.** A home screen whose honest answer is usually `○ Nichts Auffälliges` is
   the opposite of a chart, which always looks busy. The `seit du zuletzt geschaut hast` default helps
   the weekly user and does nothing for the daily one. A competitor's busy dashboard *feels* more
   valuable while telling you less.
9. **`Blattfläche im Bild` is a weak proxy for growth and saying so does not make it stronger.** It
   confounds growth with leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift, and under magenta LED it is
   near-noise. It is the headline visual metric and the softest number.
10. **The cull has a 60-second blind spot in both directions and its errors are invisible by
    definition.** A 90-second tent visit survives the burst test; a genuinely fast real change is dropped
    as a hand. The user only ever sees a count of what was removed.
11. **The majority of launch buyers have no camera** (289 € without, 319–349 € with). The Wertepaar is
    honest and complete, but "the strongest witness is a photograph" does not apply to them, and this
    concept bets the attach rate is high.
12. **One route means one component, and one component means a monolith** — the Tafel becomes the new
    `charts.page.ts` unless the art-specific Körper are separate declared components from the first
    commit. That discipline is a plan, not a guarantee, and every future request will arrive as "add a
    new art": C36's abstraction risk is inherited whole.
13. **Playing the pair re-ranks the Befund list on every frame** — at 1 Tag/s over 84 days σ₁₄ recomputes
    84 times and the sentence flickers. v1 freezes the ranking during playback and re-ranks on pause:
    correct behaviour, and a visible seam.
