# C52 — "Seit zuletzt"

**Fusion of:** C14 (*Was hat sich geändert*) · C36 (*Ein Objekt*) · C11's slider
**Centre of gravity:** one ranked feed of everything that changed while you were away — and the read/unread
boundary is a thing you can drag.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Terp Control has one screen: a ranked list of what changed since you last looked, across every kind of thing
in the tent. Tapping a change walks you into the thing it happened to. The only state the app keeps is where
the boundary between *gesehen* and *neu* sits — and that boundary is a slider.**

The feed is not a list of numbers. It is a list of Dinge that moved: a plant that changed phase, a socket that
ran too long, a Gabe someone logged, a night that was too warm, a film that finished, a target somebody
edited, a camera that went quiet. Same 64-pixel row, same Tafel, all ranked against **one number — die
Marke** — the timestamp of your last visit.

C14's discipline holds: **the product describes what changed and names what it has ruled out; it never
diagnoses from a picture.** Where it can name a remedy it does so from a deterministic rule over data it
already holds (§5), and it prints the evidence that got it there.

---

## 2. What comes from where — and THE FUSION

**From C14:** the Befund as a typographic object (prose line + value line, both true alone, one DOM node);
ranking by `|Δ|/σ₁₄` with the sort key printed on screen; the VORHER/JETZT picture pair anchored to your last
look; the snap ladder of named anchors with 44 px magnetic zones; *describe, never diagnose*.

**From C36:** one `things` collection, one union read endpoint, one Zeile shape, one Tafel shape with four
sections in the same order on every screen, `Werte {…}` as the last row for everybody; depth is
subject-distance — you never expand, you walk a named German relation; reveal by capability and by data;
corrections (`storniert_von`) instead of edits; client-minted ids.

**From C11's slider:** one `MarkeService` (`BehaviorSubject<number>`) with several projections — drag any, all
move; **pause as the disclosure gesture**, unfolding measured vs target vs delta, which sockets ran, diary
entries within ±2 h, and the frame's own exposure/sharpness verdict, persisting nothing and self-cancelling on
play; a **pinned scrub header**, never a hover tooltip.

### THE FUSION — the playhead *is* the read/unread boundary

C11's cursor scrubs a film. C14's comparison has a fixed left endpoint. C36 has no cursor at all. Fused, the
cursor scrubs **your absence**: die Marke sits at your real `lastSeen`, everything right of it is `neu`, and
dragging it left does not change the picture — **it changes what counts as news**. The feed re-ranks live under
your thumb. Drag from *zuletzt* to *1 Woche* and rows appear, merge and re-order: `Nachts wurde es wärmer`
becomes `Vier Nächte in Folge zu warm`, four Gaben collapse into `Anna 3× · Ben 1×`, and VORHER walks back a
week. Drag to *Anfang* and the feed is the whole grow, ranked.

It is a depth mechanism with no mode attached, and it needs all three sources: **C14** made "what changed since
a point in time" the primitive, so there is something for a time cursor to change; **C36** made every changed
item the same shape, so a mixed feed of pictures, plants, sockets, people and targets can re-rank under one
cursor instead of being five screens; **C11** supplied the cursor and the pause gesture, so "seit zuletzt" is
not one hardcoded query with a dropdown of three spans. Remove any one and the concept collapses into that
source.

Second-order consequence available to none of them alone: **a push notification is one feed row, and tapping it
sets the Marke to just before that row.** The app opens on exactly the change the phone woke you for, evidence
already framed.

## 3. Information architecture

Three routes. No tab bar.

| Route | Guard | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/zelte` | AuthGuard | Your Zelte. Exactly one → straight through, as today. |
| `/z/:tent_id/:thing_id?` | AuthGuard (+ share bypass) | **Everything.** No `thing_id` ⇒ the Zelt is Subjekt and the Körper is the feed (§4). Any `thing_id` ⇒ that Ding's Tafel. |
| `…?marke=<ms>` | — | the Marke is a query param, so a feed state is linkable, bookmarkable and push-deep-linkable. |

Untouched and still routed: `/login`, `/account`, `/shares`, `/demo`, `/link-expired`, `/connection-error`,
`/classes`, `/diagnostics`, `/device/:id/testmode`, and the fan/light/plug/dryer settings pages.

```
/device/:id/diary       → 301 /z/<tent_of(id)>
/device/:id/charts?<q>  → 301 /z/<tent_of(id)>/reihe-temperature?<q>   (query untouched; applyViewParams
                                                                       back-compat branch kept)
/device/:id/settings    → 301 /z/<tent_of(id)>/ziele                   (controller & fridge only)
```

`ShareLink.page` stays `'charts' | 'diary'` — no enum change. `'charts'` lands on a `reihe` Tafel, `'diary'` on
the feed with the Marke at `Anfang` and the capture buttons hidden when `editable:false`.

### The tent above the device

```ts
Tent { tent_id, owner_id, name, timezone, device_ids: string[], created_at }
```

On boot every device without a tent gets one (`name = device.name || 'Zelt'`, `device_ids:[device_id]`). Nobody
notices. From then on `Im Zelt → +` merges a second device in, and **one grow across four devices is one diary,
one day counter, one feed** — the four-disagreeing-counters bug dies here. `things.tent_id` is the scope key,
`things.device_id` optional provenance; `auth.middleware.ts` gains a tent→owner lookup and keeps its device path
for the legacy routes. `timezone` owns every day boundary and every `Tag N`.

### The one object (C36, extended)

```ts
Thing {
  thing_id: string;          // uuid v4, MINTED BY THE CLIENT (offline, §8)
  tent_id: string;  device_id?: string;
  art: ThingArt;  name: string;
  t: number;  t_end?: number|null;        // null = still open
  rel?: Record<string, string[]>;         // { an: [...], in: [...], betrifft: [...] }
  d?: Record<string, unknown>;
  bilder?: string[];  akteur?: string;    // thing_id of a `mensch`
  storniert_von?: string;
}
type ThingArt = 'zelt'|'geraet'|'dose'|'kamera'|'reihe'|'bild'|'film'
              | 'pflanze'|'lauf'|'phase'|'gabe'|'notiz'|'zettel'|'ziel'|'mensch'|'ereignis';
```

**Stored:** `pflanze lauf phase gabe notiz zettel mensch film ziel`. **Projected read-time:** `zelt` (Tent +
last Influx point) · `geraet` (Device) · `dose` (`hardwareInfo.sockets` via `parseSocketRoles()`) · `kamera`
(`hardwareInfo.webcam_did` + newest Image) · `bild` (`Image`) · `reihe` (one per measure the device reports) ·
`ereignis` (`DeviceLog`).

`ziel` is **stored**, which is the fix for "setpoint history is not recorded": every write that changes
`device.configuration` also writes `{art:'ziel', t, name:'Nachttemperatur', d:{key:'night.temperature',
von:18.0, auf:20.0}, akteur}`. No backfill exists — the chart draws the Sollwert only from the first recorded
change forward and renders everything before it as a grey dashed region labelled `kein Sollwert aufgezeichnet`.

`GET /api/v1/things?tent_id=&art=&von=&bis=&cursor=` is one cursor-paginated union. The webapp uses it, the
public API is it, they cannot drift.

### 4.1 The Marke

```ts
Visit { visit_id, user_id, tent_id, last_seen: number, prev_seen: number, ended_at?: number }
```

A visit **ends** 30 min after the last foreground beat; on the next open `prev_seen := last_seen` only if the
gap exceeds 90 min, so opening the app four times before breakfast does not shred your Marke. **Floor:** under
4 h it falls back to *gestern, gleiche Uhrzeit* with `Weniger als 4 Std her — Vergleich mit gestern 14:04`,
because a 30-minute comparison is not a comparison. It is **cloud-side, per user, per tent** — not
`localStorage` — so two club members have their own boundary on the same tent and it survives a new phone.
Dragging writes nothing; it only sets `?marke=`. Only leaving the app advances `Visit`.

### 4.2 A feed row

```
▌ ▲   Nachts wurde es wärmer                              ›
      18,1 → 20,5 °C · Ziel 18,0 · 4 Nächte in Folge
```

`▌` is a 3 px rail in `--tc-neu`, present only above the Marke — the only unread affordance. Icon column 24 px,
closed set: `▲` Abweichung · `●` Änderung · `○` unverändert/gesehen · `✎` a person did it · `◼` the machine did
it. Prose line 17/23 weight 600; value line 15/20 tabular-nums, muted. C14's rule: the prose is true without the
numbers, the numbers complete without the prose.

Tap → that Ding becomes the Subjekt. Number rows walk to a `reihe` Tafel (the chart, already spanning
Marke→jetzt); event rows to the `gabe`/`notiz`/`ziel`; image rows to the `bild`; camera rows to the `kamera`.

**Ranking**, printed as `ⓘ sortiert nach Abweichung`: `score = |Δ| / max(σ₁₄, σ_floor)`, σ₁₄ over 14 days at the
same time of day ±1 h, `σ_floor` per measure (Temperatur 0,4 K · rF 2 pp · CO₂ 40 ppm · VPD 0,05 kPa). Anything
a human did inside the span ranks top three; anything with an open Zettel attached ranks above that. Order is
computed once per Marke position and does not move while you read.

**Nothing is hidden.** Below `── gesehen ──` the same list continues, cursor-paginated, to the beginning of the
grow. Every measure the device reports appears, including `○ unverändert`.

## 5. „Was jetzt hilft" — deterministic remedies

The worst score across sixty concepts (4,93/10) was *nothing says what to do*. C14 refuses to diagnose. This
concept keeps that refusal for **images** and breaks it for **rules over data already held** — a rule that
prints the evidence it used is not a diagnosis, it is arithmetic with a sentence attached. A remedy is a **third
line** on a feed row, only when a rule fires, always shaped *[what was ruled out] → [what to do]*, with a button
that walks to the Ding it acts on:

```
▌▲ Nachts wurde es wärmer                                 ›
   18,1 → 20,5 °C · Ziel 18,0 · 4 Nächte in Folge
   Die Heizung lief in diesen Nächten 0 Min — sie ist es
   nicht. Ohne Abluftsteuerung kann das Zelt nicht kühlen.
   [ Nachtziel ändern ]      [ Dosen ansehen ]
```

| # | Fires when | Says | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | night temp > Ziel+1 K on ≥3 of 4 nights **and** heater duty = 0 | `Die Heizung lief 0 Min — sie ist es nicht.` | `Nachtziel ändern` |
| R2 | temp > Ziel while `out_light>0`, no cooling role paired | `Keine Kühlung gekoppelt. Aus deinen Daten: bei 80 % Licht lag die Temperatur im Schnitt 1,4 K tiefer.` — the number comes from **this tent's own history**; under 6 h at two dimming levels the line is omitted | `Licht dimmen` |
| R3 | `out_dehumidifier` duty > 80 % over 6 h, rF still > Ziel | `Die Dose lief fast durchgehend. In den Betriebsarten „temp" und „breed" kühlt sie und entfeuchtet nicht.` | `Betriebsart ansehen` |
| R4 | VPD outside the phase band while temp **and** rF are both inside target | `Beide Ziele sind erfüllt, VPD trotzdem daneben. Die Ziele passen nicht zum Band der Phase. Vorschlag: 25,0 °C / 55 %.` | `Ziele anpassen` |
| R5 | schedule says light on, `out_light>0`, frame brightness in the lights-off band 15 min | `Dose 3 meldet an, das Bild ist dunkel. Leuchtmittel oder Dose defekt.` | `Dose prüfen` |
| R6 | no jpeg > 3 h, camera paired | `Kamera prüfen: Strom und WLAN am Kameramodul.` | `Kamera` |
| R7 | regime step due, no `gabe` in the window | `Schritt 5 ist seit 2 Tagen fällig.` | `Gabe eintragen` |
| R8 | output duty = 100 % over 24 h, Ziel never reached | `Die Heizung lief 24 Std durchgehend und das Ziel wurde nie erreicht. Das Gerät ist zu klein oder das Zelt zu undicht.` | `Ziel ansehen` |
| R9 | a measure reports the identical value > 2 h while its output changes state | `Der Wert steht seit 2 Std 40 exakt still. Sensor prüfen.` | `Gerät` |
| R10 | MQTT gap > 20 min | `Zelt war 2 Std 10 offline — 03:12 bis 05:22. WLAN am Zelt prüfen.` | `Gerät` |
| R11 | a `ziel` exists for CO₂/rF but no socket with that role is paired | `Ziel gesetzt, aber keine Dose dafür gekoppelt — das Gerät kann es nicht erreichen.` | `Dosen` |
| R12 | two `gabe` on intersecting plants < 2 h apart, different `akteur` | `Doppelt gegossen: Anna 19:40, Ben 20:55.` | `Eintrag stornieren` |

**The anti-rule, binding:** no rule may take an image as evidence for a plant condition. Image findings stay
descriptive — `Der obere linke Bereich ist heller geworden`, never `Stickstoffmangel`. Twelve rules, all
arithmetic over Influx + `things` + `hardwareInfo`. No model, no training set, no AI advisor. When no rule fires
the row has two lines and says nothing more: **silence is a valid output.**

## 6. NO-MODES MECHANISM

**Test:** *if you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of feature, it is a mode.*

**M1 — the Marke is a time, not a user class.** Anchored to a fact (you were here at 19:12), not to an answer
about who you are. It hides nothing: the feed continues below the rule in the same scroll, same shape, paginated
to the beginning. It is a divider in a list, exactly like an unread line in a mail app. There is no *Alles als
gelesen markieren*, because there is nothing to clear.

**M2 — scrubbing is stateless and self-cancelling.** Dragging writes nothing; `?marke=` lives in the URL for the
visit; leaving advances `Visit.last_seen` and the next open is back at the honest boundary. The techie who drags
to `Anfang` and reads eleven weeks changes nothing about what the stoner sees at 2 am on the same account. **A
mode cannot promise that.**

**M3 — depth is subject-distance (C36).** No expand control, no *mehr anzeigen*, no density preference, no
remembered expansion. A Ding is one line when related and a full Tafel when it is the Subjekt. `Werte {…}` is the
last row of every Tafel for everybody, showing the literal `GET` that produced the screen.

**M4 — pause is the disclosure gesture (C11).** Tapping the frame during `▶` stops it and unfolds *die Stelle*
(§9.4). Persists nothing, reversible by the identical gesture in under 200 ms, hides no feature class.

**M5 — capability and data decide what exists.** Sockets per role from `hardwareInfo.sockets`; a missing key
fails **closed** to monitor-only with one Zeile: `Gerät meldet keine Dosen — Firmware zu alt.` CO₂ rows only
with the SCD4x. No `pflanze` ⇒ the word "Pflanze" appears in exactly one place, behind the `+` on `Im Zelt`.
None of it is reversible by a preference. `webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts` and the `app-settings-expert` key are
**deleted**, not renamed.

**Honest audit.** `── gesehen ──` is the closest call and a hostile critic will call unread/read a mode; my
defence is that it is a divider inside one continuous list, anchored to a fact, and draggable to `Anfang` (= no
boundary at all). `▶` moves the same cursor the thumb moves. The detent ladder is one physical continuum with
magnetic named positions — between detents you land on any minute and the exact timestamp is printed while
dragging. The remedy line is content, present for everyone when its rule fires. `… 340 weitere` is pagination.

- **Sollwert** is a stepped dashed `markLine` from the stored `ziel` Dinge (§3), not from today's config — the
  fix for "every chart draws today's target over last month's data". **Zielband** is a `markArea`, one source at
  a time and labelled: alarm thresholds > Sollwert ± Toleranz > the phase's `vpdRange`.
- **Tag/Nacht** from measured `out_light > 0`, `daynight` schedule as fallback, timezone named in the axis
  label. A failed contactor renders as a **missing band** — a diagnostic, and the trigger for R5.
- **Ausgänge** as state-timeline regions; length = duration, null = gap; never 0/1 value lines. The
  `Entfeuchter` region carries a tap footnote naming its double meaning in `temp`/`breed`.
- **Annotation rail**, two rows (Menschen / Gerät), 10 px clustering with count badges, full-height dashed lines
  at `phase` starts. **Tappable, not hover** — it is Zeilen, positioned; tapping makes that Ding the Subjekt.
- **Im Ziel %** server-side on **raw** samples, never `aggregateWindow(mean)`; split day/night; longest excursion
  as a duration; greyed below 80 % coverage with the coverage printed. `sampling:'minmax'` on the primary series
  (LTTB smooths away the bang-bang oscillation the grower is looking for); LTTB for the navigator only.
- Two fingers pan/zoom, one finger scrolls, tap sets the cursor. **Prerequisite, not a feature:** parameterise the
  Flux interpolation of `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval`, fix the `limit()` piped after `yield()`, constrain
  interval by timespan.

## 8. Diary, multi-plant, watering and fertilising

**There is no diary feature.** The Zelt's feed below the Marke *is* the diary; a `pflanze`'s Verlauf is that
plant's diary; a `lauf`'s is the run's. Same rows, same renderer. `ereignis`, `bild`, `film`, `phase` and `ziel`
changes are written by the machine into the same list the human writes into — which is what "es macht das
Tagebuchführen für dich" means operationally. **Every human entry gets its picture automatically:** on write the
server sets `d.auto_bild` to the nearest kept frame within ±5 min. One lookup; the user uploads nothing.

```ts
lauf    d: { name?, gestartet_t, beendet_t?, flip_t?, medium_key?, regime_key? }
pflanze d: { sorte?, quelle?:'samen'|'steckling'|'gekauft', topf_l?, keimung_t,
             ernte_t?, ernte_g?, entfernt_t? }        rel: { in: [lauf] }
phase   d: { stufe: DiaryLifecycleStage }   t / t_end   rel: { an: [pflanze…] }   // [] = whole tent
gabe    d: { wasser_l, kannen?, kanne_l?, bis_ablauf?,
             ph?, ec?, ec_basis:'absolut'|'plus_leitungswasser', ablauf_ph?, ablauf_ec?,
             produkte: [{ name, ml_pro_l?, aus_schema: boolean }],
             schema_key?, schritt?, auto_bild? }       rel: { an: [pflanze…] }, akteur
notiz   d: { text, auto_bild? }                        rel: { an: [...] }, bilder
zettel  d: { text, geschlossen_von? }   t / t_end(null = offen), akteur
mensch  d: { farbe }                    name only, tent-scoped, no account
```

Renaming never changes `thing_id` — the bug where renaming a `lifecycleName` forks the history is gone.

## 9. Camera, timelapse, the slider

### 9.1 What ships in v1

1. **Frame metrics at ingest** — two `sharp` decodes, ~50 ms/frame: `light_state` read from `out_light` in Influx
   at ts ±60 s (cross-modal; the payoff of owning the lamp), `helligkeit`, `schaerfe` (Laplacian variance),
   `phash` (8×8 dHash, Hamming < 3 = duplicate), `versatz_dx/dy` (integer SAD ±8 px), `kacheln[48]` (8×6 mean
   absolute difference). ~200 B/frame. **Refused as computer-vision fantasy:** naming a drooping leaf, diagnosing
   deficiency from colour, height in cm, counting plants, detecting pests.
2. **Culling with the reason printed** — `2 148 aufgenommen − 289 Licht aus − 41 Hand im Bild − 22 unscharf − 9
   Kamera verrutscht = 1 787`. The honesty is the feature.
3. **Normalisation** — per-channel `sharp.linear()` toward the film's own median, clamped ±8 % per frame, so a
   dimming ramp or a dirty lens ramps instead of snapping. It cannot recover colour a monochromatic lamp never
   recorded.
4. **Stabilisation** by accumulated integer `(dx,dy)` via `sharp.extract()` on a 96 % window; when residual SAD
   stays high the film **cuts to a new Kapitel** captioned `Kamera verrutscht · 12.09.` — announced, not smeared.
5. **Burn-in caption** via the existing `addOfflineOverlay` SVG-composite technique: `Blüte · Tag 24 · 12.09.
   14:02 · 24,1 °C · 58 % rF`, plus `💧 Anna · 6,0 l` when a `gabe` falls within 10 min.
6. **H.264, not H.265.** `ffmpeg -framerate 24 -f image2 -i %05d.jpg -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0
   -preset medium -crf 21 -pix_fmt yuv420p -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2" -g 48 -movflags +faststart`.
   `convertRtspStreamImagesToVideo()` encodes `libx265` today, which does not play in Chrome or Firefox —
   **today's timelapse is invisible to most customers who have one.** Both encodes switch in one commit.
7. **Two camera alarms, default on pairing**, cloud-side, no firmware: `webcam_silence` (no jpeg > 3 h) and
   `light_mismatch` (`out_light>50 %` for 15 min while brightness sits in the lights-off band). The latter is the
   thesis in one alarm: **neither the picture nor the data detects a failed contactor alone; together they do it
   in fifteen minutes.** Both appear as feed rows carrying R5/R6, and both push.

### 9.2 HOW THE FILM IS STORED

**GridFS, in the existing Mongo, bucket `films`.** Not `data: Buffer` in a document.

- `Image.data` is a `Buffer` and the BSON limit is 16 MB (`server/src/models/images.model.ts:22`). A 12-week 720p
  film is 12–25 MB. It does not fit, and loading a whole Buffer into Node is a memory problem before it is a size
  problem. There is no GridFS in `server/src` today; adding it is `new GridFSBucket(db,{bucketName:'films'})` plus
  `openDownloadStream` with a Range parser — ~120 lines.
- **Why GridFS and not a `RENDER_DIR` volume:** the api service in `docker-compose.yaml` has no volume today (only
  `mongodb` has `mongodata`), and `backup.sh` runs `mongodump --archive`, which already captures
  `films.files`/`films.chunks`. No compose change, no ops change, no second backup path, no orphaned-file class of
  bug. **Cost, stated:** the nightly dump grows by the size of kept films, so `behalten` defaults off and unkept
  renders expire after 30 days via a TTL index on `films.files.metadata.expiresAt`.
- `Film` metadata is a `thing` (`art:'film'`), so it lands in the feed the moment it finishes: `◼ Film „Blüte
  Woche 3" ist fertig · 24 Sek · 5,1 MB`.
- **Off the phone, three paths, and the UI says which one you get:** (1) `navigator.share({files:[File]})` guarded
  by `navigator.canShare`, capped at 50 MB — on iOS 15+ the share sheet's *„Video sichern"* puts it straight in
  Fotos; (2) `GET /film/:thing_id.mp4?token=<imageToken>` with `Content-Disposition: attachment`,
  `Accept-Ranges: bytes` and real Range handling; (3) a `ShareLink` with `page:'film'` — an unguarded page with
  `<video controls playsinline>` and `og:video`, so a pasted link unfurls in Telegram and Discord. Filename
  ASCII-folded: `terpcontrol_zelt-keller_tag12-tag40_2026-10-14_720p.mp4`.
- **Rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses stay in `Image`** (under 16 MB by construction) and keep working for anyone
  who never asks for a custom film. Only the custom render uses GridFS.

### 9.3 THE SLIDER — what it scrubs, what moves with it

**Der Schieber scrubs your absence: the span between the Marke and jetzt.** One `MarkeService`, four projections:

| Projection | What the cursor does to it |
| --- | --- |
| **Der Feed** | re-ranks and re-aggregates live. Rows appear, merge (`4 Nächte in Folge`, `Anna 3× · Ben 1×`) and re-order; the header count updates. |
| **Das Bildpaar** | the left frame is the newest **kept** frame at or before the cursor. VORHER walks backwards through the grow. |
| **Der Filmstreifen** | a sprite of 160×90 thumbnails positioned by timestamp, so uneven capture reads as a gap. `GET /film/strip/:tent_id?von&bis&cols=10` returns one JPEG plus `[{t,x,y}]`, cached 24 h. |
| **Die `reihe` Tafel** | the chart's x-domain is `[marke, jetzt]` and its crosshair is the same cursor. |

**Correlation runs both ways** — the owner's actual ask: tapping a thumbnail moves the cursor and the chart
follows; in the unfolded Stelle, `Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` jumps the cursor and the picture follows.
**Detents**, 44 px magnetic zones, printed under the bar while dragging: `Anfang · Phasenbeginn · 1 Woche ·
gestern · zuletzt · letzte Gabe · jetzt`. A fat thumb at 2 am cannot miss `gestern`; a deliberate finger between
detents lands on any minute, with the exact timestamp printed. **`▶` plays your absence back** — the cursor sweeps
from the Marke to `jetzt` at ~1 day/second, the right frame runs the film, feed rows drop in as they are passed,
the chart lane sweeps. A four-day absence is about four seconds.

## 10. Clubs — attribution without an auth rewrite

- A `mensch` Ding is **tent-scoped**, has a name the owner types once, and has **no account**. Every capture
  stamps `akteur`. ~2 days, no `auth.middleware.ts` surgery. The `Wer?` row renders only at ≥2 `mensch` Dinge —
  a solo grower never sees it. Free text, hint `Vorname oder Spitzname reicht`.
- **Said once, under the actor list:** `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann jeden
  Namen wählen.` Anything else is a lie about a record people rely on.
- **The Marke is per user**, so Anna's feed and Ben's feed on the same tent are genuinely different lists over
  identical data. A shared tent with per-person news is the club feature nobody else in the round has.
- **Double-feed guard** (§8.1) with the picture as evidence; **R12** as the retroactive catch.
- **Zettel** — the note taped to the tent door — is the top strip of the home screen, above the pictures, because
  a human wrote it for another human. `✓` stamps `d.geschlossen_von` + `t_end`; closed Zettel stay in the feed as
  a span. **Corrections, not edits:** editing a `gabe` writes a new one with `storniert_von`; the old row renders
  struck through and stays.
- **Not in v1: real memberships** (a `Membership` collection, `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207`, ~10 owner-scoped
  queries). The upgrade path is designed in: `Thing.akteur` points at a `mensch`; a later real account sets
  `mensch.d.user_id` and every historic row keeps resolving.

**Privacy:** harvest weight and plant count never prompted (`ernte_g` optional, empty, labelled `optional`); no
location, no analytics SDK, no community feed; films and frames behind a token; a public diary URL is opt-in per
`lauf` and pseudonymised (`Mitglied 1..n`) by default; **`Alles löschen` actually deletes** `things`, `Image`,
`films.*`, `FrameMetrics`, `DeviceLog` and the Influx series — fixing the standing lie where clearing logs removes
nothing from the diary because `deleted` is a visibility flag. Refused outright: per-member dispensing, grams
handed over, member identity, DOB. No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.

## 12. Day one and the first 48 hours

The obvious failure mode is that a feed of changes since your last visit is empty when you have never visited. So
the first 48 hours are a designed sequence, not an empty state.

**t+0 — claim.** The Marke is set to `Gerät verbunden`, not to `now`, so the very first feed already has one row
and one rail. `Im Zelt` renders whatever `hardwareInfo` reports. The illustration sits *under* the feed, not

**t+2 min — first measurement.** `◼ Erste Messung · 24,1 °C · 58 % rF · VPD 1,21`. **No delta is fabricated

## 14. Honest weaknesses

1. **Unread/read is the attack surface and I know it.** A critic will call the `── gesehen ──` rule a mode. My
   defence — one continuous list, a divider not a filter, anchored to a fact, draggable to `Anfang` — is the
   strongest available and is still a defence rather than an absence.
2. **The daily visitor gets a nearly empty feed daily.** A tent that behaved yields three `○ unverändert` rows and
   nothing else. Dragging the Marke fixes it and nobody will drag it. A competitor's busy dashboard will feel worth
   more while telling him less.
3. **Re-ranking under a moving thumb is expensive and unmeasured.** Every drag frame re-scores a mixed feed over
   `things` + Influx + `FrameMetrics`. A preloaded window plus client-side re-scoring with a refetch on release is
   a plan, not a benchmark. If it stutters on a three-year-old Android, the slider — the owner's named ingredient
   — is what feels broken.
4. **One route means one component means a monolith.** The feed Tafel becomes the new `charts.page.ts` unless the
   art-specific Körper are separate declared components from the first commit. Discipline, not a guarantee.
5. **Twelve hand-written rules will be wrong somewhere.** R2's "1,4 K tiefer at 80 % light" comes from the tent's
   own history, which is honest, but a tent whose history spans a seasonal change will produce a confident wrong
   number. A remedy people learn to distrust is worse than silence, and there is no field data to tune thresholds
   before October.
6. **GridFS grows the nightly mongodump by the size of kept films.** The 30-day TTL and `behalten` default-off
   bound it, but a user who keeps every weekly film for a year quietly adds several hundred MB to every backup, and
   nobody notices until a restore is slow.
7. **Ingest CPU is a real cost:** ~50 ms/frame × 2 880/day × 150 devices ≈ 6 CPU-hours/day on top of the existing
   ffmpeg poll, plus 60–120 s of a core per render. Today the server is one pm2 process with `pLimit(10)`. This
   needs a real queue and probably a second box before the first series sells out.
8. **The `Tent` migration touches ownership.** Every `device_id`-scoped query gains a tent lookup and
   `auth.middleware.ts` grows a second path. Right model, highest blast radius in the plan, and a bug there is a
   data-visibility bug.
9. **Most launch buyers have no camera** (289 € without, 319–349 € with). The numbers-only variant is complete and
   honest, but three of the slider's four projections are a lesser thing without one.
10. **This is still not a control surface.** Type 4 steers, and steering lives behind `Ziele` on a Tafel he walks
    to. The remedy action buttons are the mitigation and they only appear when a rule fires. He will ask for a
    settings screen and he will be right.
11. **The first 48 hours are measurably worse than the rest**, exactly when a new buyer decides whether it was
    worth the money. I designed that window honestly rather than faking data into it, and honesty is not the same
    as a good first impression.
