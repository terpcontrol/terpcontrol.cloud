# C55 — "Nachbarschaft"

**Fusion of:** C14 (Was hat sich geändert) · C36 (Ein Objekt) · C11's slider
**Centre of gravity:** a difference is explained by walking one named edge to the thing that owns it.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every difference has a neighbour that owns it, and the app's only navigation is walking to that
neighbour with the cursor still in your hand.**

C14 says the product's job is to name what changed. C36 says everything is a *Ding* with named German
relations and there is one way to look at one. Neither answers the question a grower asks half a second
after reading "nachts war es 2,4 °C wärmer": **warum**.

C55's answer: a difference is a property of an edge, not of a value. `Temperatur` is wired to
`Heizung (Dose 1)` by a relation the firmware defines (`wirkt_auf`); that socket is wired to
`Nachtziel Temperatur` by a relation the controller executes (`steuert`); that setpoint is wired to
`Ben, gestern 19:04` by a relation the server already logs (`geändert_von`). Tapping `Warum?` walks one
edge. Three taps and the chain reads: *warm because the heater ran because the night target was raised
yesterday evening.* The chain terminates on something a human did, and that is where the remedy button
lives — the only place a deterministic remedy can honestly live is next to the thing that can be changed.

The discipline this concept exists to enforce: **`weil` is a reserved word.** It is printed only where a
stored edge with a `beleg` exists. Everything else — a watering ninety minutes earlier, a picture that got
darker — appears under `Gleichzeitig`, dotted, no arrow, no verb. §3.2 is the rule, §5 is the audit.

---

## 2. What comes from where

**From C14** — the **pair of pictures** with the difference named in one German sentence; the ranked
`Befund` list where every row is prose *plus* its numbers in the same DOM node; σ₁₄ ranking with the sort
key printed; **describe, never diagnose** on anything derived from pixels (`Blattfläche im Bild`, never
`Wachstum`); the honest stale and empty states.
*Changed:* in C14 a camera-less tent gets a *different* hero — C14's own weakness #11, "two picture
modes". Here **the pair is a lane of the Spur** (§4.3): a camera gives a `Bildband` lane rendered large,
no camera gives a `Wertband` lane in the same slot, same renderer, same cursor. One code path.

**From C36** — the `Ding`; two sizes (**Zeile** when related, **Tafel** when it is the Subjekt); a fixed
section order on every Tafel; the `Werte` row printing the literal `GET` that produced the screen; reveal
by capability and by data, never by preference; per-role socket capability replacing
`deviceControlCapability()`; corrections instead of edits (`storniert_von`).
*Changed:* C36's relations are labels on lists. Here they are **typed edges with a provenance and a
proof**, split into hard and soft. C36 walks to browse; C55 walks to ask why.

**From C11's slider** — **one playhead, two projections**, drag either and both move; the **pinned scrub
header** instead of a hover tooltip, because there is no hover on touch; **pause is the disclosure
gesture**, nothing persisted, self-cancelling, hiding no feature class; correlation in both directions
(`Nächster Moment über 30 °C →`).
*Changed:* C11's cursor scrubs a film. Here it scrubs the Nachbarschaft (§9.4).

### THE FUSION — the cursor survives the walk

> **`Warum?` = walk one hard edge, keep the cursor. The neighbour's screen answers the same instant from
> the neighbour's point of view.**

- **Without C14** there is no difference to ask "why" about; the walk has no starting point and you are
  browsing a database — C36's own named failure mode.
- **Without C36** there is no edge to walk and no vocabulary to walk in; "why" collapses into four series
  on one chart and the app has to *infer* causes, which C14 forbids.
- **Without C11's cursor** the walk loses its time. Ask "why was it warm at 03:12", land on the heater, be
  shown the heater *now* — the answer to a different question. **Carrying the cursor is what makes a walk
  an explanation instead of a topic change.**

Two things exist only because all three were combined:

1. **Pause-to-unfold becomes the Kette.** C11's paused panel lists what was true at that minute. Here it
   is sorted by edge class — a `WEIL` block of stored edges with their proof, a `GLEICHZEITIG` block of
   coincidence. Same gesture, upgraded from a fact sheet into a causal statement with a visible confidence
   boundary.
2. **A remedy has an address.** Sixty concepts failed at "say what to do" because advice bolted onto a
   chart reads as a guess. At the *end of a Kette* it is not advice — it is a sentence about the specific
   setpoint you are standing on, with the button that changes it (§4.6).

## 3. Information architecture

```
/list                        Deine Zelte. Exactly one → straight through, as today.
/z/:tent_id/:ding_id?        THE BROWSER. One component. No ding_id ⇒ the Zelt is the Subjekt.
/z/:tent_id/spur?t=&subj=&von=&bis=     DIE SPUR at full height — the chart. Same cursor, same lanes.

unchanged: /login /account /shares /demo /classes /diagnostics /link-expired /connection-error
           /device/:id/testmode · /device/:id/settings kept verbatim for fan|light|plug|dryer

redirects (share links and ChartPresets are persisted user data — non-negotiable):
/device/:id         → /z/<tent_of(id)>/g_<id>
/device/:id/diary   → /z/<tent_of(id)>              (mergeDiaryQueryParams honoured)
/device/:id/charts?<q> → /z/<tent_of(id)>/spur?<q>  (applyViewParams keeps its back-compat branch)
```

Every device gets a `Tent` on migration — one tent per device, named after the device — so no URL dangles
and no user is asked to do anything. `ShareLink.page` stays `'charts' | 'diary'`; the guard chain
(`resolveDeviceAccessInfo`: `locked`, `canEdit`, `webcamAllowed`, `chartsAllowed`) applies to the tent's
member devices, all of which a share token already scopes to one of.

**The rule the IA enforces:** no screen is reachable only from a menu. You reach a Ding by tapping the
Ding and its neighbour by tapping the relation. The menu keeps six items: Zelte, Geteilte Links, Konto,
Dunkelmodus, App installieren, Abmelden.

### 3.1 The entities

```ts

  tent_id; owner_id; name;               // "Zelt Keller"
  device_ids: string[];                  // 1..n. A grow across four devices is ONE tent.
  started_at;                            // THE day counter. Not per device. Not four counters.

}

Ding {                                   // Mongo `dinge`, one collection
  ding_id;                               // uuid v4, MINTED BY THE CLIENT → offline upsert is idempotent
  tent_id; art: DingArt; name;

  d?: Record<string, unknown>;           // art-specific body — THE ONLY PART THAT VARIES

}

  | 'zelt'|'geraet'|'dose'|'kamera'|'bild'|'film'|'ziel'|'ereignis'   // projected read-time from
                                          // Device, hardwareInfo.sockets, Image, configuration, DeviceLog
  | 'pflanze'|'gabe'|'notiz'|'zettel'|'phase'|'mensch';               // stored (6)

Kante {                                  // Mongo `kanten`. THE CONCEPT.
  kante_id; tent_id; von; nach;          // ding_ids
  art: KantenArt; seit; bis?;
  quelle: 'firmware' | 'regel' | 'plan' | 'eingetragen';
  beleg?;                                // WHAT PROVES IT: DeviceLog _id, socket-role CSV, config diff
}

  | 'wirkt_auf'      // Dose → Messwert        firmware role table
  | 'steuert'        // Ziel → Dose            the controller's own rule
  | 'geaendert_von'  // Ziel → Mensch          config diff log
  | 'ausgeloest_von' // Ereignis → Ziel | Alarm
  | 'gilt_fuer' | 'gehoert_zu' | 'ging_an' | 'steht_in' | 'zeigt';   // structural only
```

### 3.2 Hard edges vs. neighbours — the rule, and what it forbids

| Relation | `weil`? | Why | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `steuert` | **yes** | We know the firmware's bang-bang rule and both operands | `Die Heizung lief, weil 20,5 °C unter dem Nachtziel 22,0 °C lag.` |
| `wirkt_auf` | **yes, as responsibility** | A socket role is a fact of wiring, not a measurement | `Heizung ist die Dose, die auf Temperatur wirkt.` |
| `geaendert_von` | **yes** | It is a record, not an inference | `Ben hat das Nachtziel gestern 19:04 von 20,0 auf 22,0 gesetzt.` |
| `ausgeloest_von` | **yes** | The alarm evaluator's own logged decision | `Der Alarm hat ausgelöst, weil 31,2 °C über 30,0 °C lag.` |
| `ging_an`, `steht_in`, `gehoert_zu`, `zeigt`, `gilt_fuer` | structural | membership, not causation | `Ging an A1, A2` |
| **Gabe → any measure** | **NEVER** | We do not measure substrate | under `Gleichzeitig` |
| **Bild → any measure** | **NEVER** | C14's rule: pixels describe, they do not diagnose | under `Gleichzeitig` |
| **any time-series correlation** | **NEVER** | No cross-correlation, no lag search, no regression | not rendered at all |

Visual grammar, in CSS not prose: hard edges use a **solid connector** `├ └` in `--tc-kante`; soft
neighbours use a **dotted** `┊` in `--tc-nachbar` and are never given an arrow. A user who reads no German
still sees two kinds of line.

**The honest terminus.** With no hard edge, `Warum?` is not rendered as a dead button — it is not rendered
at all, and the Kette ends with: `Dafür kennt die App keinen Grund. Was gleichzeitig passiert ist, steht
oben.` That sentence ships. It is the price of the reserved word.

### 4.3 Die Spur — the slider (spec)

One `SpurService: BehaviorSubject<{ t; subj; von; bis }>`. Everything renders from it. 108 px on a Tafel,
full height on `/spur`.

- **Track:** full width, playhead `●` at the right edge labelled `jetzt`. Drag track, drag playhead, or
  tap a lane — all write the same subject.
- **Große Bahn (lane 0),** ~126 px: `Bildband` (the pair) with a camera, `Wertband` (24 h min/max
  envelope) without one. Same slot, one `ngSwitch` on lane type, nothing user-settable.
- **Lanes 1..n,** 18 px: one per **hard-edge neighbour of the Subjekt**, fixed order (own measure, then
  `wirkt_auf` partners, then `steuert` partners). Solid. Below a hairline, up to two rows of **soft
  neighbours** as marks only, never curves.
- **Snap ladder** (C14's Zeitleiste, kept): `jetzt · gestern gleiche Zeit · letzte Nacht · letzte Gabe ·
  Phasenwechsel · Anfang`, 44 px magnetic zones, exact timestamp printed while dragging. One control that
  is coarse and precise in the same gesture.
- **Moves with the cursor:** the left frame · the header `ANDERS ALS <cursor>` · every difference
  sentence, recomputed client-side from cached series, debounced 200 ms · the pinned scrub header · the
  chart crosshair.
- **Span follows the Subjekt, not a setting:** `zelt` → `Tag 1 … jetzt`; `dose` → 7 days; `bild` → ±12 h;
  `gabe` → ±48 h.
- **Both directions:** `Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` jumps the cursor forward; tapping a Bildband
  thumbnail jumps it to that frame.

### 4.5 Walking — the breadcrumb

`Warum?` walks the topmost hard edge and pushes the neighbour as Subjekt **with `t` unchanged**:

```
‹  Temperatur 03:12  ›  Heizung  ›  Nachtziel
```

Each hop is tappable; back returns. The longest real chain in the product is four hops
(`Messwert → Dose → Ziel → Mensch`). The cursor is in the URL (`/z/t_7/z_night_temp?t=1756…`), so a chain
is a link one club member can paste to another.

### 4.6 Die Abhilfe — what to do about it

A remedy is a **pure function over data the app already has**, evaluated server-side once a minute per
tent and stored as a `zettel`-shaped Ding with `d.regel`. It renders at the end of its Kette *and* as a
`▲` Befund at the top of `ANDERS ALS`, because a remedy you only see if you walk is a remedy nobody sees.
Rules live in a Mongo `abhilfen` collection — a wrong one is a DB update, not a deploy — and each rendered
remedy prints its rule id in `Werte`, so it is falsifiable.

| Regel | Trigger (deterministic) | German | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `heizung-am-anschlag` | `out_heater` on ≥ 85 % of 6 h **and** temp stayed ≥ 1,0 K below target | `Die Heizung läuft fast durchgehend und erreicht das Ziel nicht. Das Zelt verliert mehr Wärme, als sie liefert.` | `[ Nachtziel auf 20,0 senken ]` `[ Als Zettel anheften ]` |
| `nachtsprung-zu-gross` | `day − night ≥ 5 K` and arrival takes > 3 h on 3 nights | `Der Sprung von Tag auf Nacht ist 6 K. Das Zelt braucht jede Nacht 3 Std 20, um anzukommen.` | `[ Nachtziel auf 21,0 setzen ]` |
| `keine-entfeuchter-dose` | RH above target > 4 h/day for 3 days **and** no `dehumidifier` role paired | `Es fehlt eine Dose mit der Rolle „Entfeuchter". Ohne sie kann das Zelt die Luftfeuchte nicht senken.` | `[ Dose einrichten ]` |
| `entfeuchter-kuehlt` | `dehumidifier` paired **and** `workmode ∈ {temp, breed}` | `Diese Dose kühlt in den Betriebsarten „temp" und „breed". Sie entfeuchtet dann nicht.` | — (the explanation is the remedy) |
| `licht-kuerzer-als-plan` | measured `out_light>0` < schedule − 15 min, 2 days running | `Das Licht lief gestern 11 Std 35 statt 12 Std 00. Prüf die Dose oder den Schütz.` | `[ Testmodus ]` `[ Als Zettel anheften ]` |
| `nachtfeuchte-schimmel` | RH > 65 % for > 2 h in 3 of 3 nights | `In drei von drei Nächten über 65 %. Nachts ist die Luft kälter und hält weniger Wasser — das ist die Schimmelnacht.` | `[ Nachtziel Feuchte auf 50 % ]` `[ Alarm einrichten ]` |
| `kamera-still` | no `jpeg` frame for > 3 h | `Seit 3 Std 12 kein Bild. Der Film bekommt hier eine Lücke.` | `[ Kamera prüfen ]` |
| `fuetterung-faellig` | regime step due, no `gabe` since | `Schritt 5 ist seit 2 Tagen fällig.` | `[ Gabe eintragen ]` prefilled |
| `zelt-offline` | last seen > 30 min | `Zelt offline seit 24.08. 14:20. Die Zahlen unten sind von da. Änderungen gehen raus, sobald es zurück ist.` | — |

Every button writes a `device.configuration` value that already exists (the push path is unchanged), opens
an existing sheet, or pins a `zettel`. **No rule invents a config key, and no rule gives horticultural
advice that is not arithmetic over the tent's own history.** No AI, no model, no "suggestion".

### 4.7 Wie es aussieht

Type scale (system stack, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on every value): 28/34 semibold Ding name ·
20/26 semibold difference prose · 17/24 Zeile name · 15/20 value line · 13/18 age and `beleg` · 11/14
uppercase `letter-spacing .06em` section headers (`ANDERS ALS 24.08. 03:12`, `IM ZELT`, `WEIL`,
`GLEICHZEITIG`).

Spacing on a 4 px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32. Section gap 24. Row height 56 (two lines) / 48 (one).
Touch target never below 44. Content inset 16; lanes bleed to 0 so the time axis is the full width.

Colour reuses the existing `--tc-text-*` tokens and adds exactly two: `--tc-kante` (`--ion-color-primary`
at 60 %, solid connectors) and `--tc-nachbar` (`--tc-text-muted`, dotted). Status is one 12 px square:
grün / gelb / rot / hollow (= veraltet). `darkMode:'auto'` on ECharts.

**One illustration exists in the whole product**: a 96 px single-weight line drawing of a tent with one
open flap and a single dot inside. It appears on `/list` with no tent and behind the day-one Spur (§12).
Nowhere else. No hero art, no mascot, no onboarding cards.

## 5. NO-MODES MECHANISM

**The falsification test:** *if you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of
feature, it is a mode.*

**M1 — depth is subject-distance and cursor-distance, never identity.** A Ding is one line as a neighbour
and a full Tafel as the Subjekt. You do not set depth, you walk. Nothing about the walk is stored, so the
techie's four-hop chain changes nothing about what the stoner sees on the same account tomorrow. A mode
cannot make that promise.

**M2 — pause is the disclosure gesture** (§4.4). Borrowed, not invented: everyone knows that stopping a
video means "I want to look at this bit". Not persisted, reversible by the identical gesture in under
200 ms, self-cancelling, and it hides no feature class.

**M3 — `WEIL` and `GLEICHZEITIG` are the only two registers and both are always on screen.** There is no
"show reasoning" control because the reasoning is the layout. Prose line and value line are the same DOM
node (C14's rule: *the prose must be true without the numbers and the numbers complete without the
prose*).

**M4 — capability and data decide existence.** `IM ZELT` lists exactly the sockets `hardwareInfo.sockets`
reports via `parseSocketRoles()` (`webapp/src/app/util/socket-info.ts:7` — five real roles), not the
three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` collapse that shows a heater-only tent a humidity target it can
never reach. A missing key fails **closed**: `Gerät meldet keine Steckdosen — Firmware zu alt.` No
`pflanze` ⇒ the word "Pflanze" appears in exactly one place, the `+` on `IM ZELT`. No second `mensch` ⇒ no
`Wer?` row anywhere. **The club feature is the existence of a second name.**

**The existing mode is deleted, not renamed.** `EXPERT_MODE_STORAGE_KEY = 'app-settings-expert'`
(`webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts:6`) and every read of it go in the first commit.

| Suspect | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| `Warum? ›` | **Not a mode.** A link whose target differs on every row; it reveals a *specific neighbour*, not a feature class, and if the neighbour does not exist the button does not exist. |
| `6 weitere Werte anzeigen` | **Not a mode.** Expands the same list in place by the printed sort key; the hidden rows are the low-ranked ones by construction. |
| `Werte {…}` | **Not a mode**, and the closest thing to a wart. Same row, same position, every screen, everybody, from install. Hiding it behind a preference is the banned pattern. |
| Film toggles (§9.3) | **Closest call in the concept.** Parameters of an artefact being produced, like an export resolution. Mitigated only by defaults that are right for everyone. |
| Bildband vs Wertband | **Not a mode** — the lane renderer picking a body by lane type, driven entirely by whether a camera is paired, changing the instant one is. |

**The setpoint is historical, and this is a fix, not a promise.** Sixty concepts said config history is
not recorded. It is — badly. `configureDevice` (`server/src/services/device.service.ts:1140-1162`) already
writes a `DeviceLog` on every change with a diff body of lines shaped `    day.temperature: 24 -> 25`,
categorised `['device','device-configuration']` — but with `deleted: true`, so nobody has ever seen it,
and unstructured, so nothing can read it. Three changes: keep `deleted: true` (the legacy diary stays
exactly as it is — non-breaking); **also** write `data: { changes: [{key, old, new}], by_user_id }`, since
`DeviceLog.data` is already `Schema.Types.Mixed` and `user_id` is already an argument of `configureDevice`
— **two lines**; and a one-time boot backfill parsing the existing text diffs into the same shape.

That yields the **Sollwertspur**: a stepped dashed `markLine` drawn from what the target *actually was*,
which is also the `geaendert_von` hard edge, which is also the third link of most Ketten. One two-line
change pays for the chart, the causal chain and the diary at once.

Everything else follows C14/C36: `markArea` priority **alarm thresholds > Sollwert ± Toleranz > phase VPD
range**, one at a time, labelled with which; Tag/Nacht from measured `out_light > 0` with the schedule as
fallback in the tent's named timezone, so a failed contactor is a *missing band*; outputs in a
state-timeline lane (region length = duration, null = gap, never 0/1 lines); annotation rail, row 1 people
/ row 2 machine, 10 px clustering, **tappable, no hover**; `sampling:'minmax'` on the primary series (LTTB
erases the bang-bang oscillation that is the signal); time-in-range **server-side on raw Influx samples**,
greyed below 80 % coverage. Non-negotiable prerequisite: parameterise the Flux interpolation of
`measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval` in `data.service.ts` and clamp interval by timespan.

**Multi-device tents:** each member device contributes its own panels, prefixed with the device name only
when more than one reports the same measure. Four devices, one x-axis, one cursor, one day counter.

## 7. Diary and multi-plant

**There is no diary feature.** The tent's `VERLAUF` is the tent's diary; a `pflanze`'s `VERLAUF` is that
plant's diary; same Zeilen, same renderer. "It does the diary keeping for you" is literal: `ereignis`,
`bild`, `film`, `phase` and every `ziel` change are written by the machine into the same list the human
writes into, so a user who logs nothing still opens a full diary.

```ts
pflanze  d: { sorte?, medium_key?, regime_key?, topf_l?, quelle?: 'samen'|'steckling'|'gekauft',
              keimung_t, ernte_t?, ernte_g?, entfernt_t? }
         name: "A3 · Wedding Cake"   // renaming never changes ding_id     Kante: steht_in → zelt
phase    d: { stufe: DiaryLifecycleStage }   t / t_end   Kante: gilt_fuer → pflanze[] (empty = tent)
gabe     d: { kannen, kanne_l, wasser_l, ph?, ec?, ec_basis?: 'absolut'|'plus_leitungswasser',
              ablauf_ph?, ablauf_ec?, produkte: [{ text, ml_pro_l?, aus_plan: boolean }],
              regime_key?, schritt? }
         akteur, bilder, auto_bild_id                    Kante: ging_an → pflanze[]
notiz    d: { text }                    bilder
zettel   d: { text }  t / t_end(null = offen)   akteur, d.geschlossen_von, d.regel?
mensch   name only, tent-scoped, no account
```

`DiaryLifecycleStage` gains a seventh value, `ernte` — harvest is not a stage today, so "how did this grow
go" is literally unanswerable. Optional field, read-time normaliser, no migration tool.

**Every entry gets its picture automatically** (C14, kept): on write the server sets `auto_bild_id` = the
nearest kept frame within ±5 min. The user uploads nothing. It appears in the entry, in the per-plant
record, in the film's burn-in and in the double-feed guard.

**The plant-ignorer is untaxed.** `ging_an` is optional; absent means the whole tent and is the default
forever. The plant UI does not exist until a plant exists — no empty state, no "0 Pflanzen" card, no nag.

**Legacy.** `DeviceLog` keeps its rows and keeps projecting as `ereignis`/`notiz`, including the seven
legacy English-prose regexes. `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a one-time idempotent backfill
emitting `pflanze` + `phase` Dinge, which kills the live bug where renaming a `lifecycleName` forks the
history. **Server-side cursor pagination for `dinge` and `DeviceLog` is a v1 prerequisite**; today's
unbounded `getDeviceLogs` cannot survive a per-event diary.

```
  ( ) Biobizz Light-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom    Düngen ab Woche 2

```

Before `start_ab_tag` the sheet prefills **no products** and shows one line —
`All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen.` A schema that cannot say "don't feed yet" is not
modelling the domain. The engine advances **by feed events** (`Schritt 5 von 12`), not wall clock, with a
soft calendar mapping (`etwa Woche 5`); growers feed Tue/Fri and slip. Every prefilled value is editable
and stored with `aus_plan: true` until touched, so months later the report distinguishes `wie im Plan`
from `abweichend` — the club's actual question. EC is canonical mS/cm with `ec_basis`; a
`plus_leitungswasser` target renders as `EC-Ziel 1,4 + dein Leitungswasser`, never as a bare ppm. Brand
names are plain text in a Mongo collection with `quelle_url` + `geprueft_am`; one brand is removable in
one DB update, and `Zuletzt geprüft` doubles as a trust feature.

**Double-feed guard.** At sheet open, query `gabe` Dinge whose `ging_an` intersects the selection inside
the window (water 6 h, feed 18 h; coco 3 h / 12 h; owner-editable). `[ Bild ansehen ]` opens Tom's
`auto_bild_id` frame — **you can see whether the soil is dark.** The camera turns a warning into evidence.

**Double-logging** is impossible by construction: `ding_id` is a client-minted uuid and the server upserts
on `{tent_id, ding_id}`. v1 has no service worker: a failed save is held in `localStorage`, retried on
focus, rendering `⏳ noch nicht gesendet`. That is not capture-in-a-cellar and the copy does not pretend it
is.

## 9. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

### 9.1 What exists (verified)

A frame every 30 s; `Image { image_id, device_id, timestamp, timestampEnd?, data: Buffer,
format:'jpeg'|'mp4'|'user/jpeg', duration:'1d'|'1w'|'1m' }` (`server/src/models/images.model.ts:22`);
rolling 1d/1w/1m timelapses rebuilt hourly; `sharp` and `ffmpeg` already dependencies; a 30-day
URL-embeddable `?token=` with `?width=` resizing; `addOfflineOverlay` already composites SVG onto frames.

### 9.2 HOW THE FILM IS STORED

**Films do not go in Mongo.** `Image.data` is a `Buffer` inside a document, the BSON limit is 16 MB, and
there is no GridFS anywhere in `server/src`. A twelve-week film is 6–20 MB, and loading it into one pm2
process to serve it is the latent bug the existing mp4 path already has.

v1 writes renders to a **named docker volume**, `RENDER_DIR=/var/lib/terpcontrol/films`, one compose line.
ffmpeg already runs via `execFile` and already writes temp files, so this is a compose change, not an
architecture change. A `film` Ding holds only metadata:

```ts
film  d: { pfad, bytes, dauer_ms, bilder_gesamt, bilder_behalten,
           verworfen: { dunkel, doppelt, besuch, unscharf, verrutscht },
           kapitel, von, bis, spec: { fps, kante_px, untertitel } }
```

Served by `GET /film/:ding_id.mp4?token=<imageToken>` with `Accept-Ranges: bytes` and **real Range
handling** (iOS Safari will not play inline without it), `Content-Disposition: attachment;
filename="zelt-keller_tag12-tag40_2026-10-14.mp4"` (ASCII-folded, lowercase, no device_id, no umlauts —
in a camera roll the filename is the only label), `Cache-Control: private, max-age=604800`. Renders expire
after 30 days unless the user ticks `behalten`. The rolling 1d/1w/1m films stay in Mongo, untouched, and
are **re-encoded from x265 to x264** — HEVC in mp4 does not play in Firefox anywhere nor in Chrome on most
Linux/Windows, so today's timelapse is invisible to a large share of the people who paid for the camera.
That flag change is half a day and is the highest-value line in this document.

### 9.3 What ships in v1

Culling uses the one thing no camera vendor has: **we own the lamp.** `out_light` at capture ±60 s goes
into a small `FrameMeta` sibling document (never inside `Image`, so scoring queries never drag Buffers):
`lum`, `sharpness`, `dhash`, `motion`, `light_pct`, `verdict`. Verdicts in order: `wartung` · `dunkel`
(`light_pct < 5` **and** `lum < 25` — two conditions, so a grower whose lamp is not on our socket does not
lose the film) · `blitz` (door open: `lum` beyond 3.5× the rolling MAD) · `besuch` (a bounded burst —
frames n…n+k each differ from n−1 by `motion > 18`, n+k+1 differs by `< 6`; **plus, free, any frame within
±3 min of a `gabe`, `notiz` or `foto`, because a human told us they were standing there**) · `unscharf` ·
`doppelt` (Hamming < 4) · else `behalten`.

Then **goldenes Fenster** (one canonical frame per day at the photoperiod midpoint ±30 min, so leaves are
always at the same point of praying/drooping — and the window moves when the grower flips 18/6 → 12/12,
which only we can do); **deflicker** (`sharp.linear`, gain change clamped to ±3 % against the previous
rendered frame — three lines, and what makes twelve weeks watchable); **white balance per lighting
regime**, not per frame, gains clamped to [0.6, 1.8] so a purple grow stays purple; **translation-only
stabilisation** with a hard cut and a title card `Kamera neu ausgerichtet · Tag 12` beyond the inset;
**burn-in** via the existing `addOfflineOverlay` SVG technique —
`Tag 24 · Blüte · 25.08. 14:02 · 24,8 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21`, bottom-right `💧 Anna · 2,0 l`.

```
ffmpeg -y -framerate 24 -f image2 -i "$DIR/%05d.jpg" \
  -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -preset medium -crf 21 \
  -g 48 -movflags +faststart -metadata title="Terp Control – Zelt Keller · Tag 1–68" out.mp4
```

Off the phone: `navigator.share({files:[File]})` where `navigator.canShare` allows (iOS 15+, Chrome
Android) → the file lands in WhatsApp/Telegram/Fotos directly; `window.location.href = url` inside the tap
handler elsewhere; on iOS without Web Share, one line — `Auf dem iPhone landet der Film in „Dateien". Von
dort: Teilen → „Video sichern".` A pasteable `?token=` link plays for 30 days with no login;
**a share that produces a login wall is not a share.** The cull report is shown before rendering, because
it is the honesty that explains why this film is watchable and the competitor's is not:

```
2 148 Bilder aufgenommen
  − 289 Licht aus   − 41 Besuch im Zelt   − 22 unscharf   − 9 Kamera verrutscht
= 1 787 Bilder → 74 Sek bei 24 B/s · 2 Kapitel
☑ Nur Tagbilder   ☑ Helligkeit angleichen   ☑ Ruhig stellen   ☑ Zahlen einblenden
```

Two camera alarms exist from the moment a camera is paired, reusing `Alarm` storage and
`handleAlarmAction` but needing their own ~80-line ticker (the evaluator only runs on inbound MQTT):
`webcam_silence` (> 3 h without a frame) and **`light_mismatch`** (`out_light > 50 %` for 15 min while
frame brightness sits in the lights-off band, or the inverse) → `Das Licht sollte an sein, das Bild ist
dunkel.` Neither the picture nor the data detects a failed contactor alone; together they detect it in
fifteen minutes — and in C55 it arrives as a hard edge, so it is a Kette rather than a notification.

### 9.4 THE SLIDER — what it scrubs, what moves, what pause unfolds

**It scrubs the Nachbarschaft of whatever you are standing on.** Not a film — a film is what the top lane
happens to be when a camera exists.

| Standing on | Cursor moves through | Große Bahn | Lanes below |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `zelt` | the whole run, `Tag 1 … jetzt` | the picture pair | every measure + every socket |
| `dose` (Heizung) | its last 7 days | on/off state timeline | the measure it `wirkt_auf`, the `ziel` that `steuert` it |
| `ziel` (Nachtziel) | its change history | step chart of what the target *was* | the measure it `gilt_fuer` |
| `pflanze` | from `keimung_t` | the frames | its `gabe` marks, VPD |
| `gabe` | ±48 h around it | frames before / after | RH and VPD, **under `Gleichzeitig`** |
| `film` | the film itself | the video | the burned-in measures as lanes |

**Moves with the cursor:** the große Bahn · the header `ANDERS ALS <cursor>` · every difference sentence
(recomputed, not re-fetched) · the pinned scrub header · the chart crosshair · and, on a walk, **itself**
— the cursor is carried into the neighbour's screen and appears in the URL.

**Pause unfolds** the Kette of §4.4: measured vs target vs delta per measure; the `WEIL` block of hard
edges with the sockets that ran and the setpoint in force *at that instant* (from the Sollwertspur, not
from today's config); the `GLEICHZEITIG` block of diary entries within ±2 h; the frame's own exposure and
sharpness verdict. Nothing persisted, self-cancels on the next drag, hides no feature class.

**Delivery.** Thumbnails come from one sprite, not 100 requests: `GET /z/:tent_id/band?von&bis&n=60` → one
JPEG of 60 tiles at 128×72 plus `band.json` = `[{t,x,y}]`, cached in a `Band` collection with a 24 h TTL
index. **In v1**, not deferred, because the Spur is the concept's primary control and 60 round-trips over
tent wifi is not a control.

## 10. Clubs — attribution without an auth rewrite

`mensch` is a tent-scoped list of names the owner types once. No accounts, no e-mail, no
`auth.middleware.ts` surgery. Capture stamps `akteur`; the last actor used from this browser is prefilled
from `localStorage`, so the 2-tap path survives. ~2 days. Stated once in the product, under the actor
list: **`Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann jeden Namen wählen.`** Real
`Membership` + rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` is later work; `mensch` gains an optional
`user_id` and upgrades in place with no migration.

What C55 adds that C14 and C36 could not: **the `geaendert_von` edge makes config changes attributable
too.** `configureDevice` already receives `user_id` and already logs the diff; storing it (§6) makes
`Ben hat das Nachtziel gestern 19:04 von 20,0 auf 22,0 gesetzt` a record, sitting in the Kette of every
warm night that followed. In a shared tent, "who moved the target" is the second-most expensive question
after "who already fed".

Also: **corrections, not edits** — editing a `gabe` writes a new one with `storniert_von` and the old
Zeile renders struck through. **Zettel** are the note taped to the tent door, opened by anyone, closed by
anyone with `✓` stamping `d.geschlossen_von` + `t_end`, pinned above the pictures. **Cumulative recall**
on the `pflanze` Tafel: `Wasser 18,5 l über 11 Gaben · Bio-Bloom 31 ml · von Anna 7 · Ben 3 · ich 1`.

**Privacy, maximal by default.** Harvest weight and plant count are **never prompted** — a per-tent
`Ernte erfassen` switch, off, and off means the field does not exist. Actor names are free text and may be
"T". No location, no analytics SDK, no community feed. **`Alles löschen`** actually deletes `dinge`,
`kanten`, `Image`, films on disk, `DeviceLog` and the Influx series — fixing the standing lie where
`DELETE /device/logs/:device_id` removes nothing because `deleted` is a visibility flag
(`device.service.ts:739`). Retention: raw frames 90 days, `FrameMeta` with them, films 30 days unless
kept. Refused outright: per-member dispensing, grams, THC content, member identity, date of birth. § 26
KCanG is the club's system of record, not ours. **No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.**

**Reaching a closed app.** There is no push and no service worker in this codebase and v1 does not pretend
otherwise. What exists is `sendMail` and a templated webhook (`applyWebhookTemplate`, `alarm.service.ts`).
v1 adds **one preset webhook destination: Telegram Bot API** — a POST to
`api.telegram.org/bot<token>/sendMessage` through the existing template machinery, plus a chat-id field.
About a day, and it is the only path in this release that reaches a phone with the app closed. Web Push
(service worker + VAPID) is v1.1, named as such.

**Export and API, for type 5.** `User.api_keys[]` with an `x-api-key` check in the existing middleware
(~half a day; no such credential exists anywhere in `server/src` today), plus `GET
/z/:tent_id/export.ndjson` (every Ding and Kante) and `GET /z/:tent_id/export.csv?measure=` (raw Influx
samples). The `Werte` row prints the exact `GET` that produced the screen, so the API is discovered by
tapping and cannot rot, because the webapp consumes it.

## 11. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

The same screen, minus what the hardware does not have. Nothing stubbed, disabled or greyed.

- **Their device becomes a Zelt with one member** on migration, named after the device. Nothing dangles.
- **Große Bahn = Wertband.** Lanes are the measures the device actually reports (`VALID_SENSORS ∩ measures
  with data in the last 7 days` — a plug owner sees two lanes, not fifteen empty ones) plus its switch
  state as a state timeline.
- **Differences are real, not filler:** `▲ Länger an — 4 Std 20 statt 3 Std 55 (+25 min)` · `▲ Das Licht
  lief 25 min länger — 12 Std 25 statt 12 Std 00 (Plan)` · `● Drehzahl höher — ⌀ 1 840 → 2 210 U/min` ·
  `▲ Gerät war 2 Std 10 offline — 03:12 bis 05:22`.
- **`Warum?` mostly does not appear, and that is correct.** A plug has no `steuert` edge and no role table,
  so there is nothing to walk to and no button is rendered. It does get one path: its `ziel` Zeilen
  (`overtemperature`, `undertemperature`, `time`, heater day/night) carry `geaendert_von`, so `▲ Länger an`
  walks to `Übertemperatur-Grenze 30 → 32 °C · Ben, gestern` — the first genuinely new thing a
  three-year-old plug has ever told its owner.
- **The payoff of `Tent`:** an old plug can be **added to a controller's tent**. Its lane then appears in
  that tent's Spur under `Gleichzeitig` — dotted, never `weil`, because we know nothing about what it is
  plugged into. That is the first time a plug owner's data has stood next to anything.
- **Settings are byte-identical.** `/device/:id/settings` stays routed and renders the existing
  `fan-settings` / `light-settings` / `plug-settings` components through the existing `ngSwitch`. `dryer`
  stays dead per `AGENTS.md` and keeps its page verbatim as one Zeile `Einstellungen (alt)`. `fridge`
  keeps its settings component and gains the camera-capable Zelt Tafel.
- **Non-breakage checklist:** `/charts` and `/diary` redirect with the query string intact · `ShareLink`
  values unchanged · `ChartPreset.query` still parses · `applyViewParams` keeps its back-compat branch ·
  the three live i18n bugs in plug settings (`settings.limits.overtemperature.enabled`,
  `.undertemperature.enabled`, `.time.enabled`) are fixed in the same pass, two lines each.

## 12. Day one and the first 48 hours

A difference engine with nothing to compare and a neighbourhood with one neighbour both render as an empty
grid. The fix: **the tent's history is genuinely short on day one, and the screen says so with
timestamps.** `ANDERS ALS …` is not shown before a comparison is possible. In its place:

```
SEIT DEM ANSCHLIESSEN
✓ 14:02   Controller Keller verbunden
✓ 14:04   Erstes Bild
✓ 14:31   Temperatur, Luftfeuchte und VPD laufen
○ heute 22:00   Erste Nacht — danach kann die App Tag und Nacht vergleichen
○ morgen 14:04  Erster Vergleich mit gestern
```

Ticked rows are facts already in `VERLAUF`; open rows are computed from the schedule and the clock, not
from a wizard. **The block deletes itself when the last row ticks** — no "Weiter", cannot be dismissed,
cannot be re-entered. The große Bahn shows the single frame with `Noch kein Vorher — das erste Bild ist
von heute 14:04.` **No delta is ever fabricated against a synthetic zero.** The Spur has one dot; the tent
illustration sits behind it at 12 % opacity.

Two remedies fire on day one and only on day one, both arithmetic rather than advice: no socket role
reported → `Keine Dose gemeldet — das Zelt kann nichts steuern.` `[ Dosen einrichten ]`; no camera and
empty `cloudSettings.rtspStream` → `Keine Kamera gekoppelt — der Vergleich zeigt nur Zahlen.`
`[ Kamera einrichten ]`, once, in the große Bahn, never again.

**Hour 0–24:** values, ages, `VERLAUF`, capture sheets and `Zettel` all work fully. **Hour 24:** the first
pair appears, the first three difference rows are real, the snap ladder gains `gestern gleiche Zeit`.
**Hour 48:** `letzte Nacht` becomes an anchor and the night/day split in the verdict strip becomes
meaningful. The first rolling film exists after 24 h. **The word "Pflanze" has still not appeared** unless
the user tapped `+`.

## 14. Honest weaknesses

1. **The hard-edge set is small, and most days nothing has a `weil`.** Three edge classes produce
   causation and they only cover measures the controller steers. On a monitoring-only tent — user type 1,
   probably the largest group — `Warum?` almost never renders and the headline mechanism is invisible.
   They get C14's differences and C36's browser, which is a good product but not *this* one.
2. **`steuert` is a model of firmware behaviour, not a reading of it.** The device does not tell us why it
   switched a socket; we reconstruct it from setpoint, measure and role. If hysteresis, minimum-runtime or
   workmode logic differs from our model — and `out_dehumidifier` already doubles as a cooler in
   `temp`/`breed`, which proves the model is lossy — we will print a confident `weil` that is subtly
   wrong. That is the most dangerous failure here, because the whole design promises `weil` is earned.
3. **Users will read `Gleichzeitig` as causation anyway.** A dotted line and a missing verb are a
   typographic defence against a cognitive habit. I can refuse to *say* it; I cannot stop it being read.
4. **The graph is a new failure surface with total blast radius.** One collection, one browser, one
   request touching `dinge`, `kanten`, `DeviceLog`, `Image` metadata and Influx. If it is slow the whole
   product is slow, and unlike C14 there is no second screen to fall back to.
5. **Nine rules is not "the app tells me what to do".** They cover the failures I can compute and say
   nothing about pests, deficiencies, light distance or pH lockout. A user who sees one remedy and then
   none for weeks will reasonably conclude the feature is thin. It is thin. It is also the most that can
   be said honestly from temperature, humidity, sockets and a clock.
6. **The Spur is a lot of pixels for a phone.** 108 px of lanes plus a 126 px große Bahn plus a scrub
   header is over a third of a 390×844 viewport before any prose — so the difference list, which C14 says
   is the answer, starts below the fold on a small phone.
7. **Carrying the cursor across a walk is unusual, and unusual costs.** Nobody has trained this gesture. A
   user who walks to the heater expecting "how is my heater" and gets "how was my heater at 03:12 last
   night" has been surprised by the cleverest idea in the concept. The breadcrumb prints the time; it does
   not remove the surprise.
8. **Films on a docker volume are operationally new.** Everything is Mongo and one process today. A volume
   changes backup scope, fills up, and fails a render silently unless someone writes the check. GridFS
   avoids that and costs a day more; I picked the volume and this is the bill.
9. **`Tent` is the right model and a real migration** — every query in `server/src` is `device_id`-scoped
   and `auth.middleware.ts` resolves ownership by device. One-tent-per-device makes migration free and the
   *benefit* zero until someone merges two devices, which most never will. I pay in v1 and collect in v2.
10. **Two developers, sixteen line items, no slack is a hope, not a plan.** Items 5, 6 and 12 are the
    differentiators and none is the item marked as slack. If 13 does not absorb the overrun, the thing
    that slips is the Kette — and without the Kette this is C14 and C36 stapled together, which is exactly
    the failure the brief warned about.
