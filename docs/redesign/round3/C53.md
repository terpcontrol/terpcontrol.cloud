# C53 — "Zwei Zustände"

**Family:** fusion (C14 × C36 × C11) · **Centre of gravity:** every thing is shown twice — damals and
jetzt — and the slider between the two is the whole navigation system.

Written against C14, C36, C11, NEW-BRIEF.md, 00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md and direct reads of
`/home/user/terpcontrol.cloud` (`server/src/models/images.model.ts`, `server/src/services/image.service.ts:583`,
`webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts`, `webapp/src/app/util/socket-info.ts`).

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every Ding in Terp Control renders as exactly two states side by side — `DAMALS` and `JETZT` — with one
German sentence naming the difference and one slider that moves the left state through time. There is no
second screen shape, because there is no second question.**

A tent, a plant, a smart socket, a target temperature, a camera, a person, a photo, a feed: each has a
state at time A and a state at time B, and the only thing anybody opens the app to ask is what moved
between them. One layout — pair, difference, remedy, relations — and the only control is where the left
handle sits.

Uniformity here comes neither from object type (C36's bet) nor from the camera (C14's bet). **It comes from
time-pairing.** Two states and a delta fits a picture, a number, a duty cycle, a growth stage and a
setpoint equally well, which is how one screen carries all of them with no mode and no new abstraction.

C14's discipline holds and is extended: **the app describes the difference, and proposes a remedy only when
the remedy is a button the user already owns.** It never diagnoses a plant.

## 2. What comes from where, and what only exists because they were combined

**From C14 — the pair and the named difference.** Two renderings with the delta stated in one German
sentence; the Befund rows and their deterministic ranking by `|Δ| / max(σ₁₄, σ_floor)`; prose and number in
the same DOM node; the refusal to diagnose; the snap ladder on the time control; the numbers-only variant
for a tent without a camera.

**From C36 — one screen shape, depth by walking a named relation.** The `Ding` object with `art`, `t`,
`t_ende`, German relations and a body that is the only varying part. Zeile when related, Tafel when
subject. `Werte {…}` at the bottom of every screen showing the literal `GET` that produced it. Capability
from `parseSocketRoles()` per role, failing closed. Reveal by data. Corrections instead of edits.

**From C11 — the shared cursor and pause-to-unfold.** One cursor, many projections; drag any and all move;
correlation in both directions. Pause as the disclosure gesture, self-cancelling, persisted nowhere. A
pinned scrub header instead of a tooltip. The `Streifen` showing kept frames, culled frames and gaps.

### THE FUSION — the three ideas that exist only because all three sources were present

**F1 · The comparison span is a property of the browser, not of a screen — and it survives the walk.**
C36 walks relations; C14 has a pair; C11 shares a cursor across projections. Put together: **the pair's two
cursors are global, they live in the URL, and every Ding you walk to opens as its own pair over the same
span.** The tent says `1,7 °C wärmer`; you tap it; the `Heizung` Dose opens showing `4 Std 10` vs
`1 Std 05` over exactly the same three days; you tap its `Ziel` and the target Ding shows `24,0 °C` vs
`25,0 °C` and who changed it. Three walks, one question, never restated. C14 has one pair on one screen,
C36 has one moment walked, C11 has one playhead on one film — none of them can do this alone.

**F2 · `Ziel` is the leftmost detent of the same slider.**
Every Ding must have two states, so the app needs a left state on day one when there is no past. The answer
is not an empty state: the left handle's first detent is **`Ziel`**, and the pair reads `SOLL | IST` in
exactly the same layout. Goal-versus-actual stops being a chart feature or a settings screen and becomes a
slider position, on every Ding, from minute four, forever. It only falls out because C14's pair had to be
generalised to C36's every-Ding, and the uniform shape forced us to find the missing left state.

**F3 · Setpoint history is not bolted on; the object model produces it.**
"Every chart draws today's target over last month's data" is a failure of all sixty concepts. Here it is
structurally unshippable: the `DAMALS` column must show the target **as it was then**, or the pair lies.
C36's `Ding` already has `t` / `t_ende`, so a target is stored as a **span** that closes and reopens on
every change. Same object, no new mechanism, and a v1 blocker rather than a v1.1 nice-to-have.

Remove any one source and the concept collapses: without C14 there is no pair and no difference sentence;
without C36 there is no uniform Ding for the pair to be *of*, and the idea degenerates to a camera feature;
without C11 the span is a per-screen setting instead of a carried cursor, which is a mode in all but name.

## 3. Information architecture

Four routes. No tab bar.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/zelte` | Your Zelte. Exactly one → straight through, as today. |
| `/z/:zelt_id/:ding_id?von=&bis=` | **The browser.** One component. `ding_id` absent ⇒ the Zelt is the Subjekt. |
| `/z/:zelt_id/chart?…` | The chart. The only non-pair screen (§6). |
| `/z/:zelt_id/film/:film_id` | One rendered film, playable and downloadable. |

`von` / `bis` are epoch ms; `bis=jetzt` is the literal default. **The span is in the URL, so a comparison
is bookmarkable and shareable** — "schau dir Tag 20 neben Tag 40 an", pasted into a club's group chat.

Kept so persisted user data resolves:

```
/device/:id/charts?<q>   → 301 /z/<zelt_of(id)>/chart?<q>   (applyViewParams back-compat branch intact)
/device/:id/diary        → 301 /z/<zelt_of(id)>
/device/:id/settings     → 301 /z/<zelt_of(id)>/ziele        (controller & fridge only)
                         → unchanged page for fan / light / plug / dryer
/login /account /shares /demo /classes /diagnostics /device/:id/testmode   untouched
```

### The tent, finally

```ts
interface Zelt {
  zelt_id: string; owner_id: string; name: string;
  device_ids: string[];            // 1..n. one grow, one diary, one day counter.
  zeitzone: string;                // "Europe/Berlin" — every day boundary resolves here
  begonnen_am: number;             // day 1 of the current run
  medium_key?: string; regime_key?: string;
}
```

**Migration is one idempotent boot job**: every claimed device with no Zelt gets one, named after the
device, `device_ids:[id]`, `begonnen_am` = its earliest Influx point. Nobody sees a migration screen.
Merging two devices into one Zelt is `+ Gerät` in `Im Zelt`, and it is the only new concept a multi-box
owner has to learn.

### The one object

```ts
interface Ding {
  ding_id: string;      // uuid v4, MINTED BY THE CLIENT → server upserts, retries idempotent
  zelt_id: string;      // scope. auth checks owner_id of the Zelt, then its device list.
  device_id?: string;   // only where it matters: a Dose, a Bild, an Ereignis
  art: DingArt; name: string;
  t: number; t_ende?: number | null;   // null = still open (a span)
  bez?: Record<string, string[]>;      // { an, in, betrifft, ersetzt }
  d?: Record<string, number|string|boolean|object>;
  person?: string; bilder?: string[];
}
type DingArt = 'zelt'|'pflanze'|'dose'|'kamera'|'bild'|'film'
             | 'gabe'|'notiz'|'zettel'|'phase'|'ziel'|'person'|'ereignis';
```

Stored: `pflanze`, `gabe`, `notiz`, `zettel`, `phase`, `person`, **`ziel`**. Projected read-time from what
exists: `zelt`, `dose` (`parseSocketRoles`), `kamera` (`hardwareInfo.webcam_did`), `bild`/`film` (`Image`),
`ereignis` (`DeviceLog`). `ziel` is stored, and is the F3 payoff:

```ts
// one open ziel per key per zelt; a change closes the old span and opens a new one
{ art:'ziel', name:'Nachttemperatur', t:1755892800000, t_ende:null,
  d:{ schluessel:'night.temperature', wert:22.0, einheit:'°C', quelle:'app'|'geraet'|'knopf' },
  person:'p_ben' }
```

Written by the app when it pushes a config change, **and** by a server-side differ that compares
`device.configuration` against the open spans on every inbound `StatusMessage` — so a change made on the
box's rotary knob or by a second browser is recorded too, with `quelle:'geraet'`. No new device-config key,
nothing sent to firmware.

**The one read API:** `GET /api/v1/dinge?zelt_id=&art=&von=&bis=&cursor=`, cursor-paginated by `t`, one
union. The webapp consumes exactly this; the public API is exactly this; they cannot drift.

### 4.1 The pair rows, precisely

One row is a `<button class="paar">`, 64 px, two lines:

- Line 1: label at 13/18 `--tc-text-muted`, delta right-aligned at 15/20 tabular-nums with a `▸` glyph.
- Line 2: `damals` value at 17/24 600-weight, an unspaced `→`, `jetzt` value at 17/24 600-weight, then the
  target pair at 13/18 muted.

**The delta is never coloured by direction.** Warmer is not worse. Colour is reserved for the goal
comparison: the `Ziel` fragment carries the 8 px status dot — `--tc-ok` inside band, `--tc-warn` outside by
less than the alarm threshold, `--tc-bad` past it, hollow when the value is stale. This is the single
strongest reason the pair does not become a scoreboard.

Rows exist only for what the Zelt reports: CO₂ only with the SCD4x, `Entfeuchter` only with a paired
`dehumidifier` role, `Pflanzen` only when a `pflanze` Ding exists. Missing `hardwareInfo.sockets` (old
firmware) fails **closed** to monitor-only with one line: `Gerät meldet keine Steckdosen — Firmware zu alt.`

### 4.2 Der Regler — what the slider moves through

A two-handle range control, 44 px tall, pinned under the pair. **It scrubs the comparison span itself:
where `damals` sits, and optionally where `jetzt` sits.**

- **Track = Datenband.** A 14 px sparkline of the primary measure over the whole run, overlaid with 1 px
  ticks where kept camera frames exist (gaps read as gaps — C11's Streifen) and 3 px dots for `gabe` Dinge.
  A picture of the run, not a chart.
- **Right handle is pinned to `jetzt`** with a 🔒. A 400 ms long-press unpins it; the right header becomes
  its date and a `[↦ jetzt]` chip appears. Two arbitrary points — `Tag 20` next to `Tag 40` — with no new
  screen and no setting.
- **Left handle detents**, 44 px magnetic zones: `Ziel · Beginn · Phase · 1 Woche · gestern · zuletzt ·
  jetzt`. Between detents any minute is reachable and the exact timestamp prints in the column header while
  dragging. A fat thumb at 2 am cannot miss `gestern`; a deliberate finger lands anywhere. Detents appear
  **as they become true** (§13) — `Phase` needs a `phase` Ding, `zuletzt` a previous session
  (`localStorage['tc-zuletzt-<zelt_id>']`, written on blur), `seit Annas Besuch` two `person` Dinge.

**Dragging is playing.** While a handle is down, at 4 Hz, the frames, all pair rows, socket duty cycles,
plant stage and the target fragments update from a precomputed hourly rollup — never raw Influx. The
headline sentence recomputes; the ranked list, `Was du tun kannst`, `Verlauf` and `Werte` collapse to a
2 px placeholder so the drag holds 60 fps. That collapse is C11's "playing" state, and it is honest: those
sections are stale for as long as the handle is moving.

**Release is pause, and pause unfolds.** 180 ms after release everything comes back *enriched* for the
exact span: the ranked `Unterschiede`, all of them, with measured vs target vs delta at both ends · `Was du
tun kannst`, re-evaluated · which sockets ran and for how long at each end · `gabe`/`notiz`/`zettel` Dinge
within ±2 h of either endpoint · the two frames' own verdict, `belichtet ×1,04 · Schärfe 0,71 · behalten`
or `verworfen (Hand im Bild)`. Nothing is persisted — no key, no preference, no segment — and the next drag
collapses it again. It hides no feature class; it is data *about the span you are pointing at*.

**Correlation runs both ways.**
`Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` in any temperature row moves the left handle to that excursion.
Tapping a frame tick on the Datenband moves the left handle to that frame.
Every diary entry carries `[ Seit hier vergleichen ]`, which sets `von` to that entry's `t` — so
*„was hat sich seit Annas Gabe geändert?"* is one tap, and that is the club's actual question.

### 4.3 States

| Situation | What the screen does |
| --- | --- |
| **Frisch / veraltet** | < 2 min filled dots; 2–10 min hollow dots, values muted, own age line. Nothing is ever blanked — a stale number is still the best number available. |
| **Offline** (> 10 min) | header `● Offline seit Mo 14:20`; `JETZT` keeps its last values, greyed, timestamped. `Gabe / Notiz / Foto / Zettel` stay enabled — logging is a cloud write. `Ziel ändern` disables: `Gerät offline — Änderung wird bei Rückkehr gesendet.` |
| **No camera** | frame cells absent, pair is value rows at full width, Datenband loses its ticks. `Keine Kamera gekoppelt.` + `[Kamera einrichten]`. No `film` Ding anywhere. |
| **Camera silent > 3 h** | both frames are the last kept ones, the right one strip-captioned `Letztes Bild vor 3 Std 12`; Regel 7 fires. |
| **Frames but no numbers** | value lines read `— (keine Daten)`; the frames still pair. The two sources fail independently and the screen says which. |
| **Nothing changed** | headline `Seit gestern hat sich wenig geändert.`, every row `○`, list still complete. |
| **Share link, `webcam:false`** | numbers-only variant; `Gabe`/`Zettel` hidden; the Regler works. |

### 4.4 How it looks

Type scale (16 px root): `28/32` Zelt name · `22/28` a single dominant number · `17/24` pair values and
prose · `15/20` deltas · `13/18` labels, ages, targets · `11/14` detent labels. `font-variant-numeric:
tabular-nums` everywhere, so the two columns align optically.

Spacing on a 4 px base: section gap 24, row gap 12, inner 8, page padding 16; touch targets ≥ 44; slider
track 4 px, handles 28 px visual / 44 px hit. The two frame cells are separated by exactly 16 px so the gap
reads as a seam, not as two cards. Colours `--tc-ok #3F8F5B`, `--tc-warn #B8791F`, `--tc-bad #A93A32`,
theme-matched pairs; status is an 8 px dot **plus** a glyph (`▲ ● ○ ✎`), never colour alone.

**Empty-state drawing**, one SVG at 160×96, used in three places: a 2 px line drawing of two picture frames
on a shelf, the left one empty with a dashed border, a slider beneath with the handle at the far left.
Caption: `Noch kein Damals. Links steht solange dein Ziel.` No illustration library.

## 5. NO-MODES MECHANISM

The falsification test: **if you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of
feature, it is a mode.**

**M1 · Two states are not two modes, because both are always on screen.**
There is no control that shows `damals` and hides `jetzt`, or vice versa. They are two columns of one
layout, rendered simultaneously, for every Ding, for every user, from install. The "second rendering" is a
column, not a state of the app.

**M2 · The slider is a position, not a preference.**
Its value lives in the URL and in a `SpanneService: BehaviorSubject<{von, bis}>`. It is not written to
`localStorage`, it does not survive a cold start (it resets to `zuletzt → jetzt`), and it changes no
feature's existence — only which two instants are rendered. A drunk user cannot get stuck in it, because
every position renders the same complete screen.

**M3 · Pause-to-unfold, C11 verbatim.**
Nothing persisted, self-cancelling, reversible by the identical gesture, hides no feature class. The
stoner reads three rows of the unfolded panel; the techie reads eleven; it is the same panel.

**M4 · Depth is subject-distance, C36 verbatim.** One Zeile when related, a full pair when Subjekt. You do
not set depth, you walk a difference — statelessly, so nothing the techie does today changes what anyone
sees tomorrow.

**M5 · Capability and data decide what exists.** Per-role sockets, not the three-bucket
`deviceControlCapability()`. CO₂ only with the sensor. No `pflanze` Ding ⇒ the word "Pflanze" appears
exactly once in the product, on the `+` in `Im Zelt`. Neither is reversible by a setting.

**`webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts` and `EXPERT_MODE_STORAGE_KEY` are deleted in the same commit as the
first screen of this concept**, along with the settings segment that reads it. Not renamed. Deleted.

### Honest audit of my own design

| Suspect | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| `6 weitere Unterschiede anzeigen` | **Not a mode** — expands the same list in place by the printed sort key; the hidden rows are the low-ranked ones by construction. |
| Unpinning the right handle | **Closest call.** It is a long-press that changes what the right column means. Defence: it produces the identical layout with a different timestamp, and no feature appears or disappears. A critic can fairly say a long-press is a hidden affordance; I accept that and mitigate with a visible 🔒 and a one-time coach line on the second session. |
| The drag-time collapse of the lower sections | **Not a mode** — it is a loading state with a duration of one gesture, and it self-cancels. |
| `Werte {…}` | **Not a mode** — same row, same position, every screen, for everybody, from install. |

## 6. The chart — the pair unfolded onto an axis

The chart is the only screen that is not a pair, and it earns that by being **the space between the two
handles, drawn**. Entering it from any pair row carries `von`/`bis`/`measure`; leaving it writes the
handles back. The playhead is the same `SpanneService`.

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line directive. `chart.js` / `ng2-charts` /
`chartjs-adapter-luxon` deleted in the same commit.

```
‹ Zelt Keller · Temperatur                22.08. – 25.08.
[96px Bild]  Sa 23.08. 03:12                     ← pinned scrub header, no tooltip
18,1 °C · Ziel 20,0 · −1,9 · Heizung an · Licht aus
░░░░████████░░░░░░░░████████░░░░                 ← Tag/Nacht
    ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒ Zielband (markArea)
 ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄ Sollwert (stepped, dashed, HISTORIC)
 ──────╮ ╭──── Ist (solid, min/max envelope)
Licht       ████████        ████████             ← state-timeline lanes
Heizung     ▄ ▄  ▄   ▄▄
Entfeuchter       ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄
▪▪  ▪   ▪③      ▪        ▪▪   ← Menschen (tappable, never hover)
 ▲       ▲          ▲         ← Gerät

längste Abweichung 2 Std 10 (Nacht, zu warm)
⟨ hier vergleichen ⟩   ← writes von/bis back from the zoom
```

- **Sollwert is drawn from the `ziel` spans**, so it steps where the user actually changed it, on the date
  they changed it. This is the F3 payoff and it is the single largest correctness fix in the product.
  Where no span exists (before this release), the line is drawn dotted-grey and labelled
  `Ziel vor 10/2026 nicht aufgezeichnet`. Honest, ugly, temporary.
- **Zielband** priority, one at a time, labelled: alarm thresholds > Sollwert ± Toleranz (±1,5 °C / ±7 % rF)
  > stage `vpdRange`. **Tag/Nacht** from measured `out_light > 0`, schedule as fallback, timezone named in
  the axis label — a missing band where the schedule says day is a failed contactor, not decoration.
- **Outputs** as state-timeline regions; length = duration, null = gap; never 0/1 lines. Tapping the
  `Entfeuchter` lane prints the truth nobody has told: `In den Betriebsarten „temp" und „breed" kühlt diese
  Dose, sie entfeuchtet nicht.`
- **Annotation rail**, two rows (people / machine), 10 px clustering with count badges, dashed full-height
  lines at `phase` starts. Tapping a mark opens that Ding — as a pair, over its own ±.
- **Time in range** server-side on **raw** Influx samples, split Tag/Nacht, longest excursion as a duration,
  greyed below 80 % coverage. `sampling:'minmax'`; LTTB for the navigator only. Two fingers pan/zoom, one
  scrolls, tap sets the cursor; axis labels never hidden.
- **Prerequisite, not a feature:** parameterise the Flux interpolation of `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval`
  and constrain interval by timespan, before anything else touches the chart.

## 7. Diary and multi-plant

**There is no diary feature.** The Zelt's `Verlauf` is the tent's diary; a `pflanze`'s `Verlauf` is that
plant's diary; same renderer. The machine writes `bild`, `film`, `ereignis`, `ziel` and `phase` into the
same list the human writes `gabe`, `notiz` and `zettel` into — which is what "es macht das Tagebuchführen
für dich" means operationally: log nothing, still have a full diary.

Every `gabe` and `notiz` gets `d.auto_bild` = the nearest **kept** frame within ±5 min, server-side, one
Mongo lookup, no upload. That frame is what the double-feed guard shows (§8) and the overlay prints (§10).

```ts
pflanze  d:{ sorte_text?, medium_key?, topf_l?, quelle?:'samen'|'steckling'|'gekauft',
             begonnen_am, geerntet_am?, ernte_g?, entfernt_am? }
         name:"A3 · Wedding Cake"          // renaming NEVER changes ding_id
         bez:{ in:[zelt_id] }

phase    d:{ stufe: DiaryLifecycleStage }   t / t_ende      bez:{ an:[pflanze…] }   // [] = whole tent

gabe     d:{ wasser_l, kannen?, kanne_l?, ec?, ph?, ec_ort?:'zulauf'|'ablauf'|'tank',
             ablauf_ph?, ablauf_ec?,
             produkte:[{ name, ml_pro_l, aus_regime:boolean }],
             regime_key?, schritt?, auto_bild? }
         bez:{ an:[pflanze…] }              // [] = "ans ganze Zelt"
         person

notiz    d:{ text, auto_bild? }             bilder
zettel   d:{ text, geschlossen_von? }       t / t_ende(null = offen)
person   d:{ farbe }                        name only. device-free, zelt-scoped, no account.
```

`DiaryLifecycleStage` gains `ernte` as a seventh stage — harvest is not a stage today, so "wie lief dieser
Durchgang" is unanswerable. Optional field, read-time normaliser, no migration tool.

`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a **one-time idempotent boot backfill** emitting `pflanze` + `phase`
Dinge from historic `diary-plant-lifecycle` rows keyed by trimmed `lifecycleName`. Nothing is deleted;
legacy `DeviceLog` rows keep projecting as `ereignis`/`notiz`, including the seven English-prose regexes.
**Server-side cursor pagination for `dinge` and `DeviceLog` is a v1 prerequisite** — the whole product is
lists and there is no log pagination today.

**The plant-ignorer is untaxed.** `bez.an: []` means the whole tent and is the default forever. No plant
chooser, no per-plant section, no "0 Pflanzen" card, no nag.

## 9. What to do about it — the ten rules

The gap every one of sixty concepts failed. **Deterministic, rule-based, from data the app already holds.
No AI, no advisor, no plant diagnosis.** Every row prints which rule fired; `ⓘ Regel 1` opens the rule text.

The governing constraint: **a remedy is proposed only when it is a control the user owns, or a note they
can write. Otherwise the app says plainly that it cannot act.**

| # | Fires when | German | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | night mean > night setpoint + 1,5 K for ≥ 2 h, heater role paired | `Nachts 2,4 °C über Ziel. Das Nachtziel steht auf 25,0 °C.` | `[Auf 22,0 °C stellen]` |
| 2 | rF > target + 5 pp for ≥ 2 h, `dehumidifier` paired, `out_dehumidifier` duty 0 % | `Der Entfeuchter ist gekoppelt, lief aber nicht. Das Feuchte-Ziel steht auf 70 %.` | `[Ziel auf 60 % stellen]` |
| 3 | rF > target + 5 pp for ≥ 2 h, **no** dehumidifier role | `Dein Zelt hat keinen Entfeuchter. Hier hilft nur mehr Abluft oder weniger gießen — stellen kann die App nichts.` | none, deliberately |
| 4 | measured `out_light` on-time differs from schedule by > 15 min | `Licht lief 25 Min kürzer als geplant. Prüf die Dose „Licht" (192.168.1.42).` | `[Dose testen]` |
| 5 | heater duty 100 % for ≥ 1 h **and** temperature still falling | `Die Heizung lief durchgehend und es wurde trotzdem kälter. Tür offen, oder die Dose schaltet nicht.` | `[Dose testen]` |
| 6 | CO₂ socket duty 100 % for ≥ 1 h and CO₂ below target | `Die CO₂-Dose lief durchgehend, das Ziel wurde nicht erreicht — vermutlich ist die Flasche leer.` | `[Zettel „Flasche leer" anlegen]` |
| 7 | no kept frame for > 3 h, camera paired | `Kamera seit 3 Std 12 ohne Bild.` | `[Testbild anfordern]` |
| 8 | `out_light > 50 %` for 15 min while frame brightness sits in the lights-off band, or the inverse | `Das Licht sollte an sein, das Bild ist dunkel.` | `[Dose testen]` |
| 9 | regime step due and no `gabe` since | `Fütterung fällig — Schritt 5, seit 2 Tagen.` | `[Gabe eintragen]` (prefilled) |
| 10 | zelt offline > 10 min | `Zelt seit 22 Min offline. Zuletzt: WLAN −78 dBm.` | `[Diagnose]` |

Rules 5, 6 and 8 are the class no competitor can build: they need the lamp schedule, the socket duty cycle
and the measurement on one clock, and rule 8 needs the picture too. **Neither the picture nor the data
detects a failed contactor alone; together they detect it in fifteen minutes.**

What is refused, permanently: naming a deficiency from leaf colour, calling a droop, measuring height,
counting plants, detecting pests, or any sentence beginning "wahrscheinlich liegt es an".

`Was du tun kannst` is absent — header and all — when no rule fires. Most days it is absent. That is
correct.

## 10. Camera, timelapse, and image correlation

### 10.1 What is already there

`sharp` 0.34.5, an ImageMagick wrapper and `ffmpeg` are already dependencies; a frame lands every 30 s;
rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses build hourly; a 30-day URL-embeddable `?token=` with `?width=` resizing
exists; `imageService.addOfflineOverlay` already composites SVG with sharp. **No new binary, no ML
runtime.** Two things are wrong and both are cheap: the encode is **`libx265`** (`image.service.ts:583`),
which does not play in Chrome or Firefox — today's timelapse is invisible to most customers who own a
camera — and no frame is ever culled.

### 10.2 The cull, per frame, at ingest (≤ 60 ms)

Two `sharp` decodes plus plain JS on ~9 KB of pixels, writing a `bildbefund` doc per frame (~200 B, thinned
with the images): `light_state` from `out_light` at ts ± 60 s (a database join, not computer vision) ·
`helligkeit` · `schaerfe` (Laplacian variance, reported as `unscharf`, never as a cause) · `phash` 8×8
dHash for dedup · `versatz_dx/dy` integer SAD ±8 px (**translation only** — a rotated camera is detected,
announced and cut to a new Kapitel, never silently warped) · `kacheln[48]` mean abs diff on an 8×6 grid,
the change map, which says **where** and never **what** · a transient test with one frame of lookahead that
catches a hand or an open door and is honestly blind to anything lasting > 60 s.

Refused permanently, as in C14: naming a deficiency, calling a droop, measuring height in cm, counting
plants, segmenting canopy, detecting pests. The product points at the tile and shows the pair; the human
does the semantics.

### 10.3 The film, and how it is stored

**The mp4 does not go in Mongo.** `Image.data` is a `Buffer` in a BSON document capped at 16 MB
(`server/src/models/images.model.ts:22`), there is no GridFS anywhere, and loading whole buffers into Node
is already a latent problem on the existing mp4 path. A twelve-week film is 12–20 MB.

**Decision, not an option list:** renders are written to a docker volume `RENDER_DIR=/data/films` and
streamed with real `Range` support. Mongo holds only metadata:

```ts
FilmRender { film_id, zelt_id, von, bis, spec{fps,lange_s,kante,untertitel},
             status:'queued'|'rendering'|'ready'|'failed', fortschritt,
             bilder_gesamt, bilder_behalten, verworfen{dunkel,doppelt,besuch,unscharf},
             kapitel, bytes, pfad, laeuft_ab_am, erstellt_von, erstellt_am }
```

GridFS is the fallback **only if ops refuses a volume**; the volume wins because Range streaming from a
file is 15 lines and Range streaming from GridFS is a chunk-cursor. Renders expire after 30 days unless the
user ticks `behalten`. The rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` films keep working, re-encoded to x264.

Encode, verbatim:

```sh
ffmpeg -y -loglevel error -threads 2 -framerate 24 -f image2 -i "$DIR/%05d.jpg" \
  -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -preset medium -crf 21 \
  -g 48 -movflags +faststart -metadata title="Terp Control – Zelt Keller · Tag 1–68" "$DIR/out.mp4"
```

Filename ASCII-folded, `^[a-z0-9_-]{1,100}\.mp4$`: `terpcontrol_zelt-keller_2026-06-14_2026-08-20_720p.mp4`.

**Off the phone, three real paths, feature-detected in order:** `navigator.share({files:[File]})` capped at
50 MB (on iOS 15+ the sheet's "Video sichern" puts it straight in Fotos) → `Content-Disposition: attachment`
download with the honest one-time iOS line `Auf dem iPhone landet der Film in „Dateien". Von dort: Teilen →
„Video sichern".` → a `ShareLink` with `page:'film'`, an **unguarded** `/f/:share_id` page with `<video
controls playsinline>` and `og:video` tags so it unfurls with an inline player in Telegram and Discord.
Names and diary text are stripped unless the owner ticks `Einträge mitzeigen`.

The cull report is shown **before** rendering — it is the honesty that explains why this film is watchable:

```
2 148 Bilder · 361 aussortiert
  289 Licht aus · 41 Hand im Bild · 22 unscharf · 9 Kamera verrutscht
= 1 787 Bilder → 74 Sek bei 24 B/s · 2 Kapitel (Kamera verrutscht am 12.09.)
```

Burn-in overlay via the existing SVG-composite technique: bottom-left `Blüte · Tag 24 · 25.08. 14:02 ·
25,1 °C · 58 % rF`, bottom-right `💧 Anna · 6,0 l` when a `gabe` falls within 10 min. Normalisation via
`sharp.linear()` toward the film's own rolling median, clamped to ±8 % per keeper — stated limit: it cannot
recover colour a magenta lamp never recorded.

### 10.4 The slider and the pictures

The Datenband's frame ticks and the pair's two frames are served by **one sprite request, not N**:
`GET /film/band/:zelt_id?von&bis&n=100` returns a single JPEG of up to 100 tiles at 160×90 plus
`…/band.json` = `[{t,x,y}]`, cached in a capped collection with a 24 h TTL. This is v1 here (C14 and C11
both deferred it) because the Datenband is on the home screen and 100 requests over tent wifi is not
shippable.

Tapping either frame opens the **Wischvergleich**: the two frames stacked with a draggable vertical wipe
handle, pinch-zoom locked together, the change-map tiles above the 90th percentile tinted at 35 % in
`--ion-color-warning` on both frames. Swipe down to dismiss. It is a rendering of the same two frames, at
the same level as pinch-zoom — not another view.

## 11. Clubs

- **`person` is a Zelt-scoped name, not an account.** Typed once, no e-mail, no `auth.middleware` surgery,
  ~2 days. Every capture stamps `Ding.person`; the last one used in this browser is prefilled, so the 2-tap
  path survives. The `Wer?` row renders only with ≥ 2 `person` Dinge — a solo grower never sees it.
- **A detent per person.** With ≥ 2 people the Regler gains `seit Annas Besuch` — the newest Ding carrying
  `person:'p_anna'`. *„Was hat sich geändert, seit Anna zuletzt da war"* in one tap, and it is the fusion's
  cleanest club feature: it needs the pair (C14), the Ding-with-a-person (C36) and the carried span (C11).
- **The double-feed guard with `[Bild ansehen]` as evidence** (§8), and **`zettel`** — the note on the tent
  door, opened by anyone, closed by anyone with `✓` stamping `d.geschlossen_von` + `t_ende`. It sits
  **above the pair**, the only element that outranks the picture, because a human wrote it for a human.
- **Corrections, not edits.** Editing a `gabe` writes a new one with `bez.ersetzt` at the old; the old
  Zeile renders struck through. Reconstructable history without cryptographic theatre.
- **Not in v1: real memberships.** That needs a `Membership` collection, a rewrite of
  `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207` and loosening ~10 owner-scoped queries. Until then a club shares one
  login. Upgrade path designed in: a later account sets `person.d.user_id` and every historic Ding resolves.
- **Privacy, default-maximal.** Harvest weight and plant count are never prompted; `ernte_g` is an optional
  field on a sheet nobody is sent to. No location, no analytics SDK, no community feed. Public film links
  are tokened and expire. `Alles löschen` on the Zelt genuinely deletes `dinge`, `Image`, `FilmRender`,
  `bildbefund`, `DeviceLog` and the Influx series — which also fixes the standing lie where "Clear logs"
  removes nothing from the diary. Refused outright: per-member dispensing, grams handed over, member
  identity. No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.

**Reaching a closed app** — the server has `sendMail` and webhooks only, no push, no service worker. v1
ships the honest answer: a **Telegram bot** (a bot token is a webhook with a chat id; ~1 day) as the path
that actually reaches a locked phone, plus the existing e-mail for everyone else, plus the existing generic
webhook for type 5. Web Push behind a service worker is v1.1 and is named as v1.1, not implied.

## 15. Honest weaknesses

1. **Two columns on a 390 px phone is genuinely cramped, and the camera is the selling point.** Two 168 px
   frames are smaller than one 358 px frame. The Wischvergleich mitigates it at the cost of a tap. On a
   phone, this concept shows the product's best asset at half size by construction.
2. **The delta invites a causal reading the app refuses to make.** `Heizung lief 3 Std weniger` sitting
   next to `2,4 °C wärmer` will be read as cause and effect. Sometimes it is; sometimes the window was
   open. I describe and I rank; users will infer, and some inferences will be wrong.
3. **Setpoint history starts on release day.** Every span reaching before October renders
   `Ziel damals — nicht aufgezeichnet`. The concept's most important correctness fix is invisible for the
   first three months and looks like a bug in the meantime.
4. **The Regler needs precomputed rollups, which are a new failure mode.** Hourly per-measure rollups per
   Zelt in Mongo are what make the drag smooth; a lagging rollup shows a stale `DAMALS` column with a fresh
   `JETZT` column, and that error is silent. Mitigation is a coverage stamp on the header, not a fix.
5. **Two handles on a small screen is a fat-thumb hazard at 2 am.** Pinning the right handle behind a
   400 ms long-press mostly solves it and makes the second-most-interesting interaction in the product
   undiscoverable.
6. **On a stable tent the honest answer is usually `wenig verändert`.** A competitor's busy dashboard feels
   more valuable while telling you less. `zuletzt geschaut` helps the weekly visitor and not the daily one —
   and it is per-browser `localStorage`, so clearing site data resets the most-used detent and two club
   members on one login disagree about what "zuletzt" means.
7. **Ten rules is a small number**, and `Was du tun kannst` will be absent on the days a grower most wants
   it. Rule 1 can also be wrong — it proposes lowering the night target when the real cause is a hot room.
   Worst case is a setting the user can undo, but "die App hat gesagt" is a support burden.
8. **No push on iOS in v1.** Telegram reaches people who opt in; e-mail reaches inboxes. An iPhone owner
   without Telegram still learns nothing while the app is closed — the failure a grow controller can least
   afford.
9. **Steering is still a walk.** Changing the night temperature is Zelt → `Ziele` → the Zeile → edit: three
   taps where today's form is two. Rendering all targets as inline `value-edit-row`s on the `Ziele` pair is
   a partial climb-down toward a settings page, and a critic will correctly call it that.
10. **`Ziel` as a slider detent is the cleverest idea here and the likeliest to confuse.** The left column
    silently changes meaning from "a past state" to "the target" at one end of the track. I think the header
    switching `DAMALS` → `SOLL` carries it. I am not certain, and it is the first thing I would put in front
    of five real growers.
