# C57 — "Die Spur"

**Family:** fusion (C14 × C36 × C11-Slider) · **Centre of gravity:** every thing carries its own trace,
and browsing is walking a trace.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Jedes Ding im Zelt hat eine Spur — seinen eigenen Verlauf in der Zeit — und die App hat genau ein
Bedienelement: einen Regler mit zwei Griffen, der eine Spur abtastet. Wie viele Punkte du abtastest,
entscheidet, was du siehst. Nichts anderes entscheidet es.**

A sensor's trace is a series of samples. The tent's trace is the film. A plant's trace is its photos and
its feeds. A socket's trace is its duty timeline. A target's trace is its own change history. A person's
trace is what they did. One primitive, one renderer, one control, at different resolutions.

The control is a rail under the body with **two handles (`Von`, `Bis`) and a playhead (`Kopf`)**. Its
geometry — which you can see, because the handles are always both on screen — decides the reading:

| Handle geometry | Reading | Which source concept this is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Griffe zusammen** | one moment: the body at that instant, fully unfolded | C36's Tafel · C11's pause panel |
| **Griffe auseinander, Kopf steht** | **das Paar**: the body rendered twice, side by side, with the difference named in one German sentence | C14 |
| **Griffe auseinander, ▶** | the Kopf sweeps Von→Bis, the body animates | C11's film |

That is the whole app. There is no "camera view", no "chart mode", no "diary tab". There is a Ding, its
Spur, and where you put your two thumbs.

C14's discipline — **describe the difference, never diagnose it** — is kept for the *picture*, and
deliberately broken for the *knobs*: when a target or a socket's duty changed inside the window, the app
does not diagnose, it **prescribes reversion**. „Ben hat gestern 19:04 das Nachtziel von 19,0 auf 20,0
gestellt. Genau das siehst du. [Zurück auf 19,0 °C]" is not a diagnosis. It is arithmetic on two
endpoints, and it is the thing sixty concepts could not say.

## 2. What comes from where — and what only exists because they were combined

| Source | What this concept takes, verbatim |
| --- | --- |
| **C14** | The **pair** as a first-class readout: two bodies side by side, difference named in one German sentence that carries its own numbers · the **Befund list** (prose line + value line in one DOM node, ranked by a printed deterministic key) · the refusal to name a cause from pixels (`Blattfläche (Bildanteil) 34 % → 37 %`, never `Stickstoffmangel`) · the default left endpoint **„seit du zuletzt geschaut hast"**. |
| **C36** | Everything is a **Ding** with a name, a time, a body and **named German relations**; **one screen shape** for all of them (Kopf · Körper · Spur · Verbindungen · Werte) · **depth is walking a relation, never switching a mode** · **`Werte`** at the bottom of every screen (raw JSON + the literal GET) · reveal by capability (per-role `parseSocketRoles`, failing closed) and by data. |
| **C11** | **One cursor, many projections** — a single `SpurCursor`; Spurband, body, chart lanes, film strip and annotation rail are all projections of it; drag any, all move · **pause is the disclosure gesture** (stops playback, unfolds the minute; unpersisted, self-cancelling, hides no feature class) · **a pinned scrub header, never a hover tooltip** · correlation in both directions (`Nächster Moment über 30 °C →`). |

### THE FUSION — four ideas that exist only because all three were combined

1. **The pair and the film are the same control at two settings.** C14's pair is a trace sampled at two
   points; C11's film is a trace sampled at n points; C36's Zeile is a trace sampled at one point. Once
   you accept C36's "one way to look at a Ding", C14 and C11 stop being two features and become **two
   positions of one slider**. That collapse is the concept.

2. **The pair works for every Ding, so the camera stops being load-bearing.** C14 needed a "numbers-only
   variant" for the 60 % of buyers with no camera. Here the pair is *a Ding body rendered at two
   timestamps* — and C36 already made the body a pure function of the Ding and a time. A tent with a
   camera pairs two frames. A tent without one pairs two sets of four numbers. A socket pairs two duty
   figures (`gestern 4 Std 20 an` / `heute 3 Std 55 an`). A plug from 2021 pairs two switch histories.
   **No fallback, no special case, no attach-rate bet.**

3. **The window walks with you.** The cursor is scoped to the *tent*, not to a widget. Set
   `Von = Umtopfen`, walk into `A3`, and A3's Spur is already showing that window. Walk on to
   `Bio-Bloom`, same window. C36 gave us walking; C11 gave us a cursor; together they give a **time
   window as a thing you carry**, which no single-screen scrubber can do.

4. **A difference has a cause you can name without diagnosing, because a target has a trace too.** C36
   says a `ziel` is a Ding; a Ding has a Spur; therefore the setpoint history exists as an object.
   C14's pair then asks "what else changed between these two endpoints?" and the answer is frequently
   *a knob a human turned*. That is how this concept says what to do (§6) — not with an advisor, with a
   join between two traces over the same window.

Remove C36 and the pair only works on the camera. Remove C14 and the slider has nothing to compare and
degrades to a scrubber. Remove C11 and there is no way to move between the two endpoints, and the
"one Ding, one screen" idea has no verb.

## 3. Information architecture

### 3.1 The tent exists

There is no tent above the device today; everything hangs off `device_id`, so one grow across four
devices becomes four diaries and four disagreeing day counters. **`Tent` is the root of this concept**,
and it costs almost nothing because a trace-merge is the primitive's native operation.

```ts
Tent {
  tent_id: string; owner_id: string;
  name: string;                       // "Zelt Keller"
  device_ids: string[];               // 1..n. order matters for value precedence
  timezone: string;                   // IANA, e.g. "Europe/Berlin". Charts label it.
  primary_device_id: string;          // which device's camera/config the tent body uses
  created_at: number;
}
```

**Migration, boot-time and idempotent:** every claimed device with no tent gets a one-device `Tent`
named after the device. Nobody notices. `device_ids.length > 1` is the only thing that makes the device
chooser render (reveal by data). Merging two tents is one screen in `Zelt → Geräte` and rewrites nothing:
a tent's Spur is the time-merge of its devices' Spuren, computed at read time. When two devices report
the same measure, `primary_device_id` wins and the other shows as a Verbindung Zeile
(`◼ Gerät 2 · 24,4 °C`) — never averaged silently.

### 3.2 Routes — four, plus the untouched ones

| Route | What |
| --- | --- |
| `/z` | Deine Zelte. Exactly one → straight through, as today. |
| `/z/:tent_id` | **The browser.** The tent is the Subjekt. |
| `/z/:tent_id/:thing_id` | Any Ding is the Subjekt. Same component. |
| `/z/:tent_id/spur` | **Die breite Spur** — the same cursor, full-screen, with lanes (§6). |

Untouched and still routed: `/login`, `/account`, `/shares`, `/demo`, `/classes`, `/diagnostics`,
`/link-expired`, `/connection-error`, `/device/:id/testmode`.

**Back-compat is not negotiable** — share links and chart presets are persisted user data:

```
/list                   → /z                        /device/:id/diary   → 301 /z/<tent>
/device/:id             → /z/<tent of id>           /device/:id/charts?<q> → 301 /z/<tent>/spur?<q>
/device/:id/settings    → /z/<tent>/ziele  (controller, fridge; kept verbatim for fan/light/plug/dryer)
```
Query format unchanged; `applyViewParams` keeps its back-compat branch for `measures`/`date`/`timespan`.
`ShareLink.page` stays `'charts' | 'diary'` — no enum change, no migration. `resolveDeviceAccessInfo`
(`locked`, `canEdit`, `webcamAllowed`, `chartsAllowed`) is evaluated per device and intersected across
`Tent.device_ids`.

### 3.3 The Dinge

Stored (all cloud-side Mongo, keyed by `tent_id`; `device.configuration` gains **no keys**):

```ts
Plant  { plant_id, tent_id, label, sort_index, started_at, ended_at?, stage,
         stage_since, strain_text?, medium_key?, regime_key?, pot_liters?,
         archived?, created_at, created_by_person? }

Mark   { mark_id, tent_id, plant_ids: string[],        // [] = das ganze Zelt
         kind: 'water'|'feed'|'both'|'train'|'observe'|'stage'|'harvest'|'note'|'photo',
         at, person_id?,
         water?: { cans: number; can_liters: number; liters: number; to_runoff?: boolean },
         nutrients?: { product_text, ml_per_l?, note_text?, from_regime: boolean }[],
         ph?, ec?, ec_basis?: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source', runoff_ph?, runoff_ec?,
         training?: 'top'|'lst'|'defoliate'|'transplant',
         note?, image_ids: string[], auto_image_id?,      // nearest kept frame within ±5 min
         client_mark_id,                                   // upsert key. an offline retry cannot double-log
         corrects_mark_id?,                                // corrections, never hard edits
         created_at, created_by_person? }

Note   { note_id, tent_id, plant_ids?, text, opened_by?, opened_at,
         closed_by?, closed_at? }                          // "Zettel" — a standing open fact

Person { person_id, tent_id, name, colour, created_at, disabled? }   // a label, not an account

TargetChange { change_id, tent_id, device_id, key, from, to, at,
               by_person_id?, source: 'app'|'device'|'unknown' }     // §6.2 — THE setpoint history

FrameMeta { image_id, tent_id, device_id, timestamp, verdict, reason?,
            lum, sharpness, dhash: Buffer, motion, light_pct?, green_frac?,
            tiles: number[] /* 48 = 8×6 */, dx, dy }

Spur   { spur_id, tent_id, subject: {kind, id}, from, to,
         kind: 'frames'|'series'|'duty'|'marks'|'targets',
         points: { t: number; image_id?: string }[],      // 24 B each. 1440 frames ≈ 35 KB.
         recipe_hash, built_at,
         render?: { path, bytes, duration_ms, expires_at } }   // §9.3 — the mp4 is NOT in Mongo
```

Projected read-time, never stored (C36's discipline): `zelt` from `Tent` + last Influx point ·
`dose` from `hardwareInfo.sockets`/`socket_ips` via the existing `parseSocketRoles()` · `kamera` from
`hardwareInfo.webcam_did` + newest `Image` · `bild`/`film` from `Image` · `ziel` from
`device.configuration` + `Alarm[]` + its `TargetChange` trace · `ereignis` from `DeviceLog`.

One read API, and the webapp uses exactly it, so it cannot drift:

```
GET /spur?tent_id=&subject=zelt|plant:<id>|socket:heater|measure:temperature|target:day.temperature
         &von=&bis=&aufloesung=punkt|paar|band&cursor=
```

`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` (`grow-report.component.ts:691-747`) stops being a runtime algorithm and
becomes a one-time idempotent boot backfill emitting `Plant` rows keyed by trimmed `lifecycleName`.
Old `DeviceLog` rows keep projecting; the seven legacy English-prose regexes keep running in the
read-time adapter. Nothing is deleted.

**Server-side cursor pagination on `/spur` is a v1 prerequisite.** The whole product is traces, and
`getDeviceLogs` is unbounded today.

### 4.2 The Körper is a function of (Ding, Zeitpunkt)

`renderBody(thing, t)`. That single signature is what makes the pair universal:

| Subjekt | Körper at one instant | Pair reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zelt`, camera paired | the kept frame + 3 value lines | two frames, wipe-comparable |
| `zelt`, no camera | four `value-display` gauges + Tag/Nacht | two columns of four numbers |
| `pflanze` | newest photo (Mark or auto-crop) + stage + Tag n | two photos of the same plant |
| `dose` | state + duty since midnight | `4 Std 20 an` / `3 Std 55 an · −25 Min` |
| `messwert` | the reading + target + delta | `24,1 → 25,0 °C · +0,9` |
| `ziel` | the value in force at t + who set it | `24,0 → 25,0 °C · Ben, gestern 19:04` |
| `mensch` | count of marks in window | `3 Gaben` / `1 Gabe` |
| `kamera` | frame + exposure/sharpness verdict | two frames + `Schärfe 0,71 → 0,42` |

There is one exception and it is deliberate: **when both handles sit on the same instant, the Körper is
rendered once, at full width, fully unfolded** (§5.2). That is the moment reading.

### 4.3 Der Δ-Knopf and the Wischvergleich
`Δ Unterschied` tints, on both frames, the 8×6 tiles whose change score exceeds that frame's own 90th
percentile, 35 % opacity in `--ion-color-warning`. It is a way of drawing the same two frames, at the
same level as pinch-zoom. Tapping either frame opens the **Wischvergleich**: both frames stacked with a
draggable vertical wipe handle, pinch to zoom (both zoom together), swipe down to dismiss.

### 4.4 Befund ranking — printed, deterministic, stable
`ⓘ nach Abweichung`. Numeric score = `|Δ| / max(σ₁₄, σ_floor)`, where σ₁₄ is that measure's standard
deviation over the last 14 days *at the same time of day ±1 h*, and `σ_floor` clamps per measure
(Temp 0,4 °C · rF 2 pp · CO₂ 40 ppm · VPD 0,05 kPa). Image findings score on tile change against the
same 14-day baseline. **A Befund that carries a remedy (§6.1) is promoted above every Befund that does
not**, because a fixable thing outranks a wobble. Human Marks inside the window always rank top-3. The
order is computed once per screen entry and does not move while you look at it.

`6 weitere Werte anzeigen` expands the same list in place, in the printed order. It reveals no feature
class; the hidden rows are the low-scoring ones *by construction*, and `○ gleich` rows are in there.

### 4.6 Wie es aussieht — the part sixty concepts skipped

**Type scale** (Inter, already `--ion-font-family`; five steps, nothing between them):

| Token | px / weight | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--tc-t-display` | 28 / 600, `tabular-nums` | the one number a moment reading is about |
| `--tc-t-title` | 20 / 600 | Ding name in the Kopf |
| `--tc-t-body` | 17 / 600 | Befund prose line, Zeile name |
| `--tc-t-value` | 14 / 500, `tabular-nums`, `--tc-text-*` | every value line, every age |
| `--tc-t-meta` | 12 / 500, letterspacing .02em, uppercase | section headers, the sort key |

**Every number in the product is `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.** A value that jitters in width
while a Kopf sweeps is the single most nauseating thing a scrubber can do.

**Rhythm:** a 4 px base grid. Card padding 16. Gap between sections 12. Zeile height 48 (44 px touch
target + 4). Spurband 44. Action row 56. Nothing in the product is at 10, 15 or 22 px.

**Colour** comes from the existing tokens — `--tc-text-danger/-warning/-success/-info` for status text on
a light surface, `--ion-color-*` only as fills. Two new tokens: `--tc-spur-kept: --ion-color-secondary`
and `--tc-spur-gap: --ion-color-step-300`.

**Die Spur, drawn exactly:** a 44 px rail, `--tc-radius-sm`, background `--tc-spur-gap`. Kept samples
are 3 px vertical ticks in `--tc-spur-kept`; culled frames are 1 px ticks at 30 % opacity; a gap wider
than 2× spacing is bare rail. Handles are 12 px wide, full height, with a 44 px invisible hit box and a
2 px vertical grab line. The Kopf is a 3 px line with a 10 px cap, `--ion-color-primary`. `prefers-
reduced-motion` stops the sweep and steps the Kopf once per second instead.

**The empty-state illustration** (there is exactly one in the whole product, and it is the same drawing
everywhere, at three sizes): **a single tick on an empty rail**, drawn in `--tc-spur-kept`, 1/3 from the
left, with the rail fading out to the right. Under it, one line in `--tc-t-value`:
`Deine Spur fängt hier an.` / EN: `Your trace starts here.` No hero, no cartoon plant, no onboarding card.

## 5. NO-MODES MECHANISM

### The falsification test
> **If you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of feature, it is a mode.**

`webapp/src/app/util/ui-mode.ts` (`EXPERT_MODE_STORAGE_KEY = 'app-settings-expert'`) is **deleted**, not
renamed, in the same commit as the shell. The key is removed from `localStorage` on first boot.

### M1 — The reading is handle geometry, and geometry is visible
You do not choose "moment / pair / film". You put two visible handles somewhere. The reading follows,
continuously, and you can see why. It is a physical continuum with detents, like the rotary knob on the
hardware.

- Nothing is persisted. No preference, no `localStorage`, no onboarding question. The cursor lives in
  memory for the session; on a fresh entry it is always `Von = seit du zuletzt geschaut hast`, `Bis = jetzt`.
- It is reversible by the identical gesture in under 200 ms.
- It hides no feature class. The moment reading and the pair reading contain **the same rows** — the pair
  shows each row twice with a delta; the moment shows it once with its target. Nobody is denied a number
  and nobody is forced to read one.

### M2 — One screen shape, and depth is subject-distance
Kopf · Körper · Spur · Verbindungen (`Im Zelt` / `Steht in` / `Ging an` / `Gilt für` / `Betrifft`) ·
Werte. A Ding is one 48 px Zeile when related to the Subjekt and fully unfolded when it *is* the
Subjekt. No expand control, no remembered expansion state. **Walking is stateless** — nothing the techie
does today changes what the stoned user sees tomorrow on the same login.

### M3 — `Werte` is the last row of every screen, for everybody
```
Werte  {…}                                                                [⎘]
GET /spur?tent_id=t_4b2e&subject=plant:p_a3&von=1755806400000&bis=1755892800000
```
Present from install, never hidden, never enabled, changes no other pixel. The techie discovers the API
by tapping it once, because the URL shown is the URL that works. Everyone else scrolls past it.

### M4 — Capability and data decide what exists
Per-role capability from `hardwareInfo.sockets` via `parseSocketRoles()`, **failing closed** to
monitor-only when the key is absent, with one honest Zeile: `Gerät meldet keine Steckdosen — Firmware zu
alt.` This kills the live bug where `deviceControlCapability()` (`grow-presets.ts:143-173`) returns
`'full'` for a heater-only tent and offers it humidity targets it can never act on. Zero `Plant` rows ⇒
„Pflanze" appears in exactly one place: the `+` on `Im Zelt`. One `Person` ⇒ no `Wer?` row anywhere.
Neither is reversible by a setting.

### Honest audit of my own design

| Suspect | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Two handles vs one | **The closest call, and I expect the push here.** A critic can read „Griffe zusammen" as simple mode and „Griffe auseinander" as expert mode. Defence: both handles are always rendered, always draggable, and neither can be removed or defaulted away; the collapsed state is a *position*, not a setting; and both readings contain the same rows. It survives the falsification test because no feature class is behind either position. |
| `6 weitere Werte anzeigen` | **Not a mode.** Expands the same list in place, in the printed sort order. |
| `Δ Unterschied` | **Not a mode.** A way of drawing one image, same level as pinch-zoom. |
| The Schnitt sheet's four toggles (§9.4) | **Second-closest.** They are parameters of an artefact being produced, like an export resolution. They change nothing about the app's interface. Mitigated only by defaults that are right for everyone. |
| Pause-to-unfold | **Not a mode** — self-cancelling, unpersisted, gesture-borrowed from every video player on earth. |

Nothing in this concept keys off "who you are", a stored preference, or an onboarding answer.

## 6. Sagen, was zu tun ist — die Handlung am Befund

**The rule: `Jeder Befund endet in einem Knopf oder in einem Grund.`** Every Befund either carries an
action that changes something, or carries one sentence saying why nothing can. No AI, no advisor, no
probability. Deterministic rules over data the app already holds, evaluated on the *pair*, which is why
this concept can do it and C14 could not: **a prescription needs a baseline, and the pair is a baseline.**

### 6.1 The rules (all evaluated over `[Von, Bis]`; all rendered as a third line + one button)

| # | Condition | Text | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | a `TargetChange` for measure *m* exists in the window **and** *m*'s mean moved in the same direction | `Ben hat gestern 19:04 das Nachtziel von 19,0 auf 20,0 °C gestellt. Genau das siehst du.` | `[ Zurück auf 19,0 °C ]` — writes the **existing** key `night.temperature` |

Constraint compliance: **every write action writes an existing `device.configuration` key** —
`day.temperature`, `night.temperature`, `day.humidity`, `night.humidity`, `lights.limit`,
`daynight.day` / `.night`, `co2.*`. No new keys, so the firmware strips nothing. Pushes go out through
the existing settings path (hourly at most, only to a device seen in the last 60 s) and the button
becomes `Wird gesendet…` until the device echoes the value back.

### 6.2 Setpoint history — the thing that makes R1 and the chart possible

Today the chart draws today's target over last month's data, because no setpoint history is recorded.
`TargetChange` fixes it for **~1.5 days of work**:

1. The settings-push service already reads `device.configuration` before writing. Diff old vs new,
   emit one `TargetChange` per changed key with `source:'app'` and the acting `person_id`.
2. A daily ticker snapshots each device's configuration and diffs it against the last snapshot, so
   changes made on the device's own rotary knob land as `source:'device'`.
3. Before the first snapshot there is no history, and the chart says so rather than lying:
   `Sollwert erst ab 12.09. bekannt` and the line is drawn dotted-grey before that date.

A `ziel` Ding's Spur **is** its `TargetChange` list. The chart's dashed Sollwert line is that trace
rendered on the axis. Same object, two resolutions — the point of the whole concept.

## 8. Tagebuch und mehrere Pflanzen

**There is no diary feature.** The tent's Spur is the tent's diary; a plant's Spur is that plant's
diary; they are the same renderer. What „es macht das Tagebuchführen für dich" means operationally:
frames, films, alarms, device events and **target changes** are written by the machine into the same
trace the human writes Marks into. A user who logs nothing still opens a full diary.

**Every Mark gets its picture for free.** On write, the server sets `Mark.auto_image_id` to the nearest
*kept* frame within ±5 min. One Mongo lookup, zero user effort, and it turns a text log into a visual
record — and it is what makes the double-feed guard's evidence (§9.3) possible.

**The plant-ignorer is untaxed.** `Mark.plant_ids: []` means the whole tent and is the default forever.
The plant UI does not exist until a plant exists — no empty state, no „0 Pflanzen" card, no nag. The
only door is `+` on the `Im Zelt` header. Create one and plant chips appear in the Gabe sheet, in Befund
rows and in `Bisher`; create none and nothing in the product mentions plants.

`DiaryLifecycleStage` gains a seventh value, `ernte` — harvest is not a stage today, so „wie lief der
Durchgang" is unanswerable. Optional field, read-time normaliser, no migration tool.

### 9.3 The double-feed guard — and why this concept's version is better

At sheet open, query `Mark` of kind ∈ {water, feed, both} touching any selected plant (or the tent)
inside the window: **water 6 h, feed 18 h; coco 3 h / 12 h** (several daily feeds are correct there),
owner-editable. If found, the warning renders and the primary button **relabels** to
`Trotzdem eintragen`, so muscle memory cannot fire it.

`[ Vorher/Jetzt ]` sets `Von` to Tom's mark and `Bis` to now and shows **the pair** — Tom's
`auto_image_id` frame beside the current one. You can see whether the soil is still dark. C14 could show
you one picture; the trace primitive gives the guard a before/after for free, and that is the club
feature this concept contributes.

**Double-*logging*** is handled by `client_mark_id`: a UUID minted in the browser at sheet open; the
server upserts on `{tent_id, client_mark_id}`, so a replayed offline queue is idempotent **by
construction** — a retry cannot produce the one thing indistinguishable from a real double feed.

## 10. Kamera, Zeitraffer, Bildkorrelation — und DER REGLER

### 10.1 What exists, and the half-day fix
Verified in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`: ffmpeg is installed and invoked via `execFile`,
`sharp` 0.34.5 is a dependency, a frame lands every 30 s, rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses are built
hourly at 25 fps with `libx265 -crf 30`, and thinning tiers plus a 30-day URL-embeddable `?token=` with
`?width=` resizing already exist. **No new binary, no ML runtime.**

**Day-one fix, half a day, highest value per line in this document:** `libx265` → `libx264 -profile:v
high -level 4.0`, add `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, add `-movflags +faststart`. HEVC-in-mp4 does not play in
Firefox anywhere or in Chrome on Linux/most Windows — today's timelapse is invisible to half the people
who paid for the camera.

### 10.2 The cull — per frame, at ingest, `sharp` + plain JS, ≤ 60 ms
`FrameMeta` (§3.3) never carries a Buffer, so scoring queries stay cheap (~200 B × 2 880/day =
576 KB/device/day, thinned alongside the images). Verdicts in order, thresholds in a tunable Mongo
`filmProfile` doc:

1. `maint` — maintenance mode or `workmode==='off'`. Rendered as a gap, never silently skipped.
2. `dark` — `light_pct < 5` **and** `lum < 25`. Two conditions, so a grower whose lamp is not on our
   socket does not lose the whole film. **`light_pct` is the trick: we own the lamp.** Everyone else has
   to infer whether the light was on; we read it off the MQTT status at the moment of capture.
3. `flash` — `lum` deviates > 3,5 × the rolling MAD of the last 60 kept frames (door open, torch).
4. `human` — a *bounded burst*: frames n…n+k each differ from n−1 by `motion > 18`, n+k+1 by `< 6` again.
   Runs over `FrameMeta` alone, no pixels touched. **Plus, free: any frame within ±3 min of a Mark is
   dropped, because a human told us they were standing there.** Nobody else has the diary and the camera
   on one clock.
5. `blur` — `sharpness < 0,35 ×` the 24 h rolling median → `Kamera unscharf – vermutlich beschlagen.`
6. `dupe` — `Hamming(dhash, last kept) < 4`, never if it would open a gap wider than 2 × spacing.
7. else `keep`.

`tiles[48]` (8×6 mean absolute difference vs the last kept frame) is the change map behind
`Δ Unterschied`. It says **where**, never **what**. `green_frac` is labelled `Blattfläche (Bildanteil)` —
never cm, never „Wachstum" as a claim. **Refused as computer-vision fantasy:** naming a drooping leaf,
diagnosing a deficiency from colour, measuring height, counting plants, detecting pests.

### 10.3 THE SLIDER — what it scrubs, what moves with it, what pause unfolds

**What it scrubs: the Spur of whatever Ding is the Subjekt** — not „the film". The film is only what a
tent's trace looks like when the tent has a camera. On a Pflanze the Kopf moves through that plant's
photos and Marks; on a Dose through duty state changes; on a Messwert through raw samples; on a Ziel
through its `TargetChange` rows (the setpoint stepping through its own history); on a Mensch through
that person's Marks; on a camera-less Zelt through 5-min samples, and playing looks like four gauges
sweeping while a Tag/Nacht band fills in behind them.

**One cursor, every projection.** `SpurCursor` is a `BehaviorSubject<{von, bis, kopf, spielt}>` scoped to
the tent and held in memory (never `localStorage` — it must not survive a session). Everything on screen
renders from it: the Körper, the Spurband, the chart lanes, the film strip, the annotation rail, the
scrub header. Drag any one, all move. Tapping a filmstrip tile moves the Kopf; the Befund
`Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` moves the Kopf to the next excursion. Correlation runs both directions.

**A pinned scrub header, never a tooltip.** `24,1 °C  Ziel 24,0  ⌀ 23,6  min 18,1  ● 14:02`. There is no
hover on a phone.

**Pause is the disclosure gesture.** Tap the body while the Kopf is running: it stops, and the body
unfolds downward into everything known about that minute.

```
        [ Bild · 20.08. 13:24 ]
  ╭────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  │ 20.08.2026 13:24 · Tag 34 · Blüte          │
  │ Temperatur   25,4 °C   Ziel 25,0    +0,4 › │  ← tap a row: that Messwert becomes the
  │ Luftfeuchte  58 %      Ziel 50 %    +8   › │     Subjekt, same window
  │ VPD          1,21 kPa  Band 1,2–1,5   ✓  › │
  │ CO₂          940 ppm   Ziel 1000    −60  › │

  │ Marken ±2 Std: Gießen 6,0 l (Anna, 12:05)  │
  │ Bild: belichtet ×1,04 · Schärfe 0,71 ·     │
  │       behalten                             │
  ╰────────────────────────────────────────────╯
```

Nothing is persisted, it self-cancels on ▶, and it hides no feature class — it is *the data about the
frame you are looking at*. And because a Ding's body is a function of a timestamp, **the same gesture
works on every Ding**: pausing on a socket's Spur unfolds why it was running at that minute (measured vs
setpoint); pausing on a plant's Spur unfolds that day's Marks. One gesture, learned once.

**Snap ladder** on the Von handle, 44 px magnetic zones: `seit zuletzt` (from
`localStorage['tc-lastseen-<tent_id>']`, written on blur) · `gestern` · `1 Woche` · `Phasenwechsel` ·
`letzte Gabe` · `Umtopfen` · `Beginn`. A fat thumb at 2 am cannot miss `gestern`; a deliberate finger
between two snaps lands on any minute, and the exact timestamp is printed under the rail as you drag.
One control that is coarse and precise in the same gesture — a rotary knob with detents.

The cull report is not a debug panel. It is the single thing that explains why this film is watchable
when the competitor's is not.

**HOW THE FILM IS STORED — the honest answer, and it falls out of the primitive:**

> **The film is not stored. The Spur is stored, and the mp4 is a disposable render of it.**

A `Spur` document holds an ordered list of `{t, image_id}` — 24 bytes per point, so a 1 440-frame
twelve-week film is **~35 KB of ids** in a normal Mongo document. It is durable, it is the thing the app
scrubs (client-side, from sprite tiles), and it never approaches the 16 MB BSON limit. The mp4 exists
only so a file can leave the product: rendered by a `pLimit(2)` worker to a `RENDER_DIR` docker volume,
streamed with Range support, `Spur.render.expires_at` at 30 days unless the user ticks `behalten`.
**The mp4 never enters Mongo** — the current `Image{format:'mp4'}` Buffer path is the latent 16 MB bug,
and this concept stops feeding it. (If ops refuses a volume, GridFS is the alternative and costs ~2 days;
this concept picks the volume and says so rather than leaving it open.)

Normalisation before encode, all `sharp`: **deflicker** — target luminance `L*` = median `lum` of kept
frames, `.linear(a,b)` with `a = clamp(L*/lum, 0.75, 1.35)` and the *change* in `a` clamped to ±3 % per
frame; three lines, and it removes the strobing that makes every DIY tent timelapse unwatchable.
**White balance per lighting regime**, not per frame (a regime = a contiguous run sharing a `light_pct`
bucket 0 / 1–49 / 50–99 / 100), gains clamped to [0,6 · 1,8] so a purple grow still looks purple.
**Stabilisation, translation only**, ±6 px integer search with a 5-frame median filter; a sustained
shift beyond the inset inserts a hard cut and a title card `Kamera neu ausgerichtet · Tag 12`, because a
film problem deserves a film-language answer. **Burn-in** reuses the already unit-tested
`imageService.addOfflineOverlay` SVG composite: `Tag 34 · Blüte · 25,4 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21`.

```sh
ffmpeg -y -framerate 24 -f image2 -i "$DIR/%05d.jpg" \
  -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -preset medium -crf 21 \
  -g 48 -movflags +faststart -metadata title="Terp Control – Zelt Keller · Tag 12–40" "$DIR/out.mp4"
```

**Three routes off the phone in v1, and the UI says which one you get:** (1)
`navigator.share({files:[new File([blob], name, {type:'video/mp4'})]})`, guarded by
`navigator.canShare?.({files})`, capped at 50 MB — on iOS 15+ the sheet's **„Video sichern"** puts it
straight in Fotos and also offers WhatsApp/Telegram/Signal; (2) `GET /film/:spur_id.mp4?token=`, with
`Content-Disposition: attachment`, real `Accept-Ranges: bytes` handling and an ASCII-folded filename —
`terpcontrol_zelt-keller_tag12-tag40_2026-10-14_720p.mp4`, because in a camera roll the filename is the
only label; (3) that same `?token=` URL, pasteable, playing for 30 days with no login — **a share that
produces a login wall is not a share.** Capacitor native write to Fotos is later, not v1;
`capacitor.config.ts` is untouched scaffold today.

**Cost, honestly:** 1 440 frames × ~25 ms sharp + ~15 s x264 ≈ 90 s of one core per film. `FrameMeta` is
the real cost: 150 devices × 2 880 captures/day × ~15 ms ≈ **1,8 CPU-hours/day**, fine on the current
single pm2 box, needs a real queue well before 5 000 devices.

### 10.5 The film strip is delivered as one sprite, not 100 requests
`GET /spur/strip?tent_id&von&bis&cols=10` → one JPEG of up to 100 tiles at 160×90 plus
`[{t,x,y}]`, cached with a 24 h TTL index. This is what makes scrubbing feel instant over tent wifi, and
it is why the Spurband can be dense on the home screen instead of decorative.

### 10.6 Camera alarms — the thing nobody built
Two, created by default the moment a camera is paired, cloud-side, no firmware. They reuse `Alarm`
storage and `handleAlarmAction` (e-mail / webhook / device log) plus the new push channel (§14), but
need their own ~80-line evaluator ticker because `alarm.service` only evaluates on inbound MQTT.

| `sensorType` | Fires when | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `webcam_silence` | no `format:'jpeg'` frame for > 3 h | „Zelt Keller: Die Kamera hat seit 3 Std kein Bild geliefert." |
| `light_mismatch` | `out_light > 50 %` for 15 min while frame brightness sits in the lights-off band, or the inverse | „Zelt Keller: Das Licht sollte an sein, das Bild ist dunkel." |

`light_mismatch` is the strongest demonstration of the whole thesis: **neither the picture nor the data
can detect a failed contactor alone, and two traces on one clock detect it in fifteen minutes.**

## 11. Clubs — attribution without an auth rewrite

- `Person` is a tent-scoped list of names the owner types once in `Zelt → Wer ist am Zelt`. No accounts,
  no e-mail, **no `auth.middleware.ts` surgery**; ~2 days. Every Mark stamps `person_id`; the last used
  in this browser is prefilled (`localStorage`), so the 2-tap path survives. Each person gets a colour
  used consistently in the diary, the annotation rail and the guard.
- **Said once, under the list:** `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann
  jeden Namen wählen.` Anything else would be a lie about a record people rely on.
- **The club feature *is* the second name.** One `Person` → no `Wer?` row, no attribution column, no
  cross-person guard. Two → all of it. That is how a club exists without a club mode.
- **Corrections, never hard edits.** Editing a Mark writes a new Mark with `corrects_mark_id`; the old
  Zeile renders struck through and stays on the Spur.
- **Zettel** (`Note`) is the standing open fact — opened from `📌 Zettel`, closed by anyone with `✓`
  stamping `closed_by`/`closed_at`, rendered as a slim amber strip **above the Körper**, the only element
  that outranks the picture, correctly, because a human wrote it for another human.
- **Upgrade path designed in:** a later real `Membership` sets `Person.user_id` and every historic row
  keeps resolving. Real memberships (rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207`, loosening ~10
  owner-scoped queries) are **not v1**, and the release notes say so.

**Privacy posture — privacy-maximal by default.** `Mark{kind:'harvest'}.weight_g` and plant counts are
**never prompted**; a per-tent `Ernte erfassen` switch, off by default, adds the field, and off means the
field does not exist. No location, no real-name enforcement, no analytics SDK, no community feed.
`Alles löschen` actually deletes `Image`, `FrameMeta`, `Spur`, renders, `Mark`, `Plant`, `Note`,
`DeviceLog` and the Influx series — which also fixes the standing lie where `DELETE /device/logs/:id`
removes nothing from the diary because `deleted` is a visibility flag (`device.service.ts:739`).
Retention: raw frames 90 days, metrics with the frames, renders 30 days, Spuren until deleted.
**Refused outright:** per-member dispensing, grams handed over, THC content, member identity, date of
birth. § 26 KCanG is the club's system of record, not ours. No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.

## 12. Alte Geräte (Typ 7) — fan, light, plug

A `fan` / `light` / `plug` is a one-device Tent with a thin body. **Same screen, minus what the hardware
does not have. Nothing stubbed, disabled or greyed.**

- **The pair still works, and that is the point.** With no camera the Körper is the device's own
  numbers, so a plug from 2021 gets `Vorher / Jetzt` on day one:
  `plug` → `▲ Länger an — 4 Std 20 statt 3 Std 55 (+25 Min)` · `light` → `▲ Das Licht lief 25 Min länger
  — 12 Std 25 statt 12 Std 00 (Plan)` and `● Dimmung geändert — 80 % → 100 %, gestern 09:12` (a
  `TargetChange`, so R1 applies and the remedy is `[ Zurück auf 80 % ]`) · `fan` → `● Drehzahl höher —
  ⌀ 1 840 → 2 210 U/min` · all types → `▲ Gerät war 2 Std 10 offline — von 03:12 bis 05:22`.
- **The Spurband is value ticks instead of frame ticks.** Same 44 px, same two handles, same Kopf, same
  snap ladder minus `Phasenwechsel` and `Umtopfen` (no lifecycle rows exist).
- **Export is a PNG, not an mp4** — `Als Bild speichern` renders the Spurband plus the pair. Der Schnitt
  does not exist, because there are no frames. Reveal by capability, not a disabled button.
- **`Ziele` renders the existing `fan-settings` / `light-settings` / `plug-settings` components through
  the existing `ngSwitch` on `device_type`, unmodified**, and `/device/:id/settings` survives verbatim for
  these four types. Zero settings regression. (`dryer` stays dead per AGENTS.md; `fridge` keeps its
  settings component and gains the camera-capable body like a controller.)
- **Nothing about plants, regimes, people or schemes appears** until the user creates one — for a plug
  owner, never. `diary-fridge-log` and the other legacy categories keep rendering through the read-time
  adapter, including the seven legacy English-prose regexes.
- **Non-breakage checklist:** `/charts` and `/diary` redirect with the query string intact ·
  `ShareLink.page` unchanged · `ChartPreset.query` still parses · `applyViewParams` keeps its
  back-compat branch · the three live i18n bugs in plug settings
  (`settings.limits.overtemperature.enabled`, `.undertemperature.enabled`, `.time.enabled`) are fixed in
  the same pass, two lines each.
- What they **gain**: notes, photos, Zettel, honest data ages, a chart with axis labels, and the first
  genuinely new thing their three-year-old plug has ever told them.

## 13. Tag eins und die ersten 48 Stunden

Most concepts look broken on day one because they render an empty version of a full screen. **A Spur that
is 40 minutes long is not an empty screen — it is a short one, and it visibly grows.** That is the entire
day-one design, and it is free.

| Time | What the home screen is |
| --- | --- |
| **t+0 min**, device claimed, first sample | Handles pinned together. The Körper renders **once, full width, fully unfolded** — the moment reading, which is the same panel §10.3 shows on pause. Spurband: **one tick**, the empty-state drawing (§4.6), `Deine Spur fängt hier an.` One Befund: `● Erste Messung — 24,1 °C · 57 % rF · noch kein Ziel gesetzt` with `[ Ziele einrichten ]`. |
| **t+15 min** | Four ticks. Nothing else changes. Nobody is asked anything. |
| **t+2 h** | The Von handle unpins to `Beginn` and **the first pair appears**: two moments 2 h apart. The difference sentence is honest and boring: `Seit dem Start: 4 Werte, nichts Auffälliges.` This is the first time the product does its trick, and it happens on the first evening. |
| **t+24 h** | The `gestern` snap becomes reachable. Tag/Nacht appears on the Spurband if `out_light` moved. If a camera is paired, the film has ~2 800 frames and `Der Schnitt` offers `Heute` — **a watchable 12-second film exists on day one**, and it is the demo that sells the hardware. |
| **t+48 h** | `seit zuletzt` becomes the default Von, because there now *is* a previous session. The snap ladder is at its full length except lifecycle anchors. |

Deliberate on day one: **no wizard, no onboarding question, no „what kind of grower are you", no sample
data, no fake plant.** Three things are offered and each is one line in `Was ist anders`, dismissible by
being scrolled past, never a card: `[ Ziele einrichten ]` (t+0), `[ Kamera einrichten ]` (only if
`hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is absent and the device reports RTSP capability), `[ Substrat & Dünger wählen ]`
(first time a `feed` Mark is written without a regime — i.e. only for someone who already feeds).

`./simulate-device.sh setup` + `history --days 30` is the verification harness for all five rows, plus
`run --camera` for the t+24 h film and a forced offline state.

## 15. Ehrliche Schwächen

1. **The two-handle slider is a learned gesture, and I am asking a stoned user at 2 am to learn it.**
   Pinch-to-collapse is the least discoverable thing here. Both handles always visible, the snap ladder
   as text, and a default window that is right 90 % of the time are mitigations, not a defence. If the
   gesture fails usability testing, the concept degrades to C14 with a scrubber — worse than either source.
2. **„Everything is a trace" flattens importance**, exactly as C36's uniformity did. A socket's duty and
   a night excursion get the same 44 px rail; all the ranking lives in one Befund list and one Zettel
   strip, and that is thin.
3. **Most days, nothing changed.** A home screen whose honest answer is usually `○ Nichts Auffälliges` is
   the opposite of a competitor's dashboard, which always looks busy while telling you less.
4. **The remedies are only as good as `TargetChange`, and it starts empty.** R1 — the best rule here, the
   one that actually says what to do — cannot fire until somebody changes a target *inside the app* after
   this ships. For the first weeks the app is back to describing.
5. **R2/R3's honest refusals will read as the app giving up.** „Hier hilft nur mehr Hardware" is true,
   correct, and exactly the sentence a user screenshots into a bad review.
6. **Playback of a non-camera trace may be pointless.** Four gauges sweeping across 30 days is a
   plausible-sounding animation I cannot prove anyone wants to watch. If it is dead weight, the ▶ button
   is dead weight on the majority of tents and the slider loses a third of its justification.
7. **`green_frac` is a weak proxy for growth, and saying so does not make it stronger.** It confounds
   growth with leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift; under magenta LED it is near-noise.
8. **The cull has a 60-second blind spot in both directions and its errors are unobservable.** A
   90-second tent visit survives; a genuinely fast real change is dropped. The user never sees what was
   removed except as a count — the worst kind of error in a feature whose pitch is honesty.
9. **`RENDER_DIR` is an ops dependency this project does not have**: a volume, a disk budget, a TTL
   sweeper, a backup story. GridFS avoids it at ~2 days and a slower stream; I picked the volume and a
   sysadmin may overrule me. Related: the tent migration is one-way in practice — merge the wrong two
   devices and v1 has no clean unmerge.
10. **`/spur` is one endpoint with no second screen to fall back on.** One request over twelve weeks
    touches `Spur`, `Mark`, `DeviceLog`, `Image` metadata and Influx; if it is slow, the whole product is
    slow. And this is still not a control surface: §6's remedies pull the commonest adjustments onto the
    home screen as one-tap buttons — a real gain over C14 — but a grower changing four values still walks
    four times, and my mitigation (`Ziele` renders all targets as inline `value-edit-row`s) is a partial
    climb-down toward a settings page, which a critic will correctly call it.
