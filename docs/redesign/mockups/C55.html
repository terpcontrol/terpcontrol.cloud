C55 · Nachbarschaft

Nachbarschaft — jeder Unterschied hat einen Nachbarn, dem er gehört

Every difference has a neighbour that owns it, and the app's only navigation is walking to that neighbour with the cursor still in your hand.

The whole product is one screen shape and one cursor. Screens 1, 2, 3 and 6 are the same component — the difference list, the Spur and the lanes never move; only the data and the top card change. The remedy on screen 2 is not advice bolted onto a chart but the last link of a chain that ends on Ben, vor 3 Tg 19:04, next to the button that changes exactly that value. „weil" is a reserved word: it is printed only where a stored edge with a proof exists (solid ├ └ connectors in amber), and coincidence is printed dotted ┊ under GLEICHZEITIG with no verb and no arrow — a watering, a picture that got darker and a lamp switching on are neighbours, never causes. Screen 6 pays that rule's price twice: the offline row gets no Warum? button at all, and the sentence „Offline": dafür kennt die App keinen Grund ships. A routine watering is 2 Taps — Gabe then Trotzdem eintragen, the guard costing nothing extra — and two cans instead of the prefilled three is 3 Taps; litres are derived from the tap count (3 Kannen · Kanne 2,0 l · 6,0 l are all stored), so there is no keyboard in a dark tent, and Bild ansehen turns the double-feed warning into evidence by opening Anna's frame from 1 Std 30 ago. Before Woche 3 the same sheet prefills no products at all and prints „All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen"; a scheme that cannot say don't feed yet is not modelling the domain. Staleness is never hidden and never merged: ● vor 41 Sek (device), Bild 08:24 (frame), von 14:20 (last contact) and Offline are four different clocks, and a stale number is greyed, never blanked.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • „Warum?" walks one edge and keeps the cursor. Every other concept answers "why was it warm at 03:12" by showing you the heater now — the answer to a different question. Here the neighbour's screen opens at the same instant, the time is in the breadcrumb and in the URL, so a chain is a link one club member can paste to another.
  • Causation has a visible confidence boundary. Three edge classes (steuert, wirkt_auf, geändert_von) may print weil; a Gabe, a picture and any time-series correlation may never. Two typographic registers, both always on screen, no "show reasoning" control.
  • The setpoint on the chart is historical, not current. configureDevice already logs every change with a diff; storing it structured (two lines) yields the stepped dashed Sollwertspur, the geändert_von edge and an attributable diary entry from one change.
  • One lane renderer, camera or no camera. The große Bahn is a Bildband when a camera is paired and a Wertband when it is not — same slot, same cursor, same code path. C14 needed two home screens for this; screens 3 and 6 are the same component as screen 5.
  • A remedy has an address. Nine deterministic rules live in a Mongo collection, render at the end of their own chain and at the top of the difference list, print their rule id in Werte, and each button writes a config key that already exists.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers the question the grower actually asks. C14 names the difference, C36 lets you browse it; only this one answers warum — and it does so from stored records rather than inference, so the answer is checkable.
  • Day one is designed, not survived. SEIT DEM ANSCHLIESSEN is built from real timestamps and a clock, deletes itself when the last row ticks, and no delta is ever fabricated against a synthetic zero. Most concepts render an empty grid here.
  • Old plugs and lamps gain something new. A three-year-old light with one sensor gets a real difference list, and its ziel rows carry geändert_von — the first time such a device has ever explained itself. Its settings page stays byte-identical.
  • The shared cursor makes the camera worth its price. Bildband and curves are one drawing with one playhead; pause names what was true at that minute, including which sockets ran and what the target was then. Sprite-based thumbnails, not 60 requests.
  • Attribution without an auth rewrite. A tent-scoped list of names, stated once as a note rather than a login — and config changes become attributable for free, which answers the club's second-most expensive question after "who already fed".
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Most days nothing has a weil. Only measures the controller actually steers produce hard edges. On a monitoring-only tent — probably the largest group — the Warum? button almost never renders and the headline mechanism is invisible. Those users get C14 and C36 stapled together, which is a good product but not this one.
  • steuert is a model of the firmware, not a reading of it. The device never tells us why it switched a socket; we reconstruct it. out_dehumidifier already doubles as a cooler in temp and breed — proof the model is lossy — so we will eventually print a confident weil that is subtly wrong. That is the most dangerous failure here, because the whole design promises the word is earned.
  • Users will read Gleichzeitig as causation anyway. A dotted line and a missing verb are a typographic defence against a cognitive habit. I can refuse to say it; I cannot stop it being read.
  • The Spur eats the phone. Große Bahn plus lanes plus a pinned scrub header is over a third of the viewport before any prose — so on a small phone the difference list, which C14 says is the answer, starts below the fold. Screens 1 and 2 are already at their limit.
  • Carrying the cursor across a walk is unusual, and unusual costs. A user who taps Warum? expecting "how is my heater" and gets "how was my heater at 03:12" has been surprised by the cleverest idea in the concept. The breadcrumb prints the time; it does not remove the surprise.
  • One graph, total blast radius, and a migration paid in v1. A single request touches dinge, kanten, DeviceLog, image metadata and Influx; if it is slow the whole product is slow and there is no second screen to fall back to. Tent is the right model but one-tent-per-device makes its benefit zero until somebody merges two devices, which most never will.
