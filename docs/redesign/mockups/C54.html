C54 · Der Befund

Der Befund — ein Befund ist ein Ding, in das man hineinlaufen kann

The primary object is a Befund: a named finding with a time span, a body of evidence, and named German relations — and a Befund is a Ding like every other Ding, so you walk from the finding to its proof, and from any thing back to the findings that cite it.

A watering is 2 Taps — Gabe → Eintragen, or Gabe → Trotzdem eintragen when the double-feed guard fires, which is the same two taps with the warning read in between. Changing 2 l to 6 l is 4 Taps (two more on the can counter, screen 4), a subset of plants is 5 Taps, and walking straight from the Fütterung fällig Befund into a prefilled sheet is 3 Taps. Litres are derived from the counter and displayed, never typed — no keyboard in a dark cellar. Screen 5 is the load-bearing one: the filmstrip, the temperature plot and the humidity plot are a single SVG with one vertical playhead at 03:41, so the picture, the excursion and the humidity lag are read off the same x. The screens show three different data ages honestly — ● vor 14 Sek for a live device, Bild vor 4 Std for a frame that is much older than the reading beside it, and Offline with Wert eingefroren · 05:22 on the 2023 lamp.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • A finding is a place, not a row. C14's ranked list was five dead ends; here the rank-1 Befund is the home screen, rendered by the same Tafel renderer as every other Ding, with Belege you walk into and a reverse edge (which findings cite me) on the socket, the target and the plant.
  • Abhilfe: remedies as walks, never as prose. Every row is a link to something inspectable and is precondition-gated — Nachtziel anheben 20,0 → 20,5 renders because a target exists, and where a role is missing the row is replaced by a stated limit: Abluft hängt an keiner Dose — nicht schaltbar. The product still refuses to diagnose.
  • Targets are versioned Dinge, so the chart draws the target that actually applied. The dashed setpoint steps at daynight and at every historical change; the pause panel can print Ziel galt seit 12.08., gesetzt von Ben instead of holding today's config against last month's data.
  • The empty state is a finding with severity 0. Ruhig seit 3 Tagen · 16 Regeln geprüft with a Ruhestreifen drawn from the same numbers the Belege list — proof of work, and something that changes daily on a screen whose honest content does not.
  • One cursor, four projections. Drag the strip and the frame, the value line and the chart cursor all move; tap a Beleg row and the cursor jumps to that evidence's timestamp. Rows never reorder while the thumb is down.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers "so what do I do" without pretending to know. The weakest score across the round was actionability; a gated list of walks is the only answer here that adds it without adding advice the hardware cannot back up.
  • Day one is designed, not survived. A different rule set produces real findings from configuration and hardwareInfo — no night target, no camera, no substrate — which resolve themselves and vanish. No wizard, no illustration, no fabricated delta against a synthetic zero.
  • Degrades to a 2023 plug without stubs. The same screen shape, the same Schieber drawn as value bars, and the Befunde are computed from numbers alone. The lamp owner learns something his device never told him: it ran 25 minutes longer than the plan.
  • Club history becomes causal, not just chronological. quittiert von Ben and Ziel geändert wegen Befund „…" answer "why was the night target raised" — a question no other concept in the round can answer at all, and it costs no auth rewrite.
  • The camera earns its premium twice. Once as the pair, once as the correlation the picture and the numbers can only do together — a lamp reporting 100 % against a frame sitting in the lights-off brightness band is a failed contactor, and neither signal finds it alone.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • The remedy catalogue is the whole bet, and a wrong remedy is worse than none. Nachtziel anheben is right for an oversized heater and wrong for a tent in a hot loft. Gating stops nonsense rows rendering; it does not make the remaining rows correct. This concept made advice navigable, not correct.
  • Four screens here are one screen. Screens 1, 2, 3 and 6 are the same component fed different data — elegant to build, and a real risk that the owner reads the whole set as "it always looks like a list of problems". 4 Befunde reads as 4 Probleme even when three are ○ ruhig, and nobody knows where the give-up threshold sits.
  • No tab bar means the film is two taps away. C36's discipline is inherited whole, including its cost: the camera buyer's favourite artefact has no permanent home in the chrome, and the only route to it is through a Befund or the Zelt's Im Zelt list.
  • The σ normaliser hides real excursions in a noisy tent, and σ_floor is a visible fudge the moment two tents are compared. Week one is thin by construction, while a competitor's busy dashboard still feels more valuable on day two while telling you less.
  • It is a looking surface, not a steering surface. Changing a night target is three taps where today's settings form is two, and still_bis (snooze) is the closest thing to a mode in a concept that claims to have none — instance-scoped and printed on the row, but a hostile reviewer can fairly call it a hiding control.
  • The rule engine is a real server cost. 16 rules on a 5-minute ticker per tent, on top of per-frame image metrics and the ffmpeg queue, against one process — the dedupe cache is the only thing between this and a melted box.
