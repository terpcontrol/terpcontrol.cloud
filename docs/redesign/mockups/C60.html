C60 · Der Zeuge

Der Zeuge — keine Aussage ohne Beleg

Nothing in Terp Control is asserted without its witness attached: every sentence, every number, every proposed remedy carries a Beleg — a named relation you can walk into — and standing on that Beleg you find a pair of pictures, a raw sample window, or the output that actually ran.

Watering is 2 Taps — Gabe then Eintragen; the counter opens at the previous Gabe's count, so the routine path touches nothing. One can more is 3 Taps, two named plants instead of the whole tent 5 Taps. When the double-feed guard fires the primary button relabels to Trotzdem eintragen (still 2 taps — the cost is deliberation, not taps) and Belegbild opens the frame auto-attached to Anna's entry, so you can see whether the soil is dark. Nothing doses; every Gabe is typed by a human.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • Eine Aussage ohne Beleg wird nicht angezeigt. Every sentence carries a muted Beleg line naming what is on the other end — 2 Bilder, 41 Werte, 14 Fenster — and tapping it walks you there. An unwitnessed claim is absent, not greyed.
  • The cursor is a span, not a point. Two handles, ◖ Vorher and ◗ Jetzt, drive every projection at once: the picture pair, the difference sentences, the chart window, the output lanes. Screen 5 draws the same vertical at the same x in the strip and the chart, because it is literally one value.
  • Remedies the app can write itself. Ten published deterministic rules over data already held. Each prints its trigger, its arithmetic and its Belege, and Übernehmen writes a ziel Ding — which is why setpoint history exists from day one and the chart stops drawing today's target over last month's data.
  • Day one has a clock on it, not an illustration. „Erstes Vorher ab 14:34", a 12-minute trace against an explicitly angenommenes target, and the setup work delivered by the same remedy mechanism as every later one — no wizard, no checklist screen.
  • Belegstufe is printed, never a percentage: gemessen · gesehen · gerechnet · angenommen. The plug screen degrades to Wertepaar with the same slider and the same sentences, no camera required.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Answers woher weißt du das? once, with one mechanism, on every screen — the complaint both the techie and the stoner are actually making, in two dialects.
  • The shared span cursor makes a claim testable: drag it and the claim's own numbers move. Screens 1, 5 and 6 are the same control, and screen 6 proves it needs no camera.
  • It can afford to propose changes, because it never proposes one you cannot tear apart first — and Nicht jetzt costs the rule 14 days, not a settings page.
  • Charts finally carry their goal: stepped setpoint from real ziel history, target band, measured day/night from out_light, output lanes where a heater that ran zero minutes is visible as an empty row.
  • Honest staleness everywhere — vor 40 Sek, vor 1 Std 24, Offline seit 14:20, Abdeckung 60 %, a chart that stops rather than interpolates.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Two handles are harder than one. On screen 1 the default span is one day out of twenty-four, so the handles sit 12 px apart at the right edge — a fat thumb at 2 a.m. grabs the wrong one. 44 px hit zones and a snap ladder are mitigations, not a fix.
  • The Beleg line is on every row and a hostile reader will call it a „mehr" control. It also adds a third typographic register to every list item; screens 1 and 6 are dense because of it.
  • Ten rules stay silent on most real problems — pests, pH lockout, light burn, a fan that stopped. And this is the first time the product writes config on the user's behalf: one bug in Übernehmen is a burned crop.
  • Most days nothing changed. Screen 1's honest answer is „nichts Auffälliges", which feels less valuable than a competitor's busy dashboard that tells you less.
  • Blattfläche im Bild is the softest number on the screen and it is the headline one — it confounds growth with leaf angle, defoliation and camera shift. And the majority of launch buyers have no camera at all, so for them the strongest witness never exists.
  • Six screens, one component. The Tafel that renders all of them becomes the new monolith unless the art-specific bodies are separate components from the first commit.
