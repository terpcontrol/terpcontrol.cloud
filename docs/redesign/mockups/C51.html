C51 · Der Unterschied

Der Unterschied — ein Ding, ein Paar, ein Regler

Every thing is a Ding with one screen shape; that shape's body is always a Vorher/Jetzt pair with the change named in one German sentence; and one slider — shared across every Ding you walk to — decides what Vorher means.

Six screens, one shape. Every body is a Vorher/Jetzt pair plus one sentence; what changes between screens is only what Vorher resolves to — a picture on screens 1, 2 and 5, the Ziel on the camera-less day one, a 24-hour Werteband on the old plug. A routine watering is 2 Taps (Gabe → Eintragen), three cans is 5 Taps, and the guarded feed drawn on screen 4 is still 2 Taps — the primary button relabels to Trotzdem eintragen instead of adding a dialog, so muscle memory cannot fire it, and Bild ansehen turns Anna's warning into evidence you can look at. Litres come from the can counter, never a keyboard. Staleness is printed three ways and never merged: the device (● Online · vor 40 Sek), the picture (Bild vor 4 Min), and on screen 6 ● Offline · vor 3 Std with the Jetzt column relabelled ZULETZT, its values greyed rather than blanked, and the chart's last three hours drawn as a grey keine Daten block instead of a straight line to the right edge. Screen 1 carries no → line at all: no rule matched, so the app says nothing — silence is the designed output. Screen 5's playhead is one x-coordinate drawn four times — the strip's tiles, the scrub handle, the chart's dashed crosshair and both time labels all sit at x = 99 — and releasing it unfolds Gemessen · Ziel · Δ, Lief and Dinge ±2 Std. The stepped dashed setpoint on every chart comes from ZielStand, the setpoint history that ziel-as-a-Ding forces into existence, which is why the day/night steps sit where they actually were instead of back-projecting today's number over last week's data.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The cursor belongs to the session, not the screen. Set Vorher = Mo 18.08. 14:02 once, then walk: the tent, plant A3, the heater socket, the Tag-Ziel and Anna all answer the same question from the same instant. Every other concept re-asks „welcher Zeitraum?“ on each screen; here the question is held and the subject changes underneath it.
  • Every body is a diff, so ranking falls out instead of being bolted on. A Ding that did not change costs one grey line; a Ding that did gets the sentence. That is the direct answer to the uniform-list problem — ◼ Heizung aus and ▲ Feuchte 40 Min über 65 % stop being the same 48 pixels.
  • A target is a thing, so targets have a history. „Ziel Tag 24,0 → 25,0 °C, von Ben, gestern“ has to be answerable, which forces ZielStand rows into existence — and that kills the standing bug where every chart draws today's setpoint over last month's data. Nobody set out to fix the chart; the fusion made it unavoidable.
  • Prescriptions only where we own the mechanism. Eleven deterministic rules over the five socket roles the firmware actually has. Each names what was measured, one concrete change, the rule id, and a Zeile that walks to the Ding — and a rule that cannot name a mechanism prints nothing at all. The picture, by contrast, may only describe: „oben links ist es heller geworden“, never „Stickstoffmangel“.
  • Day one is the same screen, not an empty state. The Vorher column becomes ZIEL and the diff is measured-against-target. The detent ladder starts with two stops and grows as history arrives, so the control learned in the first hour is the control used on day 84.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Learn one screen, know the app. Zeile when a Ding is related, Tafel when it is the subject, no third size, no expand control and no density preference. The stoner and the techie see the same thing, and nothing either does changes what the other sees tomorrow.
  • It answers the question growers actually ask. Not „what is the temperature“ but „was ist passiert, seit ich zuletzt hier war“ — and for a club, since Anna was last here, on every Ding she walks to.
  • The camera earns its keep even for the number people. Frame brightness joined against out_light catches a failed contactor in fifteen minutes, which neither the picture nor the sensor can do alone. Nobody else owns the lamp, so nobody else can copy it.
  • Degradation is designed, not apologised for. No camera, no plants, one sensor, offline, a plug from 2019: same sections, same sentence, same slider, different comparands. Only Ziel ändern ever disables, and it says why.
  • Honest instrumentation. Every value carries its own age, the cull is printed as a number rather than hidden, Werte {…} shows the literal request that produced the screen, and JSON, CSV and a per-tent read key ship from day one.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • The cursor is invisible state, and invisible state is how modes sneak back in. Someone who dragged the handle to Beginn three Dinge ago and forgot is reading a diff they did not ask for. The printed moment and the dimmed Verlauf below the hairline are mitigations, not proof — and the whole concept rests on this one control.
  • Most days, nothing changed. A home screen whose honest answer is „Seit gestern hat sich wenig geändert“ looks empty next to a chart, which always looks busy. The slider is the antidote and it requires the user to do something the daily visitor will not do.
  • The remedy rules will be wrong for somebody. H-1 cannot tell an undersized heater from an open tent door; N-3 assumes no exhaust. Eleven rules over five socket roles is a small brittle expert system, and the first time one fires wrongly the user stops reading all of them. Screens 1 and 2 show the honest cost of that: the → line and its evidence chart are present on one and absent on the other, which is the closest this design comes to a feature that appears and disappears on its own.
  • Blattfläche is a weak number in a headline position. Green-pixel fraction is confounded by leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift, and is near-noise under a magenta lamp. Labelling it „(Bildanteil)“ is honest; it does not make it a growth measurement.
  • Half the launch buyers have no camera, and the pair is the concept's most legible half. The Werteband variant on screens 3 and 6 is complete and honest, but it is visibly the lesser version. Betting the pitch on a €30 attach rate is a real strategic risk.
  • One route means one component means a monolith. The Tafel becomes the new charts.page.ts unless the art-specific bodies are separate declared components from the first commit, and one GET /api/dinge spanning Mongo, Influx, DeviceLog and image metadata has no second screen to fall back on when it is slow. Both are plans, not guarantees.
  • Steering is still a walk, not a form. Changing the night temperature is three taps where today's settings page is two, and the inline edit rows on Ziele are a partial climb-down toward the settings page this concept claims to abolish. A critic will call that correctly, and the six screens above never show a value actually being changed.
