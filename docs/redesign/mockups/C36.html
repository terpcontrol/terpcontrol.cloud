C36 · Ein Objekt

Ein Objekt — alles ist ein Ding

Everything in Terp Control is a Ding — a named thing with one time, a body and named relations — and there is exactly one way to look at a Ding, so learning the app once is learning all of it.

Every row on all three screens is the same object at two sizes. A Zeile has four slots that never collapse — status square, name, value, age — so the person who reads only the colour and the person who reads only the digits read the same 48 pixels; a Tafel is that same Ding standing as the subject. Tapping a Zeile makes it the subject, back returns, and that is the entire navigation grammar — which is why there is no tab bar to state it. A repeat feed with a schema selected is 2 Taps — Gabe, then Eintragen; the double-feed detection relabels the primary button to Trotzdem eintragen, so the deliberate override costs 3 Taps and moves the text far enough that muscle memory cannot fire it. The schema line carries zuletzt geprüft 14.07.2026 on tap, and before a medium's start_ab_tag the sheet prefills no products at all and says so: All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen. Staleness is structural rather than decorative: the tent is ● Online · vor 40 Sek with filled squares, A3's own last observation is five days old and carries a hollow one, the plant Tafel reads vor 6 Min and greys its numbers without blanking them, and the chart states Abdeckung 97 % because the verdict above it is computed on raw Influx samples, never on aggregateWindow(fn:mean). The last row of every Tafel is Werte {…}; tapped, it expands in place into the Ding's JSON and the literal GET /api/things?device_id=…&art=gabe&von=&bis= that produced the screen — the same row in the same position for every user from install, never a developer mode and never a preference.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • Three routes, one screen shape. /list, /d/:id/:thing? and /d/:id/chart. Diary, settings, maintenance, grow report and setup stop being pages: they are Dinge under the tent. The menu drops from six outline buttons to four.
  • Depth is subject-distance, not user identity. No expand control, no density preference, no remembered expansion state. The stoner walks zero steps and sees four numbers and four buttons in fixed positions; the techie walks four steps onto a ziel Ding and reads its change history. Neither was profiled and neither can change what the other sees tomorrow.
  • Capability decides what exists, never a preference. No dehumidifier role paired means the humidity ziel is not created — no target, no band, no alarm preset the hardware can never honour. Missing socket data fails closed to monitor-only with one line: Gerät meldet keine Steckdosen — Firmware zu alt.
  • Offen sits above Verlauf. A standing condition — the note pinned to the tent door, the frozen-camera zustand — outranks history, always, and the section vanishes entirely when empty.
  • The photo and the chart are the same mechanism from two ends. A bild is a Ding, so its numbers resolve read-time from Influx; scrubbing the chart moves the picture and swiping the picture moves the numbers. Image correlation costs no new storage.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Learn once, know everything. Four sections in the same order on every Tafel — relations, Offen, Verlauf, Werte — with only the Körper varying. A user who can read the tent can read a socket, a photo, a feed and a target without being taught twice.
  • One API and it cannot rot, because the webapp consumes byte-for-byte what the public read endpoint serves, and the URL is printed at the bottom of the screen it produced.
  • Renaming stops forking history. thing_id survives a rename, which kills the live bug where editing a lifecycleName splits a plant's whole record.
  • The club safety feature is cheap and real. Attribution as a label, a double-feed soft block, and client-minted ids upserted server-side so a retry can never double-log — the one failure indistinguishable from a genuine double feed.
  • Old devices gain instead of breaking. A plug or a lamp is a tent Ding with a thin body; its existing settings page stays routed, and it picks up notes, photos, honest ages and a chart with axis labels.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Uniformity flattens importance. Heizung aus and Feuchte 40 Min über 65 % are the same 48 pixels in the same list. All the ranking is carried by one status square and the Offen section, and that is thin — a ranked-density concept is straightforwardly better at this, by construction rather than by oversight.
  • Deep walks are slower than forms. Changing the night temperature is tent → Ziele → the Zeile → edit: three taps where today's settings form is two. The mitigation — rendering all targets inline on the Ziele Tafel — is a partial climb-down toward the settings page this concept claims to abolish.
  • The abstraction risk never goes away. Every future request arrives as "add a new art", and that is exactly how this becomes the database browser the brief warns about. The guard is a rule, and rules erode under deadline.
  • One screen means one performance budget. A single GET /things over a twelve-week run touches the new collection, DeviceLog, image metadata and Influx. If that request is slow the entire product is slow, and there is no second screen to fall back to.
  • Werte {…} is a wart for most users. One row of JSON at the bottom of every screen, tapped by confused people. Hiding it behind a preference is the banned pattern, so it stays visible and slightly ugly on all of them.
  • Merging is not solved. Two club members typing A3 and A 3, or Anna and anna, create two Dinge, and v1 ships no merge tool.
  • The film still assumes a fixed camera and a stable photoperiod. A knocked clamp or an autoflower under 20/4 produces jumps that are detected and announced, not fixed — and the film is the owner's named selling point.
