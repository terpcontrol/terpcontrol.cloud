Auffällig — die Auffälligkeit ist ein Ding mit Anfang, Ende und Regler

Eine Auffälligkeit ist ein Ding — mit Namen, Anfang, Ende, Körper und benannten Beziehungen. Der Startbildschirm ist die nach Wucht sortierte Liste dieser Dinge. Alles Normale ist ein einziges Ding namens Ruhe, und Ruhe hat Zahlen.

Capture costs the same here as anywhere: routine watering is 2 Taps (Gabe → Eintragen), three cans is 5 Taps, and the feed drawn on screen 4 is 5 Taps because Biobizz All-Mix · Blüte Woche 4 was chosen once on the Durchgang and prefills Bio-Bloom 2,0 ml/L = 12 ml; litres are derived from the can counter and never typed, so there is no keyboard in a dark tent. The double-feed guard costs nothing extra — it only relabels the primary button to Trotzdem eintragen so muscle memory cannot fire it — and the miss it lets through does not vanish: it becomes the ranked ▲ A3 zweimal gedüngt · Wucht 12,0 row on screen 2, which survives until somebody acknowledges it. Two clocks are printed separately everywhere: the device (● Online · vor 40 Sek) and the frame (Bild 02:40). Screen 6 is the honest degradation — a stale value is still the best value available, so 24,5 °C stays on screen in grey with Zuletzt gesehen Mi 14:02 beside it, the chart simply stops at 14:02 and marks the rest offline, and only Ziel ändern is locked. On screen 5 the whole-run bar, the Bildspur, the scrub bar and both chart lanes are drawn in one SVG, so the dashed amber playhead at 02:40 is provably at the same x in all four.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The anomaly is a stored object with a lifetime, not a badge. It has t, t_end, a body and named relations, so one thing renders as a row on the home screen, an amber region on the film's scrub bar and a markArea on the chart — three views, one record, and it stays in Verlauf after it closes.
  • The slider's range is the anomaly, not the clock. You never pick a time window. Screen 5 scrubs t − 2 Std to t_end + 2 Std and nothing else — the one thing an anxious grower at 2 a.m. cannot aim for himself. Pause is the disclosure gesture, and the panel ends in ▲ Teil von: „Nachts zu warm" ›: the scrubbed minute names the object it belongs to and one tap walks into it.
  • Ranking is the filter, and the filter has no control. No severity chips, no "only problems", no notification centre. Everything is in the list at all times, Wucht decides the order, the score is printed on every row, and sortiert ⓘ shows the formula with this screen's own numbers. A human's mistake carries a fixed 12,0 so it outranks a wobble.
  • Absence is an object too. Ruhe is stored, scrubbable and has numbers, so a perfect week is the largest thing on the screen instead of an empty state — and 1 341 Schaltvorgänge, keiner davon von dir is the only honest thing a monitoring product can show on a day when nothing happened.
  • It refuses out loud, and day one is drawn. Where no deterministic remedy exists the row prints Kein Mittel bekannt. and stops. On screen 3 the target band and the stepped setpoint are drawn across the whole day from the Ziele with no trace before 10:20 — the empty state is the real chart, and the screen says which rules are off and until when.
Stärken / Strengths
  • The quiet day is designed, not left over. Most concepts only look finished when something is wrong. Screen 1 is a normal Tuesday and it is still dense: a picture pair, a comparison slider and Ruhe with real percentages, no nag and no empty state.
  • One shape survives every device. Screen 6 is an old plug with one sensor and one output; it loses only the picture pair. The ranked list, Ruhe, the Spur, Werte and the slider are all real, and the existing plug-settings page survives verbatim behind one Zeile.
  • The guard leaves evidence. A warning that fires once and disappears protects nobody. Here the tapped-through double feed becomes a ranked object carrying Nächste Gabe nur Wasser until a person clears it — and because the model is uniform, an anomaly can be about a person without a second screen.
  • Setpoint history finally exists. Every cloud→device push writes a ziel_aenderung, so the dashed Sollwert steps from what was actually set rather than today's configuration drawn over last month's data — the defect every competitor chart has.
  • Tent above device, without an auth rewrite. One grow across four devices becomes one diary, one day count and one ranked list; the per-device permission check is unchanged and old share links keep resolving.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Ranking is a claim about importance, and a wrong ranking is worse than none. A tent with historically noisy temperature has a large σ₁₄, so a genuine excursion scores low and sinks below a trivial one. The σ-floor clamp is a fudge, visible the moment two tents are compared — and making that score the organising principle of the whole product makes the failure mode worse, not better.
  • Steuerbarkeit 0,6 demotes exactly the people who can least act. Screen 6 prints it out loud, which is honest and still means a monitor-only owner's real problems are systematically pushed down because he owns no kit to fix them. The design is kindest to the customer who already spent the most.
  • The slider on a short anomaly is decoration. Scrubbing a 40-minute humidity excursion yields eighty near-identical frames of a plant not moving. It earns its place on long nights and on Ruhe; on the common case it is a control that does nothing worth seeing.
  • „Auffälligkeit" and „Ruhe" are our words, not the grower's. Ten stored arts is how an object model turns into a database browser, and these two are the most abstract of them. The German reads well; nobody says either out loud in a tent.
  • The close rule is a guess with no data to tune it. When does „nachts zu warm" end — after one normal night or three? Too eager and the list churns every morning; too lazy and stale objects pile up on the screen the entire product is built around.
  • Mittel carries authority it has not earned. „Nachtziel prüfen" is safe; „den Fühler weg vom Heizgerät hängen" is a physical instruction from an app that cannot see where the sensor is. Ten rules written by one person, validated on zero tents — and screen 2 puts them under a primary button.
  • Most weeks nothing is auffällig. This competes against dashboards that always look busy while saying less. The picture pair carries the quiet day, and it only works for buyers who bought the camera; for everyone else a perfect week is a paragraph of numbers, and no amount of design makes that exciting without lying.
