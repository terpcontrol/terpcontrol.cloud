How to read this

The six scenarios
  • Alles in Ordnung — a normal day, the concept's central claim.
  • Etwas stimmt nicht — a real problem, and what the app offers to do.
  • Tag eins — a controller claimed minutes ago. Nothing to show yet.
  • Gießen & Düngen — the everyday capture, counted in taps.
  • Kamera & Film — the slider, and the cursor shared with the chart.
  • Altgerät oder offline — one sensor, one output, or nothing at all.
Why these six
  • Scenarios 2, 3 and 6 are where the previous sixty concepts were weakest: no remedy, a day-one screen that looked broken, and old devices waved away in a sentence.
  • Drawing them means you see the failure rather than read a promise.
  • Screen 5 must show the slider itself — a scrub bar with a visible playhead, and the chart and film strip sharing one cursor.
Judging them
  • Every concept lists its own weaknesses, written by its designer.
  • No critic scores this round — you are ranking these yourself.
  • The three originals are at the bottom of the page for comparison.

What changed
  • Stacked panels, one shared time axis — replacing 15 hidden y-axes of translucent area. Position carries the meaning; colour is secondary.
  • The setpoint is drawn, dashed and stepped, so it visibly steps at lights-on and lights-off. The soft band behind it is the tolerance.
  • Outputs are a state lane, not 0/1 lines fighting the value axis. Region length is duration; a failed contactor shows as a missing band.
  • Events live on a rail under the axis, not as columns you can only reach by hovering — there is no hover on a phone.
What it reveals
  • VPD sits below the flowering band every night — invisible on today’s chart, obvious here, and the kind of thing that costs a grow.
  • Humidity lags temperature at lights-on. Overlaying them on one axis destroys exactly this.
  • The dehumidifier runs only at night, which explains the night-time excursion rather than merely reporting it.
Honest limits
  • Time-in-range must be computed server-side on raw samples. Computing it on averaged data is silently wrong and looks fine.
  • Day/night shading needs an explicit timezone, or a 12/12 flip near a DST boundary drifts an hour.
  • Three panels is the mobile limit. A fourth needs scrolling, and scrolling breaks comparison.
Nach 50 Konzepten

Three things fifty concepts did not solve

These are not gaps in any one design below. Fifty independent attempts were made and none closed them, which makes them product problems rather than design problems — and requirements for whichever concept wins.

1 · The whole-grow film cannot be stored
  • Stills and timelapses are written as data: Buffer straight into a MongoDB document (images.model.ts:22). There is no GridFS anywhere in the server.
  • A MongoDB document stops at 16 MB. Today's 1-day, 1-week and 1-month films fit under it. A twelve-week film does not.
  • Fourteen of the fifty concepts hit this wall and none solved it. So the one film a customer most wants — the whole grow — is the one film the architecture currently forbids.
  • If the camera is the selling point, this is a prerequisite, not a detail.
2 · Nothing says what to do about it
  • Across the field, "does it tell you what to do" scored a mean of 4.93 out of 10, and not one concept of fifty reached 8.
  • Every design tells you Zu warm. None tells you which knob to turn.
  • The app already knows setpoints, socket roles, photoperiod and duty cycles, so this needs arithmetic rather than intelligence: "Dein Entfeuchter lief letzte Nacht zu 94 % und die Feuchte lag trotzdem 8 % über Ziel — er kommt nicht hinterher."
  • With a PWM lamp output, some of it can carry a button: Zu warm → Lampe auf 70 % dimmen. This is the gap between a dashboard and "ruhig schlafen".
3 · Nothing reaches a closed app
  • Twenty-four of fifty concepts do not address it at all, and several assert push notifications the codebase does not have.
  • The server has sendMail and outbound webhooks. There is no push and no service worker.
  • Your tent is at home and you are not. For the monitoring-only customer, an outbound weekly digest to Telegram would be a better product than any home screen here.
  • Related and unbuilt: nothing alarms when the camera goes silent, so a dead camera is discovered when the harvest film has a hole in the middle.
C51 · Der Unterschied

Der Unterschied — ein Ding, ein Paar, ein Regler

Every thing is a Ding with one screen shape; that shape's body is always a Vorher/Jetzt pair with the change named in one German sentence; and one slider — shared across every Ding you walk to — decides what Vorher means.

Six screens, one shape. Every body is a Vorher/Jetzt pair plus one sentence; what changes between screens is only what Vorher resolves to — a picture on screens 1, 2 and 5, the Ziel on the camera-less day one, a 24-hour Werteband on the old plug. A routine watering is 2 Taps (Gabe → Eintragen), three cans is 5 Taps, and the guarded feed drawn on screen 4 is still 2 Taps — the primary button relabels to Trotzdem eintragen instead of adding a dialog, so muscle memory cannot fire it, and Bild ansehen turns Anna's warning into evidence you can look at. Litres come from the can counter, never a keyboard. Staleness is printed three ways and never merged: the device (● Online · vor 40 Sek), the picture (Bild vor 4 Min), and on screen 6 ● Offline · vor 3 Std with the Jetzt column relabelled ZULETZT, its values greyed rather than blanked, and the chart's last three hours drawn as a grey keine Daten block instead of a straight line to the right edge. Screen 1 carries no → line at all: no rule matched, so the app says nothing — silence is the designed output. Screen 5's playhead is one x-coordinate drawn four times — the strip's tiles, the scrub handle, the chart's dashed crosshair and both time labels all sit at x = 99 — and releasing it unfolds Gemessen · Ziel · Δ, Lief and Dinge ±2 Std. The stepped dashed setpoint on every chart comes from ZielStand, the setpoint history that ziel-as-a-Ding forces into existence, which is why the day/night steps sit where they actually were instead of back-projecting today's number over last week's data.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The cursor belongs to the session, not the screen. Set Vorher = Mo 18.08. 14:02 once, then walk: the tent, plant A3, the heater socket, the Tag-Ziel and Anna all answer the same question from the same instant. Every other concept re-asks „welcher Zeitraum?“ on each screen; here the question is held and the subject changes underneath it.
  • Every body is a diff, so ranking falls out instead of being bolted on. A Ding that did not change costs one grey line; a Ding that did gets the sentence. That is the direct answer to the uniform-list problem — ◼ Heizung aus and ▲ Feuchte 40 Min über 65 % stop being the same 48 pixels.
  • A target is a thing, so targets have a history. „Ziel Tag 24,0 → 25,0 °C, von Ben, gestern“ has to be answerable, which forces ZielStand rows into existence — and that kills the standing bug where every chart draws today's setpoint over last month's data. Nobody set out to fix the chart; the fusion made it unavoidable.
  • Prescriptions only where we own the mechanism. Eleven deterministic rules over the five socket roles the firmware actually has. Each names what was measured, one concrete change, the rule id, and a Zeile that walks to the Ding — and a rule that cannot name a mechanism prints nothing at all. The picture, by contrast, may only describe: „oben links ist es heller geworden“, never „Stickstoffmangel“.
  • Day one is the same screen, not an empty state. The Vorher column becomes ZIEL and the diff is measured-against-target. The detent ladder starts with two stops and grows as history arrives, so the control learned in the first hour is the control used on day 84.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Learn one screen, know the app. Zeile when a Ding is related, Tafel when it is the subject, no third size, no expand control and no density preference. The stoner and the techie see the same thing, and nothing either does changes what the other sees tomorrow.
  • It answers the question growers actually ask. Not „what is the temperature“ but „was ist passiert, seit ich zuletzt hier war“ — and for a club, since Anna was last here, on every Ding she walks to.
  • The camera earns its keep even for the number people. Frame brightness joined against out_light catches a failed contactor in fifteen minutes, which neither the picture nor the sensor can do alone. Nobody else owns the lamp, so nobody else can copy it.
  • Degradation is designed, not apologised for. No camera, no plants, one sensor, offline, a plug from 2019: same sections, same sentence, same slider, different comparands. Only Ziel ändern ever disables, and it says why.
  • Honest instrumentation. Every value carries its own age, the cull is printed as a number rather than hidden, Werte {…} shows the literal request that produced the screen, and JSON, CSV and a per-tent read key ship from day one.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • The cursor is invisible state, and invisible state is how modes sneak back in. Someone who dragged the handle to Beginn three Dinge ago and forgot is reading a diff they did not ask for. The printed moment and the dimmed Verlauf below the hairline are mitigations, not proof — and the whole concept rests on this one control.
  • Most days, nothing changed. A home screen whose honest answer is „Seit gestern hat sich wenig geändert“ looks empty next to a chart, which always looks busy. The slider is the antidote and it requires the user to do something the daily visitor will not do.
  • The remedy rules will be wrong for somebody. H-1 cannot tell an undersized heater from an open tent door; N-3 assumes no exhaust. Eleven rules over five socket roles is a small brittle expert system, and the first time one fires wrongly the user stops reading all of them. Screens 1 and 2 show the honest cost of that: the → line and its evidence chart are present on one and absent on the other, which is the closest this design comes to a feature that appears and disappears on its own.
  • Blattfläche is a weak number in a headline position. Green-pixel fraction is confounded by leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift, and is near-noise under a magenta lamp. Labelling it „(Bildanteil)“ is honest; it does not make it a growth measurement.
  • Half the launch buyers have no camera, and the pair is the concept's most legible half. The Werteband variant on screens 3 and 6 is complete and honest, but it is visibly the lesser version. Betting the pitch on a €30 attach rate is a real strategic risk.
  • One route means one component means a monolith. The Tafel becomes the new charts.page.ts unless the art-specific bodies are separate declared components from the first commit, and one GET /api/dinge spanning Mongo, Influx, DeviceLog and image metadata has no second screen to fall back on when it is slow. Both are plans, not guarantees.
  • Steering is still a walk, not a form. Changing the night temperature is three taps where today's settings page is two, and the inline edit rows on Ziele are a partial climb-down toward the settings page this concept claims to abolish. A critic will call that correctly, and the six screens above never show a value actually being changed.
C52 · Seit zuletzt

Seit zuletzt — der Schieber ist die Grenze zwischen gesehen und neu

Terp Control has one screen: a ranked list of what changed since you last looked, across every kind of thing in the tent. Tapping a change walks you into the thing it happened to. The only state the app keeps is where the boundary between gesehen and neu sits — and that boundary is a slider.

The Marke is the whole product. On screen 1 it sits where the user actually last looked (So 19:12) and the feed is honestly boring — four rows, two of them ○ unverändert, the ranking key printed as ⓘ sortiert nach Abweichung · größte 0,4 σ. On screen 2 the same thumb has been dragged one detent left to 1 Woche and the feed re-ranked under it: four separate warm nights merged into one row carrying its own four-night evidence chart, and the count went from 4 to 41. Nothing was hidden and nothing was written — dragging only sets ?marke=, and leaving the app is the only thing that advances the boundary. A watering is 2 Taps when the volume is unchanged and 3 Taps as drawn (Gießen → one tap on the can bar → Trotzdem eintragen); the guard relabels the button rather than blocking it, and Bild ansehen opens Anna's automatically attached frame so you can see whether the soil is dark. Doses are prefilled from Biobizz All-Mix · Schritt 5 von 12 and stored resolved (2,0 ml/l × 6,0 l = 12 ml), with geprüft 14.07.2026 printed beside them. Screen 5 is one SVG on purpose: the scrub bar, the Filmstreifen, both series and both output lanes share a single x-domain, so the amber playhead at Do 13:24 is literally the same pixel column in all four — and the dashed empty frame slot is a real capture gap, not decoration. Screen 6 draws the two honest greys nobody else draws: the region before the first recorded ziel (kein Sollwert aufgezeichnet) and the block where the device stopped reporting.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The read/unread boundary is a physical object. Every other concept has a fixed "since yesterday". Here the divider between gesehen and neu is a thumb on a bar with magnetic detents (Anfang · 1 Woche · gestern · zuletzt · jetzt), and dragging it re-ranks and re-aggregates the feed live — four warm nights merge into 4 Nächte in Folge, four waterings collapse into Anna 3× · Ben 1×.
  • One list, one row shape, every kind of thing. A plant, a socket, a person, a film, a camera and an edited target all render as the same 64 px row: prose line true without the numbers, value line complete without the prose. There is no tab bar, because there is nowhere else to go — tapping a change walks you into the thing it happened to.
  • Remedies are arithmetic with a sentence attached. Twelve deterministic rules fire as a third line, always shaped [what was ruled out] → [what to do], and they print the evidence: Die Heizung lief in diesen Nächten 0 Min — sie ist es nicht. No rule may ever take an image as evidence for a plant condition; silence is a valid output.
  • The Marke is cloud-side and per user. Anna's feed and Ben's feed on the same tent are genuinely different lists over identical data — the club feature nobody else has — and it survives a new phone. A push notification is one feed row, and tapping it sets the Marke to just before that row.
  • Setpoint history is recorded. Every configuration write stores a ziel thing, so a chart draws the target that was actually set at the time and greys out the period before the first recorded change instead of painting today's number over last month's data.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers the question people actually open the app with. Not "what is the temperature" but "did anything happen while I was asleep" — and it answers with a ranked list rather than a wall of gauges the grower has to diff in their head.
  • The slider earns its place four times over. It is not a film control: it drives the feed, the picture pair, the Filmstreifen and the chart's x-domain from one service, so the owner's named ingredient works identically with and without a camera.
  • Day one is designed, not defaulted. The Marke starts at Gerät verbunden, so the very first feed has rows; no delta is fabricated against a synthetic zero, and the sort key reads nach Zeit until 48 h of σ exists. The illustration sits under the feed, not instead of it.
  • Capture is two to three taps with no keyboard. Litres come from a can counter, doses from the selected scheme, the actor from a chip row that only exists at two or more people, and the double-feed guard costs one deliberate extra tap while showing the other person's photo.
  • It degrades to an old plug without stubs. Capability and data decide what exists: no plants, no Gabe, no camera, no film — but a real feed computed from numbers alone, real remedies, honest ages, and a chart with a band, a stepped setpoint and axis labels.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Screen 1 is the honest problem. A tent that behaved yields two changes and two ○ unverändert rows. A competitor's busy dashboard will feel worth more money while telling the buyer less, and the fix — drag the Marke — is a gesture nobody will discover on their own.
  • A hostile critic will call ── gesehen ── a mode. The defence (one continuous list, a divider not a filter, anchored to a fact, draggable to Anfang) is the strongest available and is still a defence rather than an absence.
  • Re-ranking under a moving thumb is expensive and unmeasured. Every drag frame re-scores a mixed feed over things, Influx and frame metrics. If it stutters on a three-year-old Android, the one ingredient the owner singled out is what feels broken.
  • Twelve hand-written rules will be wrong somewhere. R2's "1,4 K cooler at 80 % light" comes from the tent's own history, which is honest — and a tent whose history spans a seasonal change will produce a confident wrong number. A remedy people learn to distrust is worse than silence, and there is no field data to tune thresholds before October.
  • This is still not a control surface. Steering lives behind Ziele, two taps away, and only reaches the home screen when a rule happens to fire. The grower who wants a settings screen will ask for one, and he will be right.
  • One route means one component means a monolith risk. The feed Tafel becomes the new charts.page.ts unless every art-specific body is a separately declared component from the first commit — discipline, not a guarantee. And the Tent migration touches ownership, so a bug there is a data-visibility bug.
C53 · Zwei Zustände

Zwei Zustände — damals, jetzt, und ein Satz dazwischen

Every Ding in Terp Control renders as exactly two states side by side — DAMALS and JETZT — with one German sentence naming the difference and one slider that moves the left state through time. There is no second screen shape, because there is no second question.

Every screen is the same layout: two columns, a named difference, and a remedy only where the remedy is a button the user already owns. A routine watering is 5 Taps — Gabe → three taps on the can counter → Eintragen; the same watering as last time is 2 Taps because the cans prefill; a feed on the regime's step is 4 Taps because Biobizz All-Mix · Blüte Woche 4 fills the products in, and when the guard fires it is still 2 Taps — the button just relabels to Trotzdem eintragen. Before week 3 that same sheet prefills nothing and prints All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen; the refusal is the feature. Staleness is shown three ways and never faked: ● Online · vor 40 Sek, the picture's own age burnt into its caption, and ● Offline · Zuletzt gesehen Mo 14:20 with the JETZT column greyed but still populated — a stale number is still the best number available. On screen 5 the strip, the trace, the three socket lanes, both annotation rails and the scrub bar all carry one vertical playhead at the same x, and the dashed setpoint steps at Sa 19:00 because that is when the night target was actually changed from 20,0 to 22,0 °C — the correctness fix the product is missing today.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • Every object renders as a pair, not a dashboard. Tent, plant, socket, target, camera and person all use the identical two-column layout: DAMALS → JETZT, a delta, and the target as it stood at each end. There is no second screen shape, so there is nothing to learn twice and nothing to switch between — and no Einfach/Experte toggle anywhere.
  • Ziel is the leftmost detent of the slider. On day one there is no past, so the left column holds the configured target and the header reads SOLL | JETZT. Goal versus actual stops being a chart feature and becomes a slider position — on every object, from minute four, forever. Screen 3 is a complete screen, not an empty state.
  • The comparison span lives in the URL and survives the walk. The tent says +1,7 °C; tap it and the Heizung socket opens as its own pair over exactly the same three days; tap its target and the ziel object shows 20,0 → 22,0 °C and who changed it. Three walks, one question, never restated — and the span pastes into a group chat.
  • Setpoint history is structural, not bolted on. The DAMALS column must show the target as it was then or the pair lies, so targets are stored as spans that close and reopen on every change — including a change made on the box's own knob, caught by a server-side differ. That is why screen 5's dashed setpoint steps mid-run.
  • Dragging is playing; releasing unfolds. While a handle moves, the lower sections collapse to placeholders — honest, because they are stale for the length of the gesture — and 180 ms after release everything returns enriched for that exact instant: measured vs. target, which sockets ran, diary entries within ±2 h, and the frame's own keep/cull verdict. Nothing is persisted, so it is a position, not a preference.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers the only question people open the app for. Not "what is the temperature" but "what changed since I last looked" — answered in one German sentence above the evidence, with the ranked list underneath for whoever wants it.
  • Day one is designed, not survived. No fabricated zero, no fake baseline, no +0 % delta against an invented past, no onboarding modal — the first-steps card is an ordinary Zettel that closes with the same ✓ as every other note and is then gone forever.
  • The advice is deterministic and refuses to guess. Ten printed rules, each naming itself (ⓘ Regel 5), each proposing only a control the user owns. Rule 3 says outright stellen kann die App nichts. No plant diagnosis, no "wahrscheinlich liegt es an", no AI, and Was du tun kannst is absent entirely when nothing fires.
  • Old plugs and lamps get a real page, not a greyed one. Screen 6 has no plants, no camera, no regime and no Gabe button because none exists — not because something is disabled. Capability comes from the paired socket roles and fails closed on old firmware.
  • One shared cursor beats a tooltip. Strip, trace, lanes and rails read from the same playhead; correlation runs both ways (nächster Moment über 30 °C → moves the handle, a diary entry offers seit hier vergleichen); nothing depends on hover, which does not exist on a phone.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Two columns on a phone show the best asset at half size. The camera is the selling point and this layout renders it as two ~150 px frames instead of one big one. The swipe-compare mitigates it at the cost of a tap, but by construction this concept is worse at the thing the owner most wants to sell.
  • The delta invites a causal reading the app refuses to make. Heizung lief 3 Std weniger next to 2,4 °C wärmer will be read as cause and effect. Sometimes it is; sometimes the window was open. We describe and rank; users will infer, and some inferences will be wrong.
  • Setpoint history starts on release day. The most important correctness fix in the concept is invisible for three months — every span reaching back before launch renders Ziel damals nicht aufgezeichnet, which looks exactly like a bug.
  • Two handles at 2 am is a fat-thumb hazard, and the fix hides the best interaction. Pinning the right handle behind a 400 ms long-press mostly stops the mis-grab and makes "compare Tag 20 with Tag 40" undiscoverable. A 🔒 glyph and a one-time coach line are mitigations, not a defence.
  • The smooth drag needs precomputed rollups — a new, silent failure mode. Hourly per-measure rollups are what hold 60 fps; a lagging rollup shows a stale DAMALS column beside a fresh JETZT column and nothing says so. A coverage stamp on the header is the mitigation, not a fix.
  • On a stable tent the honest answer is wenig verändert, and Ziel as a detent may simply confuse. A competitor's busy dashboard feels more valuable while telling you less. The left column silently changes meaning from "a past state" to "the target" at one end of the track; the DAMALS → SOLL header swap probably carries it, but that is a guess and the first thing to test on five real growers. Steering is also still a walk — changing the night temperature is three taps where today's form is two.
C54 · Der Befund

Der Befund — ein Befund ist ein Ding, in das man hineinlaufen kann

The primary object is a Befund: a named finding with a time span, a body of evidence, and named German relations — and a Befund is a Ding like every other Ding, so you walk from the finding to its proof, and from any thing back to the findings that cite it.

A watering is 2 Taps — Gabe → Eintragen, or Gabe → Trotzdem eintragen when the double-feed guard fires, which is the same two taps with the warning read in between. Changing 2 l to 6 l is 4 Taps (two more on the can counter, screen 4), a subset of plants is 5 Taps, and walking straight from the Fütterung fällig Befund into a prefilled sheet is 3 Taps. Litres are derived from the counter and displayed, never typed — no keyboard in a dark cellar. Screen 5 is the load-bearing one: the filmstrip, the temperature plot and the humidity plot are a single SVG with one vertical playhead at 03:41, so the picture, the excursion and the humidity lag are read off the same x. The screens show three different data ages honestly — ● vor 14 Sek for a live device, Bild vor 4 Std for a frame that is much older than the reading beside it, and Offline with Wert eingefroren · 05:22 on the 2023 lamp.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • A finding is a place, not a row. C14's ranked list was five dead ends; here the rank-1 Befund is the home screen, rendered by the same Tafel renderer as every other Ding, with Belege you walk into and a reverse edge (which findings cite me) on the socket, the target and the plant.
  • Abhilfe: remedies as walks, never as prose. Every row is a link to something inspectable and is precondition-gated — Nachtziel anheben 20,0 → 20,5 renders because a target exists, and where a role is missing the row is replaced by a stated limit: Abluft hängt an keiner Dose — nicht schaltbar. The product still refuses to diagnose.
  • Targets are versioned Dinge, so the chart draws the target that actually applied. The dashed setpoint steps at daynight and at every historical change; the pause panel can print Ziel galt seit 12.08., gesetzt von Ben instead of holding today's config against last month's data.
  • The empty state is a finding with severity 0. Ruhig seit 3 Tagen · 16 Regeln geprüft with a Ruhestreifen drawn from the same numbers the Belege list — proof of work, and something that changes daily on a screen whose honest content does not.
  • One cursor, four projections. Drag the strip and the frame, the value line and the chart cursor all move; tap a Beleg row and the cursor jumps to that evidence's timestamp. Rows never reorder while the thumb is down.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers "so what do I do" without pretending to know. The weakest score across the round was actionability; a gated list of walks is the only answer here that adds it without adding advice the hardware cannot back up.
  • Day one is designed, not survived. A different rule set produces real findings from configuration and hardwareInfo — no night target, no camera, no substrate — which resolve themselves and vanish. No wizard, no illustration, no fabricated delta against a synthetic zero.
  • Degrades to a 2023 plug without stubs. The same screen shape, the same Schieber drawn as value bars, and the Befunde are computed from numbers alone. The lamp owner learns something his device never told him: it ran 25 minutes longer than the plan.
  • Club history becomes causal, not just chronological. quittiert von Ben and Ziel geändert wegen Befund „…" answer "why was the night target raised" — a question no other concept in the round can answer at all, and it costs no auth rewrite.
  • The camera earns its premium twice. Once as the pair, once as the correlation the picture and the numbers can only do together — a lamp reporting 100 % against a frame sitting in the lights-off brightness band is a failed contactor, and neither signal finds it alone.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • The remedy catalogue is the whole bet, and a wrong remedy is worse than none. Nachtziel anheben is right for an oversized heater and wrong for a tent in a hot loft. Gating stops nonsense rows rendering; it does not make the remaining rows correct. This concept made advice navigable, not correct.
  • Four screens here are one screen. Screens 1, 2, 3 and 6 are the same component fed different data — elegant to build, and a real risk that the owner reads the whole set as "it always looks like a list of problems". 4 Befunde reads as 4 Probleme even when three are ○ ruhig, and nobody knows where the give-up threshold sits.
  • No tab bar means the film is two taps away. C36's discipline is inherited whole, including its cost: the camera buyer's favourite artefact has no permanent home in the chrome, and the only route to it is through a Befund or the Zelt's Im Zelt list.
  • The σ normaliser hides real excursions in a noisy tent, and σ_floor is a visible fudge the moment two tents are compared. Week one is thin by construction, while a competitor's busy dashboard still feels more valuable on day two while telling you less.
  • It is a looking surface, not a steering surface. Changing a night target is three taps where today's settings form is two, and still_bis (snooze) is the closest thing to a mode in a concept that claims to have none — instance-scoped and printed on the row, but a hostile reviewer can fairly call it a hiding control.
  • The rule engine is a real server cost. 16 rules on a 5-minute ticker per tent, on top of per-frame image metrics and the ffmpeg queue, against one process — the dedupe cache is the only thing between this and a melted box.
C55 · Nachbarschaft

Nachbarschaft — jeder Unterschied hat einen Nachbarn, dem er gehört

Every difference has a neighbour that owns it, and the app's only navigation is walking to that neighbour with the cursor still in your hand.

The whole product is one screen shape and one cursor. Screens 1, 2, 3 and 6 are the same component — the difference list, the Spur and the lanes never move; only the data and the top card change. The remedy on screen 2 is not advice bolted onto a chart but the last link of a chain that ends on Ben, vor 3 Tg 19:04, next to the button that changes exactly that value. „weil" is a reserved word: it is printed only where a stored edge with a proof exists (solid ├ └ connectors in amber), and coincidence is printed dotted ┊ under GLEICHZEITIG with no verb and no arrow — a watering, a picture that got darker and a lamp switching on are neighbours, never causes. Screen 6 pays that rule's price twice: the offline row gets no Warum? button at all, and the sentence „Offline": dafür kennt die App keinen Grund ships. A routine watering is 2 Taps — Gabe then Trotzdem eintragen, the guard costing nothing extra — and two cans instead of the prefilled three is 3 Taps; litres are derived from the tap count (3 Kannen · Kanne 2,0 l · 6,0 l are all stored), so there is no keyboard in a dark tent, and Bild ansehen turns the double-feed warning into evidence by opening Anna's frame from 1 Std 30 ago. Before Woche 3 the same sheet prefills no products at all and prints „All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen"; a scheme that cannot say don't feed yet is not modelling the domain. Staleness is never hidden and never merged: ● vor 41 Sek (device), Bild 08:24 (frame), von 14:20 (last contact) and Offline are four different clocks, and a stale number is greyed, never blanked.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • „Warum?" walks one edge and keeps the cursor. Every other concept answers "why was it warm at 03:12" by showing you the heater now — the answer to a different question. Here the neighbour's screen opens at the same instant, the time is in the breadcrumb and in the URL, so a chain is a link one club member can paste to another.
  • Causation has a visible confidence boundary. Three edge classes (steuert, wirkt_auf, geändert_von) may print weil; a Gabe, a picture and any time-series correlation may never. Two typographic registers, both always on screen, no "show reasoning" control.
  • The setpoint on the chart is historical, not current. configureDevice already logs every change with a diff; storing it structured (two lines) yields the stepped dashed Sollwertspur, the geändert_von edge and an attributable diary entry from one change.
  • One lane renderer, camera or no camera. The große Bahn is a Bildband when a camera is paired and a Wertband when it is not — same slot, same cursor, same code path. C14 needed two home screens for this; screens 3 and 6 are the same component as screen 5.
  • A remedy has an address. Nine deterministic rules live in a Mongo collection, render at the end of their own chain and at the top of the difference list, print their rule id in Werte, and each button writes a config key that already exists.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers the question the grower actually asks. C14 names the difference, C36 lets you browse it; only this one answers warum — and it does so from stored records rather than inference, so the answer is checkable.
  • Day one is designed, not survived. SEIT DEM ANSCHLIESSEN is built from real timestamps and a clock, deletes itself when the last row ticks, and no delta is ever fabricated against a synthetic zero. Most concepts render an empty grid here.
  • Old plugs and lamps gain something new. A three-year-old light with one sensor gets a real difference list, and its ziel rows carry geändert_von — the first time such a device has ever explained itself. Its settings page stays byte-identical.
  • The shared cursor makes the camera worth its price. Bildband and curves are one drawing with one playhead; pause names what was true at that minute, including which sockets ran and what the target was then. Sprite-based thumbnails, not 60 requests.
  • Attribution without an auth rewrite. A tent-scoped list of names, stated once as a note rather than a login — and config changes become attributable for free, which answers the club's second-most expensive question after "who already fed".
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Most days nothing has a weil. Only measures the controller actually steers produce hard edges. On a monitoring-only tent — probably the largest group — the Warum? button almost never renders and the headline mechanism is invisible. Those users get C14 and C36 stapled together, which is a good product but not this one.
  • steuert is a model of the firmware, not a reading of it. The device never tells us why it switched a socket; we reconstruct it. out_dehumidifier already doubles as a cooler in temp and breed — proof the model is lossy — so we will eventually print a confident weil that is subtly wrong. That is the most dangerous failure here, because the whole design promises the word is earned.
  • Users will read Gleichzeitig as causation anyway. A dotted line and a missing verb are a typographic defence against a cognitive habit. I can refuse to say it; I cannot stop it being read.
  • The Spur eats the phone. Große Bahn plus lanes plus a pinned scrub header is over a third of the viewport before any prose — so on a small phone the difference list, which C14 says is the answer, starts below the fold. Screens 1 and 2 are already at their limit.
  • Carrying the cursor across a walk is unusual, and unusual costs. A user who taps Warum? expecting "how is my heater" and gets "how was my heater at 03:12" has been surprised by the cleverest idea in the concept. The breadcrumb prints the time; it does not remove the surprise.
  • One graph, total blast radius, and a migration paid in v1. A single request touches dinge, kanten, DeviceLog, image metadata and Influx; if it is slow the whole product is slow and there is no second screen to fall back to. Tent is the right model but one-tent-per-device makes its benefit zero until somebody merges two devices, which most never will.
C56 · Das Blatt

Das Blatt — vier Teile, und der vierte ist die Abhilfe

Everything Terp Control knows renders as one sheet — ein Blatt — with the same four parts in the same order, always: Was es ist · Was sich geändert hat · Woher wir das wissen · Was du tun kannst.

Six screens, one shape. The four headings — Was es ist · Was sich geändert hat · Woher wir das wissen · Was du tun kannst — are literal and identical on every sheet, including the capture sheet in screen 4 and the offline plug in screen 6, so there is no second layout to learn and no mode to be in. The fourth heading is the point: a sheet whose fourth part is empty is a broken sheet, so the remedy engine cannot be the thing that gets cut under deadline. On the healthy day it prints Sonst nichts zu tun. out loud rather than going silent. A watering is 2 Taps — Gießen then Trotzdem eintragen — and the prefilled Biobizz All-Mix feed adds one, 3 Taps; litres are derived from cans and displayed, never typed, so there is no keyboard in a dark tent, and the guard shows Anna's automatically attached frame from 18:10 so you can see for yourself whether the soil is dark. Screen 5 is the fusion argument: the film strip and the temperature chart are one drawing with one playhead at one x position, so pausing the sweep unfolds the minute underneath it — measured against target, which sockets ran, which entries fell within ±2 h — and Beleg on any remedy card drops both markers onto that rule's evidence window. Two clocks are always separate: the device (● Online · vor 12 Sek) and the picture (Bild vor 28 Sek), and on screen 6 every value carries its own age while the data gap is drawn as a gap rather than interpolated away.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The remedy is a heading, not a feature. Every sheet ends in Was du tun kannst, and a deterministic rule may only fire if it can name one other sheet to walk to and one value to change there. A rule that produces prose but no destination is rejected by the engine at author time — which is why fourteen rules exist here and no advice at all exists in the three concepts this one fuses.
  • One control changes what the app advises. Die Spur has two markers — ◀ Vorher on a snap ladder and ● Jetzt as the playhead. Part 2 renders from the pair, part 3 from the playhead between them, part 4 re-evaluates over the span they enclose. Drag Vorher from gestern to 1 Woche and sentence, evidence and remedy all re-derive from one gesture.
  • Day one is remedies, not a wizard. Five setup rules run on the exact machinery that will still be there on day 400, and they disappear by being done rather than skipped. No fabricated delta against a synthetic zero — just Der erste Vergleich ist morgen um 14:04.
  • Capability decides what exists. No dehumidifier role, no humidity target sheet — and the humidity rule changes shape (H-2b) to admit the controller is out of moves. The plug in screen 6 has no plants, so the word Pflanze never appears and the bottom bar is two items, not four. Nothing is greyed out; it is absent.
  • Every finding carries prose and number in one block. The impaired reader stops at line one, the technical reader's eye lands on the tabular-nums line. Density is typography, not a setting — ui-mode.ts and the Einfach/Experte switch are deleted, not renamed.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It answers the question the whole field ducks. Everything else measures, charts and photographs; this is the one that says Nachtziel um 1,5 °C senken and points at the sheet where you do it — with the evidence count printed and the proof scrubbable.
  • Learn one sheet, know the product. A tent, a plant, a socket, a photo, a series, a target, a person and a rule of the app itself all render the same four parts in the same order. Two routes are the entire app.
  • Advice is checkable. Regel N-4 · 5 Nächte · Beleg ansehen sets both Spur markers to the evidence window and marks the qualifying nights; press play and you watch them with the heater lane running underneath. An advisor cannot be audited this way, and the rule is itself a sheet, so switching it off for this tent is a walk rather than a settings page.
  • Honest degradation is designed, not patched. Offline keeps the last values with per-value ages, disables Ziel ändern with the reason printed, and draws the data gap as a gap. Without a camera the comparison becomes value bars and says so in one line.
  • The app never writes a setpoint. Blatt öffnen pre-types the proposed value unsaved; the human presses save. Anheften turns a remedy into the club's shared note, Passt so is an auditable snooze with a visible reason.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • A wrong remedy is worse than none. Five warm nights might be an open door, a heater on a socket we do not own, or a hot August — and N-3 will still propose lowering the target, which is exactly backwards if the grower wants 20 °C and cannot reach it. The card reads as advice, and most people will never open the Beleg.
  • The fourth heading sets an expectation the data cannot meet. The requests will be "why are the leaves yellow" and "should I flush". The honest answer is silence, and silence under a permanent heading looks like a broken feature even with Sonst nichts zu tun. printed underneath it.
  • Die Spur is the hardest thing here and it is on every screen. Two draggable markers, a snap ladder, a sweep, tap-to-pause and a shared cursor into a chart, inside 56 px on a 390 px phone, one-handed, in a dark tent. Unlike C11 it is not confined to a film tab, so if it is mediocre it is mediocre everywhere.
  • Uniformity flattens importance. Heizung aus and Feuchte 40 Min über 65 % occupy the same 48 pixels in the same list. Ranking rests on one status square, part 2's ordering and the Zettel strip, and that is thin.
  • Part 4 moves a screen whose pitch is calm. Four parts plus a Zettel strip plus relations plus history is a long scroll, and the remedy card sits above the relations — so the day a rule fires, the home screen shifts under a user who was promised it never would.
  • Blattfläche is a soft number in a loud position. It confounds growth with leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift, and under magenta LED it is near noise — yet it is the first line of part 2 on screens 1 and 5.
  • Sixteen sheet types, and the ratchet turns one way. messreihe and regel were added because the chart and the rule engine needed a home; every future feature will arrive as "add another art", and the guard against that is a convention, not a mechanism.
C57 · Die Spur

Die Spur — ein Regler, zwei Griffe, jedes Ding

Jedes Ding im Zelt hat eine Spur — seinen eigenen Verlauf in der Zeit — und die App hat genau ein Bedienelement: einen Regler mit zwei Griffen, der eine Spur abtastet. Wie viele Punkte du abtastest, entscheidet, was du siehst. Nichts anderes entscheidet es.

One control, six screens. The rail under every body is the same component with the same two handles: put them together and you get one moment fully unfolded (screens 3 and 5), pull them apart and the same body renders twice with the difference named (screens 1, 2 and 6). Routine watering is 2 Taps — Gabe, Eintragen — because the can count is prefilled from the last one; the guard turns that into 2 Taps on a button that has renamed itself to Trotzdem eintragen, and a feed on the regime's step is 3 Taps. Every screen prints its data age, and the offline device keeps logging while only the config-writing remedy is disabled.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The pair and the film are one control at two settings. C14's two-picture comparison, C36's single-instant Tafel and C11's timelapse stop being three features: they are the handle geometry of one rail. Nothing else in the product chooses a view.
  • A target has a trace, so the app can prescribe instead of describe. TargetChange makes the dashed Sollwert step visibly in the past (screen 2), and the same join produces „Ben hat gestern 19:04 das Nachtziel von 19,0 auf 20,0 gestellt" with a Zurück auf 19,0 °C button that writes an existing config key.
  • The pair works without a camera, so the 2021 plug and the fan get Vorher/Jetzt on day one (screen 6): two duty figures, two dimming values. No fallback view, no attach-rate bet.
  • Day one is a short trace, not an empty screen (screen 3): handles pinned, body rendered once and fully unfolded, one tick on the rail, no wizard and no sample data.
  • Honest refusals are answers. „Der Entfeuchter lief 96 % der Zeit und kam nicht hinterher" ends in Zeitraum ansehen, not in a suggestion the hardware cannot execute.
Stärken / Strengths
  • One cursor, every projection. Screen 5 is the payoff: film strip, two charts, three output lanes and both annotation rails are cut by the same vertical playhead, and pausing unfolds the minute into exactly the panel screen 3 shows on day one. One shape, learned once.
  • The window walks with you. Set Von = Umtopfen on the tent and A3's Spur already shows that window — a time range becomes a thing you carry, which no per-widget scrubber can do.
  • Staleness is structural, not a badge. A gap in the trace is bare rail and a named keine Daten region on the chart; the offline device's last frame and last value keep their timestamps instead of being blanked.
  • The capture sheet stays two taps while carrying pH, EC, runoff, people and the regime prefill in the same fixed field order — nothing is hidden behind a mode, only further down the scroll.
  • The double-feed guard has evidence, not just a warning: Vorher/Jetzt puts Anna's auto-attached frame beside the current one so you can see whether the soil is still dark.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • The two-handle rail is a learned gesture, and it is being asked of a tired grower at 2 am. The snap ladder and a good default window are mitigations, not a defence — if it fails usability testing the concept collapses into C14 with a scrubber, which is worse than either source.
  • Everything-is-a-trace flattens importance. A socket's duty and a night excursion get the same 44 px rail; all ranking lives in one Befund list and one Zettel strip, and that is thin.
  • The best rule cannot fire on day one. R1 needs a TargetChange that only exists after somebody changes a target inside the app post-launch. For the first weeks screen 2 has no remedy button and the app is back to describing.
  • Screen 2 is genuinely dense. A frame pair, an inline chart, two prose sentences and two buttons on 320 px is at the edge; the third and fourth Befunde are below the fold and the ranking has to be right, every time, with no way for the user to correct it.
  • Playing back a camera-less trace may be pointless. Four gauges sweeping over 30 days is a plausible-sounding animation nobody has asked for — on the majority of tents the ▶ button may be dead weight, which removes a third of the slider's justification.
  • No control surface. The remedies pull the commonest adjustments onto the home screen, but a grower changing four values still walks into four Dinge, and the mitigation (Ziele as a list of inline edit rows) is a partial climb-down toward the settings page this concept claims to have removed.
C58 · Auffällig

Auffällig — die Auffälligkeit ist ein Ding mit Anfang, Ende und Regler

Eine Auffälligkeit ist ein Ding — mit Namen, Anfang, Ende, Körper und benannten Beziehungen. Der Startbildschirm ist die nach Wucht sortierte Liste dieser Dinge. Alles Normale ist ein einziges Ding namens Ruhe, und Ruhe hat Zahlen.

Capture costs the same here as anywhere: routine watering is 2 Taps (Gabe → Eintragen), three cans is 5 Taps, and the feed drawn on screen 4 is 5 Taps because Biobizz All-Mix · Blüte Woche 4 was chosen once on the Durchgang and prefills Bio-Bloom 2,0 ml/L = 12 ml; litres are derived from the can counter and never typed, so there is no keyboard in a dark tent. The double-feed guard costs nothing extra — it only relabels the primary button to Trotzdem eintragen so muscle memory cannot fire it — and the miss it lets through does not vanish: it becomes the ranked ▲ A3 zweimal gedüngt · Wucht 12,0 row on screen 2, which survives until somebody acknowledges it. Two clocks are printed separately everywhere: the device (● Online · vor 40 Sek) and the frame (Bild 02:40). Screen 6 is the honest degradation — a stale value is still the best value available, so 24,5 °C stays on screen in grey with Zuletzt gesehen Mi 14:02 beside it, the chart simply stops at 14:02 and marks the rest offline, and only Ziel ändern is locked. On screen 5 the whole-run bar, the Bildspur, the scrub bar and both chart lanes are drawn in one SVG, so the dashed amber playhead at 02:40 is provably at the same x in all four.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The anomaly is a stored object with a lifetime, not a badge. It has t, t_end, a body and named relations, so one thing renders as a row on the home screen, an amber region on the film's scrub bar and a markArea on the chart — three views, one record, and it stays in Verlauf after it closes.
  • The slider's range is the anomaly, not the clock. You never pick a time window. Screen 5 scrubs t − 2 Std to t_end + 2 Std and nothing else — the one thing an anxious grower at 2 a.m. cannot aim for himself. Pause is the disclosure gesture, and the panel ends in ▲ Teil von: „Nachts zu warm" ›: the scrubbed minute names the object it belongs to and one tap walks into it.
  • Ranking is the filter, and the filter has no control. No severity chips, no "only problems", no notification centre. Everything is in the list at all times, Wucht decides the order, the score is printed on every row, and sortiert ⓘ shows the formula with this screen's own numbers. A human's mistake carries a fixed 12,0 so it outranks a wobble.
  • Absence is an object too. Ruhe is stored, scrubbable and has numbers, so a perfect week is the largest thing on the screen instead of an empty state — and 1 341 Schaltvorgänge, keiner davon von dir is the only honest thing a monitoring product can show on a day when nothing happened.
  • It refuses out loud, and day one is drawn. Where no deterministic remedy exists the row prints Kein Mittel bekannt. and stops. On screen 3 the target band and the stepped setpoint are drawn across the whole day from the Ziele with no trace before 10:20 — the empty state is the real chart, and the screen says which rules are off and until when.
Stärken / Strengths
  • The quiet day is designed, not left over. Most concepts only look finished when something is wrong. Screen 1 is a normal Tuesday and it is still dense: a picture pair, a comparison slider and Ruhe with real percentages, no nag and no empty state.
  • One shape survives every device. Screen 6 is an old plug with one sensor and one output; it loses only the picture pair. The ranked list, Ruhe, the Spur, Werte and the slider are all real, and the existing plug-settings page survives verbatim behind one Zeile.
  • The guard leaves evidence. A warning that fires once and disappears protects nobody. Here the tapped-through double feed becomes a ranked object carrying Nächste Gabe nur Wasser until a person clears it — and because the model is uniform, an anomaly can be about a person without a second screen.
  • Setpoint history finally exists. Every cloud→device push writes a ziel_aenderung, so the dashed Sollwert steps from what was actually set rather than today's configuration drawn over last month's data — the defect every competitor chart has.
  • Tent above device, without an auth rewrite. One grow across four devices becomes one diary, one day count and one ranked list; the per-device permission check is unchanged and old share links keep resolving.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Ranking is a claim about importance, and a wrong ranking is worse than none. A tent with historically noisy temperature has a large σ₁₄, so a genuine excursion scores low and sinks below a trivial one. The σ-floor clamp is a fudge, visible the moment two tents are compared — and making that score the organising principle of the whole product makes the failure mode worse, not better.
  • Steuerbarkeit 0,6 demotes exactly the people who can least act. Screen 6 prints it out loud, which is honest and still means a monitor-only owner's real problems are systematically pushed down because he owns no kit to fix them. The design is kindest to the customer who already spent the most.
  • The slider on a short anomaly is decoration. Scrubbing a 40-minute humidity excursion yields eighty near-identical frames of a plant not moving. It earns its place on long nights and on Ruhe; on the common case it is a control that does nothing worth seeing.
  • „Auffälligkeit" and „Ruhe" are our words, not the grower's. Ten stored arts is how an object model turns into a database browser, and these two are the most abstract of them. The German reads well; nobody says either out loud in a tent.
  • The close rule is a guess with no data to tune it. When does „nachts zu warm" end — after one normal night or three? Too eager and the list churns every morning; too lazy and stale objects pile up on the screen the entire product is built around.
  • Mittel carries authority it has not earned. „Nachtziel prüfen" is safe; „den Fühler weg vom Heizgerät hängen" is a physical instruction from an app that cannot see where the sensor is. Ten rules written by one person, validated on zero tents — and screen 2 puts them under a primary button.
  • Most weeks nothing is auffällig. This competes against dashboards that always look busy while saying less. The picture pair carries the quiet day, and it only works for buyers who bought the camera; for everyone else a perfect week is a paragraph of numbers, and no amount of design makes that exciting without lying.
C59 · Gegenüberstellung

Gegenüberstellung — eine Naht, zwei Uhren

Every screen is two things next to each other with one shared needle running through both, and the app's job is to name what is different at the position the needle stands on.

A routine watering is 2 Taps — Gießen → Speichern, everything prefilled from the last one; the screen drawn above is 3 Taps because a third can was tapped in, and adding the feed on the regime's step costs 1 Tap more, not a form. The guard relabels the save button rather than blocking it, so muscle memory cannot fire it, and it argues with Anna's picture instead of with words. Staleness is printed twice and separately on every screen — the device clock (● Live · vor 20 Sek) and the picture clock (Bild vor 2 Min) — and on the plug it becomes the subject: Offline seit 24.08., 14:20, a greyed keine Daten block on the chart, a dashed Sunday bar and a cursor that cannot be dragged past the last sample. Every band and every stepped setpoint on these charts comes from the ziel history, which is why screen five can draw two setpoints — 18,0 °C for Durchgang 2, 20,0 °C for Durchgang 3 — and then name the difference and offer to undo it.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The pair is the frame, not a feature. Every screen is two things across a seam, and the left half has a fallback ladder that always terminates — der Durchgang davor → gestern → als du zuletzt geschaut hast → dein Ziel. Day one is not a broken week nine; it is the same screen on rung 4, and the app prints which rung it landed on.
  • One cursor, two clocks. The needle is a position inside a span, not a timestamp, so a single drag lands on 15.05. on the left and 13.09. on the right. Screen five runs one vertical line through both film strips, the chart, all three output lanes, both annotation rails and the scrub bar — the same x, everywhere, and no hover anywhere.
  • Setpoint history makes the strongest advice in the set possible. Every accepted config push writes a ziel Ding, so the chart draws the target that was in force then, and rule 4 can say „beim Durchgang davor stand das Nachtziel auf 18,0 °C" with one button that puts it back. Not a model — the user's own successful configuration, retrieved.
  • Pause is the disclosure gesture, so there is no Einfach/Experte switch. Tapping the frame stops playback and unfolds everything known about that instant. Nothing is persisted, play cancels it, and it hides no feature class.
  • Capability decides what exists. No dehumidifier paired ⇒ no humidity target and no empty tile. No camera ⇒ no frames, no strip, no film, and the halves become value bands instead — the old plug on screen six is the same component with an empty bilder array, not a second screen.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Day one is designed, not apologised for. Ist gegen Soll is itself a Gegenüberstellung, so the pair is never empty, and Was noch kommt makes four dated promises the app then keeps. Most concepts in this set look broken at hour zero.
  • The old plug gets the first genuinely new thing it has ever said. Diese Woche gegen letzte Woche on a three-year-old socket costs zero firmware, and its settings page survives untouched underneath.
  • One vocabulary, learned once. Zeile, Tafel, Naht, Regler, Delta-Chip. Two runs, two days, two weeks, two plants, two people — the same component, the same picker, the same URL shape, and a saved comparison is a Ding that lives in the diary next to the photos.
  • The share is the product's best artefact. A side-by-side film of two runs in lockstep, hstacked by ffmpeg, H.264 baseline so it actually plays, seam drawn and both halves captioned. Nothing else in the set produces a file a grower wants to send someone.
  • The capture sheet is short and honest. Litres derived from taps, doses prefilled by the medium+regime pair chosen once, corrections instead of edits, and a double-feed guard that shows the soil rather than arguing.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Comparison is the wrong shape for the man who just wants the number. He opens the app to read 24,8 °C and gets two 140 px pictures, a seam he did not ask for and a +0,2 K chip. Screen one is the best case for this concept and it is still busier than a single big value would be. A structural cost, not a layout bug.
  • Two runs are rarely comparable and the screen implies they are. Different strain, pot, lamp height, season. Tag 24 gegen Tag 24 invites a conclusion the data cannot support, and the honest disclaimer is a line nobody reads.
  • The axis chip is a view switch and a critic will say so. It is derived from the pair's Art, printed rather than hidden, and pushed as a route — but Uhrzeit versus Tag im Durchgang changes what is plotted. The no-modes defence rests entirely on it hiding no feature.
  • Rule 4 — the feature that sells the concept — is silent for every existing customer. ziel history starts at the deploy; the DeviceLog backfill is partial and lossy. The first comparison an owner runs after the update has no setpoint history behind it.
  • The Vorher-Leiter is a hidden dependency. Which rung fired changes what +2,4 K means, and printing a small label above the left half is thin. A fast reader absorbs the delta without absorbing what it is a delta against.
  • Most days nothing is different, and the honest answer looks like nothing. Screen one's list is one real change and two ○ gleich rows. A competitor's busy dashboard feels more valuable while telling you less, and rung 4 does not fix that.
  • It is the most expensive concept here. A new collection, a Zelt above the device, sprite sheets, GridFS, an ECharts migration and a nine-rule engine — 56 developer-days with no slack, and the sprite sheets are a second copy of the images that goes stale nightly.
C60 · Der Zeuge

Der Zeuge — keine Aussage ohne Beleg

Nothing in Terp Control is asserted without its witness attached: every sentence, every number, every proposed remedy carries a Beleg — a named relation you can walk into — and standing on that Beleg you find a pair of pictures, a raw sample window, or the output that actually ran.

Watering is 2 Taps — Gabe then Eintragen; the counter opens at the previous Gabe's count, so the routine path touches nothing. One can more is 3 Taps, two named plants instead of the whole tent 5 Taps. When the double-feed guard fires the primary button relabels to Trotzdem eintragen (still 2 taps — the cost is deliberation, not taps) and Belegbild opens the frame auto-attached to Anna's entry, so you can see whether the soil is dark. Nothing doses; every Gabe is typed by a human.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • Eine Aussage ohne Beleg wird nicht angezeigt. Every sentence carries a muted Beleg line naming what is on the other end — 2 Bilder, 41 Werte, 14 Fenster — and tapping it walks you there. An unwitnessed claim is absent, not greyed.
  • The cursor is a span, not a point. Two handles, ◖ Vorher and ◗ Jetzt, drive every projection at once: the picture pair, the difference sentences, the chart window, the output lanes. Screen 5 draws the same vertical at the same x in the strip and the chart, because it is literally one value.
  • Remedies the app can write itself. Ten published deterministic rules over data already held. Each prints its trigger, its arithmetic and its Belege, and Übernehmen writes a ziel Ding — which is why setpoint history exists from day one and the chart stops drawing today's target over last month's data.
  • Day one has a clock on it, not an illustration. „Erstes Vorher ab 14:34", a 12-minute trace against an explicitly angenommenes target, and the setup work delivered by the same remedy mechanism as every later one — no wizard, no checklist screen.
  • Belegstufe is printed, never a percentage: gemessen · gesehen · gerechnet · angenommen. The plug screen degrades to Wertepaar with the same slider and the same sentences, no camera required.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Answers woher weißt du das? once, with one mechanism, on every screen — the complaint both the techie and the stoner are actually making, in two dialects.
  • The shared span cursor makes a claim testable: drag it and the claim's own numbers move. Screens 1, 5 and 6 are the same control, and screen 6 proves it needs no camera.
  • It can afford to propose changes, because it never proposes one you cannot tear apart first — and Nicht jetzt costs the rule 14 days, not a settings page.
  • Charts finally carry their goal: stepped setpoint from real ziel history, target band, measured day/night from out_light, output lanes where a heater that ran zero minutes is visible as an empty row.
  • Honest staleness everywhere — vor 40 Sek, vor 1 Std 24, Offline seit 14:20, Abdeckung 60 %, a chart that stops rather than interpolates.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Two handles are harder than one. On screen 1 the default span is one day out of twenty-four, so the handles sit 12 px apart at the right edge — a fat thumb at 2 a.m. grabs the wrong one. 44 px hit zones and a snap ladder are mitigations, not a fix.
  • The Beleg line is on every row and a hostile reader will call it a „mehr" control. It also adds a third typographic register to every list item; screens 1 and 6 are dense because of it.
  • Ten rules stay silent on most real problems — pests, pH lockout, light burn, a fan that stopped. And this is the first time the product writes config on the user's behalf: one bug in Übernehmen is a burned crop.
  • Most days nothing changed. Screen 1's honest answer is „nichts Auffälliges", which feels less valuable than a competitor's busy dashboard that tells you less.
  • Blattfläche im Bild is the softest number on the screen and it is the headline one — it confounds growth with leaf angle, defoliation and camera shift. And the majority of launch buyers have no camera at all, so for them the strongest witness never exists.
  • Six screens, one component. The Tafel that renders all of them becomes the new monolith unless the art-specific bodies are separate components from the first commit.
Die Vorlagen

The three they came from

Kept here for comparison. Each hybrid takes the change-pair from C14, the single uniform object frame from C36, and the shared-cursor slider from C11 — and has to show what only exists because all three were combined.

C14 · Was hat sich geändert

Was hat sich geändert — zwei Bilder und ein Satz

The app's primary object is not a value, a chart or a plant — it is a pair of pictures with the difference between them named in one sentence, and everything else in the product exists to explain that difference.

The pair is the readout: two frames, the changed tiles tinted on both of them, and four Befunde that each carry a prose line and a tabular-nums line in one block — no density setting, no „Erweitert" accordion, and the sort key is printed (ⓘ nach Abweichung). Capture is the same sheet everywhere: Gießen → Speichern 2 Taps, a plan-following feed is Gießen → Auch düngen → Speichern 3 Taps, and a shareable film is one tap on Film erstellen 1 Tap. Every diary entry pulls in the kept frame nearest its timestamp with zero user effort — the blue shape on the second Tagebuch thumbnail is the watering can Tom left in shot, which is also what the double-feed guard shows you when someone is about to water twice. Staleness is stated three ways and never as the word „live": ● vor 14 Sek on Jetzt, vor 6 Min on Tagebuch, Bilder bis 14:04 on Film, plus a hard Lücke 2 Std 10 drawn into the film timeline where the camera said nothing. The EC chart is the one nobody else draws: the manufacturer plan as a stepped dashed Sollwert with its tolerance band, the measured Zulauf against it, and the Ablauf climbing 21 % above it — salt accumulation made visible out of numbers a grower already types in.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The home screen is a pair of pictures, not a dashboard. The question answered is „what is different since I last looked", not „what is the state". The left frame's endpoint defaults to the user's own previous session, so the weekly visitor gets a week and the daily visitor gets a day — one control, the Zeitleiste, with magnetic detents at gestern and 1 Woche and any minute in between.
  • Two registers in one DOM node. Every Befund is prose plus numbers, both always present: Nachts war es wärmer / 18,1 °C → 20,5 °C, Ziel 20,0 °C. Neither line summarises the other, and there is no simple/expert switch anywhere in the concept.
  • The camera is the instrument, not a tile. Culled frames, an 8×6 per-tile change map and a lights-on-aligned day boundary make the timelapse the flagship artefact — and the cull report is shown to the user instead of hidden as a debug panel.
  • Every diary entry gets its frame for free. A GrowEvent written at 19:40 is joined to the nearest kept frame within ±5 min. Nobody uploads anything; the diary becomes a visual record at the cost of one lookup.
  • The chart is not a tab. It is only ever reached by tapping the thing it explains, and it carries a film-strip lane on the same x-scale — pictures and numbers under one cursor.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Legible with no vocabulary at all. Two frames and one sentence need no units, no baseline knowledge and no sobriety — and the same block is dense enough for a techie because the numbers sit right there in tabular figures.
  • It describes, it never diagnoses. „Der obere linke Bereich hat sich am stärksten verändert" plus the pair beats a wrong deficiency call. The refusal list — no cm, no nutrient diagnosis, no pest detection — is written into the concept rather than left to discipline.
  • The film actually leaves the phone. x264 instead of today's x265, a real Content-Disposition filename, byte ranges so iOS will play it, and navigator.share with an honest per-platform fallback. A share that ends at a login wall is not a share.
  • Correlation catches what neither source can alone. light_mismatch — the output says 80 %, the frame is dark — finds a failed contactor in fifteen minutes. No competitor fuses the two clocks at all.
  • The club problem gets evidence, not a nag. The double-feed guard shows Tom's frame: you can see whether the soil is dark. Attribution is a device-scoped list of names, so it costs days rather than an auth rewrite, and it refuses to hold member data.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Most days, nothing changed. The honest answer is usually ○ Nichts Auffälliges, so a daily visitor sees a flat screen daily. A competitor's busy dashboard feels more valuable while telling you less, and this concept has no answer to that feeling.
  • The headline number is the softest one. Blattfläche im Bild is a green pixel fraction. It confounds growth with leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift, and under magenta LED it is close to noise. Labelling it honestly does not make it stronger.
  • Most launch buyers have no camera. 289 € without, 319–349 € with. The numbers-only variant is complete and honest, but the concept's entire pitch does not apply to it — this is a bet on the attach rate.
  • Culling errors are invisible by construction. A 90-second tent visit survives the transient test; a genuinely fast real change gets dropped as a hand. The user only ever sees a count, which is the worst kind of error for a feature whose pitch is honesty.
  • It is not a control surface. Steering lives in the fourth tab, which annoys precisely the type-4 and type-5 customers who spent the most money — and the σ-based Befund ranking can sink a genuine excursion in a historically noisy tent.
  • The flagship film is the worst one. The 16 MB BSON cap holds v1 to 12 MB / 90 s, so the whole-run timelapse is the one that drops the most frames. GridFS is the right fix and it is genuinely not inside the six weeks.
C36 · Ein Objekt

Ein Objekt — alles ist ein Ding

Everything in Terp Control is a Ding — a named thing with one time, a body and named relations — and there is exactly one way to look at a Ding, so learning the app once is learning all of it.

Every row on all three screens is the same object at two sizes. A Zeile has four slots that never collapse — status square, name, value, age — so the person who reads only the colour and the person who reads only the digits read the same 48 pixels; a Tafel is that same Ding standing as the subject. Tapping a Zeile makes it the subject, back returns, and that is the entire navigation grammar — which is why there is no tab bar to state it. A repeat feed with a schema selected is 2 Taps — Gabe, then Eintragen; the double-feed detection relabels the primary button to Trotzdem eintragen, so the deliberate override costs 3 Taps and moves the text far enough that muscle memory cannot fire it. The schema line carries zuletzt geprüft 14.07.2026 on tap, and before a medium's start_ab_tag the sheet prefills no products at all and says so: All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 3 nur gießen. Staleness is structural rather than decorative: the tent is ● Online · vor 40 Sek with filled squares, A3's own last observation is five days old and carries a hollow one, the plant Tafel reads vor 6 Min and greys its numbers without blanking them, and the chart states Abdeckung 97 % because the verdict above it is computed on raw Influx samples, never on aggregateWindow(fn:mean). The last row of every Tafel is Werte {…}; tapped, it expands in place into the Ding's JSON and the literal GET /api/things?device_id=…&art=gabe&von=&bis= that produced the screen — the same row in the same position for every user from install, never a developer mode and never a preference.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • Three routes, one screen shape. /list, /d/:id/:thing? and /d/:id/chart. Diary, settings, maintenance, grow report and setup stop being pages: they are Dinge under the tent. The menu drops from six outline buttons to four.
  • Depth is subject-distance, not user identity. No expand control, no density preference, no remembered expansion state. The stoner walks zero steps and sees four numbers and four buttons in fixed positions; the techie walks four steps onto a ziel Ding and reads its change history. Neither was profiled and neither can change what the other sees tomorrow.
  • Capability decides what exists, never a preference. No dehumidifier role paired means the humidity ziel is not created — no target, no band, no alarm preset the hardware can never honour. Missing socket data fails closed to monitor-only with one line: Gerät meldet keine Steckdosen — Firmware zu alt.
  • Offen sits above Verlauf. A standing condition — the note pinned to the tent door, the frozen-camera zustand — outranks history, always, and the section vanishes entirely when empty.
  • The photo and the chart are the same mechanism from two ends. A bild is a Ding, so its numbers resolve read-time from Influx; scrubbing the chart moves the picture and swiping the picture moves the numbers. Image correlation costs no new storage.
Stärken / Strengths
  • Learn once, know everything. Four sections in the same order on every Tafel — relations, Offen, Verlauf, Werte — with only the Körper varying. A user who can read the tent can read a socket, a photo, a feed and a target without being taught twice.
  • One API and it cannot rot, because the webapp consumes byte-for-byte what the public read endpoint serves, and the URL is printed at the bottom of the screen it produced.
  • Renaming stops forking history. thing_id survives a rename, which kills the live bug where editing a lifecycleName splits a plant's whole record.
  • The club safety feature is cheap and real. Attribution as a label, a double-feed soft block, and client-minted ids upserted server-side so a retry can never double-log — the one failure indistinguishable from a genuine double feed.
  • Old devices gain instead of breaking. A plug or a lamp is a tent Ding with a thin body; its existing settings page stays routed, and it picks up notes, photos, honest ages and a chart with axis labels.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Uniformity flattens importance. Heizung aus and Feuchte 40 Min über 65 % are the same 48 pixels in the same list. All the ranking is carried by one status square and the Offen section, and that is thin — a ranked-density concept is straightforwardly better at this, by construction rather than by oversight.
  • Deep walks are slower than forms. Changing the night temperature is tent → Ziele → the Zeile → edit: three taps where today's settings form is two. The mitigation — rendering all targets inline on the Ziele Tafel — is a partial climb-down toward the settings page this concept claims to abolish.
  • The abstraction risk never goes away. Every future request arrives as "add a new art", and that is exactly how this becomes the database browser the brief warns about. The guard is a rule, and rules erode under deadline.
  • One screen means one performance budget. A single GET /things over a twelve-week run touches the new collection, DeviceLog, image metadata and Influx. If that request is slow the entire product is slow, and there is no second screen to fall back to.
  • Werte {…} is a wart for most users. One row of JSON at the bottom of every screen, tapped by confused people. Hiding it behind a preference is the banned pattern, so it stays visible and slightly ugly on all of them.
  • Merging is not solved. Two club members typing A3 and A 3, or Anna and anna, create two Dinge, and v1 ships no merge tool.
  • The film still assumes a fixed camera and a stable photoperiod. A knocked clamp or an autoflower under 20/4 produces jumps that are detected and announced, not fixed — and the film is the owner's named selling point.
C11 · Der Film

Der Film — the timelapse is the product

The grow is a film, the app is its editor: the timelapse is the primary artifact Terp Control produces, sensor data is its subtitle track, and everything the grower does is a mark on the strip.

A watering is 6 Taps — FAB, Gießen, three taps on the can counter, Speichern — and it stores 3 Kannen · 2,0 l · 6,0 l, the actor, the three plants and the timestamp; litres are derived and displayed, never typed, so there is no keyboard in a dark tent. A feed is 4 Taps because the medium+regime pair chosen once on the run prefills the products, and the litres come from the same counter — the stored value is the resolved total (2,0 ml/l × 6,0 l = 12 ml), not a pointer to a plan that may be edited next month. Two clocks are shown separately and honestly on every screen: the device (● Live · vor 8 s) and the picture (Bild 17:12 · vor 6 Std) — a fresh sensor reading behind a six-hour-old frame is exactly the lie this concept must not tell. The cull is stated as a number rather than hidden: 2 380 Bilder · 968 aussortiert: 611 dunkel, 244 doppelt, 98 Besuch. The dark tiles in the Verlauf strip are not decoration — three are night, one is the ±3 Min window around the Gießen mark, dropped because a human told us they were standing in the shot. · 940 ppm would append itself to the subtitle line only on a device reporting the SCD4x; this tent has no CO₂ upgrade, so there is no CO₂ lane, no CO₂ target and no empty tile.

Was anders ist / What's different
  • The picture is the home screen, not a tile. Every other finalist attaches the camera to a chart. Here the film-so-far is full-bleed with a scrub bar under it, and the numbers are one ranked verdict sentence plus three values — the subtitle track.
  • Pause is the disclosure gesture. Tapping the frame stops the film and unfolds everything known about that minute: measured vs. target vs. delta, which sockets were running, which Klappen fell within ±2 h, and the frame's own exposure, white-balance regime and sharpness verdict. Nothing is persisted, it self-cancels on play, and it hides no feature class — so it is not a mode, and there is no Einfach/Experte switch anywhere.
  • The cull is the engineering, and only Terp Control can do it. We own the lamp, so every frame carries out_light at capture; dark, flashed, duplicate, blurry and human-visit frames are dropped by a database join, not a vision model. A frame within ±3 min of a Klappe goes too, because a human told us they were standing there.
  • The deliverable is a file, not a URL. Schnitt → 60 s, 720p, H.264 yuv420p +faststart, subtitles burned into the corner, share sheet → Video sichern. Today's shipping timelapse is HEVC with no pixel format and plays for roughly half the audience.
  • One playhead, two projections. The strip and the chart render from the same cursor; drag either and both move, a thumbnail tap jumps the chart to that instant, and Nächster Moment über 30 °C → jumps the film. Correlation in both directions.
Stärken / Strengths
  • It sells the hardware. The owner's stated biggest selling point is the camera, and this is the only concept with a concrete, checkable success condition: on day 60 a buyer taps once and has a watchable 60-second mp4 in their camera roll.
  • Half a day of it ships on day one. Three ffmpeg flags fix a defect that makes today's timelapse invisible in Firefox and on Chrome/Linux. Even total failure of everything else leaves the product better than it is now.
  • The capture sheet is the best in the set. No keyboard, litres derived from taps, doses prefilled by a scheme that can say All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt — Woche 2, noch nicht düngen, and a double-feed soft block that costs one deliberate extra tap.
  • Reveal by data gives clubs away for free. One name means no chips, no attribution, no warning; a second name switches all of it on. No club mode, no settings toggle, and no plant taxonomy for the grower who has one plant.
  • Old plugs, lamps and fans come along. Same three tabs, the existing per-type settings components hosted unmodified, and the frame area becomes a Tagesband poster that exports as PNG instead of mp4. Their share links keep resolving.
Schwächen / Weaknesses
  • Without the camera the concept collapses to a consolation prize. The camera is a €30 upgrade; if attach is a third, two thirds of buyers get a coloured bar poster as their home screen. A diary-first or chart-first concept degrades far more gracefully, and no amount of design fixes this. It is the single biggest strategic risk in the set.
  • The middle screen is a v1.1 promise, not a v1 screenshot. The ECharts migration is explicitly out of scope, so at launch this is the existing Highcharts page plus a strip, a shared playhead and day/night shading. The moving VPD band, the small multiples and the time-in-range scorecard drawn here land later — which makes the dossier's highest-leverage fix worse here at launch than in a chart-first concept. A real cost paid to buy the film.
  • Culling will throw away something the user wanted and there is no way to notice. The strip shows that frames went, not which. The human-burst rule will silently eat a legitimate 40-minute defoliation session — arguably correct, definitely surprising.
  • Pause-as-disclosure is unusual and untested. A user who never pauses never sees a setpoint. The subtitle carries three numbers, the panel carries eleven, and the bridge between them is a gesture nobody has validated. "Your techie has to discover a gesture to reach their data" is a fair hit.
  • The diary is subordinate. A written, searchable, week-bucketed record exists, but it is a list under the Verlauf tab, beneath the film. Against a diary-centric concept this one loses the record keeper, and it should.
  • Ops and privacy both get worse on purpose. Renders need a disk volume, a cleanup job and a backup answer — a second place user data lives besides Mongo and Influx. And a portable, shareable 720p film of a grow tent is a worse privacy position than a chart; 30-day expiry, stripped names and no branded footer are mitigations, not a defence. Attribution is only a label, too: anyone with the password can pick anyone's name.
