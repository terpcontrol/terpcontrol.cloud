# Round two — forty concepts

Written after round one was scored, and shaped by what that scoring exposed. Start with
[`00-brief.md`](00-brief.md): it carries the constraints these forty were designed against, and it
explains why they differ from round one.

Three things changed between the rounds.

**Separate simple and expert applications were ruled out.** Round one's C04 proposed exactly that and
the scoring made the case against it — the two lenses it was built for loved it, and the sober
non-technical majority in the middle ranked it last. So every concept here has to serve the impaired
user and the technical one through *one* interface. That is the hard problem of the round, and each
concept answers it in its own §4.

**The camera was promoted.** Round one deferred timelapse to a later release in all ten concepts,
which is untenable when it is a headline selling point. Concepts here specify what ships first, how
frames are culled and normalised, and how a finished video actually reaches someone's phone.

**Six gaps that no round-one concept filled** became requirements: the screen that shows more than one
device, a guard against doing the same thing twice, somewhere to record a standing condition, what
reaches the user when the app is closed, an export as easy as a delete, and attribution that does not
wait on an authorisation rewrite.

Concepts are grouped by centre of gravity — camera (C11–C20), diary (C21–C30), single-interface
mechanisms (C31–C36), feeding (C37–C41), and chart, structure and club concerns (C42–C50). Each ends
with its author's own account of where it is weakest.

These are proposals, not decisions. Scores and the shortlist that follows are one panel's reading.
