# C48 — "Die Schicht"

**Family:** club · **Centre of gravity:** the shift is the unit of work. Someone is on duty; a shift
opens, work is captured against it, a handover note closes it. The rota looks *forward* and escalates
when nobody shows up. For a solo grower the whole apparatus collapses into one sentence:
*"Seit deinem letzten Besuch."*

Written against `NEW-BRIEF.md`, `00-DOSSIER.md`, `00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md`, `00-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md`
and direct reads of `/home/user/terpcontrol.cloud`.

---

## 1. Thesis

**A grow is not a stream of events; it is a sequence of watches, and every value, photo and mistake
belongs to whoever was on duty when it happened.**

Every one of the first ten concepts modelled the **past**: a timeline, a report, a run comparison, a
verdict. None modelled the **next four hours** — who is going, when, and what happens if they don't.
That is the only thing a club actually argues about, and § 17 Abs. 2 KCanG (*eigenhändige Teilnahme*)
makes it a legal fact rather than an operational preference. So: make the shift a first-class object.
A shift has a planned start, a named person, an actual open, a set of captures, and a closing
handover note. Everything else hangs off it.

Three dividends make this more than club plumbing. **(a)** A shift boundary makes *"what changed
since I was last here"* computable — a question the app cannot answer for anybody today, solo or
club, because it has no notion of a visit. The solo grower opening the app at 2am gets that screen
for free, three lines long, and never sees the word *Schicht*. **(b)** The rota escalates before the
plants die rather than after. **(c)** The product knows exactly when a human was standing in the
tent — the cheapest, most reliable "suppress frames with a person in shot" signal that exists, with
no computer vision at all.

## 2. Information architecture

Five routes. The existing ones keep their paths so share links and presets survive.

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | Device list. One device → **the Schichtzettel** (§3). This is the home screen. |
| `/device/:id/verlauf` | The chart + filmstrip + shift rail (§5). Replaces `/charts`; **`/charts` stays as a redirect that preserves the query string** so every existing preset and share link keeps working. |
| `/device/:id/diary` | The record: shifts, plants, conditions, care. Three report values on the existing `?report=` param: `schichten` (default), `pflanzen`, `zettel`. Legacy `entries` / `growreport` / `co2report` still resolve. |
| `/device/:id/settings` | Unchanged in structure. Gains a **Team** card (names) and a **Rota** card, both of which render nothing until used. |
| `/device/:id/plan/:shift_id` | One shift, full page: its captures, its frames, its handover. Deep-linkable, shareable. |

Navigation: keep `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu`. Add nothing. The device page gains a three-item
segment at the top (`Jetzt · Verlauf · Aufzeichnung`) instead of six outline buttons — this is the
one navigation change and it is a deletion.

There is **no tab bar and no FAB**. The capture bar is a sticky footer on `/list` and `/plan/:id`
only, because those are the two places you are standing in the tent.

### 3.4 Stale, offline, absent — precisely

Freshness is one function of `device.lastseen` and the newest Influx point, rendered in three places
that never disagree: the header dot, the age suffix on every number, and a generated duty row.

- **frisch** (< 3 min): green dot, `● jetzt`, numbers full-opacity, no suffix.
- **alt** (3–10 min): amber dot, `● vor 6 Min`. Numbers stay full-opacity — they are still true,
  just older — and the header line gains ` · vor 6 Min`.
- **offline** (> 10 min, matching the existing offline rule): grey dot, `● offline seit 47 Min`.
  Every number greys to `--ion-color-step-500` and is prefixed `zuletzt`:
  `zuletzt 25,4 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 kPa`. A duty row is generated:
  `☐ Controller offline seit 47 Min · zuletzt gemeldet 18:12 ›`, and expanding it shows the last ten
  MQTT log lines, the firmware id, and `Letzter Verbindungsversuch`. **The Gießen button stays
  enabled** — a person standing in a tent with a dead controller still waters, and the record is the
  point.
- **absent** (device claimed, never reported): the whole numeric line is replaced by
  `Noch keine Messwerte — der Controller war noch nie online.` plus the setup-wizard entry point.
  `OFFEN` shows one row: `☐ Controller einrichten ›`.
- **no camera paired**: the `148 Bilder` line does not render. There is no empty camera tile.
- **camera paired but silent > 24 h**: the duty row in §3.1, plus a real alarm (§8.5).

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

### 4.1 The mechanism, stated once

**There is exactly one row component in this product, `<tc-duty-row>`, and it is simultaneously the
read control and the write control. Precision is a scroll *inside* the row, never a setting outside
it.**

Every row — an open duty, a completed capture, a digest line, a handover line, a history entry, an
alarm — is the same component with the same three layers:

```
COLLAPSED  ☐  A1–A3 gießen                       fällig seit 1 Tag  ›
              └ Satz (imperative German)         └ Beleg (the number that caused it)

EXPANDED   ☐  A1–A3 gießen
              Letzte Gabe: Ben, gestern 07:15, 10 l, EC 1,4
              Plan: Woche 6 Blüte · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/l · Top-Max 1 ml/l
              ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
              │   Kannen            ◯  ◯  ◯                  │   tap counter
              │   0 × 5 l = 0 l              [ Kanne: 5 l ]  │
              └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
              Pflanzen  [A1]  [A2]  [A3]  [A4]  [A5]  [A6]
              EC 1,4   pH 6,2   ml/l …            (alle editierbar)
              ▸ Rohdaten: 14 Gaben in diesem Lauf, Verlauf, CSV
```

- **The stoned user** reads the top line, taps the can circle three times, taps the checkbox. Two
  gestures, both large, both in the dark, both one-handed. They never scrolled past line 1.
- **The techie** reads the same top line, sees the evidence number *already rendered* beside it,
  taps the chevron, edits ml/l and EC, opens `Rohdaten`, and gets the series and a CSV.
- **The normal user** does whatever is in front of them.

Nobody chose anything. There is no "Erweitert", no "Mehr anzeigen" that reveals a feature class, no
preference, no onboarding question. The same tap on the same pixel does the right thing for both
people because *the collapsed state is the summary of the expanded state, not a subset of the
product* — and because the number that justifies the verdict is on the collapsed line, so nobody is
ever denied it.

Enforceable as an engineering rule, which is what makes it a mechanism rather than a slogan:
**a feature that does not fit in a `<tc-duty-row>` does not ship.** If it needs its own screen with
its own affordances, it is a mode wearing a hat.

### 4.2 Why the ordering is not "adaptive"

The brief warns that behaviour-adaptive ordering hurts the impaired user. So ordering here is
**deterministic and derived from the world, not from the user**:

```
1. safety / irreversible          Controller offline, Alarm aktiv, Temperatur über Grenze
2. overdue                        by how overdue, descending
3. due today
4. open conditions                by severity then age
5. observations (camera silent, sensor drifted)
```

Two people looking at the same tent at the same second see the same list in the same order. What
floats is the *world*, not the *habit*. That is defensible to the stoner critic; adaptive ordering
is not.

### 4.3 What is genuinely absent, and why that is not a mode

Three things do not render, and each has a data cause the user can point at:

| Not rendered | Cause | How the user makes it appear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plant chips, per-plant ledger | `Plant.count === 0` | types one name |
| Shift card, name picker, handover, rota | `Actor.count <= 1` | adds a second name in Settings → Team |
| Dehumidifier / CO₂ / light controls | role not in `hardwareInfo.sockets` | pairs the socket |

The last one is a bug fix as much as a design: `deviceControlCapability()` collapses per-role truth
into three buckets and **fails open to `'full'`** when `hardwareInfo.sockets` is missing
(`grow-presets.ts:143-173`), so heater-only tents are shown dehumidifier alarms today. This concept
uses `parseSocketRoles()` per role and fails **closed** with an explicit line:
`Ältere Firmware — Ausstattung unbekannt. [ Ausstattung angeben ]`.

### 4.4 The solo visit boundary

A solo grower never opens a shift. The server still writes one, lazily:

- first authenticated read or write after ≥ 6 h of inactivity opens `Shift{state:'offen'}` with
  `openedAt = now`, `actor_id = the owner's implicit actor`;
- it closes automatically after 6 h of inactivity, `closedAt = last activity`;
- `handover` is never drafted and never prompted for a solo device.

Consequence: `Seit deinem letzten Besuch` has a defined anchor, and the timelapse culler (§8.3)
knows a rough presence window even with nobody on a rota. The word "Schicht" is never shown.

```
 │ Bilder     ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤ ▤             │  filmstrip axis

```

**Goal vs actual.** `markLine` dashed stepped setpoint built from `device.configuration`'s
`day.temperature` / `night.temperature` stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds of
day, **UTC — convert explicitly, a 12/12 flip near a DST boundary otherwise drifts an hour**).
`markArea` for the band. Band source is chosen in one place and **labelled**: alarm thresholds if
the user set them (`Alarm 22–29`), else setpoint ± tolerance (`Band 24–26`), else the stage's
`vpdRange` on the VPD panel (`Ziel VPD 1,2–1,5`). Never two bands at once. Deviation is drawn as a
signed fill *between actual and setpoint*, never to the axis — the current
`type:'area', threshold:null` encodes nothing.

**Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0` where the series exists, falling back to the configured
photoperiod, with the fallback stated in the legend (`Tag/Nacht: nach Plan`). A failed contactor then
shows as a **missing band**, which is a diagnosis.

**Envelope, not mean.** `aggregateWindow` min/max in one combined response. A single mean line is a
lie about a bang-bang controller. `sampling: 'minmax'` for the plot, LTTB only for the navigator.

**The shift rail is what this concept adds to a chart.** Presence blocks, coloured per actor,
initials inside. It answers two questions no other chart in this round can: *why did the temperature
spike to 30,1 °C at 21:40?* — the rail says Ben was in the tent with the door open for eleven
minutes; *who was on duty when the humidity ran away for three hours?* — nobody, the rail is empty
and the planned block is drawn hatched `▚▚ Schicht 18:00 versäumt ▚▚`. Tapping a block navigates to
`/plan/:shift_id`. On a solo device the rail is a thin neutral `Besuche` lane with no names, or
absent if the window holds one visit.

**Annotation rail**, not columns in the plot: 16 px, pixel-proximity clustering at 10 px bins with
count badges, max two rows (row 1 human, row 2 device), full-height dashed lines only for lifecycle
stage changes. Tap, not hover — the current height-1 column series is unusable on touch.

**Mobile rules:** one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap holds
the crosshair. Axis labels are never hidden; below 320 px they reduce to three ticks and the unit
moves into the panel title.

### 6.2 How the diary "keeps itself"

The owner's line is *"it does the diary keeping for you"*. Here that is literal and it is the
**handover draft**. When a shift closes, the server assembles a German sentence from what actually
happened during `[openedAt, closedAt]`:

> `Anna, 19:04–19:41. A1–A3 gegossen (15 l, EC 1,4). Temperatur nachts 3 h 10 über 28 °C. CO₂-Flasche
> weiter offen.`

Template sentences, no LLM, four clauses maximum, in a fixed order: *wer/wann · was getan · was das
Zelt gemacht hat · was offen bleibt*. The user sees it in an editable textarea under
`Was soll die nächste Person wissen?` with the hint `Vorgeschlagen aus dem, was passiert ist. Ändern
ist normal.` Ninety percent of the time they tap `Beenden` without typing. That is the diary writing
itself, and it produces exactly one dated paragraph per visit — which is what a grow diary *is*.

For a solo device the same paragraph is generated but never prompted; it appears in
`/diary?report=schichten` as the day's entry and is editable there.

### 6.3 Multi-plant, and how ignoring plants costs nothing

`plantIds: []` is a first-class value meaning *the tent*. If `Plant.count === 0`:

- the plant chip row does not render in the Gießen sheet;
- the ledger says `Zelt` rather than a plant name;
- `/diary?report=pflanzen` is not in the segment.

The first plant is created inline: the Gießen sheet's plant row shows one ghost chip `[ + Pflanze ]`
which, tapped, is a text field. Typing `A1` and hitting enter creates the plant and selects it. No
wizard, no separate screen, no "set up your grow" flow.

**Cycle identity gets fixed at the same time.** `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` currently *infers*
cycles in the browser by comparing trimmed `lifecycleName` strings and detecting stage-ordinal
rollback (`grow-report.component.ts:691-747`) — renaming a plant forks the history. That function
becomes a **one-time boot-time backfill** that writes `Plant` rows, and stops being a runtime
algorithm. Its five existing specs become the backfill's tests.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

### 7.1 The capture flow, tap counts

`[ Gießen ]` opens a sheet. It is a `<tc-duty-row>` in permanently-expanded form, so it is the same
object the home screen shows.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Gießen                              19:16  │
│                                            │
│        ◉    ◉    ◉    ◯    ◯               │   ← the tap target
│                                            │
│        3 Kannen  ·  15 l                   │
│        [ Kanne: 5 l ]                      │   set once, remembered per device
│                                            │
│  Pflanzen   [A1]● [A2]● [A3]● [A4] [A5]    │
│                                            │
│  Dünger  ☑  nach Plan: Woche 6 Blüte       │
│            Bio-Bloom 4 ml/l · Top-Max 1    │
│            = 60 ml · 15 ml                 │
│  EC 1,4    pH 6,2                          │
│                                            │
│  [ Foto ]  [ Notiz ]                       │
│                          [ Eintragen ]     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The tap target is the primary control.** One 56 px ring per can, filled left to right. Tap = +1,
  long-press anywhere on the row = −1, haptic tick if available. Countable in the dark, one-handed,
  half-asleep — and it is *how people actually water*: by the can, not by the litre. `unitMl`
  (Kannengröße) defaults to 5000 and is set once by tapping the pill; it lives in the device's
  cloud-side care preferences, never in `device.configuration`.
- **Per-plant taps:** to be exact, tap a plant chip once per can given to *that* plant; the chip
  carries a superscript count and the header total updates. If you never do this, the sheet divides
  `volumeMl` evenly across the selected plants and marks the split `geschätzt`. Both are honest and
  both are stored: `tapCount`, `perPlant[]`, `volumeMl`.
- **The techie is not blocked:** tapping `15 l` makes it a numeric field. `12,5` sets
  `volumeMl = 12500` and leaves `tapCount` null. Nothing lost, nothing hidden.

### 7.2 Schedule following

`FeedRegime` is **medium + feed line**, named the way growers say it: `Biobizz All-Mix · Bio-Grow /
Bio-Bloom`, `Biobizz Light-Mix · Bio-Grow / Bio-Bloom`, `Floragard Light · Green House Powder
Feeding`, `BioTabs (Tabletten)`. Selected once per plant (or once per tent, if there are no plants),
in a picker whose subtitle carries the horticulture:

> **All-Mix** — stark vorgedüngt. Erste Düngergabe frühestens Woche 3–4.
> **Light-Mix** — leicht vorgedüngt. Düngen ab Woche 2.
> **BioTabs** — Tabletten alle 4–6 Wochen, kaum Flüssigdünger.

Numbers live in a Mongo content collection `feed_regimes`, never in the bundle, each entry carrying
`source_url`, `retrieved_at`, `chart_version`, `kind`. The picker shows `Zuletzt geprüft: 14.06.2026`
— the legal posture doubling as a trust feature. Brand names are plain text; no logos, no colours, no
brand name inside a Terp Control feature name.

The regime's only job is **prefilling** the sheet. Every prefilled value is editable and an edit sets
`deviatedFromPlan = true` without any scolding. Step advance is **by feed event** (`Gabe 8 von 14`),
not by wall-clock week, so the grower who feeds Tue/Fri and slips is not silently mis-stepped. EC is
stored canonically with `ecBasis`, because CANNA publishes `EC+` over source water and alarming
against the raw chart value is actively wrong. A bare "ppm" number is never rendered.

The stoner face of the same feature: they never open the regime picker. They see, on the home screen,
`☐ A1–A3 gießen · fällig seit 1 Tag`, tap three rings, tap the box. The plan filled the rest.

### 7.3 The double-feed guard

At `Eintragen`, before writing: query `Care` where `plantIds` intersects the selection, `kind` is
`duenger` or `beides`, and `at > now - guardWindowH` (default 6 h, editable per device, 0 disables).

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.    │
│  Bio-Bloom 4 ml/l · 15 l · EC 1,4          │
│                                            │
│  [ Abbrechen ]        [ Trotzdem eintragen]│
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Abbrechen` is the primary, filled, green button. `Trotzdem eintragen` is a plain text button on the
right — deliberately the harder tap. Proceeding stores `overrideOfCareId` on the new record, which is
what makes the club's later reconstruction honest rather than a guess. Water-only (`kind:'wasser'`)
does not trigger the guard; overwatering is recoverable, overfeeding is not.

### 7.4 Offline capture without double-logging

`care_id` is a **client-generated uuid v4**, and the write is `PUT /care/:care_id` with an upsert.
Replaying a queued capture is therefore idempotent by construction — the failure mode the brief names
(an offline retry queue that double-logs, indistinguishable from a double feed in a shared record)
cannot occur. This is ~20 lines and ships in v1. The service worker that makes the queue possible in
a cellar is v1.1 (§12); until then the sheet keeps the payload in `localStorage` and retries on the
next foreground, which covers the common "signal came back on the stairs" case.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is treated as the headline the owner says it is, and this concept has one structural advantage
over every other: **it knows when a person was in the room.**

### 8.1 The shift frame

At `openedAt` and at `closedAt` the server pins the nearest `Image` within ±90 s to
`Shift.openFrameId` / `closeFrameId`. Cost: two `findOne` calls. Result: **every shift automatically
has a before/after pair**, shown side by side on the shift page and in the handover:

```
   19:04                    19:41
 ┌──────────┐            ┌──────────┐
 │          │            │          │
 │   ▓▓▓    │     →      │   ▓▓▓    │
 │  ▓▓▓▓▓   │            │  ▓▓▓▓▓   │
 └──────────┘            └──────────┘
   Anna · 37 Min · 15 l gegossen
```

A grower who does nothing else in the product gets a dated visual record of every visit, for free.

### 8.2 What ships in v1: a timelapse you can hold

Verified today in `image.service.ts`: three rolling timelapses (`1d`/`1w`/`1m`) rebuilt on an hourly/
4-hourly/12-hourly cadence via `execFile('ffmpeg', … '-vcodec','libx265','-crf','30')` at
`TIMELAPSE_FRAME_RATE = 25` with `TIMELAPSE_DAY_FRAMEINTERVAL_MS = 2 min`, stored as Mongo `Buffer`s.
Two things break that as a *shipped* feature, and both are fixed in v1:

1. **`libx265` in an mp4 does not play in Chrome or Firefox on Android** and is unreliable in
   messengers. A file the user cannot open is not a deliverable. Exports are re-encoded
   **H.264 High @ L4.0, `yuv420p`, `+faststart`, ≤ 1920×1080, 25 fps, CRF 20**. The rolling in-app
   previews keep x265 (storage); the export does not. Two codecs, knowingly.
2. **A Mongo `Buffer` cannot exceed the 16 MB BSON limit** and a ten-week timelapse will. Exports go
   to a `/data/exports` docker volume with a 7-day TTL, cleaned on the existing hourly timer. This is
   a `docker-compose.yaml` change and a new ops surface; it is named as a cost.

**The flow.** `Zeitraffer` on the home screen or on any chart range →

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Zeitraffer erstellen                       │
│ Zeitraum   [ Ganzer Lauf ▾ ]  1. Aug–12. Okt│
│ Länge      ≈ 48 Sek · 1 214 Bilder         │
│                                            │
│ ☑ Nachtbilder weglassen           −1 908   │
│ ☑ Bilder mit Personen weglassen      −71   │
│ ☑ Fast gleiche Bilder ausdünnen     −412   │
│ ☑ Helligkeit angleichen                    │
│                                            │
│              [ Erstellen ]                 │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The counts are real and computed before the job runs, so the user sees what culling does. Every
checkbox is on by default and every one is a `<tc-duty-row>`-shaped line whose chevron explains it in
one German sentence. Job takes 30 s–4 min; the page shows progress and the result is a
`<tc-duty-row>` in `OFFEN`: `☐ Zeitraffer fertig · 48 Sek · 14 MB ›`.

### 8.3 How frames are culled and normalised

In order, on the pre-selected frame list:

1. **Nacht weg.** For each frame timestamp, look up `out_light` in Influx (one query for the whole
   range, then a step lookup). `out_light === 0` → drop. Fall back to the configured photoperiod when
   the series is missing. This alone removes ~half the frames and all the black ones.
2. **Personen weg — the shift signal.** Drop every frame inside `[openedAt, closedAt]` of any shift,
   plus a 2-min tail. **This is the concept's dividend.** No CV model, no false positives from a
   swaying leaf, and it is exactly right: a shift is open precisely when a human is in the tent.
   For undisciplined captures it is backstopped by (3).
3. **Türsprünge weg.** Frame-to-frame mean-luma delta > 25 % during a lights-on period, for fewer
   than 6 consecutive frames → a door opened. Drop the run. Computed from a 64×48 decode, ~1 ms
   per frame.
4. **Fast-Duplikate ausdünnen.** Mean absolute difference on the 64×48 thumbnails below a threshold
   (default 1.5 / 255) → drop, keeping at most one frame per 10 min in still stretches. Preserves
   growth, removes the 3am identical-frame slabs.
5. **Helligkeit angleichen.** ffmpeg stock filters, no custom code:
   `deflicker=size=10:mode=pm` for frame-to-frame luminance jitter and
   `normalize=blackpt=black:whitept=white:smoothing=50` for the slow drift across twelve weeks. This
   also substantially tames the magenta-LED-vs-daylight problem because `normalize` operates per
   channel; it does not fully solve it, and the copy says so:
   `Kunstlicht und Tageslicht bleiben unterschiedlich.`
6. **Kameraversatz.** Detected (a global thumbnail cross-correlation > 8 px sustained over 3 frames),
   **not corrected** in v1. A one-frame black title card is inserted:
   `Kamera bewegt · 12. Sept`. Honest, cheap, and better than a silently jumping video. Real
   stabilisation (`vidstabdetect`/`vidstabtransform`, two passes, needs libvidstab in the image) is
   v1.1.

### 8.4 How the video leaves the product

- Endpoint: `GET /image/:device_id/export/:job_id?token=<imageToken>` →
  `Content-Type: video/mp4`, `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="…"`, `Accept-Ranges: bytes`.
- Filename: `terpcontrol_zelt-1_2026-08-01_bis_2026-10-12.mp4` —
  `terpcontrol_<slug(device.name || plant.label)>_<von>_bis_<bis>.mp4`, ASCII-slugged, umlauts folded.
- Android/desktop: the download lands in the browser's downloads folder. Done.
- iOS Safari: an attachment mp4 opens in the player; the sheet therefore shows one honest instruction
  line — `iPhone: Video öffnen → Teilen → „Video sichern"` — rather than pretending a download
  happened.
- Where `navigator.canShare({files})` is true, the primary button is `[ Teilen ]` and passes a real
  `File`, so the Telegram/Signal/WhatsApp sheet appears with the video attached. That is a share.
  A URL to a login page is not.
- Also offered: `[ Link kopieren ]` producing a `ShareLink`-backed public URL with `webcam:true`,
  expiring in 30 days — for the forum post — and, for the same range, a `.zip` of the culled JPEGs
  for the user who wants to edit it themselves.
- **The 30-day `imageToken` is already deliberately URL-embeddable**, so the export URL needs no new
  auth mechanism, and the token's expiry is stated in the copy.

### 8.5 The camera alarm

A camera that stops three days into flower must say so. New alarm sensor type `camera_silent`, using
the existing `Alarm` machinery unchanged (`thresholdSeconds`, `cooldownSeconds`, `actionType`).
Default preset, created automatically the first time `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` appears: fires after
24 h with no `format:'jpeg'` row. It also produces a duty row and a `Condition`, so it survives being
dismissed. This is the gap the camera critic named and it costs one alarm preset.

### 8.6 Image correlation

Three places, all the same primitive: **a timestamp cursor shared by the chart, the filmstrip and the
record.**

1. **The filmstrip is an axis.** Thumbnails positioned by real timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so
   uneven capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivery is one **sprite + JSON index per window** (Mux's
   storyboard pattern: 256×160 tiles, ≤ 100 per sheet), not N image requests. `timestampEnd` on an
   mp4 row renders as a bracket, not a point.
2. **Scrub moves the picture; picking a picture shows its numbers.** Dragging the chart cursor swaps
   the strip's active tile. Tapping a tile pins the cursor and the header shows that moment's values
   — `12. Okt 03:20 · 29,8 °C · 41 % · VPD 2,1 · Licht aus`. One control, both directions.
3. **Every capture carries its frame.** A `Care` row, a `Condition`, a note — each renders the
   nearest camera frame as a 40 px thumbnail at its own timestamp. You logged a feed at 19:12; the
   picture from 19:12 is in the row. Correlation as a byproduct of attribution, not a feature anyone
   had to find.

## 9. Clubs

### 9.1 Attribution in v1, authorisation later — and the UI says which

The brief is right that attribution does not need an auth rewrite. `Actor` is device-scoped names the
owner types once. Opening a shift **is** the "who is at the tent" picker — it names the person and,
unlike a bare picker, it has a defined end, which is what makes presence-based culling and the
"since your last visit" digest possible.

The honest line is in the Team card, not in a footnote:

> **Namen sind Etiketten, keine Zugänge.** Jede Person mit dem Login kann jeden Namen wählen. Echte
> Mitgliedskonten kommen später.

When real memberships arrive, `Actor.user_id` fills in and the same rows become authorised without a
migration. Until then the product does not pretend.

### 9.2 The forward-looking half

```
Settings → Schichtplan
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Mo Di Mi Do Fr Sa So                       │
│ ▢  ▣  ▢  ▣  ▢  ▢  ▢     18:00 · 45 Min     │
│ Wer: [ Ben ▾ ]                             │
│ Wenn niemand kommt, nach [ 45 ] Min:       │
│   ☑ Notiz am Zelt öffnen                   │
│   ☑ E-Mail an [ vorstand@… ]               │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The existing 20 s ticker (`device.service.ts:110-112`, today `runRecipes()`) gains a sibling
`runRotas()` on the same interval: for each active `Rota`,
materialise the next `Shift{state:'geplant'}` 24 h ahead; at `plannedStart + graceMin` with no
`openedAt`, set `state:'versaeumt'`, open a `Condition` (`Schicht 18:00 nicht angetreten — Ben`) and
fire the escalation through the existing `mailTransport` / webhook path in `alarm.service.ts`. A
missed shift is drawn hatched on the chart's presence rail.

**This is the thing every concept in round one missed**: the app noticing that nobody came, before
the plants notice.

### 9.3 The double-feed problem

§7.3. It is the club's single most valuable feature and it exists because captures are attributed and
time-boxed. Worth restating the design decision: the guard is a **soft block with an asymmetric
button pair**, not a warning banner. A banner is read as decoration; a dialog whose safe option is
the big green one is read as a question.

### 9.4 Conditions — the note on the tent door

`/diary?report=zettel`. An open fact with an owner, not an event and not a task:

```
OFFEN
  CO₂-Flasche fast leer                     Ben · 3. Okt · 9 Tage   ›
  A3 nicht gießen — übernässt               Chris · 8. Okt · 4 Tage ›
  Steckdose 3 wackelt                       Anna · 1. Okt · 11 Tage ›
GESCHLOSSEN (12)
  Lüfter laut          Ben 2. Okt → Anna 4. Okt: „festgezogen"      ›
```

Open conditions appear at the top of every shift and in the handover draft, which is precisely what
being pinned to a tent door means. Closing one requires a name and offers a note.

### 9.5 Privacy posture

Deliberate, and it is the reason the club half of this concept is *narrower* than it could be:

- **Store:** first names, device-scoped, free text. The Team card's placeholder is `Vorname reicht`.
- **Never store:** surname, date of birth, address, membership number, per-member dispensing
  quantities, THC content, payments. § 26 KCanG is the state's record, not ours; those fields are
  arguably Art. 9 GDPR health data and a grow controller has no need for them. The product refuses.
- **Export defaults to `PSEUDONYMISIERT`** (`A.`, `B.`, `C.`), with `NAMEN` and `ANONYM` as the other
  two settings on the same three-way control. No export silently carries names.
- **Harvest weight is never prompted.** `Plant.harvest` renders only if the user entered one before,
  and there is a one-tap `Erntedaten löschen` in Settings. **No compliance badge, ever.**
- **Deletion works.** Today `deleted` on `DeviceLog` is a *visibility* flag and "Clear logs" removes
  nothing from the diary (`device.service.ts:739`, `diary.page.ts:112`). `Lösche alles` on the new
  collections is a real delete, and the old button is renamed `Geräteprotokoll ausblenden` so it
  stops lying.

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

A `device_type` of `fan`, `light` or `plug` has: no camera, no plants, no sockets to pair, no
photoperiod worth a rota, and one person. Under this concept it gets **three things and loses
nothing**.

1. **Its settings page is untouched.** `settings.page.html`'s `ngSwitch` keeps routing to
   `fan-settings` / `light-settings` / `plug-settings`. No shift, actor, plant, care or condition
   ever renders — each is gated on data that does not exist for these types, and `Actor.count` /
   `Plant.count` stay 0 forever unless someone deliberately types a name.
2. **The device card becomes a `<tc-duty-row>` list** — the only visible change. Instead of six
   outline buttons:

```
Ventilator Keller                              ● jetzt
24,1 °C · 61 %
OFFEN
  ☐ Übertemperatur-Alarm seit 12 Min · 31,4 °C        ›
SEIT DEINEM LETZTEN BESUCH · 6. Okt
    Einstellungen geändert · Grenzwert 30 → 32 °C     ›
[ Notiz ]
```

   The `Seit deinem letzten Besuch` digest works for them with zero new entities — it reads
   `DeviceLog` and `device.lastseen` against the implicit visit boundary (§4.4).
3. **Their live i18n bugs get fixed in the same pass**, because they are in the way:
   `settings.limits.overtemperature.enabled`, `.undertemperature.enabled`, `.time.enabled` and
   `devices.plug.settings.heater-day` / `-night` currently render raw keys on plug settings.

What they explicitly do **not** get: the timelapse (no camera), the chart's presence rail (one
visitor), the feed regime picker (nothing to feed), the rota. Not hidden — absent, for the same
reason a controller with no CO₂ sensor has no CO₂ row.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Monitoring only.** Never sees the word "Schicht". Home screen is the number line, an empty
`OFFEN`, and `Seit deinem letzten Besuch: nachts 3 h 10 über 28 °C, Spitze 30,1 °C`. That digest is
the entire product for them and it is new — today the app cannot tell them anything happened. The
chart gains the setpoint band, so their series stops being decoration. They are never shown a
dehumidifier control they don't own, because roles are read per-role and fail closed.

**2 — Monitoring plus camera.** Same, plus: the digest's `148 Bilder · Zeitraffer ansehen` line, the
filmstrip axis under the chart, the camera-silent alarm, and a downloadable H.264 timelapse with the
night frames and the near-duplicates already removed. Their implicit visits (§4.4) supply a rough
person-culling signal without them ever hearing the word shift. This is the type the October release
is being sold to and it is the type this concept serves second-best.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Sees exactly the target rows for the roles paired,
and a duty row when a paired role misbehaves (`☐ Heizung läuft seit 4 h durchgehend`). The humidity
target and the dehumidifier alarm preset that today appear for a heater-only tent are gone — not
because they chose simple, but because `parseSocketRoles()` says the socket is not there.

**4 — Full tent.** Gets the most out of the chart: five output lanes in the state timeline, the
setpoint step visible at lights-on, day/night derived from measured `out_light` so a failed contactor
shows as a missing band. The honest limit stands and is not papered over: **there is no humidifier
role and no exhaust-fan role in the firmware**, `out_dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler in `temp`
mode, and this concept does not invent them. What it adds is that every setting change is attributed
and lands in the handover, so "who moved the night temperature" is answerable.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** The type this concept serves worst, and it should be said plainly. They get
`Rohdaten` on every expanded row, a CSV of `Care` and of any chart range, and — because `Care` writes
are already a clean idempotent `PUT /care/:care_id` — the first genuinely public write endpoint in
the product, documented, `x-api-key`-scoped, so their own sensor rig can log an EC reading into the
same record. That is real but it is a byproduct. There is still no output write API and this concept
does not pretend to add one.

**6 — Grow club.** The type this exists for. Three names, a rota, shifts that open and close, a
handover paragraph per visit, attribution on every capture, the double-feed guard, the conditions
board, per-plant cumulative feed with who, escalation when nobody comes, and a pseudonymised export.
It refuses to be the § 26 system of record and says so.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Nothing breaks, one thing improves, three i18n bugs die.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026

Six to eight weeks, one team, against a codebase with 38 `it()` blocks and no migration tooling. What
follows is what I would actually commit to.

### Ships

1. **`<tc-duty-row>`** and the duty generator (offline, alarm active, overdue care, open condition,
   camera silent, controller never seen). One component, one deterministic ranker. ~1 week.
2. **The Schichtzettel home screen** replacing the fridge-branded overview for `device_type` in
   `{controller, fridge}`, plus the `devices.controller` i18n block that does not exist today. The
   card that says "Terp Control FRIDGE GROW" dies here. ~1 week.
3. **`Actor` + `Shift` + implicit visit boundary + `Seit deinem letzten Besuch`.** Open/close,
   handover draft (template sentences), shift frames pinned. ~1.5 weeks.
4. **`Rota` + escalation.** One case in the existing 20 s ticker, one condition, one email through
   the existing `mailTransport`. Small precisely because both halves already exist. ~3 days.
5. **`Care` capture with tap counts, the double-feed guard, client-side `care_id` idempotency**,
   and `Plant` (label + stage; strain/medium optional), created inline. ~1.5 weeks.
6. **`Condition` board.** ~2 days.
7. **`feed_regimes` content collection with five entries** (Biobizz All-Mix, Biobizz Light-Mix,
   Floragard Light + Green House Powder Feeding, BioTabs, plus Lucas Formula as an ownerless
   fallback), each with `source_url` / `retrieved_at`, prefilling the sheet. ~4 days including
   sourcing the numbers from the brands' own charts.
8. **ECharts 6 migration** with stepped setpoint, target band, day/night from `out_light`, output
   state timeline, tap-driven annotation rail, min/max envelope, **presence rail**. Delete Chart.js.
   ~2 weeks, and it is the riskiest line here.
9. **Timelapse export v1**: culling (night, shift-presence, door jumps, near-duplicates), `deflicker`
   + `normalize`, camera-move title cards, H.264/faststart, disk export with TTL, real filename,
   `navigator.share` where available. ~1.5 weeks.
10. **Camera-silent alarm preset.** ~1 day.
11. **Per-role capability, failing closed**, replacing the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()`.
    ~3 days.
12. **Server-side pagination on `getDeviceLogs`** — mandatory, because this concept multiplies rows
    and the Entries report currently fetches the entire device history on every open. ~2 days.
13. **de + en in the same commit**, throughout. Non-negotiable.

That is roughly 11–12 person-weeks of work against a 6–8 week window, which means **two people or a
cut**. The honest cut, in order: (8) descopes to setpoint + band + day/night only, deferring the
state timeline and annotation rail to v1.1; then (7) drops to three regimes.

### Does not ship

- **Push notifications.** There is no native shell (`@capacitor/core` 4.6.2 is scaffold only — no
  `android/`, no `ios/`). Escalation is email and webhook. Saying otherwise would be a lie.
- **Service worker / true offline capture.** The idempotent `care_id` ships so the queue is *safe*
  when it arrives; the queue itself is v1.1.
- **Video stabilisation** (needs libvidstab in the image, two passes).
- **Sprite-sheet filmstrip.** v1 does N thumbnail requests with a hard cap of 60 per window; the
  sprite endpoint is v1.1.
- **Real memberships / authorisation.** `Actor.user_id` is designed for it, unused in v1.
- **Public diary URL, Markdown/BBCode generator, Telegram/Discord destinations.** All are natural
  extensions of the handover paragraph and none of them are October.
- **Harvest, yield, run-over-run comparison.**
- **Anything touching firmware.** Zero firmware changes are required by this concept. That is
  deliberate and it is the main reason the estimate is credible.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The rota may simply go unused.** Two people sharing a tent will not fill in a weekly schedule,
   and a German club with a WhatsApp group already has a rota there. If the forward-looking half is
   never adopted, what remains is retrospective attribution — which C08 "Shared Grow" already does
   with less machinery. **The concept's headline is its most likely dead feature.**
2. **Nobody opens a shift before working.** People water first and log after. The mitigation —
   the first capture retroactively opens a shift backdated to the capture — means the shift is often
   *inferred*, which in turn weakens the presence-based frame culling that §8.3 depends on. A club
   member who never opens a shift is in the timelapse.
3. **Attribution without authorisation proves nothing.** Anyone with the login can pick any name. In
   the one situation a club would reach for the record — a dispute — it is worthless. The UI says so,
   which is honest but does not make it useful.
4. **The escalation channel is email**, in a product whose community lives on Telegram
   (`t.me/+w-XFn8c8pLQyNWFi`). An escalation nobody reads is theatre. A Telegram bot destination is
   cheap but needs per-user setup and is not in the October cut.
5. **The machine-drafted handover will read like a robot** and will sometimes be wrong. Four
   template clauses in German, no LLM budget assumed. A wrong sentence in the record is worse than no
   sentence, and the mitigation (an editable textarea nobody edits) is weak.
6. **The empty state is bad.** A healthy tent with a competent grower produces an empty `OFFEN`
   section, and an empty list reads as a broken app. `Nichts offen. Seit 4 Tagen alles im Band.` plus
   the digest and the chart is the answer, and it is not a great one.
7. **The solo visit boundary is arbitrary.** Six hours of inactivity is a guess. A user who opens the
   app twenty times a day gets a meaningless "since your last visit", and a user who checks once a
   week gets a digest so long it needs its own paging. Both are real and neither is solved.
8. **`DeviceLog` gets a mixed record.** Adding `actor_id` leaves every historical row with none, so
   a club's stream shows attributed and unattributed entries side by side and reads as broken data
   for the first months.
9. **Two codecs and a new disk, forever.** Rolling previews stay x265, exports become x264 — a real
   maintenance cost accepted for a real compatibility reason, and the first person to add a third
   size will get it wrong. And `/data/exports` with a TTL cleanup is exactly the kind of volume that
   fills up at 3am on launch day. Today nothing in the product writes to disk at all.
10. **Type 5 gets a byproduct, not a design.** The public `PUT /care/:care_id` is real but small, and
    the closed-loop builder still has no output write API. They will notice.
11. **The chart migration is the schedule risk, not the shift model.** ECharts 6 plus a hand-rolled
    directive plus a presence rail plus a state timeline in two weeks, against a 1205-line component
    whose URL query string is also the on-disk format of every saved preset and share link, is the
    line most likely to slip. If it slips, the home screen still ships and the product is still
    better; if it slips badly, the goal-vs-actual promise — the highest-leverage change in the
    dossier — misses October.
12. **"Schicht" is a workplace word.** For a hobby, in German, it may read as bureaucracy — the exact
    thing a grower buying a €289 box to *sleep peacefully* is not looking for. The solo path never
    shows the word, which is a mitigation and also an admission that the name is a liability.
