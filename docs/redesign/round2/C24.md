# C24 — "Die Karte je Pflanze"

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** one readable page per plant, forever.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every plant has exactly one page — die Karte — a single scrollable document that reconstructs
everything that ever happened to it, headed by cumulative product totals, readable top-to-bottom in two
minutes by someone who has never seen the tent.**

The club critic's complaint was that nobody drew the artefact. Ten concepts built capture flows, timelines,
verdicts and film strips, and none of them produced *the thing you hand to the next person*. A grow club
has a new member on Tuesday who has to water A1–A6 on Wednesday. A hobbyist opens the app in November and
wants to know what he actually did to the plant that came out best. Both need the same object: a page
about one plant, in order, with the numbers in it.

So the card is not a report generated from the diary. **The card *is* the diary.** There is no separate
entries list, no report switcher, no "Grow Bericht" tab. Writing happens on the card, at its live edge.
Reading happens on the card, scrolling down. Printing, sharing and exporting emit the same page. The
cumulative feed balance — "Bio-Bloom, 118 ml, davon 84 ml von Anna" — sits in the card's header as a
first-class block, not behind a CSV export, because that number is the single most-asked question in a
shared room and the only reason the per-plant model is worth its cost.

The camera is bound to the card by the same logic: each card carries its own crop of the tent camera and
its own downloadable film. The plant's page has the plant's picture over time in it.

## 2. Information architecture

Five destinations. Two of them are new.

```
/list                                  Geräteliste. One device claimed → straight to its home.
/device/:device_id                     ZELT — home. Vitals masthead + the active card. (NEW route)
/device/:device_id/karten              KARTEI — the rack of cards. Only shown when >1 card. (NEW)
/device/:device_id/karten/:plant_id    DIE KARTE — the page. (NEW)
/device/:device_id/charts              Der Verlauf. Existing route, extended (ECharts).
/device/:device_id/settings            Existing. Untouched by this concept except naming.
/device/:device_id/testmode            Existing, admin. Untouched.
/karte/:share_id                       Public read-only card. (NEW, reuses ShareLink)
```

`/device/:id/diary` **301s to `/device/:id/karten`**. `?report=growreport|entries|co2report` are absorbed:
`growreport` → the card, `entries` → the card scrolled to the timeline with the filter chip bar open,
`co2report` → the tent card's CO₂ section. Old share links keep working because `resolveDeviceAccessInfo`
already runs before the redirect.

**Navigation model.** The existing `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` stays (Geräte / Geteilte Links / Konto).
Inside a device there is now a **three-item segment fixed under the header** — the first tab bar this app
has ever had, and it is only three items because there are only three things:

```
┌────────────┬────────────┬────────────┐
│   Zelt     │   Karten   │  Verlauf   │
└────────────┴────────────┴────────────┘
```

`Zelt` = home, `Karten` = the rack (or the single card directly), `Verlauf` = the chart. Settings is the
gear in the header. On a wide screen the split-pane shows the rack permanently on the left at 320 px and
the card on the right — the big-screen advantage the dossier says is unexploited.

**Type 7 devices (fan / light / plug) never see the segment.** See §10.

### 3.3 Stale, offline, absent — spelled out

**Stale (last sample 10–60 min old, device still within `ONLINE_TIMEOUT`):** the Vitalzeile numbers stay
black, the age line reads `vor 34 Min` in `--tc-text-warning`. Nothing is greyed. The value is not wrong,
it is old, and saying so is the whole fix.

**Offline (no sample for > 10 min past `ONLINE_TIMEOUT`):** numbers render at 55 % opacity, the age line
becomes
`Offline seit 22.08., 03:11 · letzte Werte` in `--tc-text-danger`. The capture row **stays fully enabled** —
a dead controller does not stop you watering, and the offline event still needs recording. Charts and the
card render normally; only the Vitalzeile is degraded.

**Absent — no sensor:** a device with no CO₂ upgrade renders three vitals, not four with a dash. Reveal by
capability, from `deviceHasCo2()` / `hardwareInfo`. Same for `lux` and `leaf_temperature`.

**Absent — no events yet (a brand-new device, and the whole of user type 1):** the Bilanz block does not
render an empty table. It is replaced, in the same slot, by the **Klimabilanz**: time-in-band for the last
7 days, split day/night, plus data coverage.

```
  Zelt A · seit 6 Tagen in Betrieb

  Klimabilanz, 7 Tage
  Temperatur   im Ziel 91 %   ·  Tag 94 %  Nacht 87 %
  Luftfeuchte  im Ziel 62 %   ·  längste Abweichung 3 Std 20
  Abdeckung    98 % der Zeit gemessen

  Noch nichts eingetragen. Das erste Foto oder die erste
  Gießung startet die Karte.
```

That is the same slot doing the same job — "how is this going" — with whatever data exists. It is not an
empty state with an illustration; it is the honest answer for a monitor-only device. When the first
`CareEvent` lands, the Bilanz block appears above it and the Klimabilanz slides down into the card body as
a section. Reveal by data, and nothing is ever hidden from anybody.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

Three mechanisms, none of them a switch, applied at three different scales.

### 4.1 The row is a sentence, and the numbers are the second line of the sentence

Every event on the card renders as exactly two text lines plus an optional thumbnail:

```
┌────┐  Heute 09:12 · Anna
│ 🖼 │  gegossen · A1–A6 · je 1,5 L
└────┘  Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L · Top-Max 0,4 ml/L · EC 1,4 · pH 6,2
   ▲        ▲                    ▲
 picture  verb line          Zahlenzeile
```

Line 1 (`--ion-text-color`, 16 px, 600) is what happened, who, when. Line 2 (`--ion-color-medium`, 13 px,
tabular numerals) is every number attached to the event. The impaired reader reads line 1 and stops — the
grey line is visually subordinate and the eye skips it. The techie reads to the end of line 2 and has the
whole record without tapping anything.

**This is not "progressive disclosure" — nothing is disclosed, because nothing was hidden.** The two
readers get different amounts of information from *the same rendered pixels*, by typographic rank. No
state, no preference, no toggle, no per-user memory. A screenshot of one user's card is identical to a
screenshot of another user's card of the same plant, which is what makes it shareable inside a club.

Fields the user did not fill are not rendered as `—` or `0`. They are absent from the Zahlenzeile. If a
field was *deliberately* left out where the schedule expected it, the word `nicht notiert` appears in the
Zahlenzeile in italic. Zero and unknown are never confused.

### 4.2 Verdichtung — the page compacts by repetition, not by preference

The card's length is set by how much actually happened, not by who is reading.

Runs of **≥3 events of the same `kind` with the same product set and the same plant selection**, all older
than **14 days**, collapse into one row:

```
  02.05. – 19.05. · 7× gegossen · je ~1,5 L                 ⌄
           Bio-Grow 2 ml/L · Anna 5× · Kai 2×
```

Tapping expands the run in place, transiently (it does not persist, so the page is the same page next
time). Nothing is lost in the collapsed state: the count, the date span, the mean dose, the products and
the actors are all on the collapsed row. The collapsed row is a *summary of facts*, not a hidden feature.

Two consequences that matter:
- A hobbyist who waters twice a week for 12 weeks gets a **card of ~30 rows**, not 90. Two minutes to read
  is true because the boring parts genuinely are one line.
- A club room where five people did five different things gets a **long card**, because it should.

The last 14 days never compact. What happened recently is always fully written out, which is what both the
2 a.m. reader and the incoming volunteer need.

**The one honest edge:** the card header carries `Alles ausklappen` / `Alles einklappen`. I am calling that
out rather than hiding it, because it is the closest this concept comes to a mode. It is defensible as a
*zoom*, not a mode: both states contain the same facts (the collapsed run states its count, span, mean and
actors), it affects only rendering of already-present content, it never hides a feature or a control, and
the print/export path always renders fully expanded regardless of the setting. It is stored in
`localStorage['tc-karte-expand']` and it is not offered during onboarding, never named after a user type,
and never referenced by any other screen.

### 4.3 The same tap writes the stoner's record and the techie's record

The capture sheet (§7) is one screen with the save button pinned. The two-tap path
(`Gegossen` → `Speichern`) produces a complete, valid, honest record. The techie's extra precision lives
**inside the fields that are already on that screen, prefilled** — not behind a "Details" expander. Litres,
products and dose come prefilled from the regime and the last event; EC, pH and runoff are three empty
inputs on the same sheet, below the fold but on the same scroll.

There is no path where the techie must switch anything on to be allowed to type an EC value, and no path
where the stoner is asked for one. The stoner's record is missing EC; the card says `nicht notiert` for
it; nobody is lied to.

## 5. The chart — `/device/:device_id/verlauf`

ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision). Chart.js, ng2-charts and
chartjs-adapter-luxon are deleted in the same commit.

**Small multiples, one linked crosshair.** 3–5 stacked panels, ~110 px each on a phone, `grid[]` +
`axisPointer.link`. Never two unrelated units on one panel. Default stack for a controller:

```
┌─ Temperatur °C ────────────────────────────────┐
│  ░░░░░░ target band 23–27 ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  │  markArea, --tc-halo
│  ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ Soll (gestuft) ─ ─ ─┐               │  dashed step line
│  ▁▂▃▄▅▄▃▂▁ Ist (min/max envelope + mean)       │  sampling:'minmax'
├─ Luftfeuchte % ────────────────────────────────┤
├─ VPD kPa ──────────────────────────────────────┤
│  band moves with the plant's stage             │
├─ Ausgänge ─────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Licht  ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬        ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬        │  state timeline lane
│  Heizung  ▬  ▬▬   ▬     ▬▬▬                    │
├─ Ereignisse ───────────────────────────────────┤
│  💧  🌿 💧      📷      ⚠      💧              │  annotation rail, max 2 rows
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Goal vs actual.** The dashed line is a **stepped** setpoint built client-side from
`device.configuration.day.temperature` / `night.temperature` and `daynight.day` / `night`
(seconds-of-day, UTC → rendered in the browser's zone, named in the axis label so a DST-crossing 12/12 flip
is legible rather than mysterious). The band behind it is chosen from one source at a time and **labelled
with its source**, in priority order: the user's own alarm thresholds (`Alarmgrenzen`, hard edges) >
setpoint ± tolerance (`Soll ± 1,5 °C`) > the active stage's `vpdRange` (`Empfehlung Frühe Blüte`). A
deviation fill (signed, between Ist and Soll) is drawn at 12 % opacity. No fill to the axis, ever.

**Day/night** comes from the measured `out_light > 0` series where present, falling back to the configured
`daynight` schedule, with the fallback drawn in a lighter grey and labelled `geplant`. A failed contactor
therefore shows up as a *missing* dark band against a `geplant` outline — a diagnostic, visible without
being explained.

**Annotations are the card's events.** The rail under the x-axis carries one glyph per `CareEvent`,
clustered at 10 px bins with a count badge, max two rows (row 1 human, row 2 device). Stage changes are
full-height dashed verticals labelled `Frühe Blüte`. Tapping a glyph pins the scrubbing header and scrolls
the card underneath to that row — **the chart and the card are the same document seen two ways.**

**The plant filter is a chip row above the chart**: `( Alle ) ( A1 ) ( A2 ) …`. Selecting A3 dims every
annotation not attached to A3 to 25 % — it does not remove them, so you can still see that *something*
happened. The measured series never change, because the sensors are tent-scoped and pretending otherwise
would be a lie.

**Scrubbing header, not tooltip.** A pinned 3-row table above the panels, updated on touch-drag, because
a fingertip occludes a tooltip. One finger scrolls the page; two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`);
long-press pins the cursor.

**Backend work this needs:** a combined `{mean,min,max}` response on `/data/series` (one round trip
instead of three), the raw-sample time-in-range endpoint for the Klimabilanz (computed on raw Influx
samples — averaging first destroys the excursions the metric exists to count), and the Flux injection fix,
which is a prerequisite for widening the measure vocabulary at all.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New entities

All cloud-side in MongoDB, keyed by `device_id`. `device.configuration` is not touched. All fields
optional, read-time normalised, no migration tooling required.

```ts
Plant {
  plant_id: string;            device_id: string;
  label?: string;              // 'A3' — null for the silent plant, see 6.2
  strain?: string;             // 'Blueberry', free text
  source?: 'seed' | 'clone' | 'unknown';
  startedAt: number;           // day 0 of the card
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage;  stageSince: number;
  medium?: string;             // 'Biobizz All-Mix'
  regime_id?: string;          // → FeedRegime
  potLitres?: number;
  crop?: { x: number; y: number; w: number; h: number };   // normalised 0..1, see §8
  endedAt?: number;  archived?: boolean;
  harvest?: { wetG?: number; dryG?: number; at?: number };  // opt-in, see §9
}

CareEvent {
  event_id: string;            // CLIENT-generated uuid v4 = idempotency key
  device_id: string;
  plant_ids: string[];         // [] means tent-scoped
  kind: 'water'|'feed'|'training'|'issue'|'note'|'photo'|'measure'|'stage'|'transplant'|'harvest';
  at: number;
  actor_id?: string;  actor_name?: string;   // snapshot, so renames don't rewrite history
  litresPerPlant?: number;     // CANONICAL — never a batch total, see 6.3
  batchLitres?: number;        // what was mixed, informational
  products?: { name: string; mlPerL?: number; gPerL?: number }[];
  ec?: number; ph?: number; runoffEc?: number; runoffPh?: number;
  training?: 'topping'|'lst'|'defoliation'|'supercrop'|'flush';
  text?: string;
  image_ids?: string[];
  override_of?: string;        // the event this one deliberately double-fed past
  regimeStep?: { regime_id: string; step: number; deviated: boolean };
  source: 'user' | 'device' | 'schedule';
  correctedBy?: string;        // append-only correction chain
}

Condition {
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; plant_ids?: string[];
  text: string; openedBy: string; openedAt: number;
  closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
}

DeviceActor { actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string; colorIndex: number; archived?: boolean; }
```

Indexes: `CareEvent {device_id: 1, at: -1}`, `{device_id: 1, plant_ids: 1, at: -1}`, unique `{event_id}`.

**Pagination is not optional.** `GET /device/:id/events?plant_id=&before=<ts>&limit=50` with a cursor on
`at`. The existing log endpoints have none and the Entries report fetches whole histories; a per-plant
card multiplies rows and would make that fatal. Budget: ~1 day, and it also fixes the existing bug.

**Backfill.** Existing `DeviceLog` rows with a `diary*` category are read, not migrated, by a read-time
adapter that projects them into `CareEvent` shape (`source:'device'`, `plant_ids: []`, kind from the
category slug). A one-time idempotent boot-time job then materialises them, using
`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` **once** to assign a `plant_id` per inferred cycle — turning today's runtime
string-comparison algorithm into a migration, which is where it belongs. Rows it cannot classify land on
the tent card, which is correct and lossless.

### 6.2 The silent plant — how a user who ignores plants is untaxed

**No user is ever asked to create a plant.**

On first `CareEvent`, the server lazily creates `Plant { plant_id, device_id, label: null, startedAt: at }`
if the device has none. Its card is titled with the device name (`Zelt A`) until the user types a strain.
There is no picker, no chip row, no "Pflanze wählen" step, and the word *Pflanze* does not appear anywhere
in the single-plant UI.

The card rack (`/karten`) is not linked while `plantCount === 1`. The `Alle 6 Karten ›` link in §3.1 only
renders when there is more than one card.

Plants appear the moment there is a reason: the card header has `+ Pflanze`, and using it once does two
things — creates `A2`, and offers the silent plant the label `A1` in a single inline confirm
(`Die bisherige Karte heißt jetzt A1. [ Passt ] [ Anders benennen ]`). From then on the capture sheet
shows a plant chip row defaulting to `Alle`.

Going the other way is free too: archiving all but one card removes the chip row again.

### 6.3 Multi-plant arithmetic — the trap and the fix

`litresPerPlant` is canonical. If you mix 9 L and give 1,5 L to each of six plants, the event stores
`litresPerPlant: 1.5, batchLitres: 9, plant_ids: [A1..A6]`, and the Bilanz on **each** card adds 1,5 L.
Product totals derive: `ml = mlPerL × litresPerPlant`, summed. Storing a batch total and dividing by the
plant count is the obvious implementation and it is wrong the first time somebody skips a plant.

An event shared across plants renders on each card with `· mit A1–A6` appended to line 1, so a reader can
see the event is not exclusive to this plant without leaving the card.

### 6.4 Conditions

Open conditions pin to the top of the home screen and to the top of every card they name. They are also
injected into the capture sheet: selecting A3 in the plant chips surfaces
`⚠ A3 nicht gießen — übergossen · Kai, vor 2 Tagen` directly above the save button. That is where a
standing fact actually prevents harm — not on a wall, not in a list.

Closing one writes a `CareEvent{kind:'note'}` so the card records that it was closed and by whom.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

### 7.1 The regime

`FeedRegime` is a hot-swappable MongoDB content collection, never bundled:

```ts
FeedRegime {
  regime_id: string;
  label: string;               // 'Biobizz All-Mix · Bio-Grow / Bio-Bloom'
  medium: string;              // 'Biobizz All-Mix'
  feedLine?: string;           // 'Biobizz'
  startFeedingWeek: number;    // All-Mix: 4   Light-Mix: 2   BioTabs: 99 (= liquid feeding not part of it)
  steps: { fromWeek: number; toWeek: number; stage: DiaryLifecycleStage;
           products: { name: string; mlPerL?: number; gPerL?: number }[]; note?: string }[];
  ecBasis?: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source';
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; chart_version: string;
}
```

Selected once, on the card header (`Medium & Düngeplan`). Brand names are plain text labels; no logos, no
brand colours, no bottle imagery, and no brand name inside a Terp Control feature name. `retrieved_at`
renders in the picker as `zuletzt geprüft 14.03.2026` — the legal hygiene and the trust feature are the
same string.

The regime's only job is to prefill. An All-Mix card in week 2 prefills the feed sheet with **no products
and the note** `All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — Flüssigdünger meist erst ab Woche 4.` A grower who feeds anyway
edits it and the event records `regimeStep.deviated: true`. Deviation is recorded, never blocked, never
scolded.

### 7.2 Capture flow, with tap counts

**Watering, the common case:**

1. Tap `Gegossen` on the home screen. *(tap 1)*
2. Sheet opens, prefilled: plants = last selection (default `Alle`), `1,5 L` = last used, actor = last used
   on this browser, time = now, products = none.
3. Tap `Speichern`. *(tap 2)*

**Two taps.** Save is a fixed footer button, always visible, ≥56 px, full width — no scrolling needed to
reach it.

**Feeding, the common case:**

1. Tap `Gefüttert`. *(tap 1)* — sheet prefilled from the regime's current step: `Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L`,
   `Top-Max 0,4 ml/L`, plus `1,5 L` per plant.
2. Tap `Speichern`. *(tap 2)*

**With the double-feed guard: 3 taps.** See 7.3.

**Techie path:** the same 2 taps, plus in-place edits. Litres is a `value-edit-row` (the existing
`[−] range [+]` component). Products are rows with editable `ml/L`. EC, pH, Runoff-EC and Runoff-pH are
four numeric inputs on the same sheet, empty, below the products. No expander, no "Erweitert", no toggle.
Worst realistic techie case: 2 taps + 4 numeric entries, one screen, no navigation.

**Photo:** `Foto` → OS camera/picker *(tap 1)* → confirm *(tap 2)*. The image posts to the existing
`POST /image/:device_id` (`format: 'user/jpeg'`) and a `CareEvent{kind:'photo', image_ids:[…]}` references
it — closing today's gap where `Image` has no back-reference to anything.

### 7.3 The double-feed guard

On sheet open, `GET /device/:id/events/recent?plant_ids=…&kinds=water,feed` returns anything inside the
guard window (water 8 h, feed 20 h; both editable per device, not per user). If anything comes back:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚠  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.   │
│     Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L · je 1,5 L              │
│                                              │
│     [ Trotzdem eintragen ]     [ Abbrechen ] │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Speichern` is **disabled** until `Trotzdem eintragen` is tapped. One deliberate extra tap. The saved event
carries `override_of: <event_id>` and renders on the card as
`gefüttert · zusätzlich zu Annas Gabe von 07:40`. Nobody is prevented from doing anything; the expensive
mistake now requires an act of intent, and the record shows it was intentional.

### 7.4 Reminders — the "it thinks for me" half

The regime plus the last watering interval produce a due date. When a plant is overdue, the card header
carries one line, in the same slot as the Bilanz, above it:

```
  Fällig: gießen (zuletzt vor 4 Tagen, sonst alle 2,4)      [ Erledigt ]
```

`Erledigt` is the two-tap capture with `Speichern` skipped — one tap, because the prefill *is* the answer.
Push/e-mail reuses the existing alarm notification path with `actionType: 'info'`. There is no separate
task list and no calendar; the due line lives on the card and disappears when it is done.

### 7.5 Offline capture without a service worker

**Position: v1 does not add `@angular/service-worker`.** It adds ~60 lines instead.

The sheet writes its payload — including the client-generated `event_id` — into
`localStorage['tc-pending-events']` **before** the POST. The POST is an upsert on `event_id`. On failure
the row stays queued; on app foreground and on network regain the queue is drained. The card renders queued
events immediately with a `⟳ wird gesendet` marker on line 1.

Because `event_id` is generated on the device and the server upserts, **a retry cannot double-log**. That
matters more than offline-completeness: in a shared record a duplicated retry is indistinguishable from a
double feed, and it would poison the exact number this concept is built around. The rest of the app
(charts, settings) remains online-only, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is the card's illustration. Three deliverables: **a film you can hold, frames that correlate
both ways, and an alarm.**

### 8.1 What ships in v1: der Kartenfilm

A per-card mp4, built nightly, downloadable.

```
GET /image/:device_id/film?plant_id=…&from=…&to=…&token=<imageToken>
→ 200 video/mp4
   Content-Disposition: attachment;
     filename="TerpControl_A3_Blueberry_2026-04-14_2026-08-20.mp4"
```

- **H.264 High profile, `yuv420p`, 1280×720, 25 fps, CRF 23, `-movflags +faststart`, no audio.**
- Filename pattern: `TerpControl_<label|Zelt>_<strain|Gerätename>_<von>_<bis>.mp4`, ASCII-folded.
- The URL carries the existing 30-day URL-embeddable `imageToken`, so on iOS and Android a long-press on
  the card's `Kartenfilm speichern` button offers **Video sichern / Video herunterladen** straight to the
  camera roll. That is the requirement the camera critic set: a share that produces a URL to a login page
  is not a share. This URL is the file.
- On desktop it is a plain download. In Telegram — where this community already lives — the file pastes
  and plays inline.

**A blocker I found in the code and am flagging loudly.** `server/src/services/image.service.ts:583` encodes with
**`libx265`** at CRF 30 into an mp4 container. HEVC in mp4 does not play in Chrome on most Android devices,
does not play in Firefox anywhere, and is rejected or transcoded unpredictably by messengers. **Every
timelapse this product has ever built is, for a large share of its users, an unplayable file.** Switching
that `-vcodec` argument to `libx264` plus `-pix_fmt yuv420p -profile:v high -movflags +faststart` is a
four-token change and it is a **v1 blocker**, not a nice-to-have. Bitrate goes up ~30 % for the same
quality; at 25 fps and a 2-minute daily lapse that is single-digit MB.

**The crop is what makes it the *card's* film.** `Plant.crop` is set once by dragging a rectangle on a
live still (`Ausschnitt wählen`), stored normalised. The nightly build decodes each kept frame once and
`sharp().extract(crop).resize(1280,720)` before handing it to ffmpeg. One camera, six cards, six films.

**Honest limit, enforced in the UI:** the crop cannot be smaller than 25 % of the frame area. A 1280×720
tent shot cropped to a sixth is 320×180 upscaled to 720p, and it looks like it. The picker refuses and says
`Ausschnitt zu klein — die Kamera sieht das Zelt, nicht die einzelne Pflanze.` Better to say that than to
ship mush.

### 8.2 Frame culling and normalisation — the content problem

All of it runs on a **64×36 greyscale thumbnail** derived once per frame with `sharp` (already a server
dependency), plus one full-frame pass for the surviving frames. Five rules, in order:

1. **Lights-off cull (a data join, not image analysis).** Drop frames whose timestamp falls in a period
   where the device's own `out_light` series is 0. This is exact, free, and removes the single largest
   category of garbage frames. Toggle on the card: `Nachtbilder mitnehmen` for people running IR.
2. **Luma cull.** `sharp(buf).stats()` gives per-channel means. Drop a frame whose luma is below 0,35× or
   above 2,5× the rolling median of the last 20 kept frames. Catches the tent door open, a torch, a phone
   flash, and a lens fogged white by condensation.
3. **Transient cull — the hand in the shot.** Mean absolute difference on the 64×36 thumbnail. A frame is
   dropped when MAD to the previous kept frame > 18 % **and** the following frame returns to within 6 % of
   that previous frame. A hand, a watering can and a person are transients that revert; a plant growing is
   not; the camera being nudged is not. Three-frame lookahead, ~40 lines, no ML.
4. **Dedup.** MAD < 0,8 % → drop. A frozen stream stops contributing 400 identical frames.
5. **Flicker normalisation.** For each kept frame compute channel means; apply a per-channel gain toward
   the **rolling 21-frame median** with `sharp().linear([rG,gG,bG],[0,0,0])`. This removes the frame-to-frame
   strobing that makes tent timelapses unwatchable, **without** trying to convert magenta LED light into
   daylight — the tent stays the colour it is, it just stops flashing. Gains clamped to [0.75, 1.35] so a
   genuine light change survives as a light change.

**What v1 explicitly does NOT do: stabilisation.** Instead, rule 3's counterpart: if MAD stays above 18 %
for ≥3 consecutive frames, the build records a **`ShiftEvent`**, cuts the film there, and the card gets a
row: `12.05. · Kamera verschoben` in the timeline. Honest and diagnostic. Optical-flow stabilisation is
v1.1 at best and pretending otherwise is exactly how round one failed this feature.

Each build stores its cull statistics on the resulting `Image` document, and the card shows them under the
film in one line: `Aus 4.180 Bildern · 1.902 behalten · 2.100 Nacht · 141 Störung · 37 doppelt`. That is
the difference between a black box and something a user trusts.

### 8.3 Image correlation, both directions

**Frame → numbers.** Every timeline row with a frame within ±5 min shows a 56 px thumbnail at its left.
Tapping any thumbnail — on the card, on the chart's film strip, in the Kartenfilm's scrubber — opens the
**Bildblatt**:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              [ full frame ]              │
│                                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  18.05.2026 · 14:32                      │
│  24,3 °C · 57 % · 1,24 kPa · 980 ppm     │
│  Licht 100 % · Heizung aus · Entf. an    │
│  Tag 47 · Frühe Blüte, Woche 3           │
│                                          │
│  ‹ vorheriges          nächstes ›        │
│  [ Als Foto in die Karte ]               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The numbers come from a single Influx point-in-time query. Swiping moves frame *and* numbers together.
`Als Foto in die Karte` promotes a webcam still into a `CareEvent{kind:'photo'}` — one tap turns a
surveillance frame into a diary photo, which is the cheapest good feature in this document.

**Numbers → frame.** The chart's film strip **is an axis**: thumbnails positioned by real timestamp on the
same x-scale, so uneven capture reads honestly as gaps, sharing one cursor with the panels. Delivered as
**one sprite sheet + a JSON index per window** (256×160 tiles, 50 per window under 15 min of footage, 100
above) rather than N image requests. Dragging the crosshair moves the strip; dragging the strip moves the
crosshair. `Image.timestampEnd` already marks mp4s as spanning a range, so those render as a bracket rather
than a point.

### 8.4 The camera alarm

Checked inside the existing hourly compress loop: if the device is online and the newest
`Image{format:'jpeg'}` is older than 3 h, write a `DeviceLog` with `message-camera-silent`, severity 1, and
fire the existing notification path. Resolves automatically on the next frame. Cost: ~15 lines, and it is
the difference between "the camera died on day 3 of flower" and "the camera died on day 3 of flower and I
found out at harvest".

The card also states it plainly, above the film: `Kamera seit 3 Std still · letztes Bild 11:04`.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution without authorisation.** `DeviceActor` is a device-scoped list of names the owner types once
(`Anna`, `Kai`, `Jo`). No accounts, no invitations, no e-mail, no touch on `auth.middleware.ts`. The
capture sheet shows a chip row `Wer? ( du ) ( Anna ) ( Kai ) ( + )`, defaulting to the last actor used on
*this browser* (`localStorage['tc-actor:'+device_id]`), so the phone that lives in the club room remembers
it is Anna's phone. `actor_id` **and** an `actor_name` snapshot are stamped on the event.

This is honestly labelled in the UI as what it is:
`Namen für die Karte. Keine Konten, kein Login — jeder mit Zugang zum Gerät kann jeden Namen wählen.`
Attribution is a label. It is upgradeable to real memberships later without touching a single stored event,
because `actor_id` is already there. Cost: ~2 days.

**The double-feed problem** is §7.3, and it is the reason this is worth doing at all: the guard needs to
know *who* last fed, and a shared account cannot tell you.

**Cumulative totals, per plant, per person** — the club critic's third gap — is the Bilanz block. Tapping
`Gaben von  Anna 9× · Kai 5× · du 3×` opens the ledger: every contributing event, with actor, dose,
products, EC and the deviation flag. That is "how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had this cycle, and from whom",
answered in two taps from the home screen, and it is the single strongest argument for the per-plant model.

**Conditions** are §6.4 — the thing that gets pinned to a tent door, with an owner and a close.

**Corrections, not edits.** On a device with more than one actor, editing an event does not mutate it: it
writes a correcting event linked by `correctedBy`, and the card renders the original struck through with
the correction under it. This is opt-in by data (it activates when a second actor exists), never global,
and the **owner can still hard-delete**, because an undeletable record victimises an unregulated grower.
The card's footer carries `Karte löschen` and it means it.

**Privacy posture.**
- Harvest weight and plant count are **off by default**. `Ernte eintragen` exists on the card; the fields
  are empty and nothing prompts for them. They are the facts that separate a lawful hobby from an offence.
- The public card link (`/karte/:share_id`, reusing `ShareLink`) **strips actor names to initials** by
  default (`A.`, `K.`), hides harvest data unless separately enabled, and hides plant labels if the owner
  turns off `Pflanzen zeigen`. Expiry is the existing picker.
- No location, no real-name enforcement, no analytics SDK, no community feed. A link you can paste into
  Telegram is not a feed you must moderate.
- Terp Control does **not** become a club's § 26 system of record. No per-member dispensing, no grams
  ledger, no THC content. The card records what happened to a *plant*, not what happened to a *person* —
  which is exactly the line the dossier draws, and the per-plant model sits on the safe side of it by
  construction.

## 10. Old devices (type 7): fan, light, plug

**They do not get the card, and they are not broken.**

A `device_type` of `fan`, `light` or `plug` has no camera, no plants and no feeding. Building a card for it
would be inventing a need. Concretely, what such an owner sees after this redesign:

- **`/list`** — their device tile, unchanged in structure, with two fixes: the type label reads
  `Terp Control Steckdose` / `Lüfter` / `Licht` instead of inheriting `devices.fridge.title`, and an
  unnamed device is no longer *named* "Terp Control FRIDGE GROW". New `devices.controller`, `devices.fan`,
  `devices.light`, `devices.plug` i18n blocks in both files.
- **`/device/:id`** — the existing overview component, with the Vitalzeile freshness treatment from §3.3
  applied (`vor 12 Sek` / `vor 34 Min` / `Offline seit …`). That is the one genuinely valuable thing this
  redesign gives them and it costs nothing to extend.
- **No segment bar.** No `Karten` tab. No `Verlauf` tab change — their chart page is the same page, using
  the same new ECharts renderer with whatever measures their device reports. Small multiples and a
  scrubbing header are strictly better for a plug than 15 hidden axes were.
- **`/device/:id/settings`** — `fan-settings`, `light-settings`, `plug-settings` are untouched, including
  their four raw-key i18n bugs, which get fixed as a separate 30-minute chore
  (`settings.limits.overtemperature.enabled` and friends).
- **`/device/:id/diary`** — for type 7 this does **not** redirect. It keeps the existing entries report
  reading `DeviceLog`, because that is what their history is and rewriting it buys them nothing.

Total type-7 surface change: a naming pass, a freshness line, the chart renderer swap, four i18n fixes.
Nothing they use today moves.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app, sees four numbers with an age on them and the Klimabilanz
block (§3.3) instead of an empty Bilanz: `Temperatur im Ziel 91 %`. That is the "how is it going" answer he
came for. He never creates a plant, never sees the word, never sees a chip row. If he taps `Foto` once out
of curiosity, a silent plant is created and his home screen grows a Verlauf — one row. Nothing else changes.

**2 — Monitoring plus camera.** The card is his product. The Kartenfilm button sits under the vitals, the
cull statistics tell him why 2.100 frames were dropped, and `Kartenfilm speichern` puts a playable H.264
file in his camera roll — which today's libx265 build would not have. Tapping any thumbnail gives him the
Bildblatt with that moment's temperature and VPD, which is the correlation the owner named. He is the type
whose whole experience is the thing that was most broken in round one.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** The chart shows the setpoint band only for measures his
kit can act on: the humidity panel renders without a Soll line when no dehumidifier role is paired, and no
humidity alarm preset is offered. Per-role truth from `hardwareInfo.sockets`, not the three-bucket
`deviceControlCapability()` — which fails open today and shows him targets he cannot reach. Reveal by
capability, no setting involved.

**4 — Full tent.** Sees every panel and the output state lane with every paired role as its own band, so
"heater and dehumidifier fighting each other at 03:00" is visible as overlapping bands rather than
inferrable. Within five socket roles this is honest; the humidifier/exhaust/AC roles he actually wants are
firmware work and this concept does not pretend to deliver them.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Gets the one thing this concept can honestly give him: `CareEvent` is a documented
REST resource with a stable client-generated id, so his script can POST a watering from his own pump
controller and it lands on the card indistinguishably from a human's. `GET /device/:id/events` with a
cursor is his export. It is not the write API for outputs that he ultimately wants, and I am not claiming
it is.

**6 — Club.** The reason the concept exists. Anna feeds A1–A3 at 07:40; Kai arrives at 09:10, taps
`Gefüttert`, and is told Anna already did — one deliberate tap to proceed, recorded as an override. The new
member on Tuesday opens A3's card and reads down: started 14.04., All-Mix so no feed until week 4, topped
on day 24, 118 ml Bio-Bloom total, don't water it because Kai says it's overwatered. Two minutes.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. A correct type name, a freshness stamp, a better chart, four fewer raw
translation keys. Nothing removed.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner:* four ≥56 px verbs on the home screen, two taps to record, sentence line 1
readable at arm's length in a dark tent, and the page never rearranges itself between visits. *Normal:*
one page per plant that looks like a document, a film that plays in the messenger she pastes it into.
*Techie:* every number in line 2 of every row without tapping, EC/pH/runoff inputs on the same sheet as the
save button, cull statistics under the film, a cursor-paginated events endpoint.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026

Six to eight weeks, one or two developers, against a codebase with 38 real tests and no migration tooling.
This is what I would actually commit to.

**Ships in v1**

1. `Plant`, `CareEvent`, `Condition`, `DeviceActor` collections + cursor-paginated events endpoint.
   *(~5 days, includes fixing the missing log pagination.)*
2. The card: header, Bilanz, Verlauf with two-line rows, Verdichtung, the ledger drill-down. *(~8 days)*
3. Home screen = vitals masthead + active card; the three-item segment; freshness/stale/offline states.
   *(~4 days)*
4. Capture sheet: water / feed / photo / note, prefill, actor chips, plant chips, double-feed guard,
   localStorage idempotency queue. *(~6 days)*
5. `FeedRegime` collection seeded with the owner's five named regimes + Lucas/Jack's, prefill and the
   `Fällig` line. *(~4 days)*
6. **Camera:** the libx264 fix; the five cull/normalise rules; `Kartenfilm` build + download endpoint with
   the real filename; per-plant crop with the 25 % floor; the camera-silent alarm; the Bildblatt.
   *(~8 days)*
7. Chart: ECharts migration, small multiples, stepped setpoint + labelled band, day/night from `out_light`,
   output state lane, annotation rail bound to `CareEvent`, scrubbing header. Delete Chart.js.
   *(~10 days)*
8. Naming pass (controller ≠ fridge), the six live i18n raw-key bugs, `de`+`en` for everything new.
   *(~2 days)*
9. Backfill job projecting existing `DeviceLog` diary rows onto cards. *(~2 days)*
10. Flux injection fix + interval-vs-timespan clamp — prerequisite for 7. *(~2 days)*

That is ~51 developer-days. At two developers over seven weeks it fits with about a week of slack, which
is the right amount of slack for a team that cannot lean on a test suite.

**Explicitly NOT in v1, and why**

- **Timelapse stabilisation.** Optical flow on 12 weeks of frames is a research task. v1 cuts the film at
  a detected shift and says so.
- **Cropped rolling timelapses.** Only the nightly Kartenfilm is cropped; the 1d/1w/1m tent lapses stay
  as they are.
- **Service worker / true offline app.** Only the capture sheet is offline-safe, by queue.
- **Real multi-user auth.** `DeviceActor` is attribution, not authorisation, and the UI says so.
- **The public card URL** (`/karte/:share_id`) — the plumbing is designed against `ShareLink` but the
  privacy review it needs (initials, harvest gating, expiry defaults) is not a week-8 activity. v1.1.
- **Markdown/BBCode post generator, Telegram/Discord destinations.** v1.1; the webhook machinery exists.
- **Harvest weights UI** beyond the two opt-in fields. No yield analytics, no run-over-run comparison.
- **Sprite-sheet film strip.** v1 ships the strip with individual thumbnails at low count and the sprite
  endpoint follows in v1.1; N image requests is survivable at 50 thumbnails and not at 500.
- **VPD as an expressible goal.** Display and band only.
- **InfluxDB retention/downsampling.** Named as a launch-week ops task, not a design deliverable.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

**1. Six identical plants produce six identical cards.** The concept's whole premise is per-plant identity,
and the single most common real setup is six clones treated identically. Those six cards contain the same
events, differing only by the odd training note. The rack looks redundant and the "read one card" promise
becomes "read one card, and the other five say the same thing". The `Alle`-by-default selection keeps the
*capture* cost at zero, but I cannot claim the *reading* value is there for a uniform grow. This concept is
strongest for a club room with divergent plants and weakest for a hobbyist SOG.

**2. Per-plant totals are only as good as the selection at capture.** A tired user taps `Alle` every time.
Then every card's Bilanz is identical and the number that justifies the whole model carries no information.
The double-feed guard and the chip row help, but the failure is silent and I have no mechanism that detects
"these totals are uniform, so they are probably not real".

**3. "Two minutes" degrades with grow length.** A 90-day club grow with three actors is several hundred
events. Verdichtung and the 14-day rule cut it substantially, but a long card is still a long card, and the
honest claim is "the top third in two minutes" — header, Bilanz, open conditions and the last fortnight.
The bottom of the card is an archive, not a briefing.

**4. The camera is one fixed wide shot and the crop knows it.** A plant in the back row cropped to 25 % of
a 1280×720 frame is 640×360 upscaled, and it is soft. The 25 % floor is a refusal, not a solution. The
per-card film is genuinely better than one tent film, and it is genuinely not a portrait.

**5. The backfill is a real risk with no tooling.** `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` forks a history when a
strain is renamed and fabricates a plant on a re-veg. Running it once as a migration inherits both bugs and
freezes them into stored `plant_id`s. The read-time adapter limits the blast radius, and unclassifiable
rows land on the tent card, but somebody's history *will* come out split and there is no migration tool to
re-run.

**6. Two home-screen shapes.** With no events the home shows the Klimabilanz; with events it shows the
Bilanz. I defend that as reveal-by-data, but it means the type-1 user and the type-6 user are looking at
structurally different pages, and a support conversation across those two users starts with "which one do
you see".

**7. `Alles ausklappen` is one bad decision away from being a mode.** Today it is a zoom over facts that
are all present in both states. If anyone ever moves a field out of the collapsed row and behind the
toggle, it becomes the thing this round banned. It needs to be written down as a constraint, not left to
taste.

**8. The concept eats the entire diary surface at once.** It deletes the report switcher, the entries
report and the grow report and replaces all three with one page. If the card is wrong, there is no fallback
view left in the product — unlike a concept that adds a tab. That is a deliberate bet and it is the
riskiest thing in this document.
