# C29 — "Der Bericht"

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** the diary exists to produce the report. The report is designed
first; every capture control in the product is a hole in that document.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Terp Control's deliverable is one document per Durchgang — *Der Bericht* — written continuously by the
cloud, printed identically for every customer, and the app is that document being read while it is still
being written.**

Every other concept in this bake-off is a *view over a query*. You pick a timespan, Flux runs, something is
drawn, you leave, nothing remains. That is why the diary is abandoned in week three and why export has never
existed: there is nothing to export, only a query to re-run. C29 inverts the dependency. The artifact is
fixed first — a numbered document with a fixed section order, a fixed table of contents, real German
sentences, figures with captions, footnotes and an appendix — and then the *only* question the interface
answers is: what does this document still not know, and where do I tap to tell it?

That single inversion does three things at once. It gives the club the reconstructable history the owner
asked for ("who fed what, to which plants, when, and how did they react") because a report has to name its
sources. It gives the diary-publisher an object that leaves the product without a login, because a report is
by construction a thing you hand to someone. And it makes capture self-explaining: nobody navigates to a
form, because the form is printed inside the paragraph that is missing its number.

The report's table of contents **is** the app's navigation. There is no Charts tab, no Diary tab, no
Settings page. There are report sections, and the setpoints live inside the section that reports goal
against actual, because the goal is half of that comparison.

## 2. Information architecture

Seven routes total. Four are new; three are existing routes kept alive for old devices.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | unchanged — device list; one device → straight into its report cover |
| `/device/:id/bericht` | **the cover** = home screen. Run header, live picture, offene Punkte, TOC |
| `/device/:id/bericht/:section` | one report section, lazy-loaded. `:section ∈ klima \| licht \| wasser \| pflanzen \| ereignisse \| bilder \| ernte \| anhang` |
| `/device/:id/bericht/:section/abb/:n` | a figure, full screen, with `dataZoom`. The old charts page, reached as a figure |
| `/r/:slug` | **the public report.** No login, no guard. Read-only render of the same document |
| `/device/:id/settings` | kept, unchanged, for `fan \| light \| plug` (device type 7). Not used by `controller`/`fridge` |
| `/device/:id/testmode`, `/shares`, `/account`, `/login`, `/demo` | unchanged |

`/device/:id/charts` and `/device/:id/diary` **301 into the report** with their query string translated by a
back-compat shim (`applyViewParams` gains a `legacyChartQuery → figureState` mapper). Existing share links
and every saved `ChartPreset` keep working — the dossier is explicit that the URL query string *is* the
on-disk format of both, and breaking it is not on the table.

**Navigation model.** The TOC on the cover is the only navigator. Inside a section the header shows
`‹ Bericht    3 / 7` and swiping left/right moves between sections in TOC order — a document has an order,
so the gesture is free. The `ion-menu` stays for Geräte / Freigaben / Konto. No tab bar (a document does not
have tabs), no FAB. A persistent 56 px **Erfassen-Leiste** is pinned to the bottom of the cover and every
section: `[Gießen] [Füttern] [Notiz] [+]`.

**The section list is not fixed — it is derived.** A section exists when the device or the data can support
it. This is the reveal-by-capability rule, applied to a document rather than to a control:

```
klima      device reports temperature|humidity                     (always, for controller/fridge)
licht      out_light in the output set, OR a light socket role
wasser     always for controller/fridge — starts as a gap, not hidden
pflanzen   ≥1 Plant exists for the run                             (absent = untaxed)
ereignisse always
bilder     hardwareInfo.webcam_did is set                          (no camera → no section)
ernte      run.stage ∈ {flowering, drying, curing} OR run.endedAt  (appears when it becomes true)
anhang     always — sources, raw access, device, firmware, export
```

A CO₂-upgrade owner gets a CO₂ block inside Klima; a plug owner gets three sections (§10). Nothing is
hidden behind a toggle; sections that cannot say anything do not exist.

**Real copy, and what changes when the data does.**

*Live* (last sample < 10 min, the app's existing online definition):
`1  Klima    zu 91 % im Zielband` / `24,3 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 kPa` / picture caption `vor 12 Min.`

*Stale* (10–60 min): the sentence **changes tense** and the reading is stamped.
`1  Klima    lag zu 91 % im Zielband` / `Stand 14:02 — vor 38 Min. · 24,3 °C · 58 %`
The picture caption becomes `vor 38 Min.` in `--tc-text-warning`.

*Offline* (> 60 min, or `lastseen` says so): amber rule under the row.
`1  Klima    Gerät offline seit 12:20` / `letzte Messung 24,1 °C · 61 % · vor 2 Std 40`

*Absent* (a fresh device, nothing measured yet):
`1  Klima    noch keine Messwerte` and inside the section a printed gap:
`Sobald das Gerät misst, steht hier der Vergleich von Ziel und Ist.`

*No run started:* the cover reads

their tent. Starting a Durchgang is one sheet: name (prefilled `Durchgang 1`), start date (prefilled today,

current workmode). Four fields, all prefilled, `[Starten]`.

*Camera paired but silent > 6 h:* section 6 reads `6  Bilder    Kamera seit 3 Tagen ohne Bild` in amber and
an entry appears in Offene Punkte. A camera that quietly stopped in week 4 announces itself on the cover.

*Demo / share / view-only:* the Erfassen-Leiste is replaced by a single line
`Nur-Lesen-Ansicht · Freigabe bis 12.12.2026`. Every `[Ziel übernehmen]` button is absent, not disabled.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — Satz · Zahl · Beleg

**The mechanism is a typographic contract, applied to every assertion in the product, and all three layers
are on screen simultaneously. There is no state, no toggle, no preference, and nothing to remember.**

Every claim the report makes is one line built from three parts:

```
Die Temperatur lag in den letzten 7 Tagen zu 91 % im Zielband.                     ⁷
⟨ Ø 24,3 °C · min 18,9 · max 29,4 · Ziel 24/20 ±1,5 · Abdeckung 99 % ⟩
└─ Satz ──────────────────────────────┘ └─ Zahl ──────────────────────┘ └ Beleg ┘
```

- **Satz** — a full German sentence, 16 px, normal weight. It carries the verdict *and* the headline number
  inside the prose, so it is complete on its own. Someone impaired, one-handed, in a dark tent at 02:00
  reads this line and is finished. There is no units-parsing task and no chart-reading task.
- **Zahl** — the supporting numbers, 13 px, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, `--tc-text-muted`, on the
  line *directly beneath*, never hidden, never behind a chevron. Physically present in the same visual
  block. The normal user's eye slides down one line and gets the range.
- **Beleg** — a footnote marker: a 24 × 24 tap target rendered as a superscript number. Tapping it opens a
  bottom sheet titled `Beleg 7` containing: the exact query window in the device's timezone, `n` raw
  samples, coverage %, the aggregation used, the raw series as a table, `[Als CSV]`, and the literal API
  call (`GET /data/series?device_id=…&measure=temperature&from=…&to=…&method=raw`). The techie lives here.

Why this is not a mode wearing a hat, stated plainly:

1. **All three layers render at once, in the same block, for every user, on every screen.** Nothing is
   collapsed, nothing is behind "mehr anzeigen", nothing expands. The only thing a tap adds is *raw data
   that could not fit* — a citation, not a disclosure.
2. **There is no per-user state.** No `localStorage` flag, no onboarding question, no preference. Two users
   on the same account see byte-identical pixels. (The existing `app-settings-expert` key is deleted.)
3. **The layers survive the artifact.** In the printed/exported report the footnote markers are still there
   and the Anhang lists every source. So the layering is a property of the *document*, which is what makes
   it impossible to reintroduce as a mode: you cannot ship a document that is simple for one reader and
   detailed for another.
4. **The ordering never changes.** The TOC is fixed by section number; sections appear and disappear by
   capability, but never reorder. The dossier's warning about adaptive ordering hurting the impaired user
   is respected — nothing floats up.

**The second mechanism, and the one that makes capture disappear: die Lücke.** Where the report cannot say
something, it prints the sentence it *would* say, greyed, names the missing input, and puts the capture
control on the spot:

```
Wasser & Dünger

  Gegossen wurde bisher —.                                     ⟨ noch nichts erfasst ⟩
  Sobald du einmal Gießen einträgst, steht hier die Gesamtmenge
  je Pflanze und wann zuletzt gegossen wurde.

                                                    [ Gießen eintragen ]
```

That is the whole onboarding story. There is no empty state, no tour, no wizard step, no tooltip: the
document explains itself because a half-written report is legible in a way an empty dashboard is not. And a
user who never taps it is untaxed — the section stays one grey paragraph forever and the rest of the report
is unaffected.

## 5. The chart — figures, not a page

A chart is never a destination. It is **Abbildung n** inside the section whose sentences it supports, with a
caption above it and a footnote of its own. Migrated to Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, with a ~50-line
hand-rolled Angular directive (owner decision). Chart.js / ng2-charts deleted.

```
Abbildung 1 — Temperatur und Ziel, Tag 35–42                              ⁷

  °C ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  30 │▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░▓▓▓▓│
     │      ┌────────────────┐          ╭─╮                       │  ← Toleranzband

     │┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┐        ┌┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┐       ┌┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈│  ← Ziel (gestuft, gestrichelt)

  │ Entfeuchter    ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄            ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄              │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  ▲     ▲▲          ▲                 ▲③        ▲               │  Ereignisleiste
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

     │[Bild]│  17,4 °C · 62 % · VPD 0,71 · Heizung an · Ziel 20,0
     └──────┘
```

Concretely:

- **Small multiples, never overlaid.** One panel per physical quantity, ~110 px tall, shared x-axis,
  `axisPointer.link` for one crosshair across all of them. At most two series per panel and only when
  physically coupled (Luft- + Blatttemperatur). The current 15-hidden-axes translucent-area rendering dies.
- **Goal vs actual.** The setpoint is a **dashed stepped line** built from `daynight.day`/`daynight.night`
  (seconds-of-day, UTC → device timezone) stepping between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`. The
  tolerance band is a `markArea` behind it. The band's source is named in the caption, one at a time and in
  this priority: **Alarmschwelle** (the user's own declared limit, hard edge) > **Ziel ± Toleranz** (soft) >
  **VPD-Empfehlung der Phase** (advisory, from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS.vpdRange`). Deviation is a signed fill
  between actual and setpoint — never a fill to the axis.
- **Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0` where available, falling back to the configured schedule, with
  `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` rendered as gradient edges. A failed contactor then shows up as a **missing
  band**, which is a diagnosis rather than decoration. Timezone is explicit — a 12/12 flip near a DST
  boundary must not drift an hour.
- **Envelope, not a mean line.** `aggregateWindow` min/max/mean in one combined response (a new
  `/data/series/agg` returning `{t, mean, min, max}` replaces three round trips). `sampling: 'minmax'` for
  the plot — LTTB smooths away the exact oscillation a bang-bang controller produces — LTTB only for the
  navigator.
- **Outputs go in a state-timeline lane** beneath the value panels. Region length = duration, `null` renders
  as a gap. `out_dehumidifier` is labelled by workmode: `Entfeuchter` in `small`/`full`, `Kühlung` in
  `temp`/`breed`, because one socket has two meanings and the chart must not lie about which.
- **Annotations on a rail below the x-axis**, 16 px, two rows max (row 1 human events, row 2 device events),
  clustered by pixel proximity into 10 px bins with a count badge, severity-ranked. Lifecycle changes get a
  full-height dashed line with a label. `collectLogCategories` already exists and drives the filter chips.
- **Touch rules.** One finger scrolls the page. Two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: {type:'inside'}`). Tap or
  long-press sets the crosshair. **No tooltip** — a pinned scrubbing header sits above the figure with one
  row per panel plus a 64 × 36 camera thumbnail of the nearest frame. Axis labels are never hidden; below
  320 px they drop to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.
- **Colour** Okabe-Ito. Position is the primary channel; colour is decoration, which is itself the
  accessibility win. `darkMode: 'auto'`; the hand-maintained Highcharts dark palette and its
  `MutationObserver` are deleted.
- **Every figure has `[Als Tabelle]`** which flips it to an accessible `<table>` — and that table is also
  what the print stylesheet emits when a figure cannot be rasterised.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

Five new Mongo collections, all keyed by `device_id`, all optional-field, with read-time normalisers and no
required-field migration (the house style; there is no migration tooling).

```ts
GrowRun {                       // "der Durchgang" — the report's subject
  run_id: string; device_id: string;
  name: string;                 // "Blaubeere #3"      (default "Durchgang N")
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stageSince: number;
  mediumId?: string;            // → FeedRegime.medium
  regimeId?: string;            // → FeedRegime
  coverImageId?: string;
  reportSlug: string;           // "blaubeere-3-k7f2q"  — the public URL
  visibility: 'private' | 'link' | 'public';       // default 'private'
  showYield: boolean;           // default false — see §9
  actorMode: 'off' | 'on';      // default 'off'; 'on' shows the actor chips
}

Plant {
  plant_id: string; run_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                // "A1" — the only required field
  strain?: string; source?: 'seed'|'clone'|'unknown';
  startedAt: number; removedAt?: number;
  position?: { row: number; col: number };
}

GrowEvent {                     // the ONE new event collection
  event_id: string; device_id: string; run_id?: string;
  plant_ids: string[];          // [] === "das ganze Zelt"
  type: 'water'|'feed'|'measure'|'train'|'pest'|'transplant'|'photo'|'note'|'harvest';
  at: number; createdAt: number;
  actor_id?: string; actor_name?: string;         // denormalised, survives actor deletion
  data: Record<string, number|string|boolean>;    // per-type, validated server-side per type
  images: string[]; note?: string;
  correctsEventId?: string;     // a correction is a new row, never an edit
  overrideOf?: string[];        // set when the double-feed guard was deliberately overridden
  clientEventId: string;        // uuid minted at tap → idempotency key, see §7
}

Condition {                     // the thing pinned to the tent door
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; run_id?: string;
  text: string; severity: 0|1|2;
  openedBy?: string; openedAt: number;
  closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
  source: 'human' | 'system';   // 'system' = camera_silent, offline, co2_low …
}

RunActor {                      // "wer ist am Zelt" — names, not accounts
  actor_id: string; device_id: string;
  name: string; color: string; active: boolean; createdBy: string;
}
```

`DeviceLog` is untouched and keeps doing what it does (device messages, alarms, recipe steps). The report's
Ereignisse section renders `GrowEvent` and `DeviceLog` merged on one timeline, visually distinguished:
human rows carry an actor chip, device rows carry the device glyph. `convertEventsToGrowCycles` becomes a
**one-time backfill** that mints a `GrowRun` per inferred cycle and stops being a runtime algorithm — no
more forking history by renaming a strain.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed.** `plant_ids: []` is the default and means "the whole tent". The
capture sheets do not show a plant selector until at least one `Plant` exists. The Pflanzen section does not
exist until at least one `Plant` exists. The report prints "Gegossen: 46,0 L in 12 Gaben" instead of a
per-plant table, and that is a complete, correct report. Adding plants is a single sheet in the Pflanzen
gap: `[Pflanzen anlegen]` → a number stepper `4` and a label scheme `A1…A4 ▾` (`A1…`, `1…`, `eigene Namen`)
→ four rows created. Retroactive: existing tent-wide events stay tent-wide, and the report says so:
`vor dem 12.10. wurde nicht je Pflanze erfasst`.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — capture, with tap counts

Two dedicated buttons in the Erfassen-Leiste because these are the two things the owner named. Both are
**prefilled from what happened last time and from the regime**, and every prefilled value stays editable.

### Gießen — 2 taps

```
tap 1: [ Gießen ]                              (Erfassen-Leiste, 56 px, always visible)

┌─ Gießen ────────────────────────────── ✕ ─┐
│                                            │
│   4,0 L        ⟨ wie am 02.11. ⟩           │   ← tap the number → [−] range [+]
│                                            │
│   Alle Pflanzen ▾        A1 A2 A3 A4       │   ← only if plants exist
│                                            │
│   Wer?   ( Anna )  Tim   Sarah   + Name    │   ← only if actorMode === 'on'
│                                            │
│   jetzt · 04.11. 14:37 ▾                   │
│                                            │
│   ⌄ mehr:  Ablauf-EC · pH · Wasserquelle   │   ← one row, always visible, not a mode
│                                            │
│             [        Eintragen        ]    │   ← tap 2
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Stored:** `{type:'water', data:{ volumeL:4.0, source:'tap', runoff:false, ecRunoff?:number,
phIn?:number, ecIn?:number }}`. `volumeL` prefills from the last water event on this run; source prefills
from the run's last value; time is now.

### Füttern — 2 taps, prefilled by the regime

```
tap 1: [ Füttern ]

┌─ Füttern ─────────────────────────────── ✕ ─┐
│                                              │
│   Biobizz All-Mix · Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom       │
│   Schritt 6 von 12 · Blüte Woche 3           │
│                                              │
│   4,0 L                                      │
│   Bio-Grow      2,0 ml/l   =  8,0 ml         │   ← each row tappable
│   Bio-Bloom     4,0 ml/l   = 16,0 ml         │
│   Top-Max       1,0 ml/l   =  4,0 ml         │
│   Ziel-EC 1,6   (+ Leitungswasser 0,4)       │
│                                              │
│   Alle Pflanzen ▾        A1 A2 A3 A4         │
│   Wer?   Anna  ( Tim )  Sarah                │
│                                              │
│   ⌄ mehr:  gemessener EC · pH · Notiz        │
│                                              │
│              [       Eintragen       ]       │   ← tap 2
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Stored:** `{type:'feed', data:{ volumeL:4.0, regimeId, stepIndex:6,
products:[{name:'Bio-Grow', mlPerL:2.0},…], ecTarget:1.6, ecBasis:'delta_over_source',
ecMeasured?, ph?, sourceEc:0.4 }}`. Product names are **plain text**, numbers come from a Mongo content
collection carrying `source_url` + `retrieved_at` + `chart_version`; the Anhang prints
`Düngeplan zuletzt geprüft am 14.08.2026` — the legal posture doubles as a trust feature.

**EC is stored canonically and never rendered as a bare ppm number.** `ecBasis: 'delta_over_source'` exists
because CANNA publishes `EC+`: the chart value plus the tap-water EC. A UI that alarms against the raw chart
number is actively wrong.

**Schedule following.** The regime advances **by feed events, not by wall clock** — growers feed Tue/Fri and
slip. `stepIndex` = count of `feed` events on this run, clamped to the regime length. The Wasser section
prints:

```
Nächste Gabe: Schritt 6 von 12 — fällig seit 2 Tagen.                          ⁹
⟨ letzte Gabe 02.11. · Rhythmus 3 Tage aus 5 Gaben · Ziel-EC 1,6 ⟩

                                                 [ Jetzt füttern ]
```

For **All-Mix** and **BioTabs** the regime's early steps are literally "nicht düngen":

```
Woche 3 · Biobizz All-Mix
Noch nicht düngen — All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt.                             ⁹
Erste Gabe ab Woche 4 vorgesehen.       ⟨ Schritt 0 von 12 ⟩
                                                 [ Trotzdem füttern ]
```

That is the modelling test the owner set, passed literally: a regime that cannot express "All-Mix, so don't
feed yet" is not modelling the domain.

### Double-feed guard — capture SAFETY between people

Evaluated on `[Eintragen]` press, before the write. If any selected plant (or the whole tent, when
`plant_ids` is empty) has a `feed` event within `regime.minGapHours` (default 24 h feed / 8 h water), the
sheet does **not** submit. Instead:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚠  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.   │
│     4,0 L · Bio-Grow 2,0 ml/l                │
│                                              │
│         [ Abbrechen ]  [ Trotzdem eintragen ]│
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

One deliberate extra tap. The resulting event carries `overrideOf: [prior_event_id]`, and the report prints
it as such: `Doppelgabe bewusst eingetragen (Anna 12:07, Tim 13:37)`. A double feed is the most expensive
thing that happens in a shared room, and the record has to show that it was a decision, not an accident.

### Capture in a cellar

`clientEventId` is a uuid minted at the moment of the tap. The POST is retried by a small in-memory queue
plus `localStorage` backup; the server upserts on `clientEventId`, so a retry after a dropped connection
**cannot** double-log — which in a shared record is indistinguishable from a double feed. The sheet closes
optimistically and the event renders with a `⟳ wird gesendet` chip until the server acknowledges. This is
not a service worker and does not claim to be one: the app must be open. `@angular/service-worker` is a new
dependency and a build change, and it is v1.1.

### The cumulative question

The Pflanzen section answers it directly, per plant, from `GrowEvent` aggregation:

```
A3 · Blaubeere · seit Tag 1

Gegossen 11,5 l in 9 Gaben, zuletzt vor 2 Tagen.                             ¹⁰
Bio-Bloom gesamt 148 ml aus 11 Gaben — 7 von Anna, 4 von Tim.                ¹¹
⟨ Ø 13,5 ml je Gabe · letzte 04.11. · Ziel-EC zuletzt 1,6, gemessen 1,7 ⟩
Ein offener Punkt: nicht gießen, überwässert (Tim, 03.11.).
```

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is section 6, it is the section people will show other people, and it ships a video in v1.

### 8.1 What the report contains

**Der Streifen** — one representative frame per day of the run, laid out horizontally on the run's day axis
so an interruption reads as a visible gap, with the day's headline numbers under each frame:

```
  Tag 38    Tag 39    Tag 40    Tag 41    ─── Lücke ───    Tag 44    Tag 45
 ┌──────┐  ┌──────┐  ┌──────┐  ┌──────┐                   ┌──────┐  ┌──────┐
 │      │  │      │  │      │  │      │   Kamera ohne     │      │  │      │
 └──────┘  └──────┘  └──────┘  └──────┘   Bild            └──────┘  └──────┘
  24,1°     24,4°     25,9°     24,0°                      23,8°     24,2°
  58 %      57 %      51 %      59 %                       60 %      58 %
                        ⚠                                    ⚑
```
`⚠` marks a day whose climate left the band; `⚑` marks a day with a human event. Tapping any frame opens
**der Moment**: the full frame, every sensor value at that timestamp, output states, the events within
±30 min, and `[Zur Abbildung]` which jumps to the figure with the crosshair set there.

**Der Film** — the whole run so far as one mp4, rebuilt nightly, with a play button and `[Film sichern]`.

### 8.2 Frame culling — the content problem, solved concretely

Nightly job `buildRunFilm(run_id)`. All of it is buildable with the server's existing dependencies: `sharp`
is already a dependency and `ffmpeg` is already shelled out to (`image.service.ts:567`).

1. **Lights-on only.** Keep only frames whose timestamp falls inside a measured `out_light > 0` interval
   (Influx), falling back to `daynight.day..night` from `device.configuration`. This deletes the single
   largest garbage class — black frames — and it also removes the magenta-LED-versus-daylight problem's
   worst half, because everything kept is under the same lamp.
2. **Ramp exclusion.** Drop frames within `max(lights.sunrise, 15 min)` of lights-on and
   `max(lights.sunset, 15 min)` of lights-off. Dimming ramps produce a brightness sweep that reads as a
   flicker at 25 fps.
3. **Candidate grid.** Take the frame nearest each full hour inside the on-window. A 12 h photoperiod gives
   12 candidates/day. (Retention thinning already guarantees at least 1/h after 90 days, so the grid
   degrades gracefully rather than breaking.)
4. **Quality score, per candidate, via `sharp`:**
   - `stats()` → reject if luma mean < 25 (black) or > 235 (blown) or channel stdev < 8 (fog, lens covered,
     door closed onto the lens).
   - **Focus proxy:** `resize(320)` → `convolve()` with a 3×3 Laplacian → `stats().channels[0].stdev`.
     Reject the bottom 10 % of *this run's own* distribution — self-calibrating, no magic constant that is
     wrong for someone's camera.
   - **Disturbance (a hand, an open door, a moved pot):** 16 × 16 grayscale downsample, mean absolute
     difference against the previous kept frame. If MAD > 4 × the day's median MAD → mark `disturbed`.
     `disturbed` frames are **excluded from the film but kept in the Streifen with a `⚑`**, because "was war
     da los am Tag 33" is exactly what a grower wants to see.
   - **Near-duplicate:** MAD < 0.5 % of full scale and ≥ 6 frames already kept that day → skip.
5. **Keep** the best-scoring 8 frames/day for the film and the single best for the Streifen. 84 days ≈ 670
   film frames ≈ **27 s at 25 fps**. As a run gets long the per-day keep drops to 4 then 2 so the film stays
   under 900 frames (see the storage cliff in §13).

### 8.3 Normalisation and stabilisation

- **Stabilisation, translation-only, no OpenCV.** Downsample each kept frame to 128 × 72 grayscale, brute-force
  search `dx,dy ∈ [−12,12]` minimising sum-of-absolute-differences against the run's reference frame
  (625 offsets × 9 216 px ≈ 5.8 M byte ops — about 10 ms/frame in Node). Scale the winner back up and
  `sharp().extract()` a 92 % centre crop shifted by `(dx,dy)`. If the best SAD is still above a threshold,
  the camera genuinely moved: open a `camera_moved` Condition, re-anchor the reference frame, and mark a
  hard cut in the film — which is honest, because pretending day 12 and day 13 are the same viewpoint is a
  lie.
- **Exposure and white balance.** Per frame, compute per-channel means over the **centre 60 %** (excludes the
  lamp itself). Target = the rolling median of those means over the previous 7 kept days. Apply
  `sharp().linear(gain, offset)` per channel, gain clamped to `[0.75, 1.35]`, and smooth the gain temporally
  with an EMA (α = 0.2) so one odd frame cannot flash. This is grey-world WB plus luminance matching. It
  will **not** turn a magenta LED into daylight; it *will* stop the film strobing, which is the actual
  watchability killer over 12 weeks.
- **Burn-in.** Reuse the existing SVG-composite technique (`image.service.ts:127-160`, already shipping) to
  stamp each frame: bottom-left `Tag 42 · 04.11.2026`, bottom-right `24,3 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 kPa`. On by
  default, one toggle in the Bilder section. **This is image correlation made permanent** — the numbers ride
  inside the video and survive being forwarded into a Telegram group, where no app of ours is running.

### 8.4 How the video LEAVES the product

- **Encode:** `ffmpeg -framerate 25 -f image2 -i %d.jpeg -vf scale=1280:-2 -c:v libx264 -preset medium
  -crf 21 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart out.mp4`.
  **A real bug this fixes:** the existing timelapses are encoded `libx265` (`image.service.ts:581`), which
  Chrome on Android and Firefox cannot decode. Today's timelapse is unplayable for most of the audience. The
  report film must be H.264 + `yuv420p`.
- **Store** as `Image { format:'mp4', duration:'run', timestamp: run.startedAt, timestampEnd: now }` —
  reusing the existing collection and adding one value to the `duration` enum. Unique index
  `{device_id, format, timestamp, duration}` already fits.
- **Serve:** `GET /report/:run_id/film.mp4?token=<imageToken|share_id>` →
  `Content-Type: video/mp4`, `Accept-Ranges: bytes` (so it scrubs inline as well as downloads),
  `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="TerpControl_Blaubeere-3_Tag42_1280x720.mp4"`.
- **The button.** `[ Film sichern ]` in the Bilder section:
  - Desktop / Android Chrome: a plain authenticated download.
  - iOS Safari, where `<a download>` on a cross-origin URL does nothing: fetch the blob and call
    `navigator.share({ files: [new File([blob], name, {type:'video/mp4'})] })` — iOS 16+ opens the share
    sheet with "Video sichern" straight to the Camera Roll. Guarded by `navigator.canShare?.({files})`.
  - Fallback: open the URL in a new tab with the line `Video lange antippen → „Video sichern"`.
- **The public report** at `/r/<slug>` embeds it as `<video controls playsinline preload="metadata">` with a
  visible download link, **no login**. A share that produces a URL requiring a login is not a share.

### 8.5 Correlation, both directions

| Direction | Where | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| chart → picture | any figure | the pinned scrub header carries a 64 × 36 thumbnail of the nearest frame; dragging the crosshair changes it |
| picture → numbers | Streifen, Moment | a frame prints its own numbers; the Moment card shows every measure and output state at that timestamp |
| picture → chart | Moment | `[Zur Abbildung]` opens the figure with the crosshair on that timestamp |
| picture → forever | Film | the numbers are burned into the frames |

Delivery is one sprite, not N requests: `GET /report/:run_id/strip.jpg` (256 × 144 tiles, one per day) plus
`GET /report/:run_id/strip.json` (index + per-day numbers). One request per report load, cached by
`If-None-Match`, rebuilt when the nightly job runs.

### 8.6 The camera alarm

A `Condition{source:'system', text:'Kamera seit 3 Tagen ohne Bild'}` opens automatically when no `jpeg` has
been stored for > 6 h while `lastseen` says the device is online, and mails through the existing
`alarmService` notification path. It closes itself when a frame arrives. It appears on the cover in Offene
Punkte and in the Bilder section as `Der Film hat eine Lücke von Tag 41 bis 44`. Implemented as a Condition,
**not** as an Influx alarm — `VALID_SENSORS` is an allowlist and widening it is not worth it here.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution without an auth rewrite.** `RunActor` is a list of names the owner types once, scoped to the
device. No accounts, no invitations, no e-mail, no `auth.middleware` surgery. The capture sheets show a chip
row with the last-used name preselected; the chosen `actor_id` **and** a denormalised `actor_name` are
stamped on the `GrowEvent`. Turning it on is one switch in the Anhang: `Mehrere Personen am Zelt` — and
that switch changes the **document**, not the interface's complexity level: the chips appear in the capture
sheets and the reports gain a `von wem` column. With one person the chip row does not render at all.

**Honest boundary, stated in the UI.** Under the actor list: *"Namen sind eine Zuordnung, kein Zugang. Wer
eintragen soll, braucht bis auf Weiteres den Zugang des Besitzers."* Attribution is a label; authorisation is
a gate; v1 ships the label. `actor_id` is deliberately shaped so that a v2 membership row can adopt it
(`RunActor.user_id?` added, existing events keep resolving by `actor_id`). Everyone else reads the run
through the report share link, which is genuinely useful: a club member on their own phone sees the whole
report, the film and the open points, read-only, no account.

**Double feed** — §7. **Cumulative per plant, per person** — §7. **Conditions** — §6, printed at the top of
the cover because a standing fact outranks a number.

**Privacy posture, per run, defaulting to the safe side:**

- `visibility: 'private'` by default. `'link'` mints the unlisted `/r/<slug>`. `'public'` only adds a
  `noindex` removal — there is no directory, no feed, no discovery. A link you can paste is not a feed you
  must moderate.
- `showYield: false` by default. Harvest weight and plant count are the exact facts that separate a lawful
  hobby from an offence. They are recordable, they are in the private report, and the public report replaces
  them with `Ertrag nicht veröffentlicht` unless the owner flips the switch, which carries the explicit line
  *"Erträge und Pflanzenzahl im öffentlichen Bericht zeigen"*.
- Every export offers `Namen: vollständig / pseudonymisiert / anonym`, defaulting to **pseudonymisiert** on
  anything with a public URL (`Mitglied 2`).
- Not built, and named as not built: per-member dispensing records, membership numbers, dates of birth,
  addresses, § 26 KCanG filings, any `KCanG-konform` badge. Terp Control is the device-attested
  environmental layer a club's own compliance software consumes, not the club's system of record.
- Corrections are new rows (`correctsEventId`), not edits — an append-only history without cryptographic
  immutability, because immutability protects a *regulated* club and victimises an unregulated grower. The
  account owner can delete a run and everything under it, including its images.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Monitoring only.** He starts no Durchgang and is never asked to. His cover reads `Kein Durchgang · Das
Gerät misst seit 12.03.` with a Zeitraum picker, and three sections: Klima, Ereignisse, Anhang. What he
gets that he does not have today is a *verdict with an age on it* — `Die Temperatur lag zu 91 % im
Zielband` and `Stand 14:02 — vor 38 Min.` — plus a chart that finally draws his own alarm thresholds. He
never sees a dehumidifier tile he cannot act on, because Klima's control block only renders the roles his
`hardwareInfo.sockets` actually carries.

**2 · Monitoring with a camera.** He is the reason section 6 exists. His cover leads with today's frame; his
Streifen is the fastest way anyone has ever answered "is it growing"; and on day 42 he taps `[Film sichern]`
and has a 27-second 720p H.264 file in his camera roll with the temperature burned into it. He shares it
into the Telegram group as a video, not as a link to a login page. His correlation question — "what was it
doing when it looked like that" — is answered by tapping the frame.

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** The Klima section shows a target row and a state-timeline
lane **only for the roles he has paired**. A heater-only tent gets a temperature target, a heater trace and
no humidity target, no humidity-deviation warning and no dehumidifier alarm preset — because capability is
read per role from the sockets CSV, not collapsed into three buckets that fail open. If he has the CO₂
upgrade a CO₂ block appears in Klima with its own sentence, its own figure and the refill events on the
rail; if he does not, there is no CO₂ anywhere in his report.

**4 · Full tent.** He gets the most sections and the densest Klima: temperature, humidity, VPD and CO₂ as
four small multiples on one crosshair, a state lane per socket role, and the deviation fill that tells him
which of his kit is fighting which. He edits day/night setpoints inside the section that reports them, which
is where he was looking anyway. Honest limitation he will notice immediately and the report does not hide:
only five socket roles exist, `out_dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler, and there is no humidifier — the
report labels the socket by workmode rather than pretending, and the Anhang says plainly which of his kit
the controller cannot express.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** He goes straight to a Beleg footnote, finds the exact API call printed next to the
data it produced, and copies it. The Anhang has `Rohdaten` with the series endpoints, the CSV export for
every section and the report's own JSON (`GET /report/:run_id.json` — the whole document as data, which is
also the first published grow-diary interchange schema). He is the user who most benefits from the
citation-not-disclosure rule, because he never has to hunt for a "raw" toggle: every number in the product
already carries its source.

**6 · Grow club.** Actor chips on, four names typed once. Every water and feed carries who. The Pflanzen
section answers "how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had this cycle, and from whom" without anyone computing it. The
double-feed guard stops the expensive mistake at the moment it would happen and records the override when
someone means it. Open points are pinned to the top of the cover where the tent door would be. And the club
reads it all on their own phones through a link, which is as far as v1 goes — the report is honest that
names are attribution and not access.

**7 · Old device owner.** §10. Three sections, his existing settings page untouched, his thresholds drawn
on a chart for the first time, and three raw translation keys fixed. He is not migrated, not upsold and not
shown a single concept from the rest of this document.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **A report is retrospective; a hot tent at 02:00 is not.** The person whose temperature is 31 °C right
   now needs an alarm, not a document. Offene Punkte on the cover carry that entire load, and they are the
   thinnest part of the design. If alerting is not fixed in parallel — consolidation, severity, snooze,
   repeat-until-resolved — this concept is a beautiful record of a dead plant.
2. **Acting is second class.** Everything is a document, so "turn the light on now", test mode and
   maintenance mode end up in the Anhang. A user who wants to poke the hardware has further to walk than
   today. Defensible for a controller that is supposed to run itself; genuinely worse for anyone who
   tinkers daily.
3. **The tense-changes-with-freshness idea costs real i18n money.** German word order means
   `liegt / lag / lag zuletzt` are three separate strings per claim, not one string with a swapped verb, and
   there is no ICU support in the bundle. It roughly doubles the `report.*` key count and it is the sort of
   elegance that gets quietly dropped in week six.
4. **Footnote numbers are unstable across time.** A device that gains a CO₂ sensor grows a section and every
   later footnote renumbers. A PDF printed in October and one printed in November cite the same evidence
   under different numbers. Mitigated by keying belege internally by stable id, not fixed.
5. **The report presumes a run, and type 1 does not have one.** The date-range fallback works but is
   thinner than a dashboard, and a monitoring-only user may reasonably feel the product is built for
   somebody else. This is the type most likely to prefer C06-style tiles.
6. **Film storage has a hard cliff.** A Mongo document is capped at 16 MB. 720p CRF 21 at 900 frames lands
   around 8–14 MB — comfortable for 12 weeks, *not* comfortable for a 20-week run or for anyone who wants
   1080p. v1 mitigates by shrinking the per-day frame keep as a run lengthens, which means long runs get a
   visibly choppier film. GridFS is the correct fix and it is deferred.
7. **The image pipeline is heuristics with no test corpus.** Grey-world white balance, a Laplacian focus
   proxy and SAD stabilisation will look wrong on somebody's tent — a white grow tent with one purple bar
   is close to the worst case for grey-world. The only honest mitigation shipped is a `Rohbilder` toggle
   that renders the film with no normalisation at all.
8. **Attribution without authorisation is half a feature, and clubs will feel the half that is missing.**
   The record becomes correct while access stays wrong: to capture, a member still needs the owner's login,
   which is exactly the shared-password practice everyone already hates. The actor picker makes it *look*
   like multi-user, which arguably makes the gap worse, not better.
9. **Six regimes is not most growers.** The grower whose brand is the seventh gets "Eigenes" and free text —
   and free text does not prefill, does not schedule and does not export. The feature's whole promise
   ("it thinks for me") evaporates for that person until authoring ships.
10. **Deleting the Simple/Expert segment removes something that works today.** `docs/einfach-modus.md` is 106
    lines of a shipped, documented feature with users. `Satz · Zahl · Beleg` is a better answer in theory and
    an untested one in practice, and the people who currently rely on Simple mode will meet a denser screen
    on day one. That is a real risk taken deliberately, because the owner banned the mode.
