# C13 — "Belegt"

**Family:** camera · **Centre of gravity:** every frame carries its conditions.

---

## 1. Thesis

**A picture that does not carry the numbers that were true when it was taken is decoration; Belegt stamps
every frame at capture, so the photograph becomes the record, the search index and the export.**

Terp Control already takes a still every 30 seconds and already knows, at that same second, the temperature,
humidity, VPD, light level, output states, stage and day of run. Today those two facts pass each other in the
corridor: the JPEG goes into `images`, the numbers go into InfluxDB, and nothing ever joins them. Belegt
joins them **at the moment of capture, in the image document**, and then builds the whole product on top of
that one join.

Once a frame carries its conditions, three things that are hard become easy. **Search:** "zeig mir jedes Bild
über 28 °C" is a Mongo query, not an analysis. **Sharing:** a still that leaves the app can carry its proof
burned into the pixels, because a JPEG in Telegram has no sidecar. **The diary:** an entry made from a frame
inherits the frame's numbers verbatim — the app does the diary keeping because the camera already did it.
And the timelapse stops being a novelty loop: it becomes a 30-second film of a plant with its own climate
ticking underneath it, which is the single most shareable object this company can make and which nobody in
this market ships.

The word is deliberate. *Beleg* is German for a receipt, a voucher, a piece of evidence — the thing you keep
because it proves what happened. Not *Bild*. Not *Foto*.

## 2. Information architecture

Four surfaces per device plus one object that opens on all of them. No page hides a feature from anyone.

```
/list                          Geräte        device list (unchanged; one device → straight to Jetzt)
/device/:id/jetzt        NEW   Jetzt         the live frame and its conditions; the home screen
/device/:id/charts             Band          time as an axis: film strip + series + outputs, fused
/device/:id/diary              Buch          the record: entries, plants, feeds, Zettel, Bilanz
/device/:id/settings           Plan          goals, kit, alarms, camera, scheme, actors
   ↳ modal, from anywhere:     Beleg-Karte   one frame, its conditions, and everything reachable from it
   ↳ sheet, from Band/Beleg:   Export        still or timelapse leaves the product here
```

**Route paths are unchanged for `charts`, `diary`, `settings`.** `ShareLink.page` is a persisted
`'charts' | 'diary'` union and every share link in circulation carries a frozen query string against those
paths (dossier §1.4, §1.6). Belegt renames them in the UI only. `jetzt` is new and absorbs what `/list`
renders today when exactly one device is claimed.

**Navigation model.** Today: one `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu`, no tab bar, no back button, cross-navigation
through six outline buttons. Belegt adds a **fixed four-item bar pinned to the bottom of every device page**
— implemented as an `ion-footer` inside a new `DeviceShellComponent`, *not* `ion-tabs` (which would fight the
split-pane and the share/demo/public shells). On viewports ≥ 992 px the same four items render in the left
menu under the device name and the footer is hidden. The menu keeps Geräte / Geteilte Links / Konto.

Everything below is reachable in at most two taps from Jetzt.

### 3.2 Real German copy for Zeile 1

Zeile 1 is generated from at most three clauses, in this order, joined by ". ":

| condition | copy |
| --- | --- |
| |T − goalT| ≤ 1,5 and |RH − goalRH| ≤ 7 | `Passt.` |
| T > goalT + 1,5 | `Warm.` |
| T > goalT + 4 | `Zu warm.` |
| T < goalT − 1,5 | `Kühl.` / `Zu kühl.` (−4) |
| RH > goalRH + 7 | `Feucht.` |
| RH < goalRH − 7 | `Trocken.` |
| no goal configured (monitor-only) | `Gemessen.` |
| `light > 0` | `Licht an.` else `Licht aus.` |
| run known | `Tag 41, Blüte.` else `Kein Lauf angelegt.` |

Never more than one climate clause; temperature outranks humidity. Zeile 2 is always the full numbers,
always in the same order, always present. **Nobody is denied the number and nobody has to read it** — that
is the whole hierarchy, done with type size rather than with a toggle.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — "der Wert ist der Griff"

Belegt serves the stoned user and the techie with one interface through **two layered mechanisms, neither of
which hides anything and neither of which persists any state.**

### 4.1 The stamp is a fixed three-line stack, always fully rendered

Verdict, numbers, freshness. Same three lines on Jetzt, in the Beleg-Karte, on every diary entry, in the
pinned scrub header on Band, and burned into every export. The stoner reads line 1 and stops because a
five-word German sentence in 17 px bold beats six numbers for attention without anyone deciding it should.
The techie reads line 2 and taps into the raw series. **Nothing is collapsed, nothing expands, there is no
"mehr anzeigen", and a screenshot of any of these surfaces contains the same information for both people.**

The verdict is never alone and the number is never alone. That kills the two failure modes at once: an
opaque green dot (stoner-friendly, techie-hostile) and a naked number (the reverse).

### 4.2 Every rendered value is a live handle — and there is no filter UI anywhere

**Press and hold any number in the app for 400 ms.** The app narrows to the moments where that value held,
and shows you the frames.

- Hold `28,4 °C` on Jetzt → a chip appears at the top: `Belege über 28 °C · 214 Bilder`, and the strip on
  Jetzt, the film strip on Band, and the photo grid in Buch all re-cull to matching frames. Lift and tap
  the chip's ✕ to clear. The chip is the only state; it is per-view, never persisted, never shared.
- Hold `Blüte` → `Belege in Blüte`.
- Hold `Anna` on a diary entry → `Einträge von Anna`.
- Hold `Licht 100 %` → `Belege bei Licht an`.
- Drag while holding, before lifting, adjusts the threshold: a small readout follows the finger
  (`über 27,0 °C … über 29,5 °C`). Lifting commits. Dragging off the value cancels.

This is the techie's query language and it has no query language. **There is no search screen, no filter
drawer, no "Erweitert", no saved-filter management** — the interface for asking a question is made out of the
answer to the previous one.

**Why this is not a mode.** It creates no persistent preference, no per-user setting, no branch in the
render tree, and no second version of any screen. Two people looking at the same device see the same thing.
It is undone by lifting a finger or by one tap on a visible ✕. It cannot leave you somewhere you do not
recognise.

**The defence of the impaired user, which this round demands.** A hidden long-press that is the *only* route
to a feature is exactly the kind of cruelty this brief is against. So it is not the only route:

1. Every grippable value carries a **1 px dotted underline** in `--tc-text-info` at 40 % opacity. Not a
   button, not a chip — a texture. It is the same affordance everywhere and it costs no space.
2. The same narrowing is reachable by **tap**, from the `Auffällig` row that appears under the strip on Band
   whenever the visible window contains an excursion: `[über 28 °C · 214] [Licht aus · 640] [Tag 41 · 48]`.
   Those are ordinary tappable chips.
3. Holding is the safest gesture available to someone impaired at 2 a.m.: it commits nothing until release,
   it is cancelled by moving, and it never destroys data. Compare it to the alternative — a settings screen
   whose state a stoned user cannot see they are in.

The mechanism the brief warns about — adaptive reordering by behaviour — is **not used anywhere**. Belegt's
ordering is fixed. What floats is attention, not layout.

`ng2-charts` and `chartjs-adapter-luxon` are deleted in the same commit.

Panels share one x-axis via `axisPointer.link` and `echarts.connect()`. One finger scrolls the page, two
fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap or long-press sets the crosshair. Axis labels are **never** hidden
below 320 px — they shorten to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.

### 5.2 Goal vs actual

- **Setpoint**: a dashed stepped line, drawn from the parsed `device.configuration` — it steps at
  `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`. Server returns the
  parsed setpoints on the device access payload so the client does not re-parse a JSON string.
- **Target band** (`markArea`), one source at a time and labelled in the panel title so it is never
  ambiguous: alarm thresholds (`Alarmgrenzen`) > setpoint ± tolerance (`Ziel ±1,5 °C`) > stage VPD band
  (`Blüte 1,2–1,5 kPa`, from the existing `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS.vpdRange`).
- **Deviation fill**: signed fill *between* actual and setpoint, warm above / cool below. Never a fill to the
  axis — today's `type:'area', threshold:null` encodes nothing.
- **Envelope**: the primary measure is drawn as `min/max` band + `mean` line, using
  `sampling: 'minmax'` on the plot. A single mean line is a lie about a bang-bang controller.
- **Day/night** (`markArea`): derived from measured `out_light > 0` where present, falling back to the
  `daynight` schedule, in an explicit named timezone. A failed contactor shows as a **missing band** — a
  diagnostic rather than decoration.

### 5.3 The correlation, both directions

- **Chart → picture.** Scrub anywhere; the film strip's highlighted tile and the pinned header follow.
  The header shows **`Image.conditions` for the nearest frame**, not the resampled series value. Those
  numbers are exact: they were written when the shutter fired. When the nearest frame is more than one
  aggregation window away, the header dims and appends `≈ 4 Min. daneben`.
- **Picture → chart.** Tap a tile → Beleg-Karte. Tap any number on that card → Band opens at that instant
  with that measure's panel expanded.
- **Picture → search.** Hold any number → §4.2 narrowing; the strip re-culls in place.
- **Delivery.** Thumbnails come as **one sprite sheet + one JSON index per visible window** — 160×90 tiles,
  10×10 grid, `GET /image/:device_id/strip?from=&to=&cols=10`, cached one hour. Never N image requests.
  `Image.timestampEnd` already marks mp4s as spanning a range: those render as a bracket, not a tile.

The Auffällig row, the annotation rail (v1.1) and the VPD temperature×RH matrix with the last-24 h
trajectory (v1.1) hang off the same x-axis machinery.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant — "Buch"

### 6.1 New entities

`DeviceLog` stays exactly as it is for device events. Diary entries move to their own collection, because
per-plant foreign keys, arrays and server-side pagination cannot be layered onto a flat `Mixed` bag.
`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a **one-time idempotent backfill on boot**, not a runtime algorithm.

```ts
interface Run {                      // was: inferred in the browser by string comparison
  run_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                     // "Lauf 3" default, editable
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stageSince: number;
}

interface Plant {                    // optional. Absent → the tent is the subject.
  plant_id: string; device_id: string; run_id: string;
  label: string;                     // "A1" — short, because it is a chip
  strain?: string; medium?: string; potLitres?: number;
  startedAt: number; archivedAt?: number;
  coverImageId?: string;             // the Beleg that represents this plant
}

interface Entry {
  entry_id: string;                  // uuid GENERATED ON THE CLIENT — the idempotency key
  device_id: string; run_id?: string;
  plant_ids: string[];               // [] means "the whole tent"
  kind: 'water'|'feed'|'photo'|'note'|'measure'|'stage'|'train'|'harvest';
  at: number; actorId?: string; note?: string;
  imageIds: string[];
  conditions: FrameConditions;       // §8.1 — stamped at save, same shape as a frame's
  water?: { litres: number; phIn?: number; ecIn?: number; runoffPh?: number; runoffEc?: number };
  feed?: { schemeId?: string; step?: number; litres: number; ecTarget?: number;
           items: { product: string; mlPerL: number }[] };
  measure?: Partial<DiaryEntryData>; // the five legacy fields keep working
  deletedAt?: number;                // real deletion semantics, unlike `DeviceLog.deleted`
}

interface Actor  { actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string; colour: string;
                   createdAt: number; archivedAt?: number }
interface Zettel { zettel_id: string; device_id: string; text: string; plant_ids?: string[];
                   openedByActor?: string; openedAt: number;
                   closedByActor?: string; closedAt?: number }
```

Server-side pagination from day one: `GET /entries/:device_id?before=&limit=50&kind=&plant=&actor=`.

**Every entry carries its own thumbnail and its own three-line stamp.** That is the "it does the diary
keeping for you" promise made literal: the human typed `Gegossen`, and the record says gegossen at 14:20 on

that second. **Nothing else in this market writes that row for you.**

Days with no human entry collapse to one line `nur Kamera (288 Belege)` — the camera days are still there,
still searchable, and not noise.

### 6.3 How a user who ignores plants is untaxed

`plants.length === 0` ⇒ **no plant UI is rendered anywhere.** No selector in the capture sheet, no chips on
entries, no filter in the Buch header, no plant column in exports. `plant_ids: []` is a first-class value
meaning "the whole tent", not a null to be nagged about. There is no onboarding step that asks about plants
and no empty-state card advertising them.

Plants appear when a plant exists. The only route in is the capture sheet's plant row, which itself only
appears once you have named one — and the only route to naming one is `Plan → Pflanzen → Pflanze anlegen`,
or the natural moment: the first time two people share a device and someone needs to say *which* plants they
watered. That is reveal-by-data, and it means a solo grower with one tent never sees the word "Pflanze".

## 7. Watering & fertilising — the capture flow

### 7.1 Tap counts, counted honestly

The `+` button is 64 px, bottom-right, thumb-reachable one-handed. It opens a sheet of four 88 px targets.

| what you want | taps | sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **watered, nothing else** | **2** | `+` → `Gießen` — saves immediately with prefills |
| **watered, different volume** | **3** | `+` → `Gießen` → `[5 L]` from the preset row (auto-saves 800 ms after the last touch) |
| **fed, following the scheme** | **2** | `+` → `Düngen` — the scheme's current step is already filled in |
| **fed, edited dose** | **4–5** | `+` → `Düngen` → tap `4 ml/L` → stepper → `Speichern` |
| **photo + note** | **3** | `+` → `Foto` → shutter (device frame or phone camera) → note is optional, `Speichern` |
| **with plants, 3 of 6** | **+2** | the plant chip row is in the sheet; tapping a chip is one tap each |
| **stage change** | **3** | `+` → `Notiz` → `Blüte` from the stage row |

The prefills that make 2 taps honest:
`at` = now · `plant_ids` = whatever was selected last time on this device (shown as chips, one tap to
change) · `litres` = the last volume used on this device · `actorId` = the last actor at this device ·
`conditions` = stamped automatically · `feed.items` = the active scheme's step for the current run week ·
`imageIds` = the device frame nearest to `at`, attached automatically when a camera exists.

**Every prefill is visible in the sheet before you save, and every one is one tap from being changed.** The
grower who deviates is the normal case.

### 7.2 Schemes — selected once, then they prefill

`Plan → Schema` is a two-row picker, phrased the way growers talk:

```
Medium          [ Biobizz Light-Mix ▾ ]
Düngelinie      [ Biobizz Bio-Grow / Bio-Bloom ▾ ]

  Light-Mix ist leicht vorgedüngt. Düngen ab Woche 2.
  Quelle: biobizz.com · geprüft 14.07.2026
```

Picking `All-Mix` instead changes that line to `All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt. Erst ab Woche 3–4 düngen.` and
the scheme returns **no items** for weeks 1–3: `+ → Düngen` in week 2 on All-Mix shows
`Diese Woche laut Schema nicht düngen.` with a `Trotzdem eintragen` button. A scheme that cannot say
"don't feed yet" is not modelling the domain.

Stored in a Mongo content collection, never in the bundle. Brand names are plain text labels. Every scheme
carries `source_url` + `retrieved_at`, rendered as the `geprüft` date — a legal posture that doubles as a
trust feature.

Reminders (the "it thinks for me" face): if the active scheme's step is due and no `feed` entry exists for
this run week, Jetzt shows one row above the strip: `Düngen fällig — Woche 5` with a single
`Eingetragen` button. That is the same 2-tap save with the tap already made.

### 7.3 The double-feed guard

At save, the server checks: any `feed` (or `water`) entry with an overlapping `plant_ids` set, on this
device, within `cloudSettings.guardHours` (default: feed 12 h, water 6 h), by **any actor**. If found, the
save button becomes:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ┌────┐  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30      │
│ │▮▮▮▮│  gefüttert.                       │   ← Anna's Beleg from that moment
│ └────┘  Bio-Grow 4 ml/L · 2,0 L          │
│                                          │
│      [ Abbrechen ]  [ Trotzdem ]         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

One deliberate extra tap to proceed. The photograph is the point: you are not reading a claim that someone
fed, you are looking at the tent when they did. This is the cheapest place in the whole product where the
camera prevents an expensive mistake.

### 7.4 Offline

`entry_id` is a client-generated uuid and the write is `PUT /entries/:entry_id` — **idempotent by
construction**, so a retry can never double-log, which in a shared record is indistinguishable from a double
feed. v1 queues in `localStorage` and flushes on `online`; this covers the real case (the app is already
open, you walk into a cellar, you log, you walk out). A `@angular/service-worker` install for
cold-start-offline is v1.1 and is called out as such rather than implied.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is the concept. Everything above is downstream of it.

### 8.1 The Beleg — conditions written at capture

One new sub-document on `Image`, written in `imageService.readFromRtspStreams()` at the existing
`imageModel.create({...})` call (`server/src/services/image.service.ts:226-236`):

```ts
interface FrameConditions {
  v: 1;
  t?: number; rh?: number; vpd?: number; co2?: number; lux?: number; ppfd?: number;
  light?: number;            // out_light 0..100
  day?: boolean;
  out?: { heater?: 0|1; dehumidifier?: 0|1; co2?: 0|1 };
  stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage; runId?: string; dayOfRun?: number;
  goalT?: number; goalRh?: number;
  src: 'live' | 'influx';    // live = the device's last MQTT status; influx = reconstructed
  age?: number;              // ms between that status sample and the shutter
}
```

Source: a 5-line in-memory `Map<device_id, StatusMessage>` populated in
`deviceService.statusMessage()` (`device.service.ts:552`) — the status message arrives on MQTT anyway and is
currently thrown away after being written to Influx. Cost per frame: zero queries. On a cold server the map
is empty for a few seconds; the fallback is `dataService.getLatest()` and the frame is stamped `src:'influx'`.
`age` and `src` are the honesty fields — a stamp from a 4-second-old sample is evidence; one reconstructed
from a 5-minute Influx window says so.

Indexes: `{device_id, 'conditions.t'}`, `{device_id, 'conditions.vpd'}`, `{device_id, 'conditions.stage'}`,
`{device_id, 'conditions.dayOfRun'}`. Four new indexes on a collection that already carries the JPEG bytes —
the honest cost is in §13.

**Thinning deletes the stamp with the pixels, deliberately.** A stamp without a picture is telemetry, and
Influx already has that. Belegt does not build a second time-series store.

Diary entries get the same shape, from the same map, at save time. `user/jpeg` uploads get it too — so a
phone photo taken in the tent carries the tent's conditions.

### 8.2 What ships in v1: a timelapse someone can actually hold

Today the server builds three rolling mp4s hourly (1d / 1w / 1m) with
`libx265 -crf 30` at 25 fps (`image.service.ts:567-600`). **They exist and they are close to unusable as a
share artefact**, for one blunt reason: HEVC in MP4 does not play reliably in Chrome or Firefox on Android
or desktop, and it is a poor attachment for Telegram and Discord.

v1 makes four changes and gets a real product out of an existing pipeline:

1. **Codec.** `libx264 -profile:v main -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 23 -movflags +faststart -r 30`,
   `-vf scale=1280:-2`. Two lines. This is the difference between a file and a video someone can hold.
2. **A sampler with a rule.** Target 600–1350 frames (20–45 s at 30 fps), spread **evenly over the requested
   range but only within lights-on**, snapped to the nearest surviving frame. Concretely, an 81-day run at
   ~8 frames per lit day = 648 frames = 22 s. A single day = every surviving frame in the lit window ≈ 1,400
   = 47 s. Three named presets in the export sheet: `Tag`, `Woche`, `Ganzer Lauf`.
3. **Culling and normalisation** — §8.4.
4. **The stamp burned into the pixels** — §8.3 — which is what makes it a *Terp Control* timelapse rather
   than a timelapse.

Long renders are a job, not a request: `POST /timelapse/jobs {device_id, from, to, preset, stamp, res, fps,
plant_ids}` → `{job_id}` → `GET /timelapse/jobs/:job_id` → `{state:'queued'|'running'|'done'|'failed',
progress, url}`. The existing `pLimit(10)` and the 15-minute ffmpeg timeout apply unchanged.

### 8.3 The burn-in

Bottom bar, 13 % of frame height, `rgb(13,14,18)` at 78 %, two lines of DejaVu Sans (the font already used by
`addOfflineOverlay`):

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                │
│                 [ the frame ]                  │
│                                                │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 12.06.2026  14:22           Tag 41 · Blüte     │
│ 28,4 °C    51 %    VPD 1,64 kPa    Licht 100 % │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Implementation is a variation of the proven `imageService.addOfflineOverlay()` — `sharp().composite()` with
an SVG string (`image.service.ts:127-159`). About 40 lines. **The numbers change per frame**, so in the
finished video the climate ticks under the plant: humidity drops at lights-on, VPD climbs through the day,
the day counter walks up. That single detail is the whole selling point, it is buildable in a sprint, and no
competitor ships it.

Options in the export sheet: `Daten einbrennen` (an/aus, default an) · `Namen einbrennen` (default **aus**,
§9) · `Uhrzeit` (default an) · `Gerätename` (default aus).

In-app the stamp is **not** burned in — it is rendered in the DOM, so it is crisp, selectable, translatable
and grippable (§4.2). Burn-in exists because a JPEG that leaves the product has no sidecar.

### 8.4 Making the content watchable

A tent timelapse is mostly garbage. In order of application:

1. **Dark-frame cull — free, and it removes half of every 12/12 run.** Drop frames with
   `conditions.light < 5`. This is a Mongo predicate on data we already stored: no pixels are read. The
   ubiquitous "half the video is black" failure is solved by the stamp existing.
2. **Transient cull (hand in shot, door open, torch).** Compute mean luminance of a fixed central crop per
   frame (`sharp().stats()`, ~4 ms). Drop a frame whose luminance deviates > 35 % from the rolling median of
   its 7 neighbours **and** whose neighbours agree with each other. At 30-second cadence a hand is a one- or
   two-frame event; a sunrise ramp is not.
3. **Near-duplicate cull.** Already handled by the existing `minFrameIntervalMs` walk in
   `compressRtspStreamRange` (`image.service.ts:313-397`). Kept.
4. **Exposure and white-balance normalisation.** Per frame, mean R/G/B of the central crop; compute gains
   against the rolling 24-frame median; clamp each to ±20 %; apply with `sharp().linear(a, b)` per channel.
   No new dependency. This is what stops magenta LED / daylight-through-an-open-door flicker across 12 weeks.
5. **Stabilisation is explicitly NOT in v1**, and Belegt does not pretend otherwise. Instead it ships the
   cheap, honest half: when mean absolute frame difference spikes and **stays** shifted for ≥ 3 consecutive
   frames, the server writes a diary entry `Kamera wurde bewegt` with the two frames attached. The user finds
   out why their timelapse jumps on day 12, from the product, without watching it. `deshake` is v2.

Every cull is reversible: the export sheet has `Ungefiltert` for the user who wants all 24,000 frames. It
costs a second render and says so.

### 8.5 How a video leaves the product

Round one's named failure. Exactly:

- **Path.** `GET /image/:device_id/timelapse.mp4?job=<job_id>` with
  `Content-Disposition: attachment` and `Content-Type: video/mp4`, served same-origin, authenticated by the
  existing cookie or by the 30-day `imageToken` so a share link works.
- **Filename.** `terpcontrol_zelt-1_lauf-3_tag-1-81_2026-08-20.mp4` — device name slugified
  (`[^a-z0-9]+ → -`, lowercased, 24 chars), run label, day range, render date. Never a uuid.
- **Format.** H.264 main, yuv420p, `+faststart`, 30 fps, 1280×720 default (`1920×1080` and `854×480`
  offered). An 81-day run at 648 frames ≈ **22 s, ~11 MB**.
- **Onto the phone.** Two buttons, both real:
  - `Teilen` uses `navigator.share({ files: [new File([blob], name, {type:'video/mp4'})] })`. On iOS 15+
    and Android Chrome this is the native share sheet, including *Save to Photos* / *In Galerie speichern*.
    **This is the only route to a camera roll from a web app and Belegt uses it.** ~12 lines.
  - `Herunterladen` is a plain `<a download>`. Android Chrome → `/Download`. iOS Safari → Files → the user
    shares from there. The button's subtitle says which, per platform, rather than pretending.
  - `navigator.share` is feature-detected; where absent only `Herunterladen` renders.
- **Size honesty in the sheet.** `~11 MB · 22 Sek.` computed before render. If it exceeds 10 MB the sheet
  adds: `Für Discord (kostenlos, max. 10 MB): 720p bei 24 B/s wählen.` Telegram's 2 GB is never a problem.
- **A still leaves the same way.** Beleg-Karte → `Teilen` → a 1080×1350 JPEG with the stamp bar, filename
  `terpcontrol_zelt-1_tag-41_2026-06-12-1422.jpg`. That is the object that gets pasted into Telegram.

### 8.6 The camera alarm

The brief is right that nobody gave the camera an alarm. A new alarm `sensorType: 'camera_silence'` with
`thresholdSeconds` (default 3 h), evaluated by a watchdog in `imageService` on the existing hourly compress
tick, reusing `alarmService`'s existing email/webhook/info actions. Fires `Keine Kamera-Bilder seit 3 Std`,
resolves on the next successful frame. Created by default when a camera is first paired — a camera that
stopped three days into flower tells you.

## 9. Clubs

Belegt's answer is **attribution without accounts**, and it leans on the camera for the part that matters.

**Who is at the tent.** `Plan → Personen` is a list of names the owner types once. No invitations, no
emails, no `auth.middleware` surgery, no membership collection in v1. The capture sheet's top row is
`Wer? [Anna][Max][Kai][+]`, sticky per device in `localStorage`, stamped onto `Entry.actorId`. Two days of
work, and it delivers the thing clubs actually ask for.

**Authorisation is untouched and Belegt says so plainly.** `Device.owner_id` is still one string; everyone at
the tent is using one login. Attribution is a label, not a gate — the UI never implies otherwise (the picker
says `Wer trägt ein?`, never `Anmelden als`). Real memberships are a later migration: `actor_id` is already
the foreign key they will attach to.

**The double feed** — §7.3. The soft block with the other person's Beleg attached is the single most valuable
thing in this section.

**The cumulative question.** `Buch → [Pflanze A3] → Bilanz`:

```
A3 · Lauf 3 · Tag 41
Gegossen   14 ×   28,5 L      Anna 9 · Max 5
Gedüngt     6 ×               Anna 4 · Max 2
  Bio-Grow      84 ml
  Bio-Bloom     36 ml
Letzte Gabe  vor 1 Tag 3 Std
EC zuletzt   1,4 (Ziel 1,4)
```

One aggregation over `entries`. Nobody surfaces this today and every data model could compute it.

**Zettel — the standing condition.** Not an event, not a task: an open fact with an owner, exactly what gets
taped to a tent door. `+ → Notiz → Als Zettel anheften`. It renders pinned at the top of Jetzt and Buch until
someone closes it, and the close records `closedByActor`/`closedAt`. Examples the copy uses:
`CO₂-Flasche fast leer` · `Steckdose 3 wackelt` · `A3 nicht gießen, übergossen`.

**Privacy posture.** Private is the default and it is the strong one.

- **Actor names are never burned into an export by default.** `Namen einbrennen` is off, and the toggle
  carries the line `Ein eingebrannter Name lässt sich nicht zurücknehmen.` Belegt invented burn-in, so
  Belegt owns this.
- Export offers `Namen: vollständig | Initialen | keine`, defaulting to **Initialen**.
- No harvest weight or plant count is ever prompted; both are fields you can fill, never fields that ask.
- No real-name enforcement, no location, no analytics SDK, no community feed. Sharing is a link and a file,
  never a publish-to-network.
- `DELETE /entries/:id` and `DELETE /image/:image_id` (which exists and is never called today) are wired to
  real buttons, and `Plan → Daten löschen` wipes entries, plants and images for a device in one action. The
  record is the user's, including the right to destroy it.

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

A `fan` / `light` / `plug` owner opens the app and sees:

**Jetzt.** The same four-item bar, the same three-line stamp, the same skeleton. No camera is paired, so the
hero is the Zustandskachel with that device's own measures — a `light` shows `Licht 60 %` and `Tag/Nacht`, a
`plug` with a slave sensor shows `21,4 °C · 48 %`, a `fan` shows `1.840 U/min`. Zeile 1 for a device with no
climate goal reads `Gemessen.` and never invents a verdict. Zeile 3 is freshness, which these devices need as
much as anything else — this is the fix for the #1 complaint across the whole category.

**Explicitly removed for them, by reveal-by-capability and reveal-by-data:** no film strip, no Belege row, no
timelapse, no export sheet, no plant chips, no scheme picker, no double-feed guard, no Bilanz, no Personen
row. Not greyed out — **absent**, because `hardwareInfo` says there is no camera and because `plants.length`
is 0. There is no empty state advertising a feature they cannot have.

**Band** renders the chart with no strip panel: series, setpoint where one exists, day/night for a `light`,
the output lane. Their measures are already in `VALID_SENSORS` / `VALID_OUTPUTS`.

**Buch** works fully. They can log notes and photos from the phone, and those photos get stamped with
whatever the device measures — a plug's 21,4 °C is a genuine Beleg. Export produces a Markdown/text post and
a JPEG.

**Plan** loads the existing `fan-settings` / `light-settings` / `plug-settings` components **unchanged**.
Belegt does not touch them, does not rewrite them, and does not put them behind anything new.

**What actually gets fixed for them regardless of Belegt:** the type label. A controller today renders
`{{'devices.fridge.title'|translate}}` → "Terp Control FRIDGE GROW", and an unnamed controller is *named*
that. Belegt's device shell reads the type properly and a `devices.controller` i18n block is added in both
languages. That is the cheapest credibility fix in the project and it lands in week one.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Monitoring-only, lazy.** Opens Jetzt, reads five German words and a freshness line, closes it. His
hero is the Zustandskachel; his Band is a clean small-multiple chart with a target band on it, which is more
than he has today. Belegt costs him nothing and gives him the one thing he actually lacks: knowing whether
what he is looking at is current.

**2 — Monitoring with a camera.** This is Belegt's user and the product is built for him. His Jetzt *is* his
tent. His Band scrubs pictures against numbers. Every eight weeks he taps `Ganzer Lauf`, waits ninety
seconds, and gets a 22-second H.264 file with his climate ticking under his plant, on his phone, in his
gallery, with one share sheet. Nothing else on the market gives him that file.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂, heating).** The kit row shows only the sockets he actually paired,
because `hardwareInfo.sockets` says so — no dehumidifier tile, no humidity nag, no alarm preset he cannot
act on. His deviation row names the goal he actually set. The per-role truth already exists in the CSV and
Belegt is the first screen to use it outside the one-time wizard.

**4 — Full tent.** He gets the most complete kit row, the output state-timeline lane on Band, and a
setpoint-vs-actual picture per measure. Belegt is honest that it cannot give him what the firmware does not
have: there is still no humidifier role, no exhaust-fan role, and `dehumidifier` still doubles as the cooler.
It does not invent a UI for hardware that does not exist. What it adds is the ability to *see* his controller
working: the output lane against the deviation fill is the first view in this product that shows regulation.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Gets the least. He gets one thing he cannot get elsewhere:
`GET /belege/:device_id?from=&to=&t_gt=&stage=` returning frames-with-conditions as JSON, plus the sprite
index, which is a genuinely novel dataset — images keyed to climate. That is one endpoint and an OpenAPI
page. The read/write control API he really wants is not in this concept and Belegt does not claim it.

**6 — Club / shared tent.** Names without accounts, attribution on every row, a double-feed guard that shows
him the other person's photograph, a Bilanz that answers "how much has A3 had and from whom", and Zettel for
the facts that belong on the tent door. Belegt refuses to hold per-member dispensing records and says so.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Nothing is broken, the fridge label is fixed, freshness arrives, and every
camera feature is absent rather than teasing.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **Belegt makes the worst part of the storage architecture load-bearing.** Images are Mongo `Buffer`s
   capped by the 16 MB BSON limit, with a unique index that collides on same-millisecond writes and no
   GridFS. Belegt adds four indexes to that collection, reads thousands of documents per export, and adds a
   sprite endpoint that reads a hundred at a time. The right fix is object storage; it is not in this scope,
   and every performance problem this concept has will trace back to that sentence.
2. **Burn-in is irreversible and Belegt invented it.** A stamped still carries stage, day of run, climate
   and possibly a name. Under a prohibition regime that is a self-incriminating artefact that its author
   published by tapping twice. Defaults mitigate (names off, initials in exports, an explicit warning
   string). A wrong tap does not un-happen.
3. **The stamp lends authority to whatever the probe says.** "28,4 °C" burned into a photograph reads as
   fact. If the sensor is 15 cm under the lamp it is fiction with a serif. Belegt has no calibration story,
   no sensor-placement guidance and no plausibility check, and it makes bad data look like evidence.
   This is the criticism I would lead with.
4. **Roughly half the buyers have no camera** (€289 vs €319–349), and for them the centre of gravity is
   gone. Jetzt degrades honestly into a Zustandskachel and the diary still stamps entries, but a
   camera-family concept sold to a customer without a camera is a concept about a feature they did not buy.
   The counter-argument — that this is exactly what should drive the camera bundle — is a marketing answer
   to a design problem.
5. **Thinning silently truncates the past.** Frames older than 90 days survive at one per hour. "Zeig mir
   jedes Bild über 28 °C" therefore under-reports the past by a factor of 120 versus yesterday, and the
   query returns a confident number. Belegt renders `aus 41 von ~2.880 Bildern` under every narrowed result,
   which is honest but ugly, and a user who does not read it will draw a wrong conclusion.
6. **The culling heuristics will throw away real frames.** A genuinely dark room, a legitimately fast
   change, a grower who works in the tent daily at the same hour — all read as transients. `Ungefiltert`
   is the escape hatch and it is a v1.1 item, which means v1 has a filter with no off switch for the general
   case. That is a real defect and I am not going to dress it up.
7. **The most distinctive half of the no-modes mechanism ships second.** With only the `Auffällig` chips in
   v1, Belegt's answer to "one interface for everyone" reduces to the three-line stamp plus reveal-by-
   capability — good, defensible, and less differentiated than the document argues.
8. **Schedule risk is severe.** Four new collections, a backfill with no migration tooling, a render job
   queue, an ECharts migration, a new device shell, and two languages, in six to eight weeks, against 38
   `it()` blocks of which 24 are `should create`. Something in §12 will not land. The list is ordered so
   that what slips is chart polish and the hold gesture, not the stamp — but that is a plan, not a promise.
9. **Type 5 is barely served and type 4 is served only by being told the truth.** Belegt gives the DIY
   closed-loop grower one JSON endpoint and gives the full-tent grower a better view of hardware limits it
   cannot lift. Neither of those is a reason to buy.
