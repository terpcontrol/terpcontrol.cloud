# C33 — "Rangfolge"

**Family:** unify · **Centre of gravity:** everything is present, ordered by what matters now.
German-first (de-DE). No hiding, no modes — just ordering.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The device screen is a priority queue of fully-expanded cards whose order is a pure, published,
deterministic function of the device's state — never of who is looking — so the person who reads two
centimetres and the person who reads two metres are reading the same screen at different depths.**

Every other approach to "serve the stoner and the techie" either hides something (a mode, an accordion,
a "show more") or asks the user to choose a view. Rangfolge does neither. All twelve cards are on the
page, all of them fully open, all of their raw numbers visible without a tap. The only thing that varies
is **the order**, and the order is computed from the tent, not from the person. The stoned user at 2am
reads card 1 and puts the phone down. The techie scrolls to card 12, which is a table and a curl command.
Neither was denied anything; neither was forced to read anything.

The obvious objection is the one the brief names: adaptive ordering hurts impaired users. Correct — which
is why Rangfolge is **not adaptive**. It does not learn, does not personalise, does not A/B. It is a step
function over five urgency bands with a **constant tie-break order inside each band**, dwell requirements
in both directions, and a hard rule that the screen never rearranges while you are looking at it. Section 4
is the whole defence, in numbers.

When nothing is wrong, band 0 puts the camera at rank 1: **the top of the screen is a picture and a
verdict.** That is the calm state, and it is the state the product is in 95 % of the time.

---

## 2. Information architecture

There are five device screens and one account area. Navigation is by **card**, not by tab: each card ends
with the one or two links that belong to it. That is the whole model — you go to the chart of the thing you
were just reading about, from the thing you were just reading about.

```
/list                          Geräte        (unchanged; single device → straight to /device/:id)
/device/:id                    Rangfolge     NEW. The home screen. Replaces the fridge overview.
/device/:id/verlauf            Der Verlauf   the chart (was /charts; old path 301s, query format preserved)
/device/:id/buch               Das Buch      diary + plants + events (was /diary)
/device/:id/filme              Die Filme     NEW. camera, strip, timelapses, downloads
/device/:id/ziele              Die Ziele     goals/settings (was /settings; per device_type as today)
/account, /shares, /demo, /login, /classes, /testmode   unchanged
```

The `ion-menu` stays exactly as it is. **No tab bar is added.** To stop that becoming a trap, the bottom of
the Rangfolge — below card 12 — carries a plain `Alles auf diesem Gerät` block listing all five screens,
and every sub-page's back button returns to the Rangfolge with the originating card scrolled into view and
briefly outlined.

Sub-pages inherit the ranking. **The chart panels on `/verlauf` appear in the same order as the cards on
the Rangfolge.** The app has exactly one ordering and uses it everywhere. That is why the concept is called
Rangfolge and not "Karten".

## 3. The home screen

### 3.1 The card catalogue

Fixed, public, twelve entries. `ordnung` is the constant tie-break inside a band. A card is *present* when
its subject exists — reveal by capability and reveal by data, both explicitly permitted by the brief.

| # | `karte_id` | `ordnung` | Present when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `bild` | 10 | `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is set |
| 2 | `klima` | 20 | the device reports `temperature` or `humidity` |
| 3 | `licht` | 30 | `out_light` exists, or a `light`/`secondary_light` socket role is paired |
| 4 | `lueftung` | 35 | `device_type === 'fan'` (reports `rpm`) |
| 5 | `co2` | 40 | `deviceHasCo2(device)` or a `co2` socket role is paired |
| 6 | `giessen` | 50 | ≥1 `Ereignis` of type `giessen`/`duengen` exists, **or** a `Regime` is chosen |
| 7 | `pflanzen` | 60 | ≥1 non-archived `Pflanze` exists |
| 8 | `zettel` | 70 | ≥1 open `Zustand` exists |
| 9 | `steckdosen` | 80 | `parseSocketRoles(hardwareInfo.sockets).length > 0` |
| 10 | `plan` | 90 | a `Recipe` is running, or a stage preset was applied |
| 11 | `verbindung` | 100 | always |
| 12 | `rohdaten` | 120 | always — **always last, in every state** |

`rohdaten` is pinned to the bottom by construction: it is excluded from banding and always sorts last. It
is the honesty valve. It lists, in plain German, **which cards are not present and why**:

> **Nicht auf diesem Gerät**
> CO₂ — kein CO₂-Sensor verbaut (Upgrade)
> Pflanzen — noch keine angelegt · *Pflanze anlegen →*
> Zettel — kein offener Zustand

So "reveal by data" never means "the techie cannot find it". Everything absent is named, with its reason
and, where applicable, the way to make it appear.

### 3.2 Card anatomy

Every card, without exception, has the same five zones top to bottom. This is the second half of the
no-modes mechanism: the *card* is progressive precision vertically, exactly as the *page* is.

```
 ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ ①  KLIMA                                        28,4 °C   │  Rang · Titel · Leitzahl
 │    Zu warm. Seit 40 Min über dem Ziel.                    │  das Urteil, ein Satz
 │    Rang 1: Abweichung größer als 1,5 °C.                  │  die Begründung (immer da)
 │    ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐      │
 │    │  ▁▂▃▅▇▇▇▆  · Sollband hinterlegt · 6 Std      │      │  Beleg (Sparkline/Bild)
 │    └───────────────────────────────────────────────┘      │
 │    Soll 25,0 · Ist 28,4 · Abweichung +3,4 °C              │
 │    rF 54 % (Soll 50 %) · VPD 1,84 kPa · Taupunkt 18,3 °C  │  die Rohwerte, ohne Tap
 │    Letzte Messung vor 12 Sek                              │  das Alter, immer
 │    Verlauf öffnen →          Ziel ändern →                │  Wege (max. 2)
 └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Zone 1 answers the stoner. Zone 5 answers the techie. Nothing between them was hidden and nothing needs a
tap. A card is typically 190–260 px tall on a 390 px-wide phone; twelve of them is roughly five screens of
scrolling, which is the concept's honest cost (§13.1).

A **Rangleiste** runs down the right edge: twelve 6 px dots, coloured by band, the current one filled.
Tapping a dot jumps to that card. It is a scrollbar that means something, and it is the only concession to
the length of the page.

The camera is rank 1 in the calm state by construction (`ordnung 10`), and the calm state is most of the

to win an argument for screen real estate, it wins by default and yields only to an actual problem.

If there is no camera, `klima` is rank 1 and its Beleg zone is the sparkline instead of a picture.

### 3.4 Stale, offline, absent

**Stale (2–10 min old).** Numbers stay black. The age line changes: `Letzte Messung vor 4 Min`. Nothing is
promoted — 4 minutes is not news.

**Offline (`Date.now() - lastseen > ONLINE_TIMEOUT`, i.e. 10 min — `device.service.ts:46`).**
`verbindung` promotes to band 900 and becomes rank 1:

```
① VERBINDUNG                     seit 2 Std 14
  Der Controller meldet sich nicht.
  Rang 1: keine Daten seit 2 Std 14 Min.
  Zuletzt gesehen: heute 11:42 · Firmware 1.9.4
  Letzter Wert davor: 25,1 °C · 52 % · Licht an
  Was du prüfen kannst: Strom, WLAN am Zelt, Router.
```

Every other card keeps its position from the calm order, keeps its last known values in **grey with an
explicit `Stand: heute 11:42`**, and replaces its Urteil line with `Kein aktuelles Urteil möglich.` No card
is removed and no card lies.

This is enforced by an explicit rule, **Ursachen-Unterdrückung** (§4.4): when the cause of missing data is
itself ranked, nothing downstream of that cause may be promoted. Three "temperature sensor dead" alarms
stacked on top of one "device offline" is the single most common way a ranked screen becomes noise, and it
is designed out rather than tuned out.

**Absent (device claimed, never reported).** All present cards render with `Noch keine Messung.` and no
Beleg zone. `verbindung` is rank 1 with the setup path: `Gerät verbinden →`. `rohdaten` explains that the
catalogue will fill itself as the device reports hardware.

**Share/demo/public.** `resolveDeviceAccessInfo` already yields `locked`, `canEdit`, `webcamAllowed`,
`chartsAllowed`. Cards render identically; the Wege zone drops any link the token does not permit, and
write buttons (`Gegossen`, `Erledigt`, `Ziel ändern`) are absent rather than disabled. The Personenwähler
in the header is hidden for share/demo sessions.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

The mechanism is **ranked density with a frozen tie-break**, and the reason it is not a mode is that
**nothing on the screen is a function of the viewer**. Two people opening the same device at the same
second see byte-identical screens, in the same order, with the same numbers. There is nothing to
configure, nothing to remember having configured, and nothing that drifts.

### 4.1 The ranking function, in full

```
rang = sort_by( band DESC, ordnung ASC )      // rohdaten excluded, always last
```

`band` is a five-valued enum, not a score. There is no continuous urgency number anywhere in the system.

| Band | Wert | Bedeutung | Promotion rule (per card) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AUS` | 900 | Die Anlage meldet sich nicht | no data for the card's subject beyond its silence budget |
| `ALARM` | 800 | Ein vom Nutzer gesetzter Alarm steht an | any `Alarm.isTriggered === true` whose `sensorType` maps to this card |
| `ABWEICHUNG` | 600 | Ist weicht vom Soll ab | measured outside `[soll − tol, soll + tol]` for ≥ Δ↑ |
| `FÄLLIG` | 400 | Etwas ist zu tun | due date reached, confirmation waiting, consumable low |
| `NEU` | 200 | Etwas ist fertig | a new film / new machine-written entries, not yet opened |
| `RUHE` | 0 | In Ordnung | everything else |

Concrete per-card rules, all server-computable from data that already exists:

```
verbindung  AUS   Date.now() - device.lastseen > 10 Min
bild        AUS   newest Image{format:'jpeg'} older than 120 Min
            NEU   a Film exists with opened_at == null
klima       AUS   temperature/humidity null or NaN for > 15 Min while device online
            ALARM alarm.sensorType ∈ {temperature, humidity} and isTriggered
            ABW   |ist − soll| > 1,5 °C  or  > 7 % rF   (the tolerances grow-assistant-card
                  already uses)  — or VPD outside the stage vpdRange for ≥ 60 Min
licht       ABW   measured out_light state disagrees with the configured photoperiod
                  (daynight.day / daynight.night, tz-explicit) for ≥ 15 Min
co2         ALARM alarm.sensorType ∈ {co2, co2_valve} and isTriggered
            FÄLL  bottle estimate from co2FillingRest below 10 %
giessen     FÄLL  next due date ≤ today (regime step, or the user's own interval)
pflanzen    FÄLL  a plant's stage_seit exceeds its stage's expected span by > 14 d
zettel      FÄLL  ≥1 open Zustand with dringend === true
steckdosen  AUS   a paired socket unreachable ≥ 15 Min
plan        FÄLL  a RecipeStep with waitForConfirmation is waiting
buch/rest   RUHE
```

### 4.2 Why the tie-break is constant, and what that buys

**Inside a band, order never depends on a magnitude.** A temperature card 3,4 °C off and a humidity card
0,1 % off do not compete; they are both `ABWEICHUNG`, and `klima`(20) precedes `licht`(30) because 20 < 30,
today, tomorrow and in eighteen months. Two cards never swap because a number crept past another number.

The calm order is therefore a fixed, learnable sequence:

> **Bild · Klima · Licht · CO₂ · Gießen · Pflanzen · Zettel · Steckdosen · Plan · Verbindung · Rohdaten**

A user learns it once. Deviations from it are *the news*, and the news is always at the top. That is
strictly more legible than a tile grid, in which position is constant but urgency is invisible, and
strictly more predictable than a scored feed, in which position is a continuous function of noise.

### 4.3 Dwell in both directions

Promotion requires the condition to hold; demotion requires it to fail for longer. Asymmetric on purpose —
a problem should be slow to leave the top.

| Band | Δ↑ (promote after) | Δ↓ (demote after) |
|---|---|---|
| `AUS` | immediate at the silence budget | 2 consecutive good samples |
| `ALARM` | governed by the alarm's own `thresholdSeconds` (already exists, `alarm.service.ts:365`) | on `isTriggered → false` plus `cooldownSeconds` |
| `ABWEICHUNG` | 20 Min continuous | 30 Min continuous inside tolerance |
| `FÄLLIG` | immediate | on the action being logged |
| `NEU` | immediate | on open, or after 7 days |

A single 5-second spike never reorders anything. A grow light coming on and warming the tent for eight
minutes never reorders anything.

### 4.4 Ursachen-Unterdrückung (cause suppression)

> **Ein Rang ohne Daten ist keine Aussage.**

If `verbindung` is in `AUS`, no other card may exceed `RUHE`. If `klima` is in `AUS` (sensor dead), no
temperature/humidity `ALARM` or `ABWEICHUNG` may be raised, and `co2`'s VPD-derived states are suppressed
too. The suppressed card says so: `Unterdrückt: keine Verbindung.` This is one line in the ranking service
and it is the difference between a priority queue and an alarm storm.

### 4.5 Der Riegel — the screen does not move while you look at it

The order is computed **on screen entry, on pull-to-refresh, and on opening a push notification.** It is
*not* recomputed by the 30-second live poll. The live poll updates the numbers inside cards — because
stale numbers are a lie — but it **never** reorders.

When the poll finds that the order would change, a single 44 px bar appears under the header:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⟳  Klima weicht jetzt ab. Neu ordnen     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

You tap it, or you don't. If you don't, the affected card still shows its real state and its real Urteil
in place — the information is never withheld, only the *movement* is deferred to a moment you chose. For
band `AUS` and `ALARM` the bar is red and carries the card's name; for `NEU` it is grey and can be ignored
forever. Nothing on this screen ever animates under a thumb.

### 4.6 What is explicitly refused

- **No behavioural adaptation.** "What you touch floats up" is rejected outright. It would make the screen
  a function of the viewer, which is the thing this concept exists not to be.
- **No collapse, no accordion, no "mehr anzeigen".** Every card is fully expanded, always.
- **No density preference, no compact mode, no per-user card hiding, no drag-to-reorder.**
- **No onboarding question about what kind of user you are.**

The cost of that refusal is real and stated in §13.6: a user who genuinely never cares about CO₂ scrolls
past the CO₂ card forever.

## 5. The chart — Der Verlauf

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision). Chart.js /
ng2-charts / chartjs-adapter-luxon deleted. Panels appear **in Rangfolge order**, which means the panel you
came for is on top and the rest are where you expect them.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│ Längste Abweichung 2 Std 40 · Abdeckung 97 %           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Decisions, briefly:

- **Goal vs actual.** Dashed **stepped** setpoint from `daynight.day`/`night` + `day.temperature`/
  `night.temperature`; a `markArea` soft band at setpoint ± tolerance; a signed deviation fill *between
  actual and setpoint*, never to the axis. One band source at a time, labelled: alarm thresholds (hard)
  > setpoint ± tolerance (soft) > stage `vpdRange` (advisory).
- **Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the schedule, with ramp gradients and an
  **explicit timezone**. A *missing* band where the schedule says there should be one is hatched and
  labelled `Licht nicht bestätigt` — the same fact the `licht` card promotes to `ABWEICHUNG`.
- **Envelope.** `{mean,min,max}` from one combined query, `sampling: 'minmax'` for the plot (LTTB smooths
  away the oscillation a bang-bang controller exists to produce), LTTB only for the navigator.
- **Annotations** in a 2-row rail under the axis (row 1 human `Ereignis`, row 2 machine `DeviceLog`), 10 px
  clustering with count badges, chips from the existing `collectLogCategories`, full-height dashed lines
  for lifecycle changes. Tap, not hover.
- **Touch:** one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom, tap sets the crosshair. Axis labels are
  never hidden; below 320 px they drop to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.
- **Correlation:** the crosshair drives the Bildstreifen and the pinned header together, delivered as one
  sprite + JSON index per window, not N image requests. Colour: Okabe-Ito.
- **Prerequisites, non-negotiable:** parameterise the Flux query (`measure`, `from`, `to`, `interval` are
  interpolated raw today, `data.service.ts:80-89`), move `limit()` before `yield()`, clamp `interval` by
  `timespan`. Time-in-range must be computed **server-side on raw samples** — computing it on
  `aggregateWindow(fn:mean)` output destroys the excursions it exists to count. The URL query format is
  preserved verbatim so existing presets and share links keep working.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant — Das Buch

New Mongo collections. All fields optional-by-default with read-time normalisers; no migration tooling
exists, so nothing becomes required.

```ts
interface Pflanze {                   // collection: plants
  plant_id: string; device_id: string; name: string;        // "A3"
  sorte?: string;                     // free text, no strain DB
  quelle?: 'samen'|'steckling'|'unbekannt'; regime_id?: string; topf_liter?: number;
  gestartet_am: number; stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stage_seit: number;
  archiviert_am?: number; ordnung: number;
}

interface Ereignis {                  // collection: events — the human diary row
  event_id: string; device_id: string;
  plant_ids: string[];                // [] === das ganze Zelt
  typ: 'giessen'|'duengen'|'giessen_duengen'|'messung'|'training'
     | 'schaden'|'foto'|'notiz'|'stufe'|'ernte'|'co2';
  zeit: number;
  actor_id: string;                   // "person:<id>" | "user:<id>" | "system"
  quelle: 'mensch'|'maschine';
  menge_l?: number;                   // litres PER PLANT
  produkte?: { name: string; ml_pro_l: number }[];
  ec?: number; ph?: number; ec_ablauf?: number; ph_ablauf?: number;
  text?: string;
  images?: string[];                  // includes the auto-attached camera frame
  regime_schritt?: string;            // "Blüte Woche 3 · Schritt 5 von 13"
  storniert_von?: string;             // corrections are entries, never edits
  client_event_id: string;            // idempotency key, unique with device_id
}

interface Zustand {                   // collection: conditions — the standing fact
  condition_id: string; device_id: string;
  text: string; dringend: boolean; plant_ids: string[];
  openedBy: string; openedAt: number; closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
}

interface Person {                    // device-scoped, no accounts
  person_id: string; device_id: string; name: string; farbe: string;
}
```

`DeviceLog` is not touched. Machine events stay there; `Ereignis` is the human record. `Das Buch` merges
both streams for display. The existing `LogEntryViewerModule` renders the `DeviceLog` half unchanged.
`convertEventsToGrowCycles` becomes a **one-time backfill** that mints a `Pflanze` per inferred cycle,
rather than a runtime string-comparison algorithm that forks history on a rename.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed.** `plant_ids: []` means the tent, and it is the default
everywhere. The `pflanzen` card is not present until a plant exists. The watering sheet shows no plant
chooser when there are no plants. Nothing nags, nothing is greyed out with a "add plants to unlock", and
the `rohdaten` card names the absence neutrally. A grower who never opens the plant feature has an app with
eleven cards instead of twelve, and every one of them works.

`Das Buch` is one reverse-chronological stream with a sticky day header, filter chips
(`Gießen · Düngen · Messung · Gerät · Bild`), and a `Pflanzen` strip at the top when plants exist. Server-side
pagination lands with it (`getDeviceLogs` today has no `.limit()`, no cursor — the Entries report fetches
the entire history on every open).

## 7. Watering & fertilising — tap counts

There is no irrigation hardware. This is a capture-and-recall problem, and the metric is taps.

### 7.1 The happy path is two taps, always

```
Tap 1  —  the giessen card's primary button
          [  Gegossen  ]        [ + gedüngt ]

Tap 2  —  the sheet's save button
```

The sheet opens **fully prefilled** and every field is editable in place:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Gegossen + gedüngt      Mi 13.08. 18:40 │
├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Wer         Anna ▾                      │
│  Pflanzen    Alle 4  ▾   A1 A2 A3 A4     │
│  Menge       2,0 L je Pflanze   [−][+]   │
│  Regime      All-Mix · Blüte Woche 3     │
│              Schritt 5 von 13            │
│  Bio-Bloom       4,0 ml/L        [−][+]  │
│  Bio-Heaven      2,0 ml/L        [−][+]  │
│  EC 1,6   pH 6,3        (optional)       │
│  Notiz …                                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│            [    Speichern    ]           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Everything above the button is prefilled from the `Regime` step, the last event's volume, and the header's
current Person. Deviating costs taps and nothing else: deselecting a plant is +1 tap; changing a dose is
+1 to focus and +n on the stepper (reusing `value-edit-row`, which is already the canonical numeric row).
Photos are *not* prompted — the camera frame nearest to `zeit` is attached automatically (§8.4).

Water-only is the same sheet without the product rows: still two taps.

### 7.2 The double feed — a soft block, one deliberate extra tap

On save, the server checks the selected plants for a `duengen`/`giessen_duengen` event by a **different
`actor_id`** within `club.fenster_stunden` (default: 12 h for feeding, 8 h for watering). If any hits:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚠  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert│
│     Bio-Bloom 4,0 ml/L · 2,0 L           │
│                                          │
│     Noch einmal düngen?                  │
│                                          │
│     [        Abbrechen        ]  ← primär│
│     [  Trotzdem eintragen  ]             │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Abbrechen` is the primary green button in the position the save button occupied; `Trotzdem eintragen` is a
secondary outline button below it, and it is disabled for 400 ms after the dialog appears. Muscle memory
cannot blow through it; a deliberate second tap can. A double feed is the most expensive thing that happens
in a shared room, and it is the only place in this concept where a confirmation exists at all.

### 7.3 Schedule following

`Regime` is a **Medium + Feed-Regime pair, labelled the way growers say it out loud**: `Biobizz Light-Mix`,
`Biobizz All-Mix`, `Floragard Light`, `Green House Feeding`, `BioTabs`. It is selected once, on the plant
or on the device, and its only job is to prefill.

```ts
interface Regime {
  regime_id: string; label: string;            // "Biobizz All-Mix"
  medium: string; feed_line: string; vorbelastet: 'keine'|'leicht'|'stark';
  schritte: { ab_woche: number; phase: DiaryLifecycleStage;
              produkte: { name: string; ml_pro_l: number|[number, number] }[];
              ec_ziel?: number; ec_basis: 'absolut'|'plus_leitungswasser';
              ph_ziel?: [number, number]; hinweis_key?: string }[];
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; chart_version: string;
}
```

`ec_basis` exists because CANNA and others publish **EC +**, i.e. a delta over the source water; alarming
against the raw chart number is actively wrong. EC is stored canonically and a bare "ppm" is never rendered
(EC 1.8 reads 1260 on a Bluelab and 900 on a Hanna).

The regime advances by **feed events** (`Schritt 5 von 13`), not by wall-clock weeks, with a soft mapping —
growers feed Tuesday and Friday and slip. The 18/6 → 12/12 flip is **detected from `out_light` history** and
places the grower on the correct bloom week; no bottle brand's calculator can do that, because none of them
can see the tent.

`vorbelastet: 'stark'` is a first-class state, not an edge case. In weeks 1–3 of All-Mix the card reads:

> **GIESSEN** — All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt. Bis Woche 4 nur gießen.
> Rang 5: nächste Gabe fällig heute.
> [ Gegossen ]   [ + gedüngt ]

The `+ gedüngt` button is present and secondary. It is never removed — removing it would be hiding — but
the sentence above it is unambiguous.

Brand names are plain text labels only. The numbers live in the Mongo content collection with
`source_url` + `retrieved_at`, shown to the user as `Stand: 14.03.2026` — the legal posture and a genuine
trust feature at the same price.

### 7.4 The cumulative question

The `pflanzen` card answers it without a tap:

④ PFLANZEN                              4
  A1 Blüte T21 · 8,0 L · Bio-Bloom 92 ml · Anna, vor 2 Tagen
  A2 Blüte T21 · 8,0 L · Bio-Bloom 92 ml · Anna, vor 2 Tagen
  A3 Blüte T21 · 6,0 L · Bio-Bloom 68 ml · Ben,  vor 5 Tagen
  A4 Blüte T18 · 6,0 L · Bio-Bloom 68 ml · Ben,  vor 5 Tagen

### 7.5 Offline

Every capture generates a `client_event_id` (uuid v4) at the moment of the tap. The server upserts on the
unique index `{device_id, client_event_id}`, so replay is idempotent and **a retry cannot double-log** —
which in a shared record is indistinguishable from a double feed. v1 queues failed saves in `localStorage`
and retries on app foreground, with the queue visible on the card (`1 Eintrag wartet auf Verbindung`).
A real service worker is v1.1 (§12) because `@angular/service-worker` is a new dependency and a build change.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

### 8.1 What exists, and the defect nobody has named

The cloud already pulls a still every 30 s and rebuilds 1d/1w/1m mp4s hourly
(`image.service.ts:314-400`). Two facts matter:

1. Frames are thinned in tiers — 1 min after a day, 5 min after a week, 15 min after a month, 1 h after 90
   days, retained 3 years (`IMAGE_THINNING_TIERS`, `IMAGE_RETENTION_DAYS`). A film built later has coarser
   source material, so **films must be rendered near-live and kept**, not regenerated on demand from old
   frames.
2. **The existing timelapse is encoded `libx265 -crf 30` (`image.service.ts:578`).** H.265 in MP4 does not
   play in Chrome or Firefox on desktop or Android. The product's flagship artefact is, today, a file most
   of its customers' browsers cannot open. Fixing that is a two-line change and is in v1.

### 8.2 Der Film der Woche — what ships in October

Every Monday 04:00 in the device's timezone the cloud renders the previous ISO week into one file, and the
`bild` card promotes to band `NEU`:

```
① BILD                              vor 31 Sek
  Film der Woche ist fertig. KW 34, 44 Sekunden.
  Rang 1: neuer Film, noch nicht geöffnet.
  [ ▶ ─────────────────────────────────── 0:44 ]
  1 042 Bilder aus 20 160 · 118 verworfen
  Filme öffnen →        Film sichern ↓
```

`118 verworfen` is a link. Tapping it says exactly why, per rule, with thumbnails — because a cull the user
cannot inspect is a cull the user cannot trust.

### 8.3 The pipeline — culling, normalisation, stamping

Target: 24 fps × 45 s = **1 080 frames** from a week's ~20 160. `sharp` and `ffmpeg` are already
dependencies; nothing new is required except an ffmpeg filter graph.

**Stage 0 — Kandidatenfenster.** Only frames inside the lit period (`out_light > 0`), sampled at a fixed
phase: pick the frame nearest `lights_on + k·Δ`, where `Δ = lit_seconds_in_window / 1080`. Fixed phase
means constant exposure and constant lamp angle from frame to frame. Night frames are excluded from the
main film entirely — a dark tent is not content. If `out_light` was never on (drying, curing), fall back
to a uniform grid over the whole window. *This one rule removes the majority of the garbage.*

**Stage 1 — Belichtungs-Gate.** At ingest, each JPEG gets a cached 64×36 grayscale and its mean luma stored
on the `Image` doc (`stats.luma`, `stats.gains`). Reject a candidate whose luma falls outside
`[0.35 × M, 1.8 × M]`, where `M` is the rolling 3-day median. Kills door-open blowouts, lamp-off
transitions, and dimmer ramps caught mid-step.

**Stage 2 — Mensch-im-Zelt-Gate.** Two rules, no ML:
- *Correlation rule (the one nobody else can do):* drop every candidate within **±3 minutes of a
  human-authored `Ereignis`**. The diary already knows when a person was standing in the shot.
- *Transient rule:* compute the 64×36 absolute-difference mask against the previous kept frame; if
  > 35 % of cells exceed |Δ| > 24/255 **and** the next candidate reverts to within 8 % of the pre-change
  frame, drop both as an occlusion. A person passes this test; a defoliation does not (it does not revert).

**Stage 3 — Weißabgleich und Ton.** Gray-world gains per frame, then apply the **rolling median of the last
24 kept frames' gains** rather than each frame's own — per-frame auto white balance makes a film pulse.
Magenta LED versus daylight through an open door is a gain problem, and the median absorbs it. A single
per-frame gamma maps each frame's median luma onto the film's target median, clamped to ±0.4 stops of
step-to-step change so it can never flicker. All `sharp` in the worker, writing normalised JPEGs to a temp
dir; ffmpeg then reads `image2`.

**Stage 4 — Stabilisierung (v1.1).** Translation only, by phase correlation on the 256×144 grayscale
against the film's first kept frame. A shift > 12 % of frame width is a **re-aim**, not drift: a new
reference starts and the film gets a hard cut captioned `Kamera neu ausgerichtet`. Otherwise crop to the
common intersection with a 4 % margin. (`vidstab` is the fallback, but it must not be asked to smooth a
jump cut, which is why the re-aim detection is ours.)

**Stage 5 — Deduplizierung: deliberately not done.** Dropping near-identical frames would break the
constant time-per-frame relationship that makes the film readable as a clock. A stalled week should *look*
stalled. A designed refusal, not an omission.

**Stage 6 — Der Stempel.** The bottom 8 % of every frame is a rendered strip. This is image correlation
made physical: the numbers leave the platform inside the video.

```
 Tag 34 · Blüte      26,1 °C   58 %   VPD 1,21   Licht 100 %      Mi 13.08.  09:12
```

Where an `Ereignis` falls between two frames, a 2-second caption sits above the strip:
`A1–A4 gegossen · 4,0 L · Anna`.

**Stage 7 — Encode.**

```
-c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -pix_fmt yuv420p
-crf 21 -preset medium -r 24 -vf scale=1280:720 -movflags +faststart -an
```

H.264 High + yuv420p + faststart plays everywhere, including in a Telegram or Discord inline player. A week
film is 1 080 frames ≈ 44 s ≈ **8–14 MB**. A whole-run film is ~3 000 frames ≈ 125 s ≈ 25–40 MB, which
exceeds the 16 MB BSON limit — so **films go to a `films/` volume with a `Film` metadata document**, not
into Mongo. That is a real infrastructure addition and it is named as one.

```ts
interface Film {
  film_id: string; device_id: string;
  von: number; bis: number;
  art: 'woche' | 'auftrag' | 'durchgang';
  dauer_s: number; bytes: number; pfad: string;
  frames_gesamt: number; frames_verworfen: number;
  verworfen_grund: { belichtung: number; mensch: number; kein_licht: number; reaim: number };
  stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage; tag_von?: number; tag_bis?: number;
  opened_at?: number;
}
```

### 8.4 How a video leaves the product

```
GET /film/:film_id/datei?token=<imageToken>
  → 200 video/mp4
    Content-Disposition: attachment;
      filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_bluete_tag028-034_2026-08-17.mp4"
```

`imageToken` already exists, is valid 30 days and is deliberately URL-embeddable — so the link works in a
browser with no session, which is what "share" has to mean. On Android and desktop the button saves the
file. On iOS Safari an mp4 download opens the share sheet, from which `In Dateien sichern` and
`Video sichern` both work; the button on iOS is therefore labelled **`Film sichern`** with the one-line hint
`Öffnet die Teilen-Ansicht — dort "Video sichern".` Capacitor is not needed for v1.

A `ShareLink` with the new `page: 'film'` renders a bare public page: the `<video>`, the device's chosen
display name, the day range, and a download button. No login, no app chrome, no account. `parseSharePage`
defaults unknown values to `'charts'`, so old links are unaffected.

### 8.5 Correlation, three directions

1. **Chart → Bild.** The crosshair on `/verlauf` drives the Bildstreifen and the pinned header together.
2. **Bild → Zahlen.** Tapping any frame opens the Bildkarte: the still, its stamped numbers, the events
   within ±5 min, and `Verlauf an dieser Stelle öffnen →`.
3. **Ereignis → Bild.** Every `Ereignis` automatically carries the nearest frame within ±5 min as its
   thumbnail. **The diary illustrates itself** — nobody has to remember to take a photo, which is what
   "it does the diary keeping for you" has to mean in practice.

### 8.6 The camera alarm

A camera that stopped three days into flower must say so. A new alarm `sensorType: 'kamera'`, evaluated by
a cloud timer rather than the MQTT status path (`getSensorValue` is driven by `StatusMessage` and has no
camera case). Default silence budget 120 min. It fires through the existing e-mail/webhook/info machinery
and promotes `bild` to band `AUS`:

```
① BILD                        seit 5 Std 20
  Seit 5 Std 20 kein Bild.
  Rang 1: Kamera meldet sich nicht.
  Zuletzt: Mi 13.08. 04:02  [ letztes Bild, entsättigt ]
  Testbild anfordern →     Kamera-Einstellungen →
```

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution in v1, authorisation not.** The distinction is stated in the UI, not hidden.

- **`Person`** — device-scoped names the owner types once (`Anna`, `Ben`, `Chris`), each with a colour. No
  accounts, no e-mail, no invitations, no `auth.middleware` surgery — roughly two days of work. A
  persistent chooser sits in the Rangfolge header: `Anna ▾`, stored per device in `localStorage`, asked
  once on first capture.
- Every `Ereignis`, closed `Zustand` and settings change is stamped `actor_id: "person:<id>"`. When real
  memberships arrive the same field takes `user:<id>` and nothing migrates.
- **The double-feed block (§7.2)** is what this makes possible, and it is the single most valuable thing a
  shared tent gets.
- **`Zustand`** is the standing condition nobody had a place for — not an event, not a task, an open fact
  with an owner. The `zettel` card:

```
⑤ ZETTEL                                   2
  CO₂-Flasche fast leer          Anna, Do  [Erledigt]
  A3 nicht gießen — übergossen    Ben, Mo  [Erledigt]
  Zustand notieren →
```

  `dringend: true` promotes the card to `FÄLLIG`. `Erledigt` stamps `closedBy`/`closedAt`; closed Zustände
  stay in Das Buch forever.
- **Cumulative reporting** is §7.4 plus a per-plant CSV.

**Privacy posture — Privat is the default and it is not a mode of the UI.** It is a device-level data
setting under Ziele, and it changes what is *stored*, not what is *shown*:

- `erfassung.ernte` — **off by default.** With it off there is no `ernte` event type, no weight field, no
  prompt. With it on, wet/dry grams become a first-class field. Harvest weights and plant counts are the
  facts that separate a lawful hobby from an offence; the product does not collect them unasked.
- Exports carry a `NONE | PSEUDONYM | ANONYM` selector defaulting to **PSEUDONYM**. No real name is ever
  required — a `Person` is whatever the owner typed. No location, no analytics SDK, no community feed.
- Retention becomes explicit: raw telemetry 18 months, 5-minute downsample 5 years (today: infinite, zero
  Influx tasks), frames as today, films kept. `Alles löschen` wipes events, frames, films and telemetry for
  one device in one action, and it is never withheld from the owner.
- **No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.** The honest line is: *Terp Control dokumentiert das Zelt, nicht die
  Mitglieder.*

## 10. Old devices (type 7)

The Rangfolge replaces the **overview** for every `device_type`. **No type-7 settings screen is rewritten
in v1** — `fan-settings`, `light-settings`, `plug-settings`, `fridge-settings` stay byte-for-byte and are
reached from a card's `Einstellungen →` link. That is what makes type 7 both cheap and safe.

What each owner actually sees, exactly:

**`light`** — three cards.
```
① LICHT                                  An
   An seit 06:00. Aus um 18:00.
   Rang 1: nichts weicht ab.
   Zeitplan 06:00–18:00 · 12 Std · heute 7 Std 12 gelaufen
   Letzte Meldung vor 41 Sek
   Verlauf öffnen →     Einstellungen →
② VERBINDUNG   ③ ROHDATEN
```
Plus, free: the `ABWEICHUNG` rule that compares the measured state against the schedule. A failed
contactor is currently invisible to this user; here it becomes rank 1.

**`plug`** — `steckdosen` (single socket, on/off, on-hours today), `verbindung`, `rohdaten`.

**`fan`** — `lueftung` (rpm, target, the existing fan settings behind the link), `verbindung`, `rohdaten`.

**`fridge`** — the full catalogue minus what it lacks; `fridge-settings` unchanged behind `Ziele`.

**No card for a camera, plants, feeding or CO₂ appears on a device that has none**, and `rohdaten` names
each absence with its reason, so nothing feels withheld. A `light` owner is never shown a humidity target
they cannot act on — which is what happens today, because `deviceControlCapability()` fails open to
`'full'` when `hardwareInfo.sockets` is absent (`grow-presets.ts:158-161`). Rangfolge replaces the
three-bucket capability model with the per-role truth the CSV already carries.

Also in v1 and overdue: a `devices.controller` i18n block, so a controller's card stops being titled
**"Terp Control FRIDGE GROW"** (`overview.component.html:40`) and an unnamed controller stops being *named*
that (`overview.component.ts:91-94`).

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app once a week. Sees rank 1: a sentence and a number. `Im Soll seit
9 Std.` Closes it. When something is genuinely wrong, rank 1 is the wrong thing with a duration on it, and
the age line stops him trusting a stale number — the #1 complaint across every competitor app, fixed by a
line of text on every card.

**2 · Monitoring with a camera.** His rank 1 is a picture, every time, plus a stamped caption. On Monday it
is a finished 44-second film he can save to his camera roll and send to a friend, with the numbers burned
into it. He never opens the chart, and the product still produced something he can hold.

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Sees exactly the cards for the sockets he has paired,
per role rather than per bucket. His CO₂ card carries the bottle estimate and promotes to `FÄLLIG` below
10 %. He is never nagged about humidity, because he has no dehumidifier socket and therefore no `klima`
humidity target row he can act on — the target is shown as measured-only with `kein Stellglied` beside it.

**4 · Full tent.** Gets the most cards and the most bands in play, and the Riegel matters most to him
because his tent generates the most state changes. His honest limit is the firmware's: five socket roles,
no humidifier, `dehumidifier` doubling as cooler. Rangfolge does not pretend otherwise — the `steckdosen`
card lists the roles that exist and `rohdaten` lists the ones that do not, with the sentence
`Weitere Rollen brauchen neue Firmware.` Lying about this is what the app does today.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** Scrolls to card 12 every time, and it is always card 12. It contains the raw
latest-values table, the ranking response itself (`GET /device/:id/rangfolge` — same JSON the screen was
built from), a CSV export of any window including actuator state, the webhook editor, and a copyable curl
line. He is the only user for whom the bottom of the page is the destination, and Rangfolge is the only
concept in which he never has to enable anything to get there.

**6 · Grow club.** The header says who is at the tent. Capture is two taps. The double-feed block is the
thing that pays for the whole feature. `Zettel` is the tent door. Per-plant totals answer "how much has A3
had, and from whom". Nothing about members is stored, and the export defaults to pseudonymised.

**7 · Old device owner.** Three or four cards, the existing settings screen untouched behind one link, and
two things he did not have before: an honest freshness stamp and a `verbindung` card that says when the
device stopped talking. Nothing he used has moved.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner:* one thumb, dark tent, 2am — reads rank 1, sees a big number and one
German sentence, and the screen does not move while he reads it. *Normal:* compares this to AC Infinity —
gets a picture at the top, a film on Monday, and a chart with the target drawn on it, which AC Infinity
does not have. *Techie:* everything is on the page, the ordering rule is published, the ranking is an API
response, and nothing was hidden behind a preference he has to find.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

**13.1 The page is long.** Twelve fully-expanded cards is roughly five screens on a phone. The Rangleiste
is a mitigation, not a fix. A user who wants to compare temperature and CO₂ has to scroll between them, and
a tile grid would show both at once. **Rangfolge trades spatial comparison for urgency legibility**, and
that trade is genuinely arguable. On a 360×640 device it is at its worst: exactly one card is visible.

**13.2 Rank 1 is a single point of trust.** The whole concept rests on "the top is the thing that matters".
One spurious promotion at 3am — a sensor glitch that survives the 20-minute dwell — and the user learns
that the top is not reliable. There is no recovery mechanism for that; hysteresis lowers the probability
and does not bound the damage.

**13.3 Band cliffs are discontinuous and arbitrary.** A card at 1,49 °C deviation is rank 8; at 1,51 °C it
is rank 1. Nothing about the tent changed. The Begründung line names the threshold, which makes the jump
explicable but not less abrupt. A critic will call it a jump scare, and they will be right about the
experience even though the rule is defensible.

**13.4 The Riegel is friction that many users will not tap.** Some people will read a stale order for
minutes. The card's own numbers and Urteil are correct in place, so nothing is *wrong* — but the promise
"the top of the screen is what matters" is temporarily false, and that is the concept's central promise.

**13.5 The calm state makes the rank numbers ceremony.** Eleven cards all in band 0 means the circled
numerals encode nothing but the constant catalogue order. A user may reasonably conclude that the ranking
is decorative — right up until the day it is not, which is the worst moment to learn what it means.

**13.6 Refusing personalisation has a price and I am paying it deliberately.** A monitor-only grower with
no CO₂ sensor still scrolls past the same cards in the same order forever. A user who never uses plants
carries the `pflanzen` card the moment they create one plant, permanently, at ordnung 60. The brief offered
adaptive ordering; I refused it; the cost is a screen that never gets shorter for anyone.

**13.7 Server-side ranking is a new hot-path dependency** and a new place where the screen, the endpoint
and the notification text can diverge. If the band job lags, the Riegel says "changed" when nothing did —
the exact failure that erodes 13.4.

**13.8 The camera cull has no ground truth.** The human-in-tent heuristic will drop legitimate frames: a
heavy defoliation that changes 40 % of the frame and does not revert is safe, but a plant that falls over
and is stood back up is not. Mitigation: the cull is a *rendering* decision and never deletes anything —
the Filme screen has an `Alle Bilder` browser and `118 verworfen` shows what went and why. A first-time
user watching a film with a missing day will not go looking for that link.

**13.9 `actor_id` is a label, not a gate.** Anyone at the tent can pick "Anna" and log as her. The
double-feed block still works, but the attribution is only as good as the goodwill in the room.

**13.10 The regime data is five hand-transcribed brands.** `Stand: 14.03.2026` is only true if somebody
re-checks it. Two public sources disagree by ~3× on CANNA Terra dosing; the same error class here burns a
real crop. This needs an owner, not a feature flag.

**13.11 No push in v1 is a real hole in a product promising "ruhig schlafen".** E-mail and webhook alerts
are what exists; Rangfolge makes the state legible *when you open the app*. If push must be in October,
something in §12 comes out, and the honest candidate is the plant entity.
