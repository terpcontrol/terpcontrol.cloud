# C31 — "Eine Tiefe" (One Depth)

Family: **unify** · Centre of gravity: one screen, infinite depth · German-first (de-DE)

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every element on the one screen has five levels of depth, always the same five, and one tap moves
exactly one level — so the simple reading is never removed and the raw sample is never denied.**

The product is a single scrollable page per device. On it sit seven **Zellen** (cells). Each cell is
closed at level 0: one German sentence and the two or three numbers that sentence is about. Tapping a
cell's header adds a level *below* the sentence — it never replaces it. Level 1 is every number with
its target and its age. Level 2 is the same thing over time. Level 3 is **Herkunft**: which bucket
size, which aggregation function, how many samples, what coverage, how far off the device clock is.
Level 4 is the samples themselves, with CSV and the API call that produced them.

The stoned user reads seven sentences and stops. The techie taps down to level 4 in the one cell he
cares about and the other six stay at level 0. There is no declaration of who you are, no preference,
no segment. **A mode is a claim about the person; a depth is a state of an element.** The two people
are not served by two interfaces — they are served by the same interface stopped at different rows,
and the row where one stops is physically above the row where the other stops. Depth accretes from
your own thumb and from nothing else: no adaptive ordering, no learned defaults, no "we noticed you
are advanced".

The grammar is identical in all seven cells and on all device types. Learn the chevron once and you
have learned the whole product, including the parts you will never open.

## 2. Information architecture

There is essentially one screen. The routes that survive exist for links, not for navigation.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | **Das Regal.** One row per device — itself a cell at level 0. Level 1 shows that device's roll-up line and its two worst cells inline. A labelled row `Gerät öffnen →` navigates. With exactly one device, redirect straight to it (unchanged from today: zero navigation between a monitoring user and their numbers). |
| `/device/:device_id` | **Die Tiefe.** The one screen. Everything. |
| `/device/:device_id/film/:film_id` | Full-screen film player. The only genuine second screen, because a video at full width is not a cell. |
| `/f/:film_id?k=<hmac>` | **Unauthenticated** film delivery. No login. See §8. |
| `/device/:device_id/charts` `…/diary` `…/settings` `…/testmode` | **Kept, not deleted.** They now redirect into `/device/:id?tiefe=…` with a back-compat mapping of the old query string (§2.2). `settings` for legacy device types renders the old component unchanged (§10). |
| `/shares` `/account` `/login` `/demo` `/classes` `/diagnostics` | Unchanged. |

**Navigation model:** the `ion-menu` stays exactly as it is (Geräte / Geteilte Links / Benutzer /
Dunkelmodus / Abmelden). No tab bar, no FAB, no bottom bar. The only new navigational object is the
**Dach** — a sticky 2-line header on the device screen.

### 2.1 The Dach (sticky roof)

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ☰   Zelt 1 ▾                    ● vor 40 s    │
│ Alles im Ziel. Nächster Guss heute.    3 offen│
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Line 1: menu, device name (tap = device switcher popover, sorted worst-first), liveness dot + age.
- Line 2: the **roll-up sentence** — at most two clauses, assembled deterministically: the worst cell's
  L0 verdict, then the nearest due action. If nothing is wrong and nothing is due: `Alles im Ziel.`
- Right of line 2: `3 offen` when any cell is above level 0. Tapping it collapses everything
  (`Alles zu`). This is the escape hatch and it is always in the same place.
- Tapping line 2 scrolls to the cell the sentence came from. That is the only "ranking" in the design:
  order is fixed, attention is routed at the roof.

### 2.2 Depth state and back-compatibility

Depth lives in two places:

- `localStorage['tc-tiefe:<device_id>']` → `{"jetzt":2,"bild":1}`. Per browser, per device. Never
  synced, never shared, never sent to the server.
- URL param `?tiefe=jetzt:2,bild:1` — wins over localStorage when present, so a share link or a
  bookmark can carry a view.

Old links keep working. `applyViewParams` gains one branch: `/charts?measures=temperature,humidity&
date=…&interval=…` → `/device/:id?tiefe=jetzt:2&measures=…&date=…&interval=…`. The existing
`measures`/`date`/`dateEnd`/`interval`/`timespan`/`vpdMode`/`autoUpdate`/`share` params are read
verbatim by the Jetzt cell at level 2. **Existing chart presets and share links do not break.**

**Order is fixed and never changes.** Jetzt · Bild · Ziel · Pflanzen · Gießen & Düngen · Zettel ·
Verlauf. A user who learns "Gießen is the fifth thing" is right forever, including at 2 a.m.

**Hit targets.** Only two things in a closed cell are tappable: the header strip (44 px tall, full
width, contains the label and the chevron) and, in Gießen, the green `Gegossen` button. The verdict
line and the number line are **not** tappable. Tapping the picture in Bild does nothing. This is the
first half of the accident proof (§4.3).

**+ Zelle hinzufügen** lists only cells this device *could* have but does not: `Pflanzen anlegen`,
`Zettel schreiben`. It never lists a cell the hardware cannot feed (a plug never offers Bild).

### 3.1 Freshness, staleness, offline, absence — the exact copy

Four states, four different renderings, never conflated. `ONLINE_TIMEOUT` is 10 min
(`device.service.ts:46`); the device samples every 5 s.

| State | Dach | Cell L0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live (< 2 min) | `● vor 40 s` | `Warm, etwas feucht — im Rahmen.` / `26,1 °C · 61 % · VPD 1,32 kPa` |
| Träge (2–10 min) | `◐ vor 6 Min` | numbers in `--tc-text-warning`, suffix ` · vor 6 Min` |
| Offline (> 10 min) | `○ offline seit 14:20` | `Keine Beurteilung — die Werte sind 3 Std alt.` / `26,1 °C · 61 % · gemessen 14:20` in muted grey. **The last reading is never hidden.** A blanked value is a worse lie than an old one. |
| Nie Daten | `○ noch nie gesehen` | `Noch keine Messwerte. Sobald das Gerät im WLAN ist, meldet es sich alle 5 Sekunden.` |
| Sensor fehlt (kein CO₂-Upgrade) | — | the CO₂ row **does not exist**. Not greyed, not `—`. Absent. |

Bild follows the same rules and uses the server's existing burnt-in caption
(`imageService.addOfflineOverlay`, `image.controller.ts:166`) so the staleness survives a screenshot.

### 3.2 Verdict without a goal (monitor-only)

A device with no sockets and no declared stage still gets a real sentence, derived from the data
itself, with no target, plant or plan. Three deterministic rules, evaluated on the last 14 days of
raw samples, worst-first:

1. `Die Nächte schwanken 6,1 °C, die Tage nur 1,8 °C.` (night σ > 2× day σ)
2. `Die Feuchte steigt jede Nacht ab 02:00 auf über 70 %.` (recurring band crossing at a stable hour)
3. `Deine Spanne ist 25 % breiter als in den zwei Wochen davor.` (change point in range width)
4. Fallback: `Stabil: 21–26 °C, 52–64 % über zwei Wochen.`

None of this is AI. It is descriptive statistics over samples the product already stores.

### 3.3 Verdict with a remedy

A negative verdict never ships alone. Where the product can act (PWM lamp, a paired socket), the
remedy is a button at level 0 with a confirm:

```
│ JETZT                                       ⌄ │
│ Zu warm: 28,4 °C, Ziel 25,0 °C seit 40 Min.   │
│ 28,4 °C · 48 % · VPD 1,91 kPa                 │
│   Lampe von 100 % auf 70 % dimmen  [ Machen ] │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Where it cannot act, the sentence says so and stops: `Heizung läuft seit 3 Std durchgehend und
kommt nicht nach.` No button, no nagging, no alarm preset for kit that does not exist.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

### 4.1 The mechanism, stated

**Expansion is additive, per element, and symmetric.**

- **Additive.** Level 1 appears *below* level 0. Level 0 is never replaced, never abbreviated, never
  moved. A cell at level 4 still begins with the same German sentence it began with at level 0. There
  is therefore no state of the app in which the simple reading is unavailable.
- **Per element.** Depth is a property of a cell, not of the app, not of the account, not of the
  person. A user can be at level 4 in Gießen and level 0 in Jetzt — a combination no mode can express,
  and the most common real combination there is (people are precise about the thing they care about
  and vague about everything else).
- **Symmetric.** The same chevron that opened it closes it. One tap, one level, in both directions.

### 4.2 Why this is not a mode with a new name

A mode is a **declaration about a person** that **substitutes** one surface for another and is
**global**. Depth fails all three tests:

| A mode | A depth |
| --- | --- |
| Chosen by answering "what kind of user are you?" | Never chosen; accreted from taps on the thing itself |
| Replaces the screen | Appends under the sentence, which stays |
| One switch for the whole app | Seven independent states, per device, per browser |
| Hides a feature class ("Advanced") | Hides nothing — every level is one labelled tap from the level above, and the label says what is there |
| Sticky as an identity | Sticky as a layout; `Alles zu` in the Dach resets it always in one tap |

The sharpest test the round-two brief poses: *does the interface ever deny the number to the
non-technical user, or force the number on the impaired one?* At level 0 the headline numbers sit
next to the verdict, so nobody is denied. At level 0 nothing else is on screen, so nobody is forced.
That is the whole trick, and it is one line of layout policy, not a feature flag.

**One honest concession.** If a user opens every cell to level 2, they have built themselves
something that looks like an expert mode. It is not one: they built it with seven deliberate taps,
they can see it (`7 offen`), and one tap undoes it. What they cannot do is accidentally acquire it, or
lose the simple reading by having it.

### 4.3 Proof that a stoned user never falls into the deep end

Five structural guarantees, each checkable in code review:

1. **One tap = one level. There is no gesture that skips a level.** No double-tap, no long-press, no
   swipe, no pinch changes depth. Missing the target does nothing; hitting it twice gets you to
   level 2 with level 0 and level 1 still on screen above.
2. **Scrolling never changes depth.** The only depth affordance is the 44 px header strip and the
   labelled depth rows. Content inside a cell is inert to depth — a fat-finger on the chart pans the
   chart, it does not deepen anything.
3. **Levels 3 and 4 are not reachable by chevron.** The chevron stops at level 2. Deeper is reached
   only through an explicitly labelled row at the bottom of level 2:
   `Herkunft der Zahlen ›` and, below that inside level 3, `Rohdaten — 4.310 Messpunkte ›`. You cannot
   arrive at a wall of samples without having read the words "Rohdaten" first.
4. **Depth is read-only below level 1.** There is no control, no save, no destructive action anywhere
   at level ≥ 2. The two write surfaces are level 0 (`Gegossen`, the remedy button) and level 1 of
   Ziel (setpoint rows, which use the existing `value-edit-row` + `rangeGuard` arm/disarm pattern and
   an explicit `Speichern`). **A mis-tap into depth cannot change the grow.** This is the guarantee
   that matters at 2 a.m., and it is why depth is worth having at all.
5. **Nothing collapses or expands by itself.** No timeout, no "we tidied up for you", no reordering.
   Unpredictability is the specific thing that hurts an impaired user, so the design contains none.
   The only thing that ever changes without a tap is the roll-up sentence in the Dach, which is text.

### 4.4 Big screen, same code

At ≥ 992 px the seven cells lay out in two columns. Depth still works cell by cell, but nothing gets
pushed below the fold, so a desktop user routinely runs four cells at level 2 at once. That is the
workbench the competitors do not have — and it is the *same component at the same depth*, not a
desktop app. The mobile/desktop split is a media query, not a mode.

## 5. The chart

Lives inside **Jetzt L2** and **Ziel L2**. Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, driven by a hand-rolled
~50-line Angular directive (owner decision). `chart.js` / `ng2-charts` / `chartjs-adapter-luxon`
deleted in the same commit.

```
Jetzt · Level 2
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [24 h] 7 T  30 T  Lauf         12.09. 14:20   │  ← pinned scrubbing header, no hover tooltip
│  26,4 °C   59 %   1,41 kPa   Licht 100 %      │
│ ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│ │ °C   ░░░░░░░░ Nacht ░░░░  ╌╌╌ Ziel 20     │ │  96 px panel
│ │      ───╱▔▔╲──────╱▔▔▔╲──── Ist            │ │  min/max envelope, sampling:'minmax'
│ ├───────────────────────────────────────────┤ │
│ │ %                 ▔▔▔ Decke 60 (nur runter)│ │  one-sided: dehumidifier only
│ ├───────────────────────────────────────────┤ │
│ │ kPa  ▒▒▒ Blüte 1,2–1,5 ▒▒▒                │ │  stage band moves along x
│ ├───────────────────────────────────────────┤ │
│ │ Aus- ███ Heizung ██ ▓▓▓▓▓▓ am Anschlag    │ │  state-timeline lane
│ │ gänge ██████ Licht ██████████             │ │
│ ├───────────────────────────────────────────┤ │
│ │ ▲ ▲▲   ▲       ▲³        ▲                │ │  annotation rail, 2 rows, clustered
│ └───────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│ Herkunft der Zahlen ›                         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goal vs actual.** A **dashed stepped setpoint** line, stepping at `daynight.day`/`daynight.night`
  between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`, with a soft band at ± the alarm threshold where
  one exists, else ± tolerance. Between actual and setpoint, a **signed deviation fill** — never a
  fill to the axis. Band source is labelled: `Alarmgrenze` > `Ziel ± 1,5 °C` > `Blüte 1,2–1,5 kPa`.
- **One-sided measures get one-sided bands.** Humidity has no humidifier anywhere in the hardware, so
  it is drawn as a **ceiling**, labelled `nur runter`. Temperature is drawn as a ceiling *or* a floor
  according to `parseSocketRoles(hardwareInfo.sockets)`: heater but no dehumidifier → floor only,
  labelled `nur heizen`. A symmetric band is drawn only when both directions are actually owned.
- **Saturation.** When an output sits at 100 % and the measure is still outside the band, its lane is
  hatched and labelled `am Anschlag`. The verdict above then says `kommt nicht nach` instead of
  scoring the user red. (This is the join between the outputs lane and the deviation fill that no
  earlier concept made.)
- **Day/night** shading from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the `daynight` schedule, with
  the `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` ramps as gradients, in an explicitly named timezone. A failed
  contactor therefore shows as a *missing band* — a diagnostic, not decoration.
- **Outputs** are a state-timeline lane, region length = duration, null = gap. `out_dehumidifier` is
  split at ingest into two derived cloud-side series by the active workmode, so `Kühlen` and
  `Entfeuchten` stop being the same column forever.
- **Annotations** in a 20 px rail *under* the axis, two rows (row 1 human, row 2 device), pixel-cluster
  binning with count badges, full-height dashed lines for stage changes. Tap, not hover.
- **Interaction:** one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap sets the
  crosshair. Axis labels are never hidden — reduced to three ticks with the unit in the panel title.
- **Colour:** Okabe-Ito. Position is the primary channel because the panels are separate; colour is
  decoration, which is also the accessibility win.

**Ziel L2** is the scorecard, not a second chart: time in / above / below band as one stacked bar,
split day vs night, longest excursion **as a duration**, mean/min/max, MAD from setpoint, and the
data-coverage percentage. Computed **server-side on raw Influx samples**, never on
`aggregateWindow(mean)` output — averaging destroys the excursions the metric exists to count. Below
80 % coverage the scorecard is greyed and says `Zu wenig Daten (68 %)`.

**Herkunft (L3)** is what makes the chart honest, and no competitor ships it:

```
Bucket 5 Min · Funktion min/max (Anzeige), mean (Kennzahl)
4.310 von 4.320 erwarteten Messpunkten · Abdeckung 99,8 %
Lücken: 12.09. 03:10–03:55 (45 Min)
Geräteuhr wich beim letzten Kontakt 4 s von der Serverzeit ab
Zeitzone Europe/Berlin (UTC+2)
  Rohdaten — 4.310 Messpunkte ›
```

The clock-offset line matters: the device is an ESP with no RTC, and every excursion duration and
day boundary is wrong by that offset. Nobody shows it.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

The diary is not a tab. It is the **Verlauf** cell plus the per-plant card inside **Pflanzen**, and
crucially it is *written by the machine and confirmed by the human*.

### 6.1 New collections (all cloud-side; `device.configuration` is untouchable)

```ts
Plant   { plant_id, device_id, label, strain?, medium?, source?: 'seed'|'clone',
          pot_l?, started_at, stage: DiaryLifecycleStage, stage_since, archived_at? }
Run     { run_id, device_id, name, started_at, ended_at?, plant_ids: string[] }
Entry   { entry_id, device_id, run_id?, plant_ids: string[], type, at, actor_id?,
          text?, images: string[], data: Record<string, unknown>,
          client_id,            // UUID minted on the phone → idempotent capture
          origin: 'human'|'machine', confirmed_by?, edited_of?, created_at }
Condition { condition_id, device_id, text, plant_ids: string[],
            opened_by, opened_at, closed_by?, closed_at? }
Actor   { actor_id, device_id, name, colour, created_by, disabled? }
```

`Entry.type ∈ { note, feed, water, stage, training, issue, measurement, harvest, photo, machine }`.
Every field is optional except `entry_id/device_id/type/at/client_id`, per house style (no migration
tooling exists). A read-time normaliser presents legacy `DeviceLog` rows with `categories[0]==='diary'`
as `Entry` objects, so **nothing is migrated on day one** and `convertEventsToGrowCycles` becomes a
one-time backfill that mints `Run` rows instead of a runtime algorithm in the browser.

Three legacy bugs are fixed while we are here: `deleted` stops meaning "hidden" (a real
`hidden_at` field replaces it, so `Protokoll leeren` actually leaves the diary alone); the server's own
stage-transition entry gets the `'diary'` prefix so it is visible and editable like the manual one;
and `GET /device/logs/:id` gets a cursor (`?before=<ts>&limit=100`) because a diary-centred product
cannot fetch the entire history on every open.

### 6.2 It writes itself

Verlauf L1 is a list where **most rows were written by the machine** and carry a small `·` marker:

```
Heute
  19:04  Gegossen · 4,0 L · A1–A3 · Bio-Grow 2 ml/L      Anna
  14:30  Lichtphase auf 12/12 umgestellt                   ·
  06:00  Tag begonnen (Licht 100 %)                        ·
Gestern
  22:41  Feuchte 4 Std über 65 % (Höchstwert 71 %)         ·
  20:02  Kamera hat sich bewegt                            ·
```

Machine entries are generated from telemetry and machine state at the moment they happen: photoperiod
flips, stage transitions, excursions longer than 30 min, alarms, plan steps, config changes, camera
cuts, socket failures. They are **facts, not opinions**, and they are the substance of "es führt das
Tagebuch für dich". A `Nur meine Einträge` filter chip at L1 hides them for a user who finds them
noisy — that is a filter over a list, not a mode.

### 6.3 Per-plant, and the tax for ignoring plants

**If `Plant` count is 0, nothing anywhere asks about plants.** The Pflanzen cell reads
`Keine Pflanzen angelegt.` at level 0, its level 1 is a single row `Wie viele Pflanzen? [3] Anlegen`
(which mints A1, A2, A3 with today as `started_at`), and every feed, note and photo is stored
device-scoped with `plant_ids: []`. The feed sheet contains no plant picker. The chart contains no
plant lane. Nothing nags, nothing is greyed out, nothing says "Pro". This is reveal-by-data, and the
lazy monitoring user (type 1) never learns that plants exist.

With plants, Pflanzen L1 is one row each; L2 for a chosen plant is **die Karte** — a page a new club
member can read in two minutes: stage history with dates, cumulative product totals this run, every
feed with who gave it, training events, issues, and a photo strip. Cumulative totals are first-class,
not an export:

```
A3 · Wedding Cake · Blüte, Tag 12 · Light-Mix, 11 L
  Diesen Lauf bekommen:  Bio-Grow 84 ml · Bio-Bloom 46 ml · Wasser 31,5 L
  Zuletzt: 12.09. 19:04 · 1,3 L · Anna
  Offener Zettel: „A3 nicht gießen — zu nass" (Tom, 10.09.)
```

## 7. Watering & fertilising

No irrigation hardware exists. This is a capture-and-recall problem, and capture speed is the whole
game — with one exception, capture *safety*, which matters more.

### 7.1 The scheme prefills; it does not instruct

Selected once, in Gießen L1, as **medium + regime the way growers say it**:
`Biobizz Light-Mix` · `Biobizz All-Mix` · `Floragard Light` · `Green House Powder Feeding` ·
`BioTabs` · `Lucas` · `Nur Wasser`. Stored as `Scheme` docs in Mongo — never in the bundle — each with
`source_url`, `retrieved_at`, `chart_version`. Brand names are plain text; no logos, no brand colours,
no bottle photography, and no brand name inside a Terp Control feature name.

The scheme advances **by feed events, not by weeks**: `Schritt 4 von 12`, with a soft calendar
mapping, because people feed Tue/Fri and slip. An `All-Mix` scheme's first steps carry no dose at all
and its level 0 line reads exactly that:

```
│ GIESSEN & DÜNGEN                            ⌄ │
│ Noch nicht düngen — All-Mix ist vorgedüngt.   │
│ Ab etwa Woche 3. Heute nur Wasser.            │
```

`EC` is stored canonically as mS/cm with `ec_basis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source'`, because CANNA
publishes EC+ and a UI that alarms against the raw chart number is actively wrong. A bare "ppm" is
never rendered without its scale (500/640/700).

### 7.2 Capture: two taps

1. **Tap 1** — the green `Gegossen` button at Gießen level 0. A sheet slides up, fully prefilled:
   actor (last used, big), all plants selected, volume from last time, products and doses from the
   scheme step.
2. **Tap 2** — `Speichern`. Done. Sheet closes, the cell's L0 line becomes
   `Zuletzt: gerade eben · 4,0 L · Bio-Grow 2 ml/L`.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Gegossen                            Abbrechen│
│  [ Anna ] [ Tom ] [ Kai ]        ← nur wenn >1│
│  ● A1   ● A2   ● A3              ← nur wenn >0│
│  Menge          4,0 L                       ⌄ │  tap → value-edit-row expands in place
│  Bio-Grow       2 ml/L  = 8 ml              ⌄ │
│  Bio-Bloom      1 ml/L  = 4 ml              ⌄ │
│  EC / pH        —                           ⌄ │  optional, absent until tapped
│  Notiz                                      ⌄ │
│  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │              Speichern                  │  │
│  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The sheet **is a cell** — same chevrons, same grammar. The club's record-keeper opens EC, pH, runoff
EC and a per-plant volume split; the stoner never sees a table of ml/L because those rows are one
line each showing the prefilled result, not the arithmetic. Same sheet, no mode.

### 7.3 The double-feed guard — a third, deliberate tap

Before commit, the server is asked whether the same plants were fed inside the device's window
(default 6 h; `2 | 6 | 12 | 24` in Ziel L1). If so:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Schon gefüttert                              │
│  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert         │
│  (4,0 L · Bio-Grow 2 ml/L).                   │
│                                               │
│  [ Abbrechen ]        [ Trotzdem speichern ]  │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Soft block, never hard: the second feed is sometimes correct. It names **who** and **when** and
**what**, because that is what makes the decision possible. It fires for a solo grower too (a stoned
double-water is the commonest way a home grow dies), which is why it is not a club feature.

### 7.4 Offline, and honest about it

`Entry.client_id` is minted on the phone before the request. The server upserts on
`{device_id, client_id}`, so a retry can never double-log. That is groundwork, not offline capability:
**v1 has no service worker and capture requires a connection.** What v1 does have is detection —
when two phones sync overlapping feeds after a signal drop, the second one is accepted and flagged:
`Mögliche Doppelgabe am 14.09. — beide Einträge behalten? [Behalten] [Einen entfernen]`. Preventing it
offline needs the service worker; v1.1.

### 7.5 The cumulative question

Gießen L2 is **das Konto** — the answer nobody could give:

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is the headline, and it ships in October — a film someone can actually hold.

### 8.1 Bild's depth ladder

- **L0** — the latest frame, full cell width, plus `Vor 4 Min · Licht 100 % · 26,1 °C`. Stale or
  offline frames carry the server's burnt-in caption.
- **L1 — die Zahlen zu diesem Bild.** The complete reading at that frame's timestamp, `‹ ›` arrows to
  step frames, and `Was war anders?`: this day's deviation from the run's median for temp, RH, VPD and
  light hours. **Image → data is the entry gesture**, which is the direction everyone else omitted.
- **L2 — der Filmstreifen.** A horizontal thumbnail strip on the *same x-scale* as the Jetzt chart,
  sharing one cursor via a `TimeCursorService`. Dragging the strip moves the numbers and the chart's
  crosshair; dragging the chart moves the strip. Uneven capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivered as
  **one sprite + JSON index per window** (100 tiles, 256×160), not N image requests. Below it: the
  three rolling films (`1d`/`1w`/`1m`) inline, `Zwei Bilder vergleichen ›`, and `Film erstellen ›`.
- **L3 — Herkunft.** Capture interval, frames stored, frames culled *with reasons and counts*,
  thinning tiers, storage in MB, camera-silence state.
- **L4** — the frame list with timestamps and single-frame download.

### 8.2 Zwei Bilder vergleichen

Pick two frames from the strip → side-by-side with a drag slider, and underneath, the deltas:

```
  10.08. 12:00  ↔  12.09. 12:00        33 Tage
  Höhe geschätzt —          Temperatur ⌀ 24,8 → 25,9 °C
  VPD ⌀ 1,05 → 1,38 kPa     Licht 18 h → 12 h
  Dazwischen: 14 Gaben · 240 ml Bio-Grow · Blüte seit 12.08.
```

Two images, one CSS slider, one aggregate query. It is the cheapest thing in this document that earns
the word "correlation".

### 8.3 The film builder — v1

`Film erstellen` opens a sheet:

```
  Zeitraum   [ Ganzer Lauf ▾ ]   14.07. – 25.08.   (Blüte · Letzte 7 Tage · Frei)
  Länge      [ 30 s ▾ ]          ≈ 750 Bilder
  Nächte     [ ○ aus ]           ein Bild pro Nacht behalten
  Beschriftung [ Datum + Tag ▾ ] aus · Datum · Datum + Tag + °C
  Format     [ 1920×1080 ▾ ]     1080×1080 (quadratisch)
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │              Film erstellen             │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`POST /device/:id/film` creates a `FilmRender { film_id, device_id, from, to, spec, image_id,
share_key?, expires_at?, bytes, created_at }` and queues a job. Progress is a line in Bild L2
(`Film wird erstellt … 40 %`); when done the cell shows the player and three rows.

**How it leaves the product.** Three real exits, no login required for the last two:

1. `Video sichern` → `GET /film/:film_id/download` (same origin, `Content-Disposition: attachment;
   filename="terpcontrol-zelt1-bluete-2026-07-14_2026-08-25.mp4"`). On iOS Safari a forced download is
   unreliable, so the button opens the file in the player with the copy
   `Lange auf das Video tippen → „In Fotos sichern"`. Honest, tested, no lie.
2. `Link zum Teilen` → `https://terpcontrol.cloud/f/<film_id>?k=<hmac>` — **unauthenticated**, serves
   the mp4 with `Content-Type: video/mp4`, optional expiry, revocable from `/shares`. It plays in
   Telegram, in Discord, in a forum post, on someone's mother's phone. A share that requires a login
   is not a share.
3. `Link kopieren` for the Telegram/Discord case, where the platform fetches the URL inline. No
   branded footer, no watermark, no "Powered by" — § 6 KCanG makes an auto-appended brand string on
   thousands of German cannabis posts the single riskiest string in the product.

**Codec, stated precisely.** Exports are **H.264 High, yuv420p, 25 fps, CRF 20, `+faststart`, no
audio**. The existing pipeline encodes `libx265 -crf 30` (`image.service.ts:578`), which will not play
in Firefox and fails on many Android browsers — acceptable for an internal rolling preview, fatal for
a file the customer sends to a friend. A 30 s 1080p H.264 CRF-20 film is ~6–9 MB, which is under
Telegram's inline limit and Discord's free 20 MB.

### 8.4 The content problem: culling and normalisation

A tent timelapse is mostly garbage. Culling runs server-side at build time, in this order, using
`sharp` (already a dependency) on an 8×8 downsampled luma grid plus `stats()`:

1. **Dunkel** — `out_light == 0` at that timestamp **and** mean luma < 25 → drop. (Half of every day
   is black; this is the single biggest win.) With `Nächte ein`, keep one per night.
2. **Tür / Fremdlicht** — mean luma deviates > 35 % from the median of its ±15 min neighbours, **or**
   the mean B/R ratio shifts > 40 % (daylight through an opened door under magenta LED) → drop, and
   drop the following frame too (somebody is still in shot).
3. **Bewegung** — the 8×8 grid differs from *both* neighbours by mean |Δ| > 18 while the neighbours
   differ from each other by < 6 → something transient (a hand, a watering can) → drop.
4. **Doppelt** — differs from the previously kept frame by mean |Δ| < 2 → drop (frozen camera, nothing
   happening).
5. **Unscharf / Kondens** — Laplacian variance (via `sharp.convolve` + `stats`) below 30 % of that
   camera's 7-day median → drop.

Survivors are **normalised per light-state cohort**, never globally. Cohorts are `out_light` buckets
(`aus`, `1–40`, `41–80`, `81–100`). Within a cohort: a per-frame linear gain bringing median luma to
the cohort's rolling 7-day median (clamped ±40 %), and grey-world white balance. Normalising a magenta
LED frame against a daylight frame destroys the look; normalising it against its own cohort kills the
flicker and keeps it.

**The nudged camera.** No stabilisation pass in v1. Instead, when the best-match offset of the 8×8
grid shifts by more than 6 % of frame width, the builder marks a **Schnitt**, inserts a 0.4 s
crossfade, and writes a machine diary entry `Kamera hat sich bewegt` at that timestamp. The user
learns the camera moved on the 12th — which is more useful than silently warping the frame.

Every cull is counted and shown in Bild L3, because a culler you cannot audit is a black box:

```
  4.180 Bilder im Zeitraum · 762 im Film
  Verworfen: 2.910 dunkel · 214 Fremdlicht · 168 Bewegung · 96 doppelt · 30 unscharf
  1 Schnitt am 12.09. 20:02 (Kamera bewegt)
```

### 8.5 The camera alarm

The hourly compress loop gains one check: **device online, newest jpeg older than 3 h** → one
`DeviceLog` `message-camera-silent` at severity 1, fired once through the existing notification path
with a cooldown. Bild's level 0 then reads `Kamera stumm seit 3 Tagen — letztes Bild 09.09. 14:20` in
warning colour. Discovering a hole in the middle of a harvest film is the worst moment this product
can produce, and preventing it costs about twenty lines.

### 8.6 Storage, stated

At 30 s capture, a frame ≈ 90 KB, thinned by the existing tiers (1/min after a day, 1/5 min after a
week, 1/15 min after a month, 1/h after 90 days). A 12-week grow settles at roughly **1.1–1.4 GB per
device** in Mongo before thinning catches up, ~180 MB after. Films add ~8 MB each and are capped at 10
retained per device. This number belongs on the user's screen (Bild L3), not only in this document.

## 9. Clubs

### 9.1 Attribution in v1, authorisation in v1.1 — said plainly

**Attribution is a label; authorisation is a gate.** v1 ships the label and does not pretend to ship
the gate. `Actor { actor_id, device_id, name, colour }` — names the owner types once in Ziel L1
(`Wer ist am Zelt: Anna · Tom · Kai · + Name`). No accounts, no invitations, no email, no touch to
`auth.middleware.ts`. The picker appears in the capture sheet only when more than one Actor exists
(reveal by data). The last-used name is remembered per browser and shown large, so a wrong attribution
is visible before saving. Stamped onto `Entry.actor_id` and `Condition.opened_by`.

Cost: about two days. It gives per-person history, participation records (§ 17 Abs. 2 KCanG's
*eigenhändig* requirement, which is the one legally-motivated multi-user need worth owning), and
the double-feed guard's ability to say *who*.

v1.1 adds `Membership { device_id, user_id, role }` and rewrites `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` plus
~10 owner-scoped queries. It is not a flag and it does not get bolted on in six weeks.

### 9.2 The double-feed problem

Covered in §7.3 and §7.4. The three parts: a soft block naming who/when/what inside a window; an
idempotent `client_id` so a retry cannot forge a second feed; and post-hoc detection with a
keep/remove choice when two phones sync overlapping records. v1 detects the offline case; it does not
prevent it, and the document says so.

### 9.3 Conditions — the Zettel cell

The standing fact that gets taped to a tent door and currently lives in WhatsApp:

```
│ ZETTEL                                      ⌃ │
│ 2 offen.                                      │
│  CO₂-Flasche fast leer        Tom · 08.09.  ✓ │
│  A3 nicht gießen — zu nass    Tom · 10.09.  ✓ │
│  + Zettel schreiben                           │
```

Open until someone closes it (`✓` → `closed_by`, `closed_at`). A Zettel attached to a plant appears on
that plant's card. Open Zettel are counted in the Dach's roll-up sentence when nothing worse is
happening. Closed ones stay in Verlauf.

### 9.4 Privacy posture

Default is privacy-maximal and it is not a mode either — it is a set of defaults with visible
switches in Ziel L1:

- **No real names required, ever.** Actors are free text; "Tom" can be "T".
- **Harvest weight is off by default.** `record_weights` per device, default `false`. When off, the
  harvest entry has a date and no weight field. Turning it on is a deliberate act with plain copy
  about why it is off.
- **Plant counts** are stored because the product needs them, never exported by default.
- **Export carries `NONE | PSEUDONYM | ANONYM`, defaulting to PSEUDONYM** (actor names → `Person A`).
- **No location, no GPS, no IP-geolocation, no analytics SDK, no ad SDK.** Nothing infers where a
  device is.
- **The owner can delete anything**, including machine entries. Tamper-evidence protects a regulated
  club against its own staff and victimises an unregulated grower; immutability is club-scoped and
  opt-in, never global.
- **Retention becomes visible**: Ziel L1 shows `Rohdaten 90 Tage, danach 5-Minuten-Mittel · Bilder
  3 Jahre`, and a one-tap `Alles exportieren` sits next to `Alles löschen`. Delete without export is
  the wrong half of data portability.

The product refuses to become the club's § 26 system of record: no per-member dispensing quantities,
no THC content, no membership numbers, no dates of birth. That market is served by Cannanas and
420cloud at €0.50–1.00/member/month and winning it would mean owning 23 authority export formats.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app every few days. `/list` with one device lands him
straight on the screen. He reads the Dach sentence and the Jetzt line and closes it. He has no
sockets, so Ziel shows no targets and Jetzt's verdict is descriptive —
`Die Nächte schwanken 6,1 °C, die Tage nur 1,8 °C.` — which is a real reading of his data with no goal
required. Once a month he taps Jetzt twice to see the 30-day chart. He never learns that plants,
feeds or Actors exist, and nothing on his screen refers to them.

**2 — Monitoring with a camera.** Same, plus Bild is the second cell and shows a picture the moment
he opens the app. On day 84 he taps `Film erstellen`, picks `Ganzer Lauf` and 30 s, waits about two
minutes, and gets a 7 MB H.264 file with the date burnt in that he saves to his camera roll and pastes
into a Telegram group as a link. If his camera dies on day 40 he finds out on day 40 from the
`Kamera stumm` line and the notification, not on day 84 from a hole.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (heater only).** His humidity row has no target and no band, because
`parseSocketRoles` says he owns no dehumidifier — it is drawn as a plain series with no judgement. His
temperature band is a **floor**, labelled `nur heizen`. In July, when the tent hits 34 °C, the lane
goes hatched, `am Anschlag` appears, and the verdict says `kommt nicht nach` instead of scoring him
red for three months. He is never offered a dehumidifier alarm preset.

**4 — Full tent.** Ziel L1 is one card per bound socket: role, state, duty cycle today and this week,
its own `minimalDehumidifierOffTime` / `maxDehumidifySeconds` / `targetHumidityDiff` (live firmware
keys that have never had a UI), last command failure, and `Kurz testen`. Ziel L2's outputs lane shows
`Kühlen` and `Entfeuchten` as separate derived series, so he can finally see the heater and the
cooler fighting — the antagonism is an interval intersection the server computes and states as a
sentence.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** He lives at level 3 and 4. Herkunft gives him bucket size, function, sample
count, coverage, gaps and the device clock offset; Rohdaten gives him the samples, a CSV, and the
exact API call as a copyable `curl`. That is not a developer mode — it is the bottom of the same
ladder his girlfriend stops at on the first rung. He gets a documented read API in v1 (the endpoints
the cells already call, given an OpenAPI page) and outbound generic webhooks; write access to outputs
is v1.1 and the document does not pretend otherwise.

**6 — Club.** Three names in the Actor picker. Every feed is stamped. The double-feed guard names Anna
and the time. `Das Konto` answers "how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had this cycle, and from whom" in one
table. Zettel replaces the WhatsApp group for standing facts. They still share one login in v1, and
the document says so rather than implying otherwise.

**7 — Old device owner.** Fewer cells, the same grammar, his old settings form intact inside Ziel L1,
his old routes still working, and a `Alte Ansicht ›` link for 30 days. Nothing he does today stops
working.

**STONER lens:** seven sentences, one green button, fixed order, nothing moves, nothing collapses by
itself, and no tap below the first level can change anything.
**NORMAL lens:** it looks like a modern app, the picture is near the top, the film is real and
shareable, and the numbers are next to the words.
**TECHIE lens:** the number, its provenance and its samples are two and three taps from the verdict,
in the same place, in every cell.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **Accreted depth becomes a wall.** A user who has opened five cells to level 2 has a page four
   screens long, forever, with no ranking to help. `Alles zu` is one tap away and `3 offen` is always
   visible, but the failure is real and it is the direct cost of refusing adaptive ordering.
2. **There is no fast way to the bottom cell.** Reaching Verlauf means scrolling past everything. A
   stacking row of collapsed sticky headers helps and costs layout complexity; a jump bar would be a
   nav bar, which contradicts the thesis. This is the concept's worst ergonomic flaw and it gets worse
   the more cells are open.
3. **"Depth is read-only" is broken twice** — the remedy button at level 0 and the setpoint rows at
   Ziel level 1. Both are defensible (level 0 and level 1 are the shallow end) but the rule as stated
   is not airtight, and a critic will say so correctly.
4. **The culling thresholds are guesses.** Luma < 25, 35 % neighbour deviation, |Δ| > 18 — none of
   these has been run against a real tent. The plausible failure is over-culling a dim veg tent to
   nothing. Mitigation: the counts are shown in L3, and the builder refuses to produce a film from
   fewer than 60 survivors with the message `Zu wenige brauchbare Bilder (41)`. It still needs a week
   of real frames before launch, which the schedule barely has.
5. **Film rendering is a queue problem nobody has today.** `pLimit(10)` and a 15-minute ffmpeg timeout
   are sized for 1-day rolling clips, not for 700-frame 12-week builds with an ffmpeg filter chain.
   Two clubs pressing `Film erstellen` at once on a shared host is a real capacity risk, and the
   design has a progress line but no back-pressure story.
6. **Seven cells is a lot of new Angular 15 NgModule surface** with no OnPush discipline in the
   codebase and a mandatory `KeyedCache` for every derived-array getter. The one screen is one
   component tree, so a change-detection mistake in one cell costs the whole page.
7. **Sticky depth is per browser.** A club iPad on the shelf shows whoever touched it last their
   layout. Small, but exactly the kind of thing that reads as "the app changed by itself" to the next
   person.
8. **Two of the largest v1 items are camera-only.** If the camera attach rate on the first 150 units
   is low, the film builder and the compare view are the most expensive things in the release serving
   the fewest users. The owner's judgement that the camera is the selling point is what carries this,
   and if that judgement is wrong the scope is misallocated.
9. **Wrapping the legacy settings forms ships the old mess inside the new frame.** A plug owner's
   Ziel level 1 will look nothing like a controller's. That is the price of not breaking type 7 in six
   weeks, and it is visibly a compromise.
10. **The verdict sentences are generated German prose from templates.** Getting them to read naturally
    across the combinations of stale/absent/saturated/no-goal is fiddly, easy to get subtly wrong, and
    doubles as English strings. There is no ICU pluralisation available, so plurals are hand-rolled —
    which is where this kind of copy usually breaks first.
