# C28 — "Der Stapel"

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** the diary is an inbox of cards the machine noticed, and you
curate it by triaging, not by configuring.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Terp Control notices things and hands them to you one card at a time; you keep, annotate or discard —
and that triage, not a settings screen, is what turns a stream of telemetry into a diary worth reading.**

The owner's selling point is that the product "does the diary keeping for you". Every other diary concept
here takes that literally and *writes* the diary. The problem with a machine-written diary is that it is
unedited: it records the night the door was open and the hour the sensor glitched with the same confidence
as the day you flipped to 12/12, and nobody can tell which sentences to believe six weeks later.

Der Stapel takes the other half of the job. The machine's output is not a record — it is a **proposal**.
Each proposal is a card: one German sentence, one number, and the evidence folded underneath. You swipe.
Kept cards are the diary. Dismissed cards are still stored, just not part of the record. If you never
swipe at all, cards file themselves after seven days with a sane default per kind — so the lazy user's
diary is exactly the machine's unedited version, which is what he would have got anyway, and the diligent
user's is better because he pruned it.

The consequence that makes this more than an inbox metaphor: **triage propagates.** The chart's annotation
rail draws kept cards only. The week sheet counts kept cards only. The export contains kept cards only.
The timelapse's captions come from kept cards only. One thumb gesture, performed at the tent, decides what
the whole product says about that moment forever after. Nothing else in the product has a "hide this"
control, because triage is the only one it needs.

## 2. Information architecture

Four destinations in an `ion-tabs` bar. That is a new navigation shell — today there is no tab bar at all
(`ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu`, dossier §1.3) — so the menu survives unchanged behind the hamburger for
`/list`, `/shares`, `/account`, admin links and the dark-mode toggle.

```
/list                          device list (unchanged; 1 device → straight to the Stapel)
/device/:id/stapel      TAB 1  Der Stapel  — home. Bands: JETZT / HEUTE FÄLLIG / AUFGEFALLEN / ABGELEGT
   ↳ scroll past ABGELEGT      Alles — the full chronological stream. Same cards, no bands, filter chips.
   ↳ /karte/:card_id           one card, unfolded, full screen (deep-linkable, shareable)
   ↳ /pflanze/:plant_id        one plant: totals, its cards, its frames  (only reachable if a plant exists)
/device/:id/verlauf     TAB 2  the chart: small multiples, setpoint bands, film strip, annotation rail
/device/:id/film        TAB 3  Der Film — timelapses. HIDDEN when the device reports no camera.
/device/:id/zelt        TAB 4  the tent: targets, sockets, alarms, camera, regime, people, rules
   ↳ existing settings components live here unchanged for fan/light/plug/fridge
/device/:id/testmode           unchanged, admin only, reached from Zelt
```

Rules for the shell:

- **The tab bar is capability-derived, not preference-derived.** `Film` is present iff
  `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is set or `cloudSettings.rtspStream` is non-empty. Fan/light/plug owners get
  three tabs. Nobody is offered a tab that would be empty.
- **There is no Pflanzen tab.** Plants surface as a chip row at the top of the Stapel, and that row does
  not render until at least one `Plant` exists. Reveal by data.
- **There is no "Alles" tab either.** The archive is the bottom of the Stapel. You scroll into it. That is
  the concept's reveal-by-depth: the home screen and the diary are one document, and the difference between
  "glance" and "research" is how far you scroll.
- Share links land on `/verlauf` or `/stapel` per the existing `SharePage` union (extend
  `'charts' | 'diary'` to `'charts' | 'diary' | 'stapel'`, defaulting old links to `stapel`). All three
  restricted modes (share, demo, public) are checked per band: a share viewer sees the bands but no triage
  buttons and no `+` FAB.

### 3.2 The four bands, and why they never move

| Band | Contains | Order | Auto-files? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `JETZT` | exactly one card, the tent right now | fixed | never; not triageable |
| `HEUTE FÄLLIG` | due water/feed cards + every open `zustand` | oldest due first | never — a due card waits |
| `AUFGEFALLEN` | everything the rules noticed | rank, then newest first | after 7 days, per kind |
| `ABGELEGT` | everything already triaged, newest first | strictly chronological | — |

The band a card is in is a property of its **kind and state**, never of user weights. A card never jumps
bands while you look at it. The stack is built once on screen entry and on pull-to-refresh; the footer
prints `Stapel gebaut 14:03` so the user knows it is a snapshot. Nothing reflows underneath a thumb.

**There is no unread badge and no push for the Stapel.** The counts next to band titles are plain grey
text, never red. Push notifications remain what they are today: alarms only. An inbox that nags is an
inbox people close.

### 3.3 Copy for the states that are not "normal"

**Stale data (last sample 4 min–10 min old).** Header: `⏱ Zuletzt vor 6 Min`. The JETZT card keeps its
numbers but greys them to `--ion-color-medium` and appends one line: `Werte sind 6 Minuten alt.` No card
is generated from data in this window — rules require ≥ 80 % coverage of their window.

**Offline (> 10 min).** Header: `⚠ Offline seit 14:20`. JETZT card:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Offline seit 14:20 (vor 2 Std 10 Min)     │
│  Letzte Werte: 24,1 °C · 61 % · Licht an   │

│  fehlt. Der Controller regelt selbst.      │
│  [ Was tun? ]                              │

```
That last sentence is load-bearing and true: the firmware regulates without the cloud. A `geraet-offline`
card enters `AUFGEFALLEN` after 30 min and, unlike other kinds, never auto-files as `weg`.

**No data at all (fresh device, no history).**

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Noch nichts gemessen.                     │
│  Sobald der Controller Werte schickt,      │
│  steht hier, wie es im Zelt aussieht.      │
```

This card is the only one that is *never* generated twice and disappears on the first real card.

**Camera present but silent > 3 h.** A `kamera-still` card in `AUFGEFALLEN`, and a real alarm (§8.5):
`📷 Keine Bilder seit 03:40 — Kamera prüfen`.

**Sensor missing (CO₂ not upgraded).** No CO₂ number on the JETZT card, no `co2` rules, no CO₂-refill card
kind. Reveal by capability, from `deviceHasCo2()`, which already exists.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

### 4.1 The mechanism, in one sentence

**Every card is a claim on the front and its own proof on the back, and the tap that opens the proof is
the same tap that opens the annotation field — so the stoned user and the techie perform the identical
gesture and both get what they came for.**

### 4.2 Why the proof has to be there

A card asserts something: *"Nachts 6 Std zu feucht — 68 % statt 50 %."* Before you keep that in your
permanent record, you are entitled to see what it was based on. So the back of the card carries the rule's
**inputs**, not a prettier version of its output:

```
← Zurück                                   Karte
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
🌙  Nachts 6 Std zu feucht
    68 % statt 50 %  ·  Di 22:10 – Mi 04:20

┌───────────────┬───────────────┐
│  22:10        │  04:20        │      ← the two frames (§8.6)
│  [ Bild ]     │  [ Bild ]     │
│  67 % 21,8 °C │  69 % 20,9 °C │
└───────────────┴───────────────┘

  Feuchte, Di 20:00 – Mi 08:00
  80│      ╭──────────╮
    │ ┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈  Ziel 50 %
  40│─╯                 ╰──
    20:00        00:00       08:00
  [ Im Verlauf öffnen ]

  Regel   Nacht-Feuchte
  löst aus wenn  Feuchte > Ziel + 10 %   [ 10 % ▾ ]
  mindestens     120 Min zusammenhängend [ 120 ▾ ]
  Abdeckung      98 % (471 von 480 Werten)
  Ziel kam aus   night.humidity = 50
  [ Rohwerte ansehen ]   [ Als CSV ]

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Notiz
  [ Entfeuchter war aus, Tür stand offen___ ]
  [A1] [A2] [A3]        📷 Foto      🕑 Di 22:10
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ┌──────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────────┐
  │  ✓ Behalten      │  │  ✕ Weg               │
  └──────────────────┘  └──────────────────────┘
```

The stoner opened this because he wanted to type "Tür stand offen" and the note field is at the bottom
where his thumb is. The techie opened it because he wanted to know whether the rule averaged or sampled
raw, and the answer is four lines above, with the threshold editable in place. **Neither of them chose a
mode. They chose a scroll position.**

### 4.3 Four reinforcing non-mode mechanisms

1. **Reveal by capability.** Card *kinds* only exist for kit the device reports. No dehumidifier socket →
   no `entfeuchter-*` rules, no humidity-deviation cards, no dehumidifier tile on JETZT. This fixes the
   dossier's P0 #3 (capability collapsed to three buckets, fails open) by using the per-role CSV
   `hardwareInfo.sockets` directly and treating an **absent** key as `monitor`, not `full`.
2. **Reveal by data.** No `Plant` → no plant chips anywhere, and `Card.plant_ids = []` reads as "the whole
   tent" in every report. No second `Actor` → no attribution UI at all (§9).
3. **Progressive precision on the front of the card.** Every card front is *verdict · number · window*:
   `Nachts 6 Std zu feucht · 68 % statt 50 % · Di 22:10–04:20`. The verdict is readable at arm's length in
   the dark; the number is right next to it; the series is one tap in. Nobody is denied the number and
   nobody is forced to parse it.
4. **Ranked density.** Every card is on one screen, ordered by what matters now. Attention is the filter,
   not a toggle.

### 4.4 The adaptive part, and its fences

The brief warns that adaptive ordering hurts the impaired user. It does, if you let it run loose. Here is
exactly how far it runs:

```ts
UserCardWeight { user_id, device_id, kind, weight }   // integer, clamped [-3, +3], default 0
```

- `Ergänzen` (you wrote a note on a card of this kind) → `weight += 1`
- `Weg` → `weight -= 1`, but **only down to −1 silently**
- Below −1 requires an explicit card:
  `Du hast „Nacht-Feuchte" dreimal weggelegt.  [ Seltener zeigen ]  [ So lassen ]`

Fences, all of them enforced server-side in the stack builder:

- Weight reorders **only inside `AUFGEFALLEN`**, over ~9 kinds. `JETZT` is pinned. `HEUTE FÄLLIG` is
  ordered by due time. `ABGELEGT` is strictly chronological.
- Weight **never removes a kind.** −3 means: generated at a coarser threshold (the rule's window doubles)
  and sorted last. The card still exists, still appears in `Alles`, still appears in the export.
- Every weight is a row in `Zelt → Was dir auffällt`, in plain German, with `Zurücksetzen`:
  `Nacht-Feuchte — seltener (du hast 4 weggelegt)   [Zurücksetzen]`
- **Weights are per user, per device.** In a club, one member's triage does not reorder another's stack.
  (Triage *state* is shared; the ordering weight is not. §13 names the tension this creates.)

That is the honest line: **a mode changes what the app can do; this changes what it says first, visibly,
reversibly, and never below the level of "still there".**

- **Goal vs actual.** A stepped dashed `markLine` at `day.temperature` / `night.temperature`, stepping at
  `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC → rendered in the device timezone, explicitly
  named in the axis label so a DST flip does not silently drift). Behind it a `markArea` band. Band source,
  one at a time and labelled: **alarm thresholds** > **setpoint ± tolerance** > **stage `vpdRange`**. The
  deviation is a signed fill between actual and setpoint, never a fill to the axis.
- **Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0` where available, falling back to the schedule. A failed
  contactor then shows as a *missing* band — a diagnostic, not decoration.
- **Actual is min/max envelope + mean**, `sampling: 'minmax'`. A single mean line lies about a bang-bang
  controller.
- **The annotation rail is the concept's chart contribution.** It draws **kept cards only**. Dismissed
  cards cluster into one grey `(+3 weggelegt)` chip per pixel-bin that expands for the session. Lifecycle
  stage changes are full-height dashed lines. Two rows: row 1 human cards, row 2 machine cards.
  This is the mechanism nobody else has: *the chart is legible because you already threw away the noise,
  at the tent, with one thumb.*
- Mobile: one finger = page scroll, two fingers = pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap/long-press =
  crosshair. Axis labels shorten to 3 ticks below 320 px; they are never hidden.
- Legend/series names go through `ngx-translate` — today they are hardcoded English (dossier §1.6).

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New collections

```ts
type CardKind =
  | 'jetzt' | 'zustand' | 'notiz'                        // human / standing
  | 'giessen-faellig' | 'giessen' | 'fuettern-faellig' | 'fuettern'
  | 'abweichung' | 'phase' | 'licht-plan' | 'geraet-offline' | 'socket-weg'
  | 'kamera-still' | 'kamera-verschoben' | 'film' | 'woche' | 'co2-leer';

interface Card {
  card_id: string;
  device_id: string;                  // indexed with t
  run_id?: string;
  kind: CardKind;
  t: number;                          // the moment the card is ABOUT
  tEnd?: number;                      // for cards about a window
  createdAt: number;
  state: 'offen' | 'behalten' | 'weg' | 'auto-behalten' | 'auto-weg';
  triagedBy?: string;                 // actor_id
  triagedAt?: number;
  claim_key: string;                  // 'message-card-abweichung-humidity-night'
  claim_args: Record<string, string | number>;
  rank: number;                       // computed at build time
  proof?: {                           // typed per kind, all optional
    rule?: { id: string; params: Record<string, number>; coverage: number };
    series?: { measure: string; from: number; to: number }[];
    setpoint?: { key: string; value: number };
    frames?: { image_id: string; t: number }[];
    outputs?: string[];
  };
  payload?: WaterPayload | FeedPayload | ConditionPayload | FilmPayload;
  note?: string;
  images?: string[];                  // image_id, reuses the existing Image collection
  plant_ids?: string[];               // [] == the whole tent
  actor_id?: string;
  dedupe_key: string;                 // unique index with device_id
}

interface Plant  { plant_id; device_id; label; strain?; startedAt; medium_key?;
                   potLitres?; run_id?; endedAt?; }
interface Run    { run_id; device_id; label; startedAt; endedAt?; stage; stageSince; }
interface Actor  { actor_id; device_id; name; colour; createdAt; retiredAt?; }
interface UserCardWeight { user_id; device_id; kind; weight; }
```

All optional-by-default, read-time normalised, no required field added anywhere — there is no migration
tooling in this codebase (dossier §1.8) and this design does not need any.

### 6.2 Relationship to `DeviceLog`

`DeviceLog` stays exactly as it is and keeps serving boot messages, MQTT errors, alarms, recipe steps and
config diffs. Two bridges:

1. **Historic rows render as cards.** Any `DeviceLog` row is projected into a read-only card with
   `state:'auto-behalten'`, no triage buttons, `kind` derived from `categories[0]`. So a five-year-old
   diary opens on day one of the new app with nothing lost, and the legacy `message-*` translation service
   and its seven English-prose regexes keep working untouched.
2. **A kept card writes a mirror row.** `POST /cards/:id/keep` also inserts a `DeviceLog`
   `{categories:['diary','diary-card', kind], data:{card_id}, deleted:true}` so the existing device-overview
   log stream, existing share links and the existing grow report keep functioning during the transition.
   `data` is `Schema.Types.Mixed` with zero validation, so `data.card_id` is legal today.

Two legacy bugs this design must fix or it reads as data loss: `deleted` does not mean deleted
(dossier §1.5 #1) and the server's own stage entries are written without the `'diary'` prefix
(#2). Both are one-line fixes in `device.service.ts` plus a boot-time backfill.

### 6.3 Multi-plant, and how ignoring it costs nothing

A plant is created in exactly one place: the `⊕` FAB → `Pflanze anlegen` → a label and nothing else
required (`A1`, or `Northern Lights`). If no plant exists:

- the chip row does not render,
- `plant_ids` is `[]` on every card,
- every report, every total and every export reads `[]` as `alle Pflanzen` and prints nothing about plants,
- no card kind mentions a plant.

A grower who never makes a plant sees a product with no plant concept in it. A club with 12 plants sees
chips on every capture sheet and per-plant totals. **Same code path, different data.**

The `Run` replaces `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` — the browser-side algorithm that forks a grow's history
when you rename a strain (dossier §1.5). It becomes a one-time backfill, not a runtime heuristic.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

### 7.1 The regime — selected once, prefills forever

```ts
FeedRegime { regime_id; device_id; run_id?; medium_key; feed_key;
             strength: 'leicht'|'normal'|'kräftig'; flowerWeeks: number;
             rhythmDays: number; startedAt }

FeedPlan {                             // Mongo content collection, never in the bundle
  plan_key; label; kind: 'medium'|'feed';
  source_url; retrieved_at; chart_version;
  startFeedingWeek: number;            // All-Mix = 4, Light-Mix = 2, BioTabs = 99 (never)
  steps: [{ weekFrom; weekTo; stage;
            products: [{ name; mlPerL; note? }];
            ecTarget?; ec_basis: 'absolut'|'plus-leitungswasser'; phTarget? }]

```

Selection happens once, in `Zelt → Substrat & Dünger`, and it is phrased the way growers say it out loud:

```
  Worin wächst es?      [ Biobizz All-Mix ▾ ]
  Womit düngst du?      [ Biobizz Bio-Grow / Bio-Bloom ▾ ]
  Stärke                [ leicht ] [ normal ] [ kräftig ]
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────
  All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt. Bis Woche 4
  wird nicht gedüngt — nur gegossen.
  Quelle: biobizz.com · geprüft 12.06.2026
```

Brand names are plain text labels only. Numbers live in Mongo with `source_url` + `retrieved_at`, so any
brand comes out in one DB update. `ec_basis` exists because CANNA publishes EC**+** (add your tap water),
and alarming against a raw chart value is actively wrong.

### 7.2 Capture flow, with tap counts

**Watering, with a due card on the Stapel — 2 taps.**

1. Tap `Gegossen` on the `giessen-faellig` card. A sheet slides up, everything prefilled from the last
   watering: all plants selected, `2,0 l`, `jetzt`, actor = the person on the header chip.
2. Tap `Passt`. Saved. Sheet closes. The due card becomes a `giessen` card in `ABGELEGT`.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Gegossen                          ✕       │
│  [A1] [A2] [A3]         ← tap to deselect  │
│  Menge     [ − ]  2,0 l  [ + ]             │
│  Zeit      jetzt · 19:42                   │
│  ▾ Mehr:  Ablauf-EC, pH, Notiz, Foto       │
│  ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │              Passt                   │  │
│  └──────────────────────────────────────┘  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Feeding, with a due card — 2 taps.** Identical, but the sheet is prefilled from the regime's row for the

**Deviating from the plan — 4 taps.** …→ tap the `2 ml/l` chip → drag → `Passt`. The deviation is stored


### 7.5 Capture in a cellar

`@angular/service-worker` is added — a new dependency and build change, and it is in scope precisely
because "capture works on the tent floor" is a promise this concept makes. Scope: **capture only.** An
IndexedDB outbox holds pending keeps; the app renders them immediately as `state:'offen', pending:true`
with a `⧗` marker. Replay on reconnect.

Double-logging is prevented by construction, not by luck:

```
dedupe_key = sha1(device_id | kind | actor_id | floor(t / 60000) | plant_ids.sort().join())
```
with a unique index on `{device_id, dedupe_key}`. A replayed request returns `200` with the existing card.
An offline retry queue that can double-log is indistinguishable from a double feed, so this index is not
optional.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The owner's biggest selling point. It gets a tab, a pipeline and a file that leaves the product.

### 8.1 What exists today (read from the repo, not assumed)

`server/src/services/image.service.ts` already: pulls a frame every 30 s, stores `Image{format:'jpeg'}`,
thins old frames in four tiers (1 min after a day, 5 min after a week, 15 min after a month, 1 h after 90
days), deletes after 3 years, and rebuilds three rolling mp4s (`1d` @ 2 min/frame, `1w` @ 14 min, `1m` @
60 min) with `ffmpeg -framerate 25 -vcodec libx265 -crf 30`. `sharp` and `imagemagick` are already
dependencies. `imageToken` is a URL-embeddable 30-day JWT.

**Two things in that sentence are shipping bugs.** `libx265` in an mp4 does not decode in Chrome on
Android or in most gallery apps — the timelapse Terp Control builds today largely cannot be watched
outside Safari. And an mp4 stored as a Mongo `Buffer` is capped by the 16 MB BSON limit, which a
run-length film will exceed.

### 8.3 The content problem — culling and normalisation

Run after each hourly rebuild, in the existing `compressRtspStreamRange` path. All of it is `sharp` on a
64×36 (cull) or 128×72 (shift) grayscale downsample plus one Influx join — cheap enough to run on a frame
set the pipeline already has in a temp dir.

1. **Lights-off cull.** Join each frame's timestamp to `out_light`; drop frames where light < 5 %. Off by
   default only for `dry` workmode. This alone is the difference between a watchable film and half a film
   of black. Toggle: `Nacht ausblenden`.
2. **Transient suppression (hand, door, human).** Mean absolute difference to the previous kept frame on
   the 64×36 thumbnail. `mad > 0.18` for **≤ 4 consecutive frames** followed by a return to within `0.06`
   of the pre-event baseline = a transient → dropped. A change that *persists* is real (a plant moved, the
   camera was nudged) → kept.
3. **Dedup.** 8×8 dHash; drop a frame within Hamming distance ≤ 2 of the previous kept frame, but never
   drop below one frame per 30 min so elapsed time still reads evenly.
4. **Exposure / white balance.** Per-frame mean R/G/B on the thumbnail, then a **rolling 15-frame median
   gain per channel**, clamped to ±25 %, applied with `sharp.linear()`. The rolling median is the point:
   per-frame `normalise()` strobes; a slow-moving gain removes the magenta-LED-vs-daylight swing across 12
   weeks without flicker.
5. **Camera nudge.** dHash-derived integer translation on 128×72, applied as an integer-pixel shift with a
   4 % safety crop. Above 8 % of frame width the pipeline **cuts a chapter** instead of warping, and raises
   a `kamera-verschoben` card: `📷 Kamera hat sich bewegt — Film ab hier neues Kapitel`. Rotation is
   **not** corrected in v1. Stated plainly rather than promised.
6. **Encode.** `-c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 23 -preset medium
   -movflags +faststart -vf scale=1280:720 -r 25`. Plays everywhere, streams before it finishes
   downloading. `libx265` is kept only for the internal `1m` archive copy.
7. **Burn-in.** `drawtext` from a generated timed file: `Tag 34 · 24,6 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21`, bottom-left,
   plus a kept-card caption where one exists (`💧 gegossen 2,0 l`). Default on for downloads, toggleable.

`[ Was wurde weggelassen? ]` opens a plain list — `1.842 Nachtbilder · 63 Türöffnungen · 0 doppelt` — with
a thumbnail strip. Culling that cannot be inspected is culling nobody trusts.

### 8.4 How a video leaves the product

Storage first, because it decides the rest: films with `duration:'run'` go to **GridFS**
(`mongodb`'s `GridFSBucket`, already a transitive dependency of mongoose — no new package), keyed by
`image_id`. Under 12 MB stays inline in `Image.data` as today. This is the single largest piece of new
persistence work in the concept and §12 budgets it.

```
GET /image/:device_id/download
      ?format=mp4&run=<run_id>&caption=1&token=<imageToken>

200 OK
Content-Type: video/mp4
Content-Length: 32541220
Accept-Ranges: bytes
Content-Disposition: attachment;
  filename="terpcontrol_zelt-keller_lauf-3_tag001-047_720p.mp4"
```

- Filename is `terpcontrol_<device-slug>_<run-slug>_tag<from>-<to>_<height>p.mp4` — sortable, unambiguous
  in a Downloads folder six months later, and it contains no strain name unless the user put one in the
  run label.
- `token` is the existing 30-day `imageToken`, deliberately URL-embeddable.
- **Android / desktop Chrome:** the `Herunterladen` button is `<a href download>`; `Content-Disposition`
  puts the file in `/Download`. Works.
- **iOS Safari:** `<a download>` is inert cross-origin. The button opens the URL in a new tab; Safari's
  native player appears with a share sheet → `In "Dateien" sichern` / `Video sichern`. The button label is
  `Öffnen & sichern` on iOS. Documented, not hand-waved.
- **Teilen** produces `navigator.share({files:[File]})` where available (Android Chrome, iOS 15+) — this is
  the path that actually reaches WhatsApp and Telegram, and it is a real share, not a link to a login page.
- Alongside the mp4, `?format=csv` emits the film's `FilmFrame` table: `i,t,temperature,humidity,vpd,co2,
  light_pct,day,card_ids`. The numbers behind the pictures, for the techie, from the same button row.

### 8.5 The camera alarm

The camera today tells you it is quiet only if you happen to look. v1 adds `Alarm.sensorType:
'camera_age'` — a new string in the existing `Alarm` entity, evaluated in the existing alarm tick against
the newest `Image{format:'jpeg'}` timestamp. Default alarm, created with the camera pairing:
`Kamera meldet sich nicht · > 3 Std · Info + E-Mail`. It therefore inherits email, webhook, cooldown,
retrigger and resolution for free. It also raises a `kamera-still` card.

### 8.6 Image correlation

Correlation is computed at read time by timestamp and **materialised only for frames that made it into a
film**:

```ts
FilmFrame { film_id; i; t; temperature; humidity; vpd; co2?; lightPct; day; card_ids: string[] }
```
≤ ~3.000 rows per film. It powers the burn-in, the film scrubber, the CSV export and the sprite index.

Three places the correlation is visible, and the third is what makes it a *diary* feature rather than a
chart feature:

1. **Chart → picture.** The `axisPointer` position sets `<img src="/image/:id?timestamp=…&width=640">`,
   throttled to 300 ms. Above the chart, not a floating tooltip — a fingertip occludes a tooltip.
2. **Picture → chart.** Tapping a thumbnail in the film strip moves the crosshair. One cursor, both
   directions. Delivery is one sprite + JSON index per window (100 tiles at 256×160), not N requests.
3. **Card → pair of frames.** Every climate card carries, on its back, the frame nearest the event's start
   and the frame nearest its end, each stamped with its own numbers. *"Nachts 6 Std zu feucht"* shows you
   the tent at 22:10 and at 04:20, side by side. That pair is what a grower actually wants when he asks
   "was war da los" — and it is stored on the card, so it survives into the export and into the week sheet.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution appears when a second person exists. There is no club mode.**

```ts
Actor { actor_id; device_id; name; colour; createdAt; retiredAt? }
```

Names the device owner types once, in `Zelt → Wer ist am Zelt`. No accounts, no invitations, no email
lookup, no `auth.middleware` surgery. Roughly two days of work, and it delivers attribution in v1.

- **One actor (or none):** nothing renders. No chip in the header, no name on any card, no picker. A solo
  grower never learns the feature exists.
- **Two or more actors:** the header shows `▸ Anna`, chosen once per device per browser and remembered in
  `localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']`; tapping it re-picks. Every card gains an actor chip. The
  double-feed check turns on. Per-actor columns appear in plant totals.

This is reveal by data, applied to people. It is deliberately *not* a toggle, because a toggle would be a
mode wearing a club badge.

**Authorisation is unchanged and honestly so.** `Device.owner_id` is still one string; the actor picker is
a label, not a gate. The concept says this out loud in the UI: `Wer am Zelt ist, wird notiert — nicht
geprüft. Für echte Zugänge braucht es Konten (später).` Real memberships are a rewrite of
`auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` plus ~10 owner-scoped queries and do not land in October.

**Standing conditions** are the fifth club gap and they are a card kind:

```ts
ConditionPayload { text; openedBy; openedAt; closedBy?; closedAt? }
```

A `zustand` card sits in `HEUTE FÄLLIG`, never auto-files, and stays until someone taps `Erledigt`. It is
the note pinned to the tent door: `CO₂-Flasche fast leer`, `Steckdose 3 wackelt`, `A3 nicht gießen —
übergossen`. Created from `⊕ → Zustand`. Two taps to open, one to close. Closing writes `closedBy` so the
record shows who dealt with it.

**Privacy posture — privacy-maximal by default.**

- No harvest weight and no plant count is ever prompted. `Plant.label` is free text; a grower who types
  `A1, A2, A3` has given the cloud nothing.
- Actor names are free text with no validation — `A` and `B` are valid and the placeholder says so:
  `Name oder Kürzel — steht nur bei dir im Zelt.`
- The public diary URL (post-v1) is `/d/<slug>-<5char>` and never contains `device_id`, plant labels or
  actor names unless the user opts each in.
- No location, ever. No analytics SDK. No community feed — a link you can paste anywhere is not a feed you
  must moderate.
- Terp Control does not become the club's § 26 KCanG system of record. No per-member dispensing, no grams
  ledger, no THC content, no real names. `Erledigt` on a `zustand` card is participation evidence enough
  for § 17 Abs. 2 and stores nothing personal beyond a nickname the user chose.
- Every export carries `KEINE | KÜRZEL | ANONYM` for actor names, defaulting to `KÜRZEL`.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy, monitoring only.** He opens the app once a week. JETZT tells him the tent is fine in four
words. Below it, three or four cards that accumulated; he swipes two away and keeps one. He never scrolls
into `ABGELEGT`. If he does not open the app for a month, nothing rots: cards auto-filed after seven days
with per-kind defaults, and the week sheets built themselves. The dossier's #1 complaint across every
competitor — you cannot tell live from stale — is answered in the header before anything else loads.

**2 — Same, with a camera.** The `Film` tab is his home in practice. Every Monday a `🎞 Der Film der Woche
ist fertig` card appears in `AUFGEFALLEN`; he taps `Ansehen`, then `Teilen`, and a 9 MB H.264 file goes to
his Telegram group from the native share sheet. At harvest he downloads
`terpcontrol_zelt-keller_lauf-3_tag001-089_720p.mp4` with the values burned in. Culling means it is a film
of a plant growing, not eleven hours of black per day.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating only).** He has a heater socket and no dehumidifier, so no
humidity card kind is ever generated, no humidity target row appears, and the dehumidifier alarm preset is
not offered. Today he gets all three and can act on none. His `abweichung` cards are about temperature,
because that is what he can change, and the card's back shows the heater's state timeline for the same
window so he can see whether the socket actually fired.

**4 — Full tent.** Six sockets, so six roles on JETZT and six lanes in the outputs timeline. He is the
heaviest triager: `Ergänzen` on most cards, so his `AUFGEFALLEN` weights drift toward the deviation kinds
and away from `film`. The honest limit is unchanged and stated in `Zelt`: five socket roles exist in the
firmware, `dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler in `temp` workmode, and there is no humidifier. Cards never
claim control the hardware does not have.

**5 — DIY closed loop.** Gets the least, and this concept does not pretend otherwise. What he does get:
the rule parameters on every card back are editable and are *his* thresholds; `[ Rohwerte ansehen ]` and
`[ Als CSV ]` are on every card; the film CSV pairs every frame with its conditions; and the `camera_age`
alarm plus existing per-alarm webhooks give him an outbound event that is not a temperature. What he does
not get in v1: a write API, custom measures, MQTT access. Named as deferred, not designed around.

**6 — Club.** Two names typed into `Wer ist am Zelt` turns on everything: actor chips on cards, the
double-feed block, per-actor totals on the plant page, `zustand` cards as tent-door notes. „Wer hat was
gemacht?" is answered by the plant page header; „wie haben die Pflanzen reagiert?" by the card that pairs
a feed with the frames and the EC around it. Nothing personal is stored and no compliance claim is made.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Three tabs, the same stack, his settings page untouched, his old diary rows
still readable, and one new thing: the app now tells him when his timer misfired.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner:* one card, one sentence, two big buttons, no reflow under the thumb, no
red badges, 2-tap capture with wet hands. *Normal:* a home screen that says what happened rather than
plotting it, and a film he can put in a WhatsApp group. *Techie:* every claim carries its rule, its
coverage percentage, its raw window and a CSV, in the same place the stoner types his note.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **It is an inbox, and inboxes generate guilt.** I have removed every badge, killed the push, and made
   auto-filing loud — but the shape is still a pile of things awaiting your attention, and some people
   will hate that on sight. If the auto-file defaults are wrong even once, the promise "ignore it, it
   still records" collapses and the concept has no fallback.
2. **Rule quality *is* the product, and there is no baseline for two weeks.** These are thresholds, not
   models. A tent whose door a human opens twice a day will produce false `abweichung` cards until the
   thresholds settle. A card that is wrong twice is worse than no card at all, and the first fortnight of
   a brand-new controller is exactly when a customer decides whether to trust the app.
3. **Adaptive ordering is still adaptive.** The fences are real (fixed bands, ~9 kinds, never below "still
   there", an explicit demotion card, an inspectable list) — but they are also five mechanisms defending
   one feature, and a 2am user who notices a different order than yesterday will not read my defence.
   The safest version of this concept drops weights entirely and sorts `AUFGEFALLEN` by severity then
   recency. That version is meaningfully worse for the techie and meaningfully more predictable for
   everyone. I would ship the weights and be ready to turn them off.
4. **Triage state is a second source of truth about what the diary contains.** `weg` cards live in `Alles`
   but not in the export, the week sheet or the annotation rail. Two people will disagree about what the
   record says. In a club this is worse: one member can dismiss another member's evidence, and
   `triagedBy` records it but does not prevent it. There is no per-actor triage in v1 and there probably
   should be a "nur ich" scope later.
5. **The camera pipeline is the schedule risk and it touches storage.** GridFS is a new persistence path
   in a codebase whose house style is "optional field + read-time normaliser". Each cull/normalise stage
   has a failure mode that produces a *worse* film than no processing — over-aggressive dedup makes growth
   jerky, a bad rolling gain makes it breathe — so each needs a visible off switch, which is four more
   toggles than I want on the Film tab.
6. **Type 5 gets the least of any type.** Editable rule parameters and a CSV are not an API. The DIY
   segment is the most sceptical in the market and this concept asks it to be patient.
7. **No run-over-run comparison, by design.** The stack is about now and recently. A grower on his fourth
   cycle asking "is this better than last time" gets a plant page and a film shelf, not an answer. C10
   owns that question and this concept does not.
8. **One stack per device.** Three tents means three inboxes and no combined view. For the household with
   a veg tent and a flower tent this is a real annoyance and there is no v1 answer.
9. **`weg` cards still cost rows.** Dismissing does not delete, and `Alles` grows without bound. Pagination
   is budgeted, storage is not — and today the Influx bucket has no retention and no downsampling at all,
   so this concept adds to a cost curve that is already unmanaged.
10. **Rendering historic `DeviceLog` rows as untriageable cards is a visible seam.** Old entries look
    subtly different from new ones forever. That is honest, and it is also the kind of thing a reviewer
    calls "two systems bolted together" in a screenshot.
