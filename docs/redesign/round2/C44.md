# C44 — "Der Zusammenhang"

**Family:** chart · **Centre of gravity:** correlation is the feature.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Terp Control stops showing you what happened and starts telling you what is connected to what — in
German sentences whose numbers you can pull apart, checked against a hand-written catalogue of real
horticultural mechanisms, and never claiming more than the evidence supports.**

Every grow app draws lines. A line is a record, and a record answers "what was the temperature at 3am" —
a question nobody has. The real questions are relational: *why does humidity spike when the light comes on?
why is the heater running while the dehumidifier runs? did that feed do anything? is the plant actually
growing?* Each is a statement about **two things and the distance between them in time**. Terp Control is
the only product here that holds all three ingredients in one database — environment series, machine state,
human events — plus a camera pointed at the outcome. C44 spends the whole budget on joining them.

The unit of the interface is not a chart or a tile. It is a **Zusammenhang**: one sentence, one sparkline,
one evidence chip. The chart still exists — it is what a sentence becomes when you pull it open. And the
concept commits to honesty: it does not scan for correlations, it **checks a fixed catalogue of known
mechanisms against your tent**, reports how often each held, and says out loud that co-occurrence is not
proof.

## 2. Information architecture

Five surfaces. Four tabs plus settings. Existing routes are preserved so share links and chart presets
keep working.

```
/list                          Geräteliste (unchanged; single device → straight to /device/:id)
/device/:id                    ZELT      — the sentence stack. Replaces the fridge overview.
/device/:id/charts             VERLAUF   — ECharts small multiples. Existing route + query format kept.
/device/:id/diary              TAGEBUCH  — events, plants, cumulative totals. Existing route kept.
/device/:id/kamera             KAMERA    — NEW. Film strip, cycle timelapse, export.
/device/:id/settings           via header gear. Existing per-device-type settings, untouched in v1.
/device/:id/testmode           unchanged, admin-only.
```

**Navigation model — deliberately two paths to the same places.**

1. **A bottom `ion-tab-bar`** with four items: Zelt · Verlauf · Tagebuch · Kamera. Fixed order, always
   the same, four large targets. This is how you navigate when you are tired, one-handed, or high. It never
   reorders and never adapts.
2. **Tokens inside sentences** (§4). Tapping `24,8 °C` in a sentence on Zelt opens Verlauf on the
   temperature panel at the right window. Tapping `A3` opens that plant's sheet. This is lateral navigation
   for people who read the sentence and want the thing behind a specific word.

The tab bar is the floor; tokens are the shortcuts. Nobody is denied either. The Kamera tab is **absent**
(not disabled, not empty) when `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is unset — reveal by capability.

Persistent header on every device surface: name, freshness dot, device switcher (only when >1 device), gear.

### 3.2 The freshness grammar — how stale, offline and absent look

The sentence carries freshness in its **tense and its adverb**, not in a badge somebody has to notice.
This is the cheapest honesty mechanism in the whole concept and it costs two i18n key pairs.

| State | Dot | Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| < 5 min | `● jetzt` (green) | `Im Zelt ist es 24,8 °C warm bei 54 %, VPD 1,31 kPa.` |
| 5–10 min | `● vor 7 Min` (green) | same, present tense |
| 10 min – 2 h | `⚠ vor 42 Min` (amber) | `Im Zelt waren es zuletzt 24,8 °C bei 54 %.` — **past tense** |
| > 2 h | `⚠ vor 2 Std 14` (amber) | `Der Controller meldet sich seit 2 Std 14 nicht. Zuletzt 24,8 °C bei 54 %.` |
| Never any data | grey | `Noch keine Messwerte. Der Controller meldet sich alle 30 Sekunden, sobald er im WLAN ist.` + the claim/WLAN help link |
| Maintenance mode | `🔧 Wartung bis 14:30` | `Wartungsmodus — Regelung ist aus. Werte laufen weiter mit.` |

While offline, the Zusammenhänge block keeps rendering with its last computed links (they are historical
statements, they do not go stale in the same way), but the header of the block reads
`Stand: gestern 23:00`. The camera block, if the last frame is older than 3 h, renders the frame dimmed
with the caption `Letztes Bild vor 5 Std` and a `Kamera prüfen` link — this is the camera alarm surfaced
passively; the active one is in §8.5.

**Empty tent (device claimed, nothing logged, 20 min of data):** Conditions absent, Zusammenhänge block
absent, HEUTE absent, camera block present if paired. What remains is the state sentence, the 24 h
sparkline, and the capture bar. Below the state sentence a single line:
`Zusammenhänge brauchen ein paar Tage Daten. Der erste kommt meist nach 3 Tagen.` That is the honest
cold-start copy — see §13.1, it is this concept's worst moment.

### 3.3 Ranking of the Zusammenhänge block

Fixed, deterministic, nightly. Not behavioural, not personalised. (1) **Aktiv und schädlich** — evaluated
within 24 h and `harm: true` (the fights, the non-stop dehumidifier, the failed contactor, the flat
sensor). (2) **Neu** — first fired within 7 days, badge `neu`. (3) **Geändert** — effect moved > 50 %,
badge `hat sich geändert`. (4) **Stabil** — the rest, by `consistency × log n`.

Maximum **five** cards on Zelt; `Alle Zusammenhänge (11)` opens the full list. A scrolling wall of
correlations is exactly the failure mode this concept must avoid.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — the sentence is the query language

> **The rule: every fact in this app is a German sentence, every number inside a sentence is a handle,
> and pulling a handle expands the sentence in place into the evidence that produced it. There is no
> other view. The "simple" screen is the sentence with nothing pulled; the "expert" screen is the same
> sentence with things pulled. They are the same screen, in the same route, at the same scroll position.**

Three token types, visually distinct and consistent everywhere in the app:

| Token | Look | Tap behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wert** `24,8 °C` | dotted underline, tabular figures | Expands **in place**: a 90 px strip drops in under the sentence with the raw series for the window the sentence refers to, `min 21,4 · max 26,1 · n 288 · Ø 24,1`, and a `Im Verlauf öffnen ›` link. Tap again to collapse. |
| **Zeit** `14 Min`, `gestern Nacht`, `3. Aug` | dotted underline, italic | Snaps every time-scoped block on the current screen to that window. The sentence *is* the date picker. |
| **Ding** `Entfeuchter`, `A3`, `Bio-Grow`, `Blüte` | dotted underline, small leading glyph | Opens a bottom sheet: everything the app knows about that thing. For a socket: role, IP, on-time today, duty cycle, its links. For a plant: age, stage, totals, photos. For a nutrient: what it is, doses given, source of the schedule. |

### Four rules that keep this from becoming a mode

1. **Expansion is transient and never persisted.** No preference, no localStorage, no server field. Close
   the screen and every token closes. Tomorrow at 2am you get exactly the screen you got today. Adaptive
   interfaces punish the impaired user; this one refuses to adapt.
2. **Nothing is hidden behind expansion.** The sentence already contains the number; expansion adds
   *provenance* (series, n, spread), never a new fact. Nobody is denied information by not tapping — the
   brief's "progressive precision", literally: verdict, number, raw series.
3. **There is no second route.** Verlauf is not "the expert page"; it is where a Wert-Token goes, and the
   stoner reaches it from the tab bar too. Both people use both places.
4. **The techie's density comes from breadth, not a toggle.** At ≥ 1024 px the stack becomes two columns
   and **every card renders its sparkline expanded** — because the space exists, not because a preference
   was set. Same code path, same data, one media query.

The `(?)` chip is the same mechanism at its sharpest: the stoner reads `n=23 · 21 von 23`, understands
"usually", moves on; the techie taps and gets §5.4's evidence sheet with the confounder correction.
**Neither changed a setting.**

## 5. The chart — `/device/:id/charts`, "Verlauf"

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision). The existing URL
query format (`measures`, `date`, `dateEnd`, `vpdMode`, `interval`, `timespan`, `logs`, `share`) is
**preserved verbatim** and extended with `panel=` and `fold=` — old presets and old share links keep
resolving through `applyViewParams`.

### 5.1 Panel stack

Stacked small multiples, one shared x-axis, one linked crosshair (`axisPointer.link`), ~110 px each.
Panels are present only if the data exists — reveal by capability, not a picker.

```

├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TEMPERATUR °C                                    26 ─ │
│ ░░░░░░░░░░░░  target band (soft)                      │
│ ╌╌╌╌╌╌╌┐        setpoint, dashed, STEPPED at day/night│
│ ───────┴────  actual (solid) + min/max envelope       │
│ ▓ deviation fill between actual and setpoint      20 ─│
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ LUFTFEUCHTE %                                         │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ VPD kPa      band changes at each stage boundary       │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CO₂ ppm      (only when hardwareInfo says the SCD4x)   │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ZUSTAND   ▬▬▬▬ Licht  ▬ ▬ ▬ Heizung  ▬▬ Entfeuchter   │  state-timeline lane, regions = duration
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FILM      [▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪]         │  thumbnails at true timestamps
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ EREIGNISSE  ▲gieß  ▲düng ▲foto      ┊Blüte             │  2 rows, clustered, stage = dashed full-height
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goal vs actual.** Setpoint drawn as a **dashed stepped line** built from `device.configuration`'s
  `day.temperature` / `night.temperature` stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night`. The band behind
  it, one source at a time and **labelled with which**: the user's own alarm thresholds if set
  (`Deine Alarmgrenzen`), else setpoint ± tolerance (`Sollwert ± 1,5 °C`), else the stage's advisory
  `vpdRange` for the VPD panel (`Empfehlung Blüte`). Never fill to the axis.
- **Min/max envelope** on the primary measure — a single mean line lies about a bang-bang controller.
  `sampling: 'minmax'` for the plot, LTTB for the navigator.
- **Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to `daynight`, in an explicit timezone. A failed
  contactor then shows as a **missing band** — a diagnostic, not decoration.
- **Mobile:** one finger scrolls, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap/long-press moves the
  crosshair. Axis labels never hidden — below 320 px, three ticks and the unit in the panel title.
- Okabe-Ito colours. Series names from i18n, not hardcoded English.

### 5.3 Übereinander — the signature chart

The one picture that makes a periodic relationship visible, and one ECharts config: **fold the series by
photoperiod phase**. All 23 occurrences of "the 90 minutes after lights-on" plotted on one 90-minute
x-axis; median line, IQR band, individual days as 15 %-opacity lines, today highlighted.

```
  Übereinander · 23 × „nach Licht an"          [Temperatur ▾] [90 Min ▾]

  °C ┤        ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇███████████         ← today (solid)
     │      ▁▂▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓        ← IQR band
     │    ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━        ← median
     └────────────────────────────────
      0   15   30   45   60   75   90 Min
```

Available for any measure against any of three fold anchors: `Licht an`, `Licht aus`, `Gießen`. This is
what turns "my humidity is weird" into "my humidity does the same thing every single morning".

### 5.4 The evidence sheet — being honest about spurious correlation

Tapping `(?)` on any link opens:

```
  Wie sicher ist das?

  Beobachtet          23 mal seit dem 2. August
  Hat gestimmt        21 mal (91 %)
  Effekt              −11 % Luftfeuchte  (Streuung −8 % bis −14 %)
  Verzögerung         14 Min  (10 bis 19 Min)

  Mechanismus
  Warme Luft kann mehr Wasser halten. Die absolute Feuchte
  bleibt gleich, also fällt die relative Feuchte.

  Was das verfälschen könnte
  Beides steigt mit dem Licht. Innerhalb der gleichen
  Licht-Phase gerechnet bleibt der Effekt bei −9 %.

  ⚠ Zusammenhang ist kein Beweis. Terp Control sieht nur,
    dass die beiden zusammen auftreten.

  [ Kopplung ansehen ]  [ 7 Tage beobachten ]  [ Stimmt nicht ]
```

- **`7 Tage beobachten`** writes a `LinkWatch {device_id, link_id, started_at, baseline}`. After seven days
  the card says `Hat sich bestätigt: 6 von 7 mal` or `Hat sich nicht bestätigt: 2 von 7 mal.` That is the
  only intellectually honest response to a correlation, and it is a stored document plus a nightly job.
- **`Stimmt nicht`** suppresses the link for 30 days and stores the reason from four fixed options
  (`Ist Absicht` / `Kenne ich schon` / `Stimmt einfach nicht` / `Verstehe ich nicht`). The fourth is the
  one that tells us the sentence is badly written.

### 5.5 The link catalogue — 18 entries, hand-written, no pairwise scanning

**The engine does not discover.** It evaluates a fixed catalogue where every entry declares a mechanism in
prose before it is allowed to exist. Nothing outside the catalogue is ever shown. This forfeits surprise
and buys the right to write confident German sentences (§13.2 is honest about the trade).

| id | Sentence (shortened) | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lag.rh_after_temp` | RH folgt der Temperatur mit N Min Verzögerung bei Licht an | temp, humidity, out_light |
| `lag.light_heats` | Die Lampe heizt das Zelt in N Min um Δ °C auf | temp, out_light |
| `night.vpd_low` | VPD fällt jede Nacht unter X kPa | temp, humidity |
| `fight.heater_dehum` | Heizung und Entfeuchter laufen gleichzeitig | out_heater, out_dehumidifier |
| `fight.co2_dark` | CO₂ wird bei ausgeschaltetem Licht eingeblasen | out_co2, out_light |
| `overshoot.heater` | Nach jedem Heizzyklus steigt die Temperatur noch Δ °C weiter | temp, out_heater |
| `dehum.nonstop` | Der Entfeuchter läuft X % der Nacht und kommt nicht hinterher | out_dehumidifier |
| `contactor.missing` | Licht sollte um 06:00 angehen — die Lampe blieb aus | daynight, out_light **or** camera luma |
| `sensor.flat` | Der Feuchtesensor meldet seit X Std denselben Wert | any series |
| `co2.decay` | Ohne Nachschub fällt CO₂ um X ppm/Std — das Zelt ist undicht | co2, out_co2 |
| `ambient.night` | Die Nachttemperatur folgt dem Wetter draußen | temp + logged outside temp |
| `door.open` | Das Zelt war um HH:MM offen — danach Δ °C in N Min | camera luma+motion, temp |
| `feed.rh_after` | Nach dem Gießen steigt die Feuchte für N Std um Δ % | Event(water), humidity |
| `feed.interval` | Du gießt im Schnitt alle N Tage — der Plan sagt M | Event(water), FeedRegime |
| `feed.ec_runoff` | Der Ablauf-EC liegt Δ über dem Gieß-EC | Event(feed).ec, .runoff_ec |
| `stage.flip_detected` | Seit dem D. läuft 12/12 — im Tagebuch steht die Blüte ab dem E. | out_light, Event(stage) |
| `photo.growth` | Der Grünanteil im Bild wächst um X %/Tag | ImageMetrics.green_frac |
| `photo.camera_quiet` | Die Kamera hat seit X Std kein Bild geschickt | Image timestamps |

Evaluation is a nightly batch job per device plus an on-demand recompute when the user opens Zelt after
> 6 h. **Time-in-range, lags and overshoot are computed server-side on raw Influx samples, never on
`aggregateWindow(fn:mean)` output** — averaging destroys exactly the excursions these metrics count.

Suppression rules that stop the block becoming noise: a link needs `n ≥ 5` and `consistency ≥ 0.6` to
appear at all; `harm: true` links additionally need `n ≥ 3` in the last 7 days; a link that has been
`Stimmt nicht`-ed is gone for 30 days; and no more than two links from the same category are shown at
once.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New entities (MongoDB, cloud-side only — `device.configuration` is untouched)

```ts

```

`Event.kind ∈ 'water' | 'feed' | 'stage' | 'note' | 'photo' | 'training' | 'harvest' | 'measure' | 'device'`.
`plant_ids: []` means **the whole tent** — that is the untaxing default and the reason a plant-ignorer
never meets a plant.

Indexes: `Event {device_id, ts}`, `Event {device_id, client_uuid}` unique (offline idempotency),
`Event {device_id, plant_ids, ts}`, `ImageMetrics {device_id, ts}`.

**Migration posture:** all new collections, nothing altered. `DeviceLog` stays exactly as it is for device
messages and alarms and continues to feed the event rail. Existing diary rows are read by a **one-time
idempotent backfill in the service constructor** that converts `diary-plant-lifecycle` rows into `Cycle`
+ stage `Event`s using the existing `convertEventsToGrowCycles` logic — which stops being a runtime
algorithm and becomes a migration, exactly as the dossier recommends. The old `deleted:true` visibility
trick dies with it: new events have no such flag, and `DELETE /device/logs/:id` no longer looks like data
loss because it never touched Events in the first place.

### 6.2 How a user who ignores plants is untaxed

- **No plant exists → no plant UI exists.** No plant row in the capture sheet, no chips on the event rail,
  a flat diary. `photo.growth` never fires.
- **The first plant is created by naming one**, from `+ Pflanze` in the capture sheet or the camera ROI
  tool. No onboarding question, no wizard step, no "do you want to track plants?".
- One named plant = one preselected chip: one extra glance, zero extra taps.
- Totals, the double-feed guard and attribution all work at tent scope with `plant_ids: []`. Plants make
  them *finer*; they are not a prerequisite.

applied to capture.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

**This is a headline feature, and it is the same machinery as the correlation engine.** The metrics
computed to make a timelapse watchable are the same metrics that make an image a *measurement*.

Verified starting position in the repo: `sharp ^0.34.5` is already a server dependency;
`ffmpeg` is already invoked via `execFile` with a `pLimit(10)` concurrency gate; stills arrive every 30 s;
three rolling timelapses (`1d`/`1w`/`1m`) are already built by `compressRtspStreamRange`.
**Three verified defects to fix on the way:**

1. `convertRtspStreamImagesToVideo` encodes **libx265 CRF 30**. H.265 in mp4 does not play in Chrome on
   Android or in Firefox. Today's timelapse is unwatchable for most of the user base. → **libx264,
   `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-movflags +faststart`, CRF 21.**
2. Frame selection is pure interval sampling — no culling, no normalisation. That is the "mostly garbage
   frames" problem, unaddressed.
3. `IMAGE_THINNING_TIERS` deletes frames by interval as they age. A cycle timelapse's chosen daily frames
   must be **protected from thinning** — see `CycleFrame.protected` and §8.3.

### 8.1 ImageMetrics at ingest — the image becomes a series

On every stored still, before it is written, `sharp` resizes to 160 px wide and computes:

| Field | How | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `luma` | mean L | lights-on detection, door/leak detection, exposure normalisation |
| `hue_peak` | dominant hue from a 32-bin histogram | white-balance drift, lamp spectrum change |
| `motion` | mean abs diff vs the previous frame at 160 px | human in the tent, door open |
| `sharpness` | Laplacian variance | condensation, fog, lens spray |
| `green_frac` | fraction of pixels in a hue window, **inside the user's ROI, after WB normalisation** | growth |

Cost: ~1–2 ms CPU per frame at 160 px. At 2 frames/min × 150 devices that is 5 frames/s ≈ 1 % of a core.
Honest and small. **Metrics are stored in Mongo, not InfluxDB** — deliberately, so that the `VALID_SENSORS`
allowlist and the unfixed raw-Flux-interpolation in `data.service.ts` are not touched. A separate
`GET /image/metrics/:device_id?from&to&fields` returns day-bucketed arrays for the chart.

**And metrics outlive the pixels.** Because thinning deletes frames, the metrics series is the only
long-term record of what the camera saw. That alone justifies computing them at ingest.

### 8.2 The ROI — one drag, once

On the Kamera tab, `Pflanzenfeld festlegen` overlays the last frame with a draggable rectangle. Optional.
Skipping it costs nothing except `green_frac` (and therefore `photo.growth`). With plants, a rectangle can
be assigned to a plant (`Plant.roi`), which is how a club gets per-plant growth from one camera. Moving the
camera invalidates the ROI — the app detects it via a phase-correlation shift > 15 % of frame width and
says `Die Kamera scheint verschoben. Pflanzenfeld neu setzen?`

### 8.3 The timelapse that ships in v1 — "Der Durchgang"

A fourth duration value: `Image.duration: '1d' | '1w' | '1m' | 'cycle'`. One frame per day, normalised, for
the whole run. This is the video a person can hold.

**Frame culling, in order.** Each rule is checkable from `ImageMetrics` + the output series — no re-decode.

1. **Lights-off out.** Keep only frames where `out_light > 0` at that timestamp (from Influx). Fallback if
   the device has no light output: `luma ≥ 0.15`.
2. **Ramp out.** Drop frames inside `lights.sunrise` / `lights.sunset` ramps and the first/last 20 min of
   the photoperiod — exposure is unstable there.
3. **Humans and open doors out.** Drop frames where `motion > median_7d(motion) × 4` or
   `|luma − median_day(luma)| > 2.5 σ`. This suppresses a person in the tent and a daylight leak without
   any person detector.
4. **Fog out.** Drop frames where `sharpness < 0.4 × median_7d(sharpness)`.
5. **One per day, at a fixed phase.** Of what survives, pick the frame nearest to
   `lights_on + 0.5 × photoperiod`. **This is the single most important normaliser**: it removes the
   day/night flicker entirely, so plant growth is the only thing left changing.
6. **Gaps are honest.** If a day has no usable frame, repeat the previous day's frame and record
   `gap: true`; the export sheet reports `3 Tage ohne Bild`.

**Normalisation.** All computed from metrics, applied in one ffmpeg pass.

- **White balance:** per-channel gains that map each frame's ROI channel means to the **reference frame**
  (the first kept frame of the cycle). Fixes magenta-LED drift and daylight leak. Gains clamped to ±40 %.
- **Exposure:** scale so the frame's ROI median luma matches the reference.
- **Deflicker:** the gains are passed through a **5-frame moving median** before use. One odd day cannot
  flash. This is what makes 12 weeks watchable and it is arithmetic, not an ffmpeg filter.
- **Stabilisation:** translation-only phase-correlation alignment against the reference, ±40 px, applied as
  a shift with a 5 % safety crop. Handles "the camera got nudged on day 12". **No rotation in v1** — say so.

**Render.** Per-frame gains and shifts are written to a filter file; one ffmpeg pass:
`-r 24`, each day held **4 frames** (1/6 s) with a 2-frame crossfade, `libx264 -crf 21 -pix_fmt yuv420p
-movflags +faststart`, max 1920 px wide, source aspect preserved.

- 90-day grow → 90 × 4 = 360 frames ≈ **15 s**, ~4–6 MB at 1080p.
- Hard cap **40 s / 12 MB** (the 16 MB BSON limit). Beyond that the render refuses with
  `Zu viele Tage für ein Video in dieser Version — wähle einen Zeitraum.` GridFS is the v1.1 fix (§12).

**Burned-in data overlay — the differentiator.** Bottom-left, in the video itself, per day:

```
  Tag 43 · Blüte · 25,1 °C · 54 % · VPD 1,31
```

Drawn with `sharp`'s SVG compositing — the exact mechanism `addOfflineOverlay()` already uses, so it is
proven code. It can be turned off in the export sheet (a render option, not a mode). This makes the
exported video carry the correlation, which is the whole product thesis in one file that lands in
somebody's Telegram group.

### 8.4 How the video leaves the product

```
GET /image/timelapse/:device_id/:image_id/download?token=<imageToken>
    Content-Type: video/mp4
    Content-Disposition: attachment;
      filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_wedding-cake_2026-06-14_2026-09-12_1080p.mp4"
```

Filename = `terpcontrol_<device-slug>_<cycle-or-plant-slug>_<start>_<end>_<height>p.mp4`, slugs ASCII-folded
(`ä→ae`), lowercased, `[^a-z0-9-]` stripped.

The export sheet, reachable from the Kamera tab and from the end of a grow:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Video erstellen                             ║
║  Zeitraum   ganzer Durchgang (91 Tage)   ▾   ║
║  Bilder     88 von 91 Tagen · 3 ohne Bild    ║
║  Daten im Bild                        [ ✓ ]  ║
║  Länge      ~15 Sek · ca. 5 MB · 1080p       ║
║                                              ║
║  [        Video erstellen        ]           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

Render is queued through RabbitMQ (already in the stack) with the existing `pLimit` gate; typical 90-frame
render is 10–25 s. On completion: `[ Herunterladen ]` (a real `<a download>` to the URL above) and
`[ Link kopieren ]` (a `ShareLink`-backed public URL, `page: 'kamera'`, optional expiry). On iOS the mp4
opens in the player with the share sheet — the button copy says
`Video öffnen — dann „Video sichern"` on iOS Safari specifically, because pretending otherwise generates
support tickets.

The `1d`/`1w`/`1m` rolling timelapses keep working exactly as today (they answer "what happened
recently"), now in H.264 so they actually play.

### 8.5 Image correlation

1. **The film strip is an axis.** Thumbnails positioned by true timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so uneven
   capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivered as **one sprite sheet + JSON index per window** (256×160 tiles,
   50 tiles under a day, 100 above) — one request instead of N. `timestampEnd` mp4s render as a bracket,
   not a point.
2. **Scrub the chart → the picture moves.** Dragging the crosshair updates the big frame above; the pinned
   scrub header shows that moment's numbers. **Tap a thumbnail → the crosshair moves to it.** Bidirectional,
   one shared cursor, both directions in v1.
3. **Every picture carries its numbers.** Full-screen image viewer shows `12. Aug 13:04 · Tag 41 · 25,1 °C
   · 54 % · VPD 1,29 · Licht 100 %` and `[ Im Verlauf zeigen ]`.
4. **The image is a series.** `green_frac`, `luma` and `motion` are selectable panels in Verlauf like any
   sensor. That is what makes `photo.growth`, `door.open` and `contactor.missing` possible, and it is what
   nobody else in this market can do, because nobody else holds the frames and the telemetry in one place.
5. **The camera has an alarm.** `photo.camera_quiet` fires at 6 h with no frame — a `harm: true` link on
   Zelt plus the existing alarm channels (email/webhook). *"Eine Kamera, die drei Tage nach Blütebeginn
   ausgefallen ist, muss das sagen."*

## 9. Clubs

**Posture: attribution in v1, authorisation in v1.1, personal data never.**

### 9.1 Attribution without an auth rewrite

`Actor {actor_id, device_id, name, colour}` — the owner types names once in settings (`Wer ist am Zelt?`).
No accounts, no invitations, no e-mail, no `auth.middleware` surgery. The capture sheet shows chips;
last-used is preselected, sticky in `localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']`. `Event.actor_id` is stamped at
capture. **Two days of work, "who did what" in v1.**

The UI says plainly that this is attribution, not authorisation — the settings page carries
`Namen sind Beschriftungen, keine Zugänge. Jeder mit dem Login kann jeden Namen wählen.` A real gate
arrives in v1.1 via `Membership {device_id, user_id, role}` plus the rewrite of
`auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207`. It is not a flag and the concept does not pretend otherwise.

### 9.2 The double-feed problem

§7.2. Per-plant, per-window, one deliberate extra tap to proceed, and the resulting Event keeps
`flags:['double']` so the record is truthful rather than merely prevented. The offline caveat (§7.4) is in
the dialog itself. **A double feed is the most expensive thing that happens in a shared room, and the fix
is a query and a dialog.**

### 9.3 Conditions

`Condition` (§6.1) is what gets taped to the tent door: an open fact with an owner, neither event nor task.
Created from `✎` → `Als Hinweis anheften`. Pinned at the very top of Zelt for everyone with access, with
the opener's name and age, one tap to close (`closed_by` + `closed_at`; it stays in the diary). Nothing
renders when none are open. Must work verbatim: `CO₂-Flasche fast leer` · `Steckdose 3 wackelt` ·
`A3 nicht gießen — übergossen`.

### 9.4 Privacy posture

- **No personal data.** No real-name enforcement, no DOB, no address, no membership number, no per-member
  dispensing quantities. Terp Control does not become the club's § 26 KCanG system of record — that is
  Cannanas' and 420cloud's job and they are already doing it.
- **Actor names are free text and deletable.** `Namen entfernen` archives the actor and rewrites its events
  to `actor_id: null` — a one-button de-attribution for a room that suddenly needs to be anonymous.
- **Harvest weight and plant count are opt-in, off by default**, behind one settings switch
  (`Erntegewichte erfassen`), with the reason stated plainly rather than hidden:
  `Gewichte und Pflanzenzahlen sind rechtlich heikel. Standardmäßig speichert Terp Control sie nicht.`
- **Exports default to anonymised** with a `Namen: keine / Kürzel / voll` selector on every export.
- **No location, ever.** No GPS, no IP-geolocation persistence, no "growers near you".
- **No community feed.** A public timelapse or diary link is a URL you paste; it is not a feed anyone has to
  moderate.
- **Correlation data is not special.** The links are derived from telemetry the account already holds and
  are deleted with the device.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Monitoring only.** Opens the app once a week. Reads one sentence — `Im Zelt ist es 24,8 °C warm bei
54 %` — and two cards telling him his VPD drops every night and his dehumidifier is winning. He has never
tapped a number and never will. He gets more insight than any chart ever gave him, because somebody wrote
the sentence for him. The one thing he loses versus a tile dashboard: there is no big pretty gauge.

**2 · Monitoring plus a camera.** The best-served type in the concept. His camera stops being decoration:
the film strip is an axis under his chart, scrubbing moves the picture, `photo.growth` tells him the green
fraction is up 2.1 %/day and stalled during the week it hit 31 °C, and at the end of the run he gets a
15-second normalised 1080p H.264 file with `Tag 43 · Blüte · 25,1 °C` burned into the corner, downloaded to
his phone with one tap and pasted into the Telegram group. That video is the marketing.

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** His panels are the ones his kit can affect —
`deviceControlCapability()` becomes per-role instead of three buckets, so a heater-only tent never sees a
humidity target it cannot act on. His links are `overshoot.heater`, `lag.light_heats` and `co2.decay`, and
`co2.decay` tells him something genuinely new: how leaky his tent is, in ppm/hour, which no product on the
market reports.

**4 · Full tent.** Gets the concept's sharpest card: `Heizung und Entfeuchter arbeiten gegeneinander —
letzte Nacht 4 Std 20 gleichzeitig`, with both duty cycles on one axis and 6-of-7-nights consistency. That
is a real €40/month electricity finding that the current app cannot express even in principle. The honest
limit: the firmware still has five socket roles and no humidifier, and this concept does not add any. It
tells him about the fight; it cannot end it.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** Sceptical, wants raw data. He taps every token, gets n, IQR, lag distributions and
the confounder correction on the evidence sheet, and finds the `Stimmt nicht` button when the engine is
wrong about his deliberately swinging fridge. The concept gives him the honesty he demands and a CSV export
of Events. It does **not** give him a write API — that is out of scope for October and saying so is better
than promising it.

**6 · Grow club.** Actor chips at capture, the double-feed dialog naming Anna and the time, Conditions
pinned to the top of everyone's home screen, and the plant sheet that answers "how much Bio-Bloom has A3
had this cycle and from whom". His detailed logging is in the same sheet the casual user closes after two
taps — he just scrolls. And `feed.rh_after` finally answers the club's real question: *how did the plants
react?*

**7 · Old device owner.** §10. A plug owner gets an honest sentence and a diary; a fan owner with a temp
sensor gets three genuine correlations from hardware he bought before the controller existed. Nothing he
depends on is moved or renamed.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

**13.1 Cold start is brutal and hits at the worst possible moment.** A new €289 controller has no
Zusammenhänge for three to ten days. The headline feature is an empty section on the day the box arrives —
exactly when the customer forms an opinion. AC Infinity shows a pretty gauge in thirty seconds; this
offers honest waiting copy. Partial mitigation: `contactor.missing` and `sensor.flat` fire within a day,
`lag.rh_after_temp` needs five lights-on events. Still the worst moment in the product, and inherent to
the thesis.

**13.2 The catalogue cannot discover anything.** Refusing pairwise scanning guarantees the app never
surprises anyone with a relationship nobody thought of. A techie will say, correctly, "these are eighteen
hard-coded rules with sparklines". The defence — that unrestricted mining over 15 series and 90 days is a
p-hacking machine, and a wrong sentence is worse than a missing one — is true, and the criticism still
lands.

**13.3 A confident German sentence is more persuasive than a chart, including when it is wrong.**
`fight.heater_dehum` will fire for a user deliberately running a cooler-plus-heater swing regime, and the
app will tell them their tent is broken in plain language. `Stimmt nicht` and the confounder line reduce
this; they do not remove it. Every catalogue entry is a small liability and eighteen of them is eighteen.

**13.4 Image-derived metrics are softer than they look.** `green_frac` depends on a hand-drawn ROI, breaks
when the camera moves, and is meaningfully distorted by blurple LEDs even after white balancing. A
`2,1 % pro Tag` growth figure sounds like a measurement and is closer to an indicator. The copy deliberately
says `der Grünanteil im Bild` rather than `die Pflanze`, but users will read it as plant growth anyway.

**13.5 Server cost and a queue that does not exist.** Timelapse rendering is CPU-bound ffmpeg work with no
job queue beyond an in-process `pLimit(10)`. 150 devices is fine; a few thousand is not, and end-of-grow
renders contend with the hourly rolling timelapses. RabbitMQ is in the stack and the concept assumes it
gets used — an assumption, not a plan.

**13.6 Video in MongoDB.** `Image.data` is a BSON `Buffer` capped at 16 MB. The 40 s / 12 MB render cap is a
real product limitation that a 20-week grower will hit and resent. GridFS is the fix and it is v1.1.

**13.7 The sentence stack is the least dense screen in the market on a large display.** The two-column
media-query response is a partial answer; a techie with a 27" monitor still gets prose where a dashboard
should be. The competing concepts that lead with tiles win that comparison outright.

**13.8 Tokens are a novel affordance with no established vocabulary.** A dotted-underlined number in a
German sentence reads as a link to some people and as nothing to others. The one-time pulse animation is a
coach mark, and coach marks are a smell — if a UI needs one, the affordance is weak. Measured tap-through
on tokens is the metric that decides whether §4's mechanism actually worked, and it may not.

**13.9 The double-feed guard is best-effort, not a lock.** Two people in a cellar, both offline, both
logging: `client_uuid` prevents duplicate *submissions*, not duplicate *feeds*. Neither guard fires because
neither client can see the other. The report surfaces same-window feeds after the fact — which is
consolation, not prevention.

**13.10 Correlation maths has to run on raw Influx samples**, and the raw-sample query path is exactly the
one the dossier flags for unescaped Flux interpolation and an ineffective `limit()`. This concept's server
work is blocked behind fixing that, and that fix is not in the estimate above.

**13.11 Attribution without authorisation is a half-measure a club will notice.** Anyone with the login can
pick any name. For a two-person household that is fine and honest. For a fifteen-member Anbauverein it is
a record that cannot be relied on, and the settings-page disclaimer does not change that. Real memberships
are v1.1 and the concept is exposed until then.

**13.12 It does not fix the biggest structural gap it names.** The concept can tell user type 4 that his
heater and dehumidifier are fighting. It cannot stop them, because that needs firmware roles that do not
exist and will not exist by October. Diagnosis without a remedy is a real product, but it is a smaller one
than the marketing promise "Messen. Regeln. Ruhig schlafen." implies.
