# C47 — Zuruf

**Family:** structure · **Centre of gravity:** conversational addressing over a complete visual UI

---

## 1. Thesis

**One input line at the bottom of every screen lets you tell the tent what you did and ask it what it is
doing, in plain German — and every answer is a permanent, addressable card, not a chat bubble.**

Zuruf ("calling out to it") is a command line for people who cannot use a command line. The insight is not
that people want to chat with their grow tent — they don't. It is that the *fastest capture in the product*
and the *deepest query in the product* are the same gesture, and that gesture can be free-form text because
the destination is not a generated answer but an existing screen. `gegossen` writes a watering record in two
taps. `temp p95 nacht 14d` opens the same Temperature card the word `warm?` opens, scrolled two bands
further down. The line is an *address bar for a product that has no addresses today*, and because it always
lands on a real card that is also reachable by scrolling and tapping, nobody is ever trapped in it.

Three disciplines make it buildable and honest rather than a demo:

1. **A deterministic local grammar handles ~60 patterns and never touches the network.** That is 95 % of
   real utterances. It is instant, free, and works in a cellar.
2. **The language model, when it lands, is a parser and never an oracle.** It maps German → a structured
   command. It never sees telemetry, never writes prose, never says anything about a plant. Every word of
   every verdict comes from the same i18n template the rest of the app uses. A model that cannot generate a
   sentence cannot be confidently wrong about a crop.
3. **A capture never waits for a round trip.** The record is written locally with a client-generated id
   before anything is sent. Retries are idempotent on that id.

## 2. Information architecture

Everything is a **card**. A card is a routable object with a stable key. The home screen is a stack of
cards. A Zuruf is a jump to a card with parameters. There is no tab bar and no second app.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | Device list (unchanged). One device → straight to the stack. |
| `/device/:id` | **Der Stapel** — the home screen. All cards, ranked. Zuruf bar pinned at the bottom. |
| `/device/:id/k/:key` | One card, full screen. Deep-linkable. `?band=`, plus card-specific params. |
| `/device/:id/charts` | The big chart page (ECharts). This is `k/klima` blown up; it keeps the existing query-string view model so presets and share links survive. |
| `/device/:id/diary` | The diary, chronological. Kept as its own route because it is a stream, not a card. |
| `/device/:id/settings` | Existing per-type settings pages, unchanged. Reached from a card's `Einstellungen ▸` row. |
| `/device/:id/testmode`, `/shares`, `/account`, `/classes`, `/demo` | Unchanged. |

**Card keys (`k/:key`)** — the whole vocabulary, and also the whole product:

`klima` · `licht` · `kamera` · `film` · `pflanzen` · `pflanze/:plant_id` · `tagebuch` · `giessen` ·
`fuettern` · `steckdosen` · `zettel` · `alarme` · `co2` · `luefter` · `zuruf` (the vocabulary card itself)

A card that has nothing to show does not exist. `k/pflanzen` on a device with no plants 404s to the stack
with a one-line toast: `Noch keine Pflanzen. Sag „A1 ist Gorilla Glue".` No empty screens, no dead menu
entries.

**Navigation model.** Down = more of the same card. Tap a card in the stack = that card full screen, with a
back chevron. The Zuruf bar is present on every one of these routes and always addresses the *device*, not
the current card — so you never have to go back to ask about something else.

**Ordering.** Above the hairline sits anything urgent, by a fixed priority: firing alarm → device offline →
stale sensor → overdue feed → open condition naming a plant → another actor's capture in the last 6 h.
**Below the hairline the order never changes**: Klima, Licht, Kamera, Pflanzen, Tagebuch, Steckdosen,
Zettel. Not by usage, not by preference, not ever. Someone at 2 a.m. who has learned "Licht is the second
one" is never wrong. The only movement in the whole screen is a card *appearing above* the line, which is
coloured, carries a reason line, and is therefore not a surprise but a message.

**Stale / offline / absent — the three honest states.**

Offline (no sample for >10 min) pushes one card above the line and changes every card below it:

```
│  ○  Offline                              │
│  Zuletzt gesehen vor 3 Std 12 Min        │
│  Alle Werte unten sind von 14:22.        │
```
Below the line every age label switches from relative (`vor 40 Sek`) to absolute (`14:22`) and every number
drops to 60 % opacity. Verdict lines are replaced by the frozen last value with no verdict: `25,4 °C um
14:22` — never `Passt.`, because we do not know that any more.

Stale (a sensor reporting but that measure missing >5 min, e.g. CO₂ sensor dropped off the I²C bus):
`CO₂ — kein Wert seit 22 Min` in place of the number, card stays where it is.

Absent (device never online): the stack is two cards — `Einrichtung` and `Tagebuch` — plus the Zuruf bar,
which still works, because a note about a seed you germinated should not require hardware.
`Noch nie online. Der Controller hat sich hier noch nicht gemeldet.` + `Einrichtung öffnen ▸`.

**The Zuruf bar.** Left: the actor chip (`[Ben]`) — only rendered when more than one actor exists on the
device. Centre: a single-line text input, placeholder `Sag's dem Zelt …`. Right: two quick keys, 💧 (water)
and 📷 (photo), because the two most common captures should not require the keyboard at all. On focus, a
one-row scroller of the four most useful phrases for this device *right now* appears above the input:

```
  gegossen    gefüttert    wie warm?    foto
```

These are generated, not learned: due-feed → `gefüttert` first; alarm firing → `warum zu warm?` first.
Deterministic, so the same situation always suggests the same words.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — "Der Zuruf ist die Adresse, nicht der Modus"

Every card in the product has the same four-band anatomy. All four bands are always rendered. **The
utterance chooses where the card is anchored on open — it never chooses what the card contains.**

```
┌─ Temperatur ───────────────── vor 40 Sek ─┐
│                                            │
│  Passt. 25,4 °C, Ziel 25,0.                │  BAND 1 · URTEIL
│                                            │
│  25,4 °C   Ziel 25,0   ⌀24h 24,9           │  BAND 2 · ZAHL
│  Nacht-Ziel 20,0   Alarm ab 30,0           │
│  Letzte 24 h: 96 % im Band                 │
│ ──────────────────────────────────────     │
│  [ 24 h · Ziellinie gestrichelt         ]  │  BAND 3 · VERLAUF
│  [ Tag/Nacht schattiert · Heizung-Spur  ]  │
│  [ 6h  24h  7d  30d  Lauf ]                │
│ ──────────────────────────────────────     │
│  Roh · 24 h · 5 min · 288 Punkte        ▸  │  BAND 4 · ROH
│                                            │
│  Zuruf: temp 24h                           │  ← the footer, always
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Band 4 expanded is a table of timestamps and values, the Influx measure name (`temperature`), the exact
window and aggregation, `[ Als CSV ]`, and the read-API line to copy.

Why this is not a mode, stated as rules that can be checked:

1. **There is exactly one Temperature card.** `warm?`, `wie warm ist es`, `temp`, `temp p95 nacht 14d` and
   tapping the Klima card all open `k/klima?measure=temperature`. The last one additionally sets
   `band=3&metric=p95&window=14d&phase=night&raw=1`. Same component, same data, different scroll anchor and
   query. Both users can reach the other's position with one thumb-flick — in both directions.
2. **Nothing is persisted about the user.** No expert flag, no per-user card set, no onboarding question.
   The anchor is per-utterance and dies with the utterance. (Band 4's expanded/collapsed state *is*
   remembered per card in `localStorage` — that is a scroll position, not a capability gate, and it never
   moves bands 1–3.)
3. **The verdict and the number are in the same sentence.** `Passt. 25,4 °C, Ziel 25,0.` There is no view
   that withholds the number from the casual user and no view that withholds the meaning from the technical
   one. This is the round brief's "progressive precision", collapsed into one line.
4. **Every card teaches its own address.** The footer prints `Zuruf: temp 24h`. Someone who never types
   anything is still being shown, for free, the syntax that would have got them there faster. Someone who
   types constantly discovers card names by looking at the stack. One vocabulary, learned from both ends.
5. **The line is never the only path.** Every card is in the stack. Every capture has a button. If the
   Zuruf bar were deleted tomorrow the product would still be complete, only slower. That is the test a
   mode fails: delete "expert mode" and features disappear.

What would make this a mode, and is therefore forbidden: a "Profi-Eingabe" toggle; hiding band 4 behind a
preference; a different stack for different users; the model rewriting the verdict line for a "simpler"
audience. None of these are in the design and none of them may be added.

## 5. The chart

ECharts 6, tree-shaken, with a ~50-line hand-rolled Angular directive (owner decision). The chart appears
in three sizes and is *one* component: the sparkline in band 3 of a stack card, the full band 3 of an open
card, and `/charts`. Same options object, three sizes.

**Goal vs actual, drawn:**

- **Setpoint** — a dashed *stepped* line (`step: 'end'`), from `day.temperature` / `night.temperature`
  stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC in config, rendered in the device's
  timezone). Never smoothed — the setpoint really is a step.
- **Target band** — a `markArea` behind it. Source is chosen in one order and *labelled with which one it
  is*: the user's own alarm thresholds (`Alarm 18,0 – 30,0`) > setpoint ± tolerance (`Ziel ± 1,5 °C`) >
  the stage's `vpdRange` for the VPD panel (`Blüte 1,2 – 1,5 kPa`). One band, named.
- **Deviation** — a `visualMap` piecewise fill *between* actual and setpoint, warm above, cool below. Never
  a fill to the axis; today's `type:'area', threshold:null` encodes nothing.
- **Envelope** — `sampling: 'minmax'` plus a combined `{mean,min,max}` series. A single mean line lies
  about a bang-bang controller.

**Day/night** — `markArea` derived from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the schedule when the
measure is absent, with `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` ramp gradients at the edges. A failed contactor then shows
as a *missing* band against a scheduled one — a diagnostic, not decoration.

**Outputs** — a state-timeline lane, 18 px, below the value panels. Region length = duration, `null` is a
gap. Roles come from `parseSocketRoles(hardwareInfo.sockets)`, so a tent with no CO₂ socket has no CO₂ lane.

**Annotations** — a rail *under* the x-axis, max two rows (row 1 human events, row 2 device events), 8–12 px
pixel-proximity clustering with count badges, category chips reusing `collectLogCategories`. Lifecycle stage
changes are full-height dashed lines and carry their label. Touch: tap the rail, not hover.

**Panels** — small multiples with `axisPointer.link` and `echarts.connect()`, 90–120 px each on a phone,
shared x. At most two units per panel and only when physically coupled (Luft + Blatt; Temp + rF). No
fifteen hidden axes.

**Interaction** — one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap pins a
crosshair. The tooltip is replaced by a **pinned scrubbing header** above the chart: one row per panel,
plus the camera frame (§8). Axis labels are never hidden; below 320 px they drop to three ticks and the
unit moves into the panel title.

Zuruf into the chart: `vergleich temp diese woche letzte woche` writes existing query params and opens
`/charts`. `roh temp 14d 1min` opens band 4 with that window. The URL stays the view model, so presets and
share links in circulation keep working.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

The diary stops being a filtered view of `DeviceLog`. Three new Mongo collections, all optional-field,
read-time-normalised (no migration tooling exists).

```ts
Plant {
  plant_id: string;            // uuidv7, client-generatable
  device_id: string;
  label: string;               // "A3" — short, what people shout
  strain?: string;             // free text
  source?: 'seed' | 'clone' | 'unknown';
  started_at: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage;  // existing enum + 'harvested'
  stage_since: number;
  run_id: string;              // a real grow run, no longer inferred by string comparison
  medium_id?: string;
  regime_id?: string;
  pot_litres?: number;
  archived_at?: number;
  harvest?: { at: number; wet_g?: number; dry_g?: number };   // never prompted, see §9
}

Event {
  event_id: string;            // = utterance_id, generated on the CLIENT (uuidv7). Idempotency key.
  device_id: string;
  kind: 'water'|'feed'|'measure'|'note'|'photo'|'train'|'stage'|'harvest'|'condition';
  at: number;
  plant_ids: string[];         // [] means "the whole tent" and is fully legal
  actor_id: string;
  utterance?: string;          // the raw German, ALWAYS kept, even when parsed perfectly
  parse: { source: 'grammar'|'model'|'form'; confidence: number; grammar_version: string };
  volume_ml?: number;
  runoff?: boolean;
  feed?: { items: { product: string; ml_per_l?: number; g_per_l?: number }[];
           ec?: number; ec_basis?: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source';
           ph?: number; regime_step?: number; prefilled_from?: string;
           override_of?: string };            // set when the double-feed block was overridden
  measure?: { ph?: number; ec?: number; tds?: number; tds_scale?: 500|640|700;
              ppfd?: number; distance_cm?: number; height_cm?: number;
              where?: 'input'|'runoff'|'reservoir' };
  text?: string;
  image_ids?: string[];
  env_snapshot?: { t?: number; rh?: number; vpd?: number; co2?: number; light?: number };
  void_of?: string;            // append-only correction; edits never destroy
  created_at: number;
}

Condition {
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; text: string;
  plant_ids?: string[];
  opened_by: string; opened_at: number;
  closed_by?: string; closed_at?: number;
}
```

`env_snapshot` is frozen at write time from the device's last sample. It costs one field and makes every
report reconstructable months later without re-querying Influx — and it is half of the image-correlation
story in §8.

**The diary "does the diary keeping for you"** in three concrete senses, none of them a promise about AI:

1. **Device events are already entries.** Stage changes, alarms, firmware, socket pairing. Today they are
   in `DeviceLog` and half of them are invisible because of the `categories: ['diary-plant-lifecycle']` bug
   (`device.service.ts:714`). Fixed: one `Event` stream, one visibility rule.
2. **Every capture carries the conditions automatically** (`env_snapshot`). You wrote `gegossen`; the diary
   knows it was 25,4 °C, 58 % rF, VPD 1,26, lights on.
3. **The camera writes a picture every 30 seconds without being asked**, and §8 binds those pictures to
   entries.

**A user who ignores plants is untaxed, precisely.** `plant_ids: []` is valid on every event kind. The
capture card shows no plant chips when no plant exists. `k/pflanzen` does not exist. Reports aggregate at
tent level. Nothing ever asks "which plant?" until a plant exists, and a plant only exists when the user
creates one — `A1 ist Gorilla Glue`, or the `+` on a capture card, or the setup wizard's optional step.

**Migration.** `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` (the browser-side string-comparison cycle inference) becomes a
one-time idempotent backfill in a service constructor: it produces `Plant` and `run_id` rows once, and is
then deleted from the runtime. Existing `DeviceLog` diary rows are read alongside `Event` by the diary
route for one release, then backfilled. Server-side pagination is added at the same time — the diary
becoming central makes "fetch the entire history on open" indefensible.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — tap counts

There is no irrigation hardware. This is a capture-and-recall problem, so the only honest metrics are taps
and safety.

**Path A — Zuruf (2 taps + typing).**

1. Tap the input. Type `gegossen`.
2. The card slides up, already complete. Tap `Speichern`.

```
┌─ Gießen ─────────────────────────────┐
│  [Gießen] [ganzes Zelt] [jetzt]      │  ← what was understood, each chip editable
│                                      │
│  Menge          — · [ eintragen ]    │
│  Pflanzen       alle · [ auswählen ] │
│  Foto           [ 📷 ]               │
│                                      │
│  25,4 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,26 · Licht an│  ← env_snapshot, shown so it is not a secret
│                                      │
│  [ Abbrechen ]        [ Speichern ]  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`A1-A3 je 2 liter gegossen` gets three plant chips and `2,0 l` in the same two taps. `gegossen bissl` gets
`Menge: wenig` (stored as `volume_ml: null, text: 'wenig'` — we do not invent a number).

**Path B — no typing at all (2 taps).** 💧 on the Zuruf bar → the same card with plant chips preselected
from the regime → `Speichern`.

**Path C — feeding with a regime (2 taps).** `gefüttert`, or the Pflanzen card's `Füttern` button. The card
comes back **prefilled from the regime's current step**:

```
┌─ Füttern ─────────────────────────────┐
│  [Füttern] [A1 A2 A3 A4] [jetzt]      │
│  Schritt 5 von 12 · Blüte Woche 3     │
│                                       │
│  Bio-Bloom        4,0 ml/l   [ ± ]    │
│  Bio-Grow         2,0 ml/l   [ ± ]    │
│  Menge je Pflanze 2,0 l      [ ± ]    │
│  EC               —  · pH  —          │
│                                       │
│  Zettel: A3 nicht gießen (Ben, Mo)    │  ← open condition naming a selected plant
│                                       │
│  [ Abbrechen ]         [ Speichern ]  │
└───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Every `[ ± ]` is the existing `value-edit-row`. Editing a prefill is expected, not an error; the deviation
is stored (`feed.prefilled_from: regime_step_id` + the actual values) so a report can show "he ran 25 %
under the chart all through flower".

**The double-feed soft block.** Evaluated when `Speichern` is armed, client-side, from the last 24 h of
events held locally — so it works offline too:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30          │
│  gefüttert. Trotzdem?                 │
│  Bio-Bloom 4 ml/l · 2,0 l je Pflanze  │
│                                       │
│  [ Nur A4 ]  [ Abbrechen ]  [ Doch ]  │
└───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Nur A4` is the default-styled button: it deselects the conflicting plants and saves the rest. `Doch`
proceeds and records `feed.override_of`, so a real double feed appears in the report *as a double feed*
rather than being silently smoothed. Window: `regime.min_gap_hours` (default 12 h feed, 6 h water). The
wording names a person and a time and asks a question — it does not accuse and it does not block.

**Schedule following.** A regime advances by **feed events**, not by the calendar: `Schritt 5 von 12`. A
soft calendar mapping produces the reminder: when `days_since_last_feed ≥ regime.step_gap_days`, the
Pflanzen card subtitle becomes `Nächste Gabe: heute`, then `Gabe seit 2 Tagen fällig` in amber. One
consolidated daily push at a user-chosen hour, if enabled. No per-plant notification storm.

**Medium + regime, modelled the way growers speak:**

```ts
Medium     { medium_id, brand, name, prefertilised: 'none'|'light'|'heavy',
             feed_start_week: number, note_key: string, source_url, retrieved_at }
FeedRegime { regime_id, brand, name, kind: 'liquid'|'tablet'|'none',
             step_gap_days: number, min_gap_hours: number,
             steps: { index, stage, items: {product, ml_per_l?, g_per_l?, range?}[],
                      ec_target?, ec_basis: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source',
                      ph_target_by_medium?: Record<string,[number,number]>,
                      label_key?: string }[],
             source_url, retrieved_at, chart_version }
```

Selection copy is one line: `Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow / Bio-Bloom`. All-Mix's first three steps carry
`items: []` and `label_key: 'regime.pre-fertilised'` → the Füttern card for those weeks says
**`All-Mix ist vorgedüngt. Bis Woche 4 nur gießen.`** with the save button relabelled `Nur gießen`. A model
that cannot express "don't feed yet" is not modelling the domain. BioTabs is `kind: 'tablet'`,
`step_gap_days: 35`, and its reminder reads `Neue Tabs fällig`.

Brand names are plain-text labels in a Mongo content collection with `source_url` + `retrieved_at`, shown
as `Zuletzt geprüft: 12.03.2026` on the selection screen — a legal posture that is also a trust feature.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

**This is the headline and a timelapse ships in v1.** Ingest is unchanged — a still every 30 s over MQTT,
no firmware change. `sharp` and `ffmpeg` are already server dependencies. What is new is a nightly job and
a card called **Der Film**.

### 8.1 Culling and normalising — the content problem

Runs at 03:00 device-local over the previous 24 h (~2 880 frames), *before* `thinRtspStreamImages` reaches
them (today's tiers thin to 1/hour after 90 days — a run film built later would be ruined, so `FilmDay`
must be written nightly). Everything below is `sharp` at 64×40 greyscale plus arithmetic; ~15 ms/frame, no
model, no GPU.

1. **Photoperiod gate.** Drop every frame captured while `out_light == 0` (joined from Influx, falling back
   to the `daynight` schedule). Twelve hours of black is not a timelapse. Ramp frames are kept but flagged
   `ramp: true`, and only the two brightest per ramp survive — so sunrise reads as one beat, not 40.
2. **Reject by luminance, difference and flatness.** Against a rolling median of the last 20 kept frames:
   - `L` outside `[median_L × 0.6, median_L × 1.6]` → **door open / torch / lighting glitch**
   - `D` (mean absolute difference vs the rolling median) `> 0.18` → **a human in the tent**, a moved
     camera, or the first frame after a knock. A person occupying a 60×40 cm frame moves far more than
     18 % of pixels; a growing plant moves ~0.5 % per hour.
   - `S` (luminance std) `< median_S × 0.35` → **fogged lens**
3. **Nudge correction.** Phase-correlate a 128×128 centre crop against the day's reference via a 2D FFT →
   integer `(dx, dy)`. If `|dx|,|dy| ≤ 24 px`, translate and crop to a 94 % safe rectangle. If larger, the
   camera was genuinely moved: mark the day `reframed: true`, start a new reference, and put a one-frame
   title card in the film — `Kamera am 12. Juni neu ausgerichtet.` Honest, not hidden.
4. **White balance.** Per-channel means of the day's kept frames are scaled to a **7-day exponentially
   smoothed target** (α = 0.15). This removes the magenta-LED-vs-daylight flicker without greying the
   plant, and a genuine change (dimming for late flower) migrates over a week rather than snapping.
5. **Dedupe.** At most one frame per 4 minutes of lit time; within a window keep the lowest-`D` frame — the
   most representative, least disturbed one. A 12 h photoperiod → **180 frames/day**.
6. Write `FilmDay { device_id, run_id, date, frame_ids[], transforms[], reframed, kept, dropped_by_reason }`.

`dropped_by_reason` is user-facing, on the Film card:
`Heute 180 von 1 440 Bildern im Film. 6 verworfen (jemand im Zelt) · 2 (Tür offen) · 1 (Linse beschlagen).`
That line is how someone debugs a bad film, and it is why the pipeline is trustworthy rather than magic.
Dropped frames are retained 7 days with a `Verworfene Bilder ▸` list and a `Doch reinnehmen` action.

### 8.2 Rendering — a video someone can hold

- **Codec change, and it is a bug fix.** Today `convertRtspStreamImagesToVideo` uses `libx265 -crf 30`
  (`image.service.ts:568-583`). H.265 in mp4 does not play in Chrome or Firefox on desktop, and is a coin
  flip in third-party apps. v1 switches to `-c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 23
  -movflags +faststart`, 1280×720, 12 fps.
- **Adaptive frame budget.** A 90-day flower at 180 frames/day is 16 200 frames = 22 minutes. Nobody
  watches that. The run film targets **90 s** (bounded 60–120 s): `stride = ceil(total_kept / (90 × 12))`,
  applied proportionally across days so a long veg still *reads* as a long veg. Yesterday's segment is
  appended nightly; a full stride re-encode happens only when the budget is crossed, roughly weekly.
- **Artefacts.** `run.mp4` (the whole grow so far, re-rendered nightly) plus the existing rolling `1d` /
  `1w` / `1m` — now built from **culled** frames, which is a strict improvement to something that already
  ships.
- **Burn-in.** Bottom-left `Tag 44 · 12.08.` at 2 % height. Bottom-right, optional and on by default, a
  sensor strip `25,4 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,26`. **No branding is burned in** — § 6 KCanG makes a "Powered by"
  footer on a German user's cannabis video the single riskiest string in the product, and the owner has not
  ruled on it.
- **Storage: GridFS, not a `Buffer` in a document.** A 12 MB mp4 in a 16 MB-BSON collection is malpractice
  waiting to happen. GridFS adds no new infrastructure. `Image` keeps a pointer row so the existing
  `timestampEnd` / `duration` semantics are preserved.

### 8.3 How the video leaves the product

```
GET /film/:device_id/:run_id/run.mp4?token=<imageToken>
→ 200  Content-Type: video/mp4
       Content-Disposition: attachment;
         filename="terpcontrol_zelt1_lauf3_tag44_720p.mp4"
       Accept-Ranges: bytes
```

The Film card has one green button, `Video sichern`. On Android/desktop it is an `<a download>` on a blob
and lands in Downloads / the camera roll. On iOS Safari, where `download` is unreliable, the button reads
**`Video öffnen`** and a one-line hint says `Dann über Teilen sichern.` — we tell the truth about the
platform rather than shipping a button that silently does nothing. 90 s at CRF 23 720p is **8–14 MB**:
under Discord's 20 MB free limit and Telegram's 50 MB multipart limit, so the 1.1 posting path is real.
No login-walled URL is ever presented as a share.

### 8.4 Correlation — three real bindings

1. **The film strip is an axis.** Thumbnails positioned by timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so uneven
   capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivered as **one sprite + JSON index per window** (256×160 tiles, ≤50
   per window), not N requests. Scrubbing the chart moves the picture; tapping a thumbnail moves the
   crosshair. One cursor.
2. **Every Event freezes its conditions** (`env_snapshot`). Picking a photo shows its numbers as a local
   lookup — no query, works offline, still correct in five years when the Influx retention has downsampled.
3. **`Bild von damals`.** Any chart annotation, any diary entry, any plant row shows a thumbnail of the
   nearest *kept* frame within ±15 min plus its numbers. Tap → the Film card seeked to that exact second:
   `FilmDay.transforms` stores each frame's output index, so `t → frame → seconds` is exact, not estimated.

`wie sah A3 vor zwei wochen aus` opens the Film card at that date with the frame's numbers under it. That
one utterance is the product demo.

### 8.5 The camera gets an alarm

A synthetic sensor `camera_age` (minutes since the last kept frame, computed server-side, only counted
during lit hours). It is an ordinary `Alarm` row, so email / webhook / info and the whole existing
cooldown/retrigger machinery come free. Default preset created at pairing, threshold 6 h:
**`Kamera meldet sich nicht. Letztes Bild vor 9 Std.`** The Kamera card goes amber with the same line and
rises above the hairline. A camera that died three days into flower now tells you.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution in v1, authorisation later, and the difference is stated out loud.**

```ts
Actor { actor_id, device_id, name: string, colour: string,
        created_by: user_id, created_at, retired_at? }
```

Device-scoped names the owner types once on `k/zettel`. No accounts, no invitations, no email lookup, no
`auth.middleware` surgery. The Zuruf bar shows the current actor as a chip; tapping it opens a picker. The
choice is sticky in `localStorage` per `device_id` with a **12-hour decay** — after 12 h the next capture
asks once, `Wer ist am Zelt?`, because a phone left in the shed must not attribute Anna's work to Ben.
Every `Event` and `Condition` carries `actor_id`.

**Stated honestly, in the UI, on the actor screen:**
`Namen sind Zuordnung, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann jeden Namen wählen.`
This replaces a clipboard on the tent door, and it is exactly as trustworthy as one — which for a
five-person club is a real improvement and not a lie. Real `Membership { device_id, user_id, role }` plus
rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` is a later release; `actor_id` gains an optional `user_id` then,
and nothing is migrated away.

**Double feed** — §7. Cross-actor is the case it exists for.

**Conditions — the tent door.** `k/zettel` lists open `Condition`s with who opened them and when.
`zettel CO2-flasche fast leer` opens one; `zettel zu 1` closes it. A condition naming a plant is injected
into the capture card for that plant *before* `Speichern`, which is the only place it can actually prevent
something. Closed conditions stay in the diary.

**Cumulative** — §7's plant table, with per-actor breakdown. `wer hat A3 gefüttert` returns it directly.

**Privacy posture.** The club is a German *Anbauverein* or three people and a tent — never a business.

- **Never prompted:** harvest weight, plant count as a headline number, member identity, dispensing.
  `harvest.wet_g/dry_g` exist but the harvest card asks only for a date and shows a plain-text
  `Ertrag notieren` link. Nobody is nagged toward the numbers that separate a lawful hobby from an offence.
- Actor names are free text; the picker's placeholder is `z. B. „B."` — initials are suggested, not required.
- No location, ever. No analytics SDK. EU hosting, stated in plain German on the account screen.
- The public diary link (1.1) shows actor **initials only**, no volumes, no counts, and the screen that
  creates it says: `Ein öffentlicher Link ist öffentlich. Wir zeigen keine Namen, keine Mengen, keinen Ort.`
- The event log is **deletable by the account owner**. Tamper-evidence protects a regulated club and
  victimises an unregulated grower; immutability is never global.
- We do not become anyone's § 26 system of record and never ship a compliance badge.

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

They get the same stack. It is short, because the stack is built from capability, not from `device_type`.

**A `light`:** cards are `Licht` (schedule, dim %, on/off, `out_light` trace), `Tagebuch`, `Zettel`,
`Einstellungen ▸`. No Klima card — the device reports no temperature, so the card does not exist rather
than showing dashes. No Pflanzen, no Film, no Steckdosen, no CO₂.

**A `plug`:** `Steckdose` (state, on/off, its over/under-temperature and time limits), plus `Klima` **only
if** it reports a slave sensor. Same tail.

**A `fan`:** `Lüfter` (rpm, its own limits), `Klima` if sensed. `wie schnell` → the rpm card.

**Concretely unchanged:** `/device/:id/settings` still `ngSwitch`es to `fan-settings` / `light-settings` /
`plug-settings`. Those components are not touched. Zero rework, zero regression risk, and the stack's
`Einstellungen ▸` row is the only new link into them. The `dryer` type is dead per `AGENTS.md` and keeps
rendering exactly as today, not routed through the stack.

**Zuruf on an old device** is filtered by capability at grammar-build time, not at answer time:
`licht 60` writes `lights.limit`; `notiz filter gewechselt` writes an Event; `heizung an` answers
**`Dieses Gerät hat keine Heizung.`** immediately — a local vocabulary miss, not a network call and not a
failure.

**What they explicitly never see:** a plant prompt, a feeding regime, a club actor chip (one actor → no
chip), a film, or a nag to buy a controller. Reveal-by-capability and reveal-by-data do all of this with no
per-type code.

**And the fridge bug dies here.** A `controller` currently renders `devices.fridge.title` — its dashboard
card literally says "Terp Control FRIDGE GROW", and an unnamed controller is *named* that. The stack's card
title is the device type, so this concept cannot ship without a `devices.controller.*` i18n block in `de`
and `en`. Cheapest credibility fix in the dossier, forced by the design.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Monitoring only.** Opens the stack, sees Klima with a verdict, a number and an age. Never types
anything. The only cards present are the ones his hardware supports, so nothing offers control he cannot
perform — `deviceControlCapability`'s fail-open three-bucket collapse is replaced by per-role truth from
`parseSocketRoles`. Once a week he flicks Klima's band 3 and sees `96 % im Band`. That verdict, not the
chart, is what he came for.

**2 · Monitoring with a camera.** The Kamera card is third from the top and shows the last still. Tap →
Film. `Video sichern` puts a 90-second, watchable, colour-normalised film of his grow in his camera roll,
with `Tag 44` burned in and no strangers, no black nights and no magenta flicker. Scrubbing the chart moves
the picture. He is the user the owner says the camera sells to, and he is served in v1, not v1.1.

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** His stack has a CO₂ card and no dehumidifier card
because he has no dehumidifier socket. The Klima chart's output lane shows only the roles he owns. No
humidity target row, no dehumidifier alarm preset, no grow-assistant humidity warning he can never act on.
`co2 1000` sets the target; the card shows `Ziel 1 000 ppm · jetzt 940 · Ventil zu` with the age.

**4 · Full tent.** Steckdosen card lists all five roles with live state. The chart's output lane is his
diagnostic surface: he can see `out_dehumidifier` running as a cooler in `temp` workmode, which the card
labels honestly — `Entfeuchter (kühlt in Betriebsart „Temperatur")`. Nothing here invents socket roles the
firmware does not have; the concept refuses to draw a humidifier that cannot exist.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** Lives in band 4. Every card's raw band gives the exact measure name, window,
aggregation, a CSV button and a copyable read-API line. `roh temp 14d 1min` is one utterance. He is the
type this concept serves *least* — there is still no write API and no custom measure ingest, and band 4 is
a read surface, not a platform. Said plainly in §13.

**6 · Club.** Three people, one tent, one login. Each picks a name on the Zuruf bar. Every capture is
attributed and the double-feed block asks before a second feed inside 12 h. The Zettel card is the tent
door. `wer hat A3 gefüttert` and the plant table answer the cumulative question nobody else answers. No
member identities, no dispensing, no compliance claim.

**7 · Old device owner.** §10. Four cards, familiar settings, nothing broken, nothing nagging.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner:* two taps to log, band 1 is a sentence with the number in it, the card
order below the hairline never moves, 💧 and 📷 need no keyboard. *Normal:* a stack of honest cards with
ages on them, a real timelapse she can post, and a chart that draws the goal. *Techie:* one line of text
reaches any window of any measure, band 4 is always there, the URL is the view model, and the raw utterance
is stored beside every parsed event.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026

Six to eight weeks. What ships:

1. **Der Stapel** + the four-band card anatomy + `k/:key` routes. Reuses `ion-card`, `value-display`,
   `value-edit-row`, `rangeGuard`, `KeyedCache`.
2. **Zuruf bar, local grammar only.** ~60 patterns × de/en, stem matching with Levenshtein ≤ 2, capability-
   filtered vocabulary, the suggestion row, the `k/zuruf` vocabulary card, `Zuruf:` footers.
3. **Freshness honesty** everywhere: live / stale / offline / never-seen, relative→absolute age switching.
4. **`Event`, `Plant`, `Actor`, `Condition`** + capture cards + the double-feed block + a service worker
   with an IndexedDB outbox keyed on client-generated `event_id` (server upsert = idempotent retry).
5. **Medium + regime**, five entries seeded from brand PDFs (Biobizz Light-Mix, Biobizz All-Mix, Floragard
   Light, Green House Powder Feeding, BioTabs), prefill + due reminder + `Zuletzt geprüft`.
6. **Der Film**: cull/normalise/render, `run.mp4`, x264 codec fix, GridFS, `Video sichern`, the
   `dropped_by_reason` line, the camera alarm, `Bild von damals`, the sprite film strip.
7. **ECharts migration** for band 3 and `/charts`: setpoint step line, target band, day/night shading,
   output state lane, annotation rail, min/max envelope. Delete `chart.js` / `ng2-charts` / the adapter.
8. **Controller identity**: `devices.controller.*` in de + en; per-role capability replacing the fail-open
   three buckets.
9. **Forced infrastructure**: server-side pagination for events/logs; the Flux injection fix and a window
   guard on `/data/series` (both are preconditions, not nice-to-haves, because this design widens measures
   and centres the diary).

**Explicitly NOT in v1, and this is the honest part:**

- **The language model.** v1 is grammar-only. An unmatched utterance is saved as a note with
  `Nicht verstanden — als Notiz gespeichert. [ Als Gießen buchen ] [ Als Notiz lassen ]`. An LLM path
  cannot be evaluated against a crop-affecting product in six weeks, and shipping one unevaluated is the
  exact failure mode the brief warns about. It lands in 1.1 behind a server flag, as a parser only, with a
  per-account monthly call cap and a hard rule that it never sees telemetry and never writes prose.
- **Voice (🎤).** Web Speech API is Chrome-only and useless next to a running exhaust fan. 1.1.
- Real memberships and authorisation. VPD matrix, DLI, run-over-run comparison. Public diary URL and the
  Markdown/BBCode generator (cheap, but needs `run_id` settled first). Telegram/Discord film posting —
  though the generic webhook presets ship, since `webhookTemplate.ts` already exists.
- Rotation or sub-pixel stabilisation. Integer translation only.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **A text box promises natural language and v1 answers sixty patterns.** Every miss costs trust, and
   `Als Notiz gespeichert` is a consolation prize dressed as a feature. The suggestion row, the `Zuruf:`
   footers and `k/zuruf` reduce the miss rate; they do not fix it. The real fix is the model, which is
   exactly what v1 cannot responsibly ship. This is the concept's central bet and it may be the wrong one.
2. **German is not one language.** `gegossen`, `gegoßen`, `hab gegossen`, `bissl wasser gegeben`, Austrian
   `angossen`, Swiss `gwässerä`. Fuzzy stem matching helps and will still miss. Building the test corpus is
   real, unglamorous, unbudgeted work, and nobody on the team can write the Swiss cases.
3. **A conversational surface invites scope creep toward an assistant.** "The model is a parser, never an
   oracle" is a discipline, not an architecture. One product decision under launch pressure — "it should
   just tell them what's wrong" — turns this into a liability engine that gives confident advice about
   somebody's crop. Nothing in the code prevents that; only a rule does.
4. **Ranked density above the hairline is a real, if bounded, unpredictability.** I have argued the line and
   the colour make it a message rather than a surprise. A user at 2 a.m. can still find a card where one was
   not before. That risk is not zero and the impaired user is exactly who bears it.
5. **The culling pipeline will drop frames people wanted.** Someone photographing a plant with a phone torch
   is indistinguishable from a door left open. The 7-day dropped-frame list is the escape hatch and most
   users will never open it. The thresholds (0.6/1.6, 0.18, 0.35) are chosen from reasoning, not from a
   corpus, and will need tuning against real tents — which means the first films will be imperfect.
6. **Attribution will be read as authorisation** by at least one club, and the first dispute about who did
   what lands on us. The UI says so plainly. Nobody reads.
7. **Four bands × fourteen cards × two languages × light/dark × four data states is a lot of surface for
   six weeks.** The realistic failure is that bands 3 and 4 ship thin on half the cards — which would make
   the product look exactly like the simple/expert split it was designed to abolish. This is the schedule
   risk that most threatens the concept's own thesis.
8. **GridFS for films buys six weeks and costs a migration.** 5 000 devices × ~12 MB is 60 GB in the primary
   and every backup of it. Object storage is the right answer and is deliberately deferred.
9. **The double-feed block only knows what has synced.** Two phones in a cellar both pass it. `event_id`
   guarantees no double *logging*; it cannot prevent a double *feed*. The report shows it afterwards. That
   is the truthful ceiling of a design with no server in the loop at capture time.
10. **User type 5 is served worst.** Band 4 is a read surface with a CSV button and a curl line. They want a
    write API, custom measures and MQTT. This concept does not build any of it, and a conversational input
    line is close to the opposite of what that segment asks for.
