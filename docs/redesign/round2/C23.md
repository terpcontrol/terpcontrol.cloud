# C23 — "Logbuch"

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** an append-only ship's log. One stream, signed, never
rewritten. Corrections are entries.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Terp Control keeps one book per grow: a single chronological, append-only stream in which the device's
own observations and the grower's own acts are the same kind of row — nothing is ever edited, only
corrected by a later entry, and every screen in the product is a fold of that stream.**

A ship's log is the oldest working design for this product's exact problem: several people, a machine,
a long run, and a question asked months later that nobody can answer from memory. Chronological, because
that is the only ordering nobody has to be taught. Append-only, because a record's value dies the moment
you can quietly change what it said. Signed, because *who wrote it and when* is a different fact from
*when it happened*.

Terp Control already writes such a stream and throws away its meaning: `DeviceLog` holds boot messages,
alarm transitions, config diffs, recipe steps and the five diary categories in one undifferentiated,
unpaginated collection, half of it invisible because `deleted` means "hide". C23 does not add a diary
beside that. It promotes the stream to being the product — `Book` + `LogEntry`, one open book per device,
cursor-paginated, hash-chained — and computes the current state of everything (how much A3 has been fed,
what is broken, what the tent looks like) as a fold over it rather than storing it alongside.

The bet: growers do not want a dashboard, they want to *reconstruct*. The club needs that for § 17 Abs. 2
and for its own sanity; the techie needs it because he does not believe derived numbers he cannot trace;
the stoner needs it because his memory of Tuesday is not evidence. One artefact, three needs, and it is a
list of sentences.

## 2. Information architecture

There are five surfaces. Four of them are folds of the log; the fifth is the settings the device already has.

| Route | Name (de) | What it is | Rendered when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/list` | Geräte | unchanged. One device → straight to its Logbuch | always |
| `/device/:id` | **Logbuch** | the home screen. Live header + the stream + composer | always |
| `/device/:id/kurven` | Kurven | ECharts small multiples; the log is its annotation rail | always |
| `/device/:id/pflanzen` | Pflanzen | plant list; each plant is a filtered fold of the log | `Plant.count > 0` |
| `/device/:id/filme` | Filme | rendered films + the render form | camera paired |
| `/device/:id/settings` | Einstellungen | the existing per-type settings page, untouched | always |
| `/device/:id/buch/:book_id` | älteres Buch | a closed book, read-only | ≥1 closed book |
| `/b/:slug` | (public) | a published book, no login | v1.1 |

**Navigation.** The existing `ion-menu` stays for account/shares/devices. On a device, a four-item
`ion-segment`-styled bar sits directly under the device name: `Logbuch · Kurven · Pflanzen · Filme`.
**Items that have no referent do not render** — a monitor-only user with no camera and no plants sees
two items, and at two items the bar collapses into a single inline link (`Kurven ansehen`) in the log's
header. This is reveal-by-capability and reveal-by-data, not a setting.

`Testmode`, `Wartung`, `Setup` and `Freigaben` move into the header overflow (`⋯`), replacing the six
outline buttons on today's overview. Each writes an entry, so the log is where you find out they happened.

`/device/:id/diary` redirects to `/device/:id`. Its three reports dissolve: `entries` *is* the log,
`growreport` becomes the plant fold, `co2report` becomes a saved filter (`kinds=co2_refill`).

### Block B — the Jetzt-Zeile and its three degraded states

This is the only place in the product that answers "is it OK right now", and it must never lie.

**Fresh** (< 3 min): values in `--ion-text-color`, target beneath each in `--ion-color-medium`, footer
`vor 40 Sekunden`. Outside the target band → `--tc-text-warning`; outside an armed alarm threshold →
`--tc-text-danger`. No colour otherwise, so colour means something.

**Stale** (3–10 min): values drop to `--ion-color-medium-shade`, footer `vor 6 Minuten` in
`--tc-text-warning`. Targets still shown — a stale reading is still a reading.

**Offline** (>10 min, the existing `lastseen` rule): the numbers stay, dimmed, and a full-width line
appears above them:

> **Gerät seit 12:04 offline — 2 Std 11 Min.** Werte von 12:03.

and — the concept's move — **the disconnect is an entry**, written by the cloud when it notices:
`12:04 ⛔ Verbindung verloren`. So "how long has this been going on, and has it happened before" is
answered by scrolling, not by a status page. When it comes back, `14:15 ✓ Wieder verbunden (2 Std 11 Min
offline)` — one entry, not two hundred.

**Absent** (device never reported, or a brand-new book): the three values render `—`, no targets, footer
`Noch keine Messwerte.` The stream holds exactly one entry — `14:22 📖 Buch angefangen · Chris` — and
one line of prose beneath it:

> Noch nichts eingetragen. Alles, was du und das Gerät tun, landet hier.

No hero panel, no wizard nag, no illustration. The `Eintragen` bar is the call to action, and it is
already on screen.

### Block C — open conditions

Renders only when `Condition` documents are open; max three lines, then `+2 weitere`. This is the note
taped to the tent door — the grow-club gap nobody filled. Tapping one opens the entry that opened it,
with `[ Erledigt ]`, which writes a `condition_close`.

### The stream

Grouped by day, sticky day header carrying the run day (`Tag 41`). Runs of consecutive `source:'device'`
entries with no alarm severity collapse into one line — `⋯ 14 Gerätemeldungen` — expandable in place.
This is the only collapsing in the product; it is *by source*, never by user, and it is stateless.

Infinite scroll upward via `GET /device/:id/log?before_seq=<n>&limit=50`. **This is where the codebase
finally gets server-side log pagination**, which it does not have today (`device.service.ts:724-749`);
budget it as v1 work, not a bonus.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

The mode trap assumes the *person* is simple or expert. Here the same person is both, and which one is a
function of the hour: the man who logs a feed at 02:10 with two taps is the man who reads the runoff EC
on Sunday morning. A persisted preference is therefore wrong most of the time, for everyone. C23 moves
the axis: **completeness is a property of the entry, not of the user.**

### M1 — Die Lücke ist ein Eintrag

The composer never requires a field. Every entry can be sealed the instant the verb is chosen. What was
not captured is stored explicitly:

```ts
gaps: ['amount_l', 'ec_in']     // deliberately not captured, recorded as such
```

and rendered, in the expanded entry only, as

> Nicht erfasst: Menge, EC ein   [ ergänzen ]

The two-tap entry and the eleven-tap entry are **the same document in the same collection at different
completeness**, and either can become the other later, by anyone, with nobody configured. The stoner pays
zero taps for what he skipped. The techie is never denied a field — all of them are present, below the
fold of the same sheet, not behind a toggle. The club gets a *work queue* nobody else offers —
`Pflanzen → Unvollständig (6)` — because filling in yesterday's missing litres is real club work somebody
sober does on Sunday.

The discipline that keeps this from being a nag: **a gap never badges the home screen.** No red dots, no
banner. It is a fact about an entry, visible when you open that entry and countable when you go looking.

### M2 — Eine Zeile, zwei Leserichtungen

Every entry is one 44 px row: German prose on the left, machine margin on the right, in the same row.

```
09:41  💧 Gegossen · A1–A4 · 4,0 l                     ← Satz. Vollständige Antwort.
       Chris                     24,8° 58% 1,20 [img]  ← Rand. Beleg.
```

The sentence is a complete answer for someone who reads nothing else. The margin — actor, the context
values captured at the moment of the act, the nearest camera frame — is the evidence, same glance, no
tap. Tapping the row opens it **in place**:

```
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Wasser        4,0 l                          │
   │ EC ein        1,18      pH ein   6,2         │
   │ Plan          Woche 5 · Light-Mix · Bio-Grow  │
   │               2 ml/l — übernommen, ungeändert │
   │ Nicht erfasst Ablauf-EC        [ ergänzen ]  │
   │                                              │
   │ Erfasst 12.09.2026 09:41:33 · Chris · Handy  │
   │ #0412 · 8f2a…c19 ← 3e77…a41                  │
   │                                              │
   │ [ Korrigieren ] [ Streichen ] [ In der Kurve ]│
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Progressive precision with nothing withheld: the verdict, the number, the *provenance* of the number
(*"übernommen, ungeändert"* — the schedule filled it, the human did not touch it), the hash and its
predecessor, and a deep link into the chart at that timestamp. Every prefilled value says where it came
from, which is the techie's distrust of magic answered literally.

### M3 — Was du siehst, hängt am Gerät und an den Daten

Nothing is behind a preference; everything is behind a fact the system can check.

| Surface | Appears when |
| --- | --- |
| `Gegossen` / `Gefüttert` verbs | the device has an open `Book` (controller/fridge get one at claim; a plug does not unless asked) |
| Plant chips in the composer | `Plant.count > 0` |
| Actor name row in the composer | `Actor.count > 1` |
| `Filme` nav item, `Tagesbild`, camera alarm | `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` present |
| CO₂ column in the Jetzt-Zeile | `deviceHasCo2()` |
| Dehumidifier output lane | a socket with that role in `hardwareInfo.sockets` |
| `Schema` (feed regime) prefill | a `Regime` has been chosen for the book |

The solo grower with no plants and no camera never sees a plant chip, a film verb or a name picker — and
never turned anything off. `deviceControlCapability()`'s three-bucket collapse is replaced with per-role
truth read from the same CSV it already parses; the fail-open-to-`'full'` bug is fixed on the way past.

**Why this is not a mode under a new name:** a mode is a persisted property of the *user* that changes
what the *interface* offers. Here nothing is persisted about the user, the interface offers the same
verbs to everyone, and what varies is how much of one form a given act happened to fill. Two people with
identical hardware see an identical app. The same person, twice in a day, sees an identical app.

## 5. The chart

`/device/:id/kurven`. Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, driven by a hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive
(owner decision). Highcharts, `chart.js`, `ng2-charts` and `chartjs-adapter-luxon` all deleted.

**Small multiples, not fifteen hidden axes.** Up to five stacked panels, ~110 px each, one shared x-axis,
one linked crosshair (`axisPointer.link` + `echarts.connect`), fixed order Temperatur · Feuchte · VPD ·
CO₂ · Licht, each rendered only if the device produces that measure. Axis labels are never hidden —
below 360 px they shorten to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.

**Goal against actual, drawn three ways at once.**

```

   ────┴─────────────────────────────────────┴────────────
   Aus: ▬▬▬▬  Heizung      ▬  ▬▬   Entfeuchter
   Log: ●   💧      ⚠         ✎ ●●              📷
```

- **`markLine` (dashed, stepped)** = the setpoint, stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night`
  between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`, parsed from `device.configuration` server-side and
  returned on the device access payload.
- **`markArea` (soft fill)** = the band. Source priority, one at a time and *labelled in the legend*:
  an armed `Alarm` upper/lower threshold (hard, user-declared) > setpoint ± tolerance (soft) >
  `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS[stage].vpdRange` on the VPD panel (advisory). The VPD band **changes along the
  x-axis** as the book's stage entries move it — 0.6–1.0 in seedling, 1.2–1.5 in flowering — which is
  read straight off the log, and which nothing in this market verifiably ships.
- **Actual** is drawn as a min/max envelope with a mean line (`aggregateWindow` already supports both;
  `sampling: 'minmax'`, never LTTB, because LTTB smooths away the bang-bang oscillation that is the
  whole diagnostic).
- **Day/night** shading from measured `out_light > 0.5`, falling back to the `daynight` schedule, in an
  explicit timezone. A failed contactor then shows as a *missing band*, which is a diagnosis.
- **Outputs** get their own state-timeline lane under the axis (region length = duration, null = gap),
  not 0/1 lines fighting for the value axis.
- **The log is the annotation rail.** Under the x-axis, two 14 px rows: human entries above, device
  entries below, clustered by pixel proximity into 10 px bins with count badges, stage changes drawn as
  full-height dashed lines. Tapping a glyph opens the same expandable entry component the log uses. The
  chart and the log are the same object at two zoom levels — that is what makes this concept's chart
  cheaper to build than every other concept's.

**Mobile:** one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom:{type:'inside'}`), tap places
the crosshair. The floating tooltip is replaced by a **pinned scrubbing header** (one compact row per
panel, above the chart) — a fingertip occludes a tooltip and there is no hover on touch.

`measures`/`date`/`interval` query params are preserved verbatim so existing presets and share links keep
working; new params (`rail`, `film`, `band`) are additive and default to today's view. Before any of this
ships, the raw Flux interpolation of `from`/`to`/`interval`/`measure` and the `limit()`-after-`yield()`
no-op (`data.service.ts:80-89`) get fixed — a concept that widens the measure vocabulary owns that bug.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 The entities

All cloud-side MongoDB, all keyed by `device_id`, all optional, no required field added anywhere
(there is no migration tooling; every one of these is read-time-normalised).

```ts
Book {
  book_id; device_id; name?;            // "Herbst 26"
  started_at; ended_at?;
  seq_next: number;                     // server-assigned, monotonic per book
  head_hash: string;
  regime_id?;                           // the chosen medium+feed pair
  day_zero: number;                     // Tag 1 = this date
}

LogEntry {
  entry_id: string;                     // UUIDv7, MINTED BY THE CLIENT
  book_id; device_id;
  seq: number | null;                   // null until the server accepts it
  ts: number;                           // when it HAPPENED (user-settable)
  recorded_at: number;                  // when it was WRITTEN (never settable)
  kind: EntryKind;
  source: 'human' | 'device' | 'cloud';
  actor_id?; actor_name?;               // denormalised so a deleted actor keeps history readable
  subjects: string[];                   // plant_ids; [] = the whole tent
  body: Record<string, unknown>;        // kind-specific, validated per kind server-side
  gaps: string[];
  context?: { temperature; humidity; vpd; co2?; out_light; day_of_run; stage };
  image_ids?: string[];
  corrects?: string;  corrected_by?: string;  struck?: boolean;
  prev_hash: string;  hash: string;
}

type EntryKind =
  | 'book_open' | 'book_close' | 'watering' | 'feeding' | 'note' | 'photo' | 'plate'
  | 'stage' | 'plant_add' | 'plant_end' | 'measurement' | 'training' | 'harvest'
  | 'condition_open' | 'condition_close' | 'co2_refill'
  | 'correction' | 'strike' | 'device' | 'alarm' | 'config';

Plant     { plant_id; device_id; book_id; label; strain?; source?; medium?;
            pot_liter?; position?; started_at; ended_at?; ended_reason? }
Actor     { actor_id; device_id; name; colour; created_by; active }
Condition { condition_id; device_id; text; severity; opened_entry; closed_entry? }
```

`Book.seq_next` and `head_hash` are advanced in one `findOneAndUpdate` per accepted entry — the write
path is serialised per book, which is fine at a few entries per minute per device and must be stated as
a design limit, not discovered later.

**A user who ignores plants is untaxed.** `subjects: []` means the tent. With zero `Plant` documents the
composer has no plant step, the prose reads `Gegossen · 4,0 l` instead of `Gegossen · A1–A4 · 4,0 l`,
the `Pflanzen` nav item does not render, and nothing ever asks. Adding a plant later does not
retroactively demand anything: old entries keep `subjects: []` and read as "the whole tent", which is
true.

**Legacy is adapted, not migrated.** A read-time adapter maps `DeviceLog` rows into `LogEntry` shape
(`source:'device'`, `seq:null`, `hash:null`, `kind` from `categories[0]`), merged into the stream by
`time` under a hairline `vor dem Logbuch` rule. Everyone's history is there on day one.
`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` runs **once**, offline, to seed `Book` boundaries, then is deleted — it
stops being a runtime algorithm that forks history when somebody renames a strain. Same commit fixes the
inherited bugs: `deleted` stops meaning "hidden", the server's stage entries stop being written without
the `'diary'` category prefix, and `DELETE /device/logs/:id` either deletes or goes.

### 6.2 Is append-only hostile to somebody who mistyped? The three states of ink

**Yes, if sealing is instant. So it is not.**

**1 · Nasse Tinte (0–15 min).** A new entry sits at the top of the log with a hairline left border and a
quiet footer:

> Noch änderbar · 13 Min.

During that window, **for its own author on its own session**, the entry is fully editable and fully
deletable, with no correction row, no residue, no `seq`, no hash. It is not in the book yet. This is not
a mode — it is the state every entry passes through, for everyone. The techie likes it for the same
reason: it is a staging area. The window closes early if another entry is written after it, because at
that point somebody else may have read it.

Fifteen minutes covers the realistic mistype: you tapped `Gefüttert` instead of `Gegossen`, you typed 40
instead of 4,0, you picked the wrong plants. It does not cover "I realised on Thursday" — and it should
not, because on Thursday the record has been read.

**2 · Berichtigung (after sealing).** One tap: `Korrigieren`. It opens **the same sheet, prefilled with
the sealed values**. Change what was wrong. Save. What is stored is a new entry:

```
kind: 'correction', corrects: '<entry_id>', body: { …the corrected values… }
```

In the stream the original collapses into a single struck grey line under the correction, and the
default reading experience shows the corrected truth:

```
09:41  💧 Gegossen · A1–A4 · 1,0 l
       Chris                            24,8° 58%
       ~~4,0 l~~ berichtigt 12.09. 21:04 von Chris
```

Two lines instead of one. That is the entire visible cost of a mistake — not a scarlet letter, not a
dialog, not an admission. **Every downstream fold uses the correction**: the plant's Konto counts 1,0 l,
never 4,0 l, never both. Corrections chain (`corrects` can point at a correction), and the log renders
only the head of the chain plus one struck line, with `alle 3 Fassungen zeigen` in the expanded view.

**3 · Streichung.** For "this never happened at all" — wrong device, double entry, someone else's tent.
`Streichen` asks for a reason from three chips (`Versehen` · `Doppelt erfasst` · `Falsches Gerät`) or
free text, and writes `kind:'strike'`. The struck entry renders as one grey struck line, collapsed, and
is excluded from every fold. Reason required, one tap, no typing needed.

**And the whole book is destructible.** Append-only binds entries *to each other*; it does not bind the
book to the server. `Einstellungen → Buch löschen` destroys entries, images, films, plants and actors
with no tombstone and no server-side record that the book existed. A hash chain over nothing is nothing.
This is deliberate and it is the answer to the strongest objection in the dossier (§6.3 rule 6:
tamper-evidence *victimises* an unregulated grower): C23 makes the record honest against *its own
readers*, never against its owner. The single asymmetry accepted: an `Actor` cannot delete the book —
only the account owner can. That is the club's requirement, and its cost is that a club whose owner
leaves loses its history.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

No irrigation hardware exists. This is a capture-and-recall problem, and capture happens one-handed, in
a dark tent, possibly impaired, possibly by somebody who is not the owner.

### 7.1 The composer and its tap counts

The `Eintragen` bar (sticky, 56 px, thumb-reachable — an inline bar rather than an `<ion-fab>`, because
the codebase has zero fabs and a fab covers the newest entry) expands upward into a verb grid of 72 px
tiles:

```
┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
│    💧    │    🧪    │    ✎     │
│ Gegossen │ Gefüttert│  Notiz   │
├──────────┼──────────┼──────────┤
│    📷    │    ⚑     │    🌿    │
│   Foto   │  Zustand │  Phase   │
└──────────┴──────────┴──────────┘
```

**Fastest watering — 2 taps.** `Eintragen` → `Gegossen`. Sealed on the second tap, with a toast
`Gegossen eingetragen · rückgängig`. Body: `{ }`. Gaps: `['amount_l']`. Context captured automatically.
Prose: `Gegossen`. Expanded: `Nicht erfasst: Menge [ergänzen]`.

**Schedule-following feed — 3 taps.** `Eintragen` → `Gefüttert` opens a sheet **already filled from the
active `Regime` step**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Gefüttert                       12.09 09:41│
│ Anna   [Chris]   Ben          ← nur wenn >1│
│ Alle   [A1] [A2] [A3] [A4]    ← nur wenn   │
│                                 Pflanzen   │
│ Wasser        [ − ]  4,0 l  [ + ]          │
│ Nach Schema · Light-Mix · Woche 5          │
│   Bio-Grow      2 ml/l   →  8 ml           │
│   Bio-Heaven    2 ml/l   →  8 ml           │
│                          [ Schema ändern ] │
│           [    Speichern    ]              │
│ ─────────── weitere Felder ─────────────   │
│ EC ein        —          pH ein      —     │
│ EC Ablauf     —          pH Ablauf   —     │
│ Notiz         …                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

→ `Speichern`. **3 taps** for the full schedule-following feed with product, dose and computed
millilitres. This is the owner's "it thinks for me".

**Deviation:** +1 tap per stepper press (litres step 0,5; doses step 0,5 ml/l). A changed value renders
in `--tc-text-info` and its entry records `body.deviated: ['amount_l']`, which is why the expanded entry
can say *"übernommen, ungeändert"* or *"vom Schema abgewichen: 5,0 l statt 4,0 l"*.

**Club/techie maximal:** the four analytical fields are **below the save button**, in the same sheet,
reached by a scroll — visible, never hidden, never behind a toggle. Full capture is ~11 taps
(3 + plants 1 + EC 2 + pH 2 + Ablauf-EC 2 + Notiz). Same document, same collection, higher completeness.

### 7.2 Schedules — medium + feed regime, exactly as growers say it

```ts
Regime {
  regime_id; label;            // "Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom"
  medium_label;                // "All-Mix"     ← the substrate IS half the scheme
  feed_label;                  // "Bio-Grow / Bio-Bloom"
  feed_start_week: number;     // All-Mix: 4. Light-Mix: 2. BioTabs: null.
  steps: [{ from_week; to_week; stage?; products: [{ name; ml_per_l }];
            ec_target?; ec_basis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source'; ph_target?;
            interval_hours; note? }];
  source_url; retrieved_at; verified_at;
}
```

Selected once, at `Buch anfangen` or later from `Einstellungen → Schema`. Its **only** job is to prefill.
It never blocks, never alarms, never computes a shopping list. Brand names are plain-text labels — no
logos, no brand colours, no brand name inside a Terp Control feature name — and the numbers live in a
Mongo content collection so any brand comes out in one DB update.

Seed set: Biobizz Light-Mix, Biobizz All-Mix, Floragard Light, Green House Feeding, BioTabs, plus
`Kein Schema` and one ownerless ladder. An All-Mix book in week 2 prefills **nothing** and the sheet
says, in the schedule slot:

> All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — bis Woche 4 nur gießen.

That sentence is the whole point of modelling medium and feed together. A scheme that cannot express
"don't feed yet" is not modelling the domain.

**The reminder.** The stoner's second half of the owner's brief. When `now − last feeding on these
subjects > regime.step.interval_hours`, the log grows one entry at the top of today:

`08:00  ⏰ Fällig: gießen/füttern (zuletzt vor 3 Tagen)`  ·  `[ Jetzt eintragen ]`

It is an entry, so it scrolls away with everything else, it is in the record, and it needs no separate
notification centre. Push, if the user enabled notifications, reuses the existing alarm delivery.

### 7.3 The double feed

At the moment `Gefüttert` (or `Gegossen`) opens, the client queries the log for a feeding on any
overlapping `subjects` within `interval_hours`. If found, the sheet opens with an amber band **above
everything else**:

> ⚠ **Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.**
> 4,0 l · Bio-Grow 2 ml/l · EC 1,2
> [ Trotzdem eintragen ]     [ Abbrechen ]

A soft block: it never refuses, it costs one deliberate extra tap, and taking it stamps
`body.override_of: '<entry_id>'` so the entry renders with `⚠ bewusste Doppelgabe` and the correction
history is explicit. This is the most expensive mistake in a shared room and no round-one concept
prevented it.

### 7.4 The cumulative question

Every plant and every book carries a **Konto** — a fold of the log, not a stored counter:

```
A3 · Blüte Woche 5 · Tag 41
──────────────────────────────
Wasser        47,5 l   in 14 Gaben
Bio-Grow      94 ml
Bio-Bloom     60 ml
Letzte Gabe   12.09. 09:41 · Chris
Verteilung    Chris 8 · Anna 5 · Ben 1
Unvollständig 2 Einträge      [ ergänzen ]
EC ein        Ø 1,19  (n=9)
```

Reconstructable *because* the log is append-only with corrections: the fold has one canonical answer and
`corrects`/`struck` resolve it deterministically. Tapping any row filters the log to exactly the entries
that produced it — no number in this product is unattributable to rows.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is the headline feature, and v1 ships a file a person can hold.

### 8.1 What already exists (verified in the repo)

`image.service.ts` pulls a still every 30 s, rebuilds three rolling mp4s hourly, thins old stills in four
tiers (1/min after 1 d, 1/5 min after 7 d, 1/15 min after 30 d, 1/h after 90 d) and keeps them 3 years.
The container already carries **ffmpeg, imagemagick and ttf-dejavu**; `sharp@0.34.5` and `uuid@13` are
already dependencies; `addOfflineOverlay()` already composites SVG text onto a JPEG with `sharp`.
**The film pipeline needs no new dependency.**

One finding that matters: the existing timelapses encode **`libx265`** (`convertRtspStreamImagesToVideo`).
HEVC in mp4 does not play in Firefox at all, nor in desktop Chrome without platform support. A film that
*leaves* the product must be **H.264**, so the export is a separate encode; the rolling `1d/1w/1m` clips
stay as they are for in-app preview.

### 8.2 The film — what ships in October

**A film is a range of the log rendered as video.** There is no "timelapse feature" to find: from a day
header, a stage entry, the book header or a plant, the action is `Film daraus machen`, and the range is
whatever you were already looking at. Presets: `Ganzer Lauf` · `Diese Phase` · `Letzte 7 Tage` ·
`Von hier bis hier`.

**Frame selection — 8 slots per photoperiod day.** A tent lamp is fixed at a fixed dim percentage, so
unlike an outdoor timelapse *every hour of the photoperiod looks the same*. Multiple slots per day are
therefore free of the flicker problem, and they turn nyctinasty — leaves lifting and drooping — into
visible content. Slots are evenly spaced between `sunrise_end + 15 min` and `sunset_start − 15 min`,
from `daynight` + `lights.sunrise`/`sunset`. 84 days × 8 = **672 frames = 28 s at 24 fps**, and against
the existing thinning tiers (worst case 1/hour beyond 90 days) 8 slots/day is always satisfiable.

**Culling, in order. Every rejection is recorded.**

1. **Nacht.** Reject any candidate whose `out_light < 0.5` at ±60 s (from `FrameIndex`, below). Night is
   not a picture, it is a gap. `Nachtbilder einbeziehen` is an unchecked box on the render form — a
   parameter of one render, not a persisted preference.
2. **Rampe.** Reject frames inside the sunrise/sunset ramp windows; exposure is mid-transition.
3. **Mensch im Zelt.** No ML. **A human-authored log entry within ±8 minutes rejects the frame outright**
   — if somebody wrote "gegossen" they were standing in the tent. This is the synergy that makes the log
   the right home for the camera, it is exact, and it costs one index lookup.
4. **Struktureller Ausreißer.** dHash (8×8 → 64 bit) of the candidate vs the **median dHash of the same
   slot across the surrounding 7 days**. Hamming > 22 → reject (a person, an open door, a moved pot).
5. **Belichtungsausreißer.** Mean luminance deviating > 35 % from the 7-day slot median → reject (door
   open, flash, lamp fault).
6. **Unschärfe/Beschlag.** Variance of Laplacian on a 320 px downsample < 60 % of the 7-day slot median →
   reject (condensation).
7. On any rejection, walk outward from the slot in 5-minute steps up to ±45 min. If nothing survives,
   that slot is a **hole**, and holes are not interpolated — the film simply has fewer frames that day,
   which is honest.
8. **Dedup.** Two accepted frames with Hamming distance < 3 → keep the sharper.

**Normalisation.** Per accepted frame, over a fixed central 60 % rectangle: mean L and mean R/B. Target =
the **21-frame centred rolling median** of each, so the correction follows slow drift instead of fighting
it. Gain via `sharp().linear(a,b)`, gray-world channel scale via `.recomb()`. This removes the dimmer's
80→100 % step and an ageing LED's magenta drift without flattening the plant's actual change.

**Stabilisation, honestly limited.** Translation only: phase-correlate each frame's 256×256 grayscale
centre against the previous accepted frame, clamp ±32 px, crop a 6 % safety margin. Handles the day-12
nudge. Displacement over 32 px, or any rotation, is *not* corrected — it is detected, and the film takes
a hard cut with a burned `Kamera versetzt` caption, which reads as intentional rather than broken.
Optical flow is v1.1 and I am not pretending otherwise.

**Burn-in.** Persistent small top-left `Tag 041 · 12.09. · 24,8 °C · 58 % rF`, plus lower-third captions
for in-range entries of `kind ∈ {stage, feeding, watering, harvest, training, condition_open}`, held 12
frames (0,5 s). Both use the existing `sharp` + SVG + DejaVu path. The caption text is the same German
prose the log line shows — one string, two renderings.

**Encode.**

```
ffmpeg -framerate 24 -f image2 -i %05d.jpeg \
       -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
       -crf 23 -movflags +faststart -vf scale=1280:-2 out.mp4
```

672 frames at 720p/crf 23 ≈ 4–8 MB. Stored in a new `Film` collection with the buffer inline (under the
16 MB BSON cap, same pattern as today's timelapses). If the encode exceeds 14 MB the job re-encodes at
crf 28, then falls back to 4 slots/day. Long books past ~20 s of runtime are the reason a `films/` disk
volume is on the v1.1 list — the server container has **no volume today**, and adding one touches
`docker-compose.yaml` and `backup.sh`. Named, not hidden.

### 8.3 How a video leaves the product

```
GET /film/:film_id/download
  Content-Type: video/mp4
  Content-Length: 6291456
  Content-Disposition: attachment;
    filename="terpcontrol-zelt-1-tag-001-084.mp4";
    filename*=UTF-8''terpcontrol-zelt-1-tag-001-084.mp4
```

Filename: `terpcontrol-<slugified device name>-tag-<from>-<to>.mp4`, ASCII-slugged, run days not dates
(a grower says "day 41", not "12 September").

Each film gets **two buttons**, and the difference is spelled out in the copy because it matters on iOS:

- `[ Teilen ]` — primary. `navigator.canShare({files})` → `navigator.share({files:[file], title})`. On
  iOS 16+ Safari and Android Chrome this opens the native sheet, where **In Fotos sichern** and
  **In Dateien sichern** live. This is the path that actually reaches a camera roll.
- `[ Herunterladen ]` — a plain `<a download>` on the endpoint. Reliable on desktop; on iOS it opens the
  player, so the helper text says: *"Auf dem iPhone öffnet sich der Film — dann lange drücken und
  sichern. Oder ‚Teilen' benutzen."*

No login-walled URL is offered as if it were a share. A link to the film page also exists and is labelled
`Link zur Seite` — a different thing, named differently.

### 8.4 The camera alarm

The gap every round-one concept left. A `cloud`-source rule: if no `Image` has arrived for **6 hours**
while the photoperiod says the light is on, write

`14:02  ⛔ Kamera stumm seit 08:01`

into the log at `severity: 1` and route it through the existing alarm delivery. Resolution writes
`✓ Kamera liefert wieder (nach 6 Std 12 Min)`. Because it is an entry, it appears in the day's page, in
the film's report, and in the annotation rail — a film with a three-day hole comes with the entry that
explains the hole, in the same stream.

### 8.5 Image correlation

A new small collection is the join, and it is the same index the culler needs:

```ts
FrameIndex { device_id; ts; image_id; dhash; meanL; lap;
             out_light; temperature; humidity; vpd }
```

One document per **10-minute** candidate frame = 144/device/day (~22 k/day across the first 150 units;
trivial). Written by a job that walks new stills, reads the Influx values at that timestamp once per
batch, and stores them. This is the only place in the product where an image and a number are married,
and everything correlational reads from it:

- **In the log:** every entry's margin carries the nearest frame as a 28 px thumbnail, tap → full frame
  with the entry's context values printed under it.
- **In the chart:** a film strip glued to the x-axis, thumbnails positioned by real timestamp so uneven
  capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivered as **one sprite sheet plus a JSON index per window** (256×160
  tiles, ≤100 per sheet) instead of N requests. Moving the crosshair moves the picture above the chart;
  tapping a thumbnail moves the crosshair and re-centres the log.
- **From the picture back:** on any full-frame view, `Was war da los?` opens the log at that timestamp.
- `Image.timestampEnd` already marks an mp4 as spanning a range; those render on the rail as a bracket,
  not a point.

The correlation is bidirectional and it goes **through the log**, because the log is the only structure
in the product that holds pictures, numbers and human acts on one clock.

## 9. Clubs

The club need is reconstructability — *who did what, and how did the plants react* — and it is the exact
need an append-only log was invented for. Everything below is v1 and none of it touches
`auth.middleware.ts`.

**Attribution without an auth rewrite.** `Actor` is a device-scoped name the owner types once
(`Einstellungen → Wer ist am Zelt`). At composer open, if `Actor.count > 1`, the sheet's first row is a
name-chip row with the last-used pre-selected — **one tap, or zero if it was you last time**. Stamped to
`actor_id` + `actor_name` on every entry. Roughly two days of work, and it delivers the entire "who did
what" question in v1.

**And it is honestly labelled as a label.** Attribution is not authorisation: anyone with the account
password can post as anyone. The UI says so once, in `Wer ist am Zelt`:

> Namen sind Zuordnung, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Konto hat, kann unter jedem Namen eintragen.

Real memberships (a `Membership` collection, rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207`, loosening
~10 owner-scoped queries) are a later release. The `Actor` model upgrades cleanly: `actor_id` becomes a
`user_id` when one exists, and old entries keep their denormalised `actor_name`.

**The double feed** — §7.3. The single highest-value club feature in this document.

**Conditions** — §3 block C. `Condition {text, opened_entry, closed_entry?, severity}` with the open set
pinned above the log. "CO₂-Flasche fast leer", "Steckdose 3 wackelt", "A3 nicht gießen, zu nass". Open
facts with an owner, which is neither an event nor a task, and which nobody else modelled.

**The cumulative answer** — §7.4, including the per-actor split the club actually asks for.

**Privacy posture — what is refused, and why it does not cost the club anything.**

- No member identity beyond a free-text nickname. No DOB, no address, no membership number, no
  per-member dispensing, no grams handed over. Those are § 26 KCanG duties that drag a grow controller
  into Art. 9 GDPR for data it has no use for. `Wer ist am Zelt` says: *"Spitzname reicht."*
- Export defaults to **pseudonymisiert** (actors → `Person A/B/C`) with `Namen · Pseudonyme · Anonym` on
  the export sheet. Anonymised is one tap away and is the recommended option in the copy.
- **No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.** No compliance warranty of any kind.
- Harvest weight is a field on `kind:'harvest'` that is **empty by default and never prompted**. It is
  there for the user who wants it and absent for the user who does not.
- `Buch löschen` destroys everything, one action, no residue (§6.2).
- The public book URL (`/b/:slug`, v1.1) publishes prose, pictures and climate, and **never** actors,
  plant counts or weights.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Monitoring only.** Opens the app, reads the Jetzt-Zeile, closes it — two seconds. Once a week he
scrolls the log and finds the three lines that matter among collapsed device noise: an alarm, a stage
change, an offline gap with its duration. He never taps `Eintragen`; `Pflanzen` and `Filme` do not exist
for him. What he gains over today: a value that tells him how old it is, and a record of every time the
tent misbehaved while he was not looking, scrollable instead of hover-only.

**2 · Monitoring with a camera.** Same, plus the `Tagesbild` and a `Filme` item. On day 41 he taps
`Filme → Ganzer Lauf → Film erstellen`, waits ~90 s, taps `Teilen`, and puts a 17-second 1280×720 H.264
file in his camera roll with the day number and temperature burned into every frame; on day 84, 28
seconds. **This is the user the October release is for, and he is served on day one, not in v1.1.** He
is also told when the camera stops.

**3 · Controls certain aspects.** His Jetzt-Zeile carries a CO₂ column (`deviceHasCo2()`) and no
dehumidifier lane, because no socket holds that role — per-role truth replaces the three-bucket collapse
that today shows him humidity targets he cannot act on. His chart bands temperature and CO₂ and leaves
humidity unbanded, which is the honest picture. The bottle level is a `Condition`; `co2_refill` entries
fold into "letzte Füllung vor 19 Tagen".

**4 · Full tent.** Most output lanes, most bands, most config entries — and the log is where he finds out
the setpoint changed at 21:03 on Tuesday, by whom, from what to what, which no screen tells him today.
C23 does not fix his real problem (no humidifier role, no exhaust role, `out_dehumidifier` doubling as
the cooler); it stops hiding it — the expanded output entry reads
`Rolle: dehumidifier · im Modus "temp" kühlt dieser Ausgang`.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** He is why the hash is in the expanded entry. `GET /device/:id/log` is
cursor-paginated JSON with stable `entry_id`s, monotonic `seq` and `prev_hash`/`hash`: pull
incrementally, verify completeness, never re-fetch, and corrections arrive as new rows with `corrects` —
the only sane sync semantics. His probe still cannot become a chart series (`VALID_SENSORS` is
hard-coded), but it can become a `kind:'measurement'` entry via `POST /device/:id/log` and draw on the
annotation rail — a *write* API the product has never had, cheap because the log is already the write
surface.

**6 · Club.** Three names in `Wer ist am Zelt`. Anna feeds at 19:00; Ben opens the composer at 20:30 and
the amber band stops him. The Konto answers "wie viel Bio-Bloom hat A3 diesen Zyklus bekommen, und von
wem" with no query. On Sunday somebody works the `Unvollständig (6)` queue — as corrections, visibly, by
a named person. Export is pseudonymised by default. The club never becomes the state's reporting system.

**7 · Old device owner.** §10. Nothing is taken away, history is there on day one, and what they gain is
a readable paginated log and the ability to note which lamp they installed.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **A chronological stream is the wrong shape for "how much".** Scrolling never answers "how much
   Bio-Bloom has A3 had". The Konto fold answers it, but it is a *second* structure the user must know
   exists. C22's stored weekly sheet answers it by construction; mine answers it by query. If the Konto is
   not discoverable, the club's headline need is served by a feature nobody finds.

2. **The log is a wall of text on a phone** — two hundred device rows a day, realistically. Collapsing
   consecutive `source:'device'` runs is exactly the kind of hiding this round is suspicious of, and it
   hides what the techie came for. By-source and stateless is a real defence, but a critic is right that
   scrolling past `⋯ 14 Gerätemeldungen` fourteen times a screen is not a good time.

3. **Corrections cost the impaired reader.** Two lines where one would do, and a struck number next to the
   real one is exactly the ambiguity a 2 a.m. reader does not need. Every correction makes the record
   permanently slightly harder to read for the person least able to read it. That is the price of the
   central commitment; it can be kept small, not designed away.

4. **The hash chain is engineering nobody sees.** Serialised per-book writes, monotonic `seq`,
   `prev_hash` — several days of work, a real concurrency constraint, and zero v1 UI beyond a hex string.
   The only honest defence for shipping it at launch is "retrofitting leaves a hole in the chain".

5. **The double-feed block fails exactly when it is needed.** It is a client-side query against the log; a
   second phone with bad reception in a cellar sees nothing and the block silently does not happen. In v1
   that person cannot log at all — loud rather than silent, but not good.

6. **Attribution is a label and I ship it knowing that.** Anyone with the password posts as anyone. The
   copy says so, but a club that reads its log as evidence of `eigenhändig` participation is believing
   more than the data supports.

7. **The culler can cull the interesting frame.** A pot knocked over at 03:00 is *precisely* the
   large-structural-change signal rule 4 rejects, and a film that omits the disaster is worse than one
   that includes it. The rejection report exists; nobody reads reports. Promoting sustained structural
   change into an entry would fix it, and that is not in v1.

8. **`FrameIndex` adds 144 documents per device per day** in a system with no retention policy for
   anything — small at 150 units, an unbudgeted growth curve beside an InfluxDB bucket that already has
   no retention and no downsampling.

9. **"Append-only" will be misread as "you cannot delete your data".** In a cannabis product that is the
   worst possible first impression, and the mitigation is one sentence in a settings screen. The concept's
   name works against its own privacy posture.

10. **The book boundary is a new thing to get wrong.** Forget to close a book and the counter reads
    `Tag 340`; close one early and the plants are orphaned. `convertEventsToGrowCycles` at least guessed;
    C23 asks, which is more correct and more work for the user.
