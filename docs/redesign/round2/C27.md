# C27 — Die Erzählung

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** the diary is prose, not rows.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Terp Control writes the grow down as German prose a human would actually read — one paragraph per
day, every number in it live and tappable — and the export is that document.**

The owner's selling point is that the app "does the diary keeping for you". A table of rows is not diary
keeping; it is data entry with the labour moved from the grower to the grower. What a forum, a club's
records and the grower's own memory in February all want is a *readable account*. So the spine of the
product is a generated German text: for each day, three to six sentences assembled from what the device
measured, what the plan said and what people entered. The human edits those sentences directly.

The app then has **one reading surface**, which is how it answers this round's hard problem. The stoned
owner reads three sentences at 2 a.m. and is done. The techie taps a number *inside* a sentence and falls
through to the setpoint, the raw series and the editor — same screen, same DOM, no toggle, no preference.
Depth lives inside the sentence, not behind a chevron.

The risk of prose is confident nonsense, so §4b specifies the answer as rules rather than intentions:
**no model writes anything.** Every generated sentence is a template over facts the system can cite, and
the generator is forbidden by rule from causality, from judgement, and from an adjective without a
threshold beside it.

## 2. Information architecture

Navigation stays `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu`. Inside a device, an `ion-tab-bar` appears — and its tab
count is **decided by hardware, not by a setting**: `Heute · Kurve · Film · Plan`, where `Film` exists
only if `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is set.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | unchanged. One device claimed → straight to that device's `Heute`. |
| `/device/:id` | **Heute — Die Erzählung.** The home screen. Reverse-chronological day paragraphs. |
| `/device/:id/kurve` | The chart. Reached from the tab bar *and* from any Messwert-Token. Keeps the legacy path `/charts` as a redirect so every share link and chart preset in circulation still resolves. |
| `/device/:id/film` | The camera studio: live still, film strip, timelapse render + download. Present only when a camera is paired. |
| `/device/:id/plan` | Settings, renamed. Existing `ngSwitch` on `device_type` untouched underneath. Legacy `/settings` redirects. |
| `/device/:id/buch` | **Das Buch.** The whole grow as one continuous document, plus export and the public URL. Reached from a link at the top of `Heute`, not from the tab bar. |
| `/buch/:slug-:code` | Public read-only book. No login. New `ShareLink.page = 'buch'`. |
| `/device/:id/testmode`, `/diagnostics`, `/classes`, `/shares`, `/account`, `/login`, `/demo` | unchanged. |

`/device/:id/diary` redirects to `/buch`. The three report modes (`entries`/`growreport`/`co2report`)
survive as query params so existing diary share links keep working; `entries` becomes "Alle Einträge" —
a flat row list that stays for anyone who wants rows, and is the compatibility surface for
`LogEntryViewerModule`.

### 3.2 Copy rules that the layout above encodes

- The **eyebrow** carries weekday + date in words; the **header** carries `Tag N`, the stage and the data
  age. The grower's mental index is "Tag 43", not a timestamp.
- **Underlined tokens** (dotted 1 px, `--tc-text-info`, no icon, no button chrome): every quantity and
  every named thing — `6:00`, `24,8 °C`, `0,3 °C über dem Ziel`, `1,21 kPa`, `A1–A3`, `Bio-Bloom`, `Anna`.
- Numbers use `formatNumber(v,'de-DE','1.1-1')` — `24,8`, never `24.8`; the locale is already registered
  in `webapp/src/app/util/locale.ts`. Durations are always words (`4 Std 20 Min`), never `0:40:00`.

### 3.3 Stale, offline, absent

**Stale** (last sample 10–60 min old). Header age turns amber. Today's paragraph *stops at the last known
sample* rather than implying the present:

> Das Licht ging um 6:00 an. Bis 11:40 lag die Temperatur bei 24,8 °C, die Luftfeuchte bei 58 %.
> **Seitdem hat sich der Controller nicht mehr gemeldet — seit 2 Std 15 Min.**

Styled as a `Randnotiz`-weight aside in `--tc-text-warning`. No live tile, no number pretending to be
current.

**Offline** (> 60 min). Header reads `offline seit gestern 23:04` in `--tc-text-danger`. Heute becomes
one sentence:

> Zelt 1 ist offline. Das letzte Lebenszeichen kam gestern um 23:04. Die Geschichte geht bis dahin.

Everything below renders normally, because the book is not live data — **an offline grow diary is still a
grow diary**, which a tile dashboard is not. The action bar stays enabled; you can write a note in a
dead-wifi cellar.

**Absent** (device claimed, no telemetry yet, no notes):

> Hier steht bald deine Grow-Geschichte. Sobald der Controller misst, schreibt sie sich von selbst.
> Den ersten Satz schreibst du: Was steht im Zelt?
>
> `[ Grow anlegen ]`

`Grow anlegen` is a 3-field sheet: Name, Medium (chip row), Startdatum (defaults today). Nothing else.

**Absent camera:** the image block simply does not render, and the `Film` tab is not in the tab bar.
No empty state, no upsell tile. Reveal by capability.

**Absent plants:** every sentence drops the plant clause. "Anna hat gegossen, 2 l." No plant chip row in
the note sheet. The word *Pflanze* does not appear anywhere in the UI for a plantless grower.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — "Der Satz ist der Schalter"

**The mechanism: every quantity in the prose is a live token, and the token expands in place.**

There is exactly one rendering of the story. Both the impaired user and the techie load the identical
DOM. The difference between them is not what the app shows; it is *whether they touch anything*.

### 4.1 The three token classes

**Messwert-Token** — `24,8 °C`, `58 %`, `1,21 kPa`. Tap expands an inline panel *below the paragraph*,
pushing content down. Never a modal, never navigation.

```
  24,8 °C  ▾
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  ╭╮  ╭──╮      ╭╮                       │  88 px ECharts sparkline
  │ ╯ ╰──╯  ╰──────╯╰───   ······ Ziel 24,5 │  actual solid, setpoint dashed
  │                                         │
  │  Min 22,1 · Ø 24,8 · Max 28,1           │
  │  Im Zielbereich: 91 % der Zeit          │
  │  Abdeckung: 98 % der Messwerte          │
  │                                         │
  │  [ In der Kurve öffnen ]  [ Rohdaten ]  │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Rohdaten` is a paginated table of the raw 5 s samples. Nobody is denied the number; nobody is forced to
read it.

**Ziel-Token** — `0,3 °C über dem Ziel`, `12 Std Licht`. Tap expands the **existing `value-edit-row`**,
inline, in the sentence's place:

```
  Ziel 24,5 °C  ▾
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Tagesziel Temperatur                   │
  │  [ − ]  ────────●──────────  [ + ]      │  the real value-edit-row,
  │              24,5 °C                    │  rangeGuard directive and all
  │  [ Speichern ]                          │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Saving writes the config exactly as the settings page does and appends a *Notiert* sentence to today:
"Du hast das Tagesziel von 24,5 °C auf 25,0 °C gesetzt." **You changed a setpoint from inside a sentence,
and the change wrote itself into the story.** That loop is the concept in miniature.

**Sache-Token** — `A1–A3`, `Bio-Bloom`, `Anna`, `Blüte Woche 3`. Tap expands an entity card:
- *Pflanze*: photo strip, `Tag 43 seit Steckling`, last five sentences, `[ Nur diese Pflanze lesen ]`.
- *Produkt*: "Diesen Zyklus: 340 ml Bio-Bloom in 8 Gaben. Zuletzt vor 4 Tagen (Anna)." — the grow-club
  critic's cumulative question, answered one tap from where the product is named.
- *Person*: "Anna, letzte 7 Tage: 3× gegossen, 1× gefüttert, 1 Notiz."
- *Phase*: the stage's target band and `[ Phase wechseln ]`.

### 4.2 The provenance hatch

Long-press (or right-click) **any generated sentence** → `Woher weiß das die App?`

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Woher weiß das die App?                │
  │                                         │
  │  Satz:     narr-day-temp-excursion      │
  │  Fenster:  25.08. 06:00–18:00 (MESZ)    │
  │  Quelle:   InfluxDB status.temperature, │
  │            Rohsamples                   │
  │  Werte:    40 Min > 27,0 °C;            │
  │            Max 28,1 °C um 15:40:25      │
  │  Schwelle: Alarm „Übertemperatur",      │
  │            27,0 °C, von dir gesetzt     │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

On every generated sentence, always. It is the techie's audit trail and the mechanism that keeps the
generator honest: **a sentence that cannot fill this sheet cannot be written.**

### 4.3 Why this is not a mode — the defence

1. **There is no state.** No persisted preference anywhere — no `localStorage` key, no user field, no URL
   param, no server flag. A mode is state by definition; this has none.
2. **Nothing is hidden.** The fact is *in the sentence* at the precision a human needs. The token adds
   context — a series, a setpoint, an editor — never the fact. "Show more" hides a feature class; a token
   hides nothing.
3. **Order is fixed and chronological.** This concept deliberately *refuses* the brief's permitted
   "adaptive ordering by behaviour" mechanism, because unpredictability is the specific thing that breaks
   the impaired user. Today is at the top, forever, in the same words.
4. **The affordance costs nothing to ignore.** A dotted underline is not a control you must decide about;
   it looks like text because it is text. A chevron announces "there is something else, elsewhere, and
   you must choose."
5. **It degrades to the good outcome.** With token JS broken the page is still a complete, accurate grow
   diary. Break a mode toggle and you get half an app.

**The sharpest formulation:** *a mode asks "who are you?" before it shows you anything. A token asks
nothing and answers only when touched.*

### 4.4 The objection this concept must survive

*"Prose is the stoner's mode and the techie is being fobbed off with tap-to-expand."*

The techie's three real needs are met on this surface, not another: **density** (Das Buch is the same
sentences with no chrome, `Strg+F`-able, ~120 lines for a full grow — denser per pixel than any tile
dashboard), **precision** (every token bottoms out in raw 5 s samples and a JSON export), and
**auditability** (§4.2, which no tile dashboard offers). What they do *not* get is a wall of simultaneous
live gauges. That is a real loss, named in §13.

## 4b. Zwei Stimmen — how the generated text stays honest

This is the part critics will attack, so it is specified as rules, not intentions.

### 4b.1 Exactly two voices, visually distinct

| Voice | Rendering | Source | Editable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gemessen** | plain body text | assembled by `NarrativeTemplate` from device data + structured Notes | no (the underlying fact is, via tokens) |
| **Notiert** | 2 px left rule, `--ion-color-success` | verbatim human text, or a human-confirmed prefill | yes, always, verbatim |

**There is no third voice.** The app never writes interpretation, diagnosis or advice into the prose.

### 4b.2 Seven hard rules on the generator (each testable)

1. **No causality.** No `narr-*` string may contain *weil, deshalb, dadurch, führte zu, wegen, sorgte
   für*. Enforced by a unit test over the whole key set in both `de.json` and `en.json`.
2. **No adjective without a threshold beside it.** *zu warm* is unwritable; only "über 27 °C", with the
   threshold's origin in the provenance sheet.
3. **The plant is never the subject of an app-written sentence about its condition.** The app has no organ
   that can see a plant. "Die Pflanzen sahen gestresst aus" is structurally impossible to emit.
4. **Coverage gate.** A measure sentence is written only if that day's coverage ≥ 80 %. Below that: "Für
   Dienstag fehlen 7 Stunden Messwerte; darüber steht hier nichts." Coverage is computed on **raw**
   Influx samples, never on `aggregateWindow(fn:mean)` output.
5. **Never round away a violation.** A 40-minute excursion inside an in-band daily mean makes the
   excursion sentence *mandatory* and outranking. Selection is by rule priority, not prettiness.
6. **No cross-grow superlatives** unless computed over stored `Grow` aggregates and shown with the
   comparison set.
7. **Templates are i18n keys with named params** — no string concatenation anywhere. `de` and `en` are one
   code path; a missing key fails loudly in CI, not silently at 3 a.m.

### 4b.3 Where judgement lives: die Randnotiz

Judgement is not banned from the product — it is banned from the *prose*. It lives in a visually
separate, dismissible aside anchored to a day, which always states its rule and its evidence:

```
  ⓘ Randnotiz · Regel: VPD in Blüte 1,2–1,5 kPa
    Gemessen: 1,74 kPa über 3 Std 20 Min (14:10–17:30).
    [ Als Notiz übernehmen ]   [ Ausblenden ]
```

`Als Notiz übernehmen` opens an editable *Notiert* sentence prefilled with the grower's likely words —
"Zu trocken am Nachmittag, Luftfeuchte hochgezogen." — which the human then owns or discards.
**Randnotizen never appear in Das Buch, in any export, or on the public URL.** Only what a human wrote or
a device measured leaves the product. The v1 rule set is six boring rules: VPD out of stage band,
temp/humidity excursion beyond the user's *own* alarm thresholds, feed overdue vs. the chosen regime,
camera silent, controller offline > 1 h, socket on > 20 h/day. Each dismissible per day, mutable per grow.

### 4b.4 No LLM, and the concept does not want one

An LLM's failure mode here is a fluent, confident, wrong German sentence about someone's crop, in a
document they will paste into a forum under their own name. The template engine's failure mode is a
stilted sentence. **Choose stilted.** If an LLM is ever added it may only rewrite *Notiert* text a human
already wrote, never generate *Gemessen*, and the provenance sheet must say so.

### 4b.5 Repetition — the other quality problem

Ninety days of "Die Temperatur lag bei X °C" is wallpaper. Two structural mitigations:

- **Day collapsing.** A day gets its own paragraph only if it differs from the previous beyond a threshold
  (Δmean > 1 °C or > 5 % rF, any excursion, any Note, any stage or setpoint change). Otherwise quiet days
  merge: *"Donnerstag bis Sonntag verliefen wie Mittwoch: 24–25 °C, 55–60 %, keine Eingriffe."* One tap
  expands them. Reveal-by-data, and it fixes scroll length: a well-run 90-day grow is ~25 paragraphs.
- **Newsworthiness ranking.** Max six sentences per day: stage change > excursion > human action >
  setpoint change > light > climate summary > "Sonst ist nichts passiert."

Consequence, repeated in §13: **the document is most interesting when the grow went badly.**

## 5. The chart — `Kurve`

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, behind a hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision; no
`ngx-echarts`). Highstock stays in the repo for the legacy full-chart page until v1.1 (§12).

**Small multiples, one shared crosshair** (`grid[]` + `axisPointer.link` + `echarts.connect()`): 4–6
panels of ~100 px on a phone, at most two units per panel and only when physically coupled.

Per panel: **Ist** as a solid line with `sampling:'minmax'` (never LTTB on the plot — it smooths away the
bang-bang oscillation the grower is looking for) plus a min/max envelope; **Ziel** as a *dashed stepped*
line that steps at `daynight.day`/`night` between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature` (this data
already exists client-side and has simply never been drawn); **Zielband** as one `markArea` whose source
is chosen explicitly and named in the panel header (the user's own alarm thresholds > setpoint ±
tolerance > the stage's `vpdRange`); **Abweichung** as a signed fill *between Ist and Ziel*, never to the
axis.

**Tag/Nacht** as `markArea` derived from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the `daynight` schedule
in an explicit timezone. A failed contactor then shows as a *missing band* — a diagnostic, not decoration.

**Ausgänge** in a state-timeline lane below the panels (region length = duration, `null` = gap), labelled
with the active workmode so `out_dehumidifier`'s double meaning becomes legible: "Entfeuchter (im Modus
*temp*: Kühlung)".

**Annotationsleiste** — a 16 px rail *under* the x-axis, max two rows (row 1 human, row 2 device),
pixel-proximity clustering, category chips from the existing `collectLogCategories`, full-height dashed
lines for stage changes.

**Only in C27:**

1. **The annotation tooltip is the sentence.** Tapping a mark shows the *Notiert* or *Gemessen* sentence
   from the story, in full, with its provenance hatch. The chart and the story are two renderings of one
   corpus.
2. **Below the chart sits the day's paragraph, live.** As you scrub the crosshair, the paragraph under
   the chart re-renders to whichever day the cursor is on — the *same component* as the home screen
   paragraph. Scrubbing the chart moves the picture *and* the prose:

```
  ┌──── Ist / Ziel ─────────────────────┐
  │ ······················ Ziel dashed  │
  │  ╭─╮   ╭───╮   ╭──╮                 │
  └──┴───────────┬─────────────────────┬┘
  ▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏▏  Filmstreifen (thumbnails on the same x-scale)
  ─┴──●───────┴──────────────  Annotationsleiste
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ DIENSTAG, 24. AUGUST · Tag 42        │
  │ Das Licht lief 12 Std … 40 Minuten   │
  │ lang lag sie über 27 °C …            │
  └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Mobile:** one finger = page scroll, two fingers = pan/zoom (`dataZoom:'inside'`), tap = crosshair. The
floating tooltip is replaced by a pinned scrubbing header. Axis labels are never hidden — below 320 px
they shorten to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.

**Verdict block** at the top: time in/above/below band as one stacked bar, split Tag vs. Nacht, longest
excursion as a *duration*, mean/min/max, coverage %. Computed server-side on **raw** samples, greyed out
below 80 % coverage. Okabe-Ito palette.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New Mongo collections (all cloud-side, all fields optional, read-time normalisers)

```ts
Grow {                                   // replaces the browser-inferred "cycle"
  grow_id: string; device_id: string; name: string;   // "Northern Lights #4"
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  medium?: string;                       // 'biobizz-all-mix' (label id, plain text)
  regime_id?: string; feedStepIndex?: number;         // advanced by feed EVENTS, not wall clock
  autoflower?: boolean;                  // -> ×0.5 dose multiplier
  recordWeights?: boolean;               // default FALSE. §9 privacy.
  slug?: string; archived?: boolean;
}

Plant {
  plant_id: string; grow_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                         // "A1"
  strain?: string; source?: 'seed'|'clone'|'unknown';
  startedAt: number; removedAt?: number;
}

Note {                                   // one Notiert sentence, or one structured event
  note_id: string;                       // CLIENT-MINTED uuid v4 -> idempotent retry
  device_id: string; grow_id?: string; plant_ids?: string[];
  at: number; actor_id?: string; actorName?: string;
  kind: 'text'|'water'|'feed'|'training'|'stage'|'harvest'|'measurement'|'photo';
  text?: string;                         // the human's own prose. Stored VERBATIM.
  water?:   { volumeL?: number; perPot?: boolean; runoff?: boolean };
  feed?:    { items: {productLabel: string; mlPerL?: number; gPerL?: number}[];
              volumeL?: number; perPot?: boolean; step?: number; ofSteps?: number };
  measure?: { ph?: number; ec?: number; ppm?: number; ppmScale?: 500|640|700;
              where?: 'input'|'runoff'|'reservoir' };
  harvest?: { wetG?: number; dryG?: number };     // written only if Grow.recordWeights
  images?: string[];                     // image_id[]
  deviceLog_id?: string;                 // the mirrored DeviceLog row
  duplicateAcknowledged?: boolean;       // §8 soft block was overridden
  supersedes?: string;                   // corrections instead of edits
  deletedAt?: number;
}

Condition {                              // the open fact pinned to the tent door
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; grow_id?: string; plant_ids?: string[];
  text: string; openedBy?: string; openedAt: number;
  closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
}

Actor {                                  // device-scoped. NO accounts, NO auth change.
  actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string;
  colour?: string; createdBy: string; disabledAt?: number;
}

NarrativeDay {                           // the cached paragraph
  device_id: string; grow_id?: string;
  date: string;                          // 'YYYY-MM-DD' in the device's timezone
  dayOfGrow?: number; weekOfGrow?: number; stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage;
  sentences: { key: string; params: Record<string, string|number>;
               voice: 'gemessen'|'notiert'; note_id?: string;
               provenance: { window: [number,number]; source: string;
                             values: Record<string, number|string> } }[];
  coverage: number; generatedAt: number;
  editedText?: string;                   // if set, this is what renders. Verbatim.
}
```

Indexes: `Note {device_id, at}`, `Note {grow_id, kind, at}`, `Note {note_id} unique`,
`NarrativeDay {device_id, date} unique`, `Plant {grow_id}`, `Condition {device_id, closedAt}`.

### 6.2 The DeviceLog shadow

Every `Note` also writes a `DeviceLog` row with `categories: ['diary','diary-water'|'diary-feed'|…]` and
`deleted: true` (the existing manual-entry convention). The Note is the truth; the log row is the
compatibility surface, so `LogEntryViewerModule`, the chart annotation rail, existing share links and
"Alle Einträge" keep working unchanged. `Note.deviceLog_id` links them.

Two legacy traps fixed cheaply: the server's own stage-transition entry gets
`categories: ['diary','diary-plant-lifecycle']` (one string), and "Logs löschen" becomes "Geräte-Logs
löschen" with copy saying it does not touch the diary — because it never did.

### 6.3 Migration off `lifecycleName`

`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` (currently a 57-line runtime algorithm in the browser) becomes a **one-time
idempotent boot-time backfill** in the narrative service: scan lifecycle logs per device, emit `Grow`
rows, attach the existing logs, set `Grow.name` from the trimmed `lifecycleName`. Renaming a grow after
that no longer forks history, and a re-veg no longer fabricates a plant. Existing grow-report share
links (keyed on the cycle's start timestamp) are honoured by keeping `Grow.startedAt` as the resolution key.

### 6.4 How a user who ignores plants is untaxed

**There is always exactly one implicit `Grow` and zero `Plant`s.** A grow is created silently on first
telemetry with `name = ''` and `startedAt = firstSample`. It has no UI until the grower names it.

With `Plant.count === 0`:
- Sentences drop the plant clause entirely: "Anna hat gegossen, 2 l."
- The note sheet has no `Welche?` row.
- The word *Pflanze* appears nowhere.
- The Sache-Token class for plants simply has no instances.

Creating a plant is one field (`Label`) on a `[ + Pflanze ]` row inside the grow sheet. You do it only if
you want your sentences to name them. Nothing anywhere nags you to. That is reveal-by-data, and it is
the same mechanism that hides the camera from a camera-less device.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — with tap counts

No irrigation or dosing hardware exists. This is a *capture and recall* problem, so the design target is
tap count on the happy path and safety between people on the unhappy one.

### 7.1 The action bar

`+ Notiz | ⌁ Gegossen | ⌁ Gefüttert` — sticky at the bottom of the `Heute` viewport, 48 px targets,
always reachable one-handed. It is the only persistent control in the product.

### 7.2 Gegossen — schedule-following case: **2 taps**

```
 tap 1 → ⌁ Gegossen
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Gegossen                          ✕    │
  │  Wer:  [Anna] [Nils] [Ich]              │  actor chips, last-used preselected
  │  Was:  [A1] [A2] [A3] [A4]              │  all preselected; absent if no plants
  │  Wieviel:  2 l je Topf          [ ⌄ ]   │  prefilled from last time
  │  Wann:  jetzt                   [ ⌄ ]   │
  │                                         │
  │        [      Speichern      ]          │  green, full width, 56 px
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
 tap 2 → Speichern
```

Deviating costs 1 tap per adjustment: `Wieviel` expands into a `value-edit-row` (`[−] range [+]`,
0,5 l steps); deselecting a plant is one chip tap; `Wann` offers `jetzt / vor 1 Std / vor 2 Std /
gestern Abend` as four chips before it offers a picker. **Worst realistic case: 6 taps.**

### 7.3 Gefüttert — schedule-following case: **2 taps**

The regime prefills the whole sheet from the current step:

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Gefüttert                         ✕    │
  │  Wer:  [Anna] [Nils] [Ich]              │
  │  Was:  [A1] [A2] [A3] [A4]              │
  │                                         │
  │  Nach Plan, Schritt 6 von 13:           │
  │   Bio-Grow    4 ml/l             [ ⌄ ]  │
  │   Bio-Bloom   2 ml/l             [ ⌄ ]  │
  │   Bio-Heaven  2 ml/l             [ ⌄ ]  │
  │  Menge:  2 l je Topf             [ ⌄ ]  │
  │  Zuletzt gefüttert: vor 5 Tagen (Anna)  │
  │        [      Speichern      ]          │
  │  [ + Etwas anderes ]                    │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The stoner never sees a table of ml/L unless they open a row. The club's records get every number
regardless, because the prefill *is* what gets stored on Speichern. `+ Etwas anderes` adds a free product
row (plain-text name + number + unit chip).

### 7.4 The double-feed soft block

On sheet open, `GET /grow/:grow_id/recent?kind=feed&plants=A1,A2,A3&hours=12`. If a matching Note exists:

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  ⚠  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 Min     │
  │     gefüttert: Bio-Grow 4 ml/l,         │
  │     Bio-Bloom 2 ml/l, 2 l je Topf.      │
  │                                         │
  │     [ Abbrechen ]   [ Trotzdem eintragen ]│
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Abbrechen` is the **green primary**. `Trotzdem eintragen` is a plain outline button — one deliberate
extra tap, visually demoted, never the default. The resulting Note carries
`duplicateAcknowledged: true`, and the story writes it plainly, without euphemism:

> Nils hat A1–A3 ein zweites Mal gefüttert, 1 Std 45 Min nach Anna.

Default windows: feed 12 h, water 6 h, per-grow editable. The block is per *plant intersection*, so
feeding A4 while Anna fed A1–A3 does not warn.

### 7.5 Schedule following

The regime is a Mongo content document, never bundled into the webapp:

```ts
FeedRegime {
  regime_id: string; label: string;              // "Green House Feeding — Powder Feeding"
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number;      // shown as "zuletzt geprüft 12.06.2026"
  chart_version?: string; kind: 'official_pdf'|'official_calculator'|'manual_transcription';
  mediumHints?: string[];                        // 'coco' | 'soil' | 'allmix'
  startAfterWeeks?: number;                      // All-Mix: 3. Light-Mix: 1. BioTabs: null.
  steps: { fromWeek: number; toWeek: number; phase: 'veg'|'bloom'|'flush';
           items: { productLabel: string; mlPerL?: number; gPerL?: number; note?: string }[];
           ecBasis?: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source'; ecTarget?: [number,number];
           phTarget?: [number,number] }[];
}
```

Selected once at grow creation as **medium + regime, the way growers say it out loud**: two chip rows.
`Biobizz All-Mix` alone produces a real sentence on day 1:

> Du hast All-Mix gewählt. Das ist stark vorgedüngt — nach Plan wird die ersten 3 Wochen nicht gefüttert.

Advance is by **feed events**, not wall clock: `Grow.feedStepIndex++` per feed Note, so a Tuesday/Friday
grower who slips does not desynchronise. The due-feed sentence is generated from `feedStepIndex` + the
mean interval of the last three feed Notes.

Brand names are plain-text labels only — no logos, no brand colours, no bottle imagery, and never inside a
Terp Control feature name. The feature is called **Plan**, not "CANNA-Modus".

### 7.6 What is stored, and the cellar

Stored: the `Note` (§6.1) + its `DeviceLog` shadow. `ec`/`ph` carry `where` (input/runoff/reservoir) and
`ppm` carries its scale — a bare "ppm" number is never rendered.

**Offline capture.** `@angular/service-worker` is added (new dependency, new build step — flagged) with a
**capture-only outbox**: reads are online-only, writes are queued. Idempotency is structural: the client
mints `note_id` before the request, so a retry is an upsert against a unique index and **cannot
double-log**. The action bar stays enabled offline, and the story shows queued notes immediately with a
small `↑` marker until they land.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is the illustration of the story, and in v1 it produces a file a person can hold.

### 8.1 What exists today (verified in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`)

- 30 s stills over MQTT/RTSP/OKAM P2P → `Image {format:'jpeg'}`, corrupt-frame detection already present.
- Hourly rebuild of three rolling timelapses (`1d`/`1w`/`1m`), frame interval 2/14/60 min, 25 fps,
  **libx265 CRF 30**, stored as `Image {format:'mp4', duration, timestampEnd}`.
- Thinning tiers at 1 d / 7 d / 30 d / 90 d, retention 3 years.
- `ffmpeg`, `imagemagick` and `sharp` are all already in the server container, and `addOfflineOverlay()`
  already composites an SVG caption onto a JPEG with sharp. **The whole pipeline this section needs is
  already installed.**

Two problems with today's output, both real: **libx265 in mp4 does not play in a large share of desktop
browsers and Android WebViews**, and the three rolling timelapses have no relation to a grow.

### 8.2 v1 ships **Der Film** — a per-grow timelapse you download

**Frame selection is anchored to the photoperiod, not the wall clock.** This is the concept's substantive
camera idea. `out_light` is already in InfluxDB, so lights-on/off are known for every day; frames are
picked at fixed *fractions of the light period* (default 20/40/60/80 %). When the grower flips 18/6 to
12/12 on day 35, a wall-clock timelapse jumps and a photoperiod-anchored one stays consistent. No
competitor does this, because no competitor knows when the lights were on.

**The culling and normalisation pipeline** — all server-side, all on 64×64 grey thumbnails except the
final render, all cheap:

1. **Lichtgate.** Discard every candidate whose timestamp falls outside `out_light > 0`. Removes ~40 % of
   frames and every black/IR frame in one step.
2. **Rampengate.** Discard frames within `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` ramp duration + 10 min of a
   transition. Exposure is unstable there.
3. **Slot-Auswahl.** For each day × each of the four photoperiod offsets, take the nearest surviving
   frame within ±20 min. Missing slot → no frame; the film shows a gap, honestly.
4. **Ausreißer-Verwerfung — the hand/door/person filter, with no ML.** Compute a 64×64 grey thumbnail per
   candidate (sharp, ~2 ms) and compare it to the **median thumbnail of the same slot on the ±3
   neighbouring days**. Reject if per-pixel MAD exceeds `k·σ` of that slot's history, or if mean luminance
   deviates > 25 % from the slot's rolling median. A hand in shot, an open door, a torch and a person in
   frame all produce the same signature: a large deviation that *reverts*. A genuine *step* — the plant
   grew, the lamp was re-hung — does not trigger it, because the reference median moves with it.
5. **Duplikat-Verwerfung.** Consecutive kept frames below a thumbnail-distance floor: drop the later one.
   Catches a frozen stream and a camera pointed at a wall.
6. **Ausrichtung — translation only in v1.** Integer-pixel (dx, dy) by brute-force search over ±16 px on
   the thumbnails against the previous kept frame, scaled up, cropped with a 5 % safety margin. Rotation
   is **out of scope for v1**: a camera nudged 5° still jumps, the UI says so, and
   `[ Ab hier neu ausrichten ]` resets the alignment reference at that frame.
7. **Normalisierung.** Per-frame grey-world white balance + luminance normalisation toward the run's
   rolling median, via `sharp().linear(a,b)` and channel gains. Kills the magenta-LED-vs-daylight jump.
   **Honest limit:** full colour constancy under 3000 K LED plus a daylight door is not solvable this
   way; the film still shifts slightly. Said once, in the UI.

**The caption strip is the image correlation, baked into the pixels.** Every kept frame gets a 48 px
strip composited at the bottom with sharp + SVG, reusing the exact technique of `addOfflineOverlay()`:

```
  Tag 043 · Blüte Wo 3 · 25,1 °C · 58 % rF · VPD 1,21 kPa · 12 Std Licht
```

A grow film that leaves the product carries its own data forever, in every player, on every platform,
with no app required. That is the answer to "image correlation" that survives being posted in a forum.

**Rendering.** `ffmpeg`, 12 fps (not 25 — at 4 frames/day, 25 fps makes a 78-day grow a 12 s blur):

```
ffmpeg -framerate 12 -f image2 -i %05d.jpeg \
       -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2" \
       -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 21 \
       -movflags +faststart -an out.mp4
```

H.264 High + yuv420p + faststart plays everywhere: iOS, Android, every desktop browser, Telegram,
Discord, WhatsApp, every forum's video embed. 78 days × 4 frames = 312 frames = **26 s, ~4–7 MB at 720p**.

**The same encoder settings replace libx265 for the three rolling timelapses**, which is a bug fix, not a
feature. Existing HEVC blobs age out through the normal 3-year retention; no migration tooling needed.

### 8.3 How a video LEAVES the product

```
GET /device/:device_id/film/:grow_id.mp4
      ?token=<imageToken>&fps=12&frames=4&res=720
→ 200
  Content-Type: video/mp4
  Content-Disposition: attachment;
      filename="terpcontrol_northern-lights_tag001-078_2026-08-25.mp4"
  Content-Length: …
  (streamed from a rendered tmpfile, then unlinked)
```

Filename pattern: `terpcontrol_<grow-slug>_tag<from>-<to>_<renderdate>.mp4`. `/device/:id/film` shows:

```
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Der Film · Northern Lights #4            │
  │  Tag 1 bis Tag 78 · 312 Bilder · 26 Sek.  │
  │                                           │
  │  ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐  │
  │  │        [ video player ]             │  │
  │  └─────────────────────────────────────┘  │
  │                                           │
  │  Bilder pro Tag:  [1] [2] (4) [8]         │
  │  Auflösung:       [720p] [1080p]          │
  │                                           │
  │  [ ⤓ Film speichern ]  [ 🔗 Link teilen ] │
  │                                           │
  │  152 von 464 Bildern wurden aussortiert:  │
  │  88 nachts, 41 Ausreißer (Tür/Hand),      │
  │  23 doppelt.        [ Aussortierte zeigen ]│
  └───────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`⤓ Film speichern` is a plain `<a download href="…">`. On iOS Safari it lands in Files (two taps to
Photos); on Android, in Downloads. **A share that produces a URL requiring a login is not a share**, so
`🔗 Link teilen` mints a `ShareLink { page:'film' }` serving the same mp4 unauthenticated at
`terpcontrol.cloud/film/<slug>-<5char>` — a URL that unfurls with a video preview in Telegram and Discord.

`[ Aussortierte zeigen ]` is the honesty affordance: a contact sheet of every rejected frame with its
reason and a per-frame `[ Doch behalten ]`. The pipeline is aggressive; the grower can overrule it.

### 8.4 Image correlation inside the app

Three places, all the same underlying join on timestamp:

1. **Der Filmstreifen ist eine Achse** (§5). Thumbnails positioned by real timestamp on the chart's
   x-scale, so uneven capture reads honestly as a gap. Delivered as **one sprite + a JSON index per
   window** (256×160 tiles, 50 per sprite), not N image requests. `Image.timestampEnd` already marks
   mp4s as spanning a range — those render as a bracket, not a point.
2. **Scrubbing moves the picture and the prose.** The chart crosshair drives the film strip cursor, the
   still above it, and the day paragraph below it, on one shared time cursor.
3. **Every photo carries its numbers.** A photo attached to a `Note`, or tapped in the film strip, opens
   with the caption strip's data underneath it and a `[ Diesen Moment in der Kurve zeigen ]` button.
   Conversely, tapping a Messwert-Token's sparkline at a point offers the nearest frame.

### 8.5 The camera alarm

Freshness honesty is not enough — a camera that stopped three days into flower must say so unprompted.
`image.service` gains a check on the existing hourly `compressRtspStreams` tick: if a device has
`cloudSettings.rtspStream` or `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` set and no `format:'jpeg'` image inside 6 h,
write a `DeviceLog` with `severity: 2` and reuse `alarm.service`'s existing e-mail path. In the story it
appears as a sentence, not a badge:

> Seit dem 3. September, 14:20 kommen keine Bilder mehr von der Kamera.

Escalates in the copy at 24 h and 72 h. **Honest weakness: there is no push notification anywhere in this
product, so this arrives by e-mail.** That is weak for exactly this failure and it is named in §13.

## 9. Clubs

### 9.1 Attribution without an auth rewrite

An `Actor` is a **name the device owner types once**. No account, no invitation, no e-mail, no change to
`auth.middleware.ts:172`. Plan → `Wer arbeitet am Zelt?` → a list of free-text names.

At capture: a chip row at the top of every sheet, last-used preselected and sticky per browser for 12 h
(`localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']`). Normal case: **zero extra taps.** Someone else grabbing the
phone: **one tap.** Stamped to `Note.actor_id` + a denormalised `actorName` so a renamed or deleted actor
never rewrites history.

**Attribution shows up as the subject of the sentence** — this is where prose pays for itself. In a table
"who" is a column you have to look at; in the story it is the first word.

> Anna hat A1–A3 gegossen, 2 l je Topf.
> Nils hat den Entfeuchter-Sockel neu gekoppelt.

The app states its own limit once, in the Actor settings, in plain German:

> Namen sind Zuordnung, kein Login. Wer die App öffnen kann, kann jeden Namen wählen.

That is honest and it is the correct posture: attribution is a label, authorisation is a gate, and this
ships the label in v1 (~2 days) while the gate — a `Membership` collection plus the two-query auth
rewrite — waits for a release that can afford it. `actor_id` is forward-compatible: it can later be bound
to a `user_id` without touching a single stored Note.

### 9.2 The double-feed problem

§7.4: a soft block at the moment of capture, on the plant intersection, with a demoted override and an
honest sentence when overridden.

### 9.3 Conditions — the standing open fact

Not an event, not a task. An open fact with an owner, rendered **above** today's paragraph, styled like a
note taped to the tent door:

```
  ▲ Offen seit 3 Tagen · Anna
    CO2-Flasche fast leer                [ Erledigt ]
  ▲ Offen seit 6 Std · Nils
    A3 nicht gießen, zu nass             [ Erledigt ]
```

Created from `+ Notiz` with a `[ Bleibt offen ]` toggle. A plant-scoped condition also surfaces
*inside the capture sheet*: selecting A3 in `Gegossen` shows the condition inline above the Speichern
button. That is the second soft block, and it costs nothing extra to build — same query shape as §7.4.

Closing writes a sentence: "Nils hat 'CO2-Flasche fast leer' nach 3 Tagen erledigt."

### 9.4 Privacy posture

Default is **Privat** and it is not a jurisdiction question the user is asked at onboarding:

- **No real names required.** The owner types whatever labels they want — "Anna", "N", "Schlüssel 2".
- **Harvest weights are off.** `Grow.recordWeights` defaults `false`; the harvest sheet has no weight
  field until it is turned on, in Plan, with the reason stated in one sentence.
- **No cross-user aggregation** of plant counts. No leaderboard, no benchmark, no "growers near you".
- **Exports default to `PSEUDONYMIZED`** — actor names become stable initials ("A.", "N."). The public
  book URL is always pseudonymised, no override. `NONE` exists only on the authenticated Das Buch export.
- **No location, no analytics SDK, no ad SDK.**
- **One-tap wipe** in Plan deletes the grow, its Notes, images and narrative days — actually deletes,
  unlike today's "Logs löschen".

**Refused deliberately, stated so nobody re-proposes it:** per-member dispensing records, member identity
fields (DOB, address, membership number), grams handed over, THC content, § 26 KCanG filing. A grow
controller has no need for them and holding them drags the product into Art. 9 GDPR territory. Terp
Control is the **device-attested environmental layer** a club platform consumes — a value recorded with
the controller's own clock beats a hand-typed number, and it requires storing zero personal data.

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

A fan/light/plug owner opens the app and sees **the same product**, with the parts their hardware cannot
support simply absent. Nothing is broken, nothing is removed, nothing new is demanded of them.

**What they see, precisely:**

- **Tab bar: `Heute · Kurve · Plan`.** No `Film` (no `hardwareInfo.webcam_did`).
- **`Plan` is their existing settings page, unchanged** — `fan-settings`, `light-settings`,
  `plug-settings` still render through the existing `ngSwitch`. Zero redesign; that budget goes to the
  controller.
- **`Heute` is a story built from what their device actually reports** — the generator is fed by
  `VALID_SENSORS` / `VALID_OUTPUTS` and `DeviceLog`, all of which exist for these types:

  *light*: "Das Licht lief von 6:00 bis 18:00, 12 Std. Dimmung 80 %. Keine Störungen."
  *plug*: "Die Steckdose war 7 Std 20 Min an, in 14 Schaltungen."
  *fan*: "Der Lüfter lief zwischen 40 % und 100 %, im Schnitt 62 %. 24,1 °C, 55 % rF."
  offline: "Licht Flur war von 23:04 bis 06:12 offline (7 Std 8 Min)."

- **No Grow, no Plant, no Phase, no VPD, no Futter.** Not hidden — absent, because `Plant.count === 0`
  and the device reports no such sensors. Their action bar is `+ Notiz` alone.
- **What they gain:** notes ("Heizmatte getauscht"), conditions, day/night and setpoint drawing on
  `Kurve`, and Das Buch with export. A plug owner's maintenance history becomes a document.
- **What breaks: nothing.** Every route resolves (`/charts`→`/kurve`, `/diary`→`/buch`,
  `/settings`→`/plan` as `redirectTo`). Every share link and chart preset still applies, because the
  query-param view-state format is extended, not redefined.

**Honest cost:** six templates × four legacy types × two languages = **48 strings**, plus the
light/plug/fan day aggregators. ~3 days, budgeted in §12.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app, reads four sentences, closes it. Weeks later "how was it in
July?" is answered by scrolling, not by configuring a chart. With no sockets paired, no sentence ever
mentions a heater, a dehumidifier or a target the device cannot hit, because the generator only writes
about outputs the device reports. Today's humidity target row they can never act on simply has no
counterpart here.

**2 — Same, but with a camera.** The `Film` tab exists and the timelapse is a *product*, not a tile:
photoperiod-anchored frames, culled, normalised, captioned with the day's numbers, downloaded as an mp4
that plays anywhere. Today's frame sits inside today's paragraph; scrubbing the chart moves it; if the
camera stops, a sentence appears the same day. The concept serves this user best.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Sees the sockets they own and nothing else — roles come
from `hardwareInfo.sockets` and drive both the Ausgänge lane and the sentence set, replacing today's
fails-open three-bucket collapse. Changing the CO₂ target is: tap the number in the sentence, drag,
Speichern — and the change writes itself into the story with their name on it. No settings form.

**4 — Full tent.** Best served by the Ausgänge state-timeline, where thirty-two sockets read as durations
instead of thirty-two 0/1 lines, with every intervention named and attributed. **This user still hits the
firmware wall:** five socket roles, no humidifier, no exhaust role, `out_dehumidifier` doubling as the
cooler. C27 does not fix that; it makes it legible by labelling the lane with the workmode. Honest, and
less than this user wants.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Gets `GET /device/:id/buch.json`: the narrative corpus per day — sentences,
params, provenance windows, raw values — with actuator state included, which no competitor exports.
Publishing that schema makes Terp Control the first named grow-diary JSON in the space. No write API in
v1. Named as a gap, not papered over.

**6 — Grow clubs.** The story names people in its first word, the double-feed block sits at the moment of
capture, the Futter-Konto answers "how much has A3 had, and from whom", and conditions cover the "do not
water A3" note otherwise taped to a door — all without an auth rewrite, and with per-member dispensing
records refused. Attribution here is a label, not a gate, and the UI says so.

**7 — Old device owners.** §10. Three tabs, one action button, a readable maintenance log. Nothing taken
away; the document they gain costs nothing to ignore.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner*: four sentences at a fixed position, one green button, 48 px targets, no
state to remember. *Normal*: a diary that writes itself and a film to show people — the two things AC
Infinity lacks. *Techie*: §4.4.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **Prose is slower to scan than a tile.** "Is my tent OK *right now*?" takes two seconds with a red
   tile and six with a sentence. The header's data-age line and the conditions block mitigate it; they do
   not eliminate it. **If glanceability is the owner's number-one job, this concept loses to a tile
   dashboard and should not win the bake-off.**

2. **German grammar is a minefield, in the primary market's language.** Declension, *über dem Ziel*,
   plural agreement with no ICU in `@ngx-translate` 14, weekday capitalisation, "2 l je Topf" vs "2 Liter
   pro Topf" — every count sentence needs a hand-rolled plural helper. Expect a long tail of slightly
   wrong strings native speakers notice instantly. A German-speaking reviewer for the whole template set
   is not in the 62 days.

3. **English is second-class by construction.** German subclause order does not map onto English, so every
   sentence is authored twice, not translated. Forty templates now, two hundred by v2 — boring work, and
   the first thing cut under schedule pressure, at which point English is *worse* than today.

4. **The document is most interesting when the grow went badly.** A well-tuned 90-day grow collapses to
   ~25 paragraphs of "verlief wie gestern". Day collapsing is the right fix and it also makes the
   flagship artefact thin. The film carries that case; the prose does not.

5. **Editing prose vs. keeping structure in sync is unresolved.** Edit "2 l je Topf" to "3 l" in the text
   and `Note.water.volumeL` does not change — edited days set `editedText` and freeze; the structured
   value is edited through the token instead. Users will edit the text and expect the number to follow.
   It will not. The only clean fix is parsing German back into fields, which is worse.

6. **The paragraph is a snapshot, not a query.** Provenance values are stored so a citation still resolves
   after Influx downsampling — which means regenerating an old day can produce different text than what
   was exported. We keep the first and mark regeneration, but "your diary changed" is a bad sentence to
   have to say.

7. **The film's CPU cost is spiky.** ~312 frames through sharp + ffmpeg per render. `pLimit(10)` exists,
   but a launch week where 150 devices each render a 90-day 1080p film is a real load event on one
   server. Mitigation — nightly pre-render for active grows plus an on-demand queue with a progress row —
   is more infrastructure than the 8 days above admits.

8. **The libx265 fix has a tail.** Every rolling timelapse blob already in the field is HEVC, there is no
   migration tooling, so they age out over up to 30 days. For a month some weekly timelapses will not
   play in some browsers, and the fix will look like a regression.

9. **The token mechanism has never been tested on an impaired user.** "A dotted underline costs nothing
   to ignore" is a design assertion, and the whole no-modes argument rests on it. If it reads as clutter
   in a dark tent at 2 a.m., the honest fallback is a mode — which is banned. **Name the test now:** five
   users, dark room, one hand, phone at arm's length, task = "did anything go wrong yesterday?".

10. **No push notifications exist anywhere in this product.** The camera-silence alarm, the offline
    alarm and the due-feed reminder all arrive by e-mail or not at all. For "the camera stopped three
    days into flower" and for "the stoner wants to be reminded", e-mail is weak, and the owner explicitly
    asked for something that *reminds* people.

11. **Randnotizen build the interpretation UI and then refuse to interpret.** A critic will say we shipped
    an advice surface with no advice, and they will be describing the design accurately. The defence —
    six cited rules a grower can verify beat fifty confident guesses they cannot — is a bet, and a
    competitor with a chatty AI grow coach demos better in ninety seconds.

12. **Type 4 is still not served by the hardware; C27 only makes that visible.** Five socket roles, no
    humidifier, no exhaust role, one PWM output, `out_dehumidifier` meaning two things. Labelling the lie
    beats hiding it, but it is not fixing it, and no cloud-side concept can.
