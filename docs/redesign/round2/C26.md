# C26 — Diktat

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** voice is the primary input; one German sentence grammar runs
in both directions.

---

## 1. Thesis

**You talk to the tent, the tent talks back in the same sentences, and every number in every sentence is
a chip you can open.**

Terp Control's diary fails today because capture happens at the worst possible moment: hands wet, one hand
holding a 10-litre can, phone in a pocket, red headlamp, 40 dB of fan noise, 2 a.m. Every keyboard design
loses to that room. Speech wins it — but only if the app is honest about what speech costs. So Diktat is
built around three commitments. **One:** the microphone is only open while you hold the button; there is no
wake word and no always-listening state. **Two:** speech produces a *Beleg* — a draft slip showing exactly
what was understood, chip by chip — and nothing is written to the record until you tap `Eintragen`. Any slot
the recogniser was unsure about must be *touched* before the Beleg can be committed: silence is never
consent. **Three:** the German grammar that parses your sentence is the same grammar that renders the app.
The home screen headline, the chart's scrubbing header, a photo caption, an alarm — all are sentences
assembled from the same slot templates, and every value inside them is a tappable chip that opens the raw
thing behind it. One grammar, two directions. That is also the answer to the no-modes problem, and it is
described in §4.

---

## 2. Information architecture

Five surfaces per device. Not four tabs and a menu — four tabs, a persistent microphone, and a settings
route reached from the header.

```
/list                          Geräteliste (unchanged; one device → redirect to /device/:id)
/device/:id                    STAND     home. The sentence, the frame, the last Belege, the open Zustände.
/device/:id/buch               BUCH      the Beleg stream. Filters, plants, cumulative totals, export.
/device/:id/verlauf            VERLAUF   the new chart (ECharts). Film strip, setpoint band, scorecard.
/device/:id/film               FILM      timelapse studio. Only present when a camera is paired.
/device/:id/charts             (frozen)  the existing Highcharts page. Kept verbatim for share links.
/device/:id/settings           ZIELE     goals & settings (existing page, renamed, capability-filtered)
/device/:id/pflanzen/:plant_id            one plant. Only reachable when plants exist.
/device/:id/testmode           unchanged (admin)
/account                       + Spracheingabe, Datenschutz, Alles löschen
/shares, /diagnostics, /classes            unchanged
/g/:slug                       public read-only diary page, no login (v1.1)
```

**Navigation model.** On phones: an `ion-tab-bar` with the tabs the device actually has, and the mic docked
72 px above it, bottom-centre. On tablet/desktop the existing `ion-split-pane` menu stays and gains the same
entries; the mic moves to the header. The tab list is **built from capability, not from a preference**:
a controller with a camera gets `Stand · Buch · Verlauf · Film`; everything else gets the first three.
`Film` is absent, not greyed, when `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is unset. `Pflanzen` is not a tab at all — it
appears as a section inside Buch the moment the first `Pflanze` exists, and never before.

**Share/demo/public modes.** Every new screen consumes `resolveDeviceAccessInfo` exactly as today. In a
share session the mic is **not rendered** (`canEdit === false`), Buch is read-only, and `Film` renders but
the render button is disabled with `Zum Erstellen eines Films wird ein eigenes Konto gebraucht.` In demo,
the mic is rendered and works, but `Eintragen` shows `Im Demo-Modus wird nichts gespeichert.`

**The headline sentence is generated, not templated per case.** Deviations are computed against the active
target (`day.*`/`night.*` from `device.configuration`, plus the stage `vpdRange` from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS`),
each classified as one of five invariant predicates: `zu warm`, `zu kalt`, `zu feucht`, `zu trocken`,
`zu dunkel`. **German predicative adjectives are uninflected**, which is why this set was chosen — there is
no gender/case agreement to get wrong, so the generator is a join, not a grammar engine.

| deviations | Satz |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | `Alles im Rahmen.` |
| 1 | `Es ist zu warm.` |
| 2 | `Es ist zu warm und zu trocken.` |
| ≥3 | `Drei Werte liegen daneben.` (chips carry which) |
| an alarm is triggered | `Alarm: zu warm seit 40 Minuten.` (red, replaces the line) |

**Chips.** Every value is `<span class="tc-chip" role="button">`. Tapping `27,8 °C` slides up a sheet with:
the last 24 h of that one series (ECharts, one panel, setpoint band drawn), the current setpoint with the
`value-edit-row` control if the device can act on it, and `Alarm setzen`. Tapping `Tag 34` opens the cycle.
Tapping `Anna` opens the Akteur picker. **Nothing is hidden behind a "more" control; the number is on the
surface and the raw series is one tap under it.**

**States, with exact copy:**

- **Fresh (< 2 min):** `Gemessen vor 40 Sekunden.` Grey.
- **Stale (2–10 min):** `Gemessen vor 6 Minuten.` Amber text, amber dot in the header.
- **Offline (> 10 min):** header dot grey, label `offline`. The sentence changes tense and the freshness
  line becomes the headline's subject:
  `Das Zelt meldet sich seit 2 Stunden nicht.` / `Zuletzt: 27,8 °C · 41 % rF — um 07:14.`
  Chips stay tappable (history still works) but the setpoint editor inside them shows
  `Änderungen erreichen das Gerät erst, wenn es wieder online ist.`
- **Never online:** `Noch keine Messwerte. Das Gerät war noch nie online.` + a link to the setup wizard.
- **No CO₂ upgrade:** the `ppm` chip is simply absent. No greyed placeholder, no upsell in the sentence.
- **No camera:** the frame block is absent. No empty box.
- **Camera silent > 90 min while device online:** the frame stays but is dimmed to 40 % with an overlay line
  `Letztes Bild vor 3 Stunden.` and a `Warum?` link to the camera diagnostics. This is also an alarm (§8).
- **No Belege yet:** the "Zuletzt eingetragen" block is replaced by one line:
  `Noch nichts eingetragen. Halte das Mikrofon und sag, was du gemacht hast.`
- **No open Zustände:** the block is absent entirely.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — der Satz ist die Oberfläche

**The mechanism: every fact in the product is rendered as one German sentence in which every value is an
inline chip, and the depth of what you see is set by what you say and what you tap — never by who you
declared yourself to be.**

Three things follow, and together they cover the stoner, the normal user and the techie with one surface.

**(a) Reading: verdict and number occupy the same line.** `Es ist zu warm und zu trocken.` and
`27,8 °C · 41 % rF · VPD 1,9 kPa` are two lines of one paragraph, always both rendered. The impaired user
reads line one and stops. The normal user reads both. The techie taps `27,8 °C`, gets the 24 h series with
min/max envelope and the setpoint band, taps again into `Rohdaten` and gets the raw table with an
`Als CSV` button. Nobody was offered a toggle; nobody was denied a number. This is the brief's *progressive
precision* taken literally — the verdict, the number and the series are three depths of the **same
sentence**, not three screens.

**(b) Writing: the input sets the output density.** The Beleg has exactly as many chips as you gave facts.

- `„gegossen"` → one chip (`Gießen`), one tap to commit.
- `„A1 bis A3 mit zwei Liter gefüttert, Bio-Bloom, EC eins vier, Ablauf eins acht"` → seven chips,
  still one tap to commit.

There is no short form and no long form. The club member who dictates EC, runoff EC, pH and volume gets a
dense record because he **said** dense; the stoner who says one word gets a one-word record. The `+` row
under every Beleg offers the fields you did not mention — `+ EC`, `+ pH`, `+ Ablauf`, `+ Foto`, `+ Höhe` —
**ordered by how often you personally have filled them**, so the techie's `+` row is EC/pH/Ablauf and the
casual grower's is Foto/Notiz. That ordering is adaptive, but it is adaptive *within a row of optional
extras that are all always present* — the brief's warning about unpredictability hurting the impaired user
does not bite, because nothing the impaired user relies on ever moves. The mic, the sentence, the
`Eintragen` button and the tabs are in fixed positions forever.

**(c) The same renderer everywhere.** `SatzService.render(beleg | zustand | messwerte | alarm)` returns
`{ text: string, chips: Chip[] }` and one dumb component draws it. The home headline, a Beleg in Buch, the
chart's scrubbing header, a photo caption, the burn-in strip on an exported film and an alarm e-mail all go
through it. There is one visual vocabulary in the product, so learning the home screen teaches you the
chart.

**Why this is not a mode under another name.** A mode is a persisted user-scoped switch that changes which
features exist. Here nothing is persisted about the *user*, nothing is hidden by preference, and every
control is reachable from every state by the same gesture (tap the value). The only things that vary are
(i) capability — you see a `ppm` chip if you bought the CO₂ sensor, and (ii) data — you see a plant chip if
you have plants. Both are explicitly sanctioned mechanisms.

**Where a critic will push, and the honest answer.** Buch has a `Tabelle` toggle next to `Liste`. That looks
like a mode. It is not: both views render **the same query, the same rows and every field** — it is a sort
control's sibling, stored per-view in the URL, not per-user in a preference, and no feature exists in one
and not the other. I would rather defend this than pretend a club treasurer will reconcile 40 feeds by
reading 40 sentences.

## 5. The chart — VERLAUF

New route, new library. Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, with a ~50-line hand-rolled Angular directive (no
`ngx-echarts`). The existing `/charts` page stays on Highcharts, **untouched and frozen**, so every share
link and `ChartPreset` in circulation keeps working. Two chart libraries ship in v1; `/charts` is deleted in
v1.1 once presets are migrated. That is a deliberate, costed trade (§12).

**Layout — stacked small multiples, one shared x-axis, one linked crosshair.**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Am 12. März um 03:20 waren es 18,2 °C bei 62 % rF.          │  ← the scrubbing header
│  VPD 0,9 kPa. Licht aus. Heizung an.                         │     (SatzService, same renderer)
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Temperatur °C   ░░░░ night ░░░░        ░░░░ night ░░░░       │
│  30 ─                                                        │
│  25 ─  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄ Ziel (gestuft, gestrichelt) ┄┄┄┄┄┄         │
│  20 ─  ▁▂▃▄▅▄▃▂▁ ist (durchgezogen, min/max-Hülle)          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ rF %            (same grid, same shading)                    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ VPD kPa         band moves with the lifecycle stage          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Ausgänge  ▓▓▓▓ Licht ▓▓▓▓    ▓ Heizung ▓  ▓▓ Entf. ▓▓        │ ← state-timeline lane
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Belege    ●  ● ●        ●(3)      ●          ▌Blüte          │ ← annotation rail, max 2 rows
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Film      [▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪]           │ ← thumbnails ON the time axis
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  86 % der Zeit im Zielbereich. Längste Abweichung 3 Std 40.  │
│  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░▓▓  Tag / Nacht getrennt →  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Goal vs actual.** The setpoint is a **dashed stepped line** (it steps at `daynight.day` and
`daynight.night` between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`) with a soft `markArea` band behind it.
The actual is a solid line with a `min/max` envelope (`aggregateWindow` already supports both; one extra
query each — a single `mean` line is a lie about a bang-bang controller). Between them, a signed deviation
fill. Band source is chosen one at a time and **labelled in the panel header** so it is never ambiguous:
`Alarmgrenzen` > `Ziel ± Toleranz` > `VPD-Empfehlung (Blüte)`.

**Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0` where available, falling back to the configured schedule, in an
explicit timezone shown in the panel footer (`Europe/Berlin`). A failed contactor then shows as a *missing
band* — a diagnostic, not decoration.

**Annotations.** A rail *under* the axis, not columns in the plot. Belege cluster by pixel proximity into
8 px bins with a count badge; lifecycle changes draw a full-height dashed line with a label. Tapping a mark
opens the Beleg card. Category chips filter the rail (`collectLogCategories` already exists).

**Outputs** in a state-timeline lane, region length = duration, null = gap.

**Scorecard.** Time-in-range computed **server-side on raw Influx samples** — never on `aggregateWindow(mean)`
output, which destroys the excursions the metric exists to count. Rendered as a sentence plus one stacked
bar, split day/night, with data coverage stated and the whole block greyed below 80 % coverage:
`Nur 62 % der Zeit gemessen — die Bewertung ist unsicher.`

**Mobile rules.** One finger scrolls the page; two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`); tap or long-press
sets the crosshair. **The floating tooltip is gone** — the scrubbing header replaces it, because a fingertip
occludes a tooltip and there is no hover on touch. Axis labels are never hidden; below 320 px they drop to
three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.

**Before the migration lands**, the existing `/charts` page receives three surgical Highcharts additions in
v1 so nobody waits: `plotBands` for day/night, a dashed stepped setpoint series, and the film-strip
component below the chart. ~3 days, no library risk.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

Everything a person does is a **Beleg**. One new collection replaces the `DeviceLog`-with-a-category-prefix
convention for user-authored facts. `DeviceLog` stays exactly as it is for device events — this is additive,
nothing is migrated destructively, and the existing `LogEntryViewerModule` still renders device logs.

```ts
// collection: belege
interface Beleg {
  _id: string;                    // CLIENT-generated UUIDv4, at draft time → idempotent upsert
  device_id: string;
  art: 'giessen' | 'duengen' | 'messen' | 'beobachtung' | 'training'
     | 'phase' | 'problem' | 'foto' | 'co2' | 'korrektur';
  time: number;                   // event time (may be back-dated by "gestern")
  createdAt: number;              // when it was written — never equal to time for back-dated entries
  actor_id?: string;              // Akteur._id
  plant_ids?: string[];           // [] === "das ganze Zelt"
  satz: string;                   // the rendered German sentence, STORED, so it reads the same in 3 years
  transcript?: string;            // verbatim ASR output, stored alongside
  quelle: 'diktat' | 'tippen' | 'plan' | 'geraet';
  slots: {
    menge_ml?: number;            // ALWAYS canonical ml
    duenger?: { name: string; ml_pro_l?: number; menge_ml?: number }[];
    ec?: number;                  // ALWAYS mS/cm
    ec_ort?: 'zulauf' | 'ablauf' | 'reservoir';
    ph?: number;
    ppm?: number; ppm_skala?: 500 | 640 | 700;   // stored as said; ec is the canonical value
    hoehe_cm?: number;
    phase?: DiaryLifecycleStage;
    text?: string;
    umgebung?: { t?: number; rh?: number; vpd?: number; co2?: number };  // stamped at write time
  };
  images?: string[];
  override_of?: string;           // set when the double-feed guard was knowingly overridden
  korrigiert_durch?: string;      // beleg_id of a later Korrektur
  geprueft?: boolean;             // true once a low-confidence chip was touched
  deleted?: boolean;
}

// collection: pflanzen
interface Pflanze {
  _id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                  // "A1" — short, spoken
  name?: string;                  // "Gorilla Glue"
  sorte?: string;
  medium?: string;                // "Biobizz All-Mix"
  topf_l?: number;
  angelegt: number; beendet?: number;
  aliase?: string[];              // ASR aliases LEARNED from corrections: ["a eins","8 eins","ah eins"]
}

// collection: akteure  (device-scoped, no accounts)
interface Akteur { _id: string; device_id: string; name: string; user_id?: string;
                   aliase?: string[]; aktiv: boolean; }

// collection: zustaende  (standing conditions — the thing pinned to the tent door)
interface Zustand { _id: string; device_id: string; text: string; plant_ids?: string[];
                    geoeffnet: number; geoeffnet_von?: string;
                    geschlossen?: number; geschlossen_von?: string;
                    sperrt?: 'giessen' | 'duengen'; }

// collection: regime  (medium + feed regime, the thing selected once)
interface Regime { _id: string; device_id: string; medium: string; linie: string;
                   staerke: 'leicht'|'normal'|'kräftig'; start: number; schema_id?: string; }

// collection: feed_schemata  (hot-swappable CONTENT, never in the webapp bundle)
interface FeedSchema {
  _id: string; medium: string; linie: string; ec_basis: 'absolut' | 'delta_zur_quelle';
  schritte: { ab_tag: number; phase: string;
              produkte: { name: string; ml_pro_l: [number, number] }[]; hinweis?: string }[];
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; chart_version?: string;
  kind: 'official_pdf' | 'official_calculator' | 'manual_transcription';
}
```

All fields optional except the identity ones; read-time normalisers, no migration tooling needed, matching
house style. `belege` is indexed `{device_id, time: -1}` and `{device_id, 'plant_ids', time: -1}` and the
list endpoint is **cursor-paginated from day one** — the Entries report's whole-history fetch is exactly the
bug not to repeat.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed.** If `pflanzen` for the device is empty:
- the parser never runs the plant slot;
- the Beleg has no plant row;
- Buch has no plant filter and no `Pflanzen` section;
- the double-feed guard falls back to device scope.

Plants appear the first time you *say one*. If you dictate `„A1 gegossen"` with no plants defined, the Beleg
shows the chip `A1` with a hint underneath: `Neue Pflanze „A1" anlegen?` — one tap creates it, one tap
dismisses it and the Beleg commits as a tent-wide entry. **Reveal by data, and speech is the trigger.**

**Corrections.** A Beleg is freely editable for 24 h by its author. After 24 h an edit writes a
`art: 'korrektur'` Beleg carrying the new values and `override_of` pointing back — append-only with
compensating corrections, which is what a shared record needs and what a solo grower never notices, because
Buch renders the corrected value with a small `korrigiert` marker and the original one tap under it.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — the capture flow, with tap counts

### 7.1 The voice path

Press-and-hold the mic. Speak. Release. The Beleg appears in ~600 ms (interim results stream during the
hold, so you see it forming).

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Entwurf                                 [✕]  │
│                                               │
│   Du hast  A1 A2 A3  mit  2 l  gegossen.      │  ← chips underlined
│                                               │
│   Jetzt · 09:12 · Du                          │
│                                               │
│   „gegossen zwei liter a1 bis a3"             │  ← verbatim transcript, 12 px, grey
│                                               │
│   + EC    + pH    + Ablauf    + Foto          │
│                                               │
│   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
│   │              Eintragen                  │ │
│   └─────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
│              Nochmal sprechen                 │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Scenario | Interactions |
| --- | --- |
| `„gegossen"`, no plants defined | **hold + 1 tap** (`Eintragen`) |
| `„A1 bis A3 zwei Liter gegossen"` | **hold + 1 tap** |
| same, but `A3` came back low-confidence | **hold + 2 taps** (touch the chip, then `Eintragen`) |
| full feed with EC, pH, runoff | **hold + 1 tap** |
| a double feed is detected | **hold + 2 taps** (`Trotzdem eintragen`, then it commits) |

### 7.2 The keyboard path (no ASR, or a cellar with no signal)

The same composer, driven by chips built from *your own history*. No typing is required for the common case.

1. Tap mic → sheet: `Kein Netz — Spracherkennung geht gerade nicht.` `[ Tippen ]`
2. Tap `Gießen` (one of six large tiles, ordered by your frequency)
3. Tap `2 l` (three most-used amounts as chips, plus `Andere…`)
4. Tap `Wie letztes Mal — A1, A2, A3`
5. Tap `Eintragen`

**5 taps, no keyboard.** Typing only appears for free text and for an amount not in your top three.

### 7.3 The scheduled path — "it thinks for me"

When a `Regime` is active and a step is due, the Stand card reads:

> **Heute fällig** — Fütterung: Bio-Grow 2 ml/l, Bio-Bloom 2 ml/l  `[ Erledigt ]` `[ Später ]`

`Erledigt` = **1 tap** and writes a fully-populated Beleg with `quelle: 'plan'`, every prefilled number
present as an editable chip. Saying `„erledigt"` or `„gemacht"` into the mic does the same. `Später` pushes
the due marker 24 h and writes nothing.

**Schedule following.** The `Regime` advances **by feed event** (`Schritt 6 von 14`), not by wall-clock
weeks, because growers feed Tue/Fri and slip. A soft calendar mapping shows the expected date and the drift
(`3 Tage hinter Plan`). The 18/6 → 12/12 flip is auto-detected from `out_light` history and offered, never
applied silently:

> `Dein Licht läuft seit 3 Tagen 12/12. Blüte ab dem 12. März?`  `[ Ja ]` `[ Nein ]`

**Medium + regime, said the way growers say it.** The selectable thing is a pair, not a nutrient line:
`Biobizz All-Mix` + `Biobizz Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom` + `normal`. All-Mix is heavily pre-fertilised, so its
schema's first steps are literally `produkte: []` with
`hinweis: "All-Mix ist vorgedüngt — die ersten Wochen nur gießen."` If you dictate a feed inside that
window, the Beleg shows a **soft note, never a block**:
`All-Mix ist vorgedüngt. Sicher?` `[ Trotzdem eintragen ]`. BioTabs' "schedule" is a re-tab reminder every
5 weeks with almost no liquid feeding — the same schema shape expresses it.

**What is stored.** Volume in canonical `menge_ml`. EC in canonical mS/cm with `ec_ort` (`zulauf` /
`ablauf` / `reservoir`) — because "EC 1,8" without a location is a number nobody can use later. A spoken
`ppm` is stored as `ppm` + `ppm_skala` (asked once per device: `Welche Skala zeigt dein Messgerät? 500 / 640 /
700`) and converted to canonical EC. **A bare "ppm" is never rendered anywhere.** Feed products are stored as
plain-text names plus `ml_pro_l`; brand names appear as text labels only, never as logos, colours or feature
names, and every number comes from a Mongo content collection carrying `source_url` + `retrieved_at`, so a
brand can be removed in one DB update. Buch surfaces `Zuletzt geprüft: 14.03.2026` next to a schema — the
legal posture doubles as a trust feature.

### 7.4 The double-feed guard

Runs at commit time, before the write. Window: 6 h for `giessen`, 18 h for `duengen`, both device-configurable
as one number (`Doppelt-Schutz`). Scope: overlapping `plant_ids`, or device-wide when no plants exist.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Schon gefüttert                              │
│                                               │
│  Anna hat A1 bis A3 vor 1 Std 30 mit 2 l      │
│  Bio-Bloom gefüttert.                         │
│                                               │
│   [ Trotzdem eintragen ]                      │
│   [ Abbrechen ]                               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

`Trotzdem eintragen` stamps `override_of` so the record shows a *knowing* second feed, not an ambiguous
duplicate. A `Zustand` with `sperrt: 'giessen'` on plant A3 produces the same sheet with different copy:
`A3 ist als überwässert markiert — Ben, seit gestern.`

**Offline double-logging is structurally impossible.** The `_id` is a UUIDv4 generated when the *draft* is
created, and the server endpoint is an upsert keyed on it. Replaying the queue N times writes once.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is the reason someone buys the €319 bundle rather than the €289 one. It gets a tab.

### 8.1 What exists today, verified

`server/src/services/image.service.ts`: stills every 30 s, stored as Mongo `Buffer`s; retention 3 years;
thinning tiers to 1/min after a day, 1/5 min after a week, 1/15 min after a month, 1/hour after 90 days.
Three rolling timelapses rebuilt hourly (`1d`/`1w`/`1m`) by taking **one frame every N ms and nothing else**
— `compressRtspStreamRange` has no culling and no normalisation. Render is
`ffmpeg -framerate 25 -vcodec libx265 -crf 30`, result written back into Mongo as an `Image` with
`format:'mp4'`. **ffmpeg and `sharp` are already server dependencies** — the pipeline below adds no new
runtime.

Two findings that matter and are not in the brief:

1. **The output is h265.** HEVC does not play reliably in Chrome on Android or desktop, so a file the user
   downloads today may not play for the person they send it to. **Every exported film must be
   `libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart`.** The in-app rolling previews may stay h265.
2. **The unique index is `{device_id, format, timestamp, duration}`** and `duration` is an enum of
   `'1d'|'1w'|'1m'`. A user film needs a fourth value and a `timestamp` that cannot collide.

### 8.2 What ships in v1 — "Der Film"

A per-cycle, per-phase or per-range timelapse that is **culled, normalised, captioned, rendered to h264, and
leaves the product as a file**.

**Per-frame features, computed once at ingest** (one `sharp` resize to 64×64 + `stats()`, ~4 ms/frame; at
2,880 frames/day × 150 devices that is ~30 min of CPU per day across the fleet):

```ts
// collection: bildmerkmale
interface Bildmerkmale {
  image_id: string; device_id: string; timestamp: number;
  luma: number;                 // mean 0..255
  kanaele: [number, number, number];   // mean R,G,B → white balance
  schaerfe: number;             // variance of Laplacian on the 64×64 grey
  diff: number;                 // mean abs diff vs previous KEPT frame
  hash: string;                 // 64-bit dHash
  licht_an: boolean;            // out_light at that timestamp
  verdict: 'ok' | 'dunkel' | 'unscharf' | 'mensch' | 'doppelt' | 'quote';
}
```

**The cull, in order.** Every rule is reversible from the UI — `Film → Verworfene Bilder (412)` lists them.

1. **Lights off** — `licht_an === false` → drop. (Exception: a device in `workmode: 'dry'` has no
   photoperiod, so rule 2 alone applies.)
2. **Blown or black** — `luma < 25` or `luma > 245` → drop. Catches the sunrise/sunset ramp seconds and the
   door-open blowout.
3. **Blurry** — `schaerfe` below 40 % of the device's rolling 7-day median → drop. Catches condensation and
   a camera in motion.
4. **A human is in shot** — `diff > 12 %` of the frame **and** the frame after it reverts to within 4 % of
   the frame before it. A person is a large, short-lived change; growth is a small permanent one. A two-sided
   test over a 3-frame window. **No ML, no face detection, no person model** — that is a deliberate choice
   for a cannabis product. Honest limits: it catches most hands and open doors, it misses somebody standing
   still, and it will occasionally discard a real event (a plant knocked over). That is why rule 7 exists.
5. **Near-duplicate** — dHash Hamming distance ≤ 3 from the last kept frame → drop.
6. **Quota** — from the survivors, keep **one frame per day per target clock time** (default: mid-photoperiod,
   ±20 min tolerance), or one per hour for short films. This is the single biggest readability win: the light
   is in the same place in every frame, so the plant appears to grow instead of the room appearing to flicker.
7. **Pinned** — anything the user pinned (`+ Foto`, a diary photo, `„mach ein Foto"`) is always kept and
   always in the film, quota or not.

**Normalisation across 12 weeks**, applied with `sharp` into a temp dir before ffmpeg:

- **Exposure:** per-frame linear gain to a rolling **7-day centred median** of `luma`, so one dark day does
  not make the film jump. Clamped to ×0.6…×1.6.
- **White balance:** grey-world scaling of R and B to a reference taken from the first seven kept days.
  This is what kills the magenta-LED vs daylight-through-an-open-door jump. Clamped to ±35 % so a genuinely
  purple frame is not destroyed.
- **Stabilisation:** translation only. Phase correlation on a 128×128 downsample against the previous kept
  frame, accumulated, then a global 4 % crop absorbs the shifts. **Rotation is explicitly out of scope for
  v1.** If accumulated drift exceeds 8 % of frame width, the pipeline stops accumulating, records a `Bruch`,
  and the film gets a hard cut with a one-frame black gap. A camera nudged 5° on day 12 produces a visible
  cut, not a smeared mess — and the studio says so: `Kamera am 12. März verschoben — der Film schneidet hier.`

**Burn-in, default ON, one toggle to turn off.** A 64 px bottom strip drawn by ffmpeg `drawtext` from a
timed text file, one line per frame, produced by `SatzService`:

```
Tag 34 · 12. März · 26,8 °C · 58 % rF · VPD 1,1 kPa
```

**This is image correlation made physical**: the numbers travel with the video after it leaves the product.
**No Terp Control watermark by default** — a branded footer on thousands of German cannabis posts is the
single riskiest string in the product under § 6 KCanG. It is an opt-in toggle, off, with one line of copy.

### 8.4 How the video LEAVES the product

`POST /device/:id/film` → `{ job_id }`. A Mongo-backed job document plus a 5 s poller (matching the
codebase's style; no new queue library). One job per device at a time, reusing the existing
`pLimit(10)` ffmpeg gate.

**Storage: on disk, not in Mongo.** A 12-week 1080p h264 CRF 23 film is 8–25 MB; the BSON document limit is
16 MB and `express-fileupload` already has no size guard. Films go to
`/var/terp/films/<device_id>/<film_id>.mp4`, served by a streaming range endpoint. **This needs one new
volume in `docker-compose.yaml` — flag it as infrastructure work.** GridFS is the fallback if a deployment
cannot take a volume. A film is deleted 30 days after creation unless pinned; the studio says so.

**Download path, three branches, in this order:**

1. `navigator.share({ files: [new File([blob], name, {type:'video/mp4'})] })` — supported on iOS 15+ Safari
   and Android Chrome. This is the only path that reliably reaches an iPhone camera roll or a chat app.
2. `<a download>` on a blob URL — Android Chrome, desktop.
3. A plain link to `GET /film/:film_id?token=<imageToken>` with
   `Content-Disposition: attachment` — desktop and everything else.

**Filename**, deterministic, no strain and no plant name (privacy by default):

```
terpcontrol_zelt-1_20260112-20260308.mp4
```

`terpcontrol_<gerätename-slug>_<von:YYYYMMDD>-<bis:YYYYMMDD>.mp4`

**Format:** MP4, `libx264`, `-crf 23 -preset veryfast -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart`, no audio
track, 12 fps default (6 / 12 / 24 selectable), source resolution capped at 1080p.

**And the path that actually matters on a phone:** if the user has a Telegram destination configured, the
finished film is sent there as a document — Telegram bots accept 50 MB, and the German Terp Control
community already lives on Telegram. Discord webhooks (20 MB free tier) and a generic webhook are the other
two destinations. All three reuse `server/src/utils/webhookTemplate.ts`; what is missing is only a non-alarm
trigger and multipart attachment. The completion notice is also a Beleg on Stand:
`Dein Film ist fertig. 34 Bilder, 0:03 min.` `[ Speichern ]` `[ An Telegram ]`

### 8.5 Image correlation

- **The film strip is an axis.** Thumbnails positioned by real timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so uneven
  capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivered as **one sprite sheet + a JSON index per window** (256×160 tiles,
  ≤ 100 per sprite), not N image requests. Scrubbing the chart moves the frame; tapping a frame sets the
  chart cursor.
- **Every frame has a sentence.** `SatzService` joins `Bildmerkmale.timestamp` to the Influx series:
  `12. März, 14:02 · Tag 34 · 26,8 °C · 58 % rF · Licht an, 80 %.`
- **Every Beleg with a photo carries its conditions**, denormalised into `slots.umgebung` at write time — so
  a three-year-old photo still knows what the tent was doing after telemetry has been thinned.
- **Vorher/Nachher.** Two frames at the same clock time N days apart, side by side, with the deltas as a
  sentence. Cheap, and it is the thing people actually post.
- **The camera alarm nobody gave it.** A real `Alarm` with `sensorType: 'kamera_alter'`, threshold in
  minutes, default 90. `alarm.service.ts` already evaluates alarms in `onDataReceived` on every MQTT status
  message; the value is `(now − newest frame timestamp) / 60000`, read from a Map that `image.service`
  updates when it stores a frame. That reuses e-mail, webhook, cooldown, retrigger and maintenance-mode
  gating for free — roughly half a day of work, not a week. Copy:
  `Die Kamera hat seit 2 Stunden kein Bild geliefert.`

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution in v1, authorisation never pretended.**

**Akteure.** The owner types names once in Ziele (`Anna`, `Ben`, `Tim`). At the tent, the header shows
`[Anna ▾]`; one tap changes it. The selection sticks in `localStorage` per device for 12 h and then reverts
to `[Wer? ▾]`, because the phone at the tent may be shared and a stale actor is worse than none. Speech
overrides it per Beleg: `„Anna hat A1 gegossen"` stamps Anna without changing the picker.

**The honesty line, shown once and again in Ziele:**

> `Namen sind Notizen, keine Anmeldung. Jeder, der Zugriff auf dieses Gerät hat, kann jeden Namen wählen.`

Attribution is ~2 days of work and delivers most of the club value. **Authorisation** — real memberships —
requires a membership collection plus rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207` plus loosening ~10
owner-scoped queries. That is v2 and is stated as v2. `Akteur.user_id` is the forward hook: when real
memberships land, an Akteur that already maps to a user becomes a member with no data migration.

**The double-feed problem** — §7.4. It is the most expensive thing that happens in a shared room and it is
solved at the moment of capture with one named person, one relative time, one deliberate extra tap, and an
`override_of` stamp so the record distinguishes a decision from a duplicate.

**Conditions.** `Zustand` is an open fact with an owner, not an event and not a task. Created by voice
(`„merken: CO2-Flasche fast leer"`, `„achtung: A3 nicht gießen, überwässert"`) or by a card on Stand. It
appears in the **Offen** block on Stand until somebody closes it, and it is stamped with who opened it and
when. A `Zustand` with `sperrt` turns into a soft block at capture. This is what actually gets pinned to a
tent door, and no concept in round one had a place for it.

**The cumulative question, answered.** `/device/:id/pflanzen/:plant_id`:

> **A3 · Gorilla Glue · Blüte, Tag 34**
> `In diesem Lauf: 14,2 l Wasser, 38 ml Bio-Bloom, 26 ml Bio-Grow.`
> `Zuletzt gefüttert vor 2 Tagen. Anna 8×, Ben 3×, du 2×.`
> `EC zuletzt 1,4 (Zulauf) / 1,8 (Ablauf) — +29 %.`

One aggregation over `belege`. Every one of those numbers is a chip; tapping `38 ml Bio-Bloom` lists the
13 Belege behind it with dates and actors. That is "very detailed logging and reporting" for the club and
invisible weight for everyone else.

**Privacy posture.**

- Default **private**. No club accounts, no member identity, no DOB, no address, no membership number.
- **Refused outright**, and stated in the doc so nobody re-proposes it: per-member dispensing or consumption
  records (§ 26 Abs. 1 Nr. 5/6 KCanG). That is arguably Art. 9 GDPR health data for facts a grow controller
  has no need for. Terp Control does not become the club's system of record and never ships a
  `KCanG-konform` badge.
- **Harvest weight is off by default.** A `Beleg` with `art:'phase'`, `phase: 'drying'` never asks for grams.
  `+ Gewicht` exists on the `+` row for those who go looking, gated behind one account-level switch
  `Erntegewichte erfassen: Aus`.
- **Export defaults to anonymised.** `Buch → Exportieren` offers `Ohne Namen (Standard)` / `Pseudonyme
  (Person 1…n)` / `Mit Namen`, as CSV and as JSON. The CSV includes actuator state — no vendor in the market
  provides that.
- **One-tap wipe.** `Ziele → Alles löschen` removes `belege`, `pflanzen`, `zustaende`, `bilder`, films and
  the device's Influx series. This is the feature that matters in a prohibition jurisdiction and the reason
  the audit trail is *not* immutable by default.
- Transcripts are stored; **audio never is**. §11 covers the recogniser's own privacy cost, which is real.

## 10. Old devices (device_type 7: fan, light, plug — and fridge)

Concretely, what a `plug` owner sees the morning after the update:

- **Their settings page is byte-identical.** `plug-settings`, `fan-settings`, `light-settings` are not
  touched. The `ngSwitch` in `settings.page.html` is untouched. That is the "must not be broken" promise, and
  it is kept by not editing those files.
- **They get the new Stand**, because the sentence renderer works from whatever series the device reports.
  A plug reporting only temperature: `24,1 °C.` / `Steckdose an seit 06:00.` / `Gemessen vor 20 Sekunden.`
  A fan: `26,0 °C und 55 % rF.` / `Lüfter auf 60 %.` A light: `Licht an seit 06:00, 80 %.`
  If a device has no configured setpoint for a measure, the sentence carries the value with **no verdict** —
  it says `24,1 °C.` and not `Es ist zu warm.`, because there is nothing to be warm relative to.
- **They get Diktat and Belege.** A plug owner absolutely wants to record `„Filter gewechselt"` and
  `„Lüfter läuft laut"`. Belege are device-scoped, not controller-scoped, so this costs nothing extra and is
  a pure gain for a group that has had no diary at all.
- **They get the freshness line and the offline sentence.** This is the #1 complaint across every consumer
  brand and old devices benefit from it first.
- **Tabs:** `Stand · Buch · Verlauf`. No `Film` (no `webcam_did`). No plant section until they create a
  plant — which they may, and it works.
- **They do not get:** the setpoint band on the chart where their type has no setpoint (the panel draws the
  actual alone and says so in the header: `kein Ziel hinterlegt`); the Regime picker (hidden until a plant
  exists); the stage picker.
- **The fridge keeps being a fridge**, with its own labels. The *controller* stops being called
  `Terp Control FRIDGE GROW`: v1 adds a `devices.controller` block to `en.json` and `de.json` and fixes
  `overview.component.html:40` and `overview.component.ts:91-94`. That is the cheapest credibility fix

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens Stand, reads one sentence, closes the app. `Alles im Rahmen. 24,8 °C ·
58 % rF. Gemessen vor 30 Sekunden.` The tent has no sockets paired, so the sentence never contains a verdict
about a thing he cannot control and Ziele shows no dehumidifier row. Weeks later he opens Verlauf and gets
`86 % der Zeit im Zielbereich` with the band drawn. He never touches the mic and never sees a plant.

**2 — Same, but with a camera.** His tab bar has `Film`. He sets `Zeitraum: Ganzer Lauf`, `Ein Bild pro Tag`,
`Werte einblenden`, taps `Film erstellen`, waits ~90 s, and gets an 84-frame h264 file with the temperature
burnt into the bottom strip, delivered to his Telegram. He also scrubs the chart and watches the plant move
with it. This is the type Diktat serves best, and the timelapse is a thing he can hold in October 2026, not
in v2.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** He has a heater socket and no dehumidifier. Today the app
shows him a humidity target, a humidity deviation warning and a dehumidifier alarm preset he can never act
on. Under Diktat the sentence can still say `zu feucht` — that is a *fact* — but the humidity chip's sheet
opens with `Dieses Gerät kann die Luftfeuchte nicht senken.` and offers only an alarm, not a setpoint. The
verdict is honest; the control offer is capability-gated.

**4 — Full tent.** He gets the most out of the chart: five panels, the output state-timeline showing heater
and dehumidifier fighting, the setpoint band, the day/night shading. Diktat is where he records the things
the controller cannot see — `„Abluft auf Stufe drei gestellt"`, `„Entfeuchter Behälter geleert"` — which is
exactly the data the fused timeline is missing today. **Honest limit: this concept does not add socket
roles.** There is still no humidifier, no exhaust fan and no AC role in firmware, and Diktat does not pretend
otherwise; it turns those into logged human actions, which is the only cloud-side answer available before
October.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** The least well served, and I will not dress it up. He gets one real thing:
`belege` is a documented REST resource with a stable schema and a JSON export, so his EC probe and soil
sensor can `POST` a `Beleg` with `quelle: 'geraet'` and it appears on the chart's annotation rail next to
everything else. That is a genuine write API for the one thing the firmware cannot carry. He still has no
write path to outputs and no custom `VALID_SENSORS` entry, and both are out of scope here.

**6 — Grow club.** The type this concept was built around. Three people, one tent, one controller, no
accounts. Akteur picker at the tent, voice capture with wet hands, the double-feed guard naming Anna and the
time, `Zustand` for the CO₂ bottle and the overwatered A3, and the per-plant cumulative page that answers
"how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had and from whom". Export defaults to anonymised. What they do not get is
authorisation — anyone at the tent can pick any name — and that is said out loud rather than implied.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Nothing of theirs breaks, they gain a home screen that tells them how old the
reading is, and they gain a diary they never had. The one thing they lose is nothing.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The concept dies in Firefox and stumbles on iOS PWA.** Firefox does not implement
   `SpeechRecognition` (`media.webspeech.recognition.enable` is false by default). iOS Safari has it from
   14.5, but microphone permission in home-screen standalone mode has a history of breaking between
   releases. A voice-first product resting on a browser API that one major engine refuses is a genuine
   structural bet. Mitigation is the keyboard composer, which is why it is v1 and not v1.1 — but a fallback
   used by 30 % of users is not a fallback, it is the product for those users.
2. **A tent is a loud room.** Fans at 60 %, an inline extractor, a dehumidifier compressor. Hold-to-talk
   removes the endpointing problem but not the SNR problem, and no amount of UI fixes a bad recording. This
   needs measuring in a real tent before the design is trusted, and it has not been.
3. **German ASR mangles exactly the string clubs need.** `„A1 bis A3"` is the highest-value and
   lowest-accuracy slot in the grammar — `a eins`, `8 eins`, `ah eins`, `a drei` / `Andre`. The `aliase`
   learning loop helps after a few corrections and the low-confidence touch rule prevents silent damage, but
   the first week with a new plant set will be annoying.
4. **Speaking out loud is itself a privacy act.** In a shared flat, dictating `„A1 bis A3 gefüttert"` is
   louder than typing it. And in Chrome the audio goes to Google's servers. The consent sheet is honest about
   this, but honesty does not remove the cost, and for some German users it is disqualifying. Whisper-on-our-
   server is the answer and it is v1.1.
5. **The confirm step spends the speed that voice promised.** Two taps is not zero taps, and a
   low-confidence chip makes it three. A competing "one tap = watered" design beats Diktat on the single
   most common event. Diktat only wins once the entry carries three or more facts.
6. **Sentences are bad for scanning.** A club treasurer reconciling 40 feeds wants a table, not 40
   paragraphs. `Buch → Tabelle` answers it, and a critic will call that a mode. My defence is in §4 and it is
   a defence, not a proof.
7. **The cull will throw away something real.** Rule 4 discards large transient changes, and "plant fell
   over and was stood back up" is a large transient change. `Verworfene Bilder` makes it recoverable, but
   only if the user thinks to look.
8. **The film pipeline is the biggest single v1 risk.** ffmpeg and `sharp` exist, but the job queue, the disk
   volume, the h265→h264 change and the three-branch download path are all new, all infrastructure, and all
   in a codebase with 38 `it()` blocks and a CI that never runs `ng test`. If one item slips it is this one,
   and it is the one the owner cares most about.
9. **The parser is a maintenance treadmill.** A closed German lexicon has no graceful generalisation. Every
   user who phrases something new files a ticket that reads "the app didn't understand me", and the fix is
   always a code change. An LLM would generalise, and it would also cost money per utterance, break offline,
   send grow data to a third party and become non-deterministic. I chose the treadmill deliberately, but it
   is a real ongoing cost and it grows with the user base.
10. **The Akteur picker is theatre if a club ever needs it as evidence.** § 17 Abs. 2 KCanG wants proof of
    *eigenhändige* participation; a name anyone can select is not proof. The product says so plainly, which
    is the right posture — but it means the club-compliance story is honest rather than strong.
11. **Two chart libraries in one shipped bundle.** Defensible for one release, indefensible for two.
12. **The no-modes mechanism is unvalidated on the user it most claims to serve.** A sentence with tappable
