# C17 — Nachtwache

**Family:** camera · **Centre of gravity:** the camera as a sensor and an alarm

---

## 1. Thesis

**Ein Bild ist der einzige Messwert, den jeder lesen kann — deshalb ist die Kamera hier kein Kachel-Feature, sondern der Beleg unter jeder Aussage und der Wächter, der sich meldet, wenn er nichts mehr sieht.**

Every other concept treats the camera as content: a picture you look at because pictures are nice. Nachtwache
treats it as instrumentation. A frame is a measurement whose value happens to be 1920×1080 numbers instead of
one, and like every other measurement it has a baseline, a deviation, a threshold and an alarm. It sees the
class of failure that the sensor set structurally cannot: the lamp that draws current but emits nothing, the
door left ajar, the pot knocked over at 03:00, the lens fogged shut, the plant that stopped growing three
weeks ago. And — the thing all ten round-one concepts forgot — it says so when it stops seeing.

The second half of the thesis is the answer to this round's hard problem. A number needs a reference before it
means anything (*is 58 % good?*). A sentence needs trust in whoever wrote it. **A picture needs neither.** It
is legible to someone stoned at 2 a.m. in a dark tent and admissible to someone who wants the raw series. That
is why the camera, and not a layout trick, is what lets one interface serve everybody.

---

## 2. Information architecture

Seven screens. No hidden ones, no second app, no settings that reveal features.

| Route | Name | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/list` | Geräte | Unchanged behaviour: one device → straight to its Wache. |
| `/device/:id` | **Wache** | Home. Live frame, the numbers as its caption, open findings, today's reel. |
| `/device/:id/beleg` | **Beleg** | One moment: the frame, every reading at that timestamp, diary events ±30 min, frame stats. `?t=<ms>` addressable. |
| `/device/:id/zeitraffer` | **Zeitraffer** | Calendar of day reels, export sheet, past exports with links + expiry. |
| `/device/:id/charts` | **Werte** | The chart. Route and query-param format kept — presets and share links depend on it. |
| `/device/:id/diary` | **Tagebuch** | Events, plants, cycle, feed regime, cumulative per plant. Route kept. |
| `/device/:id/settings` | **Einstellungen** | Existing per-type settings + a new **Nachtwache** section. |

Unchanged: `/shares`, `/account`, `/demo`, `/login`, `/classes`, `/testmode`.

**Navigation model.** A bottom `ion-tab-bar` scoped to the device: `Wache · Zeitraffer · Werte · Tagebuch`.
The `ion-menu` stays for global items. This replaces the six outline buttons on today's overview. The
**Zeitraffer tab is absent when the device has no camera** (`cloudSettings.rtspStream` empty) — reveal by
capability, not a disabled tab.

**Beleg is not a screen you navigate to, it is a screen you fall into.** Tapping any frame anywhere — the
Wache picture, a chart thumbnail, a diary entry's photo, a finding's evidence — lands on `/beleg?t=…`. Back
returns you exactly where you were. It is the app's one detail view.

### 3.2 The Bildunterschrift, precisely

Line 1: the three primary readings, largest type on the screen after the picture. Tapping any one of them
opens the Beleg for the current frame with that measure's panel scrolled into view.
Line 2: `<Lichtzustand> · Tag <n> · <Alter des Bildes>`.

Age wording, exact strings:

| Frame age | de | en |
| --- | --- | --- |
| < 90 s | `gerade eben` | `just now` |
| < 60 min | `vor 12 Min.` | `12 min ago` |
| < 24 h | `vor 3 Std. 12 Min.` | `3 h 12 min ago` |
| ≥ 24 h | `vom 12.09., 21:14` | `12 Sep, 21:14` |

Above 10 minutes the second line turns `--tc-text-warning`. Above one hour a Nachtwache finding exists and
the caption is redundant — but it still says the age, because the caption never lies by omission.

### 3.3 Stale, offline, absent

**Frame stale (device online, camera quiet).** The picture stays — a 3-hour-old picture of your tent is still
information. Caption line 2: `Letztes Bild vor 3 Std. 12 Min. · Kamera antwortet nicht (7. Versuch)`. The
retry count comes from `deviceIdToLastRtspState.failureCount`, which already exists in `image.service.ts` and
today is visible nowhere.

**Device offline.** Caption becomes `Controller offline seit 08:14`. **All camera findings are suppressed** —
one failure must not produce five alerts — and a single finding sits at the top: `Controller offline · seit
2 Std. 41 Min. · Letzte Werte: 25,1 °C, 57 %`. Today the server burns an English `Offline since …` caption
into the JPEG itself (`image.controller.ts:buildOfflineCaption`, hardcoded `en-GB`). Change: the burn-in is
kept **only** for share-link and embed requests, where the app chrome is absent; the app's own requests get
the clean frame and render the caption in the user's language.

**No camera paired.** The frame area is replaced — not with a placeholder image, with the content that would
otherwise be caption:

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — "Der Beleg"

### 4.1 The rule

> **Jede Aussage in der App trägt ihren Beleg sichtbar bei sich. Der Beleg ist ein Bild und eine Zahl.
> Tippen vergrößert den Beleg an Ort und Stelle. Es erscheint nichts, was vorher nicht da war — es wird
> nur etwas größer.**

Three invariants make this a mechanism and not a mode:

1. **Nothing is shown or hidden by a setting.** Visibility depends on exactly two things: hardware the device
   reports (`hardwareInfo`, `cloudSettings.rtspStream`, socket roles) and data that exists (a plant, a
   feeding, a frame). There is no user preference anywhere in the app that changes which elements render.
2. **Expansion is per-item, transient and never persisted.** Two people opening the same device see identical
   pixels. There is no state to be in, therefore no mode to be in, therefore nothing to remember or restore.
3. **No number is hidden.** The number is next to the sentence. The raw series is one expansion away, never
   two. Nobody is denied the number and nobody is made to read it.

### 4.2 Why an image is the mechanism

This is the arguable claim and the concept stands on it.

The simple/expert split exists because designers assume the same fact must be expressed at two levels of
sophistication. That assumption is true for numbers — `VPD 1,21 kPa` is meaningless to a beginner and
essential to a grower — and it is **false for pictures**. A photograph of your plants at 06:10 is not a
beginner's version of anything. It is the highest-fidelity representation available, and the beginner and the
expert extract different things from the *same* pixels, at the same time, with no help from us.

So the app's spine is pictures, and the numbers ride along as caption. The stoned user reads the picture and
the sentence. The techie reads the picture, then taps it and gets:

```
Beleg · 14.09.2026, 06:10:32
──────────────────────────────────────
[ frame, full width ]
──────────────────────────────────────
Temperatur     24,8 °C    Soll 20,0 (Nacht)
Feuchte        61 %       Soll 50
VPD            0,98 kPa   Ziel 1,2–1,5
CO₂            —          kein Sensor
Licht          out_light 100 %
──────────────────────────────────────
Bildwerte      Helligkeit 12 · Schärfe 0,41
               Tag/Nacht: Tag (laut Zeitplan)
               Δ zum Vortag 06:10: 63 (Schwelle 41)
──────────────────────────────────────
Tagebuch ±30 Min.   (keine Einträge)
──────────────────────────────────────
[ ◀ Bild ]  [ Bild ▶ ]  [ In den Werten öffnen ]
```

Same screen for both. The stoner sees a big picture with writing under it. The techie sees the raw frame
signature and the threshold that fired. **`Helligkeit 12` next to `Licht 100 %` is the entire "Licht kam
nicht" finding, self-evidently, with no explanation.** That is a picture doing a techie's job and a stoner's
job in one glyph.

### 4.3 Where the mechanism is weaker, and what fills the slot

A device with no camera has no picture to be its own Beleg. The slot is filled by the **last hour's
sparkline** at the same size, in the same position, with the same tap behaviour (tap → the raw window). It is
a strictly weaker Beleg and the concept does not pretend otherwise. See §13.3.

## 5. The chart (Werte)

Migrated to Apache ECharts 6 per the owner decision, hand-rolled directive, Chart.js deleted.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [🎞 ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪ ▪▪▪   ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪ ] Filmstreifen  │  ← thumbnails ARE the top axis
│  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│  25,3 °C  ·  58 %  ·  VPD 1,21  ·  Mi 14.09. 06:10   │  ← pinned scrub header (no tooltip)
│  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ Temperatur °C                                        │
│  ░░░░░░░░ Nacht ░░░░░  ▁▂▃▄▅▄▃▂▁ ░░░░ Nacht ░░░░░    │
│  ╌╌╌╌╌╌╌ Soll (gestuft) ╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌       │
│ ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ Feuchte %                                            │
│ ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ VPD kPa    [Zielband wandert mit der Phase]          │
│ ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ Ausgänge   ▬▬▬▬ Licht ▬▬▬▬   ▪▪ Heizung ▪▪  ▪ Entf. │  ← state timeline
│ ──────────────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ 📷 ▲   ✎ ▲▲   ⚠ ▲          ✎ ▲                       │  ← annotation rail, 2 rows
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goal vs actual.** A dashed **stepped** setpoint line from `day.temperature`/`night.temperature`, stepping
  at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC → rendered in an explicit local timezone). Behind
  it a `markArea` target band whose source is named in the panel header, one at a time and in this priority:
  `Alarmgrenzen` (hard, the user's own declaration) → `Soll ± Toleranz` (soft) → `VPD-Band der Phase`
  (advisory). Between actual and setpoint, a signed deviation fill. Never a fill to the axis.
- **Day/night** as `markArea` derived from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the schedule when the
  device reports no output. **A missing band is the diagnostic** — and it is the *same computation* that
  fires `Licht kam nicht`. The alert and the chart must never disagree; they read one function.
- **Outputs** in a state-timeline lane, region length = duration, `null` renders as a gap. Not 0/1 lines.
- **Annotations** on a rail under the x-axis, 12–20 px, clustered by pixel proximity with count badges, two
  rows max: row 1 human events, row 2 device events. **Nachtwache findings use a camera glyph** so you can see
  where the camera complained relative to the data. Full-height dashed lines for stage changes only.
- **min/max envelope** on the primary measure (`aggregateWindow` already supports both), `sampling:'minmax'`
  for the plot and `lttb` for the navigator. A single mean line is a lie about a bang-bang controller.
- **The film strip is an axis, not a gallery.** Thumbnails positioned by timestamp on the same x-scale, so
  uneven capture reads honestly as a gap. Delivered as **one sprite + JSON index per window**
  (`GET /image/:device_id/strip?from&to&n=60` → 60 tiles at 256×144 in one JPEG, ~180 KB, one request instead
  of sixty). mp4 rows with `timestampEnd` render as a bracket, not a point.
- **Touch rules**: one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap and drag
  scrubs. The floating tooltip is replaced by the pinned header — a fingertip occludes a tooltip and there is
  no hover on a phone. Axis labels are never hidden; below 320 px they drop to three ticks and the unit moves
  into the panel title.
- **Query params**: existing format preserved verbatim. Two additions, both optional and ignorable by old
  parsers: `strip=1`, `beleg=<ms>`.
- Before any of this: `measure`, `from`, `to` and `interval` must stop being interpolated raw into Flux, and
  the `limit()` must move before `yield()`. Non-negotiable prerequisite, ~half a day.

## 6. Diary and multi-plant

New MongoDB collections, cloud-side, keyed by `device_id`. Nothing goes into `device.configuration`.
`DeviceLog` stays exactly as it is for device messages; diary content moves off it, because its `deleted`
semantics, its absent pagination and its zero validation make it a bad host for the product's centrepiece.

```ts
Cycle   { cycle_id, device_id, name, startedAt, endedAt?, flipAt? }

Plant   { plant_id, device_id, cycle_id, label, strain?, startedAt,
          stage: DiaryLifecycleStage, stageSince, medium?, feedRegime?,
          frameBox?: {x,y,w,h},          // where this plant is in the frame (v1.1)
          archivedAt? }

DiaryEvent {
  event_id, device_id, cycle_id,
  plant_ids: string[],                  // [] === the whole tent
  kind: 'watering'|'feeding'|'measurement'|'training'|'note'|'stage'|'harvest'|'condition',
  at, createdAt,
  actor_id?,
  volumeL?, runoff?,
  ec?, ph?, ecBasis?: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source',
  products?: [{ name: string, mlPerL?: number, note?: string }],
  regime_id?, regimeStep?,
  text?, image_ids?: string[],
  frame_at?,                            // ← the camera frame this event points at
  supersedes?                           // corrections, never edits
}

Condition { condition_id, device_id, text, openedBy, openedAt, closedBy?, closedAt? }
Actor     { actor_id, device_id, name, colour }        // typed once, no accounts
```

**`frame_at` is the concept's signature field.** Every event, on save, is stamped with the timestamp of the
nearest stored camera frame. **Every diary entry therefore has a photograph without anyone taking one.** That
is the literal implementation of "it does the diary keeping for you": the record writes its own illustration.

**A user who ignores plants pays nothing.** There is always exactly one implicit plant, `label: 'Zelt'`,
created lazily on the first event and **never rendered**. The plant chip row appears in the capture sheet
only when `Plant.count > 1`. The Tagebuch's plant column appears only when `Plant.count > 1`. Nothing nags,
no empty state advertises plants. Adding a second plant is one action in the Tagebuch header
(`[ + Pflanze ]`), and the moment it exists the chips appear everywhere they are relevant — reveal by data.

**Cycle boundaries are stored, not inferred.** `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a one-time backfill, not
a runtime algorithm. Renaming a plant stops forking history; a re-veg stops fabricating a plant.

**Harvest** is a `kind` with a date. Its **weight field does not render** unless
`Einstellungen → Ernte mit Gewicht erfassen` is on, off by default, with the reason written next to it:
`Gewichte und Pflanzenzahlen sind die Angaben, die rechtlich am heikelsten sind. Terp Control fragt sie nicht
von selbst.` This is the privacy default from the dossier, surfaced as an honest sentence rather than a
buried flag.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

No irrigation hardware exists. This is a capture-and-recall problem, and the metric is taps.

### 7.1 The capture flow, counted

`Gießen` and `Düngen` are on the Wache under the picture, always, one tap each. Not behind a FAB.

**Happy path, one plant, regime selected — 2 taps:**

```
  │ Gießen · jetzt                          │
  │ ┌────┐  Bild von jetzt                  │
  │ │thmb│  14.09., 18:42                   │
  │ └────┘                                  │
  │                                         │
  │  Menge      5,0 L        ⌄              │  ← value-edit-row: tap → [−][range][+]
  │  Wasser     Leitung      ⌄              │

  │            [    Passt    ]              │  ← tap 2

```

**Feeding, regime prefilled — 2 taps:**

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ Düngen · jetzt                          │
  │  Plan: Biobizz All-Mix · Woche 7 von 12 │
  │  ─────────────────────────────────────  │
  │  Bio-Grow        4 ml/L      [ nicht ]  │
  │  Bio-Bloom       2 ml/L      [ nicht ]  │

  │  ─────────────────────────────────────  │

  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Deviation costs exactly what it should: `[ nicht ]` strikes a product (1 tap), the dose number is a
`value-edit-row` (1 tap to arm, drag to change), `+ Zusatz` adds a free-text product.

**EC and pH are absent from the sheet until this device has ever recorded one.** A `[ EC / pH ]` link at the
foot adds them; from then on they render as fields, permanently. The techie enables them once; the stoner
never sees them. Reveal by data, not a preference.

### 7.2 The double-feed block

Fires on `Passt`, when any selected plant has a `feeding` (6 h window) or `watering` (3 h window) event from a
**different `actor_id`**:

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std. 30 Min.      │
  │  gefüttert.                             │
  │                                         │
  │  ┌────────────┐  Bio-Grow 4 ml/L        │
  │  │ Annas Bild │  Bio-Bloom 2 ml/L       │
  │  │  17:12     │  5,0 L                  │
  │  └────────────┘                         │
  │                                         │
  │  [ Abbrechen ]        [ Trotzdem ]      │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

One deliberate extra tap to proceed. **The camera earns its place here**: Anna's frame is shown, so you can
see whether the pots are already wet. No other product in this market can put evidence into that dialog.
Windows are per-device settings, because 6 h is a guess (§13.6).

### 7.3 Schedule following

The regime is a **medium + feed regime pair**, named as growers say it: `Biobizz All-Mix`, `Biobizz
Light-Mix`, `Floragard Light`, `Green House Feeding`, `BioTabs`. Stored in a Mongo content collection with
`source_url` + `retrieved_at` + `chart_version`, never in the bundle, brand names as plain text only.

`All-Mix` expresses **"noch nicht düngen"** as a first-class step: weeks 1–3 have no products and the
`Düngen` sheet says `Plan: All-Mix · noch keine Düngung vorgesehen (Woche 2 von 12)` with the products list
empty and `[ trotzdem düngen ]` available. A regime that cannot say "don't feed yet" does not model this
domain.

**Steps advance on feed events, not on the calendar.** The reminder is a Nachtwache finding at level *Merken*:
`Nach Plan wäre die 7. Fütterung dran. Zuletzt gefüttert vor 4 Tagen.` `[ Jetzt eintragen ]`.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is the headline. Everything below is grounded in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`, read directly.

### 8.1 What exists today, verified

- The cloud pulls one frame per device every 30 s (`IMAGE_LOAD_INTERVAL_MS`), via ffmpeg from RTSP or via the
  O-KAM P2P tunnel. Exponential backoff to 120 min on failure. `pLimit(10)`.
- Frames are `Image{format:'jpeg'}` **Buffers inside MongoDB**. Thinned to 1/min after 1 day, 1/5 min after
  7 days, 1/15 min after 30, 1/h after 90. Deleted after 3 years.
- Hourly, `compressRtspStreamRange` builds mp4s for `1d` (2 min/frame), `1w` (14 min), `1m` (60 min) windows,
  **and walks backwards building past windows too** — so a per-day mp4 archive already accumulates, and it
  survives the thinning of its source frames. This is the single most useful fact in the file.
- Encoding: `libx265 -crf 30`, 25 fps, **no `-pix_fmt`, no scaling, no colour handling, no culling**.
- Window boundaries are epoch multiples: `1d` = UTC midnight, **`1w` = Thursday** (epoch day 0 was a Thursday).

### 8.2 Three defects that block shipping a shareable video

1. **libx265 in MP4 does not play in Firefox and does not play in Chrome on many desktops.** A share feature
   built on HEVC is a support queue. Everything that leaves the product must be **H.264 High, `yuv420p`,
   `+faststart`**.
2. **No `-pix_fmt yuv420p`.** JPEG input yields `yuvj420p`; some players render it washed out or refuse it.
3. **Weekly windows start on Thursday.** Fine as an internal cache, useless as a user-facing unit. Exports are
   built from an explicit date range, never from these windows.

### 8.3 The frame signature — the camera becomes a sensor

Computed **once, at ingest**, in the promise chain that already holds the buffer. `sharp` is already a
dependency.

```ts
FrameStat {                       // ~120 bytes; 2 880/day/device ≈ 350 KB/day
  device_id, image_id, timestamp,
  lum, lumP05, lumP95,            // mean + percentiles of a 64×64 grey
  rgbMean: [r, g, b],             // 8×8×8 histogram centroid
  sharpness,                      // variance of Laplacian on a 128 px grey
  hash,                           // 64-bit dHash of a 9×8 grey
  diffPrev,                       // Hamming distance to the previous same-light-state frame
  motionCells,                    // 8×8 blocks whose mean grey moved > 12
  lightState: 'day' | 'night'     // from out_light, falling back to the schedule
}
```

Cost: `.resize(64,64).greyscale().raw()` ≈ 2 ms. 150 devices × 2 880 frames ≈ 15 min CPU/day. Backfill for
existing frames is an idempotent boot-time pass, per the codebase's house style (no migration tooling exists).

**Everything downstream — every alert, every culling decision, every normalisation gain — reads FrameStat and
never the pixels again.** That is what makes the whole feature affordable.

### 8.4 The alert vocabulary

Per-device thresholds are **self-calibrating**: each rule compares against that device's own trailing
distribution, so a wobbly tent gets a duller trigger and a rock-steady one gets a sharp one. There is no
sensitivity slider, because nobody can set one correctly.

| Finding (de) | Rule | Level | False positive, and what suppresses it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Kamera schweigt** | No stored frame for > max(60 min, current backoff interval) while the controller is online | Sofort | Maintenance mode / `workmode: off` with `maintenanceWebcamOff` already stops capture — the *same condition* must gate the alert. Deep backoff is legitimately 2 h, so the text names the retry count instead of implying death. |
| **Licht kam nicht** | 10 min after `daynight.day`, `lum` < 40 % of the trailing 7-day median day-`lum`, for 3 consecutive frames | Sofort | Camera aimed at a shaded corner → the 7-day baseline is the camera's own, so this self-corrects. A dying lamp fading 20 %/8 weeks is **invisible by construction** (§13.8). |
| **Licht ging nicht aus** | 15 min after `daynight.night`, `lum` > 3× the trailing night median, 3 consecutive frames | Sofort | Someone in the tent with a head torch. Suppressed for 20 min after any presence signal. **Still the riskiest rule** (§13.2). |
| **Tür offen** | Night, `lum` > 3× baseline, `motionCells` low, sustained > 10 min | Sofort | Same as above, longer sustain. |
| **Beschlagen** | `sharpness` < 25 % of the 7-day median **for the same light state**, ≥ 15 min | Merken | Never compare across light states — a night frame is legitimately flat. Real fog is itself worth reporting. |
| **Bild anders als gestern** | Today's 08:00 day frame vs yesterday's 08:00 day frame; Hamming `diff` above this device's 30-day p95 | Merken | This is the fallen-plant / moved-camera / harvested case. **The finding never says what changed. It shows both frames side by side and lets the human decide.** |
| **Kamera verstellt** | Large `diff` **with** a near-identical RGB histogram (same content, new framing) | Merken | Offers `[ Zeitraffer ab hier neu ausrichten ]`, which sets a reel segment boundary. |
| **Jemand war im Zelt** | `motionCells` > 20 % + large `diff`, then settling to a new stable hash | Ruhig — never notifies | Used for two things only: suppressing other findings for 20 min, and culling those frames from the reel. It also offers `[ Gegossen ] [ Gedüngt ]` on the Wache, which backdates a `DiaryEvent` to that timestamp. |

**Three levels, with real semantics:**

- **Ruhig** — recorded, visible on the Wache, never sent anywhere.
- **Merken** — one digest per day at a user-set hour (default 09:00), one message per device.
- **Sofort** — e-mail (and the existing webhook) immediately, with the frame attached.

`Nachtruhe` (22:00–07:00) silences *Merken*. **Sofort overrides it by definition — that is the whole reason
the level exists.** Every finding resolves as well as triggers: `Kamera liefert wieder Bilder. 4 Std. 12 Min.
ohne Bild.` Consolidation rule: one message per device per event, never one per sensor.

**What Nachtwache refuses to claim.** "Zelt leer" is not implemented and will not be, because an empty tent
and a camera whose view is filled by one enormous fan leaf are the same pixels. What ships instead: after a
`harvest` event or the `curing` stage, the camera enters **Ruhezustand** — only the day-over-day rule keeps
running and nothing asks about plants that are not there. Similarly, "Pflanze umgefallen" is never said; only
"Das Bild sieht heute anders aus als gestern", with both pictures.

### 8.5 Implementation route for the alerts, honestly

The existing alarm engine evaluates **only** in `alarmService.onDataReceived`, i.e. only when MQTT data
arrives, and `isThresholdExceeded` builds a **Flux measure name out of `alarm.sensorType`** — so injecting a
pseudo-sensor there would query Influx for a measure that does not exist. Verified in
`server/src/services/alarm.service.ts:374-390`.

So Nachtwache is a **separate service on a 5-minute tick**, writing findings to a new `Finding` collection and
dispatching through the existing e-mail and webhook helpers. It does not touch `Alarm`. Cost: ~1.5 days for
the service, ~1 day for dispatch and digest. Being independent of MQTT is the point: camera silence is
precisely the case where the controller is still happily reporting.

### 8.6 Der Schnitt — how a watchable timelapse is made

Once per local day boundary, over that day's frames **while they are still dense at 30 s** and before thinning
touches them. Output: one **Tagesrolle** — ~100 frames, 4 s at 25 fps, ≤ 1280×720, H.264.

**Culling, in order:**

1. **Wrong light state out.** Day frames only, by default. Night is 50 % of a flowering timelapse and it is
   black. One filter, the single biggest quality win in the pipeline. `Nachtbilder mitnehmen` exists for IR
   cameras and is off.
2. **Transitions out.** ±10 min around lights-on/off — sunrise/sunset ramps make brightness leap.
3. **Presence out.** `motionCells` high, plus the three frames either side.
4. **Blind frames out.** `sharpness` < 25 % of the day's median, or `lumP95 < 20` (dark), or `lumP05 > 235`
   (blown).
5. **Near-duplicates out.** Bucket the survivors into 8-minute bins and **keep the sharpest frame in each
   bin**. Deterministic ~90 frames from a 12 h photoperiod, and preferring sharpness quietly removes motion
   blur and half-condensed lenses for free.
6. **Under 12 survivors → no reel.** A `Merken` finding is written instead: `Am 14.09. gab es zu wenige
   brauchbare Bilder für eine Tagesrolle.` A gap in a timelapse is honest; a 3-frame flicker is not.

**Normalisation, in order:**

1. **Exposure** — per-frame linear gain so `lumP50` matches the rolling 7-day median of day-frame `lumP50`.
   Gain clamped to [0.6, 1.7] so a genuinely dark day stays dark. `sharp().linear(a, b)`.
2. **White balance** — per-channel gains so `rgbMean` matches the rolling 7-day median `rgbMean`, clamped per
   channel to [0.7, 1.4]. This is a grey-world correction **against the device's own history, not against
   neutral grey**: a magenta LED tent stays magenta, it just stops strobing when the door opens.
3. **Smoothing of the corrections** — the gains themselves get a 5-frame moving average before application.
   Without this the reel pumps, which looks worse than the flicker it replaced. This is the step nobody
   specifies and the one that decides whether the video is watchable.
4. **Stabilisation — translation only.** Integer-pixel offset from phase correlation of the 128 px grey
   against the reel's first frame, applied as a crop offset inside a 3 % safety border. **Rotation is not
   corrected.** If the offset exceeds 6 % of frame width we do not shift: we **cut the reel** at that frame
   and emit `Kamera verstellt`. v1 fixes drift, not a knock, and says so.
5. `-vf scale=1280:-2 -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -preset veryfast -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart`.

Storage: ~1,5 MB/day/device. **Once a day has a reel, its raw frames may be thinned harder** (tier 1 goes
from 1/min to 1/5 min), so net storage *falls*. The reel is the durable artefact; the frames are perishable
source.

### 8.7 Der Zeitraffer — how a video leaves the product

**Because every Tagesrolle is already the same codec, fps and resolution, a grow-long timelapse is an ffmpeg
concat-demuxer `-c copy` join.** A 90-day video assembles in ~2 seconds with no re-encode and no generation
loss. That single fact is what makes shipping this in six weeks realistic.

```
Zeitraffer
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Juni  ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪                    (13)
  Juli  ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪    (31)
  Aug.  ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪░░▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪   (29, 2 Lücken)
  Sept. ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪                   (14)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────
  83 von 90 Tagen haben Bilder.
                        [ Zeitraffer erstellen ]
```

Export sheet:

```
  Von   18.06.2026        Bis   14.09.2026
  Tempo   ( Kurz ~30 Sek. ) ( Mittel ~1 Min. ) ( Ganz 5:32 )
  Einblendung   [x] Tag, Datum, Temperatur, Feuchte, VPD
  Nachtbilder   [ ] mitnehmen
                              [ Erstellen ]
```

`Einblendung` burns a bottom bar per frame: `Tag 47 · 14.09.2026 · 25,3 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 kPa`. **That is
image correlation made physical — the numbers travel with the video after it leaves us.** It costs one
re-encode (`drawtext` with a timed text file), ~20 s for 90 days at 720p veryfast. Clean export is `-c copy`.

**No watermark, no "Powered by Terp Control".** § 6 KCanG bans cannabis advertising and sponsoring with fines
to €30,000, read broadly; a branded burn-in on thousands of German growers' cannabis posts is the single
riskiest string in the feature. It is not shipped.

**The exact path out:**

```
POST /device/:device_id/timelapse
     { from, to, speed: 'short'|'medium'|'full', caption: bool, includeNight: bool }
  → 202 { job_id }

GET  /device/:device_id/timelapse/:job_id
  → { state: 'queued'|'running'|'done'|'failed', progress, bytes, download_url, expiresAt }

GET  /timelapse/:job_id.mp4?t=<imageToken>
  → 200 video/mp4
     Content-Disposition: attachment;
       filename="terpcontrol-zelt-1-2026-06-18-bis-2026-09-14.mp4"
```

- The token is the existing 30-day `imageToken` scheme, which is already deliberately URL-embeddable, scoped
  to the job id. **The link works with no login.** A share that lands on a login page is not a share.
- Jobs and their bytes live **7 days**, then the URL returns 410 with a German page saying so.
- The file **must not** live in Mongo: a 5-minute 720p export is 45–70 MB and the BSON ceiling is 16 MB.
  Exports go to disk with a TTL sweep. This is new infrastructure and it is the concept's largest hidden cost
  (§13.4).
- Phone reality, printed in the sheet, not hidden: `Auf dem iPhone landet das Video in „Dateien". Von dort
  kannst du es in „Fotos" sichern.` Android downloads it normally.
- Telegram: the **Kurz** version (~5 MB) can be pushed to a bot; the full export cannot (50 MB bot limit).
  The UI offers Telegram only for Kurz, and says why.

### 8.8 Image correlation, both directions

- **Chart → Bild.** Dragging the chart cursor moves the film strip and the pinned header together. No 500 ms
  hover delay, because there is no hover. Sprite-delivered, one request per window.
- **Bild → Zahlen.** Tapping any frame opens the Beleg (§4.2): the readings at that instant, the setpoints
  that applied, the frame signature, the diary events within ±30 min, and frame-by-frame arrows.
- **Ereignis → Bild.** Every `DiaryEvent.frame_at` makes the diary a photo record nobody had to shoot.
- **Bild → Ereignis.** From the Beleg, `[ Hier etwas eintragen ]` opens the capture sheet **backdated to that
  frame's timestamp**. You saw something happen at 21:14 last night; you log it at 21:14, not at 09:00 today.

## 9. Clubs

Everything here is v1 and none of it touches `auth.middleware.ts`.

- **Attribution without authorisation.** `Actor` is a name the owner types once
  (`Einstellungen → Wer ist am Zelt`). At capture, a chip row; the last-used actor is preselected and sticky
  for 4 h. Stamped to `DiaryEvent.actor_id`. No accounts, no e-mail lookup, no membership collection, no
  middleware surgery. Roughly two days of work, and it delivers the thing clubs actually asked for.
- **Double feed** — §7.2. The most expensive event in a shared room, soft-blocked with one deliberate tap and
  a photograph as evidence.
- **Cumulative** — §7.4, with `von wem` on every line.
- **Conditions** — the `OFFEN` block on the Wache. `Condition {text, openedBy, openedAt, closedBy?,
  closedAt?}`. `CO₂-Flasche fast leer — Anna, 12.09.` `[✓]` closes it with the closer's name. This is what
  gets taped to the tent door, and it is neither an event nor a task.
- **Corrections, not edits.** `DiaryEvent.supersedes` — a wrong entry is superseded, both rows survive, the
  history stays reconstructable. No cryptographic immutability, because immutability protects a regulated club
  and victimises an unregulated grower.
- **Privacy posture — privat by default.** Actor names may be `A`, `B`, `C`; nothing requires a real name.
  No harvest weight is prompted (§6). No location, ever. No public gallery of anything. Exports carry
  `KEINE | PSEUDONYMISIERT | ANONYM` and default to **pseudonymisiert**. A shared timelapse link is unlisted,
  expiring, and its filename carries the device *name*, never the `device_id`.
- **What Terp Control refuses to be:** the club's § 26 system of record. No per-member dispensing, no grams
  ledger, no THC content, no member identity. The camera-attested environmental record is the thing worth
  offering to Cannanas/420cloud later, and it needs zero personal data.

## 10. Old devices (type 7)

`fan`, `light`, `plug`. (`dryer` stays dead per AGENTS.md.) Exactly what their owner sees:

- **Wache renders without a picture area** unless `cloudSettings.rtspStream` is set — which is possible for any
  device type and is respected if so. Otherwise the top block is the readings that device actually reports
  (a plug's slave temperature/humidity, a fan's rpm) plus the 24 h day/night bar, and the Beleg slot in each
  row is the last-hour sparkline.
- **Nachtwache offers exactly two findings**, both from data these devices already send: `Gerät offline` and
  `Schaltet dauernd` (reusing the existing `running_continuously` alarm preset semantics). Their Nachtwache

- **The Zeitraffer tab is absent.** Not disabled, not upsold — absent.
- **`fan-settings`, `light-settings` and `plug-settings` are not touched.** Zero diff in those three
  components. The new shell wraps them; the `ngSwitch` in `settings.page.html` keeps working unchanged.
- **No plant, no regime, no feeding prompts.** Reveal by data: an old plug that never had a plant added shows
  nothing about plants anywhere.
- What they gain for free: the tab bar, the new chart with setpoints and day/night shading, the Tagebuch with
  actors and conditions, and the offline finding they never had.
- What they lose: nothing. No route is removed, no existing setting disappears.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the Wache, sees three numbers and a day/night bar, closes it. The chart is
where the value lands: small multiples with setpoint and day/night shading answer "war alles ok" in one
glance instead of fifteen translucent areas. Nachtwache gives them `Gerät offline` and nothing else, which is
honest for a device that measures and nothing more. They are the type this concept serves least, and the
sparkline-Beleg is a consolation prize, not a gift.

**2 — Same, but with a camera.** This is the concept's centre. The picture is their home screen; the timelapse
is a file they can put in a WhatsApp group; the Beleg means every frame carries its numbers; and Nachtwache
means the camera is not a toy that quietly dies on day 14 of flower. If they buy the €319 bundle instead of
the €289 controller, this is the reason.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Reveal by capability, from the per-role truth in
`hardwareInfo.sockets` rather than the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` that fails open today: a
heater-only tent shows a heater output lane, a heater setpoint and heater alarm presets, and never a
dehumidifier tile or a humidity deviation warning it cannot act on. Nachtwache tells them when the heater
socket is on and the temperature is not moving — the same shape of "the machine claims, the world disagrees"
argument as the light rule, on a sensor rather than the camera.

**4 — Full tent.** Gets the outputs state-timeline lane, which is the first time this product has shown what
its kit actually did over a day. They will still hit the five-socket-role ceiling — no humidifier, no exhaust,
`dehumidifier` doubling as cooler — and this concept does not fix that, because it is firmware work behind an
October hardware date. What it does give them is a chart where a bang-bang controller's min/max envelope is
visible and a camera that catches the failures the roles cannot express.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Served by the Beleg screen being a URL (`/beleg?t=…`), by the frame-signature values
being visible rather than internal, and by `FrameStat` being the first thing in this product with an obvious
API shape. They get the export path (`/timelapse/:job_id.mp4?t=…`) as a real file they can script against.
They do **not** get a write API in v1, and this concept does not pretend to serve them well.

**6 — Clubs.** Actors, the double-feed block with a photograph in it, conditions on the tent door, corrections
instead of edits, and cumulative per-plant totals with names attached. The camera is the club's real answer to
"how did the plants react" — a month of reels next to a month of events reconstructs a shift nobody wrote
down. And the privacy line is drawn where the law punishes rather than requires: no member ledger, no grams,
no real names.

**7 — Old device owners.** §10. Nothing breaks, one new tab bar, a better chart, and two honest findings.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

**13.1 The detector cannot name what it detects.** `Das Bild sieht heute anders aus als gestern` is a weak
sentence, and some users will read it as the app knowing nothing. I chose it because the alternative —
claiming `Pflanze umgefallen` — would be wrong often enough to destroy the feature's credibility in a month.
But a competitor shipping a small vision model will say the specific thing, and specific beats honest in a
shop window. This is a deliberate bet and it can lose.

**13.2 The false positives arrive at night.** `Licht ging nicht aus` will fire when someone checks the tent at
23:00 with the room light on. Three consecutive frames and 20-minute presence suppression reduce it; they do
not eliminate it. One bogus 23:00 e-mail and a user switches the whole feature off — and Nachtwache switched
off is this concept switched off. I ship it at *Sofort* anyway, because a missed light leak in week 6 of
flower costs more than an annoyed user. That is a judgement call and it is the one I am least sure of.

**13.3 A camera-less customer gets a hollowed-out product.** At €289 without the camera versus €319–349 with
it, some real fraction of the first 150 units has no camera. For them the home screen's main element is
missing, the Beleg is a sparkline, and Nachtwache has two findings. I filled the hole; I did not solve it.
A critic will say Nachtwache is a concept for an accessory, and they will be partly right.

**13.4 Mongo is the wrong store for video and I am putting more video in it.** Reels are ~1,5 MB/day/device;
150 devices × 90 days ≈ 20 GB of `Buffer` fields. And a full export at 45–70 MB **cannot be an `Image`
document at all** — the BSON ceiling is 16 MB. Exports therefore need disk (or object) storage with a TTL
sweep, which this codebase does not have in any form. That is new infrastructure, new failure modes and new
ops, and it is the single largest hidden cost in this concept.

**13.5 The daily reel build can starve frame capture.** `ffmpegLimit = pLimit(10)` is shared between the
capture loop and compression today. Adding a per-device daily reel burst on top will, at some device count,
start dropping frames — and the symptom (missing frames) looks exactly like a camera problem, which
Nachtwache will then report. A separate limiter is a two-line fix nobody would think to make until the bug
report arrives, which is why it is written down here.

**13.6 The six-hour double-feed window is a guess.** I do not know how clubs feed. Too wide and it obstructs;
too narrow and it never fires. Making it configurable is an admission of ignorance, not a design.

**13.7 Compound failures collapse into one finding.** If the camera is nudged *and* a pot falls on the same
day, the day-over-day rule produces one alert. The user resolves it as "camera moved" and never learns about
the pot. Every threshold-on-a-summary-statistic design has this property; mine has it too.

**13.8 Slow drift is invisible by construction.** Everything is normalised against a rolling 7-day median, so
a lamp losing 20 % output over eight weeks is smoothed away in the reel *and* never trips `Licht kam nicht`,
because every day looks like the last seven. Detecting it needs an absolute reference — a grey card in frame
— that nobody will install. This is a real hole in "the camera is a sensor" and I have no fix for it.

**13.9 Unpairing a camera mid-grow is unhandled.** `hwinfo webcam_did=none` removes a camera and my design
leaves a half-built reel series and a `Kamera schweigt` that fires forever. It needs an explicit "Kamera
entfernt" event that closes the reel series and stands the watch down. It is not on the v1 list and it should
be; that is a scoping error I am flagging rather than hiding.

**13.10 Nothing here is protected by a test.** I am adding a tab bar, a new home screen, five Mongo
collections, a new service, a video pipeline and a chart library migration to a codebase with 38 `it()`
blocks, 24 of which are `should create`, in a CI that never runs `ng test`. The verification story is
`./simulate-device.sh run --camera` and human eyes, and for the culling and normalisation maths that is
genuinely not good enough — those are exactly the functions where a sign error produces a plausible-looking
wrong video that nobody notices for eleven weeks.
