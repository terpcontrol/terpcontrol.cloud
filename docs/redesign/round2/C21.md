# C21 — "Schreibt sich selbst"

**Family:** diary · **Centre of gravity:** the machine writes 95 % of the diary; the human confirms,
corrects and annotates the rest.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The diary is not a form you fill in — it is a page the cloud writes every day, in German sentences, out
of telemetry and machine state; you read it, and occasionally you correct it.**

Terp Control already knows almost everything a grow diary contains. It knows when the light went on and
off, when the photoperiod flipped, when the tent sat over 28 °C for 41 minutes, when the dehumidifier ran
all night, when the device went offline, when a stage changed, when the camera stopped seeing anything.
Today all of that lands in `DeviceLog` as 200 undifferentiated rows a day and nobody reads it. C21's move
is to stop treating events as entries and start treating **the day as the entry**: one page per day, a
handful of sentences, written by a server-side generator, closed at 03:00 the next morning, with the
human's own notes woven into the same page. The grower's job shrinks to three things the machine
genuinely cannot do — say what they poured, say what they saw, and say yes or no when the machine guesses.

The obvious failure mode is a diary full of machine noise nobody reads. C21's answer is not a filter, it
is **a budget**: a day sheet has room for about five sentences, so five sentences is what gets written.
Everything else stays underneath, one tap down, counted rather than hidden. A ranked-scarcity generator is
a very different thing from a log viewer with categories, and the whole concept lives or dies on it.

## 2. Information architecture

Eight surfaces. No tab bar, no FAB. The existing `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` chrome is kept unchanged.

| Route | Name (de) | What it is | Exists when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/list` | Geräte | unchanged; one device still redirects to that device | always |
| `/device/:id` | **Tagebuch** | **the home screen.** Today's sheet, then yesterday, then infinite scroll back | always |
| `/device/:id/verlauf` | Verlauf | the chart. Small multiples, target bands, annotation rail | always |
| `/device/:id/pflege` | Pflege | care history table, the Bilanz (cumulative per plant/product/person), open conditions | when ≥1 `CareEvent` or ≥1 `Condition` exists |
| `/device/:id/film` | Film | the run film, frame browser, download | when the device has a paired camera |
| `/device/:id/lauf` | Lauf | run + plant editor, medium/feed-regime selection | when a run exists |
| `/device/:id/settings` | Einstellungen | **existing pages, untouched** | always |
| `/device/:id/testmode`, `/shares`, `/account`, `/diagnostics` | | untouched | as today |

**The navigation thesis: the prose is the navigation.** Nothing is reached by hunting a menu; things are
reached by tapping the thing in the sentence that mentions them. Tapping `28 °C` in "41 Min lang über
28 °C" opens the evidence drawer; tapping "ganze Kurve" in that drawer opens `/verlauf` already zoomed to
14:12–14:53 with temperature selected. Tapping "A1–A4" opens those plants' care history. Tapping the photo
opens `/film` at that frame.

Because "tap the words" is not discoverable enough on its own for a user comparing this to AC Infinity, the
run header carries **three permanent chips — `Verlauf` · `Pflege` · `Film`** — and a `⋯` for settings,
maintenance and share. Three chips is not a tab bar and it is not a mode; it is the shortest possible
always-visible index of the other surfaces.

### 3.2 Real German copy — the sentence library

The generator does not emit strings. It emits `{ template, params }`, resolved client-side through the
existing `@ngx-translate` bundle. A representative slice of `de.json` / `en.json` additions:

```jsonc
"sheet": {
  "light.asPlanned":   "Licht ging um {{on}} an und um {{off}} aus, wie geplant.",
  "light.flip":        "Der Lichtzyklus ist von {{from}} auf {{to}} gewechselt.",
  "climate.inBand":    "Tagsüber im Schnitt {{dayT}} bei {{dayH}}, nachts {{nightT}} bei {{nightH}} — beides im Zielband.",
  "excursion.over":    "Um {{at}} war es {{minutes}} Minuten lang über {{limit}}.",
  "output.saturated":  "Der {{role}} lief in dieser Zeit durch.",
  "care.watered":      "{{who}} hat um {{at}} {{plants}} gegossen, {{volume}}.",
  "care.fed":          "{{who}} hat um {{at}} {{plants}} gedüngt: {{products}}.",
  "care.due":          "Nach dem Schema wäre heute wieder Düngen dran (Schritt {{step}}).",
  "stage.changed":     "Neue Phase: {{stage}}.",
  "offline.started":   "Um {{at}} ist das Gerät offline gegangen. Seitdem keine Daten.",
  "camera.silent":     "Die Kamera hat seit {{since}} kein brauchbares Bild geliefert.",
  "quiet":             "Ruhiger Tag. Nichts außer der Reihe.",
  "quiet.short":       "{{t}} · {{h}} · Licht {{hours}} h · kein Alarm",
  "guess.floweringStarted": "Die Blüte hat begonnen — seit {{days}} Tagen läuft 12/12.",
  "condition.open":    "Offen seit {{days}} Tagen: {{text}}"
}
```

Tone matches the existing bundle exactly: informal *du*, no exclamation marks, no adjectives of praise.
Hard rule enforced in review: **a template never contains more than two clauses.** Everything longer gets
split into two sentences, because generated German degrades fastest at the third clause.

### 3.3 Stale, offline, absent

These are not error states in C21 — they are **content**, because the machine writing the diary means the
outage documents itself.

**Fresh (< 10 min, `Device.lastseen` within `ONLINE_TIMEOUT`)**
`● 24,3 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 kPa · Licht an` / `vor 40 Sek.` — green dot, `--tc-text-success`.

**Stale (2–10 min since last sample, device still counts as online)**
Values stay black, dot turns amber, second line reads `zuletzt vor 6 Min.` in `--tc-text-warning`. No
sentence is written yet: six minutes is not a story.

**Offline (> 10 min)**
```
○ Offline seit 11:42 · vor 2 Std 18 Min
  Zuletzt gemessen: 24,8 °C · 57 % · Licht an
```
Dot hollow, values in `--ion-color-step-500` and labelled *zuletzt gemessen* — never rendered as if
current. This is the #1 complaint about every competitor and it costs a colour and four words. The day
sheet gains a sentence in real time: *"Um 11:42 ist das Gerät offline gegangen. Seitdem keine Daten."* When
it comes back: *"Um 14:03 war das Gerät wieder da. 2 Std 21 Min fehlen im Verlauf."*

**Absent (device claimed, no telemetry yet)**
```
Noch nichts zu schreiben.

Sobald dein Gerät misst, entsteht hier jeden Tag
ein Eintrag — automatisch.

[ Ersten Eintrag selbst schreiben ]
```

**Partial coverage.** `DaySheet.coverage` is the fraction of the day with telemetry. Below 0.8 the sheet
carries a plain footer: `Für 6 Std 12 Min fehlen an diesem Tag Messwerte.` Averages in the sheet are then
suffixed *"(soweit gemessen)"*. Below 0.3 no climate sentence is generated at all — an average over a third
of a day is a lie, and the honest sentence is the gap sentence.

**Share / demo / public.** All three restricted modes render the same day sheets read-only:
`＋ Notiz / ＋ Gießen / ＋ Foto` and the `[ Stimmt ]` buttons are absent (not disabled), and photos obey
`ShareLink.webcam` exactly as `getDeviceImage` already enforces.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

There is one interface. It is a page of German sentences. What separates the stoned user from the techie is
**not what they are shown — it is how deep into a single sentence they choose to press.**

### 4.1 Die Aufklappzahl (the unfoldable number)

Every quantitative claim in every generated sentence is a **Fact**, not a formatted string. Facts render
with a 1 px dotted underline in the body text colour — visible, never loud. Tapping one expands a
three-row drawer **beneath that sentence, in place**. No modal, no page change, no scroll jump.

```
Um 14:12 war es 41 Minuten lang über 28 °C.
              ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾           ‾‾‾‾
                   ↓ tap
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 28,4 °C  Spitzenwert um 14:31              │
│ ▁▂▄▇█▇▅▃▂▁  14:12 ─ 14:53                  │
│ temperature · 328 Rohwerte · Grenze 28,0   │
│ [ ganze Kurve ]  [ Bild um 14:31 ]  [ CSV ]│
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Row 1 is the value with unit and time. Row 2 is the sparkline of the exact window the claim came from
(ECharts, no axes, 40 px). Row 3 is the provenance: measure name, raw sample count, the threshold the
detector used. Three affordances: the full chart at that zoom, the nearest camera frame, and the raw CSV.

Long-press a Fact copies the underlying window as CSV to the clipboard without opening anything.

**Why this is not a mode.** There is no preference, no persisted state, no user classification, no
"advanced" label, and nothing is hidden per *feature class*: every single number in the product behaves
identically. A drawer is closed because it is closed, and it costs one tap to open — the same tap for
everybody, on the same screen, at the same scroll position. The stoner reads five sentences and never
learns the drawer exists. The techie sees the dotted underlines within three seconds and never sees a
different app. Nobody is denied the number and nobody is forced to read it.

### 4.2 Cardinality, not category

The one disclosure on each day sheet is labelled **`Alle 47 Ereignisse an diesem Tag ›`** — not
"Rohdaten", not "Details", not "Erweitert". This is a deliberate rule with a name:

> **Never label a disclosure by its audience. Label it by its count.**

"Erweitert" tells the reader which kind of person opens it, which is a mode by implication. "47" tells them
how much is there, which is information both users can act on: a stoner sees 47 and knows nothing burned
down; a techie sees 47 and opens it. Behind it is `app-log-entry-viewer`, which already exists, already
paginates and already filters by category — reached per day, scoped to that day, not persisted.

### 4.3 Depth is time, not level

The only infinite dimension here is scroll, and scrolling means going back in time. There is no second
layer of screens to get lost in and no "back to simple" to find. A user who never taps anything still gets
the whole product: a page a day, for as long as they own the device.

### 4.4 The impaired user

At 2 a.m. in a dark tent the top of the screen is four numbers, a freshness line, and today's five
sentences at 16 px. The three capture buttons are 48 px and fixed in position. Nothing on this screen
reorders itself based on behaviour — **C21 explicitly refuses adaptive ordering**, because the one user who
cannot afford an unpredictable layout is the one the owner keeps naming.

## 5. The chart

`/device/:id/verlauf`. Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive
(`ngx-echarts` ships a major per Angular major). This concept needs `markArea` + `markLine` + linked
crosshair + `sampling`, which is exactly the capability argument for the migration.

### 5.1 Goal vs actual

Small multiples, stacked, shared x-axis, one linked crosshair (`axisPointer.link`), 100 px per panel:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Temperatur °C                        24,3  ⌀ 24,1 │
│ 30 ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄  │
│    ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  ▓▓ Zielband 24–26 (Tag)        │
│ 20 ──────╱‾‾‾‾╲───────╱‾‾‾‾╲──────────────────    │
│    ┈┈┈┈┈┈ Sollwert (gestuft Tag/Nacht)            │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Luftfeuchte %                        58    ⌀ 57   │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ VPD kPa                              1,21         │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Ausgänge   ▓▓▓▓░░░▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░▓▓  Heizung           │
│            ░░▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░░░░  Entfeuchter       │
│            ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░  Licht             │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ▓ ▓  ▓        ▓▓        ▓          Ereignisrail   │
│ ▲ Phase: Blüte                                    │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Setpoint** = a dashed stepped `markLine` built from `day.temperature` / `night.temperature` stepping at
  `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC — converted in an explicit named timezone).
- **Target band** = `markArea`, one source at a time, labelled, priority: the user's own alarm thresholds
  (hard) > setpoint ± tolerance (soft) > the stage's `vpdRange` (advisory, VPD panel only).
- **Deviation fill** between actual and setpoint, signed. Never fill to the axis.
- **Envelope**: `{mean,min,max}` per window in one response; plot with `sampling: 'minmax'` so a bang-bang
  controller's oscillation survives. LTTB only in the navigator.
- **Day/night** = `markArea` derived from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the `daynight`
  schedule. A failed contactor shows as a **missing band** — a diagnosis, not decoration.
- **Outputs** get a state-timeline lane, not 0/1 lines on a value axis. Null renders as a gap.

### 5.2 The text ribbon — C21's specific chart move

There is no floating tooltip. Above the panels sits a pinned two-line header, and while you scrub, the
**upper line is the day-sheet sentence covering that moment**:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Mo 25.08. 14:31 · Tag 41                          │
│ „Um 14:12 war es 41 Minuten lang über 28 °C."     │
│ 28,4 °C   54 %   VPD 1,64   Licht an              │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Scrubbing the chart reads the diary aloud, in effect. It is the same ranked sentence set that the day sheet
uses, so the annotation rail and the diary can never disagree — one generator, two renderings. The rail
itself is 16 px, under the x-axis, clustered at 10 px bins with count badges, max two rows (row 1 human,
row 2 machine), with full-height dashed lines only for stage changes.

**Mobile rules:** one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap or long-press
sets the crosshair. Axis labels are never hidden — below 320 px they drop to three ticks and the unit moves
into the panel title.

**Correctness:** time-in-range and excursion duration are computed **server-side on raw Influx samples**,
never on `aggregateWindow(fn:mean)` output. Averaging destroys the excursions the number exists to count,
and the error is invisible by eye. This is a prerequisite for the whole detector layer, not a chart detail.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New collections (all cloud-side Mongo, all optional fields, no migration tooling required)

```ts
DaySheet {
  _id, device_id: string,
  date: string,              // 'YYYY-MM-DD' in tz
  tz: string,                // 'Europe/Berlin' — explicit; a 12/12 flip near DST drifts otherwise
  run_id?: string, dayOfRun?: number,
  sentences: Sentence[],
  coverage: number,          // 0..1 telemetry coverage of the day
  closedAt?: number,         // set 03:00 local next day
  generatorVersion: number,
  imageIds: string[]         // the 2 Tagesbilder
}

Sentence {
  sentence_id: string,
  detector: string,          // 'photoperiod' | 'excursion' | 'care' | 'human' | 'quiet' | …
  kind: 'observed' | 'inferred' | 'human',
  template: string,          // i18n key; NEVER prose
  params: Record<string, string|number>,
  facts: Fact[],
  score: number,             // 0..100
  at: number, atEnd?: number,
  status?: 'pending'|'confirmed'|'corrected'|'lapsed',   // inferred only
  confirmedBy?: string, confirmedAt?: number,
  humanText?: string,        // kind:'human' or a correction
  image_ids?: string[], plant_ids?: string[]
}

Fact { key, value, unit, measure, from, to, agg: 'mean'|'min'|'max'|'sum'|'duration' }

Run   { run_id, device_id, label, startedAt, endedAt?, stage, regime_id?, mediumLabel? }
Plant { plant_id, device_id, run_id, label, strain?, startedAt, endedAt?, archived? }
Condition { condition_id, device_id, text, openedBy, openedAt, closedBy?, closedAt?, plant_ids? }
Attendee  { attendee_id, device_id, name, addedBy, active }
```

`DeviceLog` is not replaced. It stays exactly as it is and remains the raw substrate behind
`Alle N Ereignisse`. C21 adds a layer above it; it does not migrate it. `convertEventsToGrowCycles()`
becomes a **one-time backfill** that mints `Run` rows from historical lifecycle entries and generates
back-dated day sheets — so an existing customer opens the new app and finds a year of diary already
written. That backfill is the single most persuasive demo in this concept.

### 6.2 The generator

A server job, `dayseet.service.ts`, running (a) every 15 min appending to today's open sheet and (b) at
03:00 local closing yesterday's. Each detector is a small pure function returning `Sentence[]` with scores.

| Detector | Fires on | Base score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `human` | any user note, care event, photo | **always included, unscored** |
| `condition.open` | an open Condition | **always first** |
| `stage` | lifecycle transition | 95 |
| `photoperiod.flip` | photoperiod changes ≥ 2 h vs the 3-day median of `out_light` | 92 |
| `offline` | gap > `ONLINE_TIMEOUT` | 90 |
| `camera.silent` | no accepted frame in 6 h of photoperiod | 88 |
| `excursion` | raw samples outside band, ≥ 15 min | 40 + min(45, minutes/2) |
| `output.saturated` | a role's duty > 80 % over ≥ 2 h | 55 |
| `care.due` | feed regime step due, none logged | 60 |
| `climate.summary` | always, if coverage ≥ 0.3 | 35 |
| `light.asPlanned` | photoperiod matched schedule | 30 |
| `quiet` | **only if nothing above scored > 50** | 20 |

Selection: all `human` and `condition` sentences, then top-scored machine sentences until the budget of
**six** is reached, with a floor — a sentence scoring under 25 is dropped even if there is room. Repetition
guard: a detector that produced the identical template three days running is suppressed on day four unless
its score rose by 20 (so "Der Entfeuchter lief durch" does not become wallpaper).

`quiet` matters more than it looks. **A diary that only writes when something goes wrong makes an ordinary
day look like a bug.** "Ruhiger Tag. Nichts außer der Reihe." is the most reassuring sentence in the
product and one of the reasons the owner's promise is worth anything.

### 6.3 Observed vs inferred — the confirm loop

The machine **asserts what it measured** and **asks about what it inferred**. That line is the whole
confirmation design.

- *"Licht ging um 06:00 an"* — measured. No button. No question mark.
- *"Die Blüte hat begonnen"* — inferred from 4 days of 12/12. Rendered in a dotted-rule block,
  prefixed `Vermutet:`, with `[ Stimmt ]` and `[ Ändern ]`.

`[ Stimmt ]` → `status:'confirmed'`, the `Vermutet:` prefix and the buttons disappear, and the inference's
side effect is applied (here: `Run.stage = 'flowering'`, which the recipe/stage machinery already consumes
via `logStageTransitionIfChanged`). `[ Ändern ]` → an action sheet of the six `DiaryLifecycleStage` values
plus *"stimmt nicht"*. Ignored for 7 days → `status:'lapsed'`, the sentence survives in the text as
*"Vermutet (unbestätigt): …"* and stops asking.

**Hard cap: at most one pending inference per day sheet, ever.** A page that asks you two questions is a
form, and this concept exists to not be a form.

### 6.4 Multi-plant, and how ignoring it costs nothing

With **zero plants**, the word *Pflanze* never appears anywhere. `＋ Gießen` writes
`CareEvent { plant_ids: [] }` and the sentence renders *"Du hast um 09:30 gegossen, 4 l."*

Plants come into existence by typing a name in the capture sheet — there is no plant screen to visit first,
no setup step, no onboarding question. The chip row in `＋ Gießen` starts as `[ Alle ]` and grows a
`[ ＋ ]` that accepts free text (`A1`, `Blaue`, `die im Eck`). The moment a second name exists, plant chips
appear in the capture sheet and `A1–A4` appears in the sentences. That is **reveal by data**, and it is the
only gate: the club with 30 plants and the balcony grower with one pot use one identical sheet.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

### 7.1 Capture — tap counts

**The repeat case: two taps.**

```
Tap 1: ＋ Gießen  (home screen, 48 px, fixed position)

┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Gießen                          [ ✕ ]    │
│                                          │
│ Wie letztes Mal:                         │
│ [ Alle 4 Pflanzen ]  [ 4 l ]             │
│ [ Bio-Bloom 2 ml/l ] [ Bio-Grow 1 ml/l ] │
│ [ + Wert ]        ← EC, pH, Notiz, Foto  │
│                                          │
│ Wer? [ Ich ] [ Anna ] [ Ben ]            │  only if ≥2 Attendees
│                                          │
│      [      Speichern      ]             │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘

Tap 2: Speichern.
```

Every chip is tappable to change; nothing must be. **Tap counts, honestly:**

| Case | Taps |
| --- | --- |
| Repeat exactly what you did last time | **2** |
| Same, but a different volume | 4 (chip → number pad → done → save) |
| Water only, no feed (chips already off) | 3 |
| First ever entry, with a regime selected | 2 (regime prefills everything) |
| First ever entry, no regime | 4 (volume, save, + one plant name if wanted) |
| Full club entry: 3 plants, 2 products, EC, pH, actor, photo | 11 |

The stoner path is two taps and the club path is eleven, **on the same sheet** — the difference is that the
club taps the chips and the stoner does not. No mode, no "detailed entry" button.

### 7.2 The double-feed guard

At sheet open, the client asks `GET /care/recent?device_id&plants&within=12h`. If any selected plant has a
`type:'feed'` event inside the window from **any** actor, the sheet changes shape:

```
│ ⚠ Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.  │
│                                           │
│    [   Trotzdem speichern   ]             │   ← primary turns warning-coloured
│    [ Nur Wasser ]  [ Abbrechen ]          │
```

The save button *changes identity* rather than a dialog appearing on top of it — so the deliberate extra
tap is unavoidable but the flow is not interrupted. Windows: 12 h for feed, 6 h for water, 0 for foliar.
Configurable per device, one number, in `/lauf`.

### 7.3 Schedule following

`FeedRegime` is **medium + feed line together**, named the way growers say it out loud:

```ts
FeedRegime {
  regime_id, label,              // "Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom"
  mediumLabel, feedLabel,
  preFertilised: 'none'|'light'|'heavy',
  steps: [{ fromDayOfRun, toDayOfRun, stage?, products: [{name, amount, unit:'ml'|'g', per:'l'}],
            ec?, ecBasis?: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source', note? }],
  source_url, retrieved_at, verifiedAt
}
```

Selected once in `/lauf`. All-Mix ships with `preFertilised:'heavy'` and an empty first step carrying
`note: 'nicht düngen'` — so the regime can express *"All-Mix, also noch nicht düngen"*, which is the whole
point of modelling substrate. BioTabs ships as a regime whose steps are re-tabbing every 4–6 weeks and
almost no liquid feed.

The regime does exactly two things and no more:
1. **Prefills the capture sheet** with the current step's products and amounts. Always editable.
2. **Produces the `care.due` sentence** — *"Nach dem Schema wäre heute wieder Düngen dran (Schritt 6)."*
   with a `[ Erledigt ]` that opens the prefilled sheet.

It is not a planner, not a calculator, not a compliance engine. Steps advance by **feed events**
(`Run.regimeStep++`), not wall-clock weeks, with a soft calendar mapping — growers feed Tuesday and Friday
and slip.

Legal posture, invisible in the UI: brand names are plain text labels only, never in a Terp Control feature
name, never with a logo or a brand colour; the numbers live in a Mongo content collection with
`source_url` + `retrieved_at` so any brand comes out in one DB update; the sheet shows
`zuletzt geprüft: 03/2026`, which doubles as a genuine trust signal.

### 7.4 Offline capture

`CareEvent.client_event_id` is a UUID minted **when the sheet opens**, not when it saves. The server upserts
on `{device_id, client_event_id}`. A retry after a cellar dropout is idempotent — and in a shared record a
duplicated retry is indistinguishable from a real double feed, so this is a safety fix, not a nicety. A
service worker is *not* in v1 scope; what ships is the idempotency key plus an in-memory retry with a
visible `Nicht gespeichert — erneut versuchen` chip, so the failure is at least honest.

### 7.5 The cumulative question — `/pflege`

```
Bilanz · Lauf „Northern Lights" · Tag 41

Pro Produkt          gesamt      pro Pflanze
Bio-Grow             182 ml      45,5 ml
Bio-Bloom             96 ml      24,0 ml
Wasser               142 l       35,5 l

Pro Pflanze          Wasser   Bio-Grow   Bio-Bloom   zuletzt
A1                   38 l     48 ml      26 ml       vor 2 Tagen
A2                   36 l     46 ml      24 ml       vor 2 Tagen
A3                   32 l     42 ml      22 ml       vor 6 Tagen  ⚠
A4                   36 l     46 ml      24 ml       vor 2 Tagen

```

"How much Bio-Bloom has A3 had this cycle, and from whom" is one screen and one aggregation query. A3's

sentence: *"A3 ist seit 6 Tagen nicht gegossen worden, die anderen vor 2 Tagen."*

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is not a tile in C21. It is **the illustration of the diary and one of its authors**.

### 8.1 What ships in v1: Tagesbilder and Der Lauffilm

**Tagesbilder.** Each day sheet carries exactly two frames: one from photoperiod-midpoint, one from
90 min before lights-off. Not "the latest picture" — *chosen* frames. Choosing two out of ~2,800 stills is
the primary culling act in the product, and it is what makes the page look like a diary rather than a
dashboard.

**Der Lauffilm.** One mp4 per `Run`, rebuilt nightly, composed of **three frames per day** (lights-on +1 h,
photoperiod midpoint, lights-off −1 h). A 90-day grow → 270 frames → **10.8 s at 25 fps**. That is a clip
someone can actually send to a friend, unlike the existing rolling 1d/1w/1m windows, which have no relation
to a grow at all. The existing three durations stay, unchanged, for the chart's scrubber.

The existing `compressRtspStreamRange` machinery is reused verbatim with a fourth call — a run-scoped range
with a per-day frame selection instead of a minimum interval. This is genuinely additive: ~80 lines in
`image.service.ts`.

### 8.2 Culling and normalisation — the specifics

Applied at build time, in order, with `sharp` (already a dependency, `.stats()` gives per-channel mean and
entropy):

1. **Dark reject.** Mean luma < 20/255 → drop, unless the frame is the deliberate night slot.
2. **Lamp/luma contradiction.** If `out_light` averaged over the preceding 5 min is > 0.5 but the frame's
   mean luma is < 20 → the *camera* is the problem, not the tent. Drop the frame and raise the
   `camera.blind` finding.
3. **Intrusion reject.** Mean-absolute-difference vs the last accepted frame > 25 % → a hand, the door, a
   person. Drop. **Two consecutive rejects re-baseline** — otherwise a real permanent change (defoliation,
   a moved pot) would reject every frame forever after.
4. **Dedup.** MAD < 0.5 % vs the last accepted frame → drop as near-identical.
5. **Exposure normalisation.** Target the middle-50 % luma of the accepted set to a common value via
   `sharp().linear(a, b)` per frame. Twelve weeks of drifting auto-exposure stop flickering.
6. **White balance.** Grey-world channel gains, **clamped to ±15 %**. A magenta LED tent is genuinely
   magenta and over-correcting turns it green; the clamp keeps the correction to drift, not to the light.
7. **No stabilisation in v1.** `vidstab` is not built into the container's ffmpeg and needs a two-pass
   encode. Deferred, and said so plainly.

Normalisation is applied **only to the film**. Stored stills are never modified — the evidence must stay
the evidence.

### 8.3 How a video leaves the product

This is a design object, not an assumption. Today's `getDeviceImage` sends `video/mp4` with
`Cache-Control` and **no `Content-Disposition`**, and the encoder is `libx265 -crf 30`. **HEVC in mp4 does
not play in Chrome or Firefox on the desktop**, so today's timelapse cannot be shared with anyone who is
not already using the app in a supporting browser. That is a shipped defect, verified in
`image.service.ts:567-586`, and C21 fixes it as part of the camera feature rather than as cleanup.

```
GET /image/:device_id/film?run_id=<id>&size=1080&download=1
→ 200
  Content-Type: video/mp4
  Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_tag-001-041_2026-08-25.mp4"
  Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600
```

- **Codec:** `libx264`, High profile, level 4.0, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-crf 22`, `-movflags +faststart`.
  Plays in every browser, every phone gallery, Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp.
- **Sizes:** `1080` (long edge 1080) and `720`. 720 exists because Telegram's by-URL limit is small and a
  10 s 720p clip lands around 3–6 MB.
- **Filename:** `terpcontrol_<device-slug>_tag-<from>-<to>_<builddate>.mp4`. It sorts, it says which grow,
  it says which days.
- **Optional burned-in caption** (default on): bottom-left, ffmpeg `drawtext`,
  `Tag 41 · 24,3 °C · 58 %` per frame from that day's sheet. This is the single most important detail in
  the camera feature: **it is the only way image correlation survives leaving the product.** A film with
  the numbers in the picture is still evidence in a Telegram group; a film without them is a wobbly plant.
- The `/film` page offers `[ Video speichern ]` (the download), `[ Link teilen ]` (an existing `ShareLink`
  with `page:'diary'`, `webcam:true`), and `[ Für Telegram ]` (720 + the caption pre-checked).

### 8.4 The camera alarm

`camera.silent`: no *accepted* frame for 6 h while `out_light > 0`. It writes a `DeviceLog` at
`severity: 1` with `categories: ['webcam','error']` — reusing the existing alarm path, so the existing
e-mail/webhook actions carry it — and it becomes the day sheet's top sentence:
*"Die Kamera hat seit 14:20 kein brauchbares Bild geliefert."* Note the word *brauchbares*: a camera
returning corrupt frames (`FFMPEG_CORRUPT_FRAME_PATTERN` already detects this) is silent for the diary's
purposes even though the stream is technically alive. A camera that stopped three days into flower now
tells you within six hours.

### 8.5 Image correlation

Three bindings, all cheap because every `Image` already carries a `timestamp`:

1. **Fact drawer → frame.** Every Fact drawer offers `[ Bild um 14:31 ]` — the still nearest that Fact's
   peak. Numbers acquire pictures.
2. **Chart → frame + sentence.** The `/verlauf` scrubber drives a thumbnail rail positioned by timestamp on
   the same x-scale (so uneven capture reads honestly as gaps), delivered as **one sprite + JSON index per
   window** rather than N requests. The pinned header shows the sentence; the rail shows the picture.
3. **Frame → numbers.** Tapping any still in `/film` opens it full-screen with the day sheet's sentence for
   that hour and the four values at that timestamp beneath it, and `[ Zum Verlauf ]`.

The unifying rule: **the picture, the number and the sentence share one time cursor and are never more than
one tap apart in either direction.**

## 9. Clubs

### 9.1 Attribution without an auth rewrite

`Attendee { attendee_id, device_id, name, addedBy, active }` — names the owner types once in `/lauf`. No
accounts, no invitations, no e-mail, no `auth.middleware` surgery. The picker appears in the capture sheet
**only when the device has two or more attendees** (reveal by data). The selection is stamped onto
`CareEvent.actor_id` + `actorName` and onto `Sentence.confirmedBy`, and it renders in the prose:
*"Anna hat um 18:20 A1–A3 gedüngt: Bio-Bloom 2 ml/l."*

This is attribution, and it is honest about being only that. **Authorisation is a gate and C21 does not
build one in v1**: everyone at the tent uses one login, and the day sheet says so in `/lauf`:
*"Namen sind Beschriftungen, keine Zugänge. Alle am Zelt teilen einen Login."* Real memberships are an
`auth.middleware.ts:172/:207` rewrite plus ~10 owner-scoped queries plus an account-lookup endpoint, and
they do not fit before October. `actor_id` is designed to be upgradeable to a real `user_id` later without
a migration — the field is already there and already populated.

### 9.2 The double feed

Section 7.2. It is the most expensive thing that happens in a shared room and it is the one place where
C21 deliberately adds friction.

### 9.3 Standing conditions

`Condition` is the pinned note on the tent door: an **open fact with an owner**, not an event and not a
task.

```
⚠ Offen seit 3 Tagen: CO₂-Flasche fast leer
  angelegt von Anna · 22.08.  [ Erledigt ]
```

Created via `＋ Notiz` → `[ Als offenen Punkt ]`. Open conditions pin above the day sheet **and** are
repeated as the first sentence of every day sheet until closed, so a member reading Thursday's page cannot
miss what Anna wrote on Monday. Closing writes *"Ben hat erledigt: CO₂-Flasche fast leer (3 Tage offen)."*
A self-writing diary is unusually good at this: the condition's whole lifecycle is prose without anyone
writing prose.

### 9.4 Privacy posture

- **Nicknames only.** Never a real name, never a birth year, never a membership number, never an address.
  The `Attendee` field label reads `Name oder Kürzel`.
- **No per-member dispensing records, ever.** § 26 KCanG's hand-over ledger drags this into Art. 9 GDPR
  territory for data a grow controller has no need for. That is a refusal, not a backlog item.
- **Harvest weight is opt-in and off by default**, and the `harvest` sentence renders without a number
  unless the user typed one. Plant counts are never inferred into a sentence.
- **Export** (`/pflege` → `Exportieren`) is day sheets + care events as JSON and CSV, date-ranged, with a
  `Namen: vollständig / Kürzel / anonym` selector **defaulting to Kürzel**.
- **No community feed.** A share link you can paste in Telegram is not a feed anyone must moderate.
- Terp Control does not become anyone's system of record for § 26 and ships no compliance badge.

## 10. Old devices (type 7)

A `fan`, `light` or `plug` owner opens the app and gets **the same home screen** — a day sheet, a run
header without a run, the freshness line, scroll-back. Detectors are selected by what the device actually
reports, which is the same capability probe the app already does (`device_type` + `hardwareInfo` +
`parseSocketRoles`).

**A `plug`:**
```
Dienstag, 26. August

Die Steckdose lief heute 6 Std 12 Min, in 4 Abschnitten.
Der längste ging von 03:10 bis 05:44.

Alle 12 Ereignisse an diesem Tag ›
```
Detectors active: `runtime.summary`, `output.saturated`, `offline`, `quiet`, `human`.
Absent: everything climate, plant, care, camera. `＋ Gießen` **does not exist** — not disabled, not greyed:
absent. `＋ Notiz` and `＋ Foto` remain, because a note about a heater is as valid as a note about a plant.

**A `light`:**
```
Licht 18/6, keine Abweichung. An 06:00, aus 00:00.
Dimmung heute unverändert bei 80 %.
```
Plus `light.deviation` when the schedule and the measured `out_light` disagree — which is a failed
contactor, and which no competitor surfaces at all.

**A `fan`:** rpm summary, `offline`, `quiet`.

What does **not** change for these users: `/device/:id/settings` still `ngSwitch`es to `fan-settings` /
`light-settings` / `plug-settings`, byte-for-byte. C21 adds a reading surface; it does not touch their
settings forms, their alarms, or their share links. The regression risk to type 7 is confined to the home
route, and the old overview components are kept reachable at `/device/:id/klassisch` for one release as an
escape hatch — the only concession of the kind in this concept, justified because these are existing paying
customers and October is six weeks away.

**The honest observation:** a plug's day sheet is boring. Two hundred consecutive days of "lief 6 Std
12 Min" is repetitive — and it is also a duty-cycle record of a heater that nobody has ever had before, and
it is *free*. The `quiet` detector and the repetition guard keep it from being noise.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**Type 1 — Monitor only.** No sockets, so no output sentences, no `output.saturated`, no dehumidifier tile
that lies. Their day sheet is `climate.summary` + `excursion` + `quiet`, which is exactly the "record the
values and look at them, after some time" ask, except the looking is now reading. Scrolling back three
weeks costs no interpretation, and the Fact drawer gives them the number when they want it. They never see
a control they do not have, because a detector without an actuator produces no sentence.

**Type 2 — Monitor + camera.** The best-served type in C21. Their day sheet has two chosen frames and a
sentence; their `/film` has a run film with the temperature burned into the corner and a download button
that produces a real mp4 with a real filename. The correlation the owner names is not a feature they must
find — it is the default rendering of every page they look at. `camera.silent` means a dead camera three
days into flower costs them six hours, not three weeks.

**Type 3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Detectors are per-role, derived from
`hardwareInfo.sockets`, not from the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` that fails open today. A
heater-only tent gets `output.saturated` for the heater and **no humidity sentence at all** — not a warning
it cannot act on. The one thing they care about, "did my heater keep up last night", becomes
*"Die Heizung lief heute Nacht 7 von 12 Stunden."* — which is a diary sentence and a diagnosis at once.

**Type 4 — Full tent.** Gets the most sentences, and hits the concept's ceiling fastest: with five socket
roles and one PWM output there is only so much to narrate, and `dehumidifier` doubling as the cooler means
one sentence cannot distinguish heat removal from moisture removal. C21 renders the role by its configured
workmode meaning (*Entfeuchter* vs *Kühlung*) rather than pretending, and says so in the drawer's
provenance line. The six-sentence budget is a real constraint for this user — they are the one most likely
to open `Alle 47 Ereignisse`.

**Type 5 — Closed loop / DIY.** The Fact drawer is their entry point: measure name, raw sample count, window
bounds, CSV in one long-press. The day sheet's JSON is the API — `GET /daysheet/:device_id?from&to` returns
templates and params, not rendered prose, which means it is machine-readable by construction and someone
can render it in Home Assistant. C21 does not give them a write API and does not pretend to. It gives them
a documented, stable, non-prose event stream that is strictly better than scraping `DeviceLog`.

**Type 6 — Clubs.** Attribution in the prose, the double-feed guard at the moment it matters, the Bilanz
answering the cumulative question, and `Condition` for the note on the door. The day-per-page granularity
is the friction point: a club with three visits a day reads a page, not a feed, and some will want the feed.
`Alle N Ereignisse` is that feed, but it is one tap below where they would put it.

**Type 7 — Old devices.** Section 10. Same app, fewer sentences, settings untouched, an escape hatch for
one release.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner*: five sentences, three fat buttons, nothing moves. *Normal*: a page a day
with two photos, which reads like Instagram made of their own tent and beats a dashboard on the one axis
they judge by — is it nice to look at. *Techie*: every number is dotted-underlined and two gestures from
raw; density is available everywhere and demanded nowhere.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026

Six to eight weeks, one to two developers, a hardware date that does not move.

### Ships in v1

1. **Prerequisite fixes** (~1 week, unavoidable): server-side pagination + `?since=` on `getDeviceLogs`
   (the generator reads logs constantly); the Flux injection fix and a real result bound in
   `data.service.ts`; a raw-sample excursion endpoint; the `deleted`-flag semantics documented so
   "Clear logs" stops being a data-loss trap on the app's centrepiece.
2. **DaySheet generator**, 12 detectors, 15-min append + 03:00 close, `generatorVersion` stamped.
3. **Home screen**: Jetzt-Zeile with real freshness/offline states, today's sheet, infinite scroll back,
   `Alle N Ereignisse` reusing `LogEntryViewerModule` unchanged.
4. **The Fact drawer** — one component, one ECharts sparkline, CSV, chart deep-link, nearest frame.
5. **Confirm loop** — one pending inference per sheet, `Stimmt` / `Ändern` / lapse.
6. **CareEvent** + the 2-tap capture sheet + double-feed guard + `client_event_id` idempotency.
7. **Attendee** picker (appears at ≥2 names) and **Condition** (offene Punkte).
8. **Run + Plant**, created implicitly; `/lauf` for label, stage, regime, guard windows.
9. **FeedRegime**: five seeded regimes in Mongo (Biobizz Light-Mix, Biobizz All-Mix, BioTabs, Green House
   Feeding, "kein Schema") with `source_url` + `retrieved_at`; prefill + `care.due` sentence.
10. **Camera**: Tagesbild selection, culling rules 1–4 + luma/lamp cross-check, exposure normalisation,
    nightly run film, **H.264 + faststart + `Content-Disposition` download with a real filename**,
    burned-in caption, `camera.silent` detector, `/film` page.
11. **`/pflege`** with the Bilanz and JSON/CSV export with the anonymisation selector.
12. **`/verlauf`**: ECharts 6 directive + small multiples + setpoint line + target band + day/night
    `markArea` + output lane + annotation rail + text ribbon.
13. **Backfill**: `convertEventsToGrowCycles` becomes a one-time job minting `Run`s and back-dated sheets.
14. **de + en** for ~45 sentence templates and ~120 UI keys, in the same commit.

### Deliberately not in v1

- **The old charts page is not deleted.** `/device/:id/charts` stays, with its query-string format and every
  share link and `ChartPreset` intact. `/verlauf` is new and additive. Rewriting a 1205-line component
  *and* shipping everything above in eight weeks is not credible; the old page is deleted in 1.1 once
  `applyViewParams` has a proven back-compat path.
- **The brand feed-chart library.** Five regimes ship; twenty do not, and neither does anything before
  German IP counsel has looked at UWG § 4 Nr. 3 and UrhG §§ 87a–87e.
- **Real multi-user login.** Attribution ships; authorisation does not.
- **Service worker / offline capture.** The idempotency key ships so that adding it later is safe.
- **Timelapse stabilisation** (`vidstab`, two-pass), per-plant crops, and any face/person model.
- **Public diary URL, PDF export, Telegram/Discord posting.** The download and the existing `ShareLink`
  cover October; the generator's template/params JSON makes a post generator trivial in 1.1.
- **Retention and downsampling policy.** Named as required (see weakness 5), not designed here.

### The riskiest item

The ECharts migration and the day-sheet generator both land in the same eight weeks. If one must slip, slip
`/verlauf` to a single-panel chart with the target band and no small multiples — the generator is the
product and the chart is its evidence, not the other way round.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **Generated German is a quality cliff, and there is no LLM in this stack.** Forty-five templates
   composed against real data produce sentences nobody wrote. *"Um 14:12 war es 41 Minuten lang über
   28 °C"* reads well; the two-hundredth combination may not. Mitigations — the two-clause rule, a native
   speaker reviewing every template against ten real days of simulated data, the repetition guard — reduce
   the risk and do not remove it. If the prose is bad the concept is worthless, because the prose *is* the
   concept. This is the biggest bet in the document.

2. **One page per day is wrong for the club with three visits a day.** They want a chronological feed and
   C21 gives them a page whose sentence order is by score, not by clock. `Alle N Ereignisse` is the feed
   but it is one tap below where they would put it, and a member who fed at 08:00 sees her entry as
   sentence four of a page that is still being written. Some clubs will find this backwards.

3. **The scoring is invisible and will feel arbitrary.** "Why didn't it mention my defoliation?" — it did,
   because human sentences are never scored, but the equivalent machine question ("why didn't it mention
   the humidity spike?") has an answer the user cannot see. There is no "why this sentence" affordance in
   v1 and adding one starts to look like a settings screen for a diary.

4. **Rewriting a page the user already read is uncomfortable.** A closed sheet reopens and regenerates when
   late-arriving buffered device data lifts `coverage` by more than 5 % — which is a real case, the
   `message-buffer-overflow` path. Yesterday's diary changing overnight is unsettling in a way that a log
   never is. The mitigation (`Ergänzt am 26.08.` footer) is a plaster on a genuine conceptual wound: prose
   implies finality and telemetry does not.

5. **Server cost with no retention policy underneath it.** Excursion detection reads raw Influx samples per
   device per day. At 150 units that is trivial; at 5,000 it is a real bill, and today the bucket has no
   retention and no downsample task at all. C21 makes an unbounded-storage problem load-bearing without
   fixing it.

6. **It gives the "now" one line.** The product is sold as a *controller* — "Messen. Regeln. Ruhig
   schlafen." — and C21 spends its whole home screen on the past. Types 3, 4 and 5 who adjust setpoints
   daily get an unchanged, unloved settings page reached through a `⋯`. This concept does nothing for
   *Regeln*, and a critic will say the diary tail is wagging the controller dog.

7. **Normalisation can make the film look worse.** Grey-world white balance under magenta LEDs drifts
   green; the ±15 % clamp is a guess that needs footage to tune. A second unprocessed encode is the safety
   net and it doubles the encode cost. Intrusion rejection at 25 % MAD will also occasionally drop a real
   change (a plant falling over) and will occasionally keep a hand.

8. **The day counter needs a run, and runs are implicit.** A user who never confirms a stage and never names
   a run gets calendar dates instead of "Tag 41" — a visible downgrade the machine cannot fix by itself,
   and the one place where the "95 % automatic" promise leans on a human action.

9. **The escape hatch is a smell.** `/device/:id/klassisch` for type 7 is a second interface by another
   name for one release. It is defensible (existing customers, six weeks, no regression budget) and it is
   still the thing this round's brief warns about. It must be deleted in 1.1 and someone must actually
   delete it.
