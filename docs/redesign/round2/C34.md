**Family:** unify · **Round 2** · Written against NEW-BRIEF.md, 00-DOSSIER.md, 00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md,
00-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md and direct reads of `/home/user/terpcontrol.cloud`.

## 1. Thesis

**The app is not a set of features or pages. It is a curated list of plain-language German questions the
product can answer, and every answer is one document whose depth runs downward — a sentence, then the
evidence, then the workings.**

Nobody opens a grow app to "use the charts page". They open it holding a question: *is my tent OK, what
changed, when do I feed, why is it 29 degrees, what did we give A3, how did this look last time.* Today
Terp Control makes you translate that question into a route, a measure list, a timespan and an interval,
and then answer it yourself by looking at fifteen translucent areas with the axes hidden. C34 removes the
translation step: the question is the navigation, and the answer is the content.

The move that makes this serve everybody at once is not the question list — it is the **fixed shape of
every answer**. Three parts, always in the same order, always on one scroll, never behind a toggle:
**Die Antwort** (one German sentence and a number), **Woran man es sieht** (the evidence: the chart with
the goal drawn on it, the picture, the list), **Die Rechnung** (the workings: the window, the setpoint
used, the coverage percentage, the raw counts, the exact HTTP request, a copy button). Depth is
*spatial*, not *stateful*. The stoned user reads sentence one and is finished. The techie's thumb keeps
going. Neither of them chose anything, nothing was hidden, and the same URL renders identically for both.

## 2. Information architecture

Six routes. Everything that is *content* is an answer.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | **Zelte.** One card per device: name, freshness dot, and the single top question with its one-line answer. With exactly one device it redirects into that device (today's behaviour, kept). |
| `/device/:id` | **Die Fragen.** The home screen. The question list. |
| `/device/:id/frage/:qid` | **Die Antwort.** One answer, always the three-part template. Query params carry window/selection and are shareable. |
| `/device/:id/einstellungen` | **Was das Zelt tun soll.** The one non-question surface: settings. For `fan`/`light`/`plug`/`fridge` this loads the *existing untouched* settings components. |
| `/device/:id/charts` | **Back-compat alias.** Renders `frage/wie-lief-es` and feeds the legacy query string through the existing `applyViewParams`. Every share link and `ChartPreset` in circulation keeps working. |
| `/account`, `/shares`, `/login`, `/demo`, `/link-expired`, `/connection-error` | Unchanged. |

`/device/:id/diary`, `/testmode` and `/diagnostics` are kept as redirects
(`diary → frage/was-ist-passiert`, `testmode → einstellungen?testmode=1`, `/diagnostics` unchanged,
admin-only).

**Navigation model.** The existing `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` stays. Inside a device there is exactly
one navigation gesture: **tap a question, get an answer, press back.** No tab bar, no FAB, no hierarchy
deeper than two. On a wide screen the split-pane's second pane holds the question list permanently and
answers render on the right — the big-screen advantage the dossier calls unexploited, for free.

**The question catalogue is closed, curated and translated.** It is not generated, and there is no
free-text input anywhere. Each question is a `QuestionSpec` declared in TypeScript:

```ts
interface QuestionSpec {
  id: string;                                   // 'ist-alles-in-ordnung'
  key: string;                                  // i18n key -> de.json / en.json
  order: number;                                // fixed position in the body list
  available(ctx: DeviceContext): 'yes' | 'locked' | 'no';
  urgency(ctx: DeviceContext): Promise<Urgency>; // {level: 0|1|2, line: string, action?: QuickAction}
  answer: Type<AnswerComponent>;                // lazy-loaded answer component
}
```

`available()` returns `'no'` when the question is *meaningless* (no plants logged → no plant questions;
`device_type === 'plug'` → no VPD question) and the row does not exist. It returns `'locked'` only when
the answer is unlockable by acquiring hardware — **exactly two cases: the camera and the CO₂ sensor** —
and the row renders greyed with the reason. That distinction is the whole rule and it is written down so
it cannot drift.

**v1 catalogue (11 questions).** German is canonical; the English is written independently, not translated.

| # | Frage | Question | Available when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ist alles in Ordnung? | Is everything OK? | always |
| 2 | Wie sieht es gerade aus? | What does it look like right now? | camera paired (else `locked`) |
| 3 | Was hat sich verändert? | What has changed? | ≥ 3 days of data |
| 4 | Was ist passiert? | What happened? | always |
| 5 | Wann muss ich düngen? | When do I feed? | a feed regime is selected |
| 6 | Was hat *A3* bekommen? | What has *A3* had? | ≥ 1 plant exists |

| 8 | Was macht die Technik? | What is the kit doing? | ≥ 1 output exists |

| 10 | Wie war das letzte Mal? | How was it last time? | ≥ 2 runs exist |
| 11 | Was steht an? | What is open? | always |

Plus two that only appear when true, at the very top: **„Warum ist das Zelt offline?"** and
**„Warum kommen keine Bilder mehr?"**

**Three fixed zones.**

- **Kopf.** The latest still, the age of the reading, and three numbers. With no camera the still is
  replaced by a three-up value row of the same height (`value-display` gauges, which already render `—`
  for missing values). Tapping the head opens question 2.
- **Zone A „JETZT".** At most three cards, only questions whose `urgency().level > 0`. Empty is a valid
  and common state: then the section header disappears and the head sits directly above FRAGEN. When
  something is in Zone A, its Zone-B row shows `↑ oben` instead of its answer line — **the row never
  disappears from the place you learned it is.**
- **Zone B „FRAGEN".** Every available question, in `order`, forever. Each row is question text left,
  one-line answer right, in `--tc-text-*` colour by level. This list is the spatial anchor of the whole
  product.
- **Sticky bottom bar.** `Gegossen` · `Notiz` · `Foto`. Three capture actions that are in the same place
  at 2 a.m. on every device type, every account, every state. This is the floor under the impaired user.

A search field („Frage suchen") appears above FRAGEN only once the catalogue exceeds 12 rows — which
happens when you own a camera *and* log plants *and* have two runs. It filters; it does not hide.

followed by the same evidence and workings. An honest non-answer is a first-class answer.

## 4. THE NO-MODES MECHANISM

**The mechanism is that depth is a scroll position, not a state.**

Every answer page is one document with three headed sections, all always rendered, in this order:

```
DIE ANTWORT      one sentence + the decisive number.        ~2 lines
WORAN MAN ES SIEHT  the evidence: chart, picture, list.     ~1 screen
DIE RECHNUNG     the workings: window, setpoint, coverage,  ~1 screen
                 raw counts, formula, the exact request.
```

There is **no accordion, no "Erweitert", no toggle, no preference, no remembered level.** `DIE RECHNUNG`
has a heading and a top border and sits below the fold. That is all that separates it from the verdict.

Why this is not a mode, stated as a falsifiable test:

> **Two people, same account, same second, same URL: can they see different content?**
> In C34, no. Not for any question, any device, any state.

Compare the banned patterns: `localStorage['app-settings-expert']` (persisted per-browser state — a
mode), an "Advanced" accordion (per-section persisted state — a mode), an onboarding "what kind of user
are you?" (a mode with a friendly face). C34 persists **zero** presentation state. Scroll position is not
persisted between visits either; every answer opens at the top, at the verdict, every time.

Four secondary mechanisms, all from the brief's allowed list, all working on the same surface:

1. **Reveal by capability.** `available()` reads `parseSocketRoles(hardwareInfo.sockets)` **per role**,
   not through the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` that fails open. A heater-only tent has no
   humidity question, no humidity target row, and no dehumidifier alarm preset — anywhere. Old firmware
   that reports no `sockets` key **fails closed to monitoring** and shows one Zone-A card: „Welche
   Geräte hängen dran?" opening the wizard's `connections` step. (This is a behaviour change for old
   devices and is deliberate: today they are shown controls they cannot action.)
2. **Reveal by data.** Zero plants → questions 6 and the plant chips in every capture sheet do not exist.
   One run → question 10 does not exist. The user who never logs a plant is never taxed by plants.
3. **Progressive precision.** The number is next to the verdict in `DIE ANTWORT`; the series is in
   `WORAN MAN ES SIEHT`; the raw sample count, the coverage and the query are in `DIE RECHNUNG`. Nobody
   is denied the number; nobody is made to read it.
4. **Bounded ranked density.** Zone A reorders; it is capped at 3 and every card it holds leaves a
   labelled ghost row in its permanent Zone-B position. Zone B never reorders. The capture bar never
   moves. **The unpredictable region of the screen is bounded by design at three cards.**

The one deliberate leak, named: **settings are imperatives, not questions.** "Set night temp to 20" is
not a question and pretending otherwise would be precious. Settings live at `/device/:id/einstellungen`,
reached from the bottom of the answers that discuss a target („Nachttemperatur ändern →"). It is the
weakest seam in the concept and §13 says so again.

## 5. The chart

The chart is never a page. It is the `WORAN MAN ES SIEHT` block of an answer, and it is built to answer
*that* question only. Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner
decision). Highcharts, `chart.js`, `ng2-charts` and `chartjs-adapter-luxon` are deleted in the same PR.

```
 28 ┤                          ╭────────  ← actual (solid, min/max envelope)
    │▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁│▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  ← deviation shading (outside band)
 25 ┤━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━╮        ╰          ← setpoint (dashed, STEPPED at day/night)
    │░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░   ← ±1,5 °C tolerance band (markArea)
 20 ┤                 ╰━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━ ━
    └──┬─────────────┬─────────────┬───
   ▒▒▒▒│█████████████│▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒│      ← Nacht/Tag shading, ramp gradients
  Licht ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬                  ← output state lane
  Heizung ▬▬  ▬▬   ▬▬                          (one row per role owned)
  Entf.   ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ ▲    ▲▲(3)          ▲          ┆      │ ← annotation rail, 2 rows max
  └──────────────────────────────────────┘   row1 human, row2 device, ┆ = stage change
  [img][img][img][img]      [img][img]        ← filmstrip axis, true timestamps
```

- **Goal vs actual.** A `line` series with `step: 'end'` and `lineStyle.type: 'dashed'` drawn from
  `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC → device timezone) stepping between
  `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`. Behind it a `markArea` tolerance band. Between setpoint and
  actual, a signed `visualMap` piecewise fill: neutral inside, `--tc-text-warning` outside. **Never fill
  to the axis** — today's `type:'area', threshold:null` encodes nothing.
- **Band source priority**, one at a time, always labelled in the legend: the user's own **alarm
  thresholds** (hard edge) > **setpoint ± tolerance** (soft) > **stage VPD band** from
  `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS.vpdRange` (advisory).
- **Small multiples, not overlays.** Max 4 panels, ~100 px each, `grid[]` + `axisPointer.link` +
  `echarts.connect()` for one crosshair. At most two units per panel and only when physically coupled
  (air temp + leaf temp). Axis labels are **never** hidden — below 320 px they shorten to three ticks and
  the unit moves into the panel title.
- **Day/night.** `markArea` derived from measured `out_light > 0` where available, falling back to the
  configured schedule, with `lights.sunrise` / `lights.sunset` rendered as gradient edges, in a named
  timezone (`Europe/Berlin`, printed in `DIE RECHNUNG`). A failed contactor then shows as a **missing
  band** — a diagnostic, not decoration.
- **Outputs.** A state-timeline lane below the value panels, a `custom` series drawing regions. Region
  length = duration; null renders as a gap, not as zero. One row per role the device actually reports.
- **Annotations.** A 16 px rail *under* the x-axis, never columns inside the plot. Pixel-proximity
  clustering at 10 px with count badges, two rows max (row 1: `CareEvent` + diary; row 2: device logs),
  severity-ranked, full-height dashed lines for stage changes. `collectLogCategories` already exists and
  drives the filter chips. This kills the current hover-only, touch-dead event columns.
- **Envelope.** `aggregateWindow` `min`/`max` alongside `mean`, plotted as a band. `sampling: 'minmax'`
  for the plot (LTTB smooths away the exact oscillation of a bang-bang controller); LTTB only for the
  navigator.
- **Touch.** One finger scrolls the page. Two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: {type:'inside'}`). Tap or
  long-press sets the crosshair, and values appear in a **pinned scrub header above the panels** — one
  compact row per panel — never a floating tooltip a fingertip covers.
- **Time in range** is computed server-side on **raw Influx samples**, never on `aggregateWindow(mean)`
  output. New endpoint `GET /data/scorecard`. `DIE RECHNUNG` states the raw sample count so the claim is
  checkable. This is also the fix window for the raw-Flux interpolation in `data.service.ts:80-89`, which
  must land before any new measure enters the vocabulary.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

The diary is not a tab. It is the answer to **„Was ist passiert?"** — a reverse-chronological stream of
everything on one timeline, with filter chips — and to **„Was hat A3 bekommen?"** — the same data
aggregated per plant. `DeviceLog` keeps carrying device events. Human actions move to new collections.

```ts
// all cloud-side MongoDB, all fields optional beyond the ids, read-time normalisers, no required migration
interface Plant {
  plant_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                 // "A3" — what the grower writes on the pot
  strain?: string; medium?: string; feedRegimeId?: string;
  potLitres?: number; source?: 'seed' | 'clone' | 'unknown';
  startedAt: number; stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stageChangedAt: number;
  endedAt?: number; archivedAt?: number; runId?: string;
}
interface Run {                  // replaces convertEventsToGrowCycles()'s runtime string-matching
  run_id: string; device_id: string; name: string;
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  plantIds: string[]; flipAt?: number;      // detected 18/6 -> 12/12 from out_light history
  coverImageId?: string; filmImageId?: string;
}
interface CareEvent {
  event_id: string; device_id: string; runId?: string; plantIds: string[];
  type: 'water' | 'feed' | 'train' | 'treat' | 'measure' | 'note' | 'harvest';
  at: number; actorId?: string; actorName?: string;      // name denormalised at write time
  volumeMl?: number; perPlant?: boolean;
  doses?: { productName: string; amountMl?: number; amountG?: number; perLitre?: number }[];
  ec?: number; ecBasis?: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source'; ph?: number;
  runoffEc?: number; runoffPh?: number;
  scheduleStepId?: string; prefilledFrom?: string;       // provenance of the prefill
  clientEventId: string;         // unique index {device_id, clientEventId} — idempotency
  supersedesEventId?: string;    // corrections are new rows, never edits-in-place
  note?: string; imageIds?: string[];
}
interface Condition {            // the standing fact taped to the tent door
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; plantIds?: string[];
  text: string; severity: 0 | 1 | 2;
  openedBy?: string; openedAt: number; closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
  autoSource?: 'camera-silent' | 'device-offline';       // machine-opened conditions
}
interface Actor {                // "who is at the tent" — NOT an account
  actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string; colour: string;
  createdAt: number; retiredAt?: number;
}
```

**Migration.** `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a **one-time idempotent backfill** in the service
constructor (the house style — there is no migration tooling): scan `diary-plant-lifecycle` rows, emit
`Run` + one `Plant` per distinct `lifecycleName`. Renaming a plant no longer forks history. Existing
diary rows keep rendering through `LogEntryViewerModule` untouched.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed — the exact rule.** If `Plant.countDocuments({device_id}) === 0`:
question 6 does not exist; no capture sheet shows plant chips; every `CareEvent` is written with
`plantIds: []` and reads as "the whole tent"; `Run` is created lazily and silently on the first care event
with `name: 'Lauf 1'`. **Nothing prompts you to create a plant.** A plant comes into existence only when
you type a label into the plant field of a capture sheet you deliberately opened, or from the backfill.
The single-tent single-grower user — types 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — never meets the word "Pflanze".

Conversely, a club with 12 pots creates 12 `Plant` rows in one screen (a label field with `+`, autofills
`A1, A2, A3…`), and from that moment every capture sheet shows a chip row and question 6 exists.

**Deletion honesty.** `DeviceLog.deleted` stays the visibility flag it actually is, and "Clear logs"
is renamed to what it does: „Geräteprotokoll ausblenden". `CareEvent` deletion is real deletion, exposed
per row. `DELETE /image/:image_id` is finally wired up so removing a diary photo removes the blob.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

**The premise, from the owner: there is no dosing hardware.** So this is a capture-speed and recall
problem. C34 optimises for the number of taps at the moment your hands are wet.

### The regime

Selected once, in the wizard or from question 5's answer. Modelled as the grower says it out loud:
**medium + feed line together.**

```ts
interface FeedRegime {           // MongoDB content collection, never bundled into the webapp
  regime_id: string; label: string;              // "Biobizz All-Mix" — plain text, no logo, no brand colour
  mediumLabel: string; preFertilised: 'none' | 'light' | 'heavy';
  autoflowerMultiplier?: number;                 // 0.25–0.5
  steps: {
    step_id: string; stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; fromDayOfStage: number;
    products: { name: string; perLitre?: number; unit: 'ml/L' | 'g/L'; note?: string }[];
    ecTarget?: [number, number]; ecBasis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source';
    phTarget?: [number, number]; reason?: string;
  }[];
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; chart_version: string;
}
```

`All-Mix` ships with weeks 1–3 as steps whose `products: []` and
`reason: 'Erde ist schon vorgedüngt — jetzt düngen verbrennt.'` **A regime that cannot say "do not feed
yet" is not modelling the domain.** `BioTabs` ships as a regime whose steps are re-tabbing events every
4–6 weeks with almost no liquid feeding.

**Schedule following advances by feed events, not by wall clock.** The current step is
`steps[completedFeedCount]`, clamped, with the calendar as a *soft* hint derived from day-of-stage on the
`Run`. If a due feed is three days late the answer says „Seit 3 Tagen fällig" and the step does **not**
advance. Feeding twice inside one step records both and advances once. „Schritt weiter" and „Schritt
zurück" live in the correction sheet. `DIE RECHNUNG` prints „Schritt 4 von 12 · zuletzt 21.08. ·
Quelle: biobizz.com/… (geprüft 12.06.2026)".

### Capture — tap counts

The `Gedüngt` button lives on the Zone-A card **and** at the top of question 5's answer. It **commits
first and corrects after.**

| Scenario | Sequence | Taps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed exactly as the schedule says, whole tent, me | `Gedüngt` | **1** |
| Plain watering, no schedule | `Gegossen` (bottom bar) | **1** |
| Feed, but Anna did it | `Gedüngt` → Anna chip | **2** |
| Feed, 10 L instead of 5 | `Gedüngt` → volume chip → `10 L` | **3** |
| Feed only A1–A3 of six | `Gedüngt` → A1 → A2 → A3 | **4** |
| Feed with a product deviation and an EC reading | `Gedüngt` → product row → amount → EC field → type → done | **6 + typing** |
| Full club entry: plants, volume, deviation, EC, pH, runoff, note, photo | | **~11 + typing** |

The commit fires immediately and an 8-second bar appears at the bottom:

```
 Eingetragen: Bio-Bloom 2 ml/L, 5 L, ganzes Zelt, Chris.   [ Ändern ]  [ Rückgängig ]
```

`Ändern` opens the correction sheet *over* the already-written row. Nothing is lost if the user walks
away — the common case is finished at tap one, and the correction path costs the diligent user one extra
tap versus a form-first design. This is the shape that serves the stoner and the club **with the same
button**: it is not a simplified version of the form, it is the form with the commit moved to the front.

### Double feed — the soft block

Evaluated locally at tap time from the cached event list, and again server-side on write:

```
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.      │
 │  Bio-Bloom 2 ml/L, 5 L.                      │
 │                                              │
 │  [ Abbrechen ]        [ Trotzdem eintragen ] │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Window: **6 h for `feed`, 3 h for `water`**, matched on plant overlap (or whole-tent overlap when there
are no plants). One deliberate extra tap to proceed, and the proceeding event stores
`note: 'trotz Warnung (Anna, 1h30)'` so the record shows the decision was made knowingly.

**Offline and idempotency.** `clientEventId` is a UUID minted in the browser at tap time; the server has
a unique index `{device_id, clientEventId}` and returns the existing row on a duplicate. A pending queue
lives in `localStorage['tc-pending-<device_id>']` and flushes on the next app open. **v1 has no service
worker: an offline capture is written locally and sent when you next open the app in signal — it is not
sent in the background.** The UI says so, on the pending row: „Wird gesendet, sobald du wieder Empfang
hast (2 Einträge warten)." When the flush discovers a conflict the server answers 409 with the
conflicting event and the app shows the same sheet retrospectively: `[ Doch nicht ]` / `[ Behalten ]`.
Because of `clientEventId`, a retry **cannot** double-log — which matters more in a shared record than
anywhere else, since a duplicate row is indistinguishable from a real double feed.

### What is stored

One `CareEvent` per action, `supersedes` for corrections, never an in-place edit. The prefill provenance
(`prefilledFrom`, `scheduleStepId`) is stored so the report can later say „gefüttert wie im Plan" versus
„abweichend gefüttert". EC is stored canonical in mS/cm with `ecBasis`; **a bare "ppm" number is never
rendered** — at EC 1.8 a Bluelab reads 1260 and a Hanna reads 900 and that ambiguity is the most-cited
cause of accidental over-feeding.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

**Three of eleven questions are the camera.** It is not a tile.

### What exists today (verified in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`)

Stills every 30 s; retention 3 years; thinning tiers 1/min after 1 d, 1/5 min after 7 d, 1/15 min after
30 d, 1/h after 90 d; three rolling timelapses (`1d` @ 2 min frames, `1w` @ 14 min, `1m` @ 60 min), each
~720 frames at 25 fps ≈ 29 s; encoded **libx265 CRF 30** into an mp4 blob in MongoDB; `timestampEnd`
already marks a range. `sharp` and `imagemagick` are already dependencies.

### v1 ships "Der Lauf" — a film someone can actually hold

A fourth `duration: 'run'` video, one per `Run`, rebuilt nightly and on demand.

**Content rule: one frame per photoperiod day, and it is the most boring frame of that day.**

1. **Window.** For each day of the run, take the midday window: `lightsOn + photoperiod/2 ± 30 min`,
   where `lightsOn` comes from measured `out_light` for that day, falling back to `daynight.day`.
   Lights-off days (drying) fall back to a fixed 12:00 local window.
2. **Candidates.** Up to 10 stills from that window. Each is decoded once by `sharp` to a 64×64
   greyscale buffer plus its RGB channel means — about 1 ms each.
3. **Score.** Reject on sharpness (variance-of-Laplacian below a floor: condensation, motion blur).
   Reject on exposure outliers (mean luminance more than 2σ from the run's rolling 7-day median: the tent
   door was open). Then **pick the candidate with the *lowest* mean-absolute-error against yesterday's
   chosen frame.** A hand in shot, an open door, a moved pot are exactly the frames that differ most from
   yesterday — choosing the most similar frame throws them out without a person detector.
4. **Human suppression, for free.** Any candidate within ±5 minutes of a `CareEvent` or diary write is
   deprioritised: that is precisely when somebody is standing in the tent. This is a correlation between
   two datasets Terp Control already has and nobody else does.
5. **Normalise.** Per kept frame, a linear gain `target/current` toward the run's rolling 7-day median
   luminance, clamped to `[0.7, 1.4]`; per-channel white-balance gain clamped to ±15 %. This flattens the
   slow magenta-LED-versus-daylight drift across 12 weeks without inventing colour on any single frame.
6. **Stabilise.** ffmpeg `-vf "deshake=rx=16:ry=16,crop=iw*0.95:ih*0.95"`. One flag plus a 5 % safety
   crop; it absorbs the day-12 nudge. Real optical-flow stabilisation is v1.1 and the doc says so.
7. **Gaps are gaps.** A day with no usable frame is skipped, not interpolated, and the answer states how
   many days are missing and why.
8. **Encode.** `libx264` High, `-crf 23`, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-movflags +faststart`, 1920×1080, **8 fps**.
   31 days → 31 frames → 3.9 s → ~3 MB. 84 days → 10.5 s. A burnt-in stamp per frame:
   `Tag 31 · 25.08.2026 · 24,6 °C · 57 %` drawn with the existing `sharp` SVG-overlay helper.
   **The switch from libx265 to libx264 is required, not cosmetic:** an HEVC mp4 does not reliably play in
   Chrome on Android, which is most of the audience. The `1d`/`1w`/`1m` films switch too.

### How the video leaves the product

```
GET /image/:device_id/download?duration=run&run_id=<id>&profile=full|share
  200  Content-Type: video/mp4
       Content-Disposition: attachment;
         filename="terp-zelt1-gorilla-glue-tag01-31-2026-08-25.mp4"
       Accept-Ranges: bytes
       Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600
```

Filename template: `terp-<device-slug>-<strain-slug>-tag<first>-<last>-<yyyy-mm-dd>.mp4`, ASCII-folded,
lower-case, 80 chars max. `profile=share` re-encodes to 1280×720, CRF 28, capped at **16 MB** so it
crosses Telegram and WhatsApp without being re-crushed.

Two buttons, no login wall, no "share produces a URL to a page requiring a login":

```
[  Video speichern  ]        [  Für Telegram (720p)  ]
```

Android/Chrome: straight into `Downloads`, visible in Gallery. Desktop: a file. **iOS Safari is two extra
steps and the button says so** — the response lands in Files, then „In Fotos sichern". A `<a download>`
would be worse; navigating to a URL with `Content-Disposition` is the only route Safari handles.

`Image.duration`'s enum gains `'run'`; the unique index `{device_id, format, timestamp, duration}` is
satisfied because a run film is keyed on `timestamp = run.startedAt`.

### The camera alarm

`image.service` opens a `Condition { autoSource: 'camera-silent', severity: 1 }` when no `jpeg` has been
stored for **6 h** while the device is otherwise online, and closes it on the next frame. It surfaces as
the pinned question **„Warum kommen keine Bilder mehr?"**, and — reusing the existing `mailTransport` and
the alarm webhook plumbing — as one email/webhook, once, not per poll. Freshness honesty alone tells you
the camera is quiet only if you happen to look; this tells you.

### Image correlation

## 9. Clubs

**No auth rewrite in v1.** The club need — *who did what, and how did the plants react* — is served by
attribution, which is a label, not a gate.

- **`Actor` is device-scoped and account-free.** The owner types first names or nicknames once
  („Vorname oder Spitzname reicht"). Chips appear in every capture sheet. The last actor used *on this
  browser* is remembered in `localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']` and pre-selected, so a member's
  phone defaults to that member after one use. `actorName` is denormalised onto each event at write time
  so renaming or retiring an actor never rewrites history.
- **Attribution ≠ authorisation, and C34 says which it has.** Anyone with the login can act as any
  actor. The UI never implies otherwise: the actor chip reads „eingetragen von", never „angemeldet als".
  Real memberships (a collection + rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207` + ~10 owner-scoped
  queries + an actor→user link) are v1.1 and `Actor.actor_id` is designed to survive being upgraded into
  one.
- **The double-feed problem is §7's soft block** — a device-scoped 6 h / 3 h window on overlapping
  plants, one deliberate extra tap, offline-safe by `clientEventId`. This is the single most valuable
  club feature in the concept, and it is ~200 lines.
- **The cumulative question is question 6.** „Was hat A3 bekommen?" answers with a per-product ledger for
  the current run: product, total ml, number of gifts, last gift, and **who**. Plus the same totals for
  the whole tent. Straight aggregation over `CareEvent.doses` — every data model could compute it; this
  one shows it.
- **Conditions are question 11, „Was steht an?".** Open facts with an owner and no due date: „CO₂-Flasche
  fast leer", „Steckdose 3 wackelt", „A3 nicht gießen — übergossen". Opened in two taps from the bottom
  bar's `Notiz` (a segment toggle: *Notiz* | *Steht an*), closed by anyone with one tap, and machine-
  opened ones (camera silent, device offline) close themselves. Open conditions with `severity: 2` also
  push a card into Zone A. **This is what actually gets taped to the tent door and no round-one concept
  had it.**
- **Privacy posture — deliberate and restrictive.** No real name is required or requested anywhere. No
  per-member dispensing, quantities or THC content, ever — that is § 26 KCanG's job, it is arguably
  Art. 9 GDPR health data, and a grow controller has no need for it. Harvest weight is an optional field
  on a `harvest` event that is **not prompted** and is off by default. Every export carries a
  `NONE | PSEUDONYMIZED | ANONYMIZED` toggle defaulting to anonymised („Person A", „Person B"). The
  `Actor` list is deletable in one action, which retroactively anonymises the denormalised names. **No
  „KCanG-konform" badge, no compliance warranty, no location, no analytics SDK, ever.**

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app once a week. Kopf: three numbers with an age. Zone A: empty,
which is itself the answer. Zone B: five questions, the first of which reads „Ja, seit 6 Tagen im Band".
That is the whole visit — two seconds, no chart, no configuration. When he does want history, „Hält das
Zelt seine Werte?" gives him a time-in-range verdict computed from raw samples, which is the thing today's
chart page has never given anybody.

**2 — Same, with a camera.** The Kopf is a live picture, not a number, and that is the difference the
€30 upgrade bought. „Zeig mir den ganzen Lauf" is a 4-second film with a save button that puts a real
mp4 in his phone. „Was hat sich verändert?" is the wipe-slider between two midday stills a week apart
with the climate delta underneath. This user is the one the round-one critics said all ten concepts
failed, and he is the one C34 spends the most engineering on.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** The per-role capability read means his question list
and every chart panel contain heat and CO₂ and **nothing about humidity**, because his tent cannot
change humidity. No dehumidifier tile, no humidity deviation warning, no dehumidifier alarm preset. He
is the type today's fails-open three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` insults on every screen, and the
fix is a per-role read of a CSV the firmware already sends.

**4 — Full tent.** Gets the most panels, the most output lanes, the most Zone-A traffic, and the
honest limits: five socket roles exist, `dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler, and there is no humidifier
path. C34 does not pretend otherwise — the answer to „Warum ist es zu feucht?" ends with „Das Zelt kann
nicht befeuchten. Nur abluft- oder heizungsseitig lösbar." Telling him the truth is worth more than a
control that does nothing, and it costs no firmware.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** The worst-served type in this concept and I will not pretend otherwise. He does
not want a question list; he wants a socket. What C34 gives him is real but narrow: **every answer has a
JSON twin.** `GET /api/answer/:qid?device_id=…` returns exactly the object the page rendered — verdict
string, evidence rows, and the `DIE RECHNUNG` block including the upstream query — and `DIE RECHNUNG`
shows him the request with a copy button, so the API documents itself by being visible in the UI. That
is a read API. Writes, custom measures and MQTT stay out of v1 and §13 counts that as a loss.

**6 — Club.** Actors, the soft block, the per-plant ledger, and the conditions board. Four people, one
tent, one login, and the record still reconstructs who fed what to which pots. No accounts, no auth
rewrite, no member identities, no dispensing records, no compliance claim. Two days of work for the
attribution the grow-club critic said eight of ten concepts deferred for no reason.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Four questions, the same shell, his settings form untouched, and his
untranslated checkbox labels finally fixed. He notices the app got faster and stopped calling his
controller a fridge.

**And the three capability lenses.** *Stoner:* one sentence per question, a bottom bar that never moves,
one-tap capture with undo, and a bounded three-card unpredictable region. *Normal:* a screen that looks
like an answer instead of an instrument panel, and a timelapse she can send to a friend. *Techie:*
`DIE RECHNUNG` on every single page — the window, the timezone, the setpoint used, the coverage %, the
raw sample count, the formula, the request — which is more disclosure than any competitor ships and it
arrives without him asking for a mode.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026

Six to eight weeks, one team, shipping alongside 150 controllers. What follows is what I would actually
commit to, with the cuts named.

### In

1. **The shell.** `/device/:id` question list + `/device/:id/frage/:qid` answer template, the three-part
   layout, the Zone A/B rule, the sticky capture bar, `available()`/`urgency()`. **11 questions.**
   NgModule per answer, lazy-loaded. `/charts` alias preserving every existing share link and preset.
2. **ECharts 6 migration** for the four answers that draw: goal-vs-actual, time-in-range, outputs,
   change. Dashed stepped setpoint, tolerance `markArea`, day/night from measured `out_light`, output
   state lane, annotation rail, filmstrip axis, two-finger zoom, pinned scrub header. Delete Highcharts,
   `chart.js`, `ng2-charts`, `chartjs-adapter-luxon`.
3. **Server:** `GET /data/scorecard` (raw-sample time-in-range), the Flux interpolation fix, server-side
   log pagination, `GET /image/:id/sprite`, `GET /image/:id/download`.
4. **`CareEvent` + `Actor` + `Condition`** with the one-tap capture, the undo bar, the soft block, the
   `clientEventId` idempotency index, and the `localStorage` pending queue.
5. **`Plant` + `Run`** minimal (label, stage, run membership) plus the idempotent backfill from
   `convertEventsToGrowCycles`. Question 6's ledger.
6. **`FeedRegime`** as a Mongo content collection with **five seeded regimes** — Biobizz Light-Mix,
   Biobizz All-Mix, Floragard Light, Green House Feeding, BioTabs — each with `source_url` +
   `retrieved_at`, sourced from the brand's own chart, plus Lucas and a generic coco EC ladder. Prefill
   into the capture sheet, always editable.
7. **The camera:** `duration: 'run'`, the midday most-boring-frame selection, exposure/WB normalisation,
   `deshake`, the burnt-in day stamp, **libx264 for all four durations**, the download endpoint with a
   real filename, the 720p share profile, and the camera-silent `Condition` with one email.
8. **Correlation:** filmstrip axis, shared cursor, the nearest-frame thumbnail on every diary and care
   row (free — existing endpoint).
9. **Capability per role** replacing the three-bucket fails-open helper, failing **closed** on missing
   `hardwareInfo.sockets`. Removal of the four dead controls (`daynight.floating` and friends).
10. **The naming pass:** a `devices.controller` i18n block, the fridge label gone, the six raw-key i18n
    bugs fixed, chart series names translated. de + en in the same commit, both mandatory.

### Out — and honestly out

- **Real multi-user auth.** v1 has actors, not accounts. Two people still share one login.
- **A service worker.** v1's queue survives a reload but does not send in the background, and the UI
  says so.
- **The mixing-order card, the shopping list, run-length reshaping, brand charts beyond the seeded five.**
- **VPD as an expressible goal** (translating a VPD band into day/night setpoints). Designed, not built.
- **Public diary URL, Markdown/BBCode generator, Telegram/Discord destinations, weekly export table.**
  All are v1.1 and all are cheap once `CareEvent` and `Run` exist — which is the point of building them
  first.
- **Optical-flow stabilisation, person detection, per-plant framing.** `deshake` + boring-frame selection
  is what v1 can defend.
- **A write API, custom measures, Home Assistant.** Read-only JSON twin only.
- **Blob storage migration.** Videos stay in MongoDB `Buffer`s in v1, which is wrong and is the first
  thing that will hurt at scale. GridFS or S3 is v1.1's first ticket.

**Risk I would flag to the owner on day one:** items 2 and 7 are each two weeks and they are on the
critical path together. If the schedule slips, cut the *number of questions* (11 → 7), never the chart
or the film. A question list with seven good answers is still the concept; a concept without a
downloadable timelapse in October is the exact failure the round-two brief was written to prevent.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The catalogue is an editorial bottleneck.** Every new capability needs a new curated question, in
   two languages, written independently in each because „Ist alles in Ordnung?" is warm in German and
   "Is everything OK?" is flat in English. Feature velocity becomes coupled to copywriting, and a product
   team that ships a feature without a question ships it invisibly. This is a permanent tax, not a
   launch cost.

2. **Some things are not questions and the seam shows.** Settings, alarm editing, socket pairing and
   firmware updates are imperatives. They live at `/device/:id/einstellungen` reached from the bottom of
   answers, which means the least-designed surface in this concept is the one type 4 spends the most time
   in. A critic will call this "a question app bolted onto the old settings page" and they will be
   partly right.

3. **A one-line verdict can be confidently wrong.** „Ja, alles in Ordnung" over 60 % data coverage is a
   lie, and the mitigation — degrading to „Ich weiß es nicht — nur 41 % der Messwerte liegen vor" — looks
   to a non-technical user like a broken app. There is no version of a verdict product that escapes this;
   C34 chooses honesty and pays for it in perceived reliability.

4. **Zone A is still unpredictability, just bounded.** The feed button is second today and first
   tomorrow. Three cards is a small blast radius and the ghost rows preserve spatial memory, but a
   genuinely impaired user at 2 a.m. can still tap the wrong card. The mitigation that actually works is
   the fixed bottom bar — which is why watering and notes are down there and feeding, which needs its
   schedule context, is not.

5. **The techie may find it slow.** A dashboard shows six numbers at once; a question list shows eleven
   one-line answers and makes him tap for the chart. The Kopf plus the answer lines carry ~11 numbers on
   the home screen, and `DIE RECHNUNG` gives more disclosure than any competitor — but type 5 is
   genuinely worse served here than by a dense instrument panel, and the JSON twin is a consolation, not
   an answer.

6. **Timelapse compute does not scale linearly forever.** Ten candidate decodes per device per day is
   trivial at 150 units and needs a job queue at 5,000. Worse: editing a run's start date invalidates the
   film and forces a full rebuild, and `pLimit(10)` on ffmpeg is the only backpressure in the service
   today. There is no job queue in this codebase and v1 does not add one.

7. **Switching to libx264 costs storage.** Roughly 1.5–1.7× the bytes of x265 at comparable quality, and
   those bytes go into MongoDB documents under a 16 MB BSON ceiling with `express-fileupload` mounted
   with no size limit. A 12-week run film at 1080p is fine; a 1d film of a bright, busy tent at CRF 23 is
   uncomfortably close to the wrong side of that limit and the failure mode today is ungraceful.

8. **Attribution without authorisation is a real limitation, not a clever one.** Any member can pick any
   name. For a club that trusts its members this is exactly right and costs two days; for a club that
   does not, it is worthless, and C34 has nothing for them until v1.1.

9. **Failing capability *closed* for old firmware is a behaviour change with a support cost.** A device
   that does not report `hardwareInfo.sockets` loses controls it appeared to have. It is the correct
   change — those controls did nothing — but the first week's tickets will say the app broke.

10. **`available()` returning `'no'` makes features genuinely invisible.** A user with no plants will
    never discover that per-plant logging exists, because nothing hints at it. That is the price of not
    taxing him, and the discoverability answer — a single line in the wizard and one in the release notes
    — is thin. C35 ("Wächst mit") owns this problem head-on; C34 inherits it without solving it.
