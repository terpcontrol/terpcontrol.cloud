# C14 — "Was hat sich geändert"

**Family:** camera · **Centre of gravity:** visual change detection is the product.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The app's primary object is not a value, a chart or a plant — it is a pair of pictures with the
difference between them named in one sentence, and everything else in the product exists to explain
that difference.**

Every other concept in this bake-off answers "what is the state of my tent". This one answers the
question people actually open the app to ask: *what is different since I last looked?* That question
needs no interpretation, no units, no baseline knowledge and no sobriety. Two frames side by side and a
line of German prose is a complete answer for the lazy monitoring user, and it is also — because the
sentence carries its own numbers and every element drills into raw series — a complete answer for the
techie. The camera stops being a tile in a grid and becomes the *measurement instrument the product is
built around*, with temperature, humidity, VPD, outputs and diary events acting as the annotations that
explain what the pictures show.

The bet: Terp Control already captures a frame every 30 s and already has the environment series on the
same clock. Nobody in this market fuses them. A picture pair is the only readout that is simultaneously
trivially legible and genuinely dense — and unlike a chart, it is *shareable*, which is why the film
falls out of the same machinery for free.

The discipline: **the product describes differences, it never diagnoses them.** It will say "der obere
linke Bereich ist heller geworden — Grünanteil 41 % → 33 %". It will never say "Stickstoffmangel". §8
states exactly which image operations are real and which are computer-vision fantasy.

## 2. Information architecture

Four tabs (`ion-tabs` — the codebase has none today; this is a new `TabsPageModule` wrapping the device
routes). The chart is deliberately *not* a tab: it is the evidence behind something you tapped.

```
/list                                 device list (unchanged; 1 device → straight into /device/:id)
/device/:id/jetzt      TAB 1  Jetzt      Der Vergleich — the home screen (§3)
/device/:id/film       TAB 2  Film       Timelapse studio (§8)
/device/:id/tagebuch   TAB 3  Tagebuch   Entries, plants, cumulative report (§6)
/device/:id/zelt       TAB 4  Zelt       Targets, kit, sockets, camera, alarms, regime (§10)

  pushed, never a tab — always entered from a thing you tapped:
/device/:id/verlauf?measure=…&from=…&to=…       the chart (§5)
/device/:id/moment/:ts                          one frame + its numbers + events (§8.5)
/device/:id/tagebuch/pflanze/:plant_id          one plant's record (§6)
/device/:id/film/:film_id                       one rendered film, playable + downloadable

  kept verbatim so bookmarks, ChartPresets and every ShareLink in circulation resolve:
/device/:id/charts  → 301 → /device/:id/verlauf  (query string passed through untouched)
/device/:id/diary   → 301 → /device/:id/tagebuch (mergeDiaryQueryParams honoured)
/device/:id/settings, /testmode, /shares, /account, /classes, /demo   unchanged
```

`ShareLink.page` stays `'charts' | 'diary'` — no enum change, no migration. A share token entering
`/verlauf` or `/tagebuch` hits the existing `resolveDeviceAccessInfo` guards; the Jetzt tab is
share-visible with `webcam:false` collapsing the pair to the numbers-only variant (§3.4).

Depth, not breadth. The rule the IA enforces: **there is no screen you can only reach from a menu.**
Every pushed page is reached by tapping the specific thing it is about.

### 3.2 The pair

- **Right frame = the newest kept frame.** Not the newest frame: a frame rejected by the cull (§8.2) as
  a hand-in-shot or a fog frame is never shown as "jetzt". If the newest 5 frames were all rejected, the
  right frame carries a caption `Bild verworfen (Hand im Bild) — gezeigt: 14:04`.
- **Left frame = the comparison endpoint**, default `seit du zuletzt geschaut hast`: the newest kept
  frame at or before the timestamp of the user's previous session on this device
  (`localStorage['tc-lastseen-<device_id>']`, written on tab blur). If that is under 4 h ago, it falls
  back to `gestern gleiche Zeit`, because a 30-minute-old picture is not a comparison.
- Tapping either frame opens the **Wischvergleich** full screen: the two frames stacked with a draggable
  vertical handle that wipes between them, pinch to zoom, both frames zoom together. The changed-tile
  tint follows. Swipe down to dismiss.
- `Δ Unterschied` tints tiles whose change score exceeds the frame's own 90th percentile, at 35 %
  opacity in `--ion-color-warning`. It is a rendering of the same frames, not another view.

### 3.3 Befund rows

One row = one `<button class="befund">` with two lines: **a prose line** (17 px, 600) and **a value
line** (14 px, tabular-nums, `--tc-text-*`). Both are always present. Nobody toggles anything; the two
registers coexist in one string block. Tapping pushes:

| Befund kind | Tap target |
| --- | --- |
| a number | `/verlauf?measure=temperature&from=…&to=…` with the span already applied |
| an image finding | the Wischvergleich, zoomed to the tile that scored highest |
| an event | that `GrowEvent` in the Tagebuch, with its auto-attached frame |
| a condition | the Condition, with `[Erledigt]` |

**Ranking** is deterministic and printed: `ⓘ sortiert nach Abweichung`. Score for a numeric measure is
`|Δ| / max(σ₁₄, σ_floor)` where σ₁₄ is that measure's standard deviation over the last 14 days *at the
same time of day ±1 h*, and `σ_floor` is a per-measure clamp (temperature 0.4 °C, RH 2 pp, CO₂ 40 ppm,
VPD 0.05 kPa). Image findings score on tile change relative to the same 14-day baseline. Events always
rank in the top 3 if they happened inside the span — a human action outranks a wobble. The order is
computed once per screen entry and does not move while you look at it.

**Nothing is hidden.** `6 weitere Werte anzeigen` expands the *same list* in place; it does not open a
screen and it does not reveal a feature class. Every measure the device reports is in that list with its
delta, including the ones that did not change (`○ gleich`).

### 3.4 States — stale, offline, absent

| Situation | What Jetzt shows |
| --- | --- |

The **Film tab is absent**, not disabled. |

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

### The falsification test I hold myself to

> **If you can name a control whose sole effect is to show or hide a class of feature, it is a mode.**

Three mechanisms, then an honest audit of my own design against that test.

### M1 — One sentence, two registers, no structure between them

Every Befund is a single typographic block containing prose *and* the raw numbers. The impaired user
reads the top line and stops. The techie's eye lands on the tabular-nums line. **They are looking at the
same DOM node.** There is no "show values" preference, no compact/detailed density setting, no
"Erweitert" accordion. Density is achieved by typography — weight, size, colour, font — which every
reader filters for free and no reader has to configure.

The rule that keeps it honest: *the prose line must be true without the value line, and the value line
must be complete without the prose line.* `Nachts war es wärmer` / `18,1 °C → 20,5 °C, Ziel 20,0 °C`.
Neither is a summary of the other; they are the same fact at two precisions.

### M2 — One control that is coarse and precise in the same gesture

The **Zeitleiste** sets the left endpoint of the comparison. It is a horizontally scrolling strip of
thumbnails; dragging it moves the endpoint continuously. It has a **snap ladder**: named anchors
(`seit zuletzt`, `gestern`, `1 Woche`, `Phasenwechsel`, `letzte Fütterung`, `Beginn`) with 44 px magnetic
zones. A fat thumb at 2 am cannot miss `gestern`. A deliberate finger between two snaps lands on any
minute you like, and the exact timestamp is printed under the strip as you drag.

This is one control, one gesture, no setting. It is not "simple mode = presets, expert mode = custom
range" — it is a physical continuum where the useful positions are detented, like a good rotary knob.
(The hardware has a rotary knob. The metaphor is not accidental.)

### M3 — Everything is a drill target; nothing is a disclosure control

Every atom on screen — a number, a tile of the change map, a frame in the strip, an actor chip, a
product name — is a button that navigates to *that thing's* evidence. Depth is unbounded (Befund →
chart → raw series → CSV). But **no control exists whose job is to reveal**. You never press "more"; you
press the thing you are curious about. The stoner presses nothing. The techie presses four times and is
looking at a min/max envelope over raw 5-second samples.

Corollary that makes this work for user types 3/4/5: the Zelt tab shows controls **only for kit the
device reports** (`hardwareInfo.sockets` per role, not the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability`
collapse). A heater-only tent never sees a humidity target row. That is not a mode either — it is the
truth about the hardware, and a socket paired at 3 pm changes the screen at 3 pm.

### Honest audit of my own design

| Suspect | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| `6 weitere Werte anzeigen` | **Not a mode** — it expands the same list in place, ordered by the printed sort key. It hides no feature; the hidden rows are the low-ranked ones by construction and the user can see the ranking rule. Closest analogue: a long list. |
| The four **Film toggles** (§8.3) | **Closest call in the concept.** Defence: they are parameters of an *artefact being produced*, like an export resolution, and both user types set them for the same reason (they want a watchable film). They do not change the app's interface. But a critic can fairly say `Ruhig stellen` is an expert affordance a stoner will never touch. I accept that and mitigate only by defaults that are right for everyone. |
| The feed sheet's lower fields (§7) | **Not a mode** — one scrollable column, save button pinned, nothing behind a control. Scrolling is not a disclosure control. But it is the second-closest call. |
| `Δ Unterschied` chip | **Not a mode** — it changes how one image is drawn, at the same level as pinch-zoom. |

No mechanism in this concept keys off "who you are", a stored preference, or an onboarding answer.

## 5. The chart — /verlauf

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line directive (owner decision). Chart.js/ng2-charts
deleted. **Always entered with a measure and a span already set**, from a Befund or a number.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ‹  Temperatur          23.08. 14:00 – jetzt  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 24,1 °C   Ziel 24,0   ⌀ 23,6   min 18,1      │  ← pinned scrub header, NOT a hover tooltip
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [film strip lane — thumbnails on the x-scale]│  ← 44 px, shares the cursor
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ░░░░░████████░░░░░░░░████████░░░░  ← Tag/Nacht│
│      ╭──╮                                    │
│  ────┴──┴───  actual (solid, minmax band)    │
│  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄  Sollwert (dashed, stepped)     │
│  ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒  Zielband (markArea, ±1,5 °C)   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ out_light  ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄        ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄          │  ← state-timeline lane
│ out_heater ▄ ▄  ▄   ▄▄                       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ▪▪  ▪   ▪▪▪(3)      ▪        ▪▪              │  ← annotation rail, 2 rows, tappable
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 84 % in Ziel · Tag 91 % · Nacht 71 %         │  ← time-in-range, raw samples
│ längste Abweichung 2 Std 10 (Nacht, zu warm) │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Sollwert**: a stepped dashed series from `device.configuration` (`day.temperature`/`night.temperature`
  stepping at `daynight.day`/`night`, seconds-of-day UTC, rendered in the device timezone which is named
  in the axis label). **Zielband** is a `markArea`; source priority **alarm thresholds > setpoint ±
  tolerance > stage `vpdRange`**, one at a time, labelled with which it is. No new API — the values
  already exist client-side.
- **Tag/Nacht** from measured `out_light > 0`, schedule as fallback. A failed contactor renders as a
  *missing band* — a diagnostic, not decoration.
- **Outputs** in a state-timeline lane; region length = duration, null = gap. Never 0/1 value-axis lines.
- **Annotations** in a rail under the plot: row 1 human events, row 2 device events; 10 px proximity
  clustering with count badges; full-height dashed lines for stage changes. **Tappable, not hover** —
  the current hover-only columns are unusable on the phone this product ships for.
- **Time in range** computed server-side on **raw Influx samples**, never on `aggregateWindow(mean)`,
  split day/night, longest excursion as a duration, greyed out below 80 % coverage (printed).
- `sampling:'minmax'` on the primary series (LTTB smooths away the bang-bang oscillation the grower is
  looking for); LTTB for the navigator only.
- Two fingers pan/zoom, one finger scrolls, tap = cursor. Axis labels never hidden — three ticks, unit
  in the panel title.
- The query vocabulary is **extended additively** (`from`, `to`, `measure`, `strip`, `band`);
  `applyViewParams` keeps a back-compat branch for `measures`/`date`/`dateEnd`/`timespan`, so every
  existing ChartPreset and ShareLink resolves. Flux interpolation of `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval` is
  parameterised **before** any of this ships (dossier P0 #8).

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### The one idea that makes this a diary concept

**Every entry gets its picture automatically.** The frames already exist. When a `GrowEvent` is written
at 19:40, the server attaches `auto_image_id` = the nearest *kept* frame within ±5 minutes. The user
uploads nothing. That is literally "es macht das Tagebuchführen für dich", it costs one Mongo lookup,
and it turns the diary from a text log into a visual record with zero user effort.

### New collections (all cloud-side Mongo, keyed by `device_id`; `device.configuration` untouched)

```ts
Actor {                       // "who is at the tent" — names, not accounts
  actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string;        // free text, may be "T"
  colour: string; created_at: number; disabled?: boolean;
}

Plant {
  plant_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                     // "A1"
  sort_index: number;
  started_at: number; ended_at?: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stage_since: number;
  strain_text?: string;              // plain text, optional, never required
  medium_key?: string;               // → Regime (§7)
  regime_key?: string;
  pot_liters?: number;
  archived?: boolean;
  created_at: number; created_by_actor?: string;
}

GrowEvent {
  event_id: string; device_id: string;
  plant_ids: string[];               // EMPTY ARRAY = "das ganze Zelt"
  kind: 'water'|'feed'|'both'|'train'|'observe'|'stage'|'harvest'|'note';
  at: number; actor_id?: string;
  volume_l?: number;
  nutrients?: { product_text: string; ml_per_l?: number; note_text?: string; from_regime: boolean }[];
  ph?: number; ec?: number; ec_basis?: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source';
  runoff_ph?: number; runoff_ec?: number;
  training?: 'top'|'lst'|'defoliate'|'transplant';
  note?: string;
  image_ids: string[];               // user photos
  auto_image_id?: string;            // the frame nearest `at`
  source: 'manual'|'schedule';
  client_event_id: string;           // UUID from the client — server upserts on {device_id, client_event_id}
  created_at: number; edited_at?: number;
}

Condition {                          // an open fact, not an event, not a task
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; plant_ids?: string[];
  text: string; opened_by?: string; opened_at: number;
  closed_by?: string; closed_at?: number;
}

FrameMetrics {                       // §8.2 — one per captured frame
  image_id: string; device_id: string; timestamp: number;
  ok: boolean; reject_reason?: 'lights_off'|'transient'|'blur'|'duplicate'|'camera_jump';
  phash: string; brightness: number; sharpness: number; green_frac?: number;
  tiles: number[];                   // 48 = 8×6 grid
  shift_dx: number; shift_dy: number;
  light_state: 'on'|'off'|'unknown'; light_pct?: number;

```

`DeviceLog` is left alone and keeps carrying device events. `GrowEvent` is a **new** collection because
the dossier is right that a per-plant diary cannot be layered onto a flat log. Legacy diary rows are
shown merged into the Tagebuch timeline by a read-time adapter; `convertEventsToGrowCycles` becomes a
one-time backfill into `Plant`, not a runtime algorithm.

### The user who ignores plants is untaxed

`plant_ids: []` means the whole tent, and it is the default forever. **The plant UI does not exist until
a plant exists** — no empty state, no "0 Pflanzen" card, no nag. The only door is a `+ Pflanzen anlegen`
line at the bottom of the Tagebuch tab, below the entries, which a user can scroll past for twelve
weeks. Create one and plant chips appear in the capture sheet, in Befund rows and in the per-plant
report; create none and nothing in the product mentions plants.

### Tap counts — measured, not claimed

| Path | Sequence | **Taps** |
| --- | --- | --- |

  Zuletzt geprüft: 12.08.2026 · Quelle: Herstellerplan
```

  regime_key: string; medium_text: string; feedline_text: string;   // plain text, no logos
  starts_feeding_at_week: number;                                   // All-Mix = 3
  steps: { index: number; anchor: 'week'|'stage_week'|'feed_n'; stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage;
           products: { product_text: string; ml_per_l?: number; range?: [number, number];
                       note_key?: string }[];                       // [] = "noch nicht düngen"
           ec_target?: number; ec_basis: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source';
           ph_range?: [number, number]; note_key?: string }[];
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; chart_version: string;
  kind: 'official_pdf'|'official_calculator'|'ownerless';
}
```

- The engine advances by **feed events**, not wall-clock: `Schritt 4 von 12`. Growers feed Tue/Fri and
  slip. A soft calendar mapping shows `etwa Woche 5`.
- Every prefilled value lands in the sheet as an editable number and is stored with
  `from_regime: true`. The moment the user changes it, `from_regime: false` — so the report can say
  `wie im Plan` vs `abweichend` months later, which is the club's actual question.
- EC is stored canonically in mS/cm with `ec_basis`. **A `delta_over_source` chart value is never
  compared against a raw meter reading** — the sheet shows `EC-Ziel 1,6 (+ Leitungswasser)`. No bare
  "ppm" is ever rendered.
- Autoflower: a `×0.25–0.5` strength multiplier and no 12/12 flip event. No manufacturer chart has an
  auto column; this fills a real gap with nothing borrowed.
- Reminder for the "it thinks for me" user: when a step is due and no feed event exists,
  `Jetzt` grows one Befund row — `● Fütterung fällig — Schritt 5, seit 2 Tagen` — which opens the sheet
  prefilled. No separate task list, no badge, no notification centre.
- Legal posture, invisible in the UI: brand names as plain text only, never in a feature name; the
  numbers live in Mongo with `source_url` + `retrieved_at` so one brand is removable in one DB update;
  "Zuletzt geprüft" doubles as a genuine trust feature.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

**This is the headline. It ships in v1 or the concept has no reason to exist.**

### 8.1 What exists today (verified in `server/src/services/image.service.ts`)

ffmpeg is installed and already invoked via `execFile`; `sharp` 0.34.5 and an ImageMagick wrapper are
already dependencies — **no new binary, no build change, no ML runtime.** A frame lands every 30 s;
rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses are already built at 2/14/60-min frame intervals, 25 fps,
`libx265 -crf 30`; corrupt-frame detection, thinning tiers and a 30-day URL-embeddable `?token=` with
`?width=` resizing all exist.

Three things are wrong with it here, all cheap to fix: the buckets are **UTC-aligned, not grow-aligned**;
**x265 in mp4 is the wrong codec** for a file a user will hold, share and play in WhatsApp or a phone
gallery; and **no frame is ever culled or normalised** — the "1d" film is 720 frames of whatever the
camera saw, lights-off included.

### 8.2 The cull and metrics pipeline — what is real

Runs at ingest, per frame, before storage. Two `sharp` decodes plus plain JS arithmetic on ~9 KB of
pixels. Measured cost target **≤ 60 ms/frame**.

| # | Step | How | Honest limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Downscale | `sharp(buf).resize(128,96).greyscale().raw()` and `.resize(64,48).raw()` | — |
| 2 | `light_state` | Read `out_light` from Influx at ts ± 60 s. Fallback: brightness threshold | Cross-modal, correct. This is the payoff of correlation |
| 3 | `brightness` | mean of the grey buffer | — |
| 4 | `sharpness` | variance of a 3×3 Laplacian over the 128×96 grey | Cannot distinguish fog from a defocused lens. Reported as `unscharf`, never as a cause |
| 5 | `phash` | 8×8 dHash (adjacent-pixel comparison → 64-bit). Hamming < 3 vs last kept = duplicate | Deduplication only; not similarity search |
| 6 | `shift_dx/dy` | Integer SAD search ±8 px vs a running reference, on the 128×96 grey. ~3.5 M int ops ≈ 5 ms | **Translation only.** A rotated or relocated camera is *detected* (residual SAD stays high) and becomes a chapter break, not a correction |
| 7 | `tiles[48]` | Mean absolute difference per tile of an 8×6 grid vs the last kept frame | This is the change map. It says **where**, never **what** |
| 8 | transient test | Frame t is transient if d(t−1,t) and d(t,t+1) are both > 4× the rolling median while d(t−1,t+1) < 1.5×. Needs one frame of lookahead (30 s) | Catches a hand, a door, a person walking through. **Blind to anything lasting > 60 s**, and it culls genuinely fast real changes |
| 9 | `green_frac` | Fraction of pixels in the user's crop with G > R+τ and G > B+τ | A **weak** proxy. Confounded by leaf angle, dimming, defoliation and camera shift. Under monochromatic magenta LED it is near-meaningless. Labelled `Blattfläche (Bildanteil)` — never cm, never "Wachstum" as a claim |

**What is explicitly refused as computer-vision fantasy:** naming a drooping leaf; diagnosing a
deficiency from colour; measuring height in cm; counting or identifying individual plants; segmenting
canopy from background; detecting pests. None of that is buildable honestly without a trained model, a
labelled dataset and a calibration rig, and none of it is in this concept at any version.

**What replaces it, and is genuinely better for the user:** the product points at the tile and shows the
pair. *The human does the semantics.* "Der obere linke Bereich hat sich am stärksten verändert" +
two pictures beats a wrong diagnosis every time.

**`FrameMetrics` storage:** ~200 B/frame × 2 880/day = 576 KB/device/day, thinned alongside the images.

### 8.3 Der Film — what ships in v1

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Film                                        │
│  ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │            [ poster frame ]            │  │
│  │                  ▶                     │  │
│  └────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
│  Tag 12 – Tag 40 · 71 Sek · 11,4 MB          │
│                                              │
│  [ Diese Woche ][ Diese Phase ][ Ganzer      │
│    Durchgang ][ Seit dem Umtopfen ]          │
│  ◂ ▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪▪ ▸       │
│                                              │
│  Was drin ist                                │
│    2 148 Bilder aufgenommen                  │
│      − 289 Licht aus                         │
│      −  41 Hand im Bild                      │
│      −  22 unscharf                          │
│      −   9 Kamera verrutscht                 │
│    = 1 787 Bilder → 71 Sek bei 25 B/s        │
│    2 Kapitel (Kamera verrutscht am 12.09.)   │
│                                              │
│  ☑ Nur Tagbilder                             │
│  ☑ Helligkeit angleichen                     │
│  ☑ Ruhig stellen                             │
│  ☑ Zahlen einblenden                         │
│                                              │
│  ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │           Film erstellen                │  │
│  └────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The cull report is not a debug panel. It is the product's honesty made visible, and it is the single
thing that explains why this film is watchable when the competitor's is not.

**Normalisation.** For each kept frame compute per-channel means over the crop; apply
`sharp.linear([kR,kG,kB],[0,0,0])` so each channel matches the *film's own median* channel means across
the span. This removes drift — a lamp ageing, a dirty lens, a dimming ramp — without pretending magenta
is daylight. Stated limit: **it cannot recover colour a monochromatic lamp never recorded.**

**Stabilisation.** Apply accumulated integer `(dx,dy)` via `sharp.extract()` on a 96 %-of-frame window,
so shifted frames are cropped rather than padded. When the residual stays high, the film **cuts to a new
Kapitel** with a 6-frame black gap and a caption `Kamera verrutscht · 12.09.`. Honest and visible beats
a smeared warp.

**Burn-in overlay** (`Zahlen einblenden`) uses the technique already proven in
`imageService.addOfflineOverlay`: composite an SVG per frame with sharp. Bottom-left
`Tag 24 · 14:02 · 24,1 °C · 58 % rF · VPD 1,21`; bottom-right, when a `GrowEvent` falls within 10 min,
`💧 Anna · 2,0 l`; a 3 px progress bar. ~8 ms/frame → 14 s for 1 787 frames.

**Encoding.** `ffmpeg -framerate 25 -i %d.jpeg -c:v libx264 -preset veryfast -crf 23 -pix_fmt yuv420p
-vf scale=1280:-2 -movflags +faststart -f mp4`. **Not x265** — the existing rolling timelapses use x265,
which iOS Photos, WhatsApp and half of Android will not play. A file people cannot open is not a
deliverable.

### 8.4 How a video LEAVES the product

```
POST /film/:device_id      { from, to, only_daylight, normalise, stabilise, burn_in }
                           → { film_id, status:'queued' }   (idempotent on the recipe hash)
GET  /film/:film_id/status → { status, frames_total, frames_kept, rejected:{…}, chapters, eta_s }
GET  /film/:film_id?token=<imageToken>
     Content-Type: video/mp4
     Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="zelt-1_tag12-tag40_2026-10-14.mp4"
     Accept-Ranges: bytes            ← required, or iOS Safari will not play it inline
     Cache-Control: private, max-age=86400
```

- **Filename:** `<devicename-slug>_tag<from>-tag<to>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.mp4`, ASCII-folded, lowercase,
  no device_id. `zelt-1_tag12-tag40_2026-10-14.mp4`.
- **Three real routes off the phone**, and the UI says which one you get: (1)
  `navigator.share({files:[new File([blob],name,{type:'video/mp4'})]})` where supported (Safari 15+,
  Chrome Android) → **the file lands in WhatsApp/Telegram/Fotos directly**, ~20 lines, feature-detected;
  (2) Android/desktop `<a download>`; (3) iOS without Web Share — the video opens in a tab and the UI
  says *„Lange drücken → In ‚Fotos' sichern"*. That is the actual mechanism; pretending otherwise is how
  round one failed.
- **A pasteable link:** the `?token=<imageToken>` URL plays for 30 days with no login; longer via a
  `ShareLink` with `webcam:true`. **A share that produces a login wall is not a share.**
- **Storage, honestly:** a new `Film` collection with its own `data: Buffer`. The 16 MB BSON cap means
  **v1 caps a film at 12 MB / 90 s**; longer spans drop frames and the UI says
  `Für die Länge werden Bilder ausgelassen`. GridFS is the correct fix and it is v1.1 (§13.5).
- The rolling `1d`/`1w`/`1m` timelapses keep working untouched, re-encoded to x264, and remain the
  zero-configuration film for anyone who never opens the Film tab.

### 8.5 Image correlation — five concrete couplings

1. **Chart scrub ↔ film strip.** The strip is a lane of the chart on the same x-scale, sharing one
   cursor. Uneven capture reads honestly as gaps. `Image.timestampEnd` renders an mp4 as a bracket, not
   a point. v1 fetches thumbnails through the existing `?width=160` resize (N requests, `max-age=3600`,
   HTTP/2 — survivable); a sprite+index endpoint is v1.1.
2. **Every event carries its frame.** `GrowEvent.auto_image_id`, §6. Shown in the entry, in the
   per-plant report, in the burn-in overlay and in the double-feed guard.
3. **Befund → evidence.** Numeric Befunde push `/verlauf` with the span applied; image Befunde open the
   Wischvergleich zoomed to the highest-scoring tile.
4. **Momentaufnahme.** Tapping any frame anywhere opens `/moment/:ts`: the frame full size, the readings
   at that instant, the setpoint at that instant, and any event within ±30 min. *Picking a picture shows
   its numbers.*
5. **The change series.** `FrameMetrics.tiles` summed per frame is a legitimate time series
   (`Bildänderung`) with no Influx allowlist change — it lives in Mongo keyed to `image_id`. Rendered as
   a chart lane it makes "someone was in the tent", "the light changed", "the fan moved the canopy"
   legible over weeks. **v1.1** — the metrics ship in v1, the lane does not.

### 8.6 Grow-aligned buckets

The rolling timelapse buckets are `Math.ceil(now/timeStep)*timeStep` — UTC days. This concept adds a
second alignment: **the day boundary of a film is lights-on**, taken from `daynight.day` in the device
timezone, so "Tag 24" in the overlay matches "Tag 24" in the diary and in GrowDiaries' week buckets. The
existing UTC-bucketed rolling timelapses are left alone; the custom film uses grow days.

### 8.7 Camera alarms — the thing nobody built

Two alarms, created by default the moment a camera is paired, both cloud-side, no firmware:

| `sensorType` | Fires when | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `webcam_silence` | No `format:'jpeg'` frame stored for > 3 h (configurable) | *„Zelt 1: Die Kamera hat seit 3 Std kein Bild geliefert."* |
| `light_mismatch` | `out_light > 50 %` for 15 min while frame brightness sits in the lights-off band — or the inverse | *„Zelt 1: Das Licht sollte an sein, das Bild ist dunkel."* |

They reuse `Alarm` storage and `handleAlarmAction` (e-mail / webhook / device log, all existing) but
need their own ~80-line evaluator ticker, because `alarm.service` only evaluates on inbound MQTT
`StatusMessage` and neither of these is a sensor threshold. `light_mismatch` is the strongest possible
demonstration of the correlation thesis: **neither the picture nor the data can detect a failed
contactor alone, and together they detect it in fifteen minutes.**

## 9. Clubs

### 9.1 Attribution without an auth rewrite

`Actor` is a device-scoped list of names the owner types once in Zelt → *Wer ist am Zelt*. No accounts,
no e-mail, no `auth.middleware` surgery. The capture sheet stamps `GrowEvent.actor_id`; the last actor
used from this browser is prefilled (`localStorage`), so the 2-tap path survives. Each actor gets a
colour used consistently in the diary, the annotation rail and the guard.

**Attribution is a label, not a gate** — anyone with the login can write as anyone. It costs ~2 days and
answers "wer hat was gemacht" for a household or small club. Real memberships (a `Membership`
collection, rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207`, loosening ~10 owner-scoped queries) are
separate, later work this concept does not pretend to have done.

### 9.2 The double feed

At sheet open, query `GrowEvent` for kind ∈ {water, feed, both} touching any selected plant (or the tent)
within the guard window. If found:

```
⚠ Tom hat alle Pflanzen vor 1 Std 30 gegossen (2,0 l).   [Bild ansehen]
[         Trotzdem speichern         ]
```

`[Bild ansehen]` opens Tom's `auto_image_id` frame — **you can see whether the soil is dark.** That is
this concept's specific contribution to the club problem: the camera turns a warning into evidence.

Default windows: water 6 h, feed 18 h, per-medium (coco 3 h / 12 h — several daily feeds are correct
there). Owner-editable. §13.10 is honest about why this is fragile.

**Double-*logging*** — the offline-retry hazard — is handled by `client_event_id`: a UUID minted at sheet
open, server upserts on `{device_id, client_event_id}`, so a retry is idempotent by construction. v1 has
no service worker: a failed save is held in `localStorage`, retried on next app focus, and renders
`⏳ noch nicht gespeichert`. **That is not capture-in-a-cellar.** Real offline is v1.1.

### 9.3 The cumulative question

`/tagebuch/pflanze/:plant_id`, §6 — totals per `product_text`, per plant, per cycle, with the actor and
date of the last dose. A plain aggregation over `GrowEvent.nutrients`. Also available for the tent
(`plant_ids: []`).

### 9.4 Conditions

A standing open fact with an owner — the thing that gets taped to the tent door. Rendered as a slim
amber strip **above the picture pair**, which is the only element in the product that outranks the
camera, correctly, because a human wrote it for another human.

```
▌ CO₂-Flasche fast leer · Tom, vor 3 Tagen                              ✓
▌ A3 nicht gießen — zu nass · Anna, vor 1 Tag                           ✓
```

Tap `✓` → `closed_by` / `closed_at`. Closed conditions stay in the Tagebuch as a span.

### 9.5 Privacy posture

Default is privacy-maximal, per the dossier and owner scope (clubs, no commercial):

- Actor names are free text and may be "T". No real name is required or requested. No location, no IP
  geolocation, no analytics SDK, no community feed.
- **Harvest weight and plant count are never prompted.** A per-device `Ernte erfassen` switch, off by
  default, adds the field. Off means the field does not exist.
- Films, frames and share links carry a token; nothing sits on a public unauthenticated URL by default.
  The public diary URL (v1.1) is opt-in per cycle, default `PSEUDONYMIZED` (`Person 1`, `Person 2`);
  `NONE | PSEUDONYMIZED | ANONYMIZED` on every export.
- **One-tap `Alle Daten dieses Zeltes löschen`** that actually deletes `Image`, `Film`, `FrameMetrics`,
  `GrowEvent`, `Plant`, `Condition`, `DeviceLog` and the Influx series — which also fixes the standing
  lie where "Clear logs" removes nothing from the diary.
- Retention shortened and stated: raw frames 90 days, metrics with the frames, films until deleted.
  **`green_frac` over time is a growth curve, i.e. a harvest calendar** — which is why it is not kept
  for three years.
- **Refused outright:** per-member dispensing, grams handed over, THC content, member identity, date of
  birth. § 26 KCanG is the club's system of record, not ours. No `KCanG-konform` badge, ever.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Lazy monitor-only.** Opens the app once a week. Sees two pictures a week apart and three lines:
the plants grew, one night was warm, nothing else moved. Zero interpretation, zero charts, zero
vocabulary. This is the type the concept is built for and it is the type every chart-first concept
serves worst.

**2 · Monitor + camera.** The Film tab is the product he bought. He picks `Ganzer Durchgang`, sees a cull
report telling him 361 garbage frames were removed and why, gets a 71-second x264 file with the day
number, temperature and his own watering events burned in, and shares it into Telegram with one tap.
Nobody else in this market ships that in October.

**3 · Controls one aspect (CO₂, heating).** His Zelt tab shows a CO₂ target and a heater socket and
*nothing else* — no humidity row, no dehumidifier alarm preset he cannot act on, because capability
comes from the per-role socket CSV, not the three-bucket collapse. His Befunde reference the setpoint he
actually set: `● CO₂ unter Ziel — 720 → 640 ppm, Ziel 900`.

**4 · Full tent.** Gets the most Befunde and the deepest drill-down: every socket in the output lane,
setpoint vs actual with time-in-range split day/night, the annotation rail showing his own config
changes. **His honest complaint is that steering lives in a fourth tab** (§13.9) — this concept
deliberately optimises for looking, not for adjusting, and he adjusts most.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** Gets nothing new from the pictures and will say so. What he gets: a chart with
a real setpoint line and raw min/max samples, `/verlauf` URLs that are stable and bookmarkable, and —
the one thing that matters to him — `FrameMetrics` and `GrowEvent` as clean documented JSON over the
existing REST surface, which is the first machine-readable grow-diary schema anyone has published. He is
not served by v1; he is *not blocked* by it, which is the honest position.

**6 · Club.** Actors, the double-feed guard with the picture as evidence, the cumulative per-plant
totals, conditions pinned above everything. The camera does specific work here that no other concept
uses it for: it settles "did you already water A3" in one tap. Refuses to hold member data.

**7 · Old device owner.** Sees §10: the same home screen, no camera, no film tab, no plant nagging, and
his settings page exactly as it was. The Befunde about his light's actual runtime versus its schedule
are the first genuinely new thing his three-year-old plug has told him.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **Most days, nothing changed.** A home screen whose honest answer is usually `○ Nichts Auffälliges`
   is the opposite of a chart, which always looks busy. The daily visitor sees a flat screen daily. The
   default left endpoint (`seit du zuletzt geschaut hast`) mitigates this for the weekly user and does
   nothing for the daily one. A competitor's busy dashboard *feels* more valuable while telling you less.

2. **`green_frac` is a weak proxy for growth and I have said so, which does not make it stronger.** It
   confounds growth with leaf angle, dimming level, defoliation, a moved fan and camera shift. Under
   magenta LED it is near-noise. It is the concept's headline visual metric and it is its softest number.

3. **Translation-only stabilisation fails the commonest real failure.** Someone knocks the camera and it
   *rotates*. We detect it and cut to a new Kapitel — honest, but a long film has visible cuts where a
   competitor's simply does not, because they never attempted stabilisation and nobody notices drift
   they were not promised would be fixed.

4. **The cull has a 60-second blind spot in both directions, and its errors are invisible.** A 90-second
   tent visit produces frames the transient test keeps; a genuinely fast real change is
   indistinguishable from a hand and gets dropped. **Culling errors are unobservable by definition** —
   the user never sees what was removed except as a count. That is the worst kind of error to have in a
   feature whose whole pitch is honesty.

5. **Films are capped at 12 MB / 90 s in v1** by the 16 MB BSON limit. The flagship artefact — the whole
   12-week run — is therefore the one that drops the most frames and looks the worst. GridFS is the right
   fix, it is well understood, and it is genuinely not in the six weeks.

6. **CPU and storage are a real cost, not a rounding error.** Two sharp decodes plus analysis per frame
   ≈ 50 ms × 2 880 frames/day. At 150 devices that is ~6 CPU-hours/day of metrics *on top of* the
   existing ffmpeg poll, and a film render is another 60–120 s of a core. Today the server is one pm2
   process with `pLimit(10)`. This needs a real job queue and probably a second box before the first
   series sells out.

7. **The majority of launch buyers have no camera.** 289 € without, 319–349 € with. The numbers-only
   Jetzt screen is honest and complete, but it is a lesser thing, and the concept's entire pitch does not
   apply to them. A concept whose centre of gravity is optional hardware is a concept betting the
   attach rate is high.

8. **The Befund ranking normaliser can hide real excursions.** A tent with historically noisy
   temperature has a large σ₁₄, so a genuine excursion scores low and sinks in the list. The `σ_floor`
   clamp is a fudge, and the fudge is visible the moment two tents are compared side by side.

9. **This is not a control surface.** Types 4 and 5 spend their time steering, and steering is in the
   fourth tab. That is a deliberate bet that monitoring outnumbers steering across the seven types — and
   it will annoy precisely the customers who spent the most money.

10. **The double-feed guard's windows are guesses that will be tapped through.** 6 h / 18 h is wrong for
    coco, where several daily feeds are correct. Per-medium defaults help; they do not fix it. A guard
    people learn to dismiss reflexively is worse than no guard, because it manufactures the belief that
    the app checked.

11. **Two picture "modes" of comparison** — pair vs numbers-only — could fairly be read as a mode by a
    hostile critic. My defence is that it is driven entirely by whether the hardware reports a camera and
    changes the instant one is paired, with no setting and no user choice anywhere. I think that defence
    holds. I also think it is the one place a critic will push hardest.
