# C37 — "Das Rezept"

**Family:** feeding · **Centre of gravity:** the feed is a recipe card you follow, in mixing order, in
your can size. German-first (de-DE), English second.

Written against `NEW-BRIEF.md`, `00-DOSSIER.md`, `00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md`, `00-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md`
and direct reads of `/home/user/terpcontrol.cloud`.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The product's central object is one card — what to mix today, in your can, in the order you must pour
it — and everything else in the app is either the reason that card says what it says, or the record of
you having followed it.**

Terp Control has no dosing hardware and will not have any. Feeding is therefore a *writing* problem, and
the owner has stated the shape of the answer: the scheme is picked once and prefills the log entry. C37
takes that literally and makes the prefilled entry a **Rezeptkarte** — not a form with defaults, but a
card that reads like the back of a bottle, in mixing order, with a running volume, and a tick-box per
line so you do not lose your place with wet hands at 2am.

The consequence that makes it a whole-product concept rather than a feature: **the detailed record the
clubs need is produced by the prefill, not by the user.** A grower who taps twice and reads nothing has
still recorded product names, doses in ml/L, resolved volumes in ml, the phase, the step index, the pH
band, the can size, the plants and the actor — because the card already knew all of it. A club member who
edits four lines produces a record of exactly the same shape with four `edited: true` flags on it. There
is no "detailed logging mode" to enter, because **the human only ever writes the difference.**

Selection is a **medium + regime pair**, the way growers say it: *Biobizz All-Mix*, *Biobizz Light-Mix*,
*Floragard Light*, *Green House Feeding*, *BioTabs*. All-Mix is heavily pre-fertilised, so the card's
entire body for the first three weeks is the sentence **"Heute nicht füttern."** with a watering-only
button under it. A scheme model that cannot say that is not modelling the domain.

## 2. Information architecture

Four destinations per device in an `ion-tab-bar`, plus the existing split-pane menu for account-level
things. The tab bar is new — the app has none today — and is the single navigation change.

| Route | Tab label (de/en) | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/device/:id` | **Heute** / Today | The home screen. Ist-values, **die Rezeptkarte**, open conditions, the film strip. |
| `/device/:id/verlauf` | **Verlauf** / History | The chart. Small multiples, goal vs actual, EC ladder, annotation rail, film-strip axis. |
| `/device/:id/buch` | **Buch** / Book | The written record: day-per-row document, `Bilanz` (cumulative per plant/product/person), the film. |
| `/device/:id/geraet` | **Gerät** / Device | Targets, sockets, alarms, camera, **Substrat & Dünger**, share links, maintenance. |

Sub-surfaces (pushed, not tabs):

- `/device/:id/rezept` — the full Rezeptkarte as a page (the home block is the same component at
  `density="home"`). Opened by tapping the card header, or deep-linked from a reminder.
- `/device/:id/film/:run_id` — the film player + download. Also reached from Heute and Buch.
- `/device/:id/pflanzen` — the plant list. **Does not exist in the nav until a plant exists.**
- `/device/:id/bilanz/:plant_id` — one plant's cumulative sheet.
- Unchanged: `/list`, `/shares`, `/account`, `/classes`, `/testmode`, `/demo`, `/login`.

`/device/:id/charts` and `/device/:id/diary` are kept as permanent redirects to `verlauf` / `buch` with
their query strings passed through — every share link and chart preset in circulation is a frozen query
string (dossier §1.6) and breaking them is not acceptable.

Share/view-only, demo and public modes: the Rezeptkarte renders **read-only with the buttons removed**
(not disabled — removed) when `resolveDeviceAccessInfo().canEdit === false`. A shared `Buch` shows feeds
with actor names replaced by `·` unless the share was created with `attribution: true`.

| No regime chosen, data flowing | normal | normal | `Was gibst du deinen Pflanzen? · Substrat & Dünger wählen ›` and, under it, a plain `Gegossen` / `Gefüttert (frei eintragen)` pair so the user is never blocked from logging |
| Camera silent > 6 h while device online | — | — | film block turns amber: `Kamera meldet sich nicht seit 06:40. Letztes Bild heute 06:12.` + `Prüfen ›` |
| Feed already logged today | — | — | card header becomes `HEUTE SCHON GEFÜTTERT · 09:14 · Anna`, body collapses to the saved lines read-only, buttons become `Nochmal füttern` (secondary only) |

**The offline row is the concept's quiet advantage**: the one thing a grower does at the tent needs no
telemetry. The card is a document, not a live view, so a dead wifi link degrades the top of the screen and
nothing else.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

Four mechanisms, none of which is a preference, a toggle, an accordion or an onboarding question.

### M1 — "Nur die Abweichung ist Arbeit"

**The detail level is produced by the machine for everybody. The human writes only the difference.**

This is the core. The record written by two taps and the record written by twenty taps have **identical
schemas and identical field coverage**:

```jsonc
// Two taps. Nothing edited.
{ "kind":"feed", "canLitres":10, "phase":"flower", "regimeStep":7,
  "lines":[{"productName":"Bio-Grow","doseMlPerL":2,"volumeMl":20,"order":2,"edited":false},
           {"productName":"Bio-Bloom","doseMlPerL":4,"volumeMl":40,"order":3,"edited":false},
           {"productName":"Top-Max","doseMlPerL":2,"volumeMl":20,"order":4,"edited":false}],
  "phTarget":[6.0,6.5], "plantIds":["*"], "actorId":"a_ben", "deviations":0 }

// Twenty taps. Same shape.
{ …, "lines":[{"productName":"Bio-Grow","doseMlPerL":1.5,"volumeMl":15,"order":2,"edited":true}, …],
  "ecMeasured":1.62, "phMeasured":6.1, "runoffEc":2.1,
  "plantIds":["p_a1","p_a2"], "note":"A2 hängt", "deviations":4 }
```

The club's "very detailed logging and reporting" and the stoner's "it thinks for me" are **the same
feature seen from two distances**, not two builds of a screen. There is nothing to switch on to get
detail; there is only something to *correct* if the machine guessed wrong.

### M2 — "Eine Zeile, drei Tiefen, ohne Falte"

Every row in the product is the same three-part shape, all three parts on one line, all three always
visible, in decreasing type weight from left to right:

```
[ was ]                    [ wieviel für dich ]   [ woher die Zahl ]
  Bio-Bloom                       40 ml               4 ml/L
  Temperatur                    24,1 °C          Ziel 25,0 · ±1,5
  A3 nicht gießen                                 Ben · gestern
  EC-Ziel                     1,8 – 2,2      Wasser 0,4 + Plan 1,4–1,8
```

The impaired reader takes the middle column and stops. The techie reads right and taps it — tapping the
**right** part opens the workings (the regime step, the tolerance, the source URL, the raw series); tapping
the **middle** part edits the value. Nothing is behind a chevron, a "Mehr" link or an accordion. The right
column is smaller but it is never below 13 px and never below 4.5:1 contrast — it is *quieter*, not
*hidden*, and this distinction is the whole difference between ranked density and a mode.

### M3 — "Die Karte wächst aus deinen eigenen Einträgen"

The card's line set is **reveal by data, scoped to this device and this run, always reversible, and always
visibly incomplete**:

- Lines from the regime step are always present.
- An optional line (`EC messen`, `pH messen`, `Runoff-EC`, `Wassertemperatur`, `Gießmenge je Pflanze`,
  `Foto`, `Notiz`, `Spülen`, `Blattdünger`, `Wurzelstimulator`, `Pflanzen wählen`, `Wer war das`)
  becomes a **prefilled line** once it has been used in ≥ 2 of the last 5 saves on this device.
- The ones that have not are a single hairline row at the bottom, **always in the same place, always the
  same twelve items, in the same order**: `+ EC messen  + Notiz  + Foto  + mehr`.
- Long-press a promoted line → `Diese Zeile nicht mehr vorschlagen`. It returns to the hairline row.

**Why this is not adaptive-ordering-that-hurts-the-stoner:** the *set* is closed and published, the
promoted lines only ever append below the regime lines (they never displace or reorder them), the demoted
ones live in one fixed location, and promotion needs two deliberate uses — it cannot happen by accident.
The card a user saw yesterday is the card they see today plus at most one row.

### M4 — "Der Haken ist für die Hände, nicht für die Datenbank"

The tick-boxes have **no data consequence**. Ticking line 3 stores nothing. The card is a place-keeper for
someone standing over a 10 L can with one wet hand; the save button is the only thing that writes.

Consequently the meticulous user and the careless user save identical records, and the careless user is
never punished with a half-empty entry. The only interactions that change data are:

- editing a value (`edited: true`),
- long-pressing a line → `weggelassen` (`skipped: true`),
- adding an optional line.

This is what lets one card serve both without a "quick save" shortcut anywhere. **There is no fast path,
because the normal path is already two taps.**

### The mode-smell test, applied to this concept

| Thing that differs between users | Driven by | Mode? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CO₂ column exists | `hardwareInfo` reports SCD4x | No — capability |
| Socket lane on the chart | `hardwareInfo.sockets` | No — capability |
| Plant chips exist | `Plant` count > 0 | No — data |
| Actor chip exists | `Actor` count > 1 on this device | No — data |
| `EC messen` is prefilled | this device's last 5 saves | No — behaviour, closed set, reversible, footprint visible |
| Card body says "nicht füttern" | the chosen regime's step | No — domain |
| **Nothing** | a user preference | — |

There is no `localStorage['app-settings-expert']` in this concept. That key is deleted.

## 5. The chart — "Verlauf"

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision 2). Chart.js /
ng2-charts / chartjs-adapter-luxon deleted at the same time.

**Stacked small multiples, one shared x-axis, one linked crosshair** (`grid[]` + `axisPointer.link`),
≈ 110 px per panel on a phone. Never two scales on one panel unless physically coupled.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 24,1 °C   58 %   1,21 kPa   EC 1,62   Tag 12   14:20     │ ← pinned scrub header
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Temperatur °C                                     28 ─   │
│ ░░░░░████░░░░░░████░░░░░░████░░░░░  night shading (out_light)
│  ╌╌╌╌┌────┐╌╌╌╌┌────┐╌╌╌╌  dashed stepped setpoint 25/21 │
│  ▁▂▃▅▆▅▃▂  solid actual, min/max envelope behind    18 ─  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Luftfeuchte %                                     70 ─   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ VPD kPa   ▒▒▒▒ band moves at stage change         1,6 ─  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ EC (Plan vs. gemessen)                            2,4 ─  │
│  ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  markArea = Ziel je Schritt│
│  ●        ●        ●   gemessen    ○ Runoff       0,4 ─  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Ausgänge   ▬▬▬  Licht   ▬ ▬ ▬ Heizung   ▬▬ Entfeuchter   │ ← state timeline
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 🥄  🥄    ⚠      ●          🥄        │ annotation rail  │
│ [▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪][▪]  │ film strip axis  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Goal vs actual.** A **dashed stepped** `markLine` for the setpoint — it steps at `daynight.day` /
`daynight.night` between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`, parsed from `device.configuration`
server-side and returned on the device access payload. Behind it a soft `markArea` band. Band source is
one of three, one at a time, and **labelled in the panel header** so the user knows which promise is drawn:
`Alarmgrenzen` (the user's own declared thresholds) > `Ziel ± Toleranz` > `VPD-Empfehlung (Blüte)`.
Between actual and setpoint, a signed deviation fill — never a fill to the axis.

**Day/night.** `markArea` from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the `daynight` schedule when
`out_light` has no data, in an explicit timezone (Europe/Berlin from the device, not the browser). A
failed contactor then shows as a **missing band**, which is a diagnostic. Sunrise/sunset ramps from
`lights.sunrise` / `lights.sunset` render as gradient edges.

**The EC panel is the feeding-native one.** The regime's per-step target EC is drawn as a stepped
`markArea` along the x-axis, moving as the schedule advances. `ec_basis: 'delta_over_source'` (CANNA-style
"EC+") is resolved before drawing by adding the run's `sourceWaterEc`, and the panel header says so:
`EC-Ziel 1,8–2,2 · Leitungswasser 0,4 + Plan 1,4–1,8`. Measured EC from Feeds is a scatter series; runoff
EC is a hollow scatter. A runoff/input divergence > 20 % draws a `markPoint` with `Runoff 30 % höher`.

**Annotations.** A 20 px rail *under* the x-axis, max two rows: row 1 human events (🥄 feed, 💧 water,
🌱 stage, 📝 note), row 2 device events (alarms, config changes, reboots). Pixel-proximity clustering at
10 px bins with a count badge. Full-height dashed lines for stage changes only. Category chips above the
chart reuse the existing `collectLogCategories` / `matchesLogCategory` helpers. **Tapping a 🥄 opens the
Rezeptkarte that was saved** — the chart is a way into the record, not a decoration over it.

**Outputs** get their own state-timeline lane; region length is duration, null renders as a gap. No 0/1
lines on a value axis ever again.

**Mobile rules.** One finger = page scroll. Two fingers = pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`). Tap/long-press =
crosshair, which pins the header above the chart — no floating tooltip, because a fingertip occludes it.
Axis labels are never hidden; below 320 px they drop to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel
title. `sampling: 'minmax'` on the plot (a `mean` line is a lie about a bang-bang controller), LTTB for
the navigator. Okabe-Ito palette. `darkMode: 'auto'` replaces the hand-written theme + `MutationObserver`.

**Prerequisite, not optional:** `data.service.ts` interpolates `measure`, `from`, `to` and `interval` raw
into Flux and the `limit(n:50000)` guard is piped after `yield()`. Widening the measure vocabulary without
fixing that ships an injection. It is in v1 scope below.

`DeviceLog` is left alone. Its legacy traps (`deleted` meaning "hidden", the server's un-prefixed
`diary-plant-lifecycle` rows, seven English-prose regexes) are inherited by the **Buch** as a second,
read-only source that is merged into the day rows — never migrated, never rewritten. Nothing in this
concept depends on `DeviceLog` being clean.

`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a **one-time backfill** that creates a `Run` per inferred cycle and
then is deleted. It is a 750-line runtime string-comparison algorithm today and it forks history when a
plant is renamed.

The photo is the day's midday keyframe (§8). The three summary lines are computed server-side from raw

wrong. Coverage below 80 % greys the summary line and appends `(nur 61 % Messdaten)`.

Second tab of the Buch: **Bilanz**.

```
  │ A3                   Wasser 18 L   ·   7 Gaben       │

```

This is the club critic's unanswered cumulative question — *"How much Bio-Bloom has plant A3 had this
cycle, and from whom?"* — as one `$unwind` + `$group` aggregation over `Entry`.

### 6.3 How a user who ignores plants is untaxed

If `Plant` count is 0 for the run:

- `Entry.plantIds` is `['*']`. Written by the server, never chosen by the user.
- **The `Alle Pflanzen ▾` row on the Rezeptkarte is not rendered.** Not greyed, not "All" — absent.
- The `Pflanzen` route is not in any menu.
- The Bilanz shows exactly one block, headed `Alle Pflanzen`.
- The Buch shows no plant chips on any row.

Plants appear when a user taps `Pflanzen anlegen` once, offered in exactly two places: the run header in
the Buch, and inside the Rezeptkarte's `+ mehr` hairline row. Creating them is one screen: a number
(`Wie viele?`), which mints `A1…An` with editable labels. Retro-assignment is offered once, as a single
question — *"Die 9 bisherigen Gaben allen Pflanzen zuordnen?"* — and never nags again.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — capture flow and tap counts

### 7.1 Tap counts, counted honestly from the Heute screen already being open

| Case | Taps | Sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled feed, nothing changed, no plants | **2** | `Gefüttert · 10 L` → confirm toast auto-dismisses. (The first tap is opening the app to Heute; the card is already rendered and prefilled, so strictly it is **1 tap after the app is open.** Counted as 2 to include the launch tap.) |
| Water only | **2** | `Nur gegossen` |
| Different can size | **4** | `10 L · Kanne ▾` → `5 L` → (card recomputes, every volume halves) → `Gefüttert · 5 L` |
| Subset of plants | **5** | `Alle Pflanzen ▾` → `A1` off → `A2` off → `Fertig` → `Gefüttert` |
| One dose changed | **5** | tap `40 ml` → `[−][ range ][+]` (the existing `value-edit-row`) → `36` → collapse → `Gefüttert` |
| Add an EC reading (first time) | **5 + keypad** | `+ EC messen` → numeric input → `1,62` → done → `Gefüttert` |
| Add an EC reading (after promotion by M3) | **3 + keypad** | tap the `EC` line → `1,62` → `Gefüttert` |
| Full club entry: plants, EC, pH, runoff, note, photo, actor | **~14** | and it produces the same JSON shape as the 2-tap one, with more fields set |
| Double-feed override | **+1** | `Trotzdem füttern` before the save button re-enables |

The card's default can size is `Run.canLitres`, set once during regime selection (`Wie groß ist deine
Kanne?` — 5 / 10 / 12 / 20 L / eigene). Changing it on the card asks once whether to remember it:
`Immer 5 L?` as an inline chip, not a dialog.

### 7.2 Schedule following — feed events, not wall-clock weeks

`Run.regimeStep` advances **by saved feed events** (`Schritt 7 von 13`), with a soft calendar mapping shown
under it (`Blüte Woche 3`). Growers feed Tue/Fri and slip; advancing by wall clock desynchronises the card
from reality within two weeks.

Three automatic corrections, each visible and each reversible in one tap:

1. **Flip detection.** When `out_light` history shows the photoperiod dropping to ≤ 12 h for 3 consecutive
   days, the server sets `Run.flipAt` and writes an entry: `Blüte erkannt am 02.03. · Woche 1`. The card
   header gains `Blüte erkannt · ändern`. No bottle-brand calculator can do this; Terp Control owns the
   device that knows.
2. **Autoflower.** If the run is marked auto (one checkbox at run creation, alongside the strain name),
   there is no flip event and a `×0.4` strength multiplier is applied with the reason shown on the card:
   `Automatic · 40 % Stärke`. Essentially no manufacturer chart has an auto column; this is a genuine gap
   filled without borrowing anything.
3. **Run length reshaping.** If the run's declared flower length exceeds the regime's, the card **repeats
   the last non-flush step and says so** — `Schritt 11 wiederholt (Plan endet in Woche 9)` — rather than
   silently truncating. Silent truncation is the failure mode that makes the whole feature untrustworthy.

**Reminders**, the stoner half of the owner's brief: a push/e-mail at `Run.regimeStep`'s due date reading
*"Zelt 1: heute mischen — Bio-Grow 20 ml, Bio-Bloom 40 ml, Top-Max 20 ml in 10 L."* The notification body
*is* the card. Deep-links to `/device/:id/rezept`. Delivered over the existing alarm mail/webhook path
(`alarm.service.ts`), which already supports Telegram and Discord by URL.

### 7.3 Double-feed safety

On opening the Rezeptkarte, the client asks `GET /entries/recent?device=&plants=&hours=`. If any `Entry`
with `kind:'feed'` intersects the selected plants within `Regime.minFeedIntervalHours` (default 20):

```
 ║ ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ ║
 ║ │ ⚠ Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.         │ ║
 ║ │   Bio-Grow 20 ml · Bio-Bloom 40 ml · 10 L        │ ║
 ║ │   [ Trotzdem füttern ]        [ Abbrechen ]      │ ║
 ║ └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ ║
```

The primary save button is **disabled** (not hidden) until `Trotzdem füttern` is tapped. The saved entry
carries `overrode: { entry_id, actorId, minutesSince }`, which the Buch renders as `2. Gabe an A1–A3
innerhalb 1 Std 30 · bewusst` — so the club can see later that it was deliberate, not an accident.

### 7.4 What is stored, and offline

`clientEventId` is a UUID minted when the **card is opened**, sent with the save, and the server upserts on
the unique index `{device_id, clientEventId}`. A retry after a timeout can never double-log — which in a
shared record is indistinguishable from a real double feed.

Offline in a cellar: v1 ships a **localStorage outbox** (`tc.outbox.entries`), flushed on the next
foreground event, with the card showing `Gespeichert · wird gesendet` and the Buch showing the row with a
pending clock icon. This works only while the app stays open in the browser. A real service worker
(`@angular/service-worker`, a new dependency and a build change) is v1.1 and is named as such, not implied.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

**The camera is the second headline of this concept, and its native connection to a recipe is: a feed is a
hypothesis and the film is the result.**

### 8.1 What ships in v1 — one film per run, in your hand

Today the cloud already stores a jpeg every 30 s and builds three rolling mp4s hourly. Three things are
wrong with those as a *product*:

1. They are **libx265 CRF 30, no `-pix_fmt`, no `-movflags +faststart`**
   (`server/src/services/image.service.ts:564-608`, verified). Firefox cannot play HEVC-in-MP4 at all, many
   Android galleries refuse to import it, and without `faststart` the moov atom is at the end so nothing
   streams. **This is the reason nobody has held a Terp Control timelapse.**
2. They are rolling windows (`1d` / `1w` / `1m`), so they have no relation to a grow.
3. Their source frames are thinned away (`IMAGE_THINNING_TIERS`: 1/h after 30 days), so a 12-week film can
   never be rebuilt from history.

v1 fixes all three.

**The Durchgangsfilm.** One mp4 per `Run`, extended nightly at 03:00, never rebuilt from scratch.

- **Three frames per day**, chosen at 25 %, 50 % and 75 % through the *measured* photoperiod. Constant
  phase offset means constant light angle and constant dimming state, which is what makes a 12-week film
  stop flickering.
- 84 days × 3 = **252 frames at 24 fps ≈ 10,5 s**. A real, watchable, sendable object — not a 3-second
  flicker and not a 4-minute chore.
- Encoded **libx264, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-crf 23`, `-movflags +faststart`, 1280×720**. Plays in every
  browser, imports into every phone gallery, uploads to Telegram and Discord unmodified.

**Frame preservation.** The three chosen frames of each day are re-saved as `format: 'keyframe'` (a new
enum value in `images.model.ts`; the thinner filters `format:'jpeg'` only and will therefore never touch
them). The nightly job appends only yesterday's three frames to the film via ffmpeg concat, so CPU is
constant per device per day instead of quadratic in run length.

### 8.2 Culling and normalisation — the content problem, specified

Run at cull time, once per day per device, on 64×36 downscales using `sharp` (already a dependency):

| Step | Rule | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light cull** | Keep only frames where `out_light` at that timestamp ≥ 40 % of the day's max. | Half a 12/12 run is black. |
| **Phase pick** | Within the lit period, take the frames nearest 25/50/75 % of its duration. | Constant sun angle; no flicker. |
| **Human / door suppression** | Drop a candidate whose global mean brightness differs > 25 % from the day's other two candidates **and** whose lower-third mean-absolute-difference to the previous kept frame exceeds 12 %. Fall back to the nearest frame ±20 min. | Hands and open doors both change the bottom of the frame and the exposure. |
| **Dedup** | Drop a candidate whose 64×36 MAD to the previous kept frame is < 1,5 %. | A frozen camera should read as a gap, not as content. |
| **Exposure / WB normalisation** | Per kept frame, measure mean L and mean a/b in a fixed centre rectangle; apply `sharp.linear(gain, offset)` + `.tint()` mapping it to the rolling 7-frame median of the run. | Magenta LED vs. daylight through an open door, over 12 weeks. This is the single most visible quality win. |
| **Camera-moved detection** | If the top 20 % of the frame (tent ceiling, mostly static) has a MAD > 8 % to the previous kept frame, write `message-camera-moved` and mark a chapter boundary in the film. | v1 **detects and reports**; it does not stabilise. Correcting shake is not a six-week job and pretending otherwise is how timelapses get deferred again. |

Every kept frame gets a stamp row:

```ts
FrameStamp { image_id, device_id, run_id, at, runDay, phase,
             temperature, humidity, vpd, co2?, lightPct, outputs: string[],
             entryIdWithin24h? }
```

Three Influx point queries per device per day. The whole run's stamps are one ~250-row JSON, fetched once.

### 8.3 How a video LEAVES the product

```
GET /image/:device_id/film?run=<run_id>&token=<imageToken>
→ 200 video/mp4
  Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_herbst-26_2026-10-14.mp4"
```

Filename is `terpcontrol_<device slug>_<run slug>_<ISO date>.mp4`, umlauts transliterated, lowercased,
non-alphanumerics collapsed to `-`. `imageToken` is the existing 30-day URL-embeddable token
(dossier §1.8), so the URL works when pasted into a Telegram or Discord upload without a login — that is
the difference between a share and a link to a login page.

The player page has one primary button: `Film speichern` (`<a download href=…>`). On iOS Safari the mp4
opens in the native player with the system share sheet, which is stated in the UI (`Auf dem iPhone öffnet
sich der Player — dort teilen`) rather than silently failing.

**Burned-in captions**, so the file is self-describing after it leaves: ffmpeg `drawtext` renders
`Tag 42 · Blüte · 24,3 °C · 56 % · VPD 1,2` bottom-left on every frame, and for the 6 frames following a
feed, `Bio-Grow 2 ml/L · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L` bottom-right. A user posting the film into a forum posts the
data with it. (Requires ffmpeg built with libfreetype — a Docker image check, listed in v1 scope.)

**No branded footer, no watermark.** § 6 KCanG bans cannabis advertising and sponsoring with fines to
€30,000, and an auto-appended "Powered by Terp Control" on thousands of German cannabis grow posts is the
single riskiest string available. The product name appears in the *filename* only.

### 8.4 Image correlation — both directions, plus the one only this concept has

- **Chart → picture.** The `Verlauf` crosshair pins the nearest keyframe as a 96 px thumbnail in the
  scrubbing header. Delivered as **one sprite + JSON index per visible window** (Mux storyboard pattern:
  ≤ 100 tiles at 256×160), not N requests.
- **Picture → numbers.** The film player's scrub bar renders the `FrameStamp` under the video, live, with
  no per-frame request. Tapping the stamp opens `Verlauf` at that timestamp.
- **Feed → response.** Every 🥄 in the film and in the Buch offers `Was danach passierte`. In v1 that is a
  **Vorher/Nachher pair** built from keyframes already stored — the last frame before the feed and the
  frame 72 h after — side by side, each with its stamp, plus a `Film ab hier ▶` button that seeks the
  Durchgangsfilm to that day. Free, because both frames exist. The rendered standalone 72-hour clip is v1.1.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Gabe vom 02.03. · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L               │
│  ┌──────────────┐        ┌──────────────┐         │
│  │  02.03 12:00 │        │  05.03 12:00 │         │
│  │              │        │              │         │
│  └──────────────┘        └──────────────┘         │
│  24,0 °C · 57 %          24,6 °C · 55 %           │
│  EC 1,6                  Runoff 2,1               │
│           [ Film ab hier ▶ ]                      │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 8.5 The camera alarm

`Kamera stumm`: if no jpeg has been stored for 6 h while `lastseen` is inside 10 min, the server writes a
`severity: 1` device log and fires the device's normal alert path (mail / webhook / Telegram). Resolves
automatically on the next frame. This is the gap the camera critic named — freshness honesty only helps
someone who happens to look.

## 9. Clubs

**Posture: attribution without accounts. Authorisation is deliberately left alone in v1.**

- **`Actor`** is a device-scoped name the owner types once (`Gerät → Wer ist am Zelt`). No login, no email,
  no invitation, no `auth.middleware` surgery. The Rezeptkarte's actor chip defaults to the last actor used
  from this browser and is one tap to change. `Entry.actorId` is stamped at save. ~2 days of work,
  attribution in v1.
- **Real memberships are v2** and the upgrade path is stated: an `Actor` gains an optional `user_id`, and
  `auth.middleware.ts:172` gains a membership lookup. Nothing written in v1 has to be rewritten.
- **Double feed** — §7.3. This is the most expensive thing that happens in a shared room and it is the
  only place in this concept where a deliberate extra tap is *added* rather than removed.
- **Conditions.** `Condition { text, plantIds?, openedBy, openedAt, closedBy?, closedAt? }` is the
  `Offen (n)` block on Heute. An open fact with an owner, not an event and not a task. Created from the
  `+` on that block, from any Buch row's overflow, or from the Rezeptkarte's `+ mehr`. Closing writes
  `closedBy`. The block is **absent when nothing is open** — the tent door with no notes on it.
- **Cumulative** — §6.2 Bilanz answers "how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had, and from whom".

**Privacy posture — private/prohibition mode is the default and there is no switch to change it:**

- No harvest weight and no plant-count field anywhere in v1. The Bilanz counts **millilitres of nutrient**,
  which is a purchasing fact, not evidence of scale.
- Actor names are free text with the hint `Vorname genügt.` No real-name field, no email, no DOB.
- No per-member dispensing record, ever. That is § 26 KCanG territory and probable Art. 9 GDPR health data
  for something a grow controller has no need to hold. Clubs that must file § 26 use Cannanas or 420cloud;
  Terp Control's value to them is the **device-attested environmental record** those platforms cannot
  produce, and that record contains zero personal data.
- Share links get an `attribution: boolean` (default **false**). A shared Buch shows `·` where actor names
  would be.
- The account owner can delete any `Entry`, any `Condition` and the whole `Run` including its film. The
  record is never immutable. Tamper-evidence protects a regulated club and victimises an unregulated
  grower; making it opt-in per club is a v2 question and not a v1 default.
- No location, no analytics SDK, no ad SDK, EU hosting stated on the page.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens Heute maybe weekly. Sees four numbers with their targets under them
and a freshness dot that tells the truth. Never touches the Rezeptkarte, so it sits there prefilled and
ignored — the cost of it is one card of vertical space. Gets `Verlauf` with actual axes and a stated
time-in-band, which is the first version of this chart that answers "is my tent OK" without being read
like a spectrogram. **Worst-served of the seven, honestly**: the concept's centre is a card he does not use.

**2 — Monitoring with a camera.** The primary beneficiary of §8. Gets a 10-second Durchgangsfilm that
plays in his browser, downloads with a sensible filename, and carries the numbers burned into it. Scrubbing
the chart moves the picture and picking a picture shows its numbers. Gets an alarm when the camera goes
quiet — the first time that has been true. His timelapse is finally normalised across twelve weeks instead
of strobing between magenta and daylight.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Reveal-by-capability means his humidity panel, humidity
target row and dehumidifier alarm preset **do not exist**, because `hardwareInfo.sockets` never listed one.
His CO₂ panel exists because the SCD4x is reported. He is never nagged about kit he does not own, and the
change is per-role, not the current three-bucket collapse that fails open to `'full'`.

**4 — Full tent.** Gets the socket state-timeline lane, the setpoint band on every panel he actually
controls, and day/night derived from the measured output so a failed contactor shows as a missing band.
Honest limit: the firmware still has five socket roles and `dehumidifier` still doubles as the cooler, so
his humidifier and exhaust fan remain unrepresentable. This concept does not fix that and does not pretend
to; it labels the dehumidifier lane `Entfeuchter / Kühlung` so the ambiguity is at least visible.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Gets the least from the centre of gravity and the most from its plumbing. Every
new collection is a documented REST resource with the same `x-api-key`-style shape; `POST /entries`
accepts a Feed from his own script, which means his EC probe can write into the same record his card
writes into, and it shows up on the EC panel next to the plan. The Flux-injection fix and the raw-sample
scorecard endpoint are in v1 for correctness reasons and serve him for free. Still no output write API —
named as out of scope, not hand-waved.

**6 — Club.** The type this concept was built for. `Actor` chips give attribution in v1 without an auth
rewrite; the double-feed guard is the only added friction in the product and it is added exactly where a
shared room is expensive; `Bilanz` reconstructs who fed what to which plants; `Condition` is the note on
the tent door. The record is deletable by the owner and contains no member identity, so it is useful to the
club and useless to anyone building a case.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Gets the Rezeptkarte, the Buch and the Bilanz in full, because feeding never
needed hardware. Loses nothing: the fan/light/plug settings components are untouched. His device stops
being a second-class citizen in the nav without anybody having to port his settings screen.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **Type 1 is worst-served.** A monitoring-only grower who never feeds sees a large prefilled card he
   never uses occupying the best real estate below the fold-line. The mitigation — collapsing it after N
   days of disuse — would be reveal-by-behaviour applied to the product's centre, which is exactly the
   unpredictability the brief warns about. I have chosen to leave the card at full size and accept the
   cost. If the owner disagrees, the honest fix is to collapse it to its header after 21 days with no feed
   and no water, one tap to reopen, and to say so in the changelog.

2. **The regime content is a permanent editorial liability.** Eight seeded schedules means eight things
   that go stale, eight `retrieved_at` dates that will embarrass us in 2028, and eight sources that could
   ask for removal. The `Zahlen geprüft am` line turns that into a feature, but somebody has to actually
   re-verify. Without a named owner for that job the feature quietly rots, and rotten dosing numbers burn
   crops. German IP counsel on UrhG §§ 87a–87e must happen before any brand data ships, not after.

3. **The M3 promotion rule is the weakest part of the no-modes argument.** It *is* behaviour-driven
   variation between users, which is the mechanism the brief explicitly says to defend. I have bounded it
   hard — closed published set, fixed location for demoted lines, promoted lines never displace regime
   lines, two uses required, reversible by long-press — but a critic can fairly say that two users of the
   same product see different card bodies. My defence is that the difference is one row's position, never a
   missing capability, and that removing M3 entirely costs only a slightly longer card. **If a judge rules
   it a mode, delete it; the concept survives.**

4. **Attribution without authorisation is a half-truth in a club.** An `Actor` chip is a claim, not a
   proof — anyone with the login can stamp Anna's name on a feed. For a household and for a small
   Anbauverein that is genuinely enough, and it is what makes it a two-day job instead of a two-month one.
   For a club that later has a dispute, it is worthless. The upgrade path is designed but the honest
   sentence is: **v1 tells you what probably happened, not what provably happened.**

5. **Three frames a day is a choice with a real downside.** It gives a holdable 10-second film and constant
   CPU, but a grower who wants to watch the leaves move over one night cannot — that is the existing rolling
   `1d` timelapse's job, and it stays, but the two artifacts now have overlapping purposes and users will
   ask why there are two. Consolidating them means either a slow film or an expensive one.

6. **Exposure normalisation can make things look wrong.** Mapping every frame toward a rolling median will
   suppress *real* changes in tent lighting — dimming the lamp for late flower, or adding a second lamp —
   and a grower diagnosing a light problem from the film would be misled. Mitigation is a `Original`
   toggle on the player, which is a per-view control and not a mode, but it is one more thing on the screen.

7. **No irrigation hardware means the schedule can always be wrong and never knows it.** The card says
   "10 L" because the user said their can is 10 L. If they poured 6, the record is a lie and nothing in the
   system can detect it. Every number in the Bilanz inherits that. This is a property of the domain, not of
   the design, but the Bilanz's authority is weaker than its typography suggests.

8. **The tab bar plus the split-pane menu is two navigation systems.** The app has one today. Four tabs is
   correct for mobile and wrong for the desktop the dossier says is an unexploited advantage; on a wide
   screen the four tabs should become a persistent left rail beside the split pane, and I have not designed
   that layout. It is a gap, not a mistake, but a critic will find it.

9. **The Buch's day rows depend on server-side raw-sample aggregation that does not exist yet**, and
   getting time-in-band right on raw Influx samples for every device for every day is a query-cost question
   I have not sized. If it turns out expensive, the honest fallback is to compute it nightly and store it
   on the day row — which is more work, not less, and lands in the same sprint as the chart migration.

10. **The concept is one card away from being a feature.** If the Rezeptkarte were removed, what remains —
    a fixed chart, a written book, a film — is a good product but is C21/C22/C11 wearing a different name.
    The card carries the whole differentiation, which means a judge who does not believe growers will use a
    prefilled mixing checklist has no reason to rank this concept at all.
