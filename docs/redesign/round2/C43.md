# C43 — "Die Werkbank"

**Family:** chart · **Centre of gravity:** the big screen is the unexploited advantage. Terp Control has a
web app and does not market it; every phone-only competitor cannot answer this. So design a real workbench —
small multiples on one time axis, one linked crosshair, compare, annotate, export — and let the phone be the
capture device that feeds it.

Written against `NEW-BRIEF.md`, `00-DOSSIER.md`, `00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md`, `00-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md`, and
direct reads of `server/src/services/image.service.ts`, `server/src/controllers/image.controller.ts`,
`server/src/services/data.service.ts`, `webapp/src/app/device/charts/charts.page.ts`,
`webapp/src/app/util/socket-info.ts`, `webapp/src/app/device/list/list.page.html`, `server/package.json`.

---

## 1. Thesis

**One screen, made of identical horizontal lanes on a shared time axis; the only app state is a time
selection you never have to make; and every tool the product owns — water, feed, note, compare, film,
export — is a verb applied to whatever that selection currently is.**

A workbench is not a mode-switching machine. It is a flat surface with the work piece in the middle and the
tools hanging in view. You do not "enter cutting mode"; you pick up the saw. Terp Control's redesign should
work the same way: the device's whole life — temperature, humidity, VPD, light, the camera, the events, the
plants — laid out as parallel lanes reading left to right in time, with the tools in a fixed row underneath
that never move and never hide. The stoned user reads the left edge of each lane, which says a word. The
techie drags across a lane, and every other lane, the film strip, the numbers and the export button re-scope
to what they dragged. It is the same screen, the same pixels, the same buttons. The depth is horizontal, not
behind a toggle.

The bet: nobody in this market ships a desktop analysis surface. AC Infinity, TrolMaster, Mars Hydro, Spider
Farmer and VIVOSUN are phone-only. Pulse and AROYA have web but no camera correlation. Terp Control already
has an Angular/Ionic web app, 30-second camera stills, three rolling timelapses, actuator state in InfluxDB
and setpoints in device config. **All four are already stored and none of them are on one axis.** That
fusion is the product, and it is a presentation problem, not a data-collection problem.

## 2. Information architecture

Four screens. No tabs, no accordions, no preference.

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/list` | Only when the account holds more than one device. One device → the bench, as today. |
| **`/device/:device_id/charts`** | **DIE WERKBANK.** The home screen. Absorbs today's overview + charts + grow report. |
| `/device/:device_id/pflanze/:plant_id` | Pflanzenblatt — the same bench, scoped to one plant. Lanes unchanged; events and totals filtered. |
| `/device/:device_id/journal` | The flat event list, paginated server-side. Reached from any lane's "alle zeigen". |
| `/device/:device_id/regeln` | Settings. Today's `fridge-settings`, renamed, capability-filtered, targets only for kit that exists. |
| `/account`, `/shares`, `/login`, `/demo`, `/classes` | Unchanged. |

**The URL stays `/charts`.** Every `ShareLink.query` and every `ChartPreset.query` in circulation is a
frozen query string against that route. Renaming it breaks user data with no migration tooling. `/device/:id`
becomes a redirect alias for humans; the canonical path is unchanged in v1.

**Navigation model.** The existing `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` stays. What goes away are the six outline
buttons on the device overview (Charts, Settings, Testmode, Maintenance, Diary, Setup): Charts/Diary/Overview
are now one screen; Regeln, Journal and Setup are two header links and one menu item; Testmode and Maintenance
live inside Regeln where they belong.

**View state lives in query params, extended not redefined.** Existing keys keep their meaning:
`measures, date, dateEnd, vpdMode, autoUpdate, useCustom, timespan, interval, logs, share`. Added:
`von`, `bis` (selection, epoch ms), `pflanze` (plant_id), `spur` (focused lane name), `film` (`1`).
`applyViewParams()` gains one branch: **if `von`/`bis` are absent, derive the selection from `date`/`dateEnd`/
`timespan` exactly as today.** Old presets and old share links open on the bench with the window they always
had.

### 3.2 The lane anatomy — this is the whole design

Every lane, without exception, is four parts read left to right:

1. **SPUR** — the name, then a **word** (`Im Band`, `Zu feucht`, `Läuft`, `Aus`, `Kamera stumm`, `Offline`),
   then the current number.
2. **EVIDENZ** — the small multiple. One measure, its own y-axis, axis labels always drawn (3 ticks minimum,
   never suppressed).
3. **ZAHLEN** — three derived facts, always the same three shapes: a share, a mean, an extreme or a duration.
4. Hidden until you touch it: nothing. There is no fourth part.

The word is computed from the same band the chart draws. A user who reads only the word and a user who reads
only the series are looking at the same fact through different amounts of squinting.

### 3.3 Phone (< 992 px) — the same DOM

The three columns are a flex row with `flex-wrap: wrap`. They wrap; nothing is removed, nothing is behind a
control.

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ Temperatur   Im Band  24,8 °C│  ← SPUR, one line
│ ▁▂▃▄▄▄▃▂▁░░░▁▂▃▄▅▄▃▂▁░░░     │  ← EVIDENZ, 88 px, full width
│ 92 % im Band · Ø 24,8 · ↑29,1│  ← ZAHLEN, collapsed to one line

```

`AUSWAHL` becomes a horizontally scrolling chip row. `WERKZEUGE` becomes a sticky bottom bar that scrolls
horizontally — all six buttons present, `Gießen` and `Düngen` first, none hidden behind a "…".
Interaction: one finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom the axis, long-press sets the crosshair,
long-press-and-drag sets the selection. (`dataZoom: {type:'inside'}` with `moveOnMouseMove:false` gives
exactly this; today's chart needs Ctrl to pan and Shift to zoom, which on a phone means "never".)

### 3.4 Stale, offline, absent — the real copy

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

Three mechanisms, none of which is a toggle. The load-bearing one is the second.

### 4.1 Lane anatomy is progressive precision made structural

The verdict word, the number and the raw series are **in the same row, in that order, always visible**. There
is no path where a user is denied the number and no path where a user is forced to read it. The reading
order does the filtering, not a preference. Round one's failure was treating "simple" as a smaller set of
things; here simple is the **left edge** of every thing.

### 4.2 One state variable, always defaulted, never required

The bench has exactly one piece of state: `{von, bis}` (plus optional `pflanze`). Everything on the screen is
derived from it — every lane's window, the numbers, the film strip, the diary list, what Export exports, what
Film renders.

**You never have to set it.** On every load it defaults to `Diese Phase`: from the current cycle's
`stageChangedAt` to now. That is reveal-by-data — the default is chosen by what the grow is doing, not by a
setting. A stoned user at 2am opens the app, never touches the selection, and sees a complete, correct,
current screen with six working buttons.

**A techie drags across any lane** and the entire bench re-scopes. Same lanes, same buttons, same positions —
deeper answer. The tools are the same six tools; `Film` now renders those days, `Export` now exports those
rows, `Vergleichen` now compares against that span's counterpart in the previous cycle.

This is why it is not a mode: **there is no state the interface can be in that changes which controls exist.**
The controls are constant; only their argument changes. A mode changes the vocabulary. This changes the noun
the verbs act on. That distinction is testable — screenshot the bench with any selection and the same six
buttons are in the same six pixels.

Anti-mode discipline this imposes, stated so a critic can hold us to it:
- No control anywhere may hide another control. Sheets may scroll; they may not collapse.
- No preference may change which lanes exist. Only the device's capability and the user's own data may.
- Nothing reorders itself based on behaviour. Lane order is fixed (sensors, outputs, camera, events, plants)
  so a user in a dark tent finds the same thing in the same place every night. (The brief permits adaptive
  ordering with a defence; my defence is that I refuse it.)

### 4.3 Reveal by capability — `benchLanes(device)`

One pure function decides the lane list, from facts the device reports:

```ts
benchLanes(device): Lane[]
  sensors  ← measures whose Influx field has data in the last 24 h  (temperature, humidity, vpd,
             co2 only with SCD4x, leaf_temperature only with MLX90632, ppfd only with VEML7700)
  outputs  ← parseSocketRoles(device.hardwareInfo.sockets) ∪ ['light' if PWM configured]
  camera   ← !!device.hardwareInfo.webcam_did || !!device.cloudSettings?.rtspStream
  events   ← always
  plants   ← plants.countDocuments({device_id, archivedAt: null}) > 0
```

This replaces `deviceControlCapability()`'s three-bucket collapse, which **fails open to `'full'`** when
`hardwareInfo.sockets` is absent and shows a heater-only tent a dehumidifier tile it can never act on. Per-role
truth already exists in the CSV; the bench just reads it. Absent key (old firmware) → **no output lanes and no
targets**, plus one line in Regeln: `Dein Gerät meldet noch keine Steckdosen. Firmware aktualisieren.`

## 5. The chart

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision). One `<canvas>` for
all lanes: `grid[]` with one grid per lane, `axisPointer.link: [{xAxisIndex: 'all'}]`, `dataZoom` shared
across `xAxisIndex: 'all'`. Chart.js / ng2-charts / chartjs-adapter-luxon are deleted at the same time.

**Goal vs actual.**
- `Soll` is a **dashed stepped `markLine`** built from `device.configuration.day.temperature` /
  `night.temperature`, stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC in config —
  converted to the browser timezone and the timezone is *named* on the axis, because a 12/12 flip near a DST
  boundary otherwise drifts an hour and generates bug reports).
- `Band` is a `markArea`, one source at a time, labelled in the lane legend so nobody has to guess:
  1. `Band: deine Alarmgrenzen` — from `Alarm.upperThreshold`/`lowerThreshold` for that `sensorType`. Hard
     edge, because the user declared it. (These fields exist today and the chart has never read them.)
  2. `Band: Soll ±1,5 °C` / `±7 %` — matching `grow-assistant-card`'s existing tolerance.
  3. `Band: Empfehlung Blüte 1,2–1,5 kPa` — from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS[stage].vpdRange`. Advisory, and the word
     `Empfehlung` is doing deliberate work: a monitor-only device is never told it failed at something it
     cannot control.
- **Deviation, never fill-to-axis.** A `visualMap.pieces` on the actual series colours only the segments
  outside the band. Today's `type:'area', threshold:null, fillOpacity:0.1` encodes nothing and almost
  certainly fails WCAG 1.4.11.
- **Min/max envelope behind the mean line.** `getSeries(..., 'min')` and `'max'` are already allowed methods
  in `data.service.ts` — this costs two extra round trips and no backend work. A single `mean` line is a lie
  about a bang-bang controller.
- `sampling: 'minmax'` on the plot (LTTB smooths away the oscillation the grower is looking for),
  LTTB on the navigator. **Not optional polish** — Highstock's implicit `dataGrouping` is the load-bearing
  default that disappears with the migration.

**Day/night.** `markArea` bands derived from measured `out_light > 0` where that series exists, falling back
to the `daynight` schedule. `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` render as gradient edges. **A failed contactor shows as
a missing band** — the shading is a diagnostic, not decoration.

**Annotations — the Ereignisse lane.** Two rows, 14 px each: row 1 human events (`JournalEvent`), row 2
device events (`DeviceLog`). Pixel-proximity clustering in 10 px bins with a count badge (`●③`); tap a
cluster to get the list; tap an item to open it in place. Phase changes only get a **full-height dashed
`markLine` across every lane**, labelled `→ Blüte`. Category chips above the lane reuse the existing
`collectLogCategories` / `matchesLogCategory` helpers. Touch works because everything is a tap target;
today's height-1 columns are readable only by hovering, on a mobile-first product.

**Outputs** are state-timeline lanes, not 0/1 lines fighting a value axis. `custom` series drawing rectangles:
region length = duration, `null` = gap. The Zahlen column reports `4 Std 10 an · 41 Schaltungen` — the
duration framing, because a count of alarms hides a three-hour excursion.

**Time in range** comes from a new endpoint, and the correctness trap is named:
`GET /data/scorecard/:device_id?measure=&from=&to=&low=&high=` computes on **raw Influx samples with no
`aggregateWindow`**. Averaging destroys the excursions the metric exists to count and the error is invisible
by eye. It returns `{inBandRatio, aboveRatio, belowRatio, longestExcursionMs, mean, min, max, coverageRatio}`
and the Zahlen column **greys out below `coverageRatio` 0.8** with `Datenlücken — Wert unsicher`.

**Prerequisite, flagged not hidden:** `measure`, `from`, `to` and `interval` are interpolated raw into Flux
today, and `3y × 5s` ≈ 18.9 M windows is one dropdown away with a `limit()` piped after `yield()` that does
not bound anything. Parse `from`/`to` to RFC3339, match `interval` against the existing `intervals`
allowlist, and clamp windows to 20 000 before this concept widens anything. Two hours of work; a blocker.

**Vergleichen.** Select a span → `Vergleichen` → the bench offers a second span, defaulted by data to the
same phase-day range of the previous cycle. Every lane then draws two hairlines — A solid, B dashed — on a
**day-of-selection x-axis** (`Tag 1 … Tag n`), and the Zahlen column becomes Δ. This borrows C10's
day-of-run axis as a primitive; here it is an operation on the bench, not the concept.

**Notieren.** Drag a span on any lane, hit `Notieren`, type. The note is anchored to `[von, bis]` and to the
focused lane, and renders as a labelled bracket on the Ereignisse rail. This is what a workbench is for —
you mark the wood where you cut it.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

New Mongo collections. `DeviceLog` is left alone for device events and legacy rows; human events move to a
real entity, because a per-plant diary cannot be layered onto a flat log with a string-prefix discriminator
and `deleted: true` meaning "visible".

```ts
interface Cycle {
  cycle_id: string; device_id: string;
  name: string;                         // "Herbst 2026"
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage;           // + 'harvest' added to the union
  stageChangedAt: number;
  regime_id?: string;
}

interface Plant {
  plant_id: string; device_id: string; cycle_id: string;
  label: string;                        // "A1" — short; what gets shouted across the room
  strain?: string;                      // free text
  source?: 'seed' | 'clone' | 'unknown';
  startedAt: number; archivedAt?: number;
  medium?: string; potLitres?: number;
}

interface JournalEvent {
  event_id: string; device_id: string;
  cycle_id?: string;
  plant_ids: string[];                  // [] === "das ganze Zelt"
  kind: 'giessen' | 'duengen' | 'messung' | 'training' | 'notiz' | 'bild' | 'phase' | 'ernte' | 'zustand';
  at: number; until?: number;           // until only for span notes
  actor_id?: string;
  tapCount?: number; tapUnitLitres?: number; volumeLitres?: number;
  regime_id?: string; regimeStep?: number;
  products?: { name: string; mlPerL?: number; grams?: number }[];
  ec?: number; ph?: number; runoffEc?: number; runoffPh?: number;
  measurePoint?: 'input' | 'runoff' | 'reservoir';
  note?: string; image_ids?: string[]; lane?: string;
  clientEventId: string;                // uuid minted at capture — unique index with device_id
  supersedes?: string;                  // corrections chain forward; nothing is destroyed
}

interface Condition {                   // the thing taped to the tent door
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; plant_ids: string[];
  text: string;                         // "CO2-Flasche fast leer"
  openedBy?: string; openedAt: number; closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
}

interface Actor { actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string; colour: string; archivedAt?: number; }
```

Open `Condition`s render as a persistent strip directly under the header — not an event, not a task, an open
fact with a name on it: `⚑ CO2-Flasche fast leer · Anna · seit 3 Tagen  [erledigt]`. This is the fifth
club gap and it costs one collection and one strip.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed.** `plant_ids: []` is the default on every event and means "the
whole tent". The Pflanzen lane only exists when at least one plant exists. Nothing prompts you to make one;
the Gießen sheet says `ganzes Zelt` and has a plant chip row **only if plants exist**. `Cycle` is created
implicitly on the first stage change or the first event — you never see the word. A monitor-only user with
no plants and no events never encounters any of this vocabulary.

**Migration honesty.** `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a one-time idempotent backfill in a service
constructor (the house style: optional field + read-time normaliser + boot backfill; there is no migration
tooling). It infers cycles by stage rollback and `lifecycleName` change exactly as today — **which means
anyone who renamed a plant mid-grow gets two cycles where they had one, and there is no tool to merge them
afterwards.** Mitigation: the Cycle name is editable and adjacent cycles offer `mit vorherigem
zusammenführen`. It is a manual repair, not a fix.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

No irrigation hardware exists. This is a capture-and-recall problem, and the entire design goal is that
capture costs less attention than not capturing.

### 7.1 The tap-count sheet

`Gießen` opens one full-height sheet with one giant target. It is the same sheet for `Düngen`, with a prefill
block on top.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────┐
│  GIESSEN            Wer? [Anna][Ben]+ │
│                                       │
│              ┌───────────┐            │
│              │           │            │
│              │     3     │            │  ← 220 px circle. Tap = +1.
│              │  Kannen   │            │     Long-press = −1.
│              │           │            │     Haptic per tap.
│              └───────────┘            │
│                                       │
│        1 Kanne = 5 l   [ändern]       │
│        = 15 l · ganzes Zelt           │
│                                       │
│   [ A1 ]  [ A2 ]  [ A3 ]  [ alle ✓ ]  │  ← only if plants exist
│                                       │
│   ──────────── mehr ────────────      │
│   EC …    pH …    Messpunkt: Zulauf   │  ← always here, one scroll down.
│   Notiz …                             │     Never behind a toggle.
│   Zeit: jetzt  [ändern]               │
│                                       │
│            [   FERTIG   ]             │
└───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Tap counts are the record, litres are derived.** The stored fields are `tapCount: 3` and
`tapUnitLitres: 5` at the moment of capture; `volumeLitres` is written as `15` but is a convenience copy.
If the user later corrects "eine Kanne sind eigentlich 4 l", future entries change and the raw taps of past
entries are untouched — the history says what the hand actually did. `tapUnitLitres` is set once per device
in Regeln and defaults to 5 with the label `Was ist deine Kanne?`.

The stoner path: open, tap three times, `FERTIG`. Two seconds, one hand, no reading. Everything else on the
sheet is below it and never in the way.

### 7.2 Following a schedule — medium + feed regime

The selectable thing is a **medium + feed line pair, labelled the way growers say it out loud** (owner
decision 3). Stored in a hot-swappable Mongo content collection, never in the bundle.

```ts
interface Regime {
  regime_id: string;
  label: string;                        // "All-Mix + Biobizz"
  medium: string;                       // "Biobizz All-Mix"
  feedLine: string;                     // "Biobizz"   — plain text, no logo, no brand colour
  preFertilisedWeeks: number;           // All-Mix 3 · Light-Mix 1 · Floragard Light 1 · Coco 0
  slowRelease?: { everyWeeks: number; product: string };   // BioTabs
  steps: RegimeStep[];
  ec_basis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source';
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; chart_version: string;
}
interface RegimeStep {
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; weekInStage: number;
  products: { name: string; mlPerL?: number | [number, number]; grams?: number; note?: string }[];
  ecTarget?: [number, number]; phTarget?: [number, number];
  everyNthWatering?: number;
}
```

Seed set: Biobizz Light-Mix, Biobizz All-Mix, Floragard Light, Green House Powder Feeding, BioTabs, plus
ownerless Lucas Formula and a generic coco EC ladder. Each carries `source_url` + `retrieved_at`, surfaced in
the UI as `Zuletzt geprüft: 12.08.2026` — the legal posture and a genuine trust feature in the same string.

**The Düngen sheet with a regime selected:**

```
│  DÜNGEN                               │
│  All-Mix + Biobizz · Blüte Woche 6    │
│  Bio-Bloom     1,0 ml/l               │
│  Top-Max       2,5 ml/l      [ändern] │
│  ────────────────────────────────     │
│         [ 3 ] Kannen × 5 l = 15 l     │
│  → Bio-Bloom 15 ml · Top-Max 37,5 ml  │
```

Every prefilled number is editable in place; the grower who deviates is the normal case. Deviating writes
the *actual* values to `products[]` and keeps `regime_id` + `regimeStep`, so the report can say "die
Empfehlung war 1,0, gegeben wurden 0,6".

**The All-Mix test the owner named, answered literally.** During `preFertilisedWeeks` the prefill block is
replaced by:

> **Noch nicht düngen.** All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt. Biobizz startet in Woche 4 — bis dahin nur gießen.
> `[ Trotzdem düngen ]`

**BioTabs** has no weekly ml/l at all; its prefill is `Nächste Tabs: in 11 Tagen` and the sheet defaults to
Gießen. A regime that cannot express "don't feed yet" is not modelling the domain.

**Advance by feed events, not by wall clock.** `regimeStep` is stamped on each `duengen` event; the next step
is `max(regimeStep) + 1`, with a soft calendar mapping for display only. Growers feed Tuesday and Friday and
slip; a week counter that runs on its own is wrong within a fortnight.

**The reminder** — the stoner half of the owner's requirement — is a pill on the tool button itself:
`[ Düngen ⬤ fällig ]`, computed from `now − lastFeedAt > step.intervalDays`. No inbox, no notification centre,
no badge to dismiss. The tool tells you it wants using.

**EC honesty.** `ec_basis: 'delta_over_source'` (CANNA's EC+) renders as `EC-Ziel 1,4–1,8 + dein
Leitungswasser (0,4) = 1,8–2,2`. Never a bare "ppm" number anywhere — a Bluelab and a Hanna read 1260 and 900
for the same liquid. EC is stored canonical; ppm is a display option in Regeln with the scale named.

### 7.3 The double feed

The most expensive thing that happens in a shared room. Checked **when the sheet opens**, not on save — after
you have poured is too late.

```
│  ⚠  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gegossen.                 │
│     [ Abbrechen ]        [ Trotzdem eintragen ]           │
```

Window: 20 h for `giessen`, 36 h for `duengen`, overridable per device. Soft block: the `FERTIG` button is
*replaced* by `Trotzdem eintragen` — one deliberate extra tap, never a hard stop. Scope: only the currently
selected plants, so watering A1 does not warn about A3.

### 7.4 Capturing in a cellar

Every event gets a `clientEventId` uuid minted **at the moment of capture**, with a unique index on
`{device_id, clientEventId}` and a server-side upsert. The offline outbox is an IndexedDB queue that replays
on reconnect; a replayed event is idempotent by construction, so **the retry queue cannot double-log** — which
in a shared record is indistinguishable from a double feed and is the reason this is a data-model decision,
not a networking one.

This requires `@angular/service-worker` — a new dependency and a build change, and the brief lists offline as
an undecided open question. **See §12: I scope the outbox to v1 and the full service worker to v1.1.** The
outbox alone (IndexedDB + retry on `online`) works without a service worker as long as the tab stays open,
which covers the ninety seconds between the cellar and the stairs. Say that plainly rather than promising
offline.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is not a tile. It is a lane, and it is the reason the bench exists.

### 8.1 The Filmstreifen lane is an axis

Thumbnails positioned by timestamp on the shared x-scale — so uneven capture reads honestly as gaps, not as
an evenly spaced strip that lies about time. Delivery is **one sprite sheet plus a JSON index per window**
instead of N image requests: `GET /image/:device_id/streifen?von=&bis=&n=100` returns a 10×10 sprite of
128×80 tiles plus `[{t, x, y}]`. Built with `sharp`, which is **already a dependency** (`sharp ^0.34.5` in
`server/package.json`), and `?width=` resizing already exists in `sendImage`.

`Image.timestampEnd` already marks an mp4 as spanning a range — those render as a bracket under the strip,
not as a point.

### 8.2 Image correlation — one cursor, both directions

- **Chart → picture.** The crosshair moves; the Kamera lane highlights the nearest frame and the header
  scrubber shows that frame at 320 px with the numbers at that instant. ECharts `axisPointer.link` already
  broadcasts this; the lane is a plain div listening to `updateAxisPointer`.
- **Picture → numbers.** Click any frame → the **Bildkarte**: the still at full width, and under it
  `04.10.2026 03:12 · Blüte Tag 41 · 24,8 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,19 kPa · CO2 890 ppm · Licht 100 % ·
  letzte Düngung vor 2 Tagen (Anna)`. That card is also the export unit for a forum post.
- **The differentiator: `Bilder mit …`.** A select above the strip picks a lane. Each thumbnail then gets a
  3 px baseline coloured by that lane's value at that timestamp. Scroll the strip and you *see* the two humid
  nights next to the pictures where the leaves drooped. That is image correlation as a visual rather than a
  claim, and it is the one thing in this document that no competitor can copy without owning both the sensor
  and the camera.

### 8.3 What ships in v1 — a timelapse someone can actually hold

**First, the one-line fix that matters most.** `image.service.ts:583` encodes every rolling timelapse with
`libx265`. HEVC in mp4 does not decode in Chrome or Firefox on desktop, or in Firefox anywhere. **Today's
timelapses do not play for most of the people who own one.** Changing the encode to

```
-vcodec libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -profile:v high -crf 23 -movflags +faststart
```

makes every existing 1d/1w/1m timelapse playable everywhere, in one line, for every device already in the
field. That alone is more camera value than round one shipped, and it costs an afternoon plus a rebuild pass.

**Then, `Film` as a tool on the selection.** Hit `Film`, and the current `{von, bis}` becomes a video:

```
┌── FILM ───────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Blüte Tag 12–47 · 36 Tage · 1.184 Bilder      │
│                                               │
│ Nachtbilder            [  ] aus   ← default   │
│ Belichtung angleichen  [✓] an                 │
│ Bildausschnitt         [ zuschneiden ]        │
│ Länge  ~47 s   ·  1280×720  ·  25 B/s         │
│                                               │
│            [ Film erstellen ]                 │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Rendering runs server-side. ≤ 900 frames renders synchronously (≈ 35–50 s); above that it returns `202
{job_id}` and the dialog polls, because 3 000 frames is minutes and nobody should hold a request open for it.

### 8.4 Culling and normalisation — the actual pipeline

Six passes, in order. Everything uses `sharp`, already installed.

1. **Nacht.** Drop frames where the device's own `out_light` series is 0 at that timestamp. Not a pixel
   heuristic — the controller *knows* when the lights were off. For old devices with no `out_light`, fall
   back to `sharp().stats()` mean luma < 18/255. This is the single biggest content win: a 12/12 tent is
   half black frames, and today all of them are in the film.
2. **Mensch im Zelt.** Drop frames within ±6 minutes of any `JournalEvent` timestamp. Cheap, needs no CV,
   and catches every visit the grower logged. **Honest limitation: it catches nothing when they just looked
   in.** It removes maybe half the human frames and I will not claim more.
3. **Doppelte.** 8×8 dHash per frame (`sharp().resize(9,8).greyscale().raw()`, 64-bit); drop a frame whose
   Hamming distance to the previous kept frame is ≤ 3. Kills the stretches where nothing moved.
4. **Kaputte.** The existing `FFMPEG_CORRUPT_FRAME_PATTERN` already discards torn frames at capture. Add a
   render-time guard: drop frames whose decoded dimensions differ from the modal dimensions of the set (a
   camera swap mid-grow).
5. **Belichtung und Weißabgleich.** For each frame take `sharp().stats()` → per-channel means. Compute a
   rolling median over ±12 kept frames. Apply `linear(a, b)` per channel to move each frame's channel means
   onto the rolling median. This removes the magenta-LED-versus-open-door flicker and the slow exposure drift
   across twelve weeks, which is what makes a raw tent timelapse unwatchable. ~25 ms/frame; 1 200 frames ≈ 30 s.
   `Belichtung angleichen` can be turned off — and it must be, because a grower who moved the lamp on day 30
   will otherwise have that change normalised away. The default lies a little, deliberately, and the checkbox
   is the confession.
6. **Bildausschnitt statt Stabilisierung.** No stabilisation ships. Instead the user drags a crop rectangle
   once on a representative frame; it is applied to every frame and stored on the device as
   `cloudSettings.filmCrop`. That hides the day-12 nudge at the frame edges. **It does not fix a nudge that
   happened halfway through** — one crop cannot serve both halves. Real limitation, stated.

Resample to a target length: after culling, pick evenly spaced frames so a 36-day film lands at 40–60 s
(≈ 1 000–1 500 frames at 25 fps). Nobody watches a four-minute tent video.

### 8.5 How a video leaves the product

```
GET /image/:device_id/film?von=<ms>&bis=<ms>&nacht=0&angleichen=1&breite=1280&download=1
  → 202 {job_id}  ·  poll GET /image/film/:job_id  → 200 video/mp4
```

- **Format:** H.264 High, `yuv420p`, CRF 23, `-movflags +faststart`, 25 fps, no audio track, max 1280×720
  (source-limited — the P2P cam is 1280×720).
- **Header:** `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_bluete-tag-12-47_2026-10-04.mp4"`.
  Today `sendImage()` sets **no** `Content-Disposition` at all, so the browser plays the mp4 inline and a
  right-click save produces a file called `image`. This is three lines.
- **Where it lands:** Android Chrome → Downloads, and the system share sheet reaches Telegram, Discord, or
  the gallery. **iOS Safari → Dateien, not the camera roll.** The user must share it into Fotos themselves;
  there is no web API that writes to the camera roll and I will not pretend otherwise. The dialog says so:
  `Auf dem iPhone landet der Film in "Dateien". Von dort kannst du ihn in Fotos sichern.`
- Films are **not** stored back into the `images` collection. They are written to a temp dir with a 24-hour
  TTL and served once. The existing unique index `{device_id, format, timestamp, duration}` cannot carry
  arbitrary user-cut ranges, and Mongo's 16 MB BSON limit is a hard ceiling a 50-second film will hit.
  This is a real departure from the current storage model and it is the right one.

### 8.6 The camera alarm

A camera that stopped three days into flower must say so. `image.service.compressRtspStreams()` already runs
hourly; add a check: if a device has `webcam_did` or `rtspStream` and
`max(Image.timestamp where format='jpeg') < now − 40 × IMAGE_LOAD_INTERVAL_MS` (20 min), write a `DeviceLog`
with `severity: 1, categories: ['device','camera']`, message key `message-camera-silent`, cooldown 6 h, and
send the existing alarm e-mail. German: `Kamera stumm — seit 09:14 kein Bild mehr.` Defaults on whenever a
camera is paired; switchable in Regeln. Resolves itself when a frame arrives:
`Kamera wieder da — 14 Std ohne Bild.`

## 9. Clubs

The market scope is grow clubs, not commercial cultivation. So: serve the club's own need to know who did
what, and refuse to become the state's reporting system.

**Attribution without an auth rewrite.** `Actor` is device-scoped: names the owner types once in Regeln, no
accounts, no e-mail, no `auth.middleware` surgery. The capture sheet's top row is `Wer? [Anna][Ben][Chris][+]`,
remembered in `localStorage` per device for 12 h and re-asked after that. It stamps `actor_id`. Two days of
work, delivers attribution in v1, and upgrades cleanly later: when real memberships land, an `Actor` gains an
optional `user_id` and existing rows keep meaning what they meant.

**And say what it is.** Attribution is a label; authorisation is a gate. Anyone who can reach the device can
stamp "Anna". Regeln says so in one sentence: `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Gerät
bedienen kann, kann jeden Namen wählen.` It must never be sold as an audit trail.

**The double feed** is §7.3 and is the single most valuable club feature in this document.

**The cumulative question.** `/device/:id/pflanze/A3` header, above the lanes:

```
A3 · Gorilla Glue · Blüte Tag 41 · 11 l Topf · All-Mix
Dieser Zyklus:  47 l Wasser  ·  9 Düngungen  ·  Bio-Bloom 68 ml  ·  Top-Max 152 ml
                davon Anna 6 · Ben 3                         [ alles anzeigen ]
```

Every data model could compute this; the point is that it is on the screen without being asked.

**Standing conditions** are §6's `Condition` strip. `⚑ Nicht gießen — A3 ist übergossen · Ben · seit 2 Tagen`
sits under the header on every screen for that device until someone closes it. It is not a task and has no
due date; it is a fact with an owner, which is what actually gets taped to a tent door.

**Privacy posture.** Device-scoped, thing-scoped, minimal.
- No real names required, no e-mail, no phone, no location, ever.
- **Harvest weight is off by default.** `Ernte` records a date, a plant and a note. A weight field appears
  only after the owner enables `Erträge erfassen` once per device, with the plain sentence
  `Erntemengen sind in Deutschland heikel. Standardmäßig speichern wir sie nicht.` Plant counts follow from
  plants the user chose to create; nothing counts for them.
- **No per-member dispensing records, ever.** § 26 KCanG's Name/Geburtsjahr/Gramm/THC ledger drags a grow
  controller into Art. 9 GDPR territory for data it has no reason to hold. Cannanas and 420cloud sell that;
  let them.
- **Export defaults to anonymised** — `actor_id` → `Person 1..n`, with a `[ Namen einbeziehen ]` checkbox.
- Nothing is undeletable for the account owner. Corrections chain forward via `supersedes`, which gives a
  club a legible history without giving an unregulated grower an unerasable one.
- No community feed. A public diary URL (v1.1) is a link you can paste; that is not a feed you must moderate.

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

They are not the focus and they must not be broken. Concretely, for a `device_type: 'light'`:

- `/list` renders the bench for it, not `light-overview`. `benchLanes()` returns what that device actually
  reports: **Temperatur, Luftfeuchte, Licht** (`out_light`, 0–100), **Ereignisse**. That is four lanes.
- No VPD lane unless both temperature and humidity are present (then it appears — VPD is derived, so a light
  device with an SHT21 gets it for free and that is a small upgrade, not a regression).
- No Kamera lane, no Pflanzen lane, no Heizung/Entfeuchter/CO2 lanes, no `Soll`/`Band` on temperature —
  a light controller has no temperature target, so drawing one would be the exact "promises control it cannot
  deliver" failure the dossier names.
- `WERKZEUGE` shows `[ Notieren ] [ Vergleichen ] [ Film ] [ Export ]`. `Film` renders only if a camera is
  paired; on a light device it is absent. **`Gießen` and `Düngen` are absent** — they exist on `controller`
  and `fridge` only. That is reveal-by-capability, and it means a plug owner is never shown a nutrient regime.
- **Settings are untouched.** `/device/:id/regeln` keeps `ngSwitchCase` → `light-settings` / `fan-settings` /
  `plug-settings` verbatim. Zero work, zero risk, and the two live i18n bugs in plug settings
  (`settings.limits.overtemperature.enabled` and siblings rendering raw keys) get fixed in the same commit
  because they are string additions.
- `fan` gets Temperatur, Luftfeuchte, **Lüfter** (`out_fan`, %), **Tag** (`day`, state band), Ereignisse.
  `plug` gets Temperatur, Luftfeuchte, **Relais** (`out_relais`, state band), Ereignisse.
- The `dryer` type stays dead per `AGENTS.md` and keeps its existing components.
- Their existing share links and chart presets keep working, because the route and the query-param grammar
  did not change.

Net effect for a fan owner: the same numbers, on axes that are actually labelled, with events they can tap,
and a CSV export they never had. Nothing they used disappeared.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the bench. Three lanes: Temperatur, Luftfeuchte, VPD. Each says a word,
a number, and `92 % im Band`. No targets are drawn (no sockets paired → no `Soll`), only the advisory VPD
`Empfehlung` band, so nothing nags about control they do not have. `Export` gives them the CSV nobody in this
market offers. They never touch the selection and never see a plant, a regime or a tool they cannot use.

**2 — Same, with a camera.** One extra lane, and it is the reason they bought the thing. The Filmstreifen sits
on the same axis as the temperature; the crosshair moves both. `Bilder mit Luftfeuchte` colours the strip and
they can see the humid night next to the drooping leaves. `Film` on the current phase gives them a 47-second
H.264 file with a real filename, and — the part round one never shipped — **the rolling 1-day film already
plays in their browser on day one**, because of a one-line codec change.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO2, heating).** `benchLanes()` reads `hardwareInfo.sockets` and gives them a
Heizung lane and a CO2 lane and no Entfeuchter lane. The temperature lane draws `Soll` stepped day/night with
their own alarm thresholds as the band, and the Zahlen column says `92 % im Band · 41 Schaltungen`. The
humidity lane draws no `Soll` at all, because they cannot act on it — which is the failure the current
three-bucket capability collapse produces today.

**4 — Full tent.** Six to eight lanes, and the bench is where its density pays. Every output as a state
timeline under the sensors it drives, so "the dehumidifier ran all night and the temperature still climbed"
is one glance instead of an inference. Honest limitation: only five socket roles exist and `dehumidifier`
doubles as the cooler — so this concept **labels that lane `Entfeuchter / Kühlung` and says which meaning the
current workmode gives it**, rather than pretending. Anything better is firmware work, and there is no
firmware change before October.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Gets the least. The bench shows measures; it does not let them add one, because
`VALID_SENSORS` is a hard-coded allowlist and the measure catalogue is a literal array in a component. What
they get is CSV with actuator state and setpoints included, a documented JSON export of the whole selection,
and the honest note that the write API is not in this release. **This is the type this concept serves worst,
and pretending otherwise is how you lose the most sceptical segment twice.**

**6 — Grow club.** The `Wer?` picker, the double-feed soft block, the per-plant cumulative totals, the
Condition strip, and an anonymised export. Not memberships, not roles, not accounts — those are an auth
rewrite and they are not in a six-week release. What the club gets in v1 is the thing they actually asked for:
who did what to which plants, and how the plants reacted, on one axis with the pictures.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Four lanes, their settings page untouched, their share links intact, two
long-standing i18n bugs fixed, and an export they never had.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner:* opens the app, reads four words down the left edge, taps `Gießen`, taps a
circle three times, taps `FERTIG`. Never scrolls, never reads a number, never chooses a range. *Normal:*
reads words and numbers, taps a picture, shares a film. *Techie:* drags a span, reads `min/max` envelopes and
`longestExcursionMs`, hits `Vergleichen`, exports CSV. Same screen, same six buttons in the same six pixels.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026

Six to eight weeks. This is the biggest v1 in the field and I am not going to pretend it is comfortable.

**Ships:**

1. **ECharts 6 migration + the bench shell.** Lane anatomy, three-column flex with wrap, linked crosshair,

2. **Flux parameter validation + window clamp.** Blocker, 2 hours.
3. **Goal vs actual:** stepped `Soll` from device config, `markArea` band with the three-source priority and

4. **Scorecard endpoint on raw samples** + the ZAHLEN column + coverage greying. ~3 days.
5. **Selection state + query-param extension with back-compat.** ~2 days.
6. **Camera:** H.264 re-encode (1 line), `Content-Disposition` + filename (3 lines), sprite film-strip lane,
   one-cursor correlation, Bildkarte, `Bilder mit …` colouring, camera-silence log + mail. ~1 week.
7. **`Film` on a selection** with the six-pass cull/normalise pipeline and the job+poll route. ~1 week.
8. **Journal:** `JournalEvent`/`Plant`/`Cycle`/`Condition`/`Actor` collections, server-side pagination,

9. **Regime collection** with the owner's five seeds + Lucas + a coco ladder, the `preFertilisedWeeks` rule,

10. **Controller naming pass** — kill "Terp Control FRIDGE GROW", add the `devices.controller` i18n block,
    fix the six raw-key bugs, remove the dead `daynight.floating` controls. ~2 days, and the cheapest
    credibility fix in the document.
11. **Export:** CSV (with actuator state and setpoints — nobody ships this), PNG via `getDataURL`, Markdown
    post with hot-linked image URLs. ~3 days.

**Deferred, with reasons:**

- **Service worker / true offline** → v1.1. New dependency, new build config, and an undecided open question.
  The outbox covers the realistic case without it.
- **`Vergleichen`** → v1.1. It depends on cycle boundaries being trustworthy, and the backfill (§6) will fork
  some users' history. Ship the comparison after people have fixed their cycle names, not before.
- **Club memberships / real multi-user auth** → v1.2. It is a membership collection plus a rewrite of
  `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207` plus ~10 owner-scoped queries. Not a flag, not six weeks.
- **Public diary URL, BBCode generator, Telegram/Discord destinations, published JSON schema** → v1.1. Cheap,
  high ROI, and not what the hardware launch is judged on.
- **Stabilisation** → never as CV. The crop is the answer, and it is an honest partial one.
- **A write API for type 5** → v1.2, and it should be Pulse-shaped (`x-api-key`, per-grow keys, OpenAPI).

**The cut line if week six goes badly:** drop items 7 and 9 (the film pipeline and the regime engine). What
still ships is the bench, goal-vs-actual, the scorecard, tap-count logging with the double-feed block, and —
critically — **a downloadable, playable 1-day/1-week/1-month timelapse with a real filename, which is two
days of work on machinery that already exists.** The owner's "it should be creating timelapses" is met by the
cut-down version. That is the whole point of putting the codec fix first.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **I designed desktop-first for a product used on a phone in a tent.** The reflow keeps the phone honest,
   but the bench is *best* on a 27-inch monitor and most users will never open one. The strategic bet (nobody
   else has web; it is free with this stack) may simply not be where the value is for a €289 hobbyist product.
   If the owner disagrees with that bet, the concept loses its centre of gravity and becomes C06 with lanes.
2. **Fixed cost per lane.** Eight lanes × ~150 px on a 360 px phone is a 1 200 px scroll before `WERKZEUGE`.
   The sticky toolbar fixes access to the tools; it does not fix the scroll. Ranked density means the stoner
   still passes six things to reach the seventh, and my refusal to reorder by behaviour is a deliberate
   trade of efficiency for predictability that some users will experience as tedium.
3. **"One selection" is elegant and occasionally confusing.** It lives in the URL, which means a bookmarked or
   shared link opens on a window that was current six weeks ago. Mitigation: the chip row always shows which
   preset is active and a stale custom span renders as `frei: 24.09.–04.10.` in warning colour. It is still a
   seam, and it is the seam a first-time user hits when a friend sends them a link.
4. **Advisory bands on uncontrollable measures still nag.** A monitor-only device sees a VPD `Empfehlung`
   band and a `74 % im Band` number about something it cannot change. Labelling it `Empfehlung` softens it;
   it does not remove the implicit judgement. Some users will read every band as a failing grade.
5. **The film pipeline is real CPU on a shared server.** 1 200 frames × sharp stats + linear + x264 ≈ 40–90 s,
   competing with the hourly rolling-timelapse job that already holds `pLimit(10)`. Thirty club devices
   rendering harvest films the same evening is a queue with no job infrastructure behind it. RabbitMQ is
   present for MQTT and could be pressed into service, but that is unbudgeted work I have not costed.
6. **Human-frame suppression fails exactly when it matters.** Dropping frames near logged events catches the
   visits people bothered to log. Most tent visits are not logged. It removes perhaps half, and the half it
   misses is the half where somebody just looked in — which is most of them.
7. **Exposure normalisation removes real changes.** Raising the lamp on day 30 is a genuine event the rolling
   median will flatten. The checkbox is the confession, and the default is still the lie.
8. **One crop cannot fix two nudges.** If the camera moved on day 12 and again on day 40, `filmCrop` serves
   neither half well. No stabilisation ships and none is planned.
9. **Attribution is not authorisation and never becomes it in v1.** In a German club that wants a § 17 Abs. 2
   participation record, a name anyone can pick is worth something socially and nothing evidentially. I have
   said so in the UI, but somebody will still sell it as an audit trail.
10. **The cycle backfill will fork histories.** Anyone who renamed a plant mid-grow gets two cycles, and there
    is no migration tooling to merge them automatically. The manual merge is a repair, not a fix, and it will
    generate support tickets in the first fortnight after launch.
11. **Eight ECharts grids with linked `axisPointer` and 24 series is untested at this scale on a mid-range
    Android.** The memory numbers (17 MB peak vs Highcharts' 97 MB) are encouraging, and `sampling:'minmax'`
    is mandatory rather than optional — but if it is misconfigured on one lane the phone dies, and there is
    effectively no test suite (38 `it()` blocks, 24 of them `should create`) to catch it.
12. **Type 5 gets a consolation prize.** CSV and JSON are not an API. The most technically advocacy-heavy
    segment in the market is served, in this release, by a download button.
