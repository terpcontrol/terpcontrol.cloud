# C42 — "Im Band"

*Family: chart. Centre of gravity: in-range or not, everywhere. One visual primitive for the whole product.*

---

## 1. Thesis

**The entire app is one widget — a target band with a trace through it — drawn at every size from six pixels
to full screen, so learning it once explains temperature, humidity, VPD, light hours, watering intervals,
camera freshness and the club's feeding record.**

Terp Control's real problem is not that it lacks features; it is that every screen teaches a different
vocabulary. A gauge on the overview, fifteen translucent areas on the chart, a vertical day-dot timeline in
the grow report, a settings form full of numbers with no relationship to any of it. C42 collapses all of that
into a single primitive, **das Band**: a horizontal channel that says what the value was supposed to be, a
line that says what it actually was, and a fill wherever the two disagree. Everything the product knows is
expressed that way. A room that is too warm and a plant that is overdue for water are drawn with the same
geometry and described with the same five words. The universal score is **Bandtreue** — the share of time the
trace spent inside the channel — computed on raw samples, reported with its data coverage, and shown next to
every band on every screen.

The consequence that makes this a *concept* rather than a chart style: **there is no chart page.** The chart
is a home-screen row that somebody made taller. Depth is a physical dimension of the band, not a destination.

## 2. Information architecture

Six surfaces. No new nesting depth, one new nav element (a device-scoped tab bar, which the app currently
lacks entirely).

| Route | Name in UI (de) | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/list` | Geräte | One `Bandkarte` per device: name, `5 von 6 Bändern gehalten`, six 6-px ribbons. One device → redirect to `/device/:id` |
| `/device/:id` | **Jetzt** | The band list. The home screen. New route; replaces the fridge overview |
| `/device/:id/charts` | (no separate name) | A band at full height. Reached by expanding a row; the route exists for share links and presets and back-compat |
| `/device/:id/diary` | **Verlauf** | Events, conditions, plants, cumulative totals. All band-annotated |
| `/device/:id/kamera` | **Kamera** | Filmstrip, timelapse player, film export, camera freshness band |
| `/device/:id/settings` | **Zelt** | Bands and kit. Where a band's edges are set and sockets get roles |
| `/device/:id/testmode`, `/shares`, `/account`, `/classes`, `/demo`, `/login` | | unchanged |

**Navigation model.** The existing `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` stays for global items (Geräte, Geteilte
Links, Konto, Abmelden, Theme). Inside a device an `ion-tab-bar` appears with four tabs —
**Jetzt · Verlauf · Kamera · Zelt**. The header title is the device name and is a tap target: it opens the
device switcher. This adds the back button, the device switcher and the tab bar the app has never had, and it
removes the six outline buttons currently used for cross-navigation.

`/device/:id/charts` keeps the existing query-param contract (`measures`, `date`, `dateEnd`, `vpdMode`,
`autoUpdate`, `useCustom`, `timespan`, `interval`, `logs`, `share`) so presets and circulating share links
still resolve. New optional params: `band` (which measure is expanded), `h` (its height in px), `film`
(a pinned frame timestamp). `applyViewParams` treats a missing `band` as "expand the first entry in
`measures`", which is what every old link means anyway.

### Row anatomy (the `<tc-band>` component)

Minimum height 96 px. Four lines, and **the first line never changes no matter how tall the row gets**:

1. **Kopfzeile (24 px).** `Name` · current value, `tabular-nums`, 20/600 · verdict chip.
2. **Bandfläche (32 px minimum, drag to grow).** Grey `--tc-surface-subtle` rectangle = the band. Solid
   `#0072B2` trace = the measurement over the trailing 24 h. Where the trace is outside, the area between
   trace and the nearest band edge is filled `--tc-text-danger` at 45 % — never filled to the axis.
3. **Bandleiste (6 px).** One cell per aggregation window, green inside / amber outside / hatched grey where
   there is no data. This is the *same geometry* used at 6 px in the device list and 6 px under the chart.
4. **Fußzeile (2 × 15 px).** `96 % im Band · 24 Std` and the target on the right; then the sentence.

**The verdict chip has exactly five values, product-wide.** `im Band` · `über dem Band` · `unter dem Band` ·
`kein Band` · `keine Daten`. Nothing else. The vocabulary is the interface.

### Real German copy

| State | Kopf-chip | Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inside, stable | `im Band` | `Seit 6 Std im Band.` |
| just returned | `im Band` | `Wieder im Band seit 12 Min. Davor 2 Std 10 darüber.` |
| above | `über dem Band` | `4 % über dem Band, seit 1 Std 20.` |
| below | `unter dem Band` | `1,8 °C unter dem Band, seit 40 Min.` |
| observed only (no actuator paired) | `über dem Band` | `4 % über dem Band, seit 1 Std 20.` + right-aligned muted `nur beobachtet` |
| no target set | `kein Band` | `Kein Band gesetzt.` + inline text button `Band setzen` |
| stale, 10–60 min | `keine Daten` | `Keine neuen Messwerte seit 14 Min.` |
| offline | `keine Daten` | `Offline seit 3 Std 20. Letzte Messung 05:12.` |
| coverage < 80 % | value shown, chip normal | `Zu wenig Daten für eine Bewertung — 68 % Abdeckung.` (the % badge is struck through) |
| sensor absent | *row does not exist* | — |

Stale rendering detail: the value greys to `--ion-color-medium`, the trace ends in a hollow circle, and the
band keeps drawing to *now* with nothing in it — the gap is visible as geometry, not just as text. The header
`live · 12 s` counter turns into `Offline seit 3 Std 20` and gets a hairline amber underline across the
whole toolbar. That single change is the fix for the #1 complaint across all five consumer brands.

### Which rows exist (reveal by capability, reveal by data)

Rows are produced by `bandsForDevice(device, hardwareInfo, plants, events)`:

- `temperature`, `humidity` — always, for anything that reports them.
- `vpd` — when temperature and humidity both exist. Band from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS[stage].vpdRange`.
- `co2` — only when `deviceHasCo2()`. Absent otherwise; no ghost row, no upsell tile.
- `lichtstunden` — when a `light` role socket or the PWM output exists. Band = configured photoperiod ±15 min,
  trace = measured on-time per day from `out_light`.
- `giessabstand` — only when at least one `water` GrowEvent exists. Reveal by data.
- `kamera` — only when `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` is set.
- `leaf_temperature`, `lux` — only when the sensors report.

Order is **fixed and canonical**, never re-sorted by behaviour or severity — an impaired user must find the
temperature row in the same place at 2am every time. Attention is directed instead by the **`Nicht im Band`
strip** at the top: chips naming the out-of-band measures which scroll the list to that row. Present only
when something is out; absent entirely when everything is fine, so its presence *is* the alert.

## 4. The no-modes mechanism — **die Bandhöhe**

**Every band row is continuously resizable by dragging its bottom edge, per row, persisted per row. Nothing
appears or disappears at any height. Detail is a function of pixels, not of a setting.**

That sentence is the whole answer, and the design work is in making it literally true.

| Height | What is drawn |
| --- | --- |
| 6 px (device list, chart footer) | Bandleiste only — in/out/no-data |
| 32 px (home default) | Band rect, trace, deviation fill |
| 96 px | + y-axis min/max labels, the day/night setpoint step line (dashed) |
| 160 px | + hourly x ticks, annotation pips on the baseline, the min/max envelope |
| 320 px | + the output state lane (`out_heater`, `out_dehumidifier`, `out_light`…), the film strip |
| full screen | + scrub header, annotation rail with category chips, time-range picker, `Als Tabelle`, export |

The invariants that make this **not** a mode:

1. **The information set is constant.** At 32 px the row already shows the full 24 h trace against the full
   band. A taller row does not add facts; it adds resolution to facts already on screen. A wall clock read
   from across the room and from arm's length is not two modes of clock.
2. **The top of the row is byte-identical at every height.** Name, value, chip, and — from 96 px down — the
   sentence. A user who never touches the handle loses nothing they were shown before, and a user who drags
   loses nothing either. There is no state in which the summary is unavailable.
3. **It is per-row, so it is not an identity.** You can have Temperatur at 400 px because your heater is
   flaky and rF at 32 px because you have no humidifier. "What kind of user are you?" has no answer here
   because the question is asked of a *measurement*, not of a person.
4. **It is continuous and reversible in one gesture.** No confirmation, no navigation, no preference screen,
   no localStorage key called `expert`.
5. **The number is never behind the gesture.** `24,6 °C`, `96 % im Band`, `Ziel 22–26 °C` are on the 32 px
   row. Progressive precision is satisfied at the smallest size; the drag buys *history*, not *values*.

The second half of the mechanism is **the sentence and the geometry are the same three numbers.** The
sentence `4 % über dem Band, seit 1 Std 20.` is generated from `(deviation, sinceMs, edge)` — exactly the
triple that positions the deviation fill. The stoned user reads German; the techie reads pixels; there is no
translation layer between them and no possibility of them disagreeing. This is why one interface can serve
both without a switch: the two audiences are reading different *channels of the same encoding*, which is a
property of the encoding, not a configuration of the app.

Defaults do the rest of the work without being a mode: on a viewport ≥ 992 px every row opens at 160 px
because there is room, exactly as a newspaper sets more words on a broadsheet than on a phone. And the
first time a band goes out of range, that row animates once to 160 px with a one-line hint
`Zieh die Kante nach unten für mehr Verlauf.` — a single teaching moment tied to a real event, dismissible,
never repeated. If it is missed, nothing is lost (invariant 2).

## 5. The chart — a band at full height

There is no separate chart architecture. `/device/:id/charts?band=temperature&h=full` renders the same
`<tc-band>` component with a full-screen host, plus the chrome that only fits at that size. Apache ECharts 6,
tree-shaken, driven by a hand-rolled ~50-line directive (no `ngx-echarts`).

**Goal vs actual.**

- The band is `markArea` with `yAxis: [min, max]`, `silent: true`, `--tc-surface-subtle`. It **steps over
  time**: one `markArea` segment per interval during which the band definition was constant. Day/night
  setpoints produce a step at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC → rendered in the
  device timezone); a stage change or an edited alarm threshold produces a step wherever it happened.
  The `Band` collection (§6) stores that history so a band change is visible rather than retroactive.
- **Band source priority, one at a time, always labelled** in the footer:
  `Alarmgrenzen` (the user's own declared limits, hard edges) → `Sollwert ± Toleranz` (from
  `day.temperature` / `night.temperature`, ±1.5 °C / ±7 % RH, matching what `grow-assistant-card` already
  uses) → `Phasenempfehlung` (`GROW_STAGE_PRESETS`, dashed edges, advisory) → `kein Band`.
  Tapping the label opens the band editor. Never blend two sources.
- Actual: solid 2 px, `sampling: 'minmax'` (LTTB smooths away exactly the bang-bang oscillation a grower is
  looking for; LTTB is used only for the navigator). Min/max envelope as a 20 %-opacity same-hue area from
  a single `aggregateWindow` call returning `{mean,min,max}` together.
- Deviation fill: two auxiliary series, `max(actual, bandMax)` filled down to `bandMax` and
  `min(actual, bandMin)` filled up to `bandMin`, both `--tc-text-danger` at 45 %. Computed client-side from
  the already-fetched series; costs two extra arrays, no extra request.
- Setpoint (not the band edge, the actual target) as a dashed stepped line inside the band.

**Day/night.** Full-height `markArea` in `--tc-step` grey derived from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back
to the `daynight` schedule when the output series is missing, with a gradient at `lights.sunrise` /
`lights.sunset`. Rendered in an explicit device timezone so a 12/12 flip near a DST boundary does not drift.
A failed contactor then shows as a *missing* night band — a diagnostic, not decoration.

**Annotations.** A 22 px rail *below* the plot, never columns inside it. Two rows: row 1 human events
(`GrowEvent`), row 2 device events (`DeviceLog`). Pixel-proximity clustering in 10 px bins with count badges.
Category chips from the existing `collectLogCategories`. Lifecycle stage changes are full-height dashed
verticals with a label. Tap, not hover — the current hover-only interaction is unusable on the product's
primary device.

**Outputs.** A state-timeline lane, one row per paired socket role, drawn with a `custom` series
`renderItem` emitting rectangles; region length = duration, null renders as a gap. Not 0/1 lines competing
for the value axis. Because roles come from `hardwareInfo.sockets`, a tent with only a heater gets exactly
one lane.

**Scrub header.** Pinned above the plot, replacing the floating tooltip: `14.09. 19:32 · 24,6 °C im Band ·
68 % über Band · VPD 0,92 im Band · Heizung an`. One finger scrolls the page, two fingers pan/zoom
(`dataZoom: inside`), tap or long-press moves the cursor. Axis labels are never hidden — below 320 px they
reduce to three ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.

**Comparison** (v1.1): expanding two rows at once stacks two grids with `axisPointer.link` and
`echarts.connect()`, which is the same component twice, not a new screen.

**Bandtreue is computed server-side on raw Influx samples**, never on `aggregateWindow(mean)` output —
averaging destroys the excursions the metric exists to count and the error is invisible by eye. New endpoint
`GET /data/bandscore?device_id&measure&from&to&min&max` returns
`{ inMs, aboveMs, belowMs, gapMs, coverage, longestExcursionMs, mean, min, max }`. The longest excursion is
reported **as a duration**, because "12 alarms" and "one 9-hour excursion" are different facts.

Prerequisite, non-negotiable, and it belongs in this concept's budget: `data.service.ts` interpolates
`measure`, `from`, `to` and `interval` raw into Flux, and the `limit(n:50000)` guard is piped after
`yield()`. Both are fixed before the measure vocabulary widens by one entry.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

**The band writes half of every diary entry.** That is the concrete form of "it does the diary keeping for
you": the human supplies the *action*, the device supplies the *conditions*, and they are stored together at
write time so the record survives later edits to the band.

New MongoDB collections, all device-scoped, all optional fields, read-time normalisers, no migration needed:

```ts
Plant {
  plant_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                     // 'A1' — free text, that is all a plant needs to exist
  strain?: string; source?: string;  // 'Northern Lights' / 'Steckling von Tom'
  startedAt: number; medium?: string; feedRegime?: string;   // ids into GrowScheme
  archivedAt?: number; harvestedAt?: number;
}

GrowEvent {
  event_id: string;                  // CLIENT-generated UUIDv4 — the idempotency key
  device_id: string; plant_ids: string[];         // [] means "the whole tent"
  type: 'water'|'feed'|'train'|'observe'|'pest'|'transplant'|'stage'|'harvest'
      | 'condition_open'|'condition_close'|'camera_moved';
  at: number; createdAt: number;
  actor_id?: string; actor_label?: string;        // denormalised so history survives a renamed actor
  data: {
    volumeL?: number; nutrients?: { name: string; mlPerL: number }[];
    ec?: number; ph?: number; ecRunoff?: number; phRunoff?: number;
    schemeId?: string; schemeStep?: number;
    heightCm?: number; weightG?: number;          // weight only if the device opted in
    stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage; note?: string;
  };
  images: string[];                                // image_id[]
  bandSnapshot: {                                  // WRITTEN BY THE SERVER AT INSERT TIME
    at: number;
    temperature?: { v: number; state: 'in'|'above'|'below'|'nodata'; dev: number };
    humidity?: {...}; vpd?: {...}; co2?: {...};
    lightsOn?: boolean;
  };
  supersedes?: string;                             // corrections, never edits — append-only
}

Condition { condition_id; device_id; text; plant_ids?: string[];
            openedBy?: string; openedAt: number; closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number; }

Actor { actor_id; device_id; label; colour; createdAt; disabledAt?; }   // NOT an account

Band { band_id; device_id; measure; source:'alarm'|'setpoint'|'stage'|'manual';
       min?: number; max?: number; from: number; to?: number; stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage; }

GrowScheme { scheme_id; medium: string; regime: string; label; steps: [...];
             sourceUrl: string; retrievedAt: number; kind: 'official_pdf'|'official_calculator'|'ownerless'; }

Film { film_id; device_id; from; to; mode; status; gridfsId?; filename; bytes; frames; builtAt; }
```

`DeviceLog` is untouched and keeps doing what it does (boot messages, alarms, config diffs, recipe steps).
The seven legacy prose regexes and the two lifecycle category slugs keep working. The existing
`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` browser-side inference becomes a **one-time idempotent backfill** at boot that
mints a `Plant` per inferred cycle; after that, cycle identity is a stable `plant_id`, so renaming a plant no
longer forks the history and a re-veg no longer fabricates a plant.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed.** If `Plant` count is 0 for a device, the word *Pflanze* never
appears anywhere in the UI. `plant_ids` is `[]`, the capture sheet has no plant chips, the Verlauf list has
no plant filter, and the cumulative totals are per-tent. A plant chip row appears in the capture sheet the
moment a second plant exists — reveal by data, one plant is not a set. Creating a plant is one field: a label.

**Verlauf (`/device/:id/diary`).** A reverse-chronological list of `GrowEvent` + `DeviceLog`, each row
carrying its band snapshot as three tiny chips:

```
Fr 14.09.  19:32   💧 Gegossen · 5 L · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L        Tom
                   A1 A2 A3            [24,6 °C ✓] [68 % ↑] [VPD 0,92 ✓]
                   [thumb] [thumb]
```

Above the list, three filters that are chips, not a form: `Alle · Wasser & Dünger · Pflanze · Gerät`, plus an
actor chip row when more than one actor exists. Server-side pagination (`?before=<ts>&limit=50`) — the
current unbounded `getDeviceLogs` cannot survive a per-plant event stream and its fix is in this budget.

**The cumulative question** gets a permanent answer at the top of each plant's page:

```
A1 · Northern Lights · Tag 47 · Blüte
Bisher: 34 L Wasser · 128 ml Bio-Bloom · 40 ml Bio-Grow · 12 Einträge
Zuletzt: Tom, vor 1 Tag        Bandtreue seit Tag 1: 91 % Temp · 74 % rF
```

## 7. Watering & fertilising

**Gießabstand is a band.** Target interval 2–4 days (from the scheme, editable), trace = time since the last
`water` event for the selected plants. Above the band = overdue. **Below the band = too soon — which is
exactly the double-feed guard.** The safety rule and the reminder are the same rule, drawn once.

### Capture — tap counts

Default case, no plants, following the scheme:

1. **Tap 1** — `💧 Gegossen` in the bottom bar.
2. Sheet slides up, prefilled and readable in the dark:
   ```
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Gegossen                          ✕    │
   │ jetzt · 14.09. 19:32                   │
   │                                        │
   │   [ − ]      5 L      [ + ]            │   volume stepper, 56 px targets
   │                                        │
   │ ✓ mit Dünger    Schritt 4 von 12       │
   │   Bio-Grow 2 ml/L · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L   │
   │   Biobizz All-Mix · Bio·Bizz           │
   │                                        │
   │ EC ____ mS/cm    pH ____               │   visible, empty, optional
   │ Ablauf-EC ____   Ablauf-pH ____        │
   │ Notiz ______________________  📷       │
   │                                        │
   │ Wer? ( Tom ) ( Anna ) ( + )            │   only when >1 Actor exists
   │                                        │
   │        [       Speichern       ]       │
   └────────────────────────────────────────┘
   ```
3. **Tap 2** — `Speichern`. Done. **Two taps.**

Variants and their true costs:
- With plants (>1 exists): a chip row `A1 A2 A3 | Alle` preselected to `Alle` → still **2 taps**; selecting a
  subset costs one tap per deselected plant.
- Water only, no feed: tap the `mit Dünger` checkbox off → **3 taps**.
- Feed with a deviation: change the volume with the stepper (n taps) or long-press the number for a keypad.
- Full club/techie record with EC, pH, runoff EC, runoff pH, note and photo: 2 taps + 4 field entries + the
  camera. **The techie fields are on the same sheet as the two-tap path, always visible, never behind
  "Mehr".** An empty numeric field costs a stoned user nothing but a glance; hiding it would cost the club
  everything. This is the no-modes rule applied to a form.

### The double-feed soft block

At the moment `Speichern` is pressed, the client checks `GrowEvent` for `feed`/`water` on any selected plant
inside `scheme.minIntervalHours` (default 12 h for feed, 8 h for water). If found, the sheet does not close:

```
⚠ Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gegossen — 5 L, Bio-Bloom 4 ml/L.

        [ Abbrechen ]     [ Trotzdem speichern ]
```

One deliberate extra tap, the primary button demoted to secondary green, the other person's name and dose
shown so the decision is informed. This runs client-side against a locally cached window and again
server-side on insert, so it works when the second person is on another phone.

### Schedule following

`GrowScheme` is **medium + feed regime as one selectable thing**, labelled the way growers say it:

- `Biobizz Light-Mix — leicht vorgedüngt, Düngen ab Woche 2`
- `Biobizz All-Mix — stark vorgedüngt, Düngen ab Woche 4`
- `Floragard Light + Green House Powder Feeding`
- `BioTabs — Tabletten, kaum Flüssigdünger, nachlegen alle 5 Wochen`
- `Ohne Plan — nur mitschreiben` (the default; a scheme is never forced)

Chosen once in **Zelt → Medium & Düngeplan**. The scheme advances **by feed events, not by wall clock** —
`Schritt 4 von 12` — with a soft calendar hint (`geplant war Di`). Growers feed Tuesday and Friday and slip;
a calendar-driven schedule is wrong by week three. Autoflowers get a strength multiplier (×0.25–0.5, no
12/12 flip event) because essentially no manufacturer chart has an auto column.

Brand names are plain text labels; no logos, no brand colours, no bottle imagery, never inside a Terp Control
feature name. Numbers live in the `GrowScheme` Mongo collection with `sourceUrl` + `retrievedAt`, so any brand
is removable in one DB update — and the retrieval date is shown to the user as
`Zuletzt geprüft: 12.03.2026`, which is a trust feature, not just legal hygiene. Never render a bare "ppm";
EC is canonical, and a scheme carries `ecBasis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source'` because CANNA publishes
EC+ and alarming against the raw chart number is actively wrong.

### Offline

`@angular/service-worker` + an IndexedDB outbox. **The `event_id` UUID is generated on the phone before the
sheet closes and is the server's idempotency key** (`upsert` on `event_id`), so a retry storm on a cellar
wifi cannot double-log — which in a shared record is indistinguishable from a double feed. Queued events show
in Verlauf with a `wird gesendet` chip and a hairline. This is a genuine new dependency and a build change,
and it is priced in §12.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is the headline, not a tile. Three deliverables ship in v1: **a film someone can hold**, **the
correlation**, and **the camera's own band**.

### 8.1 The camera has a band

`Kamera` is a band row like any other. Trace = age of the newest frame. Band = `≤ 10 Min`. Above the band for
more than six hours fires a real alarm through the existing `Alarm` machinery with a cloud-side pseudo
`sensorType: 'camera_age'` — `actionType: 'email' | 'webhook' | 'info'`, cooldown and retrigger for free.
Copy: `Kamera hat sich seit 9 Std nicht gemeldet.` A camera that quits on day 3 of flower must say so; today
it is silent until you happen to look.

### 8.2 What the film is made of — culling and normalisation

The device already delivers a still every 30 s and the server already runs `ffmpeg` and `sharp`
(`server/Dockerfile:8` installs `ffmpeg imagemagick ttf-dejavu`). The existing hourly `1d/1w/1m` rebuild
stays as the cheap preview. The **Bandfilm** is a separate, deliberate build:

1. **Licht aus — verworfen.** Drop frames where `out_light == 0` at that timestamp (Influx), falling back to
   mean luma < 12/255. A tent timelapse is half black otherwise. Configurable off (`Nachtbilder behalten`)
   for people running the camera with IR.
2. **Doppelgänger — zusammengefasst.** Downsample each kept frame to 32×32 grayscale (`sharp`), compare to
   the previous kept frame; mean absolute difference < 1.5/255 means nothing moved — keep one.
3. **Tür auf / Hand im Bild — verworfen.** A mean-luma jump > 25 % versus the 5-frame median, or a
   large-area 32×32 diff > 18/255 while `out_light` did not change, means the door opened or somebody is in
   shot. Drop the frame and its ±2 neighbours.
4. **Nicht im Band — verworfen** (default on for a Bandfilm, off for the daily preview). Frames captured
   while the primary band was violated are dropped. This is the thesis applied to pixels: the film shows the
   plant under the conditions you asked for. In practice these overlap heavily with (3) — an open door is
   both a bad frame and a temperature excursion — which is why the rule earns its place rather than being a
   gimmick.
5. **Belichtung & Weißabgleich — angeglichen.** For each kept frame, `sharp().stats()` over a fixed central
   50 % crop gives per-channel means. Compute gains so the frame's channel means match a **rolling 7-day
   median target**, clamp each gain to [0.7, 1.4], apply with `sharp().linear(gain, 0)`. This is what removes
   magenta-LED-versus-daylight flicker and the slow exposure drift across twelve weeks. The rolling target
   (not a global one) means a deliberate light change in week 6 is followed, not fought.
6. **Ausrichtung.** v1: a manual `Kamera wurde bewegt` marker (a `GrowEvent` of type `camera_moved`) that
   splits the film into segments and inserts a 0.5 s crossfade at the seam — cheap, honest, and it also
   explains the jump to the viewer. v1.1: phase-correlation on a 128×128 downsample against the previous kept
   frame, translation only, clamped to ±3 % of width, with a 4 % border crop. Automatic stabilisation is the
   single most expensive item in this section and it is deferred openly rather than promised.

Frames pinned by a diary entry (`GrowEvent.images`) are **never culled** — the day you defoliated looks like
garbage to every heuristic above, and a human already told us it matters.

The build reports what it did, in the UI:
`2 016 Bilder · 5 812 verworfen (Licht aus 4 100, Doppel 1 480, Störung 232) · 84 Sek`.

### 8.3 The film that leaves the product

Route: **Kamera → `Film erstellen`.**

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Film erstellen                     ✕   │
│                                        │
│ Zeitraum   ( Ganzer Grow ) ( Blüte )   │
│            ( Letzte Woche ) ( Eigen )  │
│            01.09.2026 – 12.11.2026     │
│                                        │
│ Länge      ~ 84 Sek bei 24 B/s         │
│ Größe      1280 × 720 · ca. 11 MB      │
│                                        │
│ ✓ Nur Bilder im Band                   │
│ ✓ Belichtung angleichen                │
│ ✓ Bandstreifen einblenden              │
│                                        │
│      [      Film erstellen      ]      │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Bandstreifen einblenden** is the feature that makes the video a Terp Control artefact: a 1280×72 px strip
is `vstack`-ed below the picture showing a 3-hour moving window of the temperature band with the trace and
the current timestamp — rendered per frame with `sharp` composites into `%d.jpeg`, so ffmpeg needs no filter
graph beyond the stack. The film carries the app's single primitive out into the world, which is worth more
as marketing than any watermark and, unlike a "Powered by Terp Control" footer, does not name a brand next to
cannabis content (§ 6 KCanG).

Build and delivery, concretely:

```
POST /image/:device_id/film   { from, to, fps: 24, width: 1280, mode: 'bandfilm',
                                band: 'temperature', normalise: true, strip: true }
   -> 202 { film_id, status: 'queued' }
GET  /image/:device_id/film/:film_id           -> { status, progress, frames, bytes, filename }
GET  /image/:device_id/film/:film_id/download  -> 200 video/mp4
        Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terp-northern-lights-20260901-20261112-bandfilm.mp4"
```

- Codec **libx264**, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-crf 26`, `-preset medium`, `-movflags +faststart`, no audio track.
  Not libx265 (which the hourly previews use) — x265 does not play in the Android/iOS share sheet, in
  WhatsApp, or in Telegram's inline player, and the point of this file is that it travels.
- Filename pattern: `terp-<slug(plant.label ?? device.name)>-<vonYYYYMMDD>-<bisYYYYMMDD>-<mode>.mp4`,
  ASCII-slugged, lowercase, so it survives every filesystem and every messenger.
- **Stored in GridFS, not as a Mongo `Buffer`.** A 12-week film at one frame per 30 min is ~4 000 frames,
  ~20 MB, and the 16 MB BSON limit would fail it with no graceful path — the same trap that already makes
  `express-fileupload`'s missing size limit dangerous. GridFS is native to the driver in use; this is a
  half-day, and it is required for the headline feature to work at all.
- The download is a plain authenticated GET that streams with `Content-Disposition: attachment`. On iOS
  Safari and Android Chrome this lands in Files/Downloads and can be moved to the camera roll; the Capacitor
  shell (if it ever exists) can use the native share sheet on the same URL. **A share link to a page behind a
  login is not a share, and this is not that.**
- Retention: films older than 90 days are deleted; the button rebuilds them from the surviving stills.
  Shown as `Film wird nach 90 Tagen gelöscht — jederzeit neu erstellbar.`

### 8.4 Image correlation

Two directions, both from one shared x-scale.

**Chart → picture.** At any band height ≥ 320 px a film strip appears as a lane under the plot, thumbnails
positioned *by timestamp* on the band's own x-axis, so uneven capture reads honestly as gaps and a
`timestampEnd` mp4 renders as a bracket rather than a point. Moving the scrub cursor swaps the large frame
above it. Delivery is **one sprite + JSON index per window**, not N requests:
`GET /image/:device_id/strip?from&to&cols=10&tile=192x108` → a single JPEG sprite plus
`[{ t, x, y }]`. 50 tiles under 15 minutes of footage, 100 above.

**Picture → numbers.** Tapping a frame pins a **Bandkarte** over it — the same five-word vocabulary:

```
14.09.2026 · 19:32
Temperatur  24,6 °C   im Band
rel. Feuchte  68 %    4 % über dem Band
VPD  0,92 kPa         im Band
CO₂  —                keine Daten
Licht an · Heizung aus · Entfeuchter an
[ Zum Verlauf ]  [ Als Eintrag speichern ]
```

`Als Eintrag speichern` creates a `GrowEvent` of type `observe` with that frame attached and the band
snapshot already filled — the diary keeping itself again, from a picture.

And the third direction, which nobody else has: **the diary entry already knows its picture.** Because
`GrowEvent.bandSnapshot.at` is a timestamp on the same axis, every entry in Verlauf shows the nearest camera
frame as its thumbnail even when the user attached nothing.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution without authorisation, in v1.** `Actor` is a device-scoped list of names the owner types once
in Zelt → `Wer ist am Zelt?`. No accounts, no emails, no invitations, no `auth.middleware` surgery. Every
capture sheet shows the actor chips when more than one actor exists; the last used one is preselected;
`actor_id` **and** `actor_label` are stamped on the `GrowEvent`, denormalised so history survives a rename or
a removal. Cost: roughly two days. The real membership rewrite (`auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207`, ~10
owner-scoped queries, a membership collection) is a separate, later project, and `Actor` upgrades into it
cleanly because `actor_id` is already the join key.

**The double-feed problem** is §7's soft block: named, dosed, timed, one deliberate extra tap. It is the most
expensive mistake in a shared room and it is the only place in this concept where the app deliberately gets
in your way.

**Conditions.** An open fact with an owner, not an event and not a task. `Condition { text, plant_ids?,
openedBy, openedAt, closedBy?, closedAt? }`. They live in a pinned strip at the very top of Jetzt under the
heading **An der Zelttür**, above the bands, because that is where a note taped to the tent belongs. Max two
shown, then `+2 weitere`. Each has a `✓` that closes it with the closer's name and time.
Real examples in the seed copy: `CO₂-Flasche fast leer`, `Steckdose 3 wackelt`, `A3 nicht gießen —
übergossen`. Closing one writes a `condition_close` GrowEvent, so the tent's history explains itself later.

**The cumulative question** is answered on every plant page and on the tent page (§6): litres, millilitres per
product, event count, last actor, and Bandtreue since day one. A club member asking "how much Bio-Bloom has
A3 had this cycle, and from whom?" gets it in one screen with no report builder.

**Privacy posture.** This is a design decision and the answer is privacy-maximal by default:

- `Actor.label` is a nickname the owner types. Never a real name requirement, never an email, never a
  membership number, never a date of birth. The app has no field for any of them.
- **No per-member dispensing or consumption records, ever.** That is § 26 KCanG's job and probably Art. 9
  GDPR data; a grow controller has no reason to hold it, and holding it drags the product into a DSFA and a
  DPO for nothing.
- Harvest weight is off by default. Zelt → `Ernte erfassen` is an explicit per-device opt-in with the plain
  sentence `Erntegewichte und Pflanzenzahlen sind rechtlich sensibel. Standardmäßig speichern wir sie nicht.`
- Export (`Verlauf → Exportieren`, CSV + JSON) has a three-way toggle `Namen · Pseudonyme · Anonym`,
  **defaulting to `Pseudonyme`** (`Person A`, `Person B`, stable within the export).
- The event log is append-only via `supersedes` (corrections, not edits) — but it is **deletable by the
  account owner**. Tamper-evidence protects a regulated club against its own staff; permanent immutability
  victimises an unregulated grower. Immutability is a club-scoped opt-in, never global.
- No location, no analytics SDK, no community feed, no real identities anywhere.
- Terp Control does not become anyone's § 26 system of record and ships no compliance badge.

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

No hand-waving: **an old device gets the bands its own settings can define, and nothing else.** The five
`ngSwitchCase` settings components (`fan-settings`, `light-settings`, `plug-settings`, `fridge-settings`) stay
exactly as they are. Only the overview is replaced by the band list, and `bandsForDevice()` returns a
different set per type.

**Plug** (`device_type: 'plug'`). Its settings already carry `limits.overtemperature.{enabled, limit,
hysteresis}`, `limits.undertemperature.{...}` and `limits.time.{min_on, min_off}`. So:
- `Temperatur` — band `[undertemperature.limit, overtemperature.limit]` when either is enabled;
  when only one is enabled the band is one-sided and the open edge is dashed with no number.
  When neither is enabled: `kein Band` + `Band setzen`, which deep-links into the existing plug settings
  page — the band editor for an old device *is* its old settings form. No parallel UI.
- `Einschaltzeit` — trace = minutes on per day from `out_relais`; band from `limits.time` if set.
- Bottom bar shows only `✎ Eintrag` (no `Gegossen` without a plant or a water history).

**Fan** (`device_type: 'fan'`). `Drehzahl` band from its configured min/max rpm, trace from the `rpm` series
(already in `VALID_SENSORS`). Plus `Temperatur` and `rel. Feuchte` if it reports them. Its workmode settings
page is unchanged.

**Light** (`device_type: 'light'`). `Lichtstunden` — band = configured photoperiod ±15 min, trace = measured
on-time per day derived from `out_light`. This is genuinely new value for a device bought years ago: a failed
contactor or a slipped timer shows as `unter dem Band` with a date, which today is invisible. Plus
`Dimmwert` when the PWM output is used.

If a device produces **zero** bands, the home screen shows its current values as `kein Band` rows with the
`Band setzen` affordance and nothing is faked. An owner of two old plugs sees two short pages that look and
read exactly like the controller's — same chips, same five words, same drag — with fewer rows. That is the
whole promise of a single primitive: the cheap device is not a second-class screen, it is a shorter one.

Explicitly **not** broken: the diary, the charts route, share links, `dryer` (dead per `AGENTS.md`, left
alone), `testmode`, `/classes`.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app once a week. Two rows: Temperatur and rel. Feuchte, each with a
big number, a chip and one sentence. `Nicht im Band` is absent, so nothing is wrong; that absence is the
entire report. Both bands come from `Phasenempfehlung` and say so, and both are marked `nur beobachtet`
because no sockets are paired — the app never nags about a humidity target it cannot act on. Later, the
Bandtreue percentages are the "look at them after some time" he asked for, and dragging a row down once shows
six weeks.

**2 · Same, with a camera.** Identical home screen plus a Kamera row with a thumbnail and a freshness band
that will actually shout if the camera dies. His tab is Kamera: a filmstrip he can scrub, and
`Film erstellen → Ganzer Grow → Herunterladen` gives him an 84-second 1280×720 mp4 in his phone's downloads,
culled of black night frames and door-open garbage, exposure-matched across twelve weeks, with the
temperature band scrolling underneath. He posts it to the Telegram group himself; the product does not post
anything anywhere.

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** His paired roles come from `hardwareInfo.sockets`, so he
gets a `CO₂` row (he bought the upgrade) and a heater lane inside the temperature band when he drags it to
320 px. The humidity row is present because the sensor is present, marked `nur beobachtet`, and it never
produces a warning or an alarm preset he cannot act on — which is the current app's worst nagging behaviour.
His alarm thresholds are the band edges, so editing an alarm visibly moves the grey channel.

**4 · Full tent.** Five roles paired, five output lanes, five bands. His temperature band steps at lights-on
and lights-off because day and night setpoints differ, and the step is drawn, so the 04:00 dip he worries
about is either inside the night band or it is not. He is also the type who hits the concept's honest wall:
there is no humidifier role and `dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler, so his rF band has an actuator only in
one direction and the app says `nur beobachtet` on the low side rather than pretending. He drags Temperatur
and rF to 160 px permanently and leaves everything else at 32 px.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** Reads the numbers, not the words. Every band exposes its own data:
`GET /data/bandscore` returns the raw-sample time-in-range, `Als Tabelle` on any expanded band dumps the
series, and `Verlauf → Exportieren` gives CSV and a documented JSON schema including
`bandSnapshot` — actuator state and setpoints included, which no vendor exports today. He will complain that
there is still no write API for outputs, and he will be right; this concept does not solve that.

**6 · Club.** `Wer ist am Zelt?` holds four nicknames. Every capture stamps one. The double-feed block names
Anna and her dose before it lets Tom proceed. `An der Zelttür` carries the two standing facts everyone needs
to know. Once a month somebody opens a plant page and reads the cumulative line, or exports the period as
pseudonymised CSV for the club's own records. Nothing about members, doses to people, or grams leaves the
tent, because none of it is stored.

**7 · Old-device owner.** Opens the app and finds his plug where he left it: same settings page, same
workmodes, plus a temperature band drawn from the over/under-temperature limits he set in 2023 and a
`96 % im Band` he never had. Nothing he used has moved. If he has no limits set, he sees `kein Band` and a
button, not a broken screen.

## 12. v1 scope for October 2026 — six to eight weeks

Realistic, and it is achieved by cutting the chart page, not by cutting the camera.

**Ships.**

1. `<tc-band>` — one component, three size regimes, the drag handle, the five-word chip vocabulary, the
   sentence generator, and the 6-px ribbon. This is the single largest piece of front-end work and everything
   else consumes it.
2. `bandsForDevice()` + band-source resolution (Alarmgrenzen → Sollwert ± Toleranz → Phasenempfehlung →
   kein Band) + the `Band` collection with a boot-time backfill from current settings.
3. The home screen at `/device/:id` replacing the fridge overview, plus the `devices.controller` i18n block
   (de + en) so the flagship stops calling itself a fridge.
4. Server: fix the Flux interpolation and the post-`yield()` limit; `GET /data/series` returning
   `{mean,min,max}` in one call; `GET /data/bandscore` on raw samples with coverage; server-side log
   pagination.
5. **The expanded band** at `/device/:id/charts` on ECharts 6 — band `markArea` with steps, actual with
   `sampling:'minmax'`, deviation fill, min/max envelope, day/night from `out_light`, annotation rail,
   output lane, scrub header, film strip. **Single measure at full height. Multi-panel comparison is cut.**
   Delete `chart.js`, `ng2-charts`, `chartjs-adapter-luxon`.
6. **Camera:** cull + normalise pipeline, `POST/GET /image/:id/film` with GridFS + libx264 download, the
   Bandstreifen overlay, the sprite strip endpoint, the freshness band and the `camera_age` alarm.
7. **Diary:** `Plant`, `GrowEvent`, `Condition`, `Actor` collections; the server-side `bandSnapshot` writer;
   the two-tap capture sheet with the double-feed block; Verlauf with filters and pagination; the
   `convertEventsToGrowCycles` backfill.
8. `GrowScheme` with five seeded medium+regime pairs, prefill into the capture sheet, `Schritt N von M`
   advancing by event, `Gießabstand` band.
9. Service worker + IndexedDB outbox with the client-generated `event_id` as idempotency key.
10. de + en in the same commits. `message-*` convention for everything a log renders.

**Deferred, and said out loud.**

- Automatic stabilisation (v1 ships the manual `Kamera wurde bewegt` marker).
- Multi-band comparison with a linked crosshair; run-over-run day-of-run comparison.
- Public diary URL, Markdown/BBCode generator, Telegram/Discord destinations.
- VPD as an *expressible goal* translated into temperature/humidity setpoints.
- The real membership rewrite. `Actor` covers attribution; authorisation waits.
- Per-plant bands (a plant-scoped Bandtreue needs a plant↔sensor mapping that the hardware cannot give).
- Influx retention and downsampling tasks — required within months for cost and for §6, not for launch.
- Any firmware change. Nothing above needs one.

**The honest risk on this plan** is item 5. Rewriting the chart is the thing that historically eats six
weeks on its own, and it is here only because the concept has no home screen without it — the band row *is*
the chart at another size. The mitigation is that a single-measure full-height panel is roughly a third of
what a general multi-series chart page costs, and the existing 1205-line component is deleted rather than
migrated.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **A band needs a target, and the largest user type has none.** Monitor-only owners get
   `Phasenempfehlung` bands from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS` — advisory numbers presented in the same grey channel
   as a user's own alarm thresholds. The source label mitigates this; it does not fix the fact that the app's
   central visual claim ("you were outside your target") is, for them, "you were outside a preset somebody
   else picked". The dashed advisory edge is a small signal carrying a large caveat.
2. **Bandtreue invites the wrong optimisation.** Widen the band, score 100 %. Narrow it honestly and a
   perfectly healthy grow scores 68 % and feels like a failure. There is no defence against this except
   never gamifying the number — no streaks, no badges, no leaderboard, no push notification containing a
   percentage — and that discipline has to survive whoever writes the marketing page.
3. **Some things do not band, and I band them anyway.** `Gießabstand` is a duration, not a measurement, and
   "below the band" only means anything if you are following a schedule. `Lichtstunden` is one point per day —
   a "trace" through fourteen points is a stretch of the primitive. CO₂ in a non-enriched tent and
   hand-entered pH/EC (three values in six weeks) produce bands that are honestly meaningless and are drawn
   as sparse points with `zu wenig Daten`, which is the right answer and also an admission.
4. **Uniformity is monotony.** Seven grey strips down a page is the point of the concept and also its
   aesthetic risk. A normal user who compares this to AC Infinity's colourful app and to Instagram may find
   it clinical or even medical — the visual language is knowingly borrowed from CGM dashboards, and glucose
   monitoring is not a hobby anybody enjoys looking at.
5. **The drag handle is undiscoverable to the user who needs it least and might need it most.** The
   invariant-top rule means missing it costs nothing, which is the defence — but "it's fine if you never find
   it" is also an admission that a headline interaction may go unused by most people most of the time. The
   one-time contextual hint is a mitigation, not a solution.
6. **Culling cannot tell the interesting frame from the garbage frame.** The day you defoliated, the day the
   plant fell over, and the day you left the door open are the same signal to every heuristic in §8.2.
   Pinning diary-attached frames covers the events somebody logged and nothing else.
7. **The diary is genuinely off-thesis.** A photo, a strain name, a note and a plant's identity have no band.
   §6 layers them on by making the *conditions* half of each entry band-shaped, which is real value, but the
   claim "one primitive explains the whole product" is weakest exactly where the owner asked for the most
   focus.
8. **Time-in-range is easy to compute wrong and impossible to eyeball.** It must run on raw Influx samples;
   the moment somebody optimises it onto `aggregateWindow(mean)` output the numbers get quietly better and
   nobody notices. Coverage reporting is the only tripwire and it depends on discipline.
9. **The band is drawn for things the hardware cannot close.** No humidifier role exists, and
   `dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler, so an rF band has an actuator in one direction at best. `nur
   beobachtet` is honest and also a permanent visible reminder that "ein Controller für dein ganzes Zelt"
   currently overstates the firmware.
10. **Costs.** Tree-shaken ECharts is ~195.6 KB gz against Highstock's ~132.7 KB; deleting Chart.js repays
    ~68.8 KB. The deviation fill adds two auxiliary series per band, doubling client-side point counts. The
    service worker and GridFS are both new infrastructure in a codebase with 38 `it()` blocks and no
    migration tooling.
