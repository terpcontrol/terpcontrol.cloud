# C39 — "Kein Doppelguss"

**Family:** feeding · **Centre of gravity:** capture safety between people

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every capture is a claim on named plants for a bounded window — visible to everyone before anyone
acts, safe to retry from a cellar, and stamped with a hand — so the most expensive failure in a shared
room (the same job done twice) becomes structurally hard instead of merely regrettable.**

Ten concepts optimised how *fast* you can log a feed. That is the wrong variable. In a room with two
people the cost function is not seconds, it is the double feed: two litres of Bio-Bloom into pots that
already had it this morning, discovered eight days later as clawed leaves. Nobody in this market
prevents it — not AC Infinity, not Pulse, not the ten round-one concepts, and not the current product,
whose `DeviceLog` has no actor field at all. So make capture safety the spine and let the rest hang off
it: a device-scoped list of **Hände** (no accounts), a live **presence lease** so the tent tells you
someone is already in it, a **claim** written before the form is filled, a **soft block** when the same
plants got the same treatment inside a window, a **`capture_id` minted before the network is touched**
so an offline retry cannot double-log, and — the payoff nobody expects — a **timelapse that is clean
precisely because the app knows when a human was in the tent**. Presence data culls the garbage frames.
The safety mechanism and the headline camera feature are the same mechanism.

## 2. Information architecture

Navigation stays `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` (account, share links, dark mode, install, logout). What
is new is a **four-item bottom bar scoped to a device** — not a mode switch, four places that always
exist and always mean the same thing.

```
/list                              device list · one device → straight to /tent
/device/:id/tent        Zelt       HOME. fixed slots, variable height
/device/:id/pflege      Pflege     the ledger: fällig · konditionen · verlauf · pflanzen
/device/:id/charts      Charts     ECharts rebuild: goal-vs-actual, care rail, outputs lane
/device/:id/kamera      Kamera     film strip, cycle timelapse, downloads
/device/:id/settings    (menu)     existing settings page, unchanged routing
/device/:id/haende      (from Zelt) hands list — owner edits names
/device/:id/testmode    (menu, admin) unchanged
/diary                             KEPT as an alias → /pflege?report=verlauf (share links survive)
```

Two overlays, reachable from `tent` and `pflege`: the **Erfassen-Sheet** (`ion-modal`,
`breakpoints: [0.55, 0.92]`) — the capture flow, where the whole concept lives — and the
**Zettel-Sheet**, which opens and closes a Kondition. The old `/device/:id/diary` route stays
registered and redirects, because `SharePage = 'charts' | 'diary'` and `ShareLink.query` are persisted
user data. Under a share token the bottom bar renders with Pflege and Kamera read-only (they already
resolve through `resolveDeviceAccessInfo`) and the capture button absent, not disabled-looking; demo
sessions get the full UI and a refused save.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — **Reveal by the room, not by the reader**

The interface's density is a function of **what has happened in this room and who else is involved** —
never of who is looking, never of a preference, never of a toggle. Concretely, four rules:

**R1 — Fixed slots, variable height.** Every surface (home, capture sheet, care event detail) has a
fixed ordered slot list. A slot with nothing true collapses to zero. Nothing reorders, nothing is
hidden behind "mehr anzeigen" that would hide a *class* of feature. The stoner's simple screen is the
same screen on a quiet day; the techie's dense screen is the same screen on a busy one. Neither of them
chose it. **This is the direct answer to "adaptive ordering hurts the impaired user": order is static,
only presence varies, and presence varies for a reason the user can state out loud** ("Anna is in the
tent", "there is a note on the door").

**R2 — Progressive precision inside one line, not one screen behind another.** Every fact is written
verdict → number → raw, left to right or top to bottom, on the same surface:

```
Gießen  A1 A2 A4        zuletzt vor 2 Tagen        ›
        ↑ verdict       ↑ number                   ↑ raw: the CareEvent with capture_id,
                                                     hand, source, frame, edit history
```

The stoner reads the first token and stops. The techie reads to the end of the line and taps the
chevron. Nobody was asked which one they are.

**R3 — The safety line carries the density.** The single surface everyone must pass through is the
capture confirm. It renders, always, in this order:

```
Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gedüngt.            ← verdict, plain German, 1 line
Bio-Grow 2 ml/l · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/l · 4,5 l      ← the numbers, same sheet, no tap
EC 1,4 · pH 6,2 · Kanne 5 l · 09:14              ← precision, same sheet
[ Eintrag ansehen ]                              ← raw: capture_id, source, frame, device
```

A club that must reconstruct "how much Bio-Bloom did A3 get this cycle and from whom" reads line 2 and
3 of a surface the stoner also sees. There is no reporting screen the casual user is excluded from and
no simplified sheet the club user has to escape.

**R4 — Reveal by capability and by data, ruthlessly.** No CO₂ upgrade → no CO₂ column anywhere, not
even greyed. No sockets paired → no output lane on the chart. No second plant created → **no plant
chips in the capture sheet at all**, and blocks operate at tent scope. One hand in the list → no
attribution row, no presence slot, and the block copy loses the name: *"Vor 1 Std 30 gedüngt.
Trotzdem?"* Add a second hand and the same sentence gains a subject. **The multi-user product grows out
of the single-user product one row at a time; it is not a different build.**

What this concept explicitly refuses: an "Experten"-Segment, an "Erweitert" accordion, a
`localStorage['app-settings-expert']` successor under any name, and any onboarding question about the
user. The existing `app-settings-expert` segment in fridge/controller settings is **deleted** and its
contents merged into one settings page ordered by R1 (see §12).

## 5. The chart — `/device/:id/charts`

ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line directive (owner decision). Highcharts, `chart.js`,
`ng2-charts`, `chartjs-adapter-luxon` all deleted.

**Layout — three stacked grids on one shared x-axis, one linked crosshair** (`grid[]` +
`axisPointer.link: [{xAxisIndex:'all'}]`):

```
┌ Klima ────────────────────────────────────── 150 px ┐
│  ····· Ziel (dashed, stepped at day/night)          │
│  ▬▬▬▬▬ Ist (solid, min/max envelope behind)         │
│  ▒▒▒▒▒ Abweichung (signed fill Ist↔Ziel)            │
│  ░░░░  Nacht (markArea, from measured out_light)    │
├ Ausgänge ───────────────────────────────────  56 px ┤
│  Licht   ██████████░░░░░░░░░░██████████            │
│  Heizung ░░█░░█░░██░░░░░█░░░░░░░█░░░░░░░           │
│  Entf.   ░░░░░░░░░░░████░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░           │
├ Pflege-Schiene ─────────────────────────────  22 px ┤
│      ⬤A      ⬤B  ⬤A            ⟦!⟧    ⬤A          │
│      ↑ care events, dot colour = hand                │
│                              ↑ red bracket =         │
│                                Doppelguss-Verdacht   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Goal vs actual.** The setpoint is a `type:'line', step:'end', lineStyle:{type:'dashed'}` series built
client-side from the parsed `device.configuration` (`day.temperature`, `night.temperature`,
`daynight.day`, `daynight.night` — seconds-of-day UTC, converted in an explicit device timezone). Band
source priority, one at a time, **labelled on the band itself**: alarm thresholds (`Alarmgrenze`) >
setpoint ± tolerance (`Zielband ±1,5 °C`) > stage `vpdRange` (`Blüte 1,2–1,5 kPa`). The deviation fill
is two `areaStyle` lines clipped against each other, `--tc-text-warning` above target and
`--tc-text-info` below. **Never fill to the axis.**

**Day/night.** `markArea` from measured `out_light > 0` where the series exists, falling back to the
configured `daynight` schedule, in the device's timezone. A failed contactor shows as a *missing band* —
*"Nacht laut Plan 22:00–10:00 — Licht war an."*

**Annotations = the Pflege-Schiene.** A 22 px rail *under* the plot, never columns inside it. Dots are
`custom` series elements **coloured by `hand_id`** (the presence slot's 6-colour hand palette),
pixel-clustered at 10 px with a count badge, tapping through to the CareEvent detail (§7.3) with its
correlated frame. Stage changes are full-height dashed `markLine`s. A `lateConflict` pair (§7.4) is a
red bracket spanning both events, labelled `Doppelguss-Verdacht`.

**Outputs** are a state-timeline lane (`custom` series, region length = duration, null = gap), not 0/1
lines on the value axis; only paired roles render a row (`parseSocketRoles`).

**Mobile rules.** One finger scrolls, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom:{type:'inside'}`), tap/long-press
sets the crosshair. **No floating tooltip** — a pinned scrubbing header above the top grid, one row per
grid, plus the correlated camera thumbnail (§8.4). Axis labels are never hidden; below 360 px they drop
to 3 ticks and the unit moves into the grid title. `sampling:'minmax'` on the plot (LTTB smooths away
the bang-bang oscillation the grower is looking for), LTTB on the navigator. Okabe-Ito, `darkMode:'auto'`.

**Query-string compatibility.** `measures`, `date`, `dateEnd`, `vpdMode`, `autoUpdate`, `useCustom`,
`timespan`, `interval`, `logs`, `share` all keep their meaning. New params are additive and default to
the old behaviour: `ziel=1` (setpoint on, default 1), `nacht=1`, `pflege=1`, `hand=<hand_id>` (filter
the rail). Every existing `ChartPreset` and `ShareLink` renders unchanged.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New entities (all MongoDB, cloud-side, `device_id`-keyed, all fields optional)

```ts
Plant   { plant_id, device_id, cycle_id, label,          // "A1" — free text, 12 chars
          strain?, medium?, potSizeL?, startedAt, stage: DiaryLifecycleStage,
          archivedAt? }

Cycle   { cycle_id, device_id, name, startedAt, flipAt?, endedAt? }   // "Sommer 26"

CareEvent {
  capture_id,            // UUIDv7, minted CLIENT-SIDE at button press. UNIQUE INDEX.
  device_id, cycle_id, plant_ids: string[],              // [] === tent scope
  kind: 'giessen'|'duengen'|'spruehen'|'training'|'umtopfen'|'ernte'|'wartung'|'notiz',
  at, hand_id,
  pours?, pourSizeL?, volumeL?,                          // tap counts → litres
  ec?, ph?, runoffEc?, waterTempC?,
  doses?: [{ productLabel, mlPerL?, gPerL?, amountMl?, note? }],
  scheme_id?, schemeStep?,
  note?, images?: string[], frame_ref?,                  // image_id of the nearest camera still
  overrode?: { blockedBy: capture_id|condition_id, byHand, atTapMs },
  lateConflict?: boolean,
  source: 'app'|'offline-replay',
  createdAt, editedAt?, revokedAt?, revokedBy?           // revoke, never delete
}

Condition { condition_id, device_id, plant_ids: string[], text,
            kind: 'stop'|'warn'|'info', openedBy, openedAt, closedBy?, closedAt? }
```

Plus the presence primitives in §9.

### 6.2 How the diary stops being a chore

The owner's line is *"it does the diary keeping for you"*. Concretely, three of the diary's rows are
written without a human:

1. **Care events are the diary.** Every capture is an entry. There is no separate "write a diary entry"
   act for the 90 % case.
2. **Stage changes are inferred and offered, never assumed.** A sustained 18/6 → 12/12 flip in
   `out_light` history writes a *proposal* condition: `⚑ Umstellung auf 12/12 am 14.08. erkannt —
   Blüte eintragen?  [ Ja ]  [ Nein ]`. One tap writes the `Cycle.flipAt` and the stage change for
   every plant in the cycle. This is a real capability no bottle-brand app has: we own the photoperiod.
3. **The week writes itself.** `Pflege → Verlauf` groups by ISO week and computes, per plant and per
   cycle: total litres, total ml per product, feed count, mean EC, days since last feed, and **who**.
   The club's "how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had this cycle and from whom" is a stored aggregate line, not
   a query somebody must invent.

### 6.3 The untaxed path for a grower who ignores plants

**If `Plant` count is 0, the plant chip row is not rendered anywhere.** Not collapsed — absent. Capture
writes `plant_ids: []`, blocks evaluate at tent scope, the ledger reads *"Gegossen · 10 l · vor 2
Tagen · Ben"*. The user is never asked to name a plant, never sees a plant tab, never sees an empty
state telling them to add plants.

The first plant appears one of two ways, both incidental: the setup wizard's existing `stage` step gains
one optional field `Wie viele Pflanzen? [1] [2] [3] [4] [+]` which creates `A1…An` with default labels;
or the capture sheet's overflow (`⋯ → Pflanzen anlegen`). **From two plants onward the chip row appears
and blocks become per-plant.** Reveal by data.

### 6.4 Compatibility with `DeviceLog`

Every `CareEvent` write is mirrored by the server into a `DeviceLog` row with
`categories: ['diary','diary-care']`, `data.capture_id`, `severity: 0`, `deleted: true` (the existing
"manual entry" convention). That keeps `LogEntryViewerModule`, the entries report, the grow report and
existing share links working with zero changes on day one. **The mirror is a render cache, not a second
truth** — it is written and revoked only by the server, in `careService`, and carries
`data.capture_id` so the two can always be reconciled. Cost and risk named in §13.

Two legacy bugs are fixed in the same pass: the server's stage-transition entry gets its missing
`'diary'` category prefix (`device.service.ts:714`), and `DELETE /device/logs/:device_id` is relabelled
`Geräte-Log leeren` and made to exclude `diary*` explicitly, instead of claiming to clear a diary it
never touched.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — the capture flow

### 7.1 The Erfassen-Sheet, step by step

**Press.** `[ Erledigt eintragen ]` on Zelt or Pflege. At the moment of `pointerdown`:

- a `capture_id` (UUIDv7) is minted **locally**;
- if no presence lease is open for the current user, one is opened silently (`source:'implicit'`);
- the sheet opens at breakpoint 0.55.

**Sheet, slot order (fixed):**

```
┌──────────────────────────────── Erfassen ──┐
│  Gießen   Düngen   Sprühen   Training   ⋯  │  ← kind. Last used is preselected.
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ⬤ alle    A1   A2   A3   A4               │  ← plant chips (absent if <2 plants)
│           ↑ preselected: all, or the plants
│             the FÄLLIG row named
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Kannen                                    │  ← TAP COUNTS
│      ◯  ◯  ◯                    15 l       │
│      ↑ tap the can: +1. long-press: keypad │
│  1 Kanne = 5 l                    [ ändern ]│
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Bio-Grow    2 ml/l      →  9 ml           │  ← prefilled from the bound scheme
│  Bio-Bloom   4 ml/l      →  18 ml          │     every number editable
│  All-Mix · Biobizz · Schritt 6 · Woche 5   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  EC 1,4      pH 6,2      + Foto            │  ← only if the last 5 events used them
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Anna ▾                             09:14  │  ← hand + time (absent if 1 hand)
├────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│         [      Eintragen      ]            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Tap counts, precisely.** The can row is one component, `pour-counter`. Tap the can icon → `pours++`,
haptic tick, `volumeL = pours × pourSizeL` recomputed and shown large. A long-press on the litre number
opens the numeric keypad and writes `volumeL` directly, clearing `pours`. `pourSizeL` is a per-device
setting (`CareDefaults.pourSizeL`, default 5), changed inline via `[ ändern ]`. **One widget serves the
stoner counting cans in the dark and the techie typing 4,7 l.** Both values are stored: `pours: 3,
pourSizeL: 5, volumeL: 15`.

The EC/pH row is governed by R4: it renders only if any of the last five `CareEvent`s on this device
carried `ec` or `ph`. A grower who has never measured EC never sees an EC field; a club that measures
every time always does. Nobody set a preference. The `⋯` overflow always offers `EC/pH erfassen` so the
field is reachable on the first ever use.

### 7.2 The claim and the soft block

`[ Eintragen ]` does **not** save first. It writes a `CareClaim` (or, offline, queues one) and evaluates
the block:

```ts
CareClaim { claim_id, device_id, hand_id, plant_ids, kind,
            claimedAt, expiresAt = claimedAt + 15min,
            resolvedBy?: capture_id, cancelledAt? }
```

Three block sources, evaluated in this order, first match wins:

| Source | Trigger | Copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kondition `stop`** | an open `Condition{kind:'stop'}` covers a selected plant | `A3: nicht gießen — übergossen. Anna, vor 2 Tagen.` |
| **Live claim** | an unresolved `CareClaim` overlaps plants ∩ kind | `Ben ist gerade dabei — A1–A3, seit 2 Min.` |
| **Fenster** | a `CareEvent` of the same kind on an overlapping plant inside the window | `Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gedüngt.` |

Windows live in `CareDefaults.windows`, editable per device: `giessen: 8h`, `duengen: 20h`,
`spruehen: 6h`, others: none.

**The block sheet — this is the surface §4/R3 describes:**

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚑  Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gedüngt.   │
│                                            │
│     Bio-Grow 2 ml/l · Bio-Bloom 4 ml/l     │
│     4,5 l · EC 1,4 · pH 6,2 · 09:14        │
│     [ Eintrag ansehen ]                    │
│                                            │
│  ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │  Abbrechen                           │  │  ← primary weight, full width
│  └──────────────────────────────────────┘  │
│                                            │
│           Trotzdem eintragen               │  ← text button, right-aligned,
│                                            │     32 px below. One tap.
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Deliberate design decisions here, each arguable:

- **Cancel is the primary button.** The expensive action is proceeding.
- **One extra tap, not a hold and not a typed reason.** A hold gesture fails for an impaired user with
  one hand in a dark tent; a mandatory reason field means people type "x". The brief asked for one
  deliberate extra tap and that is exactly right.
- **The reason is optional and asked *after* the save**, as a dismissible chip on the new entry:
  `Warum trotzdem?  [ Anna hat nur gegossen ] [ Nachdüngen geplant ] [ anderes ]`. Answered → stored in
  `overrode.reason`. Ignored → nothing lost.
- **`overrode.atTapMs`** records the ms between the block rendering and the override tap. Not shown to
  users. It is the single number that tells us in three months whether the block is training override
  reflex (§13.3).

Proceeding writes the `CareEvent` with `overrode` populated, and the ledger and the chart rail mark it
`⚑ trotz Block`.

```
Erfasst 09:14:07 · gespeichert 09:14:09
[ Zurücknehmen ]   [ Bearbeiten ]
```

The last block is the techie's/club's raw layer and it is on the *same sheet*, below the fold, not
behind a mode. `Zurücknehmen` sets `revokedAt/revokedBy` — a compensating correction, never a delete,
because the club case needs the history and the private grower needs the account owner to still be able
to wipe everything (§9.4).

### 7.4 Offline capture — idempotent by construction

- **`capture_id` is minted before the network exists.** `POST /care/:device_id` is an upsert on the
  unique index `{capture_id}`. Replaying the same body 50 times produces one row. **A retry queue can
  no longer double-log, so an offline replay can never be mistaken for a double feed** — which is
  precisely the failure mode the round-one critic found.
- **Queue:** IndexedDB (`tc-care-queue`), drained on app open, on `window.online`, and every 30 s while
  the tab lives. `@angular/service-worker` is added for the **app shell only** — Angular's SW has no
  background-sync hook, and the Background Sync API is Chromium-only, so we do not pretend. Honest
  failure mode, stated in the UI: closing the tab in a cellar and never reopening it loses nothing
  *locally* but sends nothing.
- **Offline block honesty.** The capture sheet's block line carries its own freshness:
  `Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gedüngt.  ·  Stand: vor 3 Std · offline`. A stale check is announced as
  stale.
- **Late-conflict reconciliation.** On replay the server re-evaluates the window against everything it
  now knows. If it finds a conflict the client could not have seen, it **still accepts the event** —
  never silently drops a human's record — sets `lateConflict: true`, and opens a
  `Condition{kind:'warn'}`:
  `⚑ Möglicher Doppelguss: A1–A3 am 12.09. um 19:04 (Anna) und 19:11 (Ben). [ Ansehen ] [ Erledigt ]`
  The chart rail draws the red bracket. **Two people offline in a cellar cannot be prevented from
  double-feeding. They can be told within seconds of the first phone reconnecting.**

### 7.5 Schedule following

`SchemeBinding { device_id, cycle_id, scheme_id, startedAt, strength: 0.25…1.5 }`, bound once during
setup: *"Womit ziehst du?"* → a list of **medium + regime pairs the way growers say it**: `Biobizz
All-Mix`, `Biobizz Light-Mix`, `Floragard Light`, `Green House Powder Feeding`, `BioTabs`,
`Eigenes Schema`.

The scheme's only job is to prefill. `FeedScheme.steps[]` carries `{ index, phase, weekFrom, weekTo,
doses[{productLabel, mlPerL|gPerL}], ecTarget?, ecBasis: 'absolute'|'delta_over_source', phTarget?,
note? }`, in a Mongo content collection with `source_url` + `retrieved_at` per entry, never bundled.

- **All-Mix expresses "don't feed yet".** Steps 1–3 have `doses: []` and
  `note: 'Vorgedüngt — noch nicht düngen'`. The FÄLLIG slot then says
  `Düngen  —  All-Mix ist vorgedüngt · ab Woche 4`, and choosing `Düngen` in the sheet shows that as
  an info line, not a block.
- **BioTabs** is `cadenceDays: 42` with a single dose row — the "schedule" is re-tabbing.
- **Advance by feed events, not by wall clock.** `schemeStep` increments when a `duengen` CareEvent is
  saved, with a soft calendar mapping shown as `Schritt 6 · Woche 5`. Growers feed Tue/Fri and slip.
- **Never render a bare ppm.** EC is canonical; `ecBasis: 'delta_over_source'` schemes show
  `EC-Ziel 1,6 + Leitungswasser` and, if the user has stored `sourceEc`, the absolute number next to it.
- Every prefilled number is editable and the edit does not "break" the schedule — deviation is normal.
- `[ Letztes Verhältnis übernehmen ]` in the `⋯` overflow copies the previous event's doses verbatim.
  That is the answer for the grower who mixes by feel and hates schemes.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

**This concept's camera thesis: the presence data that makes capture safe is also what makes the
timelapse watchable.** No other concept can cull human-in-frame shots without CV, because no other
concept knows when a human was in the tent.

### 8.1 What ships in v1 — a video someone can hold

**Zyklus-Zeitraffer.** One mp4 per `Cycle`, rebuilt nightly (and on demand, rate-limited to 1/hour),
covering `Cycle.startedAt → min(endedAt, now)`. Target length 45–90 s at 25 fps: frames are selected to
a budget of `min(2250, availableAfterCulling)`, evenly spaced by *time*, so an 80-day grow renders at
roughly one frame per 50 minutes of lights-on.

The three existing rolling durations (`1d`/`1w`/`1m`) stay exactly as they are — the code works and the
charts page uses them. The cycle timelapse is a fourth `duration: 'cycle'` value plus a `cycle_id`
field on `Image`.

### 8.2 Culling and normalising — the actual pipeline

Executed in `imageService.buildCycleTimelapse()`, reusing the existing tmpdir/ffmpeg/`sharp` machinery:

1. **Lights-off cull.** Drop every frame whose `out_light` from InfluxDB at that timestamp is < 5 %.
   Measured, not guessed from pixel brightness — the series already exists. (Fallback for monitor-only
   devices: mean luma below the 15th percentile of the cycle.)
2. **Human-in-tent cull — the differentiator.** Drop every frame captured inside an open
   `PresenceLease` widened by ±120 s, and inside ±120 s of any `CareEvent.at`. These are the door-open,
   hand-in-shot, light-leak frames. **Where presence is never used**, fall back to a difference
   heuristic: a frame whose 16×16 luma hash differs from *both* neighbours by > 18 % while the
   neighbours agree with each other is a transient and is dropped. Honest limitation in §13.2.
3. **Dedup.** Skip a frame whose 16×16 luma hash is within 2 % of the last kept frame — but force-keep
   at least one frame per 90 minutes of lights-on so growth never appears to freeze.
4. **Exposure + white-balance normalisation.** Per frame, via `sharp` (already a server dependency):
   grey-world channel gains clamped to ±25 %, then a linear luma gain to bring the frame's median luma
   onto a **rolling 24-frame target median**, clamped to ±30 %. The clamps are what keep a magenta LED
   scene from being "corrected" into grey mush; the rolling target is what removes the day-to-day
   flicker across 12 weeks.
5. **Stabilisation.** ffmpeg `deshake=rx=32:ry=32` on the assembled sequence. This fixes jitter. It does
   **not** fix a camera nudged 5° on day 12 — for that, a **`Kamera neu ausgerichtet`** care event
   (`kind:'wartung'`, subtype `camera-realigned`, one tap from the Kamera page) makes the builder insert
   a **hard cut with a 6-frame cross-dissolve** at that timestamp instead of pretending continuity.
6. **Encode.** `libx264 -crf 23 -preset medium -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart`, 1920×1080 (or the
   source's native resolution if smaller), 25 fps. **Not h265** — the existing rolling timelapses use
   libx265 which Safari plays and a lot of Android browsers and messengers do not. A file that will not
   play in WhatsApp is not a selling point.

### 8.3 How the video leaves the product

```
GET /image/:device_id/timelapse/:cycle_id/download?share=<token>
  → 200
    Content-Type:        video/mp4
    Content-Length:      <n>
    Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_2026-06-14_tag-01-68_1080p.mp4"
    Cache-Control:       private, max-age=3600
```

- The **Kamera** page shows the timelapse in a native `<video controls playsinline>` with a
  `[ Herunterladen ]` button below it that is a plain `<a download href=…>`. On iOS Safari that is a
  long-press → "Video sichern"; the button copy adapts: `Zum Sichern gedrückt halten`. On Android and
  desktop it lands in Downloads. **No login-walled URL is presented as a share.**
- Size honesty: 60 s of 1080p at crf 23 is ~8–15 MB. That does **not** fit the 16 MB BSON document
  limit reliably, so cycle timelapses are stored in **GridFS** (`fs.files`/`fs.chunks`, same Mongo, same
  `backup.sh`) with an `Image` row carrying `gridfs_id` instead of `data`. Named as real work in §12.
- A `ShareLink{page:'kamera'}` renders the same player and the same download button, no login. That is
  the actual share.

### 8.4 Image correlation — both directions, everywhere

**Chart → picture.** The pinned scrubbing header carries a 96×54 thumbnail of the frame nearest the
crosshair, swapped at ≤ 8 fps while dragging. Delivery is a **sprite sheet**:
`GET /image/:device_id/strip?from&to&n=60` returns one JPEG of 60 × 128×72 tiles plus a JSON timestamp
index — one request per window instead of sixty.

**Picture → numbers.** Every full-size frame renders its conditions underneath, read from Influx at
that timestamp:
```
12.09.2026, 09:14
24,3 °C · 57 % · VPD 1,18 kPa · CO₂ 780 ppm · Licht 100 %
Tag 41 · Blüte, Tag 12          [ Im Chart zeigen ]
```

**Care event → frame.** On save the server resolves `frame_ref` to the nearest `format:'jpeg'` still
within ±5 min. That frame is the "who watered" evidence *and* the reason the same frame is excluded
from the timelapse — one dataset, two opposite uses.

**Film strip as an axis.** On `/kamera`, thumbnails sit by timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so uneven
capture reads honestly as gaps; care events sit on the same axis as coloured dots; an mp4 with
`timestampEnd` renders as a bracket, not a point.

**Camera alarm.** A `webcam_silent` check runs with the hourly compress pass: device online, last `jpeg`
older than 12 h → a `Condition{kind:'warn'}` plus the existing alarm path (`email`/`webhook`) with
`message-webcam-silent`, resolved by a new frame or a human closing it. **A camera that stopped three
days into flower now says so.**

## 9. Clubs

### 9.1 Attribution without an auth rewrite

```ts
Hand          { hand_id, device_id, name, colorIndex, createdBy, createdAt, archivedAt? }
PresenceLease { lease_id, device_id, hand_id, startedAt, expiresAt, endedAt?,
                source: 'explicit'|'implicit' }
```

Hands are **names the owner types once**, on `/device/:id/haende`. No accounts, no invitations, no
email, no `auth.middleware` surgery — `owner_id` stays a single string and stays the only write gate.
The login is shared, as it already is in every club and every couple; the hand picker sits in the
capture sheet (`Anna ▾`) and remembers per browser (`localStorage['tc-hand-<device_id>']`). **Cost:
~2 days**, exactly the round-one critic's point.

`[ Ich bin am Zelt ]` opens a lease with `expiresAt = +45 min`, auto-extended by any capture, closed by
`[ Ich gehe ]` or expiry. The lease is what makes the block *live* rather than retrospective, what
powers the timelapse cull, and what puts "someone is in the tent" on everybody's home screen.

### 9.2 The double feed — the whole point, restated for the club

Three overlapping defences, weakest to strongest:

1. **Ambient.** The presence slot on the home screen. You see Anna is in the tent before you leave the
   sofa. No interaction required.
2. **Live.** The claim: Ben taps `Düngen` on A1–A3 while Anna's claim is open → *"Ben ist gerade dabei
   — A1–A3, seit 2 Min."*
3. **Retrospective.** The window: same plants, same kind, inside 20 h → the block sheet.

And when all three fail because both phones were offline: **late-conflict reconciliation** (§7.4),
which converts a silent disaster into a warning on the tent door within seconds of reconnection.

### 9.3 Conditions — the note taped to the tent door

`Condition` is not an event and not a task: an open fact with an owner. Opened from the Zettel-Sheet
(`+` in slot 2, or `⋯ → Zettel schreiben` in the capture sheet), one line of text, optional plant
chips, kind:

- `stop` → red, blocks captures on those plants (soft, one extra tap, names the condition and its
  author).
- `warn` → amber, shown but does not block. This is what the camera alarm and late-conflict use.
- `info` → grey. *"CO₂-Flasche fast leer"*, *"Steckdose 3 wackelt"*.

Anyone can close one; the closing hand is recorded. A closed condition stays in the ledger.

### 9.4 Privacy posture

Deliberately privacy-maximal, matching the dossier's §6 line:

- **Hands are pseudonyms by design.** The field label is `Name oder Kürzel`, the placeholder is `A`, and
  the helper text is `Ein Kürzel reicht — der Name verlässt dieses Gerät nicht.` Nothing forces a real
  name. No email, no DOB, no membership number, no address. **Per-member dispensing quantities and THC
  content are not modelled and never will be** — that is § 26 KCanG territory and Art. 9 GDPR risk for
  data a grow controller has no use for.
- **Every export defaults to pseudonymised.** `Export → Namen: [ Kürzel ] [ Hand 1, Hand 2 ] [ keine ]`,
  default `Hand 1, Hand 2`.
- **Human frames are opt-in and off by default.** `frame_ref` on a CareEvent stores a picture that may
  contain a person standing in a cannabis tent. The setting is on the Kamera page:
  `Beweisbild zum Eintrag speichern  ( ) aus  (•) nur Zeitstempel  ( ) Bild` — default *nur
  Zeitstempel*, which stores the correlated **numbers** and no `frame_ref`. Opting in shows the plain
  sentence `Das Bild kann Personen zeigen.` This is a real hazard in a prohibition jurisdiction and the
  concept refuses to default it on. (The timelapse cull works either way — it needs the lease, not the
  frame.)
- **The owner can wipe.** `Einstellungen → Alles löschen` removes hands, leases, care events, conditions
  and images for the device. Revoke-not-delete is the *within-history* rule; it is never a barrier to
  the account owner erasing the record.
- No location, no analytics SDK, no community feed, no public identities.

**Not built, deliberately:** memberships, roles, permissions, per-member ledgers, § 26 exports,
compliance badges. If a club needs a legal system of record, it buys Cannanas — and the honest
integration path is Terp Control publishing device-attested environmental data *into* it (dossier §6.4).

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens Zelt. Slots 1, 2, 3 are empty and gone. Four numbers with an age on
them, a 24 h sparkline with the target band behind it, and no camera slot. No plants, no scheme, no
hands, no capture prompt beyond a button they can ignore forever. The one thing they gain over today is
that a stale reading now says it is stale. Charts gains the setpoint line, which turns their decoration
into a verdict.

**2 — Monitoring with a camera.** The same, plus slot 5 and the Kamera tab. Their headline is the
Zyklus-Zeitraffer: a real mp4, culled of lights-off and door-open frames, exposure-normalised across
twelve weeks, downloadable as
`terpcontrol_zelt-1_2026-06-14_tag-01-68_1080p.mp4`. They never used presence, so the human-in-frame cull
runs on the difference heuristic and is imperfect — §13.2. Scrubbing the chart moves the picture;
tapping a picture shows its numbers. The camera alarm tells them when it stopped.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** R4 does the work: only paired roles get an output row,
only real sensors get a column, only actionable targets appear in settings. No humidity target row and
no dehumidifier alarm preset for a heater-only tent — the per-role truth in `hardwareInfo.sockets` is
read directly instead of collapsed into three fail-open buckets. Small fix; this concept depends on it.

**4 — Full tent.** Gets the densest home screen and the full outputs lane, and — honestly — this concept
does not solve their real problem, which is that only five socket roles exist and `dehumidifier` doubles
as the cooler. What it does add is the *diagnosis*: day/night bands drawn from measured `out_light`, the
deviation fill, and care events on the same axis, so "my humidity spikes every night at lights-off"
becomes visible rather than suspected. Their firmware gap is named, not papered over.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Least served, and this concept says so. What they get: `capture_id`-keyed
`POST /care/:device_id` is a clean idempotent write endpoint, and `GET /care/:device_id?since=` returns
the ledger as JSON — the first real write API in the product, arriving as a side effect of the offline
queue. No custom measures, no output control, no MQTT. A partial down payment, not a solution.

**6 — Clubs and two-people-one-tent.** The concept is built for them. Hands in five minutes with no
accounts, the presence signal, the claim, the block, the cumulative per-plant answer, conditions on the
door, and a ledger that reconstructs the season. What they must accept is that attribution here is a
courtesy: anyone with the shared login can pick any hand. That is a deliberate trade for shipping in
October instead of shipping an auth rewrite (§13.1).

**7 — Old devices.** §10. Same shell, three slots, existing settings untouched, one genuine gain
(conditions and attributed maintenance notes in a shared cellar) and one small gain (freshness).

## 13. Honest weaknesses

**13.1 Attribution is a courtesy, not evidence.** A hand is a name in a list behind one shared password.
Anyone can pick "Anna". For a household this is exactly right and cost two days. For a club that wants a
§ 17 Abs. 2 participation record that survives an inspection, it is theatre, and the UI must never imply
otherwise. The concept's own defence — that a real membership model is an auth rewrite this release
cannot afford — is true and is also an admission that the club story is half-built.

**13.2 The camera cull degrades exactly for the user who wants the camera most.** User type 2 is
"monitoring plus camera", explicitly a solo, low-effort user. They will never open a presence lease, so
the elegant human-in-frame cull falls back to a luma-difference heuristic that will occasionally drop a
genuine change (a bent branch, a sudden defoliation) and occasionally keep a hand. The concept's best
camera idea is strongest for the user who needs it least.

**13.3 The soft block will train override reflex.** A coco grower waters daily; an 8 h window fires on
every second capture. Within a week `Trotzdem eintragen` is muscle memory and the block has become a
speed bump that protects nothing. `overrode.atTapMs` measures this, and the honest v1 mitigation is
weak: the window defaults are editable per device and the block does not fire when the *same* hand
repeats within the window (only cross-hand repeats block). Learning cadence per plant is the real fix
and it is deferred, which means v1 ships the version most likely to annoy.

**13.4 Both block scopes are wrong in opposite directions.** With zero plants the block compares
"anything done to this tent" and false-positives whenever one person waters half of it; with plants it
depends on the grower picking the right chips at 2am. Neither direction is designed away.

**13.5 Two people offline cannot be prevented, only told.** The whole prevention story assumes one of
the two phones has connectivity within the window. In a cellar with no signal, C39 delivers a good
post-mortem and no prevention. The design is honest about it in the copy, which does not make the
double feed cost less.

**13.6 The claim step costs taps in a flow whose obvious competitor is a one-tap chat log.** C05 Thumb
Journal captures in one tap. C39 captures in three-to-five and inserts a confirm surface. That is a
deliberate trade — safety over speed — and every speed-focused critic will score it down, correctly.
The mitigation (kind and plants preselected from the FÄLLIG row; the confirm sheet is skipped entirely
when nothing blocks) narrows the gap but does not close it.

**13.7 Evidence frames are a privacy hazard the product created.** Storing a picture of a person inside
a cannabis tent, in a cloud, keyed to a timestamp and a name, is a worse artefact than anything else in
this document. Default-off and pseudonymous hands are the right posture; a user who turns it on because
it is useful has materially increased their exposure and one sentence of helper text is thin protection.

**13.8 Two sources of truth for every diary row.** The `DeviceLog` mirror buys day-one compatibility
with `LogEntryViewerModule`, the entries report, the grow report and existing share links. It also means
every CareEvent exists twice, and a revoke that fails halfway leaves a ghost entry. It needs a
reconciliation job keyed on `data.capture_id` from the first week, and it is technical debt the moment
it ships.

**13.9 Storage and CPU, and two under-served types.** Cycle timelapses in GridFS at 8–15 MB each,
rebuilt nightly per device, on top of three rolling timelapses that already rebuild hourly, plus
per-frame `sharp` normalisation over thousands of frames — fine at 150 units, not linear-friendly, and
there is no retention policy in the product at all. Separately, type 5 gets almost nothing (a
read/write care endpoint that arrived by accident) and type 4's actual blocker — five socket roles,
`dehumidifier` doubling as the cooler — is firmware and untouched.

**13.10 The load-bearing assumption is unvalidated.** The whole concept rests on the claim that the
double feed is the most expensive event in a shared room. That came from one critic, not from users.
If clubs turn out to coordinate fine by WhatsApp and their real pain is reporting, C39 has optimised a
non-problem with considerable care.
