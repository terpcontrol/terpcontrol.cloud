# C35 — "Wächst mit"

**Family:** unify · **Centre of gravity:** the app is a growing thing. It reveals itself as the grow
accumulates data, never as a setting. German-first (de-DE), English second.

---

## 1. Thesis

**A feature exists in the interface exactly when the grow contains the data that justifies it — and its
empty footprint is visible, at full size, in its final position, from day one.**

Every other concept solves "simple vs. complex" by choosing what to hide. This one hides nothing and
shows nothing prematurely, by exploiting a fact the product already has: a grow is a *sequence*. On day
one there is a device, a temperature and nothing else — no plant, no photo, no feed, no second person,
no harvest, not because the user is a beginner but because those things do not exist yet. So the app
shows what is, plus **one empty slot** for the next thing that could be, drawn as a real panel with its
real name in its final position, with a single button that creates it. Log a plant and the plant panel
is there tomorrow morning where the empty one stood. Pair a camera and the Film appears without anyone
asking for it.

The screen only ever grows: nothing that appears disappears, nothing re-orders, nothing sits behind a
switch. Complexity is *earned by the grow*, not *chosen by the user* — which is why it is not a mode:
growth is a property of the device and its history, so two people sharing one tent open the identical
app on the identical day. The hard part is discoverability, and §4 answers it head-on rather than
hoping tooltips will do the work.

## 2. Information architecture

Five routes. Two of them are new. The existing route shapes are kept so share links and chart presets
survive (`ShareLink.query`, `ChartPreset.query`).

| Route | New? | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/list` | existing | Device list; one device → straight to its home. Unchanged behaviour. |
| `/device/:id` | **new** | **Zelt** — the home screen. The grown surface. Today this is `/list`'s embedded overview; it gets its own route so a device is linkable and the back button works. |
| `/device/:id/verlauf` | existing path `/charts` | **Verlauf** — the chart. Same query-param vocabulary, extended additively. `/charts` stays as an alias forever. |
| `/device/:id/diary` | existing | **Buch** — events, plants, reports. `report=entries\|plants\|plant\|growreport\|co2report\|konto`. |
| `/device/:id/film` | **new** | **Film** — only reachable when a camera is paired. Timelapse, contact strip, cuts. |
| `/device/:id/settings` | existing | **Einstellungen**. Targets, sockets, alarms, plan. Unchanged shell. |
| `/device/:id/waechst` | **new** | **"Was dein Grow noch kann"** — the inventory of all rungs. The discoverability door. |

**Navigation model.** The existing `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` stays for account-level things
(Geräte, Geteilte Links, Konto). Device-level navigation becomes a **five-item `ion-tab-bar` at the
bottom of any `/device/:id/*` route**: `Zelt · Verlauf · Buch · Film · Mehr`. There is no tab bar today
and adding one is the single largest navigational improvement available for the money.

**The Film tab is the concept made visible.** Before a camera is paired there are four tabs; after, five.
Nobody enabled anything. This is also why the bar must not be a fixed five-slot control with a greyed
member: a greyed tab is a nag, an absent tab that appears the day the hardware does is a reward.

Big screen (≥992 px): the tabs become a left rail inside the split-pane and `Zelt` renders in two
columns — panels left, film strip + chart right. A media query, not a second design.

The order of panels is **fixed and identical for every account**: Urteil · Werte · Verlauf · Film ·
Pflanzen · Heute · Ereignisse · [slot] · Tür. Panels appear at their fixed position when earned. **They
never re-order and never leave.** This is the deliberate refusal of "adaptive ordering by behaviour":
for the 2 a.m. one-handed user, the value of knowing where the thing is beats any relevance gain.

### 3.3 Stale, offline, absent — the three states that are not "a number"

Freshness is computed per value from the sample timestamp, never from page-load time.

- **Frisch** (< 90 s): value in full colour, footer `vor 12 s`.
- **Veraltet** (90 s – 10 min): value drops to `--tc-text-warning`, footer `vor 4 min — veraltet`, and
  the tile gains a 2 px left border. The verdict line becomes „Es passt. — Werte sind 4 min alt".
- **Offline** (> 10 min, the existing `ONLINE_TIMEOUT`): every tile's value renders as `—` with the
  last known value beneath it in small type: `zuletzt 24,3 °C um 21:14`. The verdict is
  „Das Gerät meldet sich nicht. Zuletzt gesehen: gestern 21:14." The chart, film and event panels stay
  fully usable — history is not stale. **This is the single cheapest credibility fix in the dossier
  (P0 #5) and it is load-bearing here**, because a growing app must never grow a lie.
- **Absent** (the sensor does not exist — no CO₂ upgrade, no lux): **the tile is not drawn at all.**
  There is no greyed CO₂ tile. This is reveal-by-capability, and it is the same mechanism as the ladder
  with a different precondition source (`hardwareInfo` instead of collection counts).
- **Absent (no data yet)**: on a brand-new device with under two hours of samples, the Verlauf panel
  shows the axis, the target band, and the copy „Noch keine 2 Stunden Daten. Die Kurve baut sich
  auf." — not a spinner and not a blank card.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

### 4.1 The mechanism, stated precisely

**Reveal is a pure function of the device's data, evaluated server-side, identical for every viewer.**

```ts
// server/src/services/ladder.service.ts  (new, ~200 lines)
type RungId =
  | 'werte' | 'verlauf' | 'ziel' | 'ereignisse' | 'geraete' | 'alarme'
  | 'pflanzen' | 'film' | 'schnitt' | 'plan' | 'konto' | 'leute'
  | 'zettel' | 'durchgang';

interface RungState {
  id: RungId;
  eligible: boolean;   // precondition met — may be offered
  filled: boolean;     // the data exists — the panel is real
  dismissed: boolean;  // user said "not this grow" — never offered as THE slot again
  seed?: { labelKey: string; action: string };   // exactly one action, and it writes data
}

GET /device/:device_id/ladder  ->  { rungs: RungState[], nextSlot: RungId | null }
```

The fixed order, with the precondition and the seed action for each:

| # | Rung | eligible when | filled when | seed action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `werte` | always | device ever reported | — (automatic) |
| 2 | `verlauf` | always | ≥ 2 h of samples | — |
| 3 | `ziel` | `configuration.workmode !== 'off'` | same | „Ziel setzen" |
| 4 | `ereignisse` | always | ≥ 1 `DeviceLog` | — |
| 5 | `geraete` | `hardwareInfo.sockets` ≠ `''`/`none` | ≥ 1 role parsed | — |
| 6 | `alarme` | always | ≥ 1 `Device.alarms[]` | „Alarm anlegen" |
| 7 | `pflanzen` | always | ≥ 1 `Plant` | „Pflanze eintragen" |
| 8 | `film` | `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` set | ≥ 40 usable frames | — (automatic) |
| 9 | `schnitt` | rung 8 filled | ≥ 1 `Cut` | „Film schneiden" |
| 10 | `plan` | ≥ 1 `Plant` | ≥ 1 `FeedRegime` | „Erde & Dünger wählen" |
| 11 | `konto` | ≥ 3 water/feed `GrowEvent` | same | — (automatic) |
| 12 | `leute` | always | ≥ 2 `Actor` | „Wer gießt hier noch?" |
| 13 | `zettel` | always | ≥ 1 `Condition` | „Offenen Punkt notieren" |
| 14 | `durchgang` | ≥ 1 harvest event, or plant age ≥ 60 d | ≥ 1 closed run | „Durchgang abschließen" |

The home screen renders every **filled** rung as its panel, in this order, and **exactly one** empty
slot: the lowest-numbered rung that is `eligible && !filled && !dismissed`. Never two. Never zero
until the ladder is complete.

### 4.2 Why this is not a mode — four commitments

1. **Growth is a property of the grow, not of the person.** The ladder is keyed by `device_id` and
   holds no per-user state at all. A club with six people at one tent sees one app in one state. A mode
   is something a *person* is in; nobody is in anything here. That is the test every "beginner/pro
   preference" fails.

2. **Reveal governs entities, never precision.** What the ladder gates is the *existence of a thing*
   (a plant, a camera, a second person). It never gates a number, a unit, a decimal place, a raw
   series, an axis, or a threshold. On day one, tapping the temperature tile opens the same full chart
   with the same interval picker down to 5 s that a three-year account gets. **The techie is never
   asked to grow the app to reach the data**, which is what makes this different from progressive
   disclosure. Progressive precision (verdict → number → series) is present on every panel from the
   first minute and does not participate in the ladder.

3. **Append-only, never re-order, never remove.** A filled rung stays filled forever, even if its data
   is deleted — archiving every plant leaves the panel reading „Keine aktiven Pflanzen ·
   [Pflanze eintragen]". Panels sit at fixed indices; the only concession to novelty is a **„Neu"
   ribbon** that clears on first scroll-past. The guarantee for the impaired user: *what you used
   yesterday is where it was, and something may have been added below it.*

4. **Nothing in the ladder is a switch.** Open `/waechst` and every row's control is a verb that
   creates data: `Pflanze eintragen`, `Wer gießt hier noch?`, `Alarm anlegen`. There is no
   `Pflanzen aktivieren` anywhere in the product. You cannot enable a feature; you can only do the
   thing the feature is about. This is what stops the inventory screen from silently becoming the
   settings screen the owner banned.

### 4.3 The discoverability problem, answered

This concept's own hardest objection is: *if a feature isn't on screen, how does anyone know it
exists?* Four answers, layered, and none of them is a tooltip.

**(a) The slot is the advertisement, and it is the feature's own footprint.** An unearned feature is
neither hidden nor summarised — it is drawn at its final size, in its final position, with its final
name, dashed instead of filled. You learn plants exist by looking at the place plants will be, which
teaches *location* and *existence* at once. A settings list teaches neither.

**(b) One permanent counted door at the bottom of home.** „Was dein Grow noch kann · 7 offen" — present
on day one, on day 400, and after everything is unlocked („alle 14 freigeschaltet"), so it never
vanishes. It is the answer to "what else does this app do", which is the question users actually ask.

**(c) `/waechst` lists all fourteen rungs — filled ones too.** The app's map, not a shop.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ‹ Zelt      Was dein Grow noch kann       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FERTIG                                   │
│ ✓ Werte             → Zelt                │
│ ✓ Verlauf           → Verlauf             │
│ ✓ Ziel und Ist      → Verlauf             │
│ ✓ Ereignisse        → Buch                │
│ ✓ Geräte (3)        → Einstellungen       │
│ ✓ Film              → Film                │
│                                          │
│ OFFEN                                    │
│ ○ Pflanzen                                │
│   Damit weiß das Zelt, was hier wächst.   │
│                    [ Pflanze eintragen ]  │
│ ○ Wer gießt hier noch                     │
│   Namen, damit man sieht, wer was macht.  │
│                    [ Namen eintragen ]    │
│ ○ Offene Punkte                           │
│                    [ Punkt notieren ]     │
│                                          │
│ NOCH NICHT MÖGLICH                       │
│ · Düngeplan — braucht mindestens eine     │
│   Pflanze                                 │
│ · Der Schnitt — braucht 3 Tage Bilder     │
│ · Gieß- und Düngekonto — braucht 3        │
│   Einträge                                │
│ · Durchgang abschließen — braucht eine    │
│   Ernte oder 60 Tage                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The third section is the important one and no other concept has it: **it names the precondition in
plain German.** "Braucht mindestens eine Pflanze" teaches the causal model of the whole app in one
line, so the user stops experiencing reveal as randomness. A techie can read this page once and fill
the entire ladder in about four minutes (plant → regime → two names → a condition → cut the film),
which is the honest answer to "I paid €289, give me everything now": *here is the list, here are the
buttons, go.*

**(d) Some rungs fill themselves and say so.** Pairing a camera fills `film` with no user action. When
it does, the server writes a `DeviceLog` with `message-film-ready` („Der Film ist da — 63 Bilder aus
den letzten 2 Tagen"), which shows in Ereignisse and, if push/e-mail notifications are configured,
reaches the phone. The feature announces its own arrival. This is also exactly how the camera alarm
arrives (§8.5) — the same channel, so users learn one channel, not two.

**Dismissal — the seam, named.** Each slot has a small, non-primary „Nicht für diesen Grow" affordance.
It removes that rung from the *home slot rotation* only; it stays in `/waechst` under OFFEN forever
with the same button. This is the closest thing in the concept to a preference and I would rather ship
it than ship a plant nag that a monitor-only user stares at for three years. It is per-device, per-rung,
reversible, and it never changes what anything else looks like — but §13 admits it as the weakest seam.

## 5. The chart — `Verlauf`

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive (owner decision). The URL query
vocabulary is extended additively; `measures`, `date`, `dateEnd`, `interval`, `timespan`, `logs`,
`share` keep their meaning so existing `ChartPreset.query` and `ShareLink.query` strings still parse.

**The chart grows too — with the same rule, and no toggles.** Lanes appear when their data exists:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  24 Std   3 Tage   Woche   Monat   Lauf      ⟳ live │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 24,6 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 · 842 ppm   Do 18:04      │ ← pinned scrub header
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ °C  30│                                             │
│       │▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒target band 24–26▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒  │
│     25│╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╮   setpoint (stepped, dashed)│
│       │ ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ╰╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌   │
│     20│      actual (solid, min/max envelope)       │
│       └──────────────────────────────────────────── │
│ %   80│  [second panel, same x, linked crosshair]   │
│ kPa   │  [third panel: VPD, band moves with stage]  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Licht  ████████████░░░░░░░░████████████░░░░░░░░     │ ← output state lane
│ Heizen ░░░█░░░█░░░░░░░░░░░░░░█░░░░█░░░░░░░░░░░      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ●    ●●³      ▲              ●        ▲             │ ← annotation rail, 2 rows
│ ▲ = du · ● = Gerät                                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [◧][◧][◧][◧]   [◧][◧][◧]      [◧][◧][◧][◧]          │ ← film strip AS an axis
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goal vs actual.** The setpoint is a **stepped dashed line** built client-side from
  `configuration.day.temperature` / `night.temperature`, stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night`
  (seconds-of-day, UTC → rendered in the browser's zone, with the zone named on the axis). Behind it, a
  `markArea` band, source chosen in this priority and **always labelled with which one it is**:
  (1) the user's own alarm thresholds → „dein Alarmbereich"; (2) setpoint ±1,5 °C / ±7 % →
  „Ziel ± Toleranz"; (3) the stage's `vpdRange` from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS` → „Richtwert Blüte".
  Between setpoint and actual, a **signed deviation fill** — never a fill to the axis, which encodes
  nothing. This needs no new API: every input already exists client-side (dossier P1 #15).
- **Small multiples, not fifteen hidden axes.** At most two units per panel and only when physically
  coupled (air + leaf temperature). One linked crosshair via `axisPointer.link` + `echarts.connect()`.
  Axis labels are never hidden on narrow screens — they shorten to three ticks and the unit moves into
  the panel title.
- **Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0` where present, falling back to the configured schedule,
  with the fallback said out loud in the legend („aus dem Plan, nicht gemessen"). A failed contactor is
  then a **missing band** — a diagnosis, not decoration.
- **Annotations** live in a rail under the x-axis, max two rows: row 1 human `GrowEvent`s (▲), row 2
  device `DeviceLog`s (●), clustered by pixel proximity in 10 px bins with a count badge, tappable (not
  hoverable — there is no hover on a phone). Lifecycle changes draw a full-height dashed line with the
  stage name. Tap → the pinned header swaps to that event's card.
- **Outputs** get their own state-timeline lane. Region length = duration, `null` = gap.
- **`sampling: 'minmax'`** for the plot, LTTB only for the navigator: a single `mean` line is a lie
  about a bang-bang controller, and Highstock's implicit `dataGrouping` disappears with the migration,
  so explicit sampling replaces a load-bearing default rather than polishing one.
- **Correctness precondition:** `from`/`to`/`interval`/`measure` are interpolated raw into Flux today
  (`data.service.ts:80-89`) and `limit(n:50000)` is piped after `yield()`. Both fixed in §12.

**Time-in-range verdict** sits above the chart as one stacked bar, split day vs night, with the longest
excursion as a **duration** and a data-coverage %, greyed below 80 % coverage. It must be computed
server-side on **raw** samples — `aggregateWindow(mean)` destroys exactly the excursions the metric
counts, and the error is invisible by eye.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant — `Buch`

### 6.1 New collections (all cloud-side; `device.configuration` cannot carry new keys)

```ts
Plant {
  plant_id: string;            // uuid
  device_id: string;           // scope
  label: string;               // "A1" — auto-suggested, editable
  strain?: string;             // free text, "Gorilla Glue"
  source?: 'seed' | 'clone' | 'auto';
  startedAt: number;           // ms
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage;  // reuses the existing six-value union
  stageSince: number;
  medium?: string;             // "biobizz-allmix"  -> RegimeCatalog key
  potLiters?: number;
  endedAt?: number; archived?: boolean;
}

GrowEvent {
  event_id: string;
  client_event_id: string;     // UNIQUE INDEX. client-generated at arm time.
  device_id: string;
  plant_ids: string[];         // [] == "the whole tent"
  type: 'water' | 'feed' | 'note' | 'photo' | 'stage' | 'training'
      | 'measure' | 'pest' | 'harvest' | 'condition-open' | 'condition-close';
  at: number;
  actor_id?: string; actor_name?: string;   // denormalised name, survives actor deletion
  source: 'user' | 'device' | 'schedule';
  data: Record<string, number|string>;      // liters, ec, ph, runoffEc, products[], grams
  note?: string;
  images: string[];            // image_id[]
  supersedes?: string;         // corrections are new rows, never edits
}

Condition { condition_id; device_id; text; plant_ids: string[];
            openedBy?: string; openedAt: number; closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number }

Actor { actor_id; device_id; name; color; createdAt; archived? }
```

`DeviceLog` is left alone. Device-generated events keep flowing into it; `GrowEvent` is the human
record. The Ereignisse view merges both streams by timestamp and renders them with the existing
`app-log-entry-item` (`LogEntryViewerModule`) for `DeviceLog` rows and a sibling item component for
`GrowEvent`. `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` becomes a **one-time backfill** into `Plant`, not a runtime
algorithm — which fixes the bug where renaming a strain forks the history.

### 6.2 Multi-plant that does not tax anyone who ignores plants

Three rules, and together they are the whole answer:

1. **Zero plants:** no plant panel, no plant chips anywhere, no plant column in any report. Every
   `GrowEvent` is written with `plant_ids: []`. The product is a tent monitor with a diary and it is
   complete on its own terms.
2. **Exactly one plant:** the plant panel appears (one row), but **no picker is ever shown**. Capture
   auto-targets that plant. The tax is zero taps.
3. **Two or more plants:** capture sheets grow a chip row, pre-selected to **all active plants**,
   because "I fed everything" is the overwhelmingly common case. Deselecting is the exception.

The plant panel is a list, not a grid of cards — four plants must fit above the fold on a phone.
Tapping a plant opens `report=plant&plant_id=…`: one scrolling page with its own events, its own
photos, its own cumulative account, and a day counter.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

### 7.1 The Erfassen bar

Permanently at the bottom of `Zelt`, above the tab bar, from day one. Four fixed items:
`⊕ Gießen · ⊕ Füttern · ⊕ Notiz · ⊕ ⋯`. **Fixed order, never adaptive** — the muscle memory of the
2 a.m. user is worth more than the 200 ms saved by re-ranking.

Tapping one **arms** a sheet that is already filled in. Committing is a second tap. That two-step is
what makes the double-feed guard possible without ever costing a tap in the safe case.

### 7.2 Tap counts

### 7.3 The double-feed guard

At **arm** time (not commit time), the client asks
`GET /device/:id/grow-events/recent?types=water,feed&plants=…&since=6h`. If any target plant has a
matching event inside the window from a *different* `actor_id`, the primary button changes:

```
│ ⚠︎ Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert. │
│           [ Trotzdem ]   [ Abbrechen ]    │
```

en: „Anna fed A1–A3 1 h 30 ago. Anyway?" The window is 6 h for `feed`, 4 h for `water`. Same actor →
no block (you know what you did). This is a **soft block with one deliberate extra tap**, never a
refusal — and it addresses the club critic's #1 gap: ten concepts optimised capture speed, none
optimised capture safety between people.

### 7.4 Following a schedule (the „denkt für mich" path)

A `FeedRegime` is chosen once, as **medium + feed line together**, because that is how growers speak:

```
Erde und Dünger
( ) Biobizz Light-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom
(•) Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom
( ) Floragard Light + Green House Feeding
( ) BioTabs (Tabletten, kaum flüssig düngen)
( ) Eigener Plan
( ) Keiner — ich dünge nach Gefühl
    ⓘ zuletzt geprüft 14.06.2026
```

All-Mix is heavily pre-fertilised, so the regime's first three to four weeks contain **no feed step at
all**, and the „Heute" card says so out loud: „Noch nicht düngen — All-Mix trägt bis ~Woche 4."
A regime that cannot express *don't feed yet* is not modelling the domain.

- Steps advance by **feed events**, not wall-clock weeks („Schritt 4 von 11"), with a soft calendar
  mapping — growers feed Tue/Fri and slip. The bloom flip is auto-detected from `out_light` history to
  place the plant on the right bloom week; no bottle-brand calculator can do that because none of them
  can see the device.
- Every prefilled number is editable and the deviation is stored, not corrected: `data.products` holds
  what was *actually* given.
- EC is stored canonically in mS/cm. **No bare "ppm" is ever rendered** (500 vs 640 vs 700 scales
  differ by 40 % for the same liquid). Where a regime's source publishes `EC+` (CANNA-style), the entry
  carries `ec_basis: 'delta_over_source'` and the UI adds the user's source-water EC before comparing.
- The catalogue lives in a MongoDB collection `RegimeCatalog`, never in the webapp bundle, each entry
  carrying `source_url` + `retrieved_at` + `kind`. Brand names are plain text labels only — no logos,
  no brand colours, no brand name inside a Terp Control feature name. The „zuletzt geprüft" date is
  both the legal posture and a genuine trust feature.

### 7.5 Capture in a cellar

There is no service worker and v1 does not add one. What ships instead is honest and cheap: the commit
handler writes the payload (with its `client_event_id`) to a `localStorage` queue **before** the POST,
and flushes on app foreground and on `online`. The unique index on `client_event_id` makes retries
idempotent by construction, so the retry queue **cannot** double-log — which in a shared record is
indistinguishable from a double feed. The limitation, stated in the UI as „Wird gesendet, sobald du
wieder Empfang hast": the app must already be loaded. If you walk into the cellar with the tab open,
capture works. If you open the app cold with no signal, it does not. A real service worker is v1.1.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

This is the headline, not a tile, and the ladder gives it two rungs so it delivers twice.

### 8.1 What already exists (verified in the repo)

`image.service.ts` polls every 30 s, stores JPEGs as Mongo `Buffer`s, and **already builds three
rolling timelapses hourly** with `ffmpeg` (`compressRtspStreamRange`: one frame per 2 min for `1d`, ×7
for `1w`, ×30 for `1m`), stored as `format:'mp4'` with `duration` and `timestampEnd`. `sharp` ^0.34.5
is already a dependency and already composites an SVG offline caption onto stills. **The pipeline
exists. What is missing is content quality and an exit.**

### 8.2 Frame culling and normalisation

Two new optional fields on `Image`, computed on ingest (no migration; old rows simply lack them):

```ts
Image.luma?: number;    // 0..255, sharp().stats() mean of the grey channel
Image.dhash?: string;   // 16 hex chars — 8x8 difference hash, sharp resize 9x8 greyscale
```

`sharp` costs ~8 ms per frame on the 9×8 path; at one frame per 30 s per device that is free.

Culling rules, applied when a **cut** is built (never at storage time — nothing is deleted):

1. **Lights-off** — `luma < 12`. Dropped from a day cut. A dedicated night cut can include them.
2. **Blown / door open / head torch** — `|luma − median(luma at the same clock-minute over 7 days)| > 60`.
3. **Near-duplicate** — `hamming(dhash, lastKept) < 4`. Deduplicates a still tent overnight.
4. **A human in shot** — `hamming(dhash, prev) > 22` **and** the *next* frame returns within 8 of the
   frame before the change. That signature is precisely "something was in the tent for 30 s and then
   was not". Dropped.
5. **The camera was nudged** — `hamming > 22` and the following five frames stay near the *new* frame.
   This is not dropped: the cut gets a **hard cut marker**, and the server writes
   `message-camera-moved` („Die Kamera steht seit 12.07. anders — der Film hat dort einen Schnitt").
   Pretending a re-frame did not happen is worse than showing it.

Normalisation, applied **at render time only, never to the stored original**:

- **Exposure**: `sharp().linear(a, b)` per frame to bring mean luma to 128 (day) — this is what kills
  the flicker across 12 weeks of slightly different lamp output.
- **White balance**: grey-world per-channel gain from `stats().channels[i].mean`, **clamped to ±20 %**.
  The clamp matters: a full-magenta LED tent genuinely *is* magenta, and unclamped grey-world turns it
  swamp green. A per-cut `[ Farben lassen ]` control on the cut sheet disables it.
- **Stabilisation**: not in v1. `vidstab` is not in the ffmpeg build and a nudge is handled as a cut,
  which is more honest anyway. Said plainly in §13.

### 8.3 The Cut — a timelapse someone can actually hold

New collection:

```ts
Cut { cut_id; device_id; from; to; plant_id?;
      frameCount; fps; width; height;
      spec: { nightFrames: boolean; captions: boolean; whiteBalance: boolean };
      mp4_image_id;      // -> Image { format:'mp4', duration: undefined }
      bytes; builtAt; label }
```

Defaults, chosen so the result is holdable and shareable: **1280×720, H.264 (`libx264`), `yuv420p`,
`-crf 23`, `-movflags +faststart`, 25 fps, target length 20 s ⇒ ~500 frames.** Over a 63-day flower run
that is ~8 frames per lit day, i.e. one frame every ~3 h of light. Result: **4–8 MB.**

> **This is a change to shipping code and it is required.** Today the encoder is `libx265 -crf 30` with
> no `-pix_fmt` and no `faststart` (`image.service.ts:564-606`). HEVC in an MP4 will not play in
> Firefox, is inconsistent on Android Chrome, and is a poor thing to hand someone as a file. The
> rolling `1d/1w/1m` previews move to x264 as well; storage goes up perhaps 25 % at the same visual
> quality, and that is the right trade for a headline feature.

**Captions burned in — this is the image correlation, and it survives leaving the product.** When
`spec.captions` is on (default), each frame gets an SVG lower-third composited by `sharp` — the exact
mechanism already used for the offline overlay, reusing `escapeXml`:

```
Tag 34 · Blüte · 24,6 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21 kPa
```

The values come from one Influx query for the whole cut window at the cut's frame interval, joined to
frames by nearest timestamp. A caption is omitted (not faked) where no sample exists within ±10 min.

**How the video leaves the product** — designed, not assumed:

1. `POST /device/:device_id/cuts` with `{from, to, plant_id?, spec}` → 202 + `cut_id`. Built in the
   existing `pLimit(10)` ffmpeg queue. A 500-frame cut with sharp per-frame work takes ~40–70 s.
2. The Film tab shows the cut with a real progress state, then:
   `[ ▶ Abspielen ]  [ ⤓ Speichern ]  [ ⤴ Teilen ]`
3. `⤓ Speichern` →
   `GET /image/:device_id?format=mp4&cut_id=<id>&download=1&token=<imageToken>` → the server adds
   `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terpcontrol_zelt-1_gorilla-glue_tag01-tag63_720p.mp4"`
   and `Content-Type: video/mp4`. That is a **one-header change** to `ImageController.sendImage`; the
   URL shape and the 30-day URL-embeddable `imageToken` already exist
   (`devices.service.ts:251`). Android Chrome writes it to Downloads. Desktop writes it to Downloads.
4. `⤴ Teilen` is the primary control on a phone: fetch the blob, wrap in a `File`, call
   `navigator.share({ files:[file], title })`. On iOS and Android this opens the system share sheet,
   which contains **„Video sichern" / Save to Photos** — i.e. it lands in the camera roll, and it can
   go straight to Telegram, where this product's community already lives. Feature-detect
   `navigator.canShare({files})`; fall back to (3). On iOS Safari the plain download opens a new tab
   with a share sheet, which is why (4) is primary and (3) is the fallback, not the other way round.
5. No new dependency for any of this.

**A share link is not a share.** A `ShareLink` to the Film page is offered *in addition*, never instead
— „Ein Link ist kein Video. Speicher dir den Film, dann kannst du ihn überall hinschicken."

### 8.4 The contact strip and correlation both ways

- **Chart → picture.** The film strip under `Verlauf` is positioned on the *same x-scale* as the chart,
  so uneven capture reads honestly as gaps. Scrubbing the chart moves the strip's highlight and swaps
  the large frame above it. Delivery is one sprite + a JSON index per window (50–100 tiles at
  256×160), not N image requests. Culled frames appear greyed with a strip-local
  „Alle Bilder zeigen" toggle — because the frame with a hand pointing at a sick leaf is sometimes the
  one you want.
- **Picture → chart.** Tapping any frame pins the header to that timestamp: `24,6 °C · 58 % ·
  VPD 1,21 · Licht 100 % · Tag 34 · Blüte`, plus every `GrowEvent` within ±2 h („Anna hat gefüttert").
  A `[ Als Foto in den Verlauf ]` button copies that frame into the diary as a `GrowEvent` of type
  `photo` at that timestamp — which is how a webcam frame becomes part of the human record.
- `Image.timestampEnd` already marks mp4s as spanning a range; those render on the strip as a bracket,
  not a point.

### 8.5 The camera gets an alarm

A camera that stopped three days into flower must say so, not be quietly absent. A new alarm preset,
`sensorType: 'camera_age'`, evaluated by the existing `alarm.service` tick: triggered when the newest
`format:'jpeg'` row for the device is older than 3 h while the device is online. Uses the existing
`actionType: 'email' | 'webhook' | 'info'` machinery, cooldown and resolve semantics — no new
notification system. Default: created automatically the moment `film` fills, `actionType:'info'`,
which puts it in the event stream, and users who already configured e-mail alerts get it there too.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution without authorisation, in v1.** A device-scoped `Actor` list — names the owner types
once, no accounts, no `auth.middleware.ts` surgery, no membership collection, no owner-query rewrite.
Roughly two days of work, and it delivers the club critic's #2 point in this release.

- The `leute` rung's seed action is „Wer gießt hier noch?" → a text field → done. Once ≥ 2 actors
  exist, every capture sheet grows a `Wer?` chip row, pre-selected from `localStorage['actor:'+device_id]`
  (this browser's last choice). `actor_id` **and** the denormalised `actor_name` are stamped at
  capture, so deleting an actor never orphans history.
- The Ereignisse stream then reads „Anna hat A1–A3 gefüttert · 4 ml/l Bio-Bloom" instead of an
  anonymous row.
- **Honest label on the door**: „Namen, keine Zugänge. Wer den Link hat, kann eintragen." Attribution
  is a label and this concept says so in the UI rather than implying a gate. Real memberships are a
  later upgrade: `Actor.user_id?` is left in the schema unused, so an actor becomes an account without
  a migration.

**The double-feed problem** — §7.3. It is the most expensive thing that happens in a shared room and it
is solved at the moment of capture, in the arm→commit gap, at a cost of zero taps when nothing is wrong.

**The cumulative question.** The `konto` rung fills itself after three feed/water events and adds a
report `report=konto`:

```
Gieß- und Düngekonto — Durchgang seit 14.06.
                       Wasser   Bio-Grow  Bio-Bloom  Einträge
A1 Gorilla Glue        41,0 l    148 ml    96 ml       23
A2 Gorilla Glue        41,0 l    148 ml    96 ml       23
A3 Zkittlez            38,0 l    140 ml    96 ml       21
A4 Zkittlez            26,0 l     96 ml    48 ml       14  ⚠︎ nicht gießen (Chris, 12.08.)
                       ─────────────────────────────────
von Anna 61 %  ·  von Chris 39 %              [ CSV ]
```

One Mongo aggregation over `GrowEvent`. This answers "how much Bio-Bloom has A3 had this cycle and from
whom" — which every data model in round one could compute and none surfaced.

**Standing conditions.** `Condition` is a first-class open fact with an owner — not an event, not a
task. „CO₂-Flasche fast leer", „Steckdose 3 wackelt", „A4 nicht gießen, übergossen". Open conditions
render inline on the plant they name (see §3.1) and as a pinned block at the top of Ereignisse. Closing
one writes a `GrowEvent` of type `condition-close`, so the record shows who closed it and when. This is
what actually gets taped to a tent door.

**Privacy posture.** Default is privacy-maximal, and it is a default, not a mode:

- Harvest weight is a field that exists and is **never prompted for**. No card asks for it; typing it
  is an action you take. Plant counts are never aggregated across devices and never leave the device
  scope.
- Actor names are stored per device, are free text, and default to **initials** in every export and in
  any public link. „Anna" becomes „A." unless the exporter ticks otherwise.
- No location, ever. No analytics SDK. No community feed with identities.
- Corrections are new rows with `supersedes`, not edits — append-only history the **account owner can
  still delete**. Immutability protects a regulated club against its own staff and victimises an
  unregulated grower, so it stays opt-in and club-scoped, never global.
- Never anyone's § 26 KCanG system of record: no per-member dispensing, no THC content, no membership
  numbers, no compliance badge.

## 10. Old devices — type 7 (fan / light / plug)

The ladder is per-device and its preconditions read that device's real capabilities, so an old device
gets a *small honest app* instead of a controller dashboard with dead controls. Concretely:

**A `plug` (Terp Control SMART SOCKET, its own temp/humidity sensor, `workmode` incl. `watering`):**

```
│  Es passt.                               │
│  [ 22,1 °C ]  [ 54 % ]                   │  ← its two real sensors. No VPD tile
│                                          │     unless both are present (they are).
│  ── Verlauf ────────────── 24 Std ▸ ──── │  ← same chart component. The setpoint
│  ── Schaltzeiten ─────────────────────── │     line is drawn from the plug's own
│  Heizen  ░░█░░░█░░░░░░░█░░░░░░░░░       │     on/off points, not from day/night.
│  ── Ereignisse ──────────────── alle ▸ ─ │
│  ╭─ Noch leer ─ Offene Punkte ─────────╮ │
│  Was dein Grow noch kann        3 offen ▸│
│ ⊕ Notiz   ⊕ Foto                         │  ← no Gießen/Füttern: no plants,
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘     the rung is eligible but unfilled
```

Their ladder has **five eligible rungs**: `werte`, `verlauf`, `ereignisse`, `alarme`, `zettel`, plus

**A `fan` (Terp Control AIR):** identical, plus an `rpm` tile and an rpm panel in Verlauf, and its

**What does not happen to them:** no fridge branding, no humidity target they cannot act on, no

`light-settings`, `plug-settings`) are untouched in v1 — the `ngSwitch` in `settings.page.html` keeps

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app maybe weekly. Sees „Es passt. seit 3 Std im Zielbereich",
three tiles with ages on them, a chart with a target band and a time-in-range bar. Their ladder shows
one slot at a time, and after they dismiss `pflanzen` and `leute` it shows the door at „2 offen" and
stops asking. What they gained over today: freshness honesty and a verdict. What they never see:
plants, feeds, actors, cuts. The app stayed the size of their grow.

**2 — Same, with a camera.** The Film tab appears the day they pair the cam, with a diary entry saying
so. Within two days there is a watchable rolling timelapse; on day three the `schnitt` slot offers
„Film schneiden" and produces a 6 MB, 20 s, 720p H.264 file with the day, stage, temperature and VPD
burned into the bottom of every frame. They save it to their camera roll and post it to Telegram
without ever making an account for anything. The camera alarm tells them when it stops. **This is the
user the concept serves best.**

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** Their `geraete` rung fills from
`hardwareInfo.sockets`, and the home screen shows lanes and target rows **only for the roles they
actually paired**. A heater-only tent is never shown a humidity target, a humidity deviation warning,
or a „Entfeuchter läuft dauernd" alarm preset — a bug today, since `deviceControlCapability()` returns
`'full'` for a heater-only tent and fails open on old firmware. Reveal-by-capability is the same
mechanism as reveal-by-data with a different precondition source.

**4 — Full tent.** Most rungs filled, the densest home screen: six panels, an output lane per role,
device and human events interleaved in Ereignisse. The honest limit stays — five socket roles exist,
`dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler, there is no humidifier path. This concept invents no roles; it
stops showing controls for absent ones, and the output lane makes "cooler and dehumidifier are the same
socket" visible instead of implied.

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** Their complaint about a growing app is "stop deciding for me". The answer is
`/waechst`: fourteen named rungs, each precondition stated, each button one tap, whole thing filled in
four minutes. Then they get what they actually came for — a chart with real axes, min/max envelopes,
`interval` down to 5 s, and a documented `GET /device/:id/grow-events` + `GET /device/:id/ladder`.
`GrowEvent.data` is an open bag, so their EC probe's readings land there rather than nowhere. What they
still do not get in v1: a write API for outputs. Say so on the page.

**6 — Grow club.** Two days of work buys them names, attribution on every row, a double-feed guard at
the moment of capture, standing conditions, and a per-plant cumulative account with a CSV button.
Everything is device-scoped, so the club's controller is the unit and nobody needs an account. What
they do not get: real authorisation (anyone with the link can write), and this is said in the UI rather
than implied. What they must not get, by design: per-member dispensing records.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. The device does not grow into a controller and is not asked to. Their
door says „3 offen" and the three are real.

**Capability lenses.** *Stoner*: fixed positions that never re-order, a verdict in plain German before
any number, one-tap „Erledigt", a two-step commit that catches the mistake they are most likely to
make at 2 a.m. *Normal*: the app is never bigger than their grow and it explains its own growth in one
sentence per rung. *Techie*: nothing is behind a preference, everything is reachable in one screen, the
raw series is one tap from every tile on day one, and the ladder is a documented JSON endpoint.

- **Run-over-run comparison** (`durchgang` rung's filled state). The rung exists and its precondition is
  60 days, so nobody reaches it before December anyway. Ship the seed action, build the report in v1.1.
- **Real multi-user authorisation.** Membership collection + `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` rewrite +
  ~10 owner-scoped queries. Not a flag. `Actor` delivers 80 % of the club value for 3 % of the cost.
- **`vidstab` stabilisation** and per-plant crops in cuts.

**Two things that must land even though they are not in the concept:** the InfluxDB retention/downsample
task (raw 5 s samples are kept forever today, which is a cost curve *and* an evidentiary liability), and
seeding the `/demo` device fully grown — see §13.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The empty slot is a nag with better manners.** It is a call-to-action in a fixed position that a
   monitor-only user did not ask for and cannot make disappear without an explicit dismissal — and that
   dismissal is the closest thing in the product to the preference the owner banned. My defence is that
   it is per-rung, per-device, reversible, and changes nothing else on any screen; a critic can
   reasonably call it a mode with fourteen tiny switches. It is the seam I would attack.

2. **The demo is empty, and the demo sells the product.** A €289 controller with a public no-login demo
   at `/demo` cannot show an app that has almost nothing on it. `demoDevice: true` must be seeded with
   plants, a camera, 63 days of frames, feed events and two actors, so the demo shows a *grown* app.
   That is a real content-authoring job (a day of `simulate-device.sh` scripting) and if it is skipped,
   this concept's marketing surface is its worst screen. Same problem, smaller, for the first ten
   minutes after unboxing.

3. **The screen changes shape over weeks, and that is genuinely bad for the impaired user.** Append-only
   and fixed positions mitigate it; they do not eliminate it. A user who last opened the app in week 2
   and returns in week 9 finds four new panels. The „Neu" ribbon is a thin answer.

4. **Preconditions cost queries on the hottest path.** The ladder needs counts across `Plant`,
   `GrowEvent`, `Condition`, `Actor`, `Cut`, `Image`, plus an Influx coverage check — on every home
   load. It must be cached per device (60 s) and invalidated on write, and if that caching is wrong the
   app *appears to lose features*, which is a far scarier bug than a stale number. There is no
   migration tooling in this codebase to fix a bad ladder state after the fact.

5. **Frame culling throws away frames some users wanted.** The heuristics in §8.2 are thresholds tuned
   against a simulated tent, not against 150 real ones. `luma`/`dhash` will mis-fire on a tent with a
   window, on an oscillating fan moving leaves, and on a plant that genuinely grows into frame. Nothing
   is deleted and the strip can show culled frames, but the *cut* — the artefact people actually share
   — will silently omit things, and there is no per-frame "keep this" control in v1.

6. **Grey-world white balance is wrong for full-spectrum-magenta tents.** The ±20 % clamp keeps it from
   being catastrophic and „Farben lassen" is one tap, but the default will look wrong to some people
   and they will judge the headline feature by it.

7. **Storage.** JPEGs live in MongoDB as `Buffer`s (16 MB BSON limit, `express-fileupload` mounted with
   no size limit), moving the timelapses to x264 raises size ~25 %, and each Cut is another 4–8 MB row
   nobody prunes. The image thinning tiers cover `format:'jpeg'` only; `user/jpeg` diary photos and
   `Cut` mp4s accumulate forever. v1 must at minimum add a per-device Cut cap (keep the newest 10) and
   should get images out of Mongo eventually. Not costed above.

8. **Attribution without authorisation is a half-truth that some club will misread.** Anyone with a
   share link can write; „Anna" is a label anyone can pick. The UI says so, but a club treating this as
   a participation record for § 17 Abs. 2 purposes would be relying on something that does not
   authenticate. This concept deliberately ships the label anyway, because the alternative is nothing
   for another release.

9. **`GrowEvent` multiplies rows and the API has no rate limiting outside `/auth`.** Pagination is in
   the v1 budget; rate limiting is not.

10. **The concept's core claim is falsifiable in exactly one way, and it should be tested before
    committing.** If users experience the ladder as *the app withholding things* rather than *the app
    growing with them*, the whole idea inverts into the thing the owner banned. That is a five-person
    hallway test with a fresh account and a week-9 account, and it should happen in week one of the
    build, not after.
