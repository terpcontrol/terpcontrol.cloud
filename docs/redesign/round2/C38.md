# C38 — "Das Konto"

**Family:** feeding · **Centre of gravity:** the dose ledger is the spine of the product.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Jede Pflanze führt ein Konto. Jede Gabe ist eine Buchung. Der Saldo — was diese Pflanze in
diesem Zyklus bekommen hat, je Produkt, kumulativ, und von wem — steht auf der Startseite.**

A grow controller records what the *room* had. Nobody records what the *plant* had. The club's
literal question — "Wer hat was gemacht? Wie haben die Pflanzen reagiert?" — is a bookkeeping
question, and bookkeeping has been a solved problem since 1494. Das Konto uses the solved part:
accounts, postings, balances, and one trick nobody in this market has applied — **the mixing can
is itself an account**. You mix 10 L at 2 ml/L Bio-Grow (20 ml in the can) and pour it over four
plants; the can is debited, four plant accounts are credited, and the per-plant cumulative dose
is arithmetic instead of memory. Nobody weighs anything. Nobody types four times.

The consequence is the thing round one could not answer: overfeeding stops being a feeling. It is
a running total that is already on screen when you open the app, next to the plan it is being
compared against. `A3 · 14 Gaben · 331 ml · 34 % über Plan` is a sentence no competitor's data
model can produce, and Terp Control's can — because the device already knows the photoperiod, the
stage and the day count, so the ledger knows *which* step of the regime it is measuring against.

including a plug: a fan owner also waters plants and there is no cost to letting him. Nothing is
hidden from a person; things are absent when they have no content.

the regime gives a range the row reads `am oberen Rand` / `im Plan` / `unter Plan` instead of a
percentage. See weakness #3 — this rule exists because of it.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM

> **Genauigkeit ist eine Eigenschaft des Belegs, nicht des Benutzers.**

Three parts, all of them properties of the ledger rather than of a setting.

### 4.1 One capture sheet. Nothing hidden. Everything prefilled.

The Buchung sheet has **no accordion, no "Erweitert", no "mehr anzeigen"**. Every field a techie
could want is on screen, in a fixed order that never changes, from the first Buchung to the
thousandth. The fields are *prefilled from the regime* and every prefilled field is already a
valid value. An untouched field still books a number.

The stoner's speed does not come from fewer fields. It comes from:
- the primary button being a sticky 64 px bar at the **bottom** (one-handed thumb reach, dark tent),
- the two decisions that matter (`Kanne: 10 L`, `Konten: alle`) being the first two rows,
- everything below them already being right.

The techie's precision does not come from a switch. It comes from typing into rows that were
always on screen.

A mode changes *what exists*. This changes only *what you typed*.

### 4.2 Provenance marks inside the number.

Every `Posting` carries `source: 'gemessen' | 'plan' | 'geschaetzt'`. A balance made only of
measured postings renders `214 ml`. A balance containing any plan-prefill renders **`≈214 ml`**.
Tapping the `≈` opens a list: *"9 von 11 Gaben sind Planwerte."*

Nobody is lectured, nobody is blocked, and the number never pretends. This is the mechanism that
lets one balance serve a person who taps Buchen and a person who measures EC to two decimals:
**they contribute to the same account, and the account is honest about which is which.**

### 4.3 Extra effort buys accuracy, never access.

Both users write the same record shape — same `txn_id`, same `Posting[]`, same report, same export,
same chart lane. The stoner's Buchung is 1 can / 4 accounts / equal split / `source:'plan'`; the
techie's is 1 can / 4 accounts / per-account litres typed / EC measured / `source:'gemessen'`.
The techie's work makes his own numbers tighter. It does not unlock a screen, a series, a column or
a button. **No feature in this concept is reachable only by supplying more data** — a checkable
test: every route in §2 is reachable from an account built entirely of 2-tap Buchungen.

### 4.4 Reveal by data, applied to the one thing that would tax people

If you never name a plant there is exactly **one** account, labelled with the device name, created
implicitly at the first Buchung. The sheet has no chip row (nothing to choose between). Block 5 on
Heute is one line. The word "Pflanze" does not appear anywhere. Plant machinery appears only when
a second account exists, and a second account only exists because you made one.

### 4.5 What I deliberately did *not* do

No adaptive reordering. Block order on Heute is fixed forever — an impaired user needs the Buchen
button in the same pixel at 2 a.m. as at noon. Block 5's rows sort by `label`: alphabetical,
stable, never by "attention".

`chart.js` / `ng2-charts` / `chartjs-adapter-luxon` deleted. Stacked small multiples, one shared

**Goal vs actual, climate.** Setpoint is a `series` of `step: 'end'` with `lineStyle.type:'dashed'`,
built client-side from the parsed `device.configuration`: it steps between `day.temperature` and
`night.temperature` at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night` (seconds-of-day, UTC → rendered in an
explicit device timezone). Behind it a `markArea` target band, source chosen in this priority and
**labelled with its source**: alarm thresholds (`Dein Alarm`) > setpoint ± tolerance (`Soll ± 1,5 °C`)
> the stage's `vpdRange` from `GROW_STAGE_PRESETS` (`Empfehlung Blüte`). Between actual and
setpoint, a signed deviation fill. Never fill to the axis.

**Goal vs actual, feeding.** The Kontoband panel. `y` = cumulative ml of the selected product for
this cycle, one monotone step line per account, plus one dashed line for the regime's cumulative
plan positioned by feed-event index mapped onto the account's actual feed dates. The gap between
the lines *is* the overfeed, drawn. Product picker is a chip row above the panel, defaulting to the
product with the largest cumulative volume.

**Day/night shading**: from measured `out_light > 0` when the series exists, falling back to the
`daynight` schedule with `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` ramp gradients at the edges. A failed contactor
shows as a missing band — a diagnostic, not decoration.

**Annotation rail**: under the x-axis, not inside the plot. Two rows max (row 1 human postings,
row 2 device logs), pixel-proximity clustering into 10 px bins with count badges, category chips
reusing `collectLogCategories` / `matchesLogCategory`. Stage changes get a full-height dashed line.
Tap a tick → the pinned scrub head is replaced by the posting's row, with its Vorher/Nachher
thumbnails. **No hover anywhere.**

**Mobile interaction**: one finger scrolls the page; two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: {type:'inside'}`);
tap or long-press sets the crosshair. Axis labels are never hidden — below 320 px they drop to
3 ticks and the unit moves into the panel title.

**Sampling**: `sampling: 'minmax'` on the plot (LTTB smooths away exactly the bang-bang oscillation
a grower is looking for), LTTB on the navigator. This is not polish — it replaces Highstock's
implicit `dataGrouping`, which is load-bearing today and disappears with the migration.

**Server deltas**: a combined `{mean,min,max}` response; parameterised Flux for
`measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval` (interpolated raw today — must be fixed before widening the
measure vocabulary); `limit()` piped *before* `yield()`; interval clamped by timespan.
**Query-string compatibility**: `applyViewParams` keeps parsing every existing key, so saved
`ChartPreset`s and circulating `ShareLink`s survive. New keys are additive: `panels`, `konto`,
`produkt`.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

**The diary is not a feature in this concept. The Kontoauszug *is* the diary** — filtered to
"alle Konten" it is a reverse-chronological list of everything that happened, and every one of its
rows was created as a side effect of doing something, not as an act of diary-keeping. That is the
owner's "it does the diary keeping for you", made structural.

### New MongoDB collections (all additive, all optional fields, no migration tooling needed)

```ts

  device_id: string;
  cycle_id: string;
  txn_id: string;                // groups the postings written by one Buchung

  items?: PostingItem[];
  ec?: number; ph?: number;
  probe?: 'eingang' | 'runoff' | 'reservoir';   // what was measured — today's diary cannot say
  note?: string;

  stage?: DiaryLifecycleStage;
  source: 'gemessen' | 'plan' | 'geschaetzt';
  can_id?: string;
  client_txn_id: string;         // idempotency key, generated when the SHEET OPENS

  legacy_log_id?: string;        // set by the backfill from DeviceLog
}

// cans — the mixing can, an account with a balance
interface Can {
  can_id: string; device_id: string; actor_id: string; mixedAt: number;

// conditions — the standing open fact (round-one gap #4)

  condition_id: string; device_id: string; account_id?: string;
```

**Indexes** (postings will be the largest collection in the product):
`{device_id, time: -1}`, `{account_id, time: -1}`, `{device_id, client_txn_id}` **unique**,
`{cycle_id, kind}`. Server-side cursor pagination by `time` from day one — the existing log
endpoint has none and the Entries report fetches entire histories today.

**`DeviceLog` is not touched.** Device events stay where they are; the Kontoauszug read merges
`postings` ∪ `DeviceLog` at query time and renders both through the existing
`app-log-entry-item`. A one-time idempotent backfill (boot-time, house style) converts historic
`categories[0] === 'diary'` rows into postings with `legacy_log_id` set, so the reader can
de-duplicate. If the backfill never runs, the merge still shows everything. That is deliberate: the
backfill is a v1.1 item and v1 does not depend on it.

**How a user who ignores plants is untaxed.** Zero configuration. The first Buchung creates
`Account{kind:'zelt', label: device.name}`. The chip row is not rendered when
`accounts.length === 1`. `Konten` tab shows one row. No word "Pflanze" appears. Naming a plant is
an explicit act: `Konten → + Konto`.

**Splitting later** (`Konto teilen`): creates N accounts and back-books history proportionally into
them, marking every back-booked posting `source: 'geschaetzt'`. It is arithmetic fiction and the UI
says so once, in plain German: *"Die alten Gaben werden gleichmäßig verteilt. Das ist eine
Schätzung."* See weakness #5.

### What "following the schedule" means

The regime advances by **feed events, not by wall clock**: `Gabe 12 von 24`. The step index is
`count(postings where kind='duenger' and cycle_id=… and account_id=…)`, offset by the stage. The
"due" prediction uses the **median of that account's own last five feed intervals**, falling back to
`RegimeStep.everyNthFeed`. Growers feed Dienstag und Freitag and slip; the app learns the rhythm
rather than nagging on a calendar.

- **All-Mix**: `startFeedingAtWeek: 4`. Weeks 1–3 the fällig card reads
  `Nur Wasser — All-Mix ist vorgedüngt.` with an empty product list. Booking books water. This is
  the owner's own test case and the model expresses it directly.
- **BioTabs**: `steps` are mostly `waterOnly: true` with a `gPerPlant` re-tab step every ~6 weeks.
  The card then reads `Neu tabben — 2 Tabs je Pflanze`.
- **Autoflower**: `Cycle.strengthFactor = 0.4` multiplies every `mlPerL`, and no 12/12 flip event is
  expected. No manufacturer chart has an auto column; this is a genuine gap filled without borrowing.
- **EC honesty**: when `ec_basis === 'delta_ueber_leitungswasser'` (CANNA-style `EC+`), the prefilled
  EC target is `chart value + your source-water EC`, and the field hint says
  `Plan 1,4–1,8 + dein Leitungswasser 0,4`. A UI that alarms against the raw chart number is wrong.
- **PPM is never rendered bare.** EC is canonical. If a user types ppm, the scale (500/640/700) is a
  required adjacent picker. At EC 1.8 a Bluelab reads 1260 and a Hanna reads 900.

### What is stored by one Buchung

One `Can`, one `txn_id`, N `Posting`s (one per selected account). Water splits into equal shares of
`(kanneL − restL)`; products split proportionally to water. A posting's `source` is `'gemessen'`
only if every numeric field on it was touched, else `'plan'`. A `Condition` is written if the
checkbox was set. Photos use the existing `Image{format:'user/jpeg'}`.

**Editing**: within 15 minutes and by the same actor, edit in place. After that, `Stornieren` writes
a counter-posting (`voids`) and the original stays visible with a strike-through. Never a silent
edit in a shared record.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The owner calls this the biggest selling point. Round one shipped none of it. Here is what ships.

### 8.1 Der Zyklusfilm — a film someone can actually hold

Not a rolling 1d/1w/1m buffer. **A film of *this grow*,** from `Cycle.startedAt` to now, rebuilt
nightly at 03:00 device-local and on demand from a button with a real progress state.

`ffmpeg` and `sharp` are already dependencies and already used exactly this way
(`server/src/services/image.service.ts` — `execFile('ffmpeg', …)` at :566, `sharp().composite()` at
:151). This is not new infrastructure; it is a second consumer of a working pipeline.

### 8.2 Frame culling and normalisation — the content problem, specified

A tent timelapse is mostly garbage. Seven stages, each cheap and deterministic. No ML.

1. **Dunkel raus.** Drop frames where `out_light < 0.5` in the surrounding 60 s (one Influx range
   per day), falling back to the `daynight` schedule when `out_light` is absent. Removes the black
   half of every day.
2. **Kandidaten wählen.** `framesPerDay ∈ {1, 6, 24}`, default **6**, evenly spaced across each
   photoperiod. 90 days × 6 = 540 frames = 21,6 s at 25 fps.
3. ★ **Mensch raus — der Kontoauszug sagt der Kamera, wann jemand im Zelt war.** Drop every
   candidate within ±4 min of a `Posting` with `kind ∈ {wasser, duenger, training, foto}`. No motion
   detection, no false positives on a swaying leaf, and it is exact — the person who was in the tent
   told the ledger. Image correlation running in the *useful* direction, and the cheapest good idea
   here. Backstop for unlogged visits: drop a candidate whose MAD against the median of its 5
   neighbours exceeds a threshold **in the lower third of the frame**, where an arm enters.
4. **Belichtungs-Ausreißer raus.** Mean luminance per candidate; drop anything >2σ from the 7-frame
   rolling median. Kills door-open daylight blowouts and condensation-white frames.
5. **Weißabgleich angleichen.** Mean R/G/B over a fixed central rectangle; per-channel linear gain
   to match the film's reference (median of the first 7 kept frames) via
   `sharp().linear([rG,gG,bG],[0,0,0])`. Tames magenta-LED vs daylight drift across 12 weeks.
   Deterministic, ~8 ms/frame.
6. **Doppelte raus.** Drop a frame whose MAD against the previous kept frame is below a floor —
   catches a frozen camera re-serving the same JPEG.
7. **Stabilisierung: nicht in v1, ehrlich gesagt.** Instead a **`Kamera verschoben` detector**: a
   sustained MAD step writes a `Condition` — *"Kamera hat sich am 12.8. bewegt — der Film springt ab
   Tag 34."* — and offers `[ Film ab hier neu beginnen ]`. `vidstabdetect`/`vidstabtransform` is a
   v1.1 candidate and needs a build check (not in every ffmpeg build).

**The counters are shown to the user**, which is the only defence against silent bad culling:

> `Von 4.312 Bildern sind 540 im Film.`
> `2.980 waren dunkel · 214 zeigten jemanden im Zelt · 61 zu hell · 517 doppelt`

### 8.3 The ledger footer — the timelapse shows the ledger

Optional, **on by default** for the Zyklusfilm, one toggle for the plain film. Each kept frame gets
an SVG lower-third composited by `sharp` — literally the same code path as `addOfflineOverlay`
(`image.service.ts:127-159`), which already builds an SVG string, escapes it with `escapeXml` and
composites it onto a JPEG:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                          │
│                    [ tent frame ]                        │
│                                                          │
│  Tag 34 · Blüte Wo 3     ████████████░░░░░░  214 ml      │
│                          ▏  ▏ ▏   ▏  ▏   ▏               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
     day counter          cumulative-dose bar   ticks = Gaben
```

A growing bar and a tick per feed, both computed from the postings at that frame's timestamp. It is
the concept's centre of gravity rendered as pixels, and it is the single most shareable thing in the
product: a video that shows *what the plant was given while it grew*. Nobody in this market ships it.

### 8.4 How the video leaves the product

The gap round one had: no download path exists. `sendImage` (`image.controller.ts:179-206`) sets
only `Content-type` and `Cache-Control` — there is **no `Content-Disposition` anywhere in the
server**. The delta is small and exact:

```
GET /image/film/:device_id?film_id=…&token=<imageToken>

Content-Type: video/mp4
Content-Length: 5 214 880
Accept-Ranges: bytes                        ← required for inline playback in iOS Safari
Content-Disposition: attachment;
    filename="terpcontrol-zelt1-lauf3-tag1-89.mp4";
    filename*=UTF-8''terpcontrol-zelt1-lauf3-tag1-89.mp4
```

- **Filename**: `terpcontrol-<device slug>-<cycle slug>-tag<from>-<to>.mp4`, ASCII-folded
  (`ä→ae`), lowercased, non-alnum → `-`, with an RFC 5987 `filename*` for the umlaut case.
- **Token**: the existing `imageToken` (30 days, deliberately URL-embeddable) so the link works in
  a phone browser, in Telegram, from a laptop, with **no login**. A share that requires a login is
  not a share.
- **On the phone**: the Film tab offers two things side by side.
  `[ Teilen ]` calls `navigator.share({files:[new File([blob], name, {type:'video/mp4'})]})` —
  supported on iOS 16+ Safari and Android Chrome — which lands the file directly in the OS share
  sheet → Fotos, Telegram, WhatsApp. Feature-detected; when absent, `[ Herunterladen ]` is a plain
  `<a href download>` against the `attachment` response, which lands in Downloads and can be saved
  to Photos from there. **This is the only path that works without a native app, and Capacitor is
  untouched scaffold today** (`io.ionic.starter`, no `android/`, no `ios/`).
- **Format**: 1280×720, libx265, `crf 30`, 25 fps — the encoder settings already in the file.
  540 frames ≈ 4–8 MB. Under Telegram's 50 MB bot limit and Discord's 20 MB free tier with room.
  H.265 is a real caveat: it does not play in Firefox and not in every Android browser. **v1 encodes
  H.264 (`libx264`, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-profile:v baseline`) for films specifically** and leaves
  the existing x265 rolling timelapses alone. That costs ~40 % more bytes and buys universal playback,
  which for the artefact you send to people is the right trade.

**Storage**: films do **not** go in Mongo — `Image.data` is a BSON `Buffer` capped at 16 MB with no
graceful failure path, and a 200-day 24 fpd film exceeds it. Films go to a `films` docker volume,
referenced by a `Film` document:

```ts
interface Film {
  film_id: string; device_id: string; cycle_id: string;
  from: number; to: number; framesPerDay: 1 | 6 | 24; ledgerFooter: boolean;
  path: string; bytes: number; durationS: number; width: number; height: number;
  frameCount: number; builtAt: number;
  culled: { dunkel: number; mensch: number; belichtung: number; doppelt: number };
  status: 'wartet' | 'baut' | 'fertig' | 'fehler'; error?: string;
}
```

New volume in `docker-compose.yaml` — one line, flagged as real infra work.

### 8.5 The camera alarm

Round one gave the camera no alarm. A check on the existing hourly tick: if the device is online and
no `jpeg` has arrived for 6 h, raise an alarm through the existing `alarm.service` pipeline
(`sensorType: 'camera'` — a new value in the sensor vocabulary, cloud-side only, no firmware) so it
inherits e-mail, webhook, cooldown and retrigger for free, and open a `Condition`. Heute's picture
tile shows `Letztes Bild vor 3 Tagen` in `--tc-text-warning`. A camera that stopped three days into
flower tells you.

### 8.6 Image correlation, four directions

1. **Chart → Bild.** The film strip is an *axis*: thumbnails positioned by timestamp on the same
   x-scale, so uneven capture reads honestly as gaps. Delivered as **one sprite + a JSON index per
   visible window** (`GET /image/strip/:device_id?from&to&cols&rows` → a 256×144 tile grid plus
   `{tiles:[{t,x,y}]}`), so scrubbing 90 days is two requests, not five hundred. mp4 rows with
   `timestampEnd` render as a bracket, not a point.
2. **Bild → Zahlen.** Tapping a still opens the **Bild-Karte**: the picture, the timestamp, the
   climate at that instant, **and the account balances as of that moment** —
   *"12.7. 13:04 · A3 hatte 180 ml Bio-Bloom · 8 Gaben"*. Nobody else can render that line.
3. **Buchung → Bild.** Every posting row in the Kontoauszug carries two thumbnails: the frame
   nearest *before* the posting and the frame ~24 h *after*. `Vorher / 24 Std später.` The club's
   literal question — "wie haben die Pflanzen reagiert?" — answered by construction, at zero
   capture cost.
4. **Foto → Zeitachse.** A photo taken in the Buchung sheet is a `Posting{kind:'foto'}` with an
   `Image{format:'user/jpeg'}`, so hand photos sit on the same strip as webcam frames and can be
   included in the film (`Eigene Fotos mitnehmen` toggle).

**No camera at all**: the Film tab shows the user's own photos and says so —
`Keine Kamera gekoppelt. Der Film besteht aus deinen 14 Fotos.` Fourteen hand photos still make a
film, and this is reveal-by-data, not a degraded mode.

## 9. Clubs

**Attribution without an auth rewrite.** `Actor` is a device-scoped name the owner types once in
Settings → *"Wer ist am Zelt"*. No account, no invitation, no e-mail, no `auth.middleware.ts`
surgery. The Buchung sheet's first row is a chip row; the last-used actor is remembered per browser
in `localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']`, so in practice it costs **zero extra taps**. Every
posting carries `actor_id`. When real memberships land later, `Actor.user_id` is filled in and the
chip row becomes an identity — the data written in v1 stays valid.

Field hint under the name input: `Vorname oder Spitzname reicht.` The product never asks for a
real name.

**The double-feed problem.** When the sheet opens, one query: postings for the selected accounts
within `min(12 h, ½ × your median feed interval)`.

- Different actor → above the footer, in warning colour:
  `Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gefüttert.` and the footer button becomes **`Trotzdem buchen`**.
  One deliberate extra tap. Not a dialog, not a blocker, nothing to dismiss.
- Same actor within 30 min → `Du hast das gerade schon gebucht.` same treatment.
- Never a hard block. A grower who genuinely wants to feed twice is not wrong; a grower who forgot
  is not punished.

**Offline capture in a cellar.** `@angular/service-worker` is added — a genuinely new dependency and
a build change, flagged. The Buchung is written to IndexedDB and POSTed with
`Idempotency-Key: client_txn_id`, generated when the sheet **opened**. The server upserts on the
unique index `{device_id, client_txn_id}`. **A retry cannot double-log** — which matters more here
than anywhere, because in a shared record a duplicate row is indistinguishable from a double feed.
Queued rows appear immediately in the Kontoauszug with `⏳ nicht gesendet`. If a different actor's
posting for the same accounts landed while you were offline, sync surfaces one card:
`Möglicher Doppelguss am Do 19:41` → `[ Behalten ]` `[ Stornieren ]`.

**Conditions.** The `OFFEN` block. Created from the sheet's `☐ Als offenen Punkt merken`, or from
the block's `+`. A condition with `account_id` set and `blocksFeeding` shows inline next to that
account's chip in the sheet. Closing is one tap (`Erledigt`), stamped `closedBy` / `closedAt`. This
is what is currently pinned to the tent door with tape.

**Storno, not edit** (`voids` / `voidedBy`). After 15 minutes a posting is append-only. The shared
record is trustworthy without cryptographic immutability — which the dossier is right to warn
against, because immutability protects a *regulated* club and victimises an *unregulated* grower.

**Privacy posture — the line, in one sentence:**

> Das Konto verzeichnet, was in die Pflanzen hineingeht. Niemals, was an Menschen herausgeht.

Concretely: no per-member dispensing, no grams handed over, no THC content, no membership numbers,
no birth dates, no addresses, no location, no analytics SDK, no compliance claim, no § 26 export, no
`KCanG-konform` badge. `kind:'ernte'` exists but is **never prompted**. Default posture is
**privat**: attribution on (it is a name you typed, not an identity), retention configurable per
device (12 / 24 / unbegrenzt Monate — and it really deletes postings and stills), plus one
`Alle Daten dieses Zelts löschen` that really deletes.

Export (`Konten → Export`, CSV + JSON): `Namen: voll / Initialen / anonym (A, B, C)`, defaulting to
**Initialen**. Columns: `zeit, konto, akteur, art, wasser_l, produkt, ml, ec, ph, probe, quelle, notiz`.
That CSV is also the thing a club can hand its own bookkeeping — without Terp Control ever becoming
the club's system of record.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app, sees a picture (if he has a camera), one line of
verdict, three numbers with an age on them. Blocks 4–7 are absent because he never booked anything.
His app is a verdict and a chart. The ledger costs him nothing because it does not exist until he
uses it. What he gains over today: an honest data age, a setpoint drawn on his chart, and a
`Verlauf` he can read on a phone.

**2 · Same, but with a camera.** The tab he cares about is Film, and it is a headline, not a tile.
He gets a Zyklusfilm of his current grow, culled and colour-normalised, with a download and a Web
Share button that puts an mp4 in his camera roll. If he never books a feed, the ledger footer is
just a day counter — still useful, still his. Scrubbing his chart moves the picture. This is the
type this round was written for and he is fully served in v1.

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂, heating).** His Heute shows only what he owns: `parseSocketRoles`
says heater + co2, so there is no humidity target row, no dehumidifier tile and no
"Entfeuchter läuft dauerhaft" alarm preset offered. Verlauf's output lane shows two regions, not
five. He can book a CO₂ bottle change as a `zustand` posting and it lands on the annotation rail.
The fails-open capability bug is fixed as part of this: absent `hardwareInfo.sockets` renders
*nothing* rather than everything.

**4 · Full tent.** He gets the most out of Verlauf: four panels, setpoint bands, a state timeline
showing the heater and the dehumidifier fighting, and the Kontoband under it so a feed is visible
three days before a VPD drift. His honest ceiling is the firmware's — five socket roles, no
humidifier, no exhaust role, `dehumidifier` doubling as the cooler — and this concept does not
pretend otherwise: the dead controls are removed from the UI rather than shown.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** He wants an API and raw data. He gets: a documented `POST /postings` with
`Idempotency-Key` (the same endpoint the app uses — no private API), a cursor-paginated
`GET /postings`, CSV + JSON export, and the `Film` build endpoint. He can push his own EC probe
readings in as `kind:'messung'` postings and they appear on the annotation rail and in the export —
which is the first time a custom measure has been able to enter this product at all. He does not
get output write access; that is honestly out of scope and firmware-gated.

**6 · Club.** The concept was built for his question. Actors give attribution in ~2 days of work
with no auth rewrite. The double-feed soft block is at the exact moment it matters. Conditions
replace the note taped to the tent door. Storno makes the record trustworthy between people. The
Kontoauszug with Vorher/Nachher thumbnails answers "how did the plants react". The export gives him
a club history with names reduced to initials by default. What he does **not** get, deliberately:
member dispensing records, § 26 filing, roles, or a rota — the first is refused on principle, the
rest are v1.1.

**7 · Old device owner.** See §10. Two tabs, no new required fields, no migration, and his device
finally stops calling itself a fridge.

*(Lenses.)* **Stoner**: 2 taps in the dark, one 64 px button at the bottom, block order that never
moves, a soft block before a double feed. **Normal**: a film for the camera roll and a balance that
is a sentence. **Techie**: every field on screen without asking, an `≈` he can audit, raw postings
over an API, CSV, and a chart with real axes.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The balance is mostly prefills, and the prefill is the plan.** Someone who taps Buchen twice a
   week has a ledger saying he followed the schedule perfectly — because it recorded the schedule,
   not the pour. The headline promise is weakest for exactly the user most likely to overfeed, and
   `≈` is his permanent state. This is the deepest flaw and no UI fixes it; only a scale or a flow
   meter would, and no such hardware exists.

2. **It taxes anyone with more than a handful of accounts.** Four chips is fine; a club room with
   twenty plants is a wall of chips on every Buchung. Groups help and do not solve it — picking the
   wrong group writes a wrong record, which is worse than no record.

3. **"34 % über Plan" claims a precision the source data lacks.** Manufacturer charts give ranges,
   conditionals and literal strings. Refusing the percentage when the step is a range is the honest
   mitigation, but it means the flagship number is *absent* for several seeded regimes.

4. **The vocabulary is cold and possibly alarming.** Konto, Buchung, Saldo, Storno is Finanzamt
   German for a hobby. Some growers will find it clinical, a few evidence-shaped, and no privacy
   posture removes the feeling of keeping books on your plants. Softening it costs the clarity.

5. **Splitting an account retroactively fabricates history.** Proportional back-booking is the only
   option and it is arithmetic fiction; `geschaetzt` is honest and still means part of a "true
   cumulative" is invented.

6. **The ledger footer on the film is an unhedged bet, defaulted on.** It reads as unique and
   charming or as a watermark ruining the one artefact a user wants to show off. A toggle does not
   undo a default.

7. **The culling heuristics have no test corpus.** MAD thresholds, 2σ luminance and posting
   proximity will be wrong on somebody's tent, and a wrongly dropped frame is invisible. The
   counters are a diagnostic, not a fix — and posting proximity has a perverse edge: a diligent
   logger loses more frames than a lazy one.

8. **Four tabs plus a FAB is more navigation than this app has ever had**, with zero precedent in
   the codebase. The stoner gains from a fixed FAB; he does not obviously gain from choosing between
   Verlauf and Film.

9. **I propose cutting the chart migration to make the date**, which means October may ship the
   P1-flagged chart unchanged. A critic may fairly call v1 "a ledger bolted onto the old chart", and
   on the bad branch of that decision they would be right.

10. **Actors are attribution without authorisation, and people will tap the wrong chip.** Fine for
    two people; for a fifteen-member club it degrades within a season, and trusting who did what is
    the ledger's whole value. Real memberships are not optional for type 6 — merely not in v1.
