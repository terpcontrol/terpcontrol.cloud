# C50 — "Das Etikett"

**Family:** club · **Centre of gravity:** attribution is a label, not an account. Push labels as far as
they go — and mark, in the UI itself, the exact line where a label stops being enough.

Written against `NEW-BRIEF.md`, `00-DOSSIER.md`, `00-OWNER-DECISIONS.md`, `00-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md`,
and direct reads of `/home/user/terpcontrol.cloud`. Code facts carry a file reference.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every fact in this product wears a label saying who put it there, when, and where it came from — and
that one label line is also the door to every level of detail, so there is only ever one interface.**

Eight of ten round-one concepts deferred clubs because multi-user "needs an auth rewrite". It does not.
Authorisation is a gate: the question "may this request write?" — today answered by exactly one line,
`deviceModel.find({ owner_id: req.user_id, device_id })` in `auth.middleware.ts:172`. Attribution is a
label: the question "whose hands did this?" — answered by a string on the row. C50 refuses to conflate
them. The gate stays exactly as it is; the label ships in v1.

The consequence is bigger than clubs. Once every object carries a label — a reading, a photo, a
setpoint, a feed, a timelapse frame — you have a single repeated component, **die Etikettenzeile**, that
is simultaneously the plain-language summary (`Anna · vor 1 Std`) and the entry point to the raw
provenance (actor id, device clock vs server clock, source, edit history, raw value). One line. The
stoned user reads three words off it. The techie taps it and gets the record. Nobody is in a mode.

The concept's honesty requirement is symmetrical: labels are **Namensschilder, keine Unterschriften** —
name tags, not signatures. C50 says so on screen, refuses to sell the record as compliance evidence,
and ships a one-tap path to strip all attribution. That refusal is what makes the cheap version
defensible rather than negligent.

## 2. Information architecture

Navigation stays the Ionic `ion-split-pane` + `ion-menu` shell (constraint: no framework surgery).
Inside a device, a **4-item bottom tab bar** appears — it does not exist today (`<ion-fab>` = 0 hits,
no tab bar anywhere) and is the cheapest fix for the six-outline-buttons cross-navigation.

```
/list                              Geräte           (unchanged shell, new card + Etikettenzeile)
/device/:id                        Zelt             NEW home. Replaces the fridge overview.
/device/:id/verlauf                Verlauf          chart. /charts kept as a permanent alias.
/device/:id/tagebuch               Tagebuch         diary. /diary kept as a permanent alias.
/device/:id/kamera                 Kamera           camera + Filme. NEW.
  ├ /device/:id/kamera/film/:film_id                one produced film, with its download
/device/:id/pflanzen               Pflanzen         ONLY rendered if ≥1 Plant exists
  ├ /device/:id/pflanze/:plant_id                   one plant, its Konto, its Etiketten
/device/:id/leute                  Leute            the roster + Wer-hat-was
/device/:id/einstellungen          Einstellungen    settings (ngSwitch by device_type, as today)
/device/:id/testmode  /wartung                      unchanged, admin/owner
/leute                             (menu)           roster management across devices — v1.1
```

Alias rule, non-negotiable: `/charts` and `/diary` keep working with their full query-string vocabulary
— that string *is* the persisted format of every `ChartPreset` and `ShareLink`, so `applyViewParams`
gains a redirect, not a redefinition. **Tabs** are `Zelt · Verlauf · Tagebuch · Kamera`: fixed order,
fixed labels, never adaptive. Pflanzen and Leute hang off the Zelt screen, because they do not exist for
every device.

**The FAB is the signature control.** A green pill, bottom-right, above the tab bar, on every device
route:

```
╭───────────────────────╮
│  ＋   als  Anna  ▾    │
╰───────────────────────╯
```

Left two-thirds (`＋ als`) = capture. Right third (`Anna ▾`) = the roster picker. One control that does
attribution and capture together, and states who you currently are without a settings screen. When no
roster exists it reads `＋ Eintrag` and the name half is absent — reveal by data, not by mode.

### 3.4 Stale, offline, absent — the rules

The dossier names "nothing distinguishes live from stale from offline" as the #1 complaint across five
consumer brands (§8 P0 #5). C50 makes it structural: **freshness is not a badge, it is the second field
of every label**, so it cannot be forgotten on a new screen.

- **Fresh** (< 90 s): full opacity, label `vor 12 Sek`.
- **Stale** (90 s – `ONLINE_TIMEOUT`): numbers in `--tc-text-warning`, label `vor 14 Min`, verdict swaps
  to `Zuletzt gemessen vor 14 Min`. Values stay visible — hiding a number from someone who wants it is a
  mode by another name.
- **Offline**: values intact at 55 % opacity, verdict `Gerät offline seit Mo 03:12`, device-writing
  buttons disabled with `Gerät nicht erreichbar — Eintrag wird trotzdem gespeichert.` **Capture is never
  disabled**: logging what you did by hand does not need the device.
- **Absent** (e.g. `co2 = -1` without the SCD4x): the tile is not rendered. Not greyed, not "Upgrade
  kaufen". Reveal by capability via `deviceHasCo2()`.
- **Empty account**: the existing `.tc-hero` onboarding stays.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — die Etikettenzeile

### 4.1 The mechanism, stated exactly

**One component, `<tc-etikett>`, renders under every fact in the product. Closed, it is one line of
plain German. Tapped, it unrolls in place into the full provenance record for that one object. The
state is per-object, per-tap, and never remembered.**

That last clause is what makes it not a mode. No preference, no localStorage key, no "Expertenansicht"
segment — today's `localStorage['app-settings-expert']` is deleted by this concept. Opening the label
under the temperature tells the app nothing about you and changes nothing else on screen. The techie who
opens twelve labels gets twelve records; the stoner who opens none loses nothing, because the closed
line already carries the two facts that matter: **who** and **how long ago**.

### 4.2 The same pixels, two readings

```
CLOSED — what everyone sees, always:

   24,3 °C
   Soll 24,0
   ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
   Gerät · vor 12 Sek · SHT21          ›

OPEN — after one tap on the line, same card, pushed down:

   24,3 °C
   Soll 24,0
   ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
   Gerät · vor 12 Sek · SHT21          ⌄
     Rohwert        24,31 °C
     Gemessen       25.08.2026 14:03:07  (Gerätezeit)
     Empfangen      25.08.2026 14:03:09  (Server, +2,0 s)
     Quelle         SHT21, 5-s-Takt, Mittel über 60 s
     Soll kommt von night.temperature = 24 · Plan Schritt 9
     Zuletzt geändert  Anna · So 21:40 · 20,0 → 19,0
     Alarm          keiner auf temperature
     [ Im Verlauf zeigen ]   [ Rohdaten kopieren ]
```

The bottom two buttons are the techie's exit hatches and they are *inside the same line every user
already sees*. `Rohdaten kopieren` puts a JSON object on the clipboard — the dossier says type 5 writes
their own Go apps; give them a clean object without an API to wait for.

### 4.3 The label is the same on every object

| Object | Closed label |
| --- | --- |
| Sensor reading | `Gerät · vor 12 Sek · SHT21` |
| Setpoint row in settings | `Anna · So 21:40 · vorher 20,0` |
| Diary/action row | `Ben · Mo 14:22 · Foto` |
| Photo (user upload) | `Anna · Mo 14:22 · A2` |
| Timelapse frame | `Kamera · Di 09:14 · 24,1 °C · Licht 100 %` |
| Produced film | `Anna · Sa 11:02 · 41 Tage, 612 Bilder, 118 verworfen` |
| Condition | `Ben · Mo 14:20 · offen seit 3 Tagen` |
| Alarm | `Gerät · Di 03:07 · Schwelle 28,0, Spitze 29,4` |
| Prefilled feed value | `Plan · Bio-Bloom, All-Mix Woche 5` |

Nine object types, one component, one interaction. That repetition is the accessibility story: after the
first tap anywhere the user knows the whole app.

### 4.4 The three other non-mode mechanisms C50 leans on

1. **Reveal by capability.** Tiles, target rows, alarm presets and socket controls come from
   `parseSocketRoles(hardwareInfo.sockets)` — the per-role truth `deviceControlCapability()`'s three
   buckets throw away (`grow-presets.ts:143-173`). A heater-only tent shows a heater and no dehumidifier.
   **Missing key (old firmware) fails to `monitor`, not `full`**: the current fail-open is a bug C50
   fixes, with the honest copy `Dein Gerät meldet keine Steckdosen. Nur Messwerte.`
2. **Reveal by data.** Zero plants → no Pflanzen strip, no plant chips, no Konto. Zero roster names → no
   `als …` on the FAB, no attribution column, no double-feed prompt. A solo hobbyist never meets the club
   machinery and is never asked whether they are one.
3. **Progressive precision.** Verdict word → number → Soll → sparkline → label → raw record → chart. Six
   depths on one card, each one tap, none gated.

### 4.5 Why this is not "Advanced" with a German name

An "Advanced" accordion hides a *feature class* behind a *global* switch. `<tc-etikett>` hides
*provenance for one object* behind a *local* tap — and the summary is not a teaser. If the open state
were deleted entirely, `Anna · vor 1 Std` would still be the most valuable line on the screen. That is
the test a disclosure control has to pass; a mode never passes it.

## 5. The chart — `/device/:id/verlauf`

Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line directive (owner decision). Delete `chart.js`,
`ng2-charts`, `chartjs-adapter-luxon`.

**Stacked small multiples, one linked crosshair** (`grid[]` + `axisPointer.link` + `echarts.connect()`),
90–120 px per panel on mobile. Never 15 hidden axes.

```
┌ Ablesekopf (pinned, replaces the hover tooltip) ───────────┐
│ Di 26.08. 14:03    T 24,3   RH 58   VPD 1,12   Licht 100 % │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌ Temperatur °C ─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│         ░░░░░░░░ target band (day 23–25) ░░░░░░░░          │
│  ─ ─ ─ ─┐                         ┌─ ─ ─ ─  Soll, dashed   │
│  ▂▃▄▅▄▃▂└──────── night 19 ───────┘▂▃▄▅▄▃▂  Ist, solid     │
│  ▒▒ signed deviation fill between Ist and Soll ▒▒          │
│ 20 ─────────────────────────────────────────────── 26      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌ Luftfeuchte % ──────── (min/max envelope + mean) ──────────┐
┌ VPD kPa ──── band moves with the stage: Veg 0,8–1,2 ───────┐
├ Ausgänge (state timeline) ─────────────────────────────────┤
│ Licht   ████████████████░░░░░░░░░░░░░░████████████         │
│ Heizung ░░░█░░░░░░██░░░░░░░░░░░█░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░         │
├ Etiketten-Schiene (annotation rail, max 2 rows) ───────────┤
│ Reihe 1 (Menschen)   ⓐ    ⓑ  ③        ⓐ                   │
│ Reihe 2 (Gerät)        !        ▌Phase→Blüte               │
├ Filmstreifen ──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [▪][▪][▪]  [▪][▪]      [▪][▪][▪][▪]   [▪][▪][▪]           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Goal vs actual.** Dashed **stepped** setpoint (`markLine` per segment, stepping at `daynight.day` /
  `night` between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature`), a soft `markArea` band behind it, and a
  signed deviation fill between the two lines. Never `threshold: null` fill-to-axis — it encodes nothing.
  Band source, one at a time, named in the panel subtitle: alarm thresholds > setpoint ± tolerance >
  stage `vpdRange`.
- **Day/night** from measured `out_light > 0`, falling back to the `daynight` schedule, in an explicit
  timezone. A failed contactor renders as a **missing band** — a diagnosis, not decoration.
- **Annotations are labels, and they carry the actor.** The rail under the x-axis (12–20 px, two rows,
  8–12 px pixel-proximity clustering) draws human events as **circled initials** — `ⓐ` Anna, `ⓑ` Ben, a
  count badge `③` for a cluster. In a shared tent you read who did what against the climate without
  opening anything. Tap → the pinned Ablesekopf swaps to that action's `<tc-etikett>`, open.
- **Outputs** get their own state-timeline lane; region length = duration; null = gap.
- **min/max envelope** on the primary measure (`aggregateWindow` already supports min/max) and
  `sampling: 'minmax'` for the plot — LTTB smooths away the oscillation of a bang-bang controller.
- **Time-in-range scorecard** above the chart: one stacked bar split Tag/Nacht, longest excursion as a
  *duration*, data-coverage %, greyed below 80 %. **Computed server-side on raw Influx samples** —
  computing it on `aggregateWindow(mean)` output destroys the excursions the metric exists to count.
- Fix first: `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval` are interpolated raw into Flux (`data.service.ts:80-89`)
  and `limit(n:50000)` is piped after `yield()`. Any widened measure vocabulary is blocked on this.
- Mobile: one finger scrolls, two fingers pan/zoom (`dataZoom: inside`), tap sets the crosshair. Axis
  labels are never hidden — shortened to 3 ticks, unit moved into the panel title.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant

### 6.1 New entities (all cloud-side MongoDB, all optional, no required field anywhere)

```ts
Actor {                      // a name tag on a rack. Device-scoped. No account, password or email.
  actor_id: string;          // uuid, stable forever — the upgrade hook
  device_id: string;
  name: string;              // Vorname or nickname, maxlength 24 (see §9.4)
  colour: string;            // one of 8 Okabe-Ito hexes; initial derived for the chart rail
  created_at: number; archived_at?: number;
  linked_user_id?: string;   // ALWAYS null in v1. The seam for real memberships.
}

Plant {
  plant_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;             // "A1" — the pot label, not a strain
  strain?: string; started_at: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stage_since: number;   // reuse the existing 6-value union
  regime_id?: string; archived_at?: number;
  harvest?: { at: number; wet_g?: number; dry_g?: number; note?: string };  // opt-in, §9.4
}

Action {                     // one human act. THE new spine of the diary.
  action_id: string; device_id: string;
  client_event_id: string;   // UUID minted in the browser. UNIQUE INDEX. Kills double-logging.
  type: 'giessen'|'duengen'|'messung'|'training'|'notiz'|'foto'|'ernte'|'phase'|'bedingung';
  at: number;                // when it HAPPENED (editable), separate from created_at
  created_at: number;
  actor_id?: string;         // the label. absent = "Ohne Namensschild"
  plant_ids: string[];       // [] = the whole tent
  tap_count?: number;        // THE RAW GESTURE, stored (see §7)
  vessel_ml?: number;        // the can size at capture time
  amount_ml?: number;        // derived = tap_count * vessel_ml. Both stored.
  products?: { name: string; ml_per_l?: number; source: 'plan'|'hand' }[];
  ec?: number; ec_basis?: 'absolut'|'ueber_leitwert'; ph?: number;
  sample?: 'zulauf'|'ablauf'|'reservoir';   // fixes "pH of WHAT?"
  note?: string;
  images?: string[];         // image_id strings, as DeviceLog already does
  prefilled_from?: { regime_id: string; step_index: number };
  despite?: { action_id: string; actor_id?: string; minutes: number };  // deliberate double feed
  corrects?: string;         // action_id — corrections, never destructive edits, in club mode
}

Condition {                  // the note taped to the tent door
  condition_id: string; device_id: string;
  text: string;              // max 120 chars
  plant_ids: string[]; severity: 0|1;
  opened_by?: string; opened_at: number;
  closed_by?: string; closed_at?: number; closing_note?: string;
}

MediumRegime {               // owner decision 3: medium + feed line as ONE selectable thing.
  regime_id: string;         // content collection, hot-swappable, never bundled into the webapp
  label: string;             // "Biobizz All-Mix + Bio-Grow/Bio-Bloom"
  medium_note_key: string;   // i18n key -> "stark vorgedüngt, erste 3-4 Wochen nicht düngen"
  feed_start_week: number;
  steps: { index: number; phase: DiaryLifecycleStage; week_from: number; week_to: number;
           products: { name: string; ml_per_l?: number|[number,number]|string }[];
           ec_hint?: [number, number]; ec_basis: 'absolut'|'ueber_leitwert';
           ph_hint?: [number, number]; interval_days: number }[];
  source_url: string; retrieved_at: number; kind: 'official_pdf'|'official_calculator'|'ownerless';
}
```

`Action` does **not** replace `DeviceLog` — device events, alarms and config diffs stay there. The
Tagebuch view merges the two streams by time and renders both through one `<tc-etikett>`-bearing row
component. `DeviceLog` gains exactly two optional fields: `actor_id?: string` and
`client_event_id?: string`. That is the whole schema change to the existing collection.

### 6.2 The screen

`/device/:id/tagebuch` is one reverse-chronological stream grouped by day, day header
`Di 26.08. · Tag 41 · Blüte Tag 19`, filter chips reusing `collectLogCategories`/`matchesLogCategory`,
and server-side pagination (`?before=&limit=50`) — today `getDeviceLogs` has no `.limit()` at all. A row:

```
 14:22   Anna  gegossen · A1 A2 A3
         3× Kanne 5 l  =  15 l  (5 l je Pflanze)
         [foto] [foto]
         Anna · vor 1 Std · gespeichert offline, gesendet 14:24     ›
```

### 6.3 How a user who ignores plants is untaxed

If `Plant.count == 0` for a device:
- `plant_ids` is always `[]`; the capture sheet shows **no plant chips at all** (not a greyed row, not
  "alle Pflanzen").
- The Pflanzen tab-strip on the Zelt screen is not rendered; `/pflanzen` returns a redirect to `/`.
- The day header shows only `Di 26.08.`, no day-of-run counter.
- Nothing ever asks "Willst du Pflanzen anlegen?" There is exactly one entry point: a `Pflanze anlegen`
  row at the bottom of the capture sheet's plant section — which only becomes visible once you scroll
  a capture sheet that has plants. For a plantless user, it lives at the bottom of `Einstellungen`.

The first plant is created as a side effect of the first stage change, backfilled from the existing
`lifecycleName` string via a **one-time boot backfill** of `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` — the dossier
is right that this must stop being a runtime algorithm that forks history when you rename a plant.

## 7. Watering & fertilising — tap counts

### 7.1 The capture flow

Trigger: the FAB (`＋ als Anna`), or the `Jetzt düngen als Anna` button on a FÄLLIG block, which enters
the same sheet with the plan already applied.

```
STEP 0  who        SKIPPED unless the roster is stale (§9.3)
STEP 1  was        6 tiles, 72 px tall, 2 columns:
                   Gießen · Düngen · Messen · Notiz · Foto · Bedingung
STEP 2  welche     plant chips + [Alle].  ABSENT if no plants exist.
STEP 3  wie viel   the tap pad:
                          ╭────────────────╮
                          │      3 ×       │   200 px square
                          │     Kanne      │   tap +1 · long-press −1
                          ╰────────────────╯   haptic tick per tap
                            = 15 l              derived, live
                            5 l je Pflanze
                          Kanne 5 l ›           (tap to change)
STEP 4  die Mischung   (Düngen only, prefilled from the regime)
                   Bio-Grow   2,0 ml/l → 30 ml   Plan · All-Mix Woche 5  ›
                   Bio-Bloom  4,0 ml/l → 60 ml   Plan · All-Mix Woche 5  ›
                   ＋ Mittel
                   EC 1,6   pH 6,1   Probe: Zulauf ▾
STEP 5  speichern  ╭──────────────────────────────╮
                   │      Speichern als Anna      │   56 px, green
                   ╰──────────────────────────────╯
                   Zeit: jetzt ›      Foto ＋
```

**Why tap counts.** The grower is in a dark tent, one hand on a 5 l can, possibly stoned, at 2am. Nobody
measures 14,7 litres. They think in cans. The pad captures the gesture directly — no keyboard, no
slider, no decimal point, 200 px of target, haptic confirmation per tap. `Kanne 5 l` is set once per
device (`Device.cloudSettings` is cloud-side and already free-form, or a small `TentSettings` doc).

**What is stored.** Both `tap_count: 3` **and** `amount_ml: 15000` **and** `vessel_ml: 5000`. Never only
the derived litres. If the owner later corrects the can size from 5 l to 4,5 l, every historical row
recomputes correctly *and* the record still says what the person actually did: three cans. The techie
gets `15 l`; the club report gets `3× Kanne 5 l`; the raw gesture survives.

Two taps is `2 ×`. Zero taps with a plant selected and a note is a valid record (`Action` with no
amount) — a note is not a failed watering.

### 7.2 Schedule following, the way growers actually slip

The regime advances **by feed events, not wall clock**: `step_index` increments when a `duengen` Action
referencing it is saved, and FÄLLIG comes from `last_feed_at + step.interval_days < now`. Growers feed
Tue/Fri and slip; a plan that insists "you are in week 6" after four feeds is wrong, and they stop
trusting it.

**All-Mix must be expressible as "don't feed yet."** `feed_start_week: 4` with
`medium_note_key: 'regime.allmix.prefertilised'` renders, for the first three weeks, not a FÄLLIG block
but a Bedingung-styled information row:

```
  ℹ  All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt.
     Düngen ab Woche 4 — noch 11 Tage.
     Plan · zuletzt geprüft 14.03.2026      ›
```

`zuletzt geprüft` is `retrieved_at` from the content collection. It is simultaneously the legal posture
(§5.3 of the dossier), a genuine trust feature, and free.

BioTabs is expressible in the same shape: `interval_days: 42`, products = one tablet row, almost no
liquid steps.

**Every prefilled number is editable and the label says it was prefilled** (`Plan · All-Mix Woche 5`).
Once edited, the label flips to `Anna · geändert von 4,0` — deviation is the normal case, and the record
shows it as a deliberate act rather than silently overwriting the plan.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation

The camera is where C50's primitive pays off twice: **a frame is a labelled object**, and **the label of
a frame includes who was in the room**.

### 8.1 What exists today (verified, `server/src/services/image.service.ts`)

Stills every 30 s stored as Mongo `Buffer`; three rolling timelapses rebuilt hourly
(`compressRtspStreamRange`, `1d`/`1w`/`1m`, 2 min × N frame interval, 25 fps); thinning tiers 1/5/15/60
min after 1/7/30/90 days; 3-year retention. **The encoder is `libx265 -crf 30` in an mp4**
(`convertRtspStreamImagesToVideo`, ~line 567).

**That is a shipping bug, not a nitpick.** HEVC in mp4 does not play in Chrome on most Android devices,
not at all in Firefox, and on iOS depends on the `hvc1`/`hev1` tag ffmpeg's defaults here do not
guarantee. The headline selling point is currently a file many customers cannot open.

### 8.3 Frame culling and normalisation — the content problem

A new collection, computed once per still on ingest (a `sharp` pass, ~15 ms/frame):

```ts
FrameLabel {
  image_id: string; device_id: string; timestamp: number;
  luma_mean: number;            // 0..255
  luma_p05: number; luma_p95: number;
  hue_dominant: number;         // 0..360, from a downsampled 32x18 histogram
  sharpness: number;            // variance of Laplacian on a 320px grey copy
  delta_prev: number;           // mean abs diff vs previous kept frame, 0..1
  offset_px: [number, number];  // phase correlation vs the day's median frame
  lights_state: 'an'|'aus'|'rampe';   // from out_light at that timestamp
  person_suspected: boolean;
}
```

Culling rules, in order:

1. **Lights.** Keep `lights_state === 'an'` only; a tent timelapse is 12 h of black otherwise. Ramp
   frames (the `lights.sunrise`/`sunset` window) are dropped — they are the flicker.
2. **Exposure outliers.** Drop `luma_mean` outside ±2,5 σ of the day's lights-on median: the door-open
   daylight blast and the frame caught mid-switch.
3. **Blur.** Drop `sharpness < 0,4 ×` the day's median — condensation and refocus frames.
4. **Humans.** `person_suspected = delta_prev > 0,18 AND (an Action exists within ±3 min OR the delta
   sits in the lower third)`. **This is where C50's own primitive does camera work no other concept
   can**: the app knows a human was in the tent because a human stamped their name 90 s ago.
5. **Near-duplicates.** Within a day, drop `delta_prev < 0,004` beyond the first, so a dark veg week
   collapses instead of eating the 30-second budget.
6. **Even distribution.** Then pick N = 30 s × 25 fps = 750 frames nearest 750 evenly spaced target
   timestamps, so week 2 and week 9 get the same screen time. That is what makes it watchable.

Normalisation, applied at encode time:

- **Luma**: per-frame gain matching a 7-day rolling target — a smooth ramp, not per-frame
  auto-exposure, which is itself the cause of flicker.
- **White balance**: no fixed grey reference exists in a magenta LED tent, so each frame's dominant-hue
  histogram is matched to the run's median. Honest limitation, stated on the sheet:
  `Farben werden angeglichen, nicht korrigiert.`
- **Stabilisation**: translate by `-offset_px`, integer pixel, cropping 2 % per edge. Handles the
  camera-nudged-on-day-12 case as translation only; rotation is not corrected in v1 and the film's label
  says `3 Bilder verschoben, nicht gedreht`. ffmpeg's `deshake` is not used — the offsets are already
  computed, so a per-frame `crop`+`pad` is cheaper and deterministic.

### 8.4 How a video LEAVES the product

```
POST /device/:device_id/film            -> { film_id, status: 'queued' }
GET  /device/:device_id/film/:film_id   -> { status, progress, frames_used, frames_dropped, bytes }
GET  /device/:device_id/film/:film_id/download?token=<imageToken>
```

- `Image { format:'mp4', timestamp, timestampEnd }` already models a span, but its unique index
  `{device_id, format, timestamp, duration}` collides for two user films over the same range, and a 60 s
  1080p film exceeds the 16 MB BSON limit. **Ship a separate `Film` collection on GridFS.**
- **Codec: H.264 High profile, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-movflags +faststart`, 25 fps, CRF 23.** Plays
  everywhere. The existing x265 stills-timelapses keep working for in-app playback but every *produced*
  film is H.264. This is a bug fix, not a feature.
- Download sets `Content-Disposition: attachment;
  filename="TerpControl_Zelt-1_Gelato_2026-06-15_2026-08-26.mp4"` — slugified `device.name` and
  `plant.strain`, ISO dates, no spaces, umlauts transliterated.
- **`[ ⤴ ]` uses `navigator.share({ files: [File] })`.** On iOS Safari 15+ and Android Chrome this opens
  the OS share sheet: camera roll, Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp. That is the honest answer to "a share
  that produces a URL to a login page is not a share". Fallback when `navigator.canShare` is false: the
  `[ Speichern ]` button, a plain `<a download>` to the token URL.
- `imageToken` (30 days, deliberately URL-embeddable, `auth.service.ts:229`) makes the URL work in a
  chat app without a login. Say so plainly in the UI: `Der Link funktioniert 30 Tage ohne Anmeldung.`

### 8.5 The camera has an alarm

New synthetic sensor evaluated in the existing alarm loop: `sensorType: 'kamera_alter'`, value = minutes
since the newest `jpeg`. Default alarm created when a camera is paired: `upperThreshold: 720`,
`actionType: 'info'`, cooldown 24 h. Copy: `Kamera meldet sich seit 12 Std nicht.` The whole existing
alarm machinery (email / webhook / info, cooldown, retrigger, resolve) is reused. Half a day of server
work for the camera critic's fourth complaint.

### 8.6 Image correlation

- **A frame's label IS its conditions.** `<tc-etikett>` under any frame:
  `Kamera · Di 09:14 · 24,1 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,12 · Licht 100 % · Tag 41 · Anna war hier`. Open it for
  raw values, the setpoints in force, the exact capture time and the nearest Action.
- **`Anna war hier`** appears when an `Action` carrying an `actor_id` sits within ±10 min of the frame;
  tap to jump to it. This is image correlation *with people in it* — what a club needs, and what no
  other concept in this bake-off can produce.
- **One cursor.** The Filmstreifen shares the ECharts `axisPointer`: scrub the chart and the strip
  scrolls; tap a thumbnail and the crosshair and Ablesekopf move. Delivery is **one sprite sheet + JSON
  index per window** (Mux's storyboard pattern, 50 tiles under 15 min / 100 above, 256×160) from a new
  `GET /device/:id/filmstreifen?from=&to=` → `{sprite_url, tiles:[{t,x,y}]}`, not N image requests.
  Tiles are positioned by timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so uneven capture reads honestly as a gap.

### 9.2 The double-feed prevention

At save time, before the write, the client queries
`GET /device/:id/actions/recent?type=duengen&plant_ids=A1,A2,A3&within=1200` and, if a match exists:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ⚠  Schon gedüngt                      │
│                                        │

│                                        │

└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The safe path is the big button. `Trotzdem eintragen` is one deliberate extra tap and writes
`despite: { action_id, actor_id, minutes: 90 }`, which renders forever in the record as
`Trotzdem gedüngt — Anna hatte 1 Std 30 vorher gedüngt.` A double feed that was intentional is not a
mistake and the record should not call it one; a double feed that was an accident should have been
stopped by the big button.

Windows per type: `giessen` 6 h, `duengen` 20 h, `blattspray` 12 h, `messung` 0 (never blocks).
Per-plant, not per-tent: watering A4 when A1–A3 were done is normal.

**The soft block is not club-only.** One person who fed at 1am and forgot gets the same prompt, with
`Du hast … gedüngt`. It costs a solo user nothing and saves them the same plant.

### 9.3 Who am I — the honest handling of a sticky label

The FAB name persists in `localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']`.

- **Personal browser** (`Gemeinsames Gerät` unticked, the default): sticks for 12 h of inactivity.
- **Shared tablet by the tent** (ticked): re-arms after 90 min of inactivity. The FAB reads `＋ als …?`
  and the first tap opens the roster picker before the capture sheet.
- Saving with no name selected is allowed and stamps nothing. The row reads
  `Ohne Namensschild · Di 22:10`, in grey, with no fake attribution. Refusing to save is worse than an
  unattributed record.

This is the concept's weakest joint and §13 says so.

### 9.4 Privacy posture

- **Device-scoped and owner-deletable.** `Alle Namensschilder entfernen` nulls `actor_id` on every row
  and deletes every `Actor` — a real one-tap wipe, not an archive. Confirmation: `Alle Namen werden
  entfernt. Die Einträge bleiben, aber ohne Namen. Das lässt sich nicht rückgängig machen.`
- **Never a compliance claim.** No `KCanG-konform` badge, no "§ 17 Nachweis", no export labelled
  `Behörde`. The §9.1 banner is load-bearing and must survive product review.
- **No per-member dispensing, ever.** There is no grams-to-a-person field — Art. 9 GDPR territory for
  data a grow controller has no reason to hold.
- **Harvest weights are opt-in per device**, off by default, behind `Erntegewicht erfassen` with the
  hint `Gewichte und Pflanzenzahlen sind rechtlich heikel. Standardmäßig aus.` Off, the Ernte type has a
  date, a note and photos and no weight field. That resolves Open Question 5 privacy-maximally while
  still leaving a club its numbers.
- **Corrections, not deletions — only if asked.** `Einträge unveränderlich` (default off) makes an edit
  write a `corrects` row instead of mutating. Immutability protects a *regulated* club against its own
  staff and victimises an unregulated grower, so it is opt-in and club-scoped, never global.

### 9.5 The upgrade path to real authorisation

`Actor.linked_user_id` is null in v1 and is the entire migration. When memberships land (a `Membership`
collection + rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207` + loosening ~10 owner-scoped queries), an
invited real account opens the roster and taps `Das bin ich` on their name. From then on only that
account may stamp that label, and the row renders `✓` instead of `●`. **Every historical row keeps its
`actor_id` and needs no migration**: a name tag becomes a name tag backed by a signature, and the
history reads continuously across the boundary. That continuity is the argument for shipping labels
first rather than waiting.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 — Lazy monitoring-only.** Opens the app once a week. Sees the verdict word, three numbers, and
`Gerät · vor 12 Sek`. That is the whole product for them and it fits above the fold. Their tent has no
sockets, so `deviceControlCapability` (fixed to fail *closed*) shows no Soll rows and no dehumidifier
tile — they are never nagged about kit they do not own. The Verlauf tab gives a time-in-range scorecard,
so "war mein Zelt in Ordnung" has an answer that is a sentence, not a chart-reading exercise. They never
add a plant, never meet the roster, never see a FAB with a name on it.

**2 — Same, but with a camera.** The Kamera tab exists for them because `webcam_did` is set. Three
rolling films are ready on day one with no configuration. Six weeks in they tap `Film erstellen`,
accept every default, and two minutes later hold a 30-second H.264 file that plays in their gallery and
goes into Telegram from the share sheet. Every frame's label carries its conditions, so the moment they
ask "was war da los" they tap the frame, not the chart. If the camera stops, the `kamera_alter` alarm
tells them within 12 hours instead of three weeks later.

**3 — Controls certain aspects (CO2 or heating).** Their Zelt screen shows a heater tile and a CO2 tile
because those sockets are paired, and shows neither a dehumidifier nor a humidity Soll row. The chart's
target band is drawn only on the measures they actually steer. The `<tc-etikett>` on a setpoint tells
them who last moved it and what it was — which in a two-person household is the single most-asked
question. Alarm presets are filtered by role, so the "Entfeuchter läuft durchgehend" preset they can
never act on is not offered.

**4 — Full tent.** They own the most sockets and therefore see the most tiles, ordered by the fixed
rank. C50 does not pretend to fix the firmware gap: there are five socket roles, `dehumidifier` doubles
as the cooler, and there is no humidifier path. What C50 adds is that the outputs lane on the chart
shows what each socket actually did, the label under each Soll shows who set it, and the state timeline
makes "cool AND dehumidify is not expressible" visible as a fact instead of a mystery. Honest copy on
the dehumidifier tile in `temp` workmode: `In diesem Modus kühlt diese Steckdose.`

**5 — Closed-loop DIY.** They get no API in v1 and C50 will not claim otherwise. What they get is
`Rohdaten kopieren` inside every label — a clean JSON object with raw value, both clocks, source and
setpoint — plus `client_event_id` on every write, which means the day a POST endpoint appears their
retry logic is already idempotent. The dead controls they currently see (`daynight.floating`,
`lights.maintenanceOn`, `co2.sunsetOff`, `daynight.linearChange` — read by no firmware anywhere) are
removed, because a techie who finds one dead control stops trusting the rest.

**6 — Club.** This is C50's home. Twelve name tags, typed once. Every action stamped. The double-feed
prompt in front of the most expensive mistake in a shared room. A per-plant Konto that answers "how much
Bio-Bloom has A3 had, and from whom". Conditions taped to the tent door digitally. A weekly export that
reconstructs the month. And a banner that refuses to call any of it evidence — which is the difference
between a tool a club uses and a liability it inherits.

**7 — Old device owner.** §10. Their app gets shorter, more honest and slightly more useful (notes with
names), and nothing they rely on moves.

*Capability lenses.* **Stoner**: one verdict word, a 200 px tap pad, a name they already picked, a green
button that says `Speichern als Anna`, and a soft block in front of the mistake they are most likely to
make at 2am. **Normal**: three numbers with Soll under them, photos, a film they can post, a diary that
mostly writes itself. **Techie**: every label opens, every number has a raw value and two clocks, the
chart draws goal against actual with an envelope, and the clipboard works.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The attribution is unauthenticated, and it looks authoritative.** Anyone with the shared login can
   stamp Anna's name. The UI says so once, at the top of `Leute`, and nowhere else — a banner people
   read once and then forget while a screen full of confident names accumulates. If a club ever uses
   this record in a dispute, C50 has helped them build something that reads like evidence and is not.
   The mitigation is honest copy and a refusal to export it as a compliance document; the mitigation is
   not sufficient, and it is the price of the whole concept.

2. **The sticky-name problem has no good answer and I chose a mediocre one.** 12 h stickiness on a
   personal phone silently mis-attributes when two people share a phone. 90 min on a shared tablet still
   mis-attributes for up to 89 minutes. A prompt on every single capture would be correct and would be
   ignored, skipped, or route people to `Ohne Namensschild` — which is the failure mode that destroys
   the feature's value. Every option here is bad; I picked the one that is bad quietly rather than
   loudly, and that is a real criticism.

3. **Notifications cannot be per person, and that is the wall.** The single `owner_id` means Anna cannot
   get her own alert. This is the most-requested household feature in the dossier (§8 P1 #23) and C50
   structurally cannot deliver it. A critic will say the concept solves the cheap half of multi-user and
   declares victory. That is fair. The defence is only that the cheap half is 90 % of the daily value
   and 5 % of the cost, and that the expensive half is not additionally harder afterwards.

4. **The camera plan is the biggest line item and the least certain estimate.** `FrameLabel` on every
   30 s still is a new per-frame CPU cost across every device with a camera; the culling thresholds
   (2,5 σ, 0,18 delta, 0,4× sharpness) are educated guesses that will need tuning against real tents,
   and the first films will have artefacts. Eleven days is optimistic and it is the item most likely to
   slip past October.

5. **A label under every fact is a lot of grey text.** Nine call sites × one extra line each is real
   visual noise, on a product whose promise is *ruhig schlafen*. If the typography is even slightly
   wrong the Zelt screen reads as busy rather than calm, and the concept's core primitive becomes its
   core complaint. C50 mitigates with `--tc-text-*` at 12 px/60 % opacity and a dotted rule above, but
   this needs a real design pass with real German string lengths (German labels run ~25 % longer than
   the English ones sketched here).

6. **Row volume.** Per-plant actions in a club with 3 tents × 12 plants × 2 waterings/week is thousands
   of rows per season on a collection with no server pagination today. Item 11 budgets the fix, but the
   Konto aggregation and the merged Tagebuch stream are new query patterns on an unindexed-ish
   collection and the first slow-query report will come from a club, i.e. the most important customer.

7. **Deleting an actor is a genuine dilemma with no clean resolution.** Keep the name and the deletion
   was a lie; null it and the club's record loses the thing it was for. C50 chooses the wipe (privacy
   wins) and therefore ships a feature whose most valuable data the owner can destroy in two taps —
   including data other members contributed.

8. **`Ohne Namensschild` will be commoner than the design assumes**, because people skip the picker. A
   record that is 30 % unattributed is worse than none — it invites false confidence about the other
   70 % — and v1 has no measurement that would tell us it is happening.

9. **The regime seed list is six entries and the owner said "..."**. The seventh brand is a
   content-collection row someone must source from an official chart and verify — ongoing editorial work
   with no owner assigned, and the `zuletzt geprüft` date the UI proudly shows starts going stale the
   month after launch.
