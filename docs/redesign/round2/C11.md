# C11 — "Der Film"

**Family:** camera · **Centre of gravity:** the timelapse is the product, not a tile.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The grow is a film, the app is its editor: the timelapse is the primary artifact Terp Control
produces, sensor data is its subtitle track, and everything the grower does is a mark on the strip.**

Every other concept treats the camera as evidence attached to a chart. This one inverts it. The picture
is what a grower actually wants to look at, show a friend, and still have in three years; the numbers are
what explain the picture. So the home screen is the film-so-far with a play button, the chart is the
soundtrack that moves under the playhead, and the diary is a clapperboard — you mark a take when you do
something to the tent. The product's success condition is brutally concrete: **on 1 October 2026 a person
who bought the camera bundle can, on day 60, tap one button and end up with a watchable 60-second mp4 in
their phone.** Not a URL behind a login. A file.

That forces the concept to solve the thing all ten round-one concepts skipped: a raw tent timelapse is
mostly garbage. Black frames, a hand in shot, the door open, condensation, the camera nudged on day 12,
magenta LED against daylight. **Making it watchable is the engineering, and it is where the differentiation
lives** — because Terp Control owns the lamp, and therefore knows, for every single frame, whether the
light was on, at what dimming level, and whether a human had just logged that they were standing in the
tent. No camera vendor has that. It turns an unsolved computer-vision problem into a database join.

## 4. NO-MODES MECHANISM — **pause is the disclosure gesture**

There is one screen. **What you see depends on whether the film is running, and that is a property of the
playhead, not of you.**

- **Playing** → picture, one verdict sentence, three numbers. That is the entire surface. Nothing to read,
  nothing to decide, one hand, dark tent, 2am.
- **Paused** (tap the frame, or stop scrubbing) → the frame under the playhead **unfolds downward** into
  everything known about that minute:

```
        [ frame at 20.08. 13:24 ]
  ╭─────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  │ 20.08.2026 13:24 · Tag 34 · Blüte            │
  │                                              │
  │ Temperatur   25,4 °C     Ziel 25,0    +0,4   │
  │ Luftfeuchte  58 %        Ziel 50 %    +8     │  ← tap a row = that series, this window
  │ VPD          1,21 kPa    Band 1,2–1,5   ✓    │
  │ CO₂          940 ppm     Ziel 1000    −60    │
  │                                              │
  │ Läuft: Licht 100 % · Entfeuchter an          │  ← only sockets that exist
  │ Klappen ±2 Std: Gießen 6,0 l (Anna, 12:05)   │
  │                                              │
  │ Bild: belichtet ×1,04 · WB-Regime „Licht     │  ← the techie's floor. always present.
  │ 100 %" · Schärfe 0,71 · behalten             │
  │ [ Rohdaten ]  [ Bild verwerfen ]             │
  ╰─────────────────────────────────────────────╯
```

**Why this is not a mode, stated for the critic who will accuse it of being one:**

1. **Nothing is persisted.** There is no preference, no `localStorage` key, no onboarding question, no
   segment control. The app is never "in" the dense state between sessions.
2. **It is reversible by the identical gesture**, in under 200 ms, from anywhere, and it self-cancels when
   you press play. A mode you cannot get stuck in and never chose is not a mode.
3. **It hides no feature class.** The panel is not "advanced settings" — it is the *data about the frame
   you are looking at*. The techie and the stoner get the same panel; one reads three rows of it, the other
   reads eleven. Nobody is denied a number and nobody is forced to read one.
4. **The gesture is borrowed, not invented.** Every human on earth knows that stopping a video means "I
   want to look at this bit". We are not teaching a UI convention; we are inheriting one.

Three reinforcing non-mode mechanisms carry the rest of the product:

- **Reveal by capability.** CO₂ appears only with the SCD4x. Socket rows appear only for paired roles
  (`parseSocketRoles`, per-role, not the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()` that fails open today).
  The camera section is absent without `cloudSettings.rtspStream` or `hardwareInfo.webcam_did`.
- **Reveal by data.** No `Plant` rows → no plant picker anywhere, ever. No second `Actor` → no name chips,
  no attribution, no double-feed warning. **The club feature *is* the existence of a second name.** No
  second `Run` → no run picker. Nothing is hidden; it does not exist yet.
- **Progressive precision, three levels deep, always in the same direction (down).** Verdict → number →
  raw series. Each level is one tap and every level is on the same screen or one push.

**Explicitly rejected**: any `ion-segment` labelled Einfach/Experte (the existing
`localStorage['app-settings-expert']` precedent is deleted, not renamed), any "Mehr anzeigen" that hides a
feature class, any onboarding question about experience.

## 5. The chart — "Verlauf", the soundtrack

The film's playhead is a single `PlayheadService` (`BehaviorSubject<number>`). The strip writes to it, the
chart writes to it, both render from it. **Pressing play on the Film tab and switching to Verlauf shows the
crosshair sweeping.** That is what "sensor data is the soundtrack" means operationally: it moves with the
picture, on the same clock.

```
  ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  ← Filmstreifen: sprite thumbnails placed by timestamp
  └──┬──────────────┬────┘     uneven capture = visible gaps, not stretched tiles
     │              ⋮ playhead
  ┌──┴──────────────┬────┐
  │ Temperatur  °C  │ 25,4│   pinned scrub header (no tooltip — there is no hover on touch)
  │ ░░░ Zielband ░░░│     │   markArea = target band, markLine dashed = stepped setpoint
  │ ─── ist ────────│     │   solid actual + signed deviation fill between the two
  ├─────────────────┴─────┤
  │ Luftfeuchte %         │   small multiple, ~110 px, shared x-axis, linked crosshair
  ├───────────────────────┤
  │ VPD kPa (Band wandert │   band changes along x as the run changes stage
  │ mit der Phase)        │
  ├───────────────────────┤
  │ Licht ███████░░░░███  │   state-timeline lane, not a 0/100 line
  │ Entfeuchter ░░█░░█░░  │   region length = duration; null renders as a gap
  ├───────────────────────┤
  │ ▪ ▪▪   ▪    ▪▪    ▪   │   annotation rail, 2 rows max: user Klappen / device events
  └───────────────────────┘
     Nacht ▨▨▨   Tag ░░░     day/night from measured out_light>0, fallback daynight schedule
```

Concretely, per the dossier §7.2: ECharts 6 `markArea`/`markLine`, `sampling:'minmax'` (never LTTB on the
primary — a bang-bang controller's oscillation is the signal), min/max envelope, `axisPointer.link` across
grids, `darkMode:'auto'`. Day/night bands are drawn from **measured `out_light`** with the configured
`daynight` schedule as fallback and an explicit timezone, so a failed contactor shows as a *missing band*.
Above the chart, an AGP-style scorecard: `Im Ziel 82 % · zu warm 11 % · zu kalt 7 % · längste Abweichung
3 Std 20`, split Tag/Nacht, greyed out below 80 % data coverage, **computed server-side on raw Influx
samples** (averaging destroys the excursions the metric exists to count).

Annotations: Klappen are the rail's row 1, device events row 2, clustered at 10 px with count badges;
stage changes are full-height dashed lines. A day-of-run x-axis toggle (`Tag 1…Tag 84`) — the C10 primitive,
borrowed as permitted.

**Honest scoping:** the ECharts migration does **not** land by October (§12). In v1 the Verlauf page is the
existing Highcharts page with the filmstrip lane, the shared playhead, the pinned scrub header and the
day/night shading added. Setpoint bands, small multiples and the scorecard land in v1.1 with ECharts.

## 6. The diary and multi-plant — "Klappen"

A **Klappe** is a clapperboard mark: a stamp on the film saying what happened at this frame. It replaces
the five hard-coded diary categories with one entity that has a `kind`.

```ts
// all cloud-side Mongo, all fields optional except the four keys — no migration tool exists (§3.5)
interface Run {                     // "Rolle" — one grow run = one reel
  run_id: string; device_id: string; name?: string;      // default "Rolle 3"
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stageChangedAt: number;
  flipAt?: number;                  // 18/6 → 12/12, detected from out_light history
  medium?: string;                  // 'biobizz-allmix'  (medium + regime, §7)
  regime?: string;                  // 'biobizz-biobloom'
  coverImageId?: string; createdBy?: string; createdAt: number;
}
interface Plant {
  plant_id: string; run_id: string; device_id: string;
  label: string;                    // "A1"
  strain?: string;                  // plain text only. no brand DB, no SeedFinder (dead since 2024).
  source?: 'seed' | 'clone'; potLitres?: number;
  startedAt: number; removedAt?: number;
}
interface Mark {                    // "Klappe"
  mark_id: string; device_id: string; run_id?: string; t: number;
  kind: 'note'|'water'|'feed'|'train'|'measure'|'stage'|'harvest'|'photo';
  plantIds?: string[];              // ABSENT = whole tent. this is the untaxing.
  actorId?: string; actorName?: string;
  text?: string; images?: string[];
  water?: { taps?: number; canLitres?: number; litres?: number; runoff?: boolean;
            phIn?: number; ecIn?: number; phOut?: number; ecOut?: number };
  feed?:  { regimeId?: string; stepId?: string; litres?: number; ec?: number; ph?: number;
            items: { product: string; mlPerL?: number; gPerL?: number; totalMl?: number }[] };
  measure?: Partial<DiaryEntryData>;
  clientMarkId?: string;            // idempotency key — an offline retry cannot double-log
  createdAt: number; editedAt?: number; deletedAt?: number;   // deletedAt actually deletes
}
interface Condition {               // "Zettel" — the standing fact pinned to the tent door
  condition_id: string; device_id: string; run_id?: string; plantIds?: string[];
  text: string; openedBy?: string; openedAt: number; closedBy?: string; closedAt?: number;
}
interface Actor {                   // device-scoped "who is at the tent". NOT an account.
  actor_id: string; device_id: string; name: string; colour: string;
  createdAt: number; archivedAt?: number;
}
```

**Back-compat, cheaply:** every saved `Mark` is *mirrored* one-way into `DeviceLog` with today's
`categories: ['diary', 'diary-plant-log' | …]`, so the existing diary page, grow report, log viewer and
every live share link keep working untouched while the new surfaces read `Mark`. `DeviceLog` becomes a
read-model. `convertEventsToGrowCycles()` runs **once**, as a boot-time backfill that creates `Run` rows,
and is then deleted from the browser — the string-comparison cycle inference that forks history on a
rename dies with it.

**How a plant-ignorer is untaxed.** `Mark.plantIds` absent means the whole tent, and absent is the default.
The plant picker row **is not rendered** until a `Plant` row exists for the run. Nothing ever prompts you
to create one; the only way in is tapping `+ Pflanzen` in the run editor, which is itself only visible in
the run editor. A single-plant grower goes their whole life without seeing the word Pflanze. A club with
twelve plants taps `A1 A2 A3` chips in the Klappe sheet and gets per-plant history for free.

## 7. Watering & fertilising

**Capture flow.** FAB (clapperboard icon, aria-label `Eintrag hinzufügen`) → a sheet, five big targets:

```
  Klappe – was ist passiert?
  [ 💧 Gießen ] [ 🧪 Düngen ] [ 📷 Foto ] [ ✎ Notiz ] [ 📐 Messen ]
```

**Gießen — the tap counter.** No keyboard. One hand.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Gießen                        Abbrechen   │
│                                            │
│    ╭────────────────────────────────────╮  │
│    │  ● ● ● ○ ○ ○ ○ ○                   │  │  ← TAP HERE. one tap = one can.
│    │  3 Kannen · 6,0 l                  │  │     long-press = −1. hold = repeat.
│    ╰────────────────────────────────────╯  │     72 px tall, full width.
│                                            │
│  Kanne  2,0 l ▾        ☐ bis Ablauf        │  canLitres remembered per device
│                                            │
│  Wer?   ( Anna ) ( Chris )                 │  ← only if ≥2 Actor rows exist
│  Pflanzen  [Ganzes Zelt] [A1][A2][A3]      │  ← only if Plant rows exist
│                                            │
│  ⚠ Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gegossen.   │  ← the double-feed soft block
│    [ Trotzdem eintragen ]                  │
│                                            │
│               [ Speichern ]                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Stored: `water.taps: 3`, `water.canLitres: 2`, `water.litres: 6`, `plantIds`, `actorId`, `t`. Litres are
derived and shown, never typed. pH/EC in and out are two collapsed rows under the counter — present, not
in the way (progressive precision, not a mode).

**Düngen — the regime prefills.** The scheme is chosen **once**, on the run, as a `medium + regime` pair
labelled the way growers say it out loud:

```
  Medium & Dünger      (Rolle 3)
  ( Biobizz Light-Mix ) ( Biobizz All-Mix ) ( Floragard Light ) ( eigenes )
  ( Green House Feeding ) ( BioTabs ) ( kein Schema )
```

The sheet then opens already knowing the answer:

- All-Mix at day 12 → **`All-Mix ist stark vorgedüngt. Woche 2 – noch nicht düngen.`** and the product list
  empty with `[ Trotzdem düngen ]`. A scheme that cannot say "don't feed yet" is not modelling the domain.
- Light-Mix at day 16 → products prefilled, `Bio-Grow 2 ml/l`, `Bio-Heaven 2 ml/l`; the litres come from the
  **same tap counter**, and the total is the big number: **`Bio-Grow 2 ml/l × 6,0 l = 12 ml`**.
- BioTabs → the "schedule" is `Nachtabben fällig in 9 Tagen`, not a dose table.
- Every prefilled value is editable. Deviating is the normal case.

**Schedule following advances by feed events, not the calendar.** `FeedRegimeState {run_id, stepIndex,
advancedAt}` increments when a `feed` Mark saves; a soft calendar mapping produces `fällig seit 2 Tagen`,
which becomes subtitle rule 5 on the Film tab. That is the stoner's "it thinks for me / it reminds me" and
the club's audit trail, from one row.

**Double-feed prevention (the club critic's gap 1).** On save, query Marks of kind `water`/`feed` within a
window (`water` 6 h, `feed` 20 h) whose `plantIds` intersect this one's (whole-tent intersects everything).
If a *different* actor matches → the warning above, soft-blocked, one deliberate extra tap. If the *same*
actor matches → informational, not blocking (`Du hast vor 40 Min gegossen.`) because self-repeats are
usually mis-taps. Window lengths live in a Mongo `filmProfile` doc, tunable without a deploy.

**Cumulative recall (gap 3).** `GET /run/:run_id/summary` → shown on the run header and on any plant chip:
`Rolle 3 · 46 l gegossen · 8× gedüngt · Bio-Grow 96 ml · zuletzt Anna, 20.08.`

**Zettel (gap 4).** `+ Zettel` in the Klappe sheet writes a `Condition`. Open Zettel pin to the top of the
Film tab as the strip in §3, max 3 with a `✓ 2` overflow. Tap → `Erledigt`. Not an event, not a task: an
open fact with an owner and a close.

**Cellar capture (gap 5).** `clientMarkId` is a uuid minted in the browser; `POST /mark` upserts on it. A
service worker (`@angular/service-worker`, a genuinely new dependency and build change — flagged) queues
saves and replays them. Because the server upserts, a replayed queue cannot produce the one thing that is
indistinguishable from a double feed in a shared record.

## 8. Camera, timelapse and image correlation — the headline

### 8.1 What exists today (verified by reading `server/src/services/image.service.ts`)

One still every 30 s via ffmpeg from RTSP or the O-KAM P2P tunnel; stored as `Image{format:'jpeg'}` Buffers
in Mongo; thinned on an age ladder (1 min after a day → 1 h after 90 days); deleted after 3 years. Three
rolling timelapses rebuilt hourly at `1d`/`1w`/`1m` with frame intervals 2 min / 14 min / 60 min.

**Two shipping defects that make "watchable" false today, both in `convertRtspStreamImagesToVideo`:**

```
-vcodec libx265 -crf 30          ← HEVC in MP4 does not play in Firefox anywhere, nor in
                                   Chrome on Linux/most Windows. Half the audience sees nothing.
(no -pix_fmt)                    ← MJPEG input yields yuvj420p/yuvj444p; many players and every
                                   upload pipeline reject it.
(no -movflags +faststart)        ← moov atom last: no progressive playback, no preview card.
```

Fixing those three flags is **half a day and is the single highest-value change in this entire document.**

### 8.2 The content pipeline — four stages

**Stage A · at capture (adds ~15 ms to an existing 30 s job).** For every stored still, write one small
sibling document. Kept out of `Image` deliberately so scoring queries never drag Buffers.

```ts
interface FrameMeta {
  image_id: string; device_id: string; timestamp: number;
  lum: number; lumP05: number; lumP95: number;        // 64-bin histogram over a 320 px grey raw buffer
  rMean: number; gMean: number; bMean: number;        // sharp .stats()
  sharpness: number;                                  // stdev after a 3×3 Laplacian .convolve()
  dhash: Buffer;                                      // 8 bytes, 9×8 grey difference hash
  motion: number;                                     // Hamming(dhash, previous dhash), 0..64
  lightPct?: number;                                  // out_light at capture — WE OWN THE LAMP
  workmodeOff?: boolean; maintenance?: boolean;
  verdict: 'keep'|'dark'|'dupe'|'human'|'blur'|'flash'|'maint'|'manual-out';
  reason?: string;
}                                                     // index { device_id: 1, timestamp: 1 }
```

`lightPct` is the whole trick. Every competitor has to *infer* whether the light was on. We read it off the
device status we already receive over MQTT at the moment of capture.

**Stage B · verdict.** In order; thresholds live in the tunable `filmProfile`:

1. `maint` — maintenance mode or `workmode==='off'` at capture. Visible as a gap, not silently skipped.
2. `dark` — `lightPct < 5` **and** `lum < 25`. Two conditions, so a grower whose lamp is *not* on our socket
   does not lose the whole film. Without `lightPct`, fall back to `lum < 25` alone.
3. `flash` (door open / torch / daylight) — `lum` deviates > 3.5 × the rolling MAD of the last 60 kept
   frames, or `lumP95 > 250 && (max−min channel mean) < 0.15 × lum`.
4. **`human` — solved without a person detector.** A human in a tent produces a *bounded burst*: frames
   `n…n+k` each differ from frame `n−1` by `motion > 18`, and frame `n+k+1` differs from `n−1` by `< 6`
   again. The whole burst is dropped. This catches the hand, the watering can, the door, the phone torch,
   somebody adjusting a clip fan. It runs at render time over `FrameMeta` alone — no pixels touched.
   **Plus, free: any frame within ±3 minutes of a Klappe is dropped, because a human told us they were
   standing there.** Nobody else can do this because nobody else has the diary and the camera on one clock.
5. `blur` — `sharpness < 0.35 ×` the 24 h rolling median. If it is *also* below 0.5 × the 7-day median for
   6 h, this escalates to the condensation alarm (§8.5).
6. `dupe` — `Hamming(dhash, last kept) < 4` **and** the last kept frame is newer than the target spacing.
   Never culls if it would open a gap wider than 2 × spacing.
7. else `keep`.

**Stage C · normalisation, per selected frame, in one `sharp` pipeline (~25 ms).**

- **Deflicker (this is what actually makes 12 weeks watchable).** Target luminance `L*` = the median `lum`
  of kept frames in the run. Apply `.linear(a, b)` with `a = clamp(L*/lum, 0.75, 1.35)`, `b` set so `lumP05`
  maps to ≥ 4 (no crushed blacks). Then clamp the *change* in `a` versus the previously rendered frame to
  **±3 %**. That temporal clamp is the deflicker; it is three lines and it removes the strobing that makes
  every DIY tent timelapse unwatchable.
- **White balance per lighting regime, not per frame.** Grey-world per frame fights a magenta LED and
  produces colour crawl. A *regime* is a contiguous run of frames sharing a `lightPct` bucket (0 / 1–49 /
  50–99 / 100). Per regime, gains from the regime's median channel means: `gR = gMed/rMed`,
  `gB = gMed/bMed`, **clamped to [0.6, 1.8]** so a purple grow still looks purple. Applied with `.linear()`.
  Day 1 and day 84 under the same lamp then match. Frames under a *different* light (door open) were culled
  in stage B, so they never enter a regime.
- **Stabilisation, translation only.** Cross-correlate a 64×48 grey thumbnail against the reference frame's
  over a ±6 px integer search (169 candidates × 3072 px ≈ 500 k ops, under 2 ms). Median-filter `(dx,dy)`
  over 5 frames so real plant motion does not jitter the crop; `.extract()` an 8 px-inset crop shifted by
  `(−dx,−dy)`; resize back. **Rotation is not corrected.** Instead: a sustained shift beyond the 8 px inset
  for > 6 h re-baselines the reference and inserts a **hard cut with a title card** —
  `Kamera neu ausgerichtet · Tag 12`. A film problem gets a film-language answer, and it is honest.
- Resize `fit:'cover'` to the output size, `.jpeg({quality: 92, mozjpeg: true})`.
- **Subtitle burn (default on).** An SVG composited bottom-left: `Tag 34 · Blüte · 25,4 °C · 58 % · VPD 1,21`,
  date bottom-right, on a 55 %-black plate. Composited with `sharp`, not `drawtext` — the container already
  ships `ttf-dejavu` for the offline caption and an SVG composite has no fontconfig fragility.

**Stage D · pacing and frame choice — the part that decides whether it is *good*.**

- **One canonical frame per day at the photoperiod midpoint ±30 min.** Leaves pray at midday and droop at
  lights-off; a timelapse mixing both twitches. Fixing the phase of the sample removes the twitch. Costs
  nothing, and only we can do it, because `daynight` and `out_light` are ours.
- **Time is spent on the interesting part.** Film length is allocated by stage, not by wall clock:
  germination+seedling 10 %, vegetative 30 %, flowering 50 %, drying+curing 10 %. Within a stage, frames are
  evenly spaced over the `keep` set, extra frames taken at 25 % and 75 % of the photoperiod as budget allows.
- Defaults: **60 s at 24 fps = 1440 frames**. Over an 84-day run, ~17 frames/day.
- **A Klappe's own photo is never culled** and is force-inserted at its timestamp. The day you topped the
  plant survives the cull, because you photographed it.

### 8.3 The export — "Der Schnitt"

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Der Schnitt                     Abbrechen  │
│                                             │
│  Zeitraum   ( Heute ) ( Woche ) (●Ganze     │
│             Rolle ) ( eigener Zeitraum )    │
│  Länge      ( 30 s ) (● 60 s ) ( 2 Min )    │
│  Größe      ( 480p ) (● 720p ) ( 1080p* )   │  *nur wenn die Kamera es hergibt
│  ☑ Untertitel einbrennen (Tag, Klima)       │
│                                             │
│  1 412 Bilder aus 2 380 · 968 aussortiert   │  ← the cull, stated honestly
│  ▸ 611 dunkel · 244 doppelt · 98 Besuch     │
│    · 15 unscharf                            │
│                                             │
│              [ Film erstellen ]             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        ↓
  Wird erstellt … 40 %          (poll every 2 s)
        ↓
  Fertig · 1:00 Min · 14,2 MB
  [ ▶ Ansehen ]  [ ⤓ Speichern ]  [ ↗ Teilen ]  [ 🔗 Link ]
```

**Server side.** New `FilmRender {render_id, device_id, run_id?, from, to, spec{fps,longEdge,subtitles,
lengthSec}, status:'queued'|'rendering'|'ready'|'failed', progress, frameCount, culled{dark,dupe,human,blur},
bytes, durationMs, path, error?, createdBy, createdAt, expiresAt}`, worker with `pLimit(2)` alongside the
existing `ffmpegLimit`.

**The mp4 does not go in Mongo.** A 12-week 720p film is 12–20 MB, and the 16 MB BSON cap plus loading whole
Buffers into Node is exactly the latent problem the current mp4 path already has. Renders are written to a
`RENDER_DIR` docker volume and streamed with Range support. (GridFS is the alternative if ops refuses a
volume; say which, do not leave it undecided.)

**Encode, verbatim:**

```sh
ffmpeg -y -loglevel error -threads 2 \
  -framerate 24 -f image2 -i "$DIR/%05d.jpg" \
  -vf "scale='min(1280,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,format=yuv420p" \
  -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -level 4.0 -preset medium -crf 21 \
  -g 48 -movflags +faststart \
  -metadata title="Terp Control – Rolle 3 · Tag 1–68" \
  "$DIR/out.mp4"
```

`high@4.0` + `yuv420p` plays on every iPhone, every Android, every browser, WhatsApp and Telegram.
`+faststart` gives progressive playback and an unfurl preview. `min(1280,iw)` never upscales a 640×360 cam.

**Filename** (ASCII only — umlauts are folded ä→ae; WhatsApp, Telegram and Android download managers mangle
non-ASCII, and in a camera roll the filename is the only label):

```
terpcontrol_rolle-3_2026-06-14_2026-08-20_720p.mp4
```

`^[a-z0-9_-]{1,100}\.mp4$`, run slug from `Run.name`, ISO dates, resolution suffix.

**How it reaches a camera roll — four paths, ranked, all specified.**

1. **`navigator.share({files})` — the real answer, ships v1.** `fetch(url) → blob → new File([blob],
   filename, {type:'video/mp4'})`, guarded by `navigator.canShare?.({files:[f]})`. On iOS 15+ the share
   sheet's **"Video sichern"** puts it straight in Photos; it also offers WhatsApp/Telegram/Signal directly.
   On Chrome Android likewise. **Hard cap at 50 MB** — above that the Blob fetch is a memory risk on a
   mid-range phone — falling through to (2). CORS: the API already runs `cors()` with no options and the
   `token` query param means no credentials are needed, so the cross-origin fetch works.
2. **Download, ships v1.** `GET /film/:render_id.mp4?token=<imageToken>`, `Content-Type: video/mp4`,
   `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="terpcontrol_…mp4"; filename*=UTF-8''…`,
   `Accept-Ranges: bytes` with real Range handling, `Cache-Control: private, max-age=604800`. The UI does a
   `window.location.href = url` inside the tap handler (an `<a download>` is unreliable cross-origin).
   The `imageToken` is the existing 30-day URL-embeddable token, so this works with no session cookie.
   **Honest UI copy on iOS, shown once:** `Auf dem iPhone landet der Film in „Dateien". Von dort:
   Teilen → „Video sichern".` On Android it lands in /Downloads and Google Photos picks it up automatically.
3. **Filmlink — a share that is actually a share, ships v1.** `ShareLink.page` gains `'film'` (extending an
   existing enum, back-compatible). `https://terpcontrol.cloud/f/<share_id>` is an **unguarded** page: a
   `<video controls playsinline>`, the run name, the date range, a big `Herunterladen` button, and
   `og:video`/`twitter:player` tags so it unfurls with an inline player in Telegram and Discord. No login,
   no device_id in the URL, default expiry 30 days, revocable from `/shares`. Actor names and Klappe text
   are stripped unless the owner ticks `Klappen mitzeigen`.
4. **Capacitor native write to Photos — later, not v1.** `Filesystem.writeFile` + `@capacitor/share`.
   `capacitor.config.ts` is untouched scaffold today and there are no `android/`/`ios/` projects.

**Cost, honestly:** 1440 frames × ~25 ms sharp + ~15 s x264 ≈ **90 s of one core per 60 s film**. At 150
units and one render per user per week that is nothing. `FrameMeta` is the real cost: 150 devices × 2880
captures/day × 15 ms ≈ **1.8 CPU-hours/day**, fine on one server, needs a queue before 5 000 devices.
Renders expire after 30 days unless the user ticks `behalten`.

### 8.4 Image correlation

- **The strip is an axis, not a widget.** Thumbnails are positioned by timestamp on the chart's x-scale, so
  an uneven capture interval reads as a gap rather than being stretched.
- **Delivery is one sprite, not 100 requests.** `GET /film/strip/:device_id?from&to&cols=10` returns a single
  JPEG of up to 100 tiles at 160×90, plus `…/strip.json` = `[{t,x,y}]`. Cached in a `FilmStrip` collection
  with a 24 h TTL index. This is what makes scrubbing feel instant on a phone over tent wifi.
- **One cursor, two views.** `PlayheadService` is the single state; the strip and the chart are both
  projections of it. Drag either, both move.
- **Picture → numbers.** Tapping a thumbnail pauses and opens the §4 panel. New endpoint
  `GET /data/at/:device_id?t=…&measures=…` returns the nearest **raw** sample per measure plus the setpoint
  in force at that instant. (This must land *after* the Flux-injection fix in `data.service.ts:80-89` —
  dossier P0 #8 — because it widens the measure vocabulary reaching Flux.)
- **Numbers → picture.** In the paused panel, `Nächster Moment über 30 °C →` jumps the playhead to the
  nearest kept frame at the next excursion. Correlation in both directions, which is the owner's actual ask.

### 8.5 Camera alarms — the critic's fourth gap

Two checks in the existing hourly `compressRtspStreams` loop, both cheap:

- **Stalled.** Device has `cloudSettings.rtspStream` and the newest `format:'jpeg'` is older than **3 h** →
  a `DeviceLog {title:'message-camera-stalled', severity:1, categories:['webcam','error']}` once per 12 h,
  and it fires any user alarm bound to it via the existing `alarmService`. Surfaces as the red strip in §3.
  A camera that died three days into flower now tells you instead of waiting to be noticed.
- **Blurry.** Kept-frame `sharpness` under 50 % of the 7-day median for 6 h →
  `message-camera-blurry` → `Kamera unscharf – vermutlich beschlagen.` Amber, not red.

## 9. Clubs

- **Attribution without an auth rewrite.** The owner types names once (`Zelt → Wer ist am Zelt`), creating
  `Actor` rows. The Klappe sheet then shows coloured name chips; the last-used is preselected, sticky in
  `localStorage['tc-actor-<device_id>']`. `actorId`/`actorName` are stamped at capture. **~2 days, no
  `auth.middleware.ts` surgery.**
- **Attribution is a label; authorisation is a gate — and we say so in the product, once**, under the actor
  list: `Namen sind eine Notiz, keine Anmeldung. Wer das Passwort hat, kann jeden Namen wählen.` Anything
  else would be a lie about a record people might rely on. Real `Membership {device_id, user_id, role}` plus
  rewriting `auth.middleware.ts:172`/`:207` is v1.1; `Actor` gains an optional `user_id` and upgrades in
  place with no data migration.
- **The club feature IS the second name.** One `Actor` → no chips, no attribution column, no double-feed
  warning, nothing. Two `Actor`s → all of it appears. **That is how a club exists without a club mode.**
- **Double feed:** §7. **Conditions/Zettel:** §7. **Cumulative:** §7.
- **Privacy posture — private by default.**
  - Harvest weight and plant count are **never prompted** and never appear on any shareable surface. They
    exist as optional fields on `Mark{kind:'harvest'}` for the user who types them.
  - The Filmlink strips actor names, Klappe text and all plant labels unless explicitly ticked.
  - No location, ever. No analytics SDK. No community feed (dossier §3.11).
  - `Alles löschen` actually deletes — including images — fixing the `deleted`-means-hidden lie that becomes
    a data-loss bug the moment the diary is the centrepiece.
  - Renders expire in 30 days by default. **The film is the most legible artifact this product has ever
    made**; short-lived and revocable is the only defensible default (§13.9).
  - No `KCanG-konform` badge, no branded footer on user posts (§ 6 KCanG, fines to €30 000).

## 10. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug

`device_type ∈ {fan, light, plug}`. (`dryer` is dead per `AGENTS.md:17`.) They get the **same three tabs**
and **zero settings rewrite**.

- **Film tab.** These devices have no `cloudSettings.rtspStream` and no `hardwareInfo.webcam_did`, so the
  frame area is the **Tagesband**: one horizontal bar per day, coloured green/amber/red by time-in-target,
  newest at the bottom, the same subtitle line underneath. Press play and the playhead sweeps 20 seconds
  across the run while the numbers change. It is a poster, not a video, and the app says so — it exports as
  a **PNG**, not an mp4, and the Schnitt sheet is replaced by `Als Bild speichern`. What each type's
  Tagesband is made of: a `plug` → on/off state timeline; a `light` → the photoperiod and dim level; a `fan`
  → whatever of temp/humidity/rpm has data in the last 7 days.
- **Verlauf tab.** Same chart, only the series the device actually reports — the measure list is
  `VALID_SENSORS ∩ {measures with data in the last 7 days}`, so a plug owner sees two lanes, not fifteen
  empty ones. No filmstrip lane (no frames). Day/night shading only if the device knows about light.
- **Zelt tab.** The **existing** `fan-settings` / `light-settings` / `plug-settings` components, unmodified,
  hosted inside the new shell. That is the whole compatibility promise: new frame, old picture. Their four
  live i18n bugs (`settings.limits.overtemperature.enabled` etc.) get fixed in the naming pass because they
  are three strings.
- **Klappe works.** It is device-scoped, not controller-scoped. `Filter gewechselt` on a fan is a real
  entry, and its photos become that device's film.
- **Nothing** on their screens mentions plants, runs, feeding schemes, VPD, CO₂ or cameras — none of those
  rows exist, because no `Plant`/`Run`/`Actor` row and no camera capability exists. Reveal by data and by
  capability do the entire job here with no type-specific branching.
- **Their existing share links keep resolving** (`page:'charts'|'diary'` redirect into `/verlauf`), and their
  chart presets keep working because the query-string format is extended, not redefined.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough

**1 · Lazy monitoring-only.** No camera, no sockets. Home is the Tagesband: 34 green bars with two amber
ones, `Tag 34 · alles im Ziel · 25,4 °C · 58 %`. He looks at it once a week, sees the two amber days, taps
one, gets the excursion with its duration. He never learns what VPD is and is never asked to. He exports a
PNG for the forum. **He is not the target of this concept and gets a competent consolation prize.**

**2 · Same, with a camera.** This is the concept's protagonist. Day 60 he taps `Ganze Rolle → Film
erstellen`, waits 90 seconds, taps `Teilen`, chooses `Video sichern`, and has a 60-second 720p film in his
camera roll with the day counter burned into the corner. It does not strobe, it does not flash white every
time he opened the door, the plant does not twitch between praying and drooping, and it did not use the 611
black frames. **That is the sale, and it is the thing no competitor ships.**

**3 · Controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating).** He has a `co2` socket and a `heater` socket and nothing
else. His Zelt tab shows exactly two output rows and two target rows; the humidity target and the
dehumidifier alarm preset that today's `deviceControlCapability()` shows him do not appear, because
capability is read per role from `hardwareInfo.sockets`, not from a three-bucket collapse that fails open.
On the chart he gets a `CO₂` lane with a target band and a `CO₂` state-timeline showing how long the
solenoid actually ran. In the paused panel he can see, for any frame, whether CO₂ was injecting.

**4 · Full tent.** Five roles is all the firmware has, and this concept does not pretend otherwise: he sees
his five, plus the PWM `out_light`, and a `Zelt` tab that names honestly which of his kit the controller can
and cannot reach. His win is the state-timeline lane — `Entfeuchter ░░█░░█░░` next to humidity — because
that answers "is it actually running" and "the app says the fan is on but it isn't" is the #1 complaint
across all five competitor brands. His film is the best-looking one, because a fully controlled tent has the
most stable light and therefore the cleanest WB regimes.

**5 · Closed-loop DIY.** He gets the paused panel's bottom rows (`belichtet ×1,04 · WB-Regime · Schärfe ·
behalten`), `Rohdaten`, and — the thing he actually wants — a documented `GET /film/frames/:device_id`
returning `FrameMeta` as JSON, plus `GET /film/:render_id.mp4` with a stable filename and a token he can put
in a cron job. He will build his own timelapse anyway; giving him our per-frame verdicts and letting him
override them (`POST /film/frame/:image_id/verdict`) converts the most sceptical segment into the one that
writes the Home Assistant card for us. **No write API for outputs in v1**, and we say so rather than implying.

**6 · Club.** Two names typed once. Anna waters at 18:40, taps three times, hits save. Chris arrives at
20:10, opens Gießen and reads `Anna hat A1–A3 vor 1 Std 30 gegossen. Trotzdem eintragen?` and doesn't. On
Saturday somebody pins a Zettel `CO₂-Flasche fast leer`, which sits on everyone's home screen until closed.
At the end of the run, `Rolle 3 · 46 l · 8× gedüngt · Bio-Grow 96 ml · zuletzt Anna` reconstructs the run,
and the film of it is the thing they show at the members' meeting. **Nothing about members, grams, THC or
identities is stored** — the club's own need to know who did what, without becoming the state's ledger.

**7 · Old device owner.** §10. His app looks better, his settings page is byte-identical, his share links
still resolve, and nothing new is demanded of him. The only thing he notices is that his fan is no longer
described as a fridge.

## 13. Honest weaknesses

1. **The concept is worth much less without the camera, and the camera is a €30 upgrade.** If the
   attach rate on the 349 €/319 € bundle is a third, two thirds of buyers get the Tagesband consolation
   prize as their home screen. **This is the single biggest strategic risk and no amount of design fixes
   it.** A diary-centric or chart-centric concept degrades far more gracefully.
2. **Culling will throw away something the user wanted, and there is almost no way to notice.** The coverage
   strip shows *that* frames went, not *which*. Force-inserting Klappe photos and the manual
   `Bild verwerfen`/`behalten` override are mitigations, not answers. The `human` burst rule in particular
   will drop a legitimate 40-minute defoliation session — arguably correct, definitely surprising.
3. **`FrameMeta` puts a `sharp` pass on the hot path of every 30-second capture on every device.** 1.8
   CPU-hours/day at 150 devices is fine; at 5 000 it needs a real queue, and the code today has no job
   infrastructure at all (`pLimit` + `setTimeout` recursion is the whole scheduler).
4. **Renders on a disk volume introduce an ops surface this project has deliberately avoided** — a volume,
   a cleanup job, a backup question, and a second place where user data lives besides Mongo and Influx.
5. **Pause-as-disclosure is unusual and untested.** A user who never pauses never sees a setpoint. The
   subtitle carries one number and the panel carries eleven, and the bridge between them is a gesture we did
   not test with anybody. A critic saying "your techie has to discover a gesture to get their data" is right.
6. **"Klappe" is a naming gamble.** In German it also means "shut up". The FAB is icon-only with an
   aria-label for exactly that reason and the word only appears inside a sheet where it can explain itself —
   but if user testing hates it, the fallback string is `Eintrag` and the metaphor survives without it.
7. **We cannot promise one tap to the camera roll on every phone.** Web Share Level 2 with files gets there
   on iOS 15+/Chrome Android under 50 MB; everything else is a download to Files/Downloads plus two taps,
   and the UI has to say so in German instead of pretending.
8. **Stabilisation is translation-only.** A camera that gets rotated produces a title card and a hard cut,
   which is a defensible film choice and will still be read by some users as a bug.
9. **We are building a beautiful, portable, shareable artifact of an activity that is legally fragile in
   several markets.** Short default expiry, stripped names, no branded footer and a private-by-default
   posture are the mitigations; the honest statement is that a downloadable 720p film of a grow tent is a
   worse privacy position than a chart, and we chose it deliberately because the owner says it is the
   biggest selling point.
10. **Multi-user is attribution only.** Anyone with the password can pick anyone's name. Better than nothing
    (today `DeviceLog` has no actor field at all) and worse than a gate; the product says so, which does not
    make it stop being true.
11. **Type 7 for the diary keeper.** A written, searchable, week-bucketed record exists here — but it is a
    list under the Verlauf tab, subordinate to the film. Against a diary-centric concept this loses that
    user, and it should.
12. **Keeping Highcharts in v1 means the goal-vs-actual chart — the dossier's single highest-leverage
    change — is worse in this concept than in a chart-first one for the launch release.** That is a real
    cost paid to buy the film, and it is a defensible trade only if the owner's "biggest selling point"
    judgement is correct.
