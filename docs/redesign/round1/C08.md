# C08 — "Shared Grow" / „Gemeinsamer Grow"

**Design philosophy:** multi-user and attribution are the foundation, not a feature.
**Author's stance:** every grow has at least two actors — a person and a machine. Most have two people.
The app's job is to keep the story of what each of them did, on one timeline, and to hand that story to
whoever opens the door next.

---

## 1. Thesis

**Every entry in Terp Control is signed and time-boxed, so the app can answer the only question a shared
grow ever asks — "what happened while I wasn't here?" — and, as a side effect, tell a solo grower which of
their alarms were caused by their own hand.**

### The big idea

Today's diary is a flat list of atomic rows on one device. That is the wrong unit. What actually happens is
a **visit**: somebody opens the tent at 19:04, waters two plants, tops up the humidifier, notices a drooping
leaf, and closes it at 19:21. Everything in that window belongs together, was done by one person, and —
critically — **is visible in the sensor data**, because opening a tent spikes RH and drops temperature.

So C08 makes the *Visit* (de: „Zeltbesuch") the spine of the product:

1. It is the correct unit of **attribution** — the club's § 17 Abs. 2 "eigenhändig mitwirken" record and the
   household's "who watered on Tuesday" are the same object.
2. It is the correct unit of **handover** — the home screen's top card is not a gauge, it is
   „Seit deinem letzten Besuch" / "Since your last visit".
3. It is a **diagnostic lane on the chart**. A visit is a time *span*, drawn as a bracket. The
   time-in-range scorecard can then say: *"3 of 4 humidity excursions this week overlapped a tent visit"* —
   which converts attribution from bureaucracy into the thing every grower actually wants: an explanation
   for a spike. **A solo user gets that value with zero social machinery.** That is how the concept
   degrades gracefully: the same primitive that serves a 40-member Anbauverein tells a lone grower that
   their dehumidifier is fine and they are the problem.

Second consequence of taking sharing seriously: **people join a *grow*, not a *device*.** A Grow is a
cloud-side object that owns plants, journal, feed schedule and members, and points at zero or more devices.
That one indirection also solves three problems nobody asked it to: the old-device owner with a Terp light
*and* a Terp fan in one tent finally has one journal; a club can start logging before the controller
arrives; and a re-veg stops forking the history, because plant identity is an id, not a string.

Third: **the privacy line is a design element, not a footnote.** This concept stores *actors*, never
*people*. Display names are per-grow pseudonyms. Real names, email addresses, DOB, membership numbers,
grams and dispensing records are refused by design — see §8.3, which enumerates the refusals.

- No avatar stack, no grow switcher (one grow).
- Handover card headline stays „Seit deinem letzten Besuch" but the body loses all names:

### 3.4 Stale / offline / absent — one component, four states

A shared `app-freshness` component owns the tri-state and is the *only* place the rule lives. The rule
matches the existing product definition (a device is offline ten minutes after its last sample):

| State | Condition | Header | Value tiles | Output row | Chart |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **live** | last sample < 2 × expected interval | `● Live` (green) | normal | live states | normal |
| **stale** | 2 × interval … 10 min | `◐ Vor 4 Min.` (amber) | values normal weight, **unit row greys**, timestamp shown under the grid | states shown, dimmed | trailing region hatched |
| **offline** | > 10 min | `○ Offline seit 2 Std.` (grey) | **last known value, dimmed to `--ion-color-step-500`, with an explicit absolute time under it: „19:04"** — never rendered as if current | **„Zustand unbekannt"** — outputs are *unknown*, not off | data ends at the last sample; a grey "no data" band runs to now |
| **absent** | no sample ever | — | „Noch keine Messwerte" | hidden | empty-state with the setup link |

This kills the dossier's P0 #5 ("the app will say a fan is running at 10 speed but the fan will not be
running") and it is nearly free. **The output row is the important half**: today a controller that has been
offline for two hours still draws its last known socket states as fact.

The handover card degrades independently of device state: with no device at all (a grow created before the
controller arrives, or a hand-logged club grow) the „JETZT" block, the webcam block and the „GERÄTE" row
are simply absent, and the home screen is handover + due + journal. This is a legal configuration.

## 4. The chart overhaul — `/grow/:grow_id/chart`

Library: **Apache ECharts 6, tree-shaken, hand-rolled ~50-line Angular directive** (owner decision 2).
Delete `chart.js` / `ng2-charts` / `chartjs-adapter-luxon` in the same commit. Be honest internally: the
bundle grows (195.6 KB gz vs 132.7 KB gz Highstock; Chart.js repays 68.8 KB). It is justified by licence

### 4.2 Goal vs. actual — the exact drawing rule

- **Setpoint**: a dashed **stepped** line (`step: 'end'`), stepping at `daynight.day` / `daynight.night`
  between `day.temperature` and `night.temperature` (likewise humidity). Resolved server-side from
  `device.configuration` and returned on the chart payload so the client does not re-parse a JSON string.
- **Band**, one source at a time, labelled in the panel header so the user knows what they are looking at:
  1. **the user's own alarm thresholds** for that `sensorType` (hard edge) → „Band: deine Alarmgrenzen"
  2. else **setpoint ± tolerance** using the values the grow-assistant already applies (±1.5 °C, ±7 % RH)
     → „Band: Sollwert ± 1,5 °C"
  3. else **the stage's `vpdRange`** for the VPD panel only → „Band: Blüte 1,2–1,5 kPa"
- **Deviation fill** between actual and setpoint, signed: warm tint above, cool tint below, 12 % opacity,
  `markArea` between the two series. **Never fill to the axis** — today's `type:'area', threshold:null`
  encodes nothing at all.
- **Min/max envelope** on the primary measure (`aggregateWindow` already supports `min`/`max`; one combined
  `{mean,min,max}` response replaces three round trips). A single mean line is a lie about a bang-bang
  controller.

### 4.3 Day/night shading

Derived from measured `out_light > 0` where the series exists, falling back to the `daynight` schedule,
with `lights.sunrise` / `lights.sunset` ramp gradients at the edges, **in an explicit timezone stored on
the Grow** (`Grow.timezone`, IANA). `daynight.day`/`night` are seconds-of-day in **UTC**; a 12/12 flip near
a DST boundary otherwise drifts an hour and generates bug reports. A failed contactor then shows as a
*missing band* — a diagnostic, not decoration.

### 4.4 The PERSONEN lane — the concept's own contribution

Each visit is a bracket `├────┤` on its own 22 px lane, tinted with the actor's **person colour** and
labelled with a 2-letter monogram. Tapping a bracket scrubs to it and opens the visit in the journal.

Two rules that make it more than decoration:

1. **Excursion attribution.** The scorecard cross-references excursion intervals against visit intervals
   server-side and reports `excursionsOverlappingVisit / excursions`. The home card surfaces it in one
   sentence. This is the single feature that pays the multi-user machinery back to a solo user.
2. **Honesty about inference.** A visit that was *inferred* (§5.4) is drawn with a dotted bracket and reads
   „vermutet" / "inferred" in the tooltip. A visit the user confirmed is solid. We never present a guess as
   a record — because if the visit log is half-right, the excursion attribution is *misleading*, which is
   worse than absent.

### 4.5 The AKTIONEN rail

Under the x-axis, not inside the plot (Highcharts flags' `stackDistance` does not survive 90 days of logs;
neither would ECharts `markPoint` without help). 16 px, **max two rows**: row 1 = human events, row 2 =
device events. Pixel-proximity clustering in 10 px bins with a count badge. Severity ranking picks the
representative glyph. Category filter chips reuse the existing `collectLogCategories` /
`matchesLogCategory` / `filterLogsByCategory` helpers verbatim.

Glyphs: `●` Gießen · `◆` Düngen · `▲` Training (Topping/LST/Entlauben) · `✚` Messung (EC/pH/Höhe) ·
`■` Beobachtung · `◇` Umtopfen · `★` Ernte. **Lifecycle stage changes are a full-height dashed vertical
line**, labelled once at the top („Blüte").

Tapping any pip on touch opens a bottom sheet with the entry. **No hover anywhere** — the current design's
event columns are readable only by hovering, on a mobile-first product.

### 4.6 Camera film strip

Thumbnails positioned **by timestamp on the same x-scale**, so uneven capture intervals read honestly as
gaps. One shared cursor with the chart: scrubbing moves the strip, tapping a thumbnail scrubs the chart.
`Image.timestampEnd` already marks mp4 timelapses as spanning a range — those render as a bracket, not a
point. Delivery: **v1 ships thumbnail-per-request** (honest: it is N requests and it will be visibly slow
on 30 days); **v1.1 ships one sprite + JSON index per window** (Mux's storyboard pattern, 50 tiles under
15 min of span, 100 above, 256×160 px). I am not going to claim the sprite endpoint ships in October.

### 4.7 Mobile interaction

| Gesture | Result |
| --- | --- |
| one finger drag | **page scrolls** (the page must always scroll) |
| two fingers | pan / zoom (`dataZoom: {type:'inside'}`) |
| tap | place crosshair, fill the pinned scrub header |
| long-press + drag | continuous scrub |
| double-tap | reset zoom to the active range preset |

**The floating tooltip is deleted.** It is replaced by the pinned scrub header above the chart — a compact
row, one entry per panel. There is no hover on touch and a fingertip occludes a tooltip. Range presets stay
the primary control at ≥44 px.

**Axis labels are never hidden.** Today they vanish below 320 px, which produces N overlapping translucent
areas with no axis at all. Instead: reduce to 3 ticks and move the unit into the panel title.

### 4.8 Colour

- **Sensors: Okabe-Ito at full chroma.** `#0072B2` blue = Temperatur, `#56B4E9` sky = Luftfeuchte,
  `#009E73` green = VPD, `#E69F00` orange = CO₂, `#D55E00` vermillion = Blattemperatur,
  `#F0E442` yellow = Lux. Fixes today's `#e05a4e` next to `#b0743c` deuteranopia collision and the
  `Lights`/`Day` and `out_fan`/`out_fan-internal` duplicate hexes.
- **Outputs: neutral.** Grey track, one accent fill for "on". Outputs are state, not quantity.
- **People: a separate 30 %-saturation ramp**, deliberately disjoint from the sensor hues.
  On a chart, hue belongs to physics; people get low-chroma tints plus a monogram. This is a rule, not a
  preference: **a member colour must never be confusable with a measure colour.**
- `darkMode: 'auto'` + `setDarkMode()` replaces the hand-maintained 30-property Highcharts theme and its
  `MutationObserver`.
- Every panel offers **„Als Tabelle anzeigen" / "View as table"** — no OSS chart library matches
  Highcharts' accessibility module, and Terp does not load that module today anyway.

### 4.9 §7.2 techniques — adopted / rejected

| 1 | Small multiples, shared x, linked crosshair | **Adopt.** Few's dual-axis paper is unqualified and Terp currently runs fifteen hidden scales as filled areas. Two units per panel only when physically coupled (temp + leaf temp). |

| 4 | Day/night from `out_light`, explicit timezone | **Adopt.** |
| 5 | Annotation rail under the axis, clustered, 2 rows | **Adopt.** |
| 6 | Outputs as a state timeline | **Adopt.** |

## 5. The multi-plant diary

### 5.1 New entities (all cloud-side MongoDB, keyed as the house style does: `<thing>_id`)

`device.configuration` cannot carry new keys — the firmware strips unknown keys on echo. Everything below
is server-side. Every field is **optional with a read-time normaliser**, because there is no migration
tooling in this repo.


and keeps receiving device/system messages; the journal reads GrowEvents **union** adapted DeviceLog rows.

### 5.2 Migration, not rewrite

`convertEventsToGrowCycles()` (today a 57-line runtime string-comparison algorithm in the browser) becomes
a **one-time idempotent backfill in a service constructor** — the house pattern, since there is no
migrate-mongo:

1. One `Grow` per claimed device, `name = device.name ?? default`, `device_ids = [device_id]`,
   `createdBy = owner_id`, `migratedFrom: 'device'`.
2. One `Membership{role:'owner', displayName: <account's local part>}` per existing `owner_id`.
3. Run the existing cycle inference **once** over `diary-plant-lifecycle` rows; each distinct trimmed
   `lifecycleName` becomes one `Plant` with `stageHistory` assembled from those rows in time order.
   Because identity is now `plant_id`, the rename-forks-history bug and the re-veg-fabricates-a-plant bug
   both disappear at the moment of migration.
4. Existing `DeviceLog` rows are **not moved**. A read-time adapter presents them as GrowEvents with
   `legacyLogId`. The seven legacy English-prose regexes in `log-translate.service.ts` keep working.

Also fixed in the same pass, because the diary becomes the product's centrepiece: **„Protokoll löschen"
currently deletes nothing from the diary** (`deleted` is a visibility flag, and the diary always asks for
hidden rows). `GrowEvent.deletedAt` means deleted.

### 5.3 Creating plants, and how the plantless user is not taxed

- **A grow with zero plants is valid forever.** `plant_ids: []` means "the whole grow", and every capture
  flow defaults to it.
- The plant chip row appears in an entry sheet **only when the grow has ≥2 active plants.**
- With exactly one plant, the plant is implicit and its name never appears in a capture flow — only in the
  report.
- For a monitoring-only user the word „Pflanze" appears in exactly two places in the whole product: one
  skippable onboarding card, and one overflow-menu item.

Creating a plant is one screen: `[+ Pflanze]` → name (optional; a label `A1` is assigned regardless) ·
Sorte (free text) · Herkunft `Samen | Steckling` · Topfgröße (optional) · Phase (defaults to the grow's).

- **Clone:** on a plant, „Steckling nehmen" → count. Creates N plants with `source:'clone'`,
  `motherPlantId`, `startedAt = now`. The mother keeps everything.
- **Re-veg:** just set the stage back. `stageHistory` gets `{stage:'vegetative', at, by}`. No fork, no new
  plant, no broken share link. (Today, cycle identity in URLs *is* the cycle's start timestamp, so editing
  an entry's time breaks share links in circulation — grow-scoped ids end that.)
- **Rename:** `Plant.name` is free. Chart/report fall back to `label` when a name is empty or duplicated.
- **Harvest:** a `type:'harvest'` GrowEvent sets `stage:'harvested'` and `archivedAt`.
  `payload.wetGrams` / `dryGrams` **exist but are never prompted by default** — see §8.3.

## 6. Watering & fertilising

This is the shared operation. It is also the one where the owner named two irreconcilable-sounding
audiences — "the stoners just want something easy that thinks for them" and "the big customers need very
detailed logging" — so both must be the *same entry*, differing only in how much of it is expanded.

### 6.1 Everyday capture — count the taps

**Stoner path: one tap.**

```
Home  →  [ Gegossen ✓ ]
```

That single tap writes a `GrowEvent{type: 'feed' | 'water'}` with:
- `plant_ids: []` (all plants),
- `payload.volumeLitres` = the last volume this grow used,
- `payload.mix` = the active regime's current step, verbatim, if a regime is active (then `type:'feed'` and
  the button reads „Gefüttert ✓"); otherwise plain water,
- `payload.ecTarget` from the step,
- `actor_id` = you, `at` = now,
- and it **opens or extends the implicit visit** (§6.4).

A toast appears for 6 s: „Gespeichert · Ändern". Tapping „Ändern" opens the full sheet with everything
pre-filled. So: **1 tap to log, 2 taps to log and start correcting.** If the due card is showing, the
`[ Erledigt ✓ ]` button on it is the same one tap and additionally advances `Grow.feedStepIndex`.

**Detailed path (club / normal): 4–7 taps** — long-press the button, or Futter → „Neuer Eintrag":

### 6.2 Following a schedule

A **regime is a MEDIUM + FEED LINE pair, labelled the way growers say it out loud** — because the owner's
own examples (Biobizz Light-Mix, Biobizz All-Mix, Floragard Light, Green House Feeding, BioTabs) mix
substrates and feed products, and that is horticulturally correct: All-Mix is heavily pre-fertilised, so
feeding it in week 2 burns plants, while Light-Mix wants feeding from ~week 2, and BioTabs is a
slow-release regime whose "schedule" is re-tabbing every 4–6 weeks, not a weekly ml/L table.

```ts
export interface FeedRegime {                 // MongoDB CONTENT collection — never in the bundle
  regime_id: string;
  label: string;                              // „Biobizz Light-Mix" — plain text, no logo, no brand colour
  mediumKind: 'soil-light' | 'soil-rich' | 'coco' | 'hydro' | 'slow-release' | 'other';
  steps: FeedStep[];
  ecBasis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source';  // CANNA publishes EC+, not EC. Alarming on the raw value is wrong.
  source_url?: string; retrieved_at?: number; chart_version?: string;
  kind?: 'official_pdf' | 'official_calculator' | 'ownerless' | 'user';
  derivedFrom?: string;                       // editing forks a private copy
  owner_id?: string;                          // set only for user copies
}
export interface FeedStep {
  index: number;
  stage: 'veg' | 'flower' | 'flush' | 'none';
  labelKey: string;                           // 'flower-week-3' → translated, NEVER brand prose
  mix: { product: string; amount: number; unit: 'ml/L' | 'g/L' | 'tab' }[];
  ecTarget?: [number, number];
  phTarget?: [number, number];
  note?: 'water-only' | 'flush' | 'retab';
}
```

- **Steps advance by feed events, not by wall clock** — „Schritt 7 von 13" — with a soft calendar mapping,
  because growers feed Tue/Fri and slip. The due card fires on elapsed days since `lastFedAt`.
- **The closed loop nobody else can build:** a photoperiod change in `out_light` history (18/6 → 12/12) is
  detected server-side and offers, once, one tap: „Blüte gestartet? Schema auf Blüte Woche 1 setzen."
  A bottle brand's calculator cannot do this because it has no device. This is the single strongest
  argument for the feature living in a *controller*.
- **Autoflower**: a strength multiplier (×0.25–0.5, no flip event). Essentially no manufacturer chart has
  an autoflower column, so this is a real gap fillable with nothing borrowed.
- **Every prefilled value stays editable.** The grower who deviates is the normal case. The diff against
  the prefill is stored as `payload.deviation`, which is what lets a report say
  „Kim hat drei Wochen lang auf 80 % gedüngt" — deviation as a first-class recorded fact is the club's
  „wer hat was gemacht" answered at the level that matters.

### 6.3 What is drawn on the chart

- Water `●` and feed `◆` pips on the AKTIONEN rail, tinted with the actor's person colour.
- **A dedicated `EC gemessen` panel** whenever measured EC exists: scatter points for measurements, a
  **stepped dashed target line from the schedule** — the *same* goal-vs-actual grammar as temperature,
  applied to a quantity with no sensor behind it. That is the honest answer to "improve the visualisation
  of all things water and fertilizer related": there is no EC hardware, so the visualisation is of a human
  record against a human plan, drawn in the product's one visual language.
- pH the same way, on the same panel with a second axis (temp+RH-style coupling exception — pH and EC are
  both reservoir facts, sampled at the same instant, and never confusable by magnitude).
- **EC is stored canonically in mS/cm.** ppm is *rendered* with an explicit scale suffix
  („1260 ppm (700)"), never as a bare number — at EC 1.8 a Bluelab reads 1260 and a Hanna reads 900, and
  this is repeatedly the #1 cause of accidental over/under-feeding.

### 6.4 Brand posture (invisible in the UI)

Brand names appear as **plain text labels only** — no logos, no brand colours, no bottle imagery, and never
inside a Terp Control feature name. Numbers live in the MongoDB content collection above so any single
brand is removable in one DB update. Each regime shows „Zuletzt geprüft: 12.03.2026" in the picker (which
doubles as a genuine trust feature) and every regime card carries a one-line „Melden" contact.
Ownerless regimes ship too (Lucas Formula, Jack's 321, generic per-medium EC ladders) because they cost
nothing legally. Never seeded from retailer or blog transcriptions — two sites disagree by ~3× on CANNA
Terra Vega dosing, and wrong numbers here burn real crops.

**Not in v1:** a nutrient calculator, a shopping list, a mixing-order checklist, run-length curve reshaping.
Those are the planner product; this is the *prefill* product, which is what was asked for.

## 7. Camera & timelapse

Images relate to **time** natively, to **plants** by adoption, and to **stages** by span.

- **Automatic 30 s stills stay device- and time-scoped.** The camera sees a tent, not a plant. Pretending
  otherwise would be the kind of fabricated fact this concept refuses.
- **The film strip is an axis** (§4.6). Scrubbing the chart moves the image; tapping an image scrubs the
  chart. Gaps in capture render as gaps.
- **„Kamerabild übernehmen" / "Use the camera's picture"** — one tap on any entry sheet attaches the still
  nearest to the entry's timestamp (`GrowEvent.images` gets the existing `image_id`; no upload, no
  `express-fileupload`, no 16 MB BSON risk). *This is the literal implementation of "it does the diary
  keeping for you"*: the lazy user's journal ends up illustrated without them ever taking a photo.
- **Timelapse scopes.** Existing `1d` / `1w` / `1m` stay. New scopes built from the same stills:
  **Phase** (bounded by `Plant.stageHistory`), **Grow** (bounded by `Grow.startedAt`/`endedAt`), and
  **Besuch** (a visit window — 20 minutes at 30 s intervals is ~40 frames, a genuinely watchable clip of
  somebody working). Timelapses gain a back-reference so they stop being orphans: today they have no
  relation to a cycle, phase or entry at all.
- **Privacy, stated plainly.** A camera in a club room records *people*, which is a DSFA trigger under
  Art. 35 GDPR independent of anything cannabis-related. So:
  - Turning on club mode shows a one-time notice: „Die Kamera nimmt auch Personen auf." / "The camera also
    records people."
  - Club mode **defaults** to „Kamera pausieren, solange jemand im Zelt ist" — extending the existing
    `cloudSettings.maintenanceWebcamOff` precedent to visits. The visit bracket then explains the gap in
    the film strip, which is exactly right.
  - `DELETE /image/:image_id` exists today and is never called by the webapp; user photos accumulate
    forever and the retention thinner only filters `format:'jpeg'`. Fixed: user photos are deletable from
    the viewer, and `user/jpeg` enters the retention policy.

## 8. Multi-user / clubs

### 8.1 Roles — four, mapped to what people actually do

| Role | DE | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `owner` | Inhaber | everything: delete the grow, transfer ownership, rotate the API key, set retention, wipe | — |
| `steward` | Betreuer | change setpoints/alarms/schedule, manage plants, invite, export | delete the grow, remove the owner |
| `grower` | Gärtner | **log their own entries**, read all room data, correct their own entries, see the member list | change any setpoint, invite, export, edit others' entries |
| `viewer` | Zuschauer | read the room and the journal | write anything, see the member list |

`grower` is the `:self` tier, copied from the one genuinely reusable idea in club software
(`member:journals:self-checkin`, `charges:create:self`): **a member logs their own work, sees the room, and
does not administer anyone.** For a two-person household, `steward` is the natural second seat and
`grower` is the "my flatmate waters when I'm away" seat.

**Multi-user is free.** Pulse charges $10/user and got a public backlash thread for it; every other vendor
says share the password. Charging for the second seat in a *household* would be the single most obviously
wrong monetisation decision available.

### 8.2 Attribution and invites

- `GrowEvent.actor_id` is **always** stored — the server knows who made the request from the JWT anyway,
  and pretending otherwise would be theatre. What is configurable is *display*:
  `Grow.attributionVisible` is off at n=1 (nothing to disambiguate), on by default at n≥2, forced on in
  club mode.
- **Display names are per-grow pseudonyms.** The join flow requires one and pre-fills it with nothing.
  „Klarnamen speichern wir nicht." / "We don't store real names."
- **A member's account identifier (`username`, effectively their email) is never shown to other members.**
  Consequence, and it is a feature: **there is no invite-by-email and no account lookup endpoint.** Invites
  are a **link + 6-character code**, single-use, 48 h expiry, role chosen at creation. This sidesteps the
  account-enumeration abuse problem the dossier flags as an open question, and it removes an endpoint that
  does not exist today and would have to be designed defensively.
- **Leaving.** A member who leaves chooses: keep my entries under my display name, or reassign them to
  „Ehemaliges Mitglied" / "Former member" (`Membership.tombstone`). The grow's record stays coherent; the
  person does not. **Attribution is deletable.** An immutable log the user cannot delete protects a
  *regulated* club against its own staff and *victimises* an unregulated grower — so immutability is never
  global, and never a default.
- **Corrections, not edits.** `GrowEvent.correctsEventId` — a correction is a new row that supersedes an
  old one, both visible in the journal with the second marked „korrigiert". This is how tamper-evidence is
  achieved in practice everywhere it matters, it costs one field, and it does not require hash chaining
  (which nothing in the KCanG demands and which cuts against the user in a prohibition jurisdiction).
- **Access log** (`/grow/:id/people`, visible to owner + steward, deletable with the grow): who joined, who
  opened a share link, who exported. Not who read what — that would be surveillance of members by members.

### 8.3 The privacy posture — what this stores and what it refuses

**Stores** (device-observed and thing-scoped): climate time series; setpoints, schedules, actuator states,
alarms; visits with a pseudonymous actor and a duration; watering/feeding events with volumes, products,
EC/pH; plant labels, free-text strain names, stage history; photos the user chose to keep; a per-grow
access log.

**Refuses — enumerated, and these are product decisions, not settings:**

1. **Real names, first names, dates of birth, addresses, membership numbers.** The join flow has no field
   for any of them.
2. **Per-member dispensing or consumption records** (§ 26 Abs. 1 Nr. 5/6): who received how many grams at
   what THC content. Arguably Art. 9 GDPR health data, triggers a DSFA and probably a DPO, carries Art. 82
   damages exposure — for data a grow controller has no need for.
3. **A grams inventory ledger.** Terp Control does not become the club's system of record for § 26. Clubs
   are already served by ~20 SaaS products at €0.50–1/member/month, one claiming >250 licensed clubs. That
   race is lost and should not be entered; owning 23 differing authority export formats is not a design
   problem, it is a permanent liability.
4. **Location.** No GPS, no IP-geolocation persistence, no persisted timezone-from-IP (the Grow's timezone
   is *chosen*), no "growers near you".
5. **A public feed with identities.** A shareable link is not a feed. Zero of ~200 competitor App Store
   reviews praised a community feed, and it is permanent moderation cost — but more importantly, a feed
   with real identities is how a hobby becomes a target list.
6. **Harvest weights and plant counts by default.** The fields exist (`payload.wetGrams`, `dryGrams`) and
   are **never prompted** unless the grow explicitly enables „Erträge erfassen". These are the exact facts
   separating a lawful hobby from an offence in many jurisdictions, and § 28 Abs. 2 KCanG lets German
   authorities seize digital data with **no prior judicial approval**.
7. **Indefinite telemetry retention.** Today the Influx bucket is created with no retention and there are
   zero downsample tasks — raw 5-second samples are kept forever, which is both a cost curve and a legible
   harvest calendar. Proposed default: **raw 30 days → 5-minute aggregates 400 days → deleted**; auto
   stills 90 days; user photos until deleted. Configurable per grow, downward.
8. **Analytics and ad SDKs.** None, ever.
9. **Compliance claims.** No „KCanG-konform" badge, no warranty, no legal advice. Even the club platforms'
   own AI compliance endpoints say "Not legal advice."

**Club mode** (opt-in, per grow) turns on: forced attribution display; the **participation export**
(CSV/PDF: date · display name · duration · what was done · plants — with a `NONE | PSEUDONYMISIERT |
ANONYMISIERT` selector defaulting to anonymised), which serves § 17 Abs. 2's "eigenhändig mitwirken"
requirement and *nothing else*; the camera-people notice and the visit pause; explicit retention.
It never turns on anything from the refuse list.

**One-tap wipe.** `/grow/:id/settings` → „Grow endgültig löschen" removes the grow, its plants, its events,
its visits, its images and its Influx series. A confirmation typing the grow's name. No soft delete, no
30-day recovery window, no "are you sure you don't want to keep a backup".

### 8.4 The authorisation rewrite — costed honestly

This is not a flag. `deviceModel.find({ owner_id: req.user_id, device_id })` at
`auth.middleware.ts:172` **is** the authorisation model, and it is repeated at `:207`.

Work required: (a) a `Membership` collection with a compound index `{user_id:1, grow_id:1}` and
`{grow_id:1, leftAt:1}`; (b) a `resolveGrowAccess(user_id, device_id | grow_id) → {grow, role} | null`
helper replacing both middleware queries; (c) loosening ~20 owner-scoped queries — `device.service.ts`
`:752 :763 :792 :912 :1198 :1215 :1258`, `device.controller.ts` `:650 :669 :687 :718`,
`share.controller.ts` `:36 :56 :66 :83 :97`, `chartpreset.controller.ts` `:13 :31 :38 :53`; (d) keeping
`Device.owner_id` as the *billing/claim* owner so `claimDevice` and `unClaimDevice` are untouched;
(e) adding the missing index on `Device.owner_id` while in there. Also worth doing and cheap: refuse to
claim an already-owned device (legitimate transfer already goes through `unClaimDevice`, which clears
`owner_id` first).

`RecipeTemplate.name` is currently **globally unique across all users** — two growers cannot both have an
"Autoflower" template. That must be scoped before anything multi-user ships, or the first club to save a
template blocks the name for everyone.

## 9. Old devices (type 7) — fan, light, plug, fridge

The promise, stated as a constraint rather than a sentiment: **no route an old-device owner uses today
moves, no setting they rely on changes, and every `ngSwitch` settings component stays exactly where it is.**
`/device/:id/settings` continues to `ngSwitch` on `device_type` into `fan-settings` / `fridge-settings` /
`light-settings` / `plug-settings`. What they gain is the shell above it.

Concretely, per type:

**`plug` — a smart socket, no sensors.**
- Tabs: `Zelt` · `Journal`. **Two tabs.** No `Verlauf` unless the device reports `out_relais` history —
  when it does, `Verlauf` is a single lane: when was it on, with the day/night shading from its own timer.
- Home: header (freshness) → handover card → `[ Ich war im Zelt ]` → one output tile
  („Steckdose · An seit 4 Std." / „Zustand unbekannt" when offline) → `[ Eintrag hinzufügen ]`.
- The „JETZT" grid and webcam block are absent, not empty.

**`light` — PWM lamp + photoperiod.**
- Tabs: `Zelt` · `Verlauf` · `Journal`.
- `Verlauf` shows the output lane + day/night shading + the **schedule as the setpoint**: the dashed
  stepped line is the configured photoperiod and the solid lane is what the lamp actually did. **A failed
  contactor appears as a missing band.** That is a real diagnostic a light-only owner has never had.
- Home: „Licht 100 % · Sonnenuntergang 20:00 · Nacht in 3 Std." with the same freshness rules.
- DLI stays absent unless a lux sensor exists (§4.9 #12).

**`fan` — temp/humidity sensors + fan speed.**
- Tabs: `Zelt` · `Verlauf` · `Journal` (+ adaptive 4th).
- Full panel stack minus CO₂ and minus outputs it does not have. `fans.external` / `fans.internal` are
  **real** for a fan device — they are only hidden-and-ignored for controllers — so they are drawn.
- Gains everything else in the concept for free: setpoint bands, scorecard, visits, journal, sharing.

**`fridge` — the actual fridge grow.**
- Keeps `fridge-settings` untouched and keeps being *called* a fridge — which becomes correct again once
  the controller stops borrowing its identity. This is also type 5's closest existing product.

**And the thing they actually get that they cannot get today: one Grow, several devices.**
A tent with a Terp light and a Terp fan is two unrelated device pages today with two unrelated log streams.
Under C08 they are one Grow: one journal, one chart, one set of members. The chart draws **one panel per
device per measure, labelled by device name** — never an average, because averaging two sensors in
different corners of a tent is a fabricated number.

Explicit non-promises for type 7: they are never required to create a plant, choose a feed regime, or
invite anyone; no old device gets new firmware; the `dryer` type stays dead as `AGENTS.md` instructs.

## 10. Onboarding

Capability is **derived, never interrogated.** `hardwareInfo.sockets` already carries per-role truth;
sensors are known from which values report; the camera from `webcam_did`. Today that per-role truth is
collapsed into three buckets, shown only inside a one-time wizard step, and — worse — **fails open to
`'full'` when the key is absent**, so a heater-only tent is shown humidity targets, a humidity-deviation
warning and a dehumidifier alarm preset it can never act on.

C08 **fails closed with exactly one recovery question**, and that is the only capability question in the
entire flow.

```
0. [only if nothing claimed]   Claim code — unchanged.

1.  „Wie heißt dein Grow?"                                  [Mein Grow      ] → Weiter
    (prefilled and valid; a stoner taps Weiter)

2.  „Das haben wir gefunden"                                → Passt
    ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │ ✓ Temperatur      ✓ Luftfeuchte            │   derived, not asked
    │ ✗ CO₂ (nachrüstbar)  ✓ Licht (PWM)         │
    │ Steckdosen: Heizung · Entfeuchter          │
    └────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    ── only when `hardwareInfo.sockets` is ABSENT (old firmware): ──
    „Was hängt an den Steckdosen?"  [Heizung][Entfeuchter][Licht][2. Licht][CO₂][nichts]
    one tap each, and the answer is CACHED on the Grow so it is never asked twice.

3.  „In welcher Phase bist du?"                             → Weiter / Überspringen
    the existing `stage-preset-picker` (5 cards, each already showing 24/21 °C · 70 % · 18 h · VPD 0,4–0,8)
    + a sixth card: „Weiß nicht / nur messen"  → sets monitor mode, hides every target the device
      cannot act on, and never nags again.

4.  „Erde & Dünger?"                                        → Später (prominent) / choose
    medium+regime picker. „Später" is the default-weighted button.

5.  „Noch jemand dabei?"                                    → Nein, nur ich (large) / Einladen
```

**Five screens, five default taps, ~20 seconds.** Every one of screens 3–5 is skippable and none of them
can be wrong in a way that costs the user anything later — all three are editable from the overflow, and
„Einrichtung wiederholen" re-enters the flow (fixing the un-discoverable wizard).

The existing 30 MB of already-shot wizard videos in `assets/wizard/` (`connect.mp4`, `teachin.mp4`,
`sockettype_*.mp4`, …) are copied into every production build and referenced nowhere. Screen 2's socket
question and screen 0's claim step use them. That is a large asset budget already being paid for silence.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough — the first week

**Type 1 — lazy, monitoring only.**
Day 1: claims, taps through onboarding in 20 s, picks „Weiß nicht / nur messen". Three tabs. No plants, no
feed, no people. Home is: freshness, „JETZT" grid with reference values and no unachievable targets, a
webcam still if they bought one, and a chart. Day 3: opens the app, sees „Die letzten 3 Tage · 88 % im
Zielbereich" — the first time this product has told them whether the numbers were *good*. Day 6: gets a
push that the device went offline; the home screen says „Offline seit 2 Std." and the outputs say
„Zustand unbekannt" instead of lying. **What they never see all week:** the word „Pflanze", the word
„Futter", a member list, an invite, a role.
*Honest assessment: this type is taxed by the Grow indirection and gets the least from the concept's spine.
The scorecard and the freshness fix are what they actually bought, and both are P0 anyway.*

**Type 2 — the same user, with a camera.**
Same as type 1 plus a `Kamera` 4th tab. Day 2: discovers the film strip is an axis — dragging the chart
drags the picture, and the 03:00 humidity spike has a photo of the tent at 03:00 attached to it. Day 5: the
grow timelapse now spans since day 1 rather than an arbitrary „1m" window, because timelapses finally have
a scope. Day 7: an entry gets a picture with one tap („Kamerabild übernehmen") without them ever having
opened a camera app.
*Honest assessment: C08 gives the camera a good home but does not make it the centre of the product. A
camera-first concept beats this one for type 2.*

**Type 3 — controls certain aspects (CO₂ or heating only).**
Day 1: onboarding derives „Steckdosen: Heizung" — and **only** the temperature target row appears. No
humidity target, no dehumidifier tile, no humidity-deviation warning, no dehumidifier alarm preset. Day 2:
humidity spikes to 78 %. Instead of nagging them about a lever they don't have, the scorecard reports the
excursion and the PERSONEN lane shows the spike started at 19:05 — when they were in the tent. Day 4: they
add a humidity alarm anyway, purely as an alert, and it immediately becomes the chart's band, labelled
„Band: deine Alarmgrenzen".
*This type is served very well: the fail-closed capability model plus excursion attribution answers their
two real questions — what can I actually change, and why did that happen.*

**Type 4 — the whole tent.**
Day 1: derives four socket roles. Sets a flowering stage preset; the chart immediately draws the stepped
day/night setpoint and the band. Day 2: two plants at different stages, so the VPD panel notes
„2 Pflanzen in verschiedenen Phasen" and draws the outer envelope, with a plant chip row to pick one. Day 3:
their partner joins by link; the header grows an avatar stack, and `attributionVisible` flips on
automatically. Day 5: they get a temperature excursion at 21:00 and — for the first time in any grow app —
the app tells them their partner was in the tent at 20:58. **This is the fight the concept prevents**: not
"who left the tent open" as an accusation, but "the dehumidifier is fine, that was a door."
Day 7: the GERÄTE state timeline shows the heater cycling 40 times in one night; they raise
`minimalDehumidifierOffTime`. *Best-served type after clubs.*

**Type 5 — DIY closed loop (the fridge builder).**
Day 1–3: builds their loop, uses the `fridge` type as it exists today with settings untouched. Gains the
journal, the visit log, the setpoint drawing and the min/max envelope — the last of which matters most to
them, because a mean line hides exactly the bang-bang oscillation they are tuning. Day 4: wants EC from
their own probe and finds it can only enter as a manual measurement entry. Day 5: asks for an API and is
told v1.1. **They are the least-served type in this concept and I am not going to pretend otherwise.** The
per-grow API key + documented read/write API is the right answer for them and it is not in the October
scope.

**Type 6 — the grow club. This is the concept's home.**
Day 1: the founder claims the controller, names the grow „Raum 1", switches on club mode. Reads the
one-time notice that the camera records people and accepts the default that capture pauses during visits.
Sets retention to 400 days explicitly. Creates four invite links with role `Gärtner` and hands them out in
person. Day 2: four members join, each picking a pseudonym; nobody's email is visible to anybody.
Day 3: Marek waters. One tap. The grow's home screen for everyone else now reads
„Marek war heute 18:12–18:31 im Zelt · Gegossen · 6 L · alle Pflanzen". Day 4: Anna feeds and drops the
dose by 20 %; the deviation is recorded, and the report will say so. Day 5: the founder opens
`/grow/:id/report`, exports the participation log anonymised, and files it with the club's own records —
Terp Control does **not** file anything with anybody. Day 6: somebody asks for the § 26 dispensing register
and the answer is a documented no, with a pointer to the club-management SaaS they already pay for.
Day 7: a member leaves; their entries become „Ehemaliges Mitglied" and the room's history stays readable.
*Served best. Also the smallest segment, which is §15's first weakness.*

**Type 7 — old devices (air, light, socket).**
Day 1: opens the app after the update. Their fan is where it was, their settings screen is unchanged, and
the device is now inside a Grow named after it. Nothing broke. Day 2: notices they can drag their old light
into the same Grow as their fan — one journal for the tent, for the first time. Day 3: their light's chart
now draws the photoperiod as a dashed setpoint against what the lamp actually did, and they discover a
40-minute gap on Tuesday. Day 5: they add a plant, or they don't — nothing asks them to. Day 6: they invite
their partner, free, because that is not a paid seat.
*Not the focus group, and the concept costs them nothing while handing them the multi-device grow, which is
the one thing they have quietly wanted.*

## 12. Stoner / normal / techie

The existing precedent is a two-value `ion-segment` persisted in `localStorage['app-settings-expert']`,
simple by default, documented in German at `docs/einfach-modus.md`. C08 keeps two stored modes and adds a
third tier that is not a UI mode at all.

**Stoner — „Einfach" (default).**
- The home screen's top third is one card with at most one button. Every primary action is
  *confirm-a-guess*: `[ Gegossen ✓ ]`, `[ Erledigt ✓ ]`, `[ Ich war im Zelt ]`.
- No number is ever entered to complete a task. The schedule prefills, the volume repeats, the plant
  selection defaults to all.
- The chart's default is 3 panels, 4 range chips, and a one-sentence verdict under it.
- Reminders are one line: „Fällig: Gießen + Blüte Woche 3". Never a table of ml/L.
- Language is concrete and physical: „im Zelt", „gegossen", „Erde", not „session", „irrigation event",
  „substrate".

**Normal — „Einfach" with the cards expanded.**
- Same screens. The „JETZT" tiles show targets alongside values; the due card shows the mix; the chart
  shows all panels and the scorecard; the entry sheet shows volume, EC and pH.
- The switch between stoner and normal is **not a mode** — it is which cards they choose to look at. That
  is deliberate: a mode switch is a decision, and the difference between these two people is attention,
  not intent.

**Techie — „Alles" plus machine access.**
- The `Alles` toggle reveals, in the *same* screens rather than separate ones: raw `device.configuration`
  values with their firmware-read status marked (and the four dead controls — `daynight.floating`,
  `lights.maintenanceOn`, `co2.sunsetOff`, `daynight.linearChange` — **removed, not marked**, because
  shipping a control nothing reads is worse than not shipping it); the recipe editor; per-role socket
  detail with IPs; alarm editor with webhook payload templates; the chart's raw interval/aggregation
  controls; „Als Tabelle anzeigen" and CSV export **including actuator state**, which no vendor in the
  market provides and which an AC Infinity reviewer asked for by name.
- **Machine access is the real answer for techies and it is not a UI tier**: a per-grow API key
  (`x-api-key`, copying Pulse's shape), outbound event webhooks (reusing `webhookTemplate.ts`'s existing
  `{{placeholder}}` substitution and the alarm webhook machinery — what is missing is only a non-alarm
  trigger and a preset library), and a documented JSON diary schema. **v1.1**, and stated as v1.1 rather
  than implied for October.

The one place all three tiers meet is the entry sheet: the stoner never opens it, the normal user opens it
and fills three fields, the techie fills nine and attaches a runoff reading. **It is one sheet.**

## 13. v1 scope for October 2026 vs. later

The controller ships 2026-10 and the software ships with it. Scope accordingly.

### v1 — must ship with the hardware

**Correctness (all P0; none of it is optional and most of it is small):**
- Controller identity: a `devices.controller` i18n block and a controller shell so a controller's dashboard
  card stops reading „Terp Control FRIDGE GROW" and an unnamed controller stops being *named* that.
- The freshness tri-state everywhere, including „Zustand unbekannt" on outputs.
- Per-role capability, **failing closed** with the single recovery question.
- Delete the four dead controls.
- „Protokoll löschen" actually deletes; server-written stage entries become visible and editable.
- Parameterise the Flux query; clamp interval × timespan; move the `limit()` guard before `yield()`.
- Server-side log pagination (the Entries report fetches the entire history on every open).
- Influx retention + a downsample task.
- The six raw-key i18n bugs; translate the hardcoded English chart series names.

**The concept:**
- `Grow` entity + grow-scoped routing + device-route aliases + the one-time backfill.
- `Plant` (optional) with `stageHistory`; clone, re-veg, rename, archive.
- `GrowEvent` + `Visit` + `actor_id`; the journal screen; the handover card; the home screen.
- Water/feed capture (1 tap) + `FeedRegime` content collection seeded with **~6 regimes**
  (Biobizz Light-Mix, Biobizz All-Mix, Green House Feeding, BioTabs, Lucas Formula, a generic coco EC
  ladder), schedule prefill, due reminder, photoperiod-flip detection.
- Chart on ECharts: small multiples, setpoint + band + deviation, day/night, output lane, PERSONEN lane,
  AKTIONEN rail, film strip (per-request thumbnails), EC/pH panel, min/max envelope, Okabe-Ito,
  two-finger interaction, pinned scrub header, view-as-table.
- Scorecard from a new **raw-sample** server endpoint, including excursion-overlaps-visit.
- `Membership` + 4 roles + link/code invites + the `resolveGrowAccess` rewrite.
- Retention default, one-tap wipe, pseudonymous display names, corrections-not-edits.
- de + en for all of it, in the same commits.

**The de-risking statement, because this is a lot:** the membership/invite/roles layer is the single
biggest schedule risk in the plan. **`actor_id` and `Visit` ship regardless** — they are two fields and one
small collection, they carry the excursion-attribution feature that pays back to solo users, and if the
authorisation rewrite slips to v1.1 the product degrades to a solo app whose actor always says „Du". It
still works. **That is the fallback, and it is designed in rather than discovered in September.**

## 14. Non-goals

1. **No two-way grow-diary sync. Ever.** GrowDiaries has no public API and is Cloudflare-hardened; Grow
   with Jane's ToS forbids automation and its backend is Firebase; SeedFinder's API has been dead since
   July 2024. The honest features are export, a public link, and a post generator. Renaming this correctly
   before launch prevents a permanent support-ticket category.
2. **No Instagram, Threads, TikTok or Reddit auto-posting.** A failed Discord post means a revoked webhook.
   A failed Instagram post means the *user* loses their account with no appeal and Terp Control was the
   proximate cause. TikTok without an audit silently restricts every post to private — a feature that
   appears to work and posts nothing anyone can see.
3. **No "Powered by Terp Control" footer on user posts.** § 6 KCanG bans cannabis advertising and
   sponsoring with fines to €30,000, read broadly. It is the single riskiest string in the feature and
   nothing about it is worth the exposure.
4. **No community feed, no comments, no follower graph, no "growers near you".**
5. **No § 26 compliance module**: no dispensing register, no grams ledger, no THC content, no authority
   export templates. C08 refuses to be the state's reporting system inside a grower's app.
6. **No real-name identity, no email visible between members, no member directory, no account lookup.**
7. **No irrigation or dosing automation.** There is no such hardware. Watering and feeding are capture and
   recall. A pump on a Tasmota socket is a socket.
8. **No new firmware socket roles in v1** — no humidifier, no exhaust fan, no AC/chiller, no pump role.
   Everything here works within the existing five roles. (`dehumidifier` doubling as the cooler stays a
   known wart; C08 labels it honestly in the outputs lane rather than pretending the data distinguishes
   heat-removal from moisture-removal.)
9. **No per-seat pricing.** The second household member is free.
10. **No native app and no offline mode.** No service worker exists and `capacitor.config.ts` is untouched
    scaffold; adding either is a build change and a dependency, not a design decision.
11. **No cryptographic immutability of the log.** Corrections, not hash chains.
12. **No commercial-cultivation vocabulary** — no rooms, zones, racks, benches, crop steering,
    labour tracking, cost-per-gram. The owner narrowed the market to hobbyists and clubs.
13. **No fabricated numbers.** No DLI without a light sensor, no averaged multi-device temperature, no bare
    ppm without its scale, no "state: off" for an offline device.

## 15. Honest weaknesses

**1. The concept spends its October budget on the smallest segment.**
Types 1–5 are solo growers. Type 6 is the only one that inherently needs multi-user, and the owner's own
market narrowing puts clubs alongside hobbyists rather than above them. The authorisation rewrite — a
membership collection, `auth.middleware.ts:172` and `:207`, ~20 owner-scoped queries, invite flows, role
gating on every new screen — is the most expensive single line item in the whole redesign, and a
monitoring-only user's experience of it is a menu entry they never tap. My defence is the excursion
attribution and the multi-device grow, both of which pay back at n=1. It is a defence, not a refutation,
and a reviewer is right to press on it.

**2. The Visit is a metaphor that may not survive contact with a solo indoor grower.**
Somebody with a tent in their bedroom walks past it eight times a day. "Visit" is a club and household
frame. If inferred visits fragment into eight 4-minute brackets a day, the PERSONEN lane becomes noise and
the excursion attribution becomes *misleading* — which is worse than absent, because the whole thesis is
that recorded facts are true. The 30-minute auto-close threshold is a guess with no data behind it, and
tuning it is not something a design document can do.

**3. Nobody will press "start visit", so visits must be inferred, and inference is exactly what a club
cannot rely on.** The two audiences want opposite things from the same primitive: the household wants zero
friction and accepts fuzziness; the club needs a declared, defensible record for § 17 Abs. 2. C08 splits
the difference (dotted = inferred, solid = confirmed) which is honest but means the club's export contains
a mix of qualities and the household sees a distinction they don't care about.

**4. Attribution in a household is socially loaded.**
"The app says you didn't water on Tuesday" is a fight the software started. C08 mitigates by framing
attribution as explanation rather than accounting ("that spike was a door, not a fault") and by making
attribution display-optional — but the data is there, and someone will use it as evidence in an argument.
That is a real product risk that no amount of copy fixes.

**5. Camera plus people is a GDPR problem the concept creates and only partly solves.**
Visit-pausing capture and a one-time notice are mitigations, not compliance. A club with a camera needs a
DSFA regardless, and offering a "visit timelapse" — a clip of a named person working — is arguably the
worst-idea-per-line-of-code in the document. It is v1.1 specifically so it can be dropped.

**6. Grow-centric routing risks user data.**
Share links and chart presets both store a frozen `/device/:id/...?query`, the query vocabulary is
duplicated in five places in the codebase, and there is no test baseline to catch a regression (38 `it()`
blocks, 24 of them a lone `should create`, CI never runs `ng test`, and `app.component.spec.ts` cannot
pass). If the alias layer is subtly wrong, paying customers' saved views break silently. This is the
highest-probability way C08 damages something that works today.

**7. The Grow indirection adds a join to nearly every query, in a codebase with no migration tooling.**
The house pattern — optional field, read-time normaliser, idempotent boot-time backfill — works, but the
backfill runs the existing browser-side cycle-inference algorithm over production data exactly once, and
if it is wrong, it is wrong permanently and invisibly.

**8. Clubs will ask for the § 26 register on day one and we say no.**
Some will leave for a platform that says yes. The refusal is right — Art. 9 exposure, 23 authority export
formats, 5-year retention, an Art. 28 AVV with every club — but it means C08's best-served segment is also
the one most likely to churn to Cannanas or 420cloud, both of whom already name grow controllers as data
sources they want. C08's real club play is to be the layer *they* consume, and that is a business
development dependency (P3), not something a design can deliver.

**9. Type 5 gets almost nothing structural.**
The DIY closed-loop builder is the highest-advocacy, most sceptical segment in the market, and C08's answer
to them — a documented API and webhooks — is explicitly v1.1. In October they get a min/max envelope and a
journal.

**10. Type 2 is served better by a camera-first concept, and type 1 is taxed.**
C08 gives the camera a good home (film strip as an axis, one-tap adoption, scoped timelapses) but the
camera is a lane, not the spine. And club mode actively *pauses* the camera, which is the correct privacy
call and the wrong answer for somebody whose whole reason for buying was correlating pictures with numbers.

**11. Two-tab and three-tab variants mean the product looks different on different accounts.**
Adaptive navigation is defensible per-user and awkward for support, screenshots, documentation and the
Telegram community, where "tap the Futter tab" is advice half the readers cannot follow.

## 16. Verification

Every claim about behaviour in this document is checkable locally before it is built:
`./simulate-device.sh setup` then `run` gives a real controller over real MQTT — forced sensor values
(`send --set temperature=31.5`), forced outputs (`send --set out_light=0`), history backfill
(`history --days 30`), camera stills (`run --camera`), socket pairing and `hwinfo co2=off` for the
capability paths. The offline state is produced by stopping `run`. There is no excuse for shipping the
freshness tri-state, the capability fail-closed behaviour, or the day/night shading unverified — and given
the state of the test suite, local verification against a simulated device is the only real gate this
project has.
