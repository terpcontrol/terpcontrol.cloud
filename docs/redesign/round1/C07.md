# C07 — "Der Plan" / The Plan

**Design philosophy:** the grow plan is the centre of gravity.
**Author's stance:** this is the most opinionated of the ten concepts and the one most likely to be
rejected by a grower who likes knobs. It is written to be attacked. Every place it is weak is named in §15,
and §13 is deliberately smaller than the concept, because the ship date is **six weeks away**.

---

## 1. Thesis

**A Terp Control device does not have settings — it has a Plan, and every screen in the app is a
projection of that Plan: the home screen is *where am I and what do I do next*, the chart is *plan vs
reality with the future drawn on it*, and the diary is the plan's execution record, written for you.**

The big idea. Today the product's model of a grow is a JSON blob of setpoints plus a pile of log rows,
and every question a grower actually asks — *is this going the way it should? what do I do today? what did
I do last time?* — has to be reconstructed by the human. C07 inverts that: a `GrowPlan` is a first-class
cloud entity that owns the stage timeline, the climate setpoints per stage, the photoperiod, the feed
steps and the recurring tasks. `device.configuration` stops being the truth and becomes a **compiled
artifact** — what the plan renders at time *T*. Because the plan is a timeline and not a snapshot, the
chart can draw the *future* target line to the right of "now"; because it emits scheduled tasks, the home
screen always has exactly one thing to say; and because completing a task, passing a stage gate, or
overriding a setpoint each produce a journal row with the numbers pre-filled, the diary genuinely writes
itself. That is the owner's "it does the diary keeping FOR YOU" promise implemented as a mechanism rather
than as a slogan. The existing `Recipe` engine is the seed and stays the executor — the plan **compiles
down to a Recipe** — which is why this is buildable inside a codebase with 38 tests and no migration tool.

## 2. Information architecture

### 2.1 Navigation model

Today: one `ion-menu` + `ion-split-pane`, no tab bar, no back button, no device switcher, and six outline
buttons on the device overview doing the work of navigation.

C07 introduces **four device-scoped tabs**, because a plan-centric app has exactly four verbs:
*see now, see history, see what happened, change what should happen.*

- The `ion-menu` survives for account-level things (Geräte/Devices list, Shared links, Konto/Account,

- The header carries a **device switcher** (`ion-select` styled as a title button) and the **liveness

- `ion-tabs` is Ionic 6 native and NgModule-friendly. On desktop the tab bar becomes a left rail inside
  the existing split-pane, no second layout system.
- Back behaviour: `ion-back-button` on every pushed page (stage editor, plant detail, entry). Tabs never
  push each other.

### A — header / liveness chip (the most repeated element in the product)

| State | Rule | DE | EN | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Live | `lastseen` < 90 s | `● Live` | `● Live` | `--ion-color-success` dot, solid |
| Verzögert / Delayed | 90 s – 10 min | `● vor 4 Min.` | `● 4 min ago` | amber dot, tile values at 70 % opacity |
| Offline | > 10 min | `● Offline` | `● Offline` | grey dot, values keep numbers + age caption |
| Lange offline | > 60 min | `● Offline seit 3 Std.` | `● Offline for 3 h` | values become `—`, caption keeps last reading |

This directly answers the dossier's P0 #5 ("nothing distinguishes live from stale from offline", the #1
complaint across all five consumer brands). It is one component (`app-liveness-chip`) reused in the header,
on every tile caption, and in the device list.

### B — the plan band

The signature element. A single-row stage ribbon with a "you are here" marker, proportional to
`plannedDays`, with the elapsed portion filled.

- Tap → `/device/:id/plan`.
- Copy, DE / EN: `Blüte · Woche 2` / `Flowering · week 2`; `Tag 34` / `Day 34`;
  `Nächste Phase in 30 Tagen` / `Next stage in 30 days`.
- **Waiting at a gate:** the marker turns amber and the caption becomes
  `Warte auf dich: 12/12 umstellen?` / `Waiting for you: switch to 12/12?` with two inline buttons
  `Ja, weiter` / `Yes, continue` and `Noch nicht` / `Not yet`.
- **Grace running out:** `Der Plan geht in 3 Tagen von selbst weiter.` /
  `The plan continues on its own in 3 days.`
- **Plan step not yet on the device:** amber pill under the band,
  `Planschritt wartet auf den Controller` / `Plan step waiting for the controller` — because the recipe
  executor only pushes to a device seen in the last 60 s. This is the honest surfacing of a real limit.
- **Device drifted from the plan** (someone turned the rotary knob on the controller):
  `Der Controller läuft anders als der Plan.` / `The controller is running different values than the plan.`
  with `[Plan anpassen]` / `[Adopt into plan]` and `[Plan wiederherstellen]` / `[Restore plan]`.
- **No plan:** the band is replaced by one card:
  `Kein Plan aktiv — dieses Gerät zeichnet nur auf.` /
  `No plan running — this device is only recording.`
  with `[Plan starten]` / `[Start a plan]` and a small `Nur beobachten, danke` / `Just watching, thanks`
  which sets `Device.plannerDismissed = true` and never shows the prompt again on this device.

### C — the next-action card

Shows **at most one** task. Ordering: overdue > due today > due within `windowHours` > gate waiting >
nothing. If nothing is due:

`Nichts zu tun. Nächste Aufgabe: Gießen, morgen.` / `Nothing to do. Next: water, tomorrow.`

If more than one is due, the card gets a `+2 weitere` / `+2 more` chip that expands the list in place.
This is deliberate: the stoner lens forbids a task inbox on the home screen.

`Erledigt` writes the journal row with the planned numbers. `⋯` opens the detail sheet (§6). `Später`
snoozes to the next photoperiod boundary (lights-on or lights-off, whichever is next), because "tomorrow"
is meaningless to a 12/12 tent — copy: `Später — nach dem Lichtwechsel` / `Later — after lights change`.

### D — climate tiles

Reuses the existing `value-display` SVG gauge (it already draws a target line and an average line — it was
built for this and never fed a target). Three tiles max on mobile, wrapping to a 2×N grid on desktop.
Which tiles render is driven by **per-role capability**, not the three-bucket `deviceControlCapability()`:

- humidity tile shows a target **only** if a `dehumidifier` socket is bound; otherwise it shows the value
  with the caption `nur gemessen` / `measured only`.
- CO₂ tile renders only if `hardwareInfo.co2 !== 'off'`.
- VPD tile renders whenever temp+RH exist, with the leaf-offset stated in the caption:
  `Blatt = Luft − 2,0 °C` / `Leaf = air − 2.0 °C`.
- Missing sensor entirely: the tile is not drawn. Sensor present but no value:
  `Kein Wert` / `No reading` — never a stale number without an age.

`im Ziel 91 %` / `in range 91 %` is the time-in-range figure for the last 24 h, computed **server-side on
raw Influx samples** (see §4.5). If coverage < 80 % it renders greyed as
`im Ziel — (zu wenig Daten)` / `in range — (not enough data)`.

### E — outputs

State bars, not toggles. Duration is the primary number (`an seit 6 Min` / `on for 6 min`) because that is
what diagnoses a failing dehumidifier. Only sockets that are actually bound appear.

### F — journal peek

Three most recent rows, mixed automatic and manual, using the existing `app-log-entry-item`.

## 4. The chart overhaul — "Verlauf" / Timeline

Library decision: **migrate to Apache ECharts 6**, tree-shaken, with a hand-rolled ~50-line Angular
directive (no `ngx-echarts`). Delete `chart.js` / `ng2-charts` / `chartjs-adapter-luxon`. I adopt the
dossier §7.1 recommendation in full, and I adopt its honesty requirement: **the bundle gets bigger**
(195.6 KB gz vs 132.7 KB gz; ~68.8 KB repaid by deleting Chart.js). The decisive argument is licensing —
Highcharts Stock is proprietary, the repo has no LICENSE file, and terpcontrol.com advertises a public
no-login demo and calls the cloud "quelloffen". A concept that plans a chart rewrite on top of a
proprietary Stock licence, in a repo intended to be open, is planning a rewrite that has to be done twice.
I also adopt Appendix A's correction: **`sampling: 'minmax'` is not polish, it is the replacement for
Highstock's implicit `dataGrouping`,** which is currently the only thing keeping a 4,320-point render cheap
and which nobody configured.

### 4.2 The distinctive part: the chart has a future

Everything right of the `jetzt` line is drawn from the plan, on a hatched/desaturated background:

- the planned setpoint line continues (including the next stage's step change and the flip date),
- planned feed and water tasks appear as **hollow** annotation markers,
- the stage ribbon shows the planned remaining stages,
- the planned end of run is a terminal marker: `Ernte geplant 04.11` / `Harvest planned 04 Nov`.

No competitor found in the research draws a future target. It costs nothing extra to compute — the plan is
already a timeline — and it converts the chart from a rear-view mirror into the plan's contract.

### 4.3 Techniques adopted from dossier §7.2

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Stacked small multiples, shared x, linked crosshair (`grid[]` + `axisPointer.link`) | **Adopt.** 3–5 panels, 96 px each on mobile. Kills the 15-hidden-axes overlay. At most two units per panel and only when physically coupled (air + leaf temp). |
| 2 | Dashed **stepped** setpoint + soft band + signed deviation fill | **Adopt, and it is the core of C07.** The setpoint comes from the *plan*, so it is correct historically too — a step is drawn where the plan actually changed, not where today's config says. | 3 | AGP-style time-in-range scorecard, split day/night, longest excursion as a **duration**, coverage % | **Adopt.** Rendered above the panels on desktop, collapsed behind a chip on mobile. Greyed below 80 % coverage. |

### 4.4 Range presets

`[24 h] [7 T] [30 T] [Phase] [Durchgang]` — the last two are plan-derived and are new:
`Phase` = the running stage's actual start → now; `Durchgang` = the whole run. These become the default
entry points, because "since the flip" is the range a grower actually wants and no one offers it.
The interval is **clamped by timespan** server-side (the `3y × 5s` = 18.9 M-window self-DoS is a P0 fix).

### 4.5 Backend work this needs

1. `GET /data/scorecard?device_id&from&to&measure` — time-in-range on **raw** samples. Non-negotiable:
   computing it on `aggregateWindow(fn:mean)` destroys the excursions the metric exists to count, and the
   error is invisible by eye.
2. `GET /data/series` returning `{mean,min,max}` in one response instead of three round trips, with
   `measure`/`from`/`to`/`interval` **parameterised, not interpolated** into Flux (P0 injection fix), and
   `interval` clamped to `max(range/2000, floor)`.
3. `GET /plan/:device_id/timeline?from&to` — the setpoint step function, stage ribbon, gates and planned
   task markers for a window. This is what makes the setpoint line historically correct.

### 4.6 Query-string compatibility

`measures`, `date`, `dateEnd`, `vpdMode`, `autoUpdate`, `useCustom`, `timespan`, `interval`, `logs`,
`share` all keep their meaning. New optional params: `panels` (panel layout), `plan` (`0|1`, default 1),
`future` (`0|1`), `film` (`0|1`), `plants` (comma-separated ids filtering the annotation rail).
`applyViewParams` gains a back-compat branch: an old preset with 15 measures and no `panels` is mapped to
**one panel per unit family**, not 15 panels. Old share links keep working; they just look better.

## 5. The multi-plant diary

### 5.1 The rule that keeps it from taxing anyone

**Plants are opt-in, and the app never asks about them.** A device with zero `Plant` rows behaves exactly
as today: entries are tent-scoped, no plant chips, no Plants route in the tab rail. The plant selector
appears in the entry sheet only when `plants.length > 1`. Type 1 and Type 7 users never see the word
"Pflanze" unless they go looking.

### 5.2 New entities

Every field is optional or defaulted — there is no migration tooling in this repo, so a required field is a
breaking change with no tool to apply it.

```ts
// collection: plants
interface Plant {
  plant_id: string;           // stable identity — NOT a name
  device_id: string;
  owner_id: string;
  label: string;              // "Topf 3", user-editable, renaming NEVER forks history
  strain?: string;            // free text; no SeedFinder — its API is dead since 2024-07
  source: 'seed' | 'clone' | 'unknown';
  sourcePlantId?: string;     // clone parent
  startedAt: number;
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage;
  stageChangedAt: number;
  stageOffsetDays: number;    // default 0 — how far this plant lags the device plan
  planId?: string;
  medium?: 'soil' | 'coco' | 'hydro' | 'other';
  potLitres?: number;
  colour: string;             // auto-assigned Okabe-Ito index, used on chart + journal
  archivedAt?: number;
  archiveReason?: 'harvested' | 'dead' | 'removed';
  harvest?: { date: number; wetGrams?: number; dryGrams?: number; note?: string };  // §8 privacy-gated
}

// collection: runs   (replaces convertEventsToGrowCycles' runtime inference)
interface Run {
  run_id: string; device_id: string; owner_id: string;
  planId?: string;
  name: string;               // default: "Durchgang 3 · Sep 2026"
  startedAt: number; endedAt?: number;
  plantIds: string[];
}
```

Journal entries stay in `DeviceLog`. That is a deliberate, defensible choice: it keeps
`LogEntryViewerModule`, `LogTranslateService`, the `message-*` i18n convention, the chart annotation
pipeline and the share-link read path working unchanged. `DeviceLog` gains three optional indexed fields:

```ts
plant_ids?: string[];   // absent = tent-scoped
actor_id?: string;      // who did it (§8)
entry_type?: string;    // 'water'|'feed'|'measure'|'train'|'ipm'|'note'|'photo'|'stage'
                        // |'gate'|'override'|'setpoint'|'harvest'|'week'
```

`entry_type` is the discriminator the diary has never had; today the split is a string-prefix convention on
`categories[0]`. A read-time normaliser derives `entry_type` from legacy `categories` so nothing needs
backfilling to be readable.

`DiaryEntryData` gains: `waterVolumeL`, `inputEc`, `inputPh`, `runoffEc`, `runoffPh`, `potsTreated`,
`feedProgramId`, `feedStepIndex`, `trainingKind`, `heightCm`. `getDiaryDataFieldUnit()` gains the matching
units, and `phMeasurement` finally gets one (today it has no unit case at all). **The server gets a
validator keyed on `entry_type`** — `DeviceLog.data` is `Schema.Types.Mixed` with zero validation today,
which is fine for five hand-written entry types and unacceptable once the plan writes rows automatically.

```ts
// collection: weeksummaries — this table IS the export
interface WeekSummary {
  device_id: string; run_id: string; weekIndex: number; plantIds: string[];
  weekStart: number; weekEnd: number; dayOfRunStart: number;   // both index schemes, per §4 of the dossier
  stage: DiaryLifecycleStage; stageWeek: number;
  metrics: { tempDayAvg; tempNightAvg; rhDayAvg; rhNightAvg; vpdAvg; co2Avg?;
             photoperiodHours; dli?; timeInRangePct; coveragePct };
  human: { waterLitres?; feedSteps?: number[]; ec?: number[]; ph?: number[]; heightCm?; noteLogIds: string[] };
  heroImageId?: string;
  generatedAt: number;
}
```

Storing **both** a day counter and week buckets is required: GrowDiaries is week-bucketed, Grow with Jane
is day-indexed with named stages. Store one and an export is permanently lossy.

### 5.3 Creating plants

From the Plan screen or the journal sheet, one control:

```
Pflanzen anlegen / Add plants
  Anzahl  [ − ]  3  [ + ]
  Benennen als:  ( ) Topf 1, Topf 2, Topf 3
                 (•) Sorte + Nummer    [ Wedding Cake ]
  [ Anlegen ]
```

Three taps for three plants. Existing history is backfilled once by a migration that runs
`convertEventsToGrowCycles` **server-side, one time**, creating a `Run` per detected cycle and one `Plant`
per distinct `lifecycleName`. After that the function is deleted from the browser. It should never have
been a runtime algorithm.

### 5.4 Re-veg, clone, rename — the three cases that break today's model

| Case | Today | C07 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rename** ("My Strain" → "Wedding Cake") | Cycle detection compares trimmed name strings, so the history **forks into two grows** | `label` is a field on a stable `plant_id`. Renaming changes a label. Nothing forks. The rename writes an `entry_type:'note'` row so the diary shows when the name changed. |
| **Re-veg** (flowering → vegetative) | Stage ordinal rolls back → **a new cycle is fabricated** | `Plant.stage` moves backwards; that is legal. The run continues. The plan gets a `[Phase wiederholen]` / `[Repeat stage]` action that inserts a copy of the veg stage after the current one. |
| **Clone** | Impossible | `POST /plants { sourcePlantId }`. New plant, `source:'clone'`, `startedAt = now`. Both plants get a cross-linked journal row: `Steckling von Topf 2` / `Clone of Pot 2`. The clone's `stageOffsetDays` is computed from the parent so the feed lane starts in the right place. |

### 5.5 What "the diary writes itself" actually means

Concretely, these rows are produced with no user action:

| Trigger | `entry_type` | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan advances a stage | `stage` | `Phase → Blüte` + the new targets. **Fixes the P0 bug where the server writes `categories:['diary-plant-lifecycle']` without the `'diary'` prefix, making its own entries invisible and uneditable.** |
| Gate confirmed / auto-passed | `gate` | `12/12 umgestellt (bestätigt)` / `(automatisch nach 3 Tagen)` |
| Task marked done | task's kind | volume / EC / pH pre-filled from the plan step, `planned: true` if untouched |
| Setpoint override | `override` | old → new, scope, expiry, actor |
| Plan config reaches the device | `setpoint` | the existing config-diff log, re-typed and made visible |
| Sunday 23:59 local | `week` | the `WeekSummary` row, with a hero image auto-picked as the lights-on frame nearest midweek |
| Camera capture | — | images are indexed by time and stage; not a journal row (that would be 2,880 rows/day) |

The user's job shrinks to photos, notes, and correcting numbers. **That is the mechanism behind the owner's
promise, and it is only possible because a plan exists to generate events from.**

## 6. Watering & fertilising

There is no irrigation or dosing hardware. This is capture-and-recall, and the only thing that matters is
**how fast capture is** and **how good recall is**.

### 6.1 Everyday capture — counting the taps

**Path A — the task is due (the common case).** From "Jetzt":

1. Tap `Erledigt`. → **1 tap.** A journal row is written with the plan's planned volume, EC, pH,
   all plants, timestamp now, `planned: true`. A toast: `Gegossen — 2,0 L · [Ändern]` /
   `Watered — 2.0 L · [Change]`, dismissible, 6 s.

**Path B — the task is due and I measured something.**

1. Tap `⋯` → sheet opens, pre-filled from the plan.
2. Adjust what differs (stepper on volume, numeric on EC/pH; `value-edit-row` already does the tap-to-arm
   `[−] range [+]` pattern with ARIA and the 8 s `rangeGuard` disarm).
3. Tap `Speichern`. → **3 taps + the edits.**

**Path C — unplanned watering.** FAB on "Jetzt" → `Gießen` → sheet → `Speichern`. **3 taps.**

The sheet:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Gießen                       12.09 19:14│
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Menge pro Topf   [ − ]  2,0 L  [ + ]   │
│ Pflanzen  [Alle ✓] [Topf 1 ✓] [Topf 2 ✓]│   ← only if plants.length > 1
│ ─────────────────────────────────────  │
│ Nährlösung                             │
│ EC ein   [ 1,8 ]      pH ein  [ 6,0 ]  │
│ ▸ Ablauf messen (EC/pH)                │   ← collapsed by default
│ ─────────────────────────────────────  │
│ ▸ Mischreihenfolge (5 Schritte)        │   ← collapsed; §6.3
│ ─────────────────────────────────────  │
│ 📷 Foto      📝 Notiz                   │
│            [ Speichern ]               │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

DE/EN key strings: `Gießen`/`Water`, `Düngen`/`Feed`, `Menge pro Topf`/`Amount per pot`,
`EC ein`/`EC in`, `Ablauf messen`/`Measure runoff`, `Mischreihenfolge`/`Mixing order`,
`Nichts gemessen — nur gegossen`/`Nothing measured — just watered`.

### 6.2 The schedule-following flow

The feed program is **anchored to the plan's stages**, not to wall-clock weeks, so "Blüte Woche 3" resolves
without asking. The Terp-only trick, which no bottle-brand calculator can do because none of them can see a
device: **auto-detect the 18/6 → 12/12 flip from `out_light` history** and place the grower on the correct
bloom week. If the detected flip disagrees with the plan by more than 2 days, the plan band offers
`Blütestart auf 02.09 korrigieren?` / `Correct flower start to 02 Sep?`.

Steps advance **by feed event ("Schritt 7 von 13"), with a soft calendar mapping** — growers feed Tue/Fri
and slip, and a chart that advances by wall clock will be wrong within a fortnight.

### 6.3 Domain details that decide whether growers trust this

- **EC is stored canonically in mS/cm. A bare "ppm" number is never rendered.** Three PPM scales are in
  circulation; at EC 1.8 a Bluelab reads 1260 and a Hanna reads 900 for the same liquid. Display is
  `1,8 mS/cm (900 ppm₅₀₀)` with the scale in the user's profile.
- **`FeedProgram.ecBasis: 'absolute' | 'delta_over_source'`.** CANNA publishes `EC +`, not EC: *add the EC
  of your tap water*. A UI that alarms against the raw chart value is actively wrong. With
  `delta_over_source` and a stored source-water EC of 0.4, the target renders as
  `Ziel EC 1,8 (+ Leitungswasser 0,4 = 2,2 am Messgerät)` /
  `Target EC 1.8 (+ tap water 0.4 = 2.2 on your meter)`.
- **A dose is not a scalar.** `amount: number | [min,max] | string` — because real charts contain `5–13`,
  `0` meaning stop, and literal strings like `nur Wasser` / `Flush only`.
- **Run-length adaptation is explicit, never silent.** If the program is 8 weeks and the plan's flower
  stage is 10, the plan asks once: `Letzte Woche wiederholen` / `Kurve strecken` / `Selbst anpassen`.
  Silently truncating an 8-week chart onto a 10-week cultivar is the failure that makes the feature
  untrustworthy.
- **Mixing order as an ordered checklist** with a running volume: silica → base A → base B → Cal-Mag →
  additives → **pH last**. Cheapest possible fix for a genuine failure mode.
- **Autoflower multiplier ×0.25–0.5 with no 12/12 flip event.** Essentially no manufacturer chart has an
  auto column — a real gap fillable with nothing borrowed.
- **Flush is never presented as necessary.** Copy: `Dein Plan sieht hier eine Spülwoche vor.` /
  `Your programme calls for a flush week here.` The Rx Green Technologies 2019 trial found no significant
  difference in terpenes, THC or yield, and panels statistically preferred un-flushed.
- Every computed dose carries `Werte prüfen, niedrig anfangen.` / `Check the values, start low.`

### 6.4 Drawn on the chart

Two mechanisms, both plan-aware:

1. **Annotation rail, row 1.** Water = droplet outline, feed = filled droplet with a dot. Clustered.
   Hollow markers to the right of `jetzt` are planned events.
2. **An optional "Nährlösung" / "Nutrient solution" panel.** Point series for input EC, runoff EC and pH,
   over a **target EC band that steps along the feed programme**. Runoff-vs-input divergence > 20 % draws a
   caution marker. This is the ask "it should also be visualized in the graph", answered properly: the
   target moves with the plan instead of being a static line.

### 6.5 Brand-schedule posture

**v1 ships zero brand data.** The engine is brand-agnostic and user-editable, seeded with **ownerless**
programmes only — Lucas Formula (0-5-10 / 0-8-16), Jack's 321, and generic per-medium EC ladders for soil,
coco and hydro. That is useful on day one with nothing borrowed and zero IP risk.

Brand charts, **if ever**, are a separate versioned MongoDB content collection (`growlibrary`) fetched at
runtime, never baked into the bundle, so any single brand is removable in one DB update. Every entry
carries `source_url`, `retrieved_at`, `chart_version`, `kind`. Word marks only — no logos, no brand
colours, no bottle photography, nothing suggesting sponsorship — and **never** a brand name in a Terp
Control feature name ("CANNA-Modus" would forfeit the referential-use defence). Never copy brand prose;
paraphrase every description. A takedown address and a documented notice-and-action policy ship *before*
any brand data does. And the honest reason this is safe to defer: **the chart data is the commodity, the
closed loop is the product** — the value (flip detection, moving EC alarm bands, target-vs-measured
overlay) is not where the legal exposure is.

## 7. Camera & timelapse

The camera today is a tile with no relation to a cycle, phase or entry. In C07 an image is a point on the
plan's timeline.

- At capture, the server denormalises onto `Image`: `run_id`, `plan_id`, `stage`, `dayOfRun`,
  `isLightsOn` (from `out_light` at that instant). All optional, all derivable, no firmware change.
- **Film strip** (§4.1 lane 6) shares the chart's x-scale and its cursor. Uneven capture intervals read
  honestly as gaps. `timestampEnd` already marks mp4 timelapses as spanning a range — those render as a
  bracket, not a point.
- **Two new timelapse durations** alongside `1d|1w|1m`: `'stage'`, generated when a stage ends, and
  `'run'`, generated at harvest. Server job change only.
- **Lights-on filtering by default** for run timelapses — the existing simulator already draws the tent lit
  by the light output, and a timelapse that strobes through 12 h of darkness is unwatchable.
- The **hero image** on each `WeekSummary` is auto-picked: the lights-on frame nearest the week's midpoint.
  It is the image the export uses and the one the journal shows. Overridable with one tap.
- **Plants and photos.** A webcam still is of the tent, not of a plant, so automatic images are
  tent-scoped. **User-uploaded** photos inherit the `plant_ids` of the entry they are attached to. Do not
  pretend otherwise: per-plant automatic photography would require per-plant framing the hardware does not
  have.
- Cleanup work this forces: `DELETE /image/:image_id` exists and is never called; retention filters
  `format:'jpeg'` only, so `user/jpeg` blobs accumulate forever; `express-fileupload` is mounted with no
  size limit against a 16 MB BSON ceiling; and the unique index collides on two same-millisecond uploads.
  All four are on the critical path once the app invites photos.

## 8. Multi-user, clubs, privacy

### 8.1 Roles

`Device.owner_id` is a single unindexed string and the entire authorisation model is one equality test at
`auth.middleware.ts:172`. This cannot be a flag.

```ts
// collection: memberships
interface Membership {
  device_id: string; user_id: string;
  role: 'owner' | 'grower' | 'viewer';
  addedAt: number; addedBy: string;
  displayName?: string;   // per-device pseudonym; NOT the account email
}
```

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **owner** | everything, incl. members, plan deletion, device transfer, privacy mode |
| **grower** | complete tasks, write journal entries, edit the running stage, start overrides. Cannot delete the device, change members, or change privacy mode |
| **viewer** | read. This is what a share link already is; share links become a `viewer` membership with a token instead of an account |

The migration is: create one `owner` membership per existing device, rewrite `auth.middleware.ts:172`
and `:207` to a membership lookup, loosen ~10 owner-scoped service queries. Two people and one tent is the
far larger case than clubs and is unserved by every competitor (Pulse charges $10/user and got a public
backlash thread). **It should be free.**

### 8.2 Attribution

`DeviceLog.actor_id`. Rendering is governed by privacy mode, not by the role.

### 8.3 Privacy posture — a design decision, made here

`Device.privacyMode: 'private' | 'club'`, **default `private`**.

| | **private** (default) | **club** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attribution shown | no — entries read `Gegossen 2,0 L`, actor stored but not rendered | yes, as `displayName` |
| Harvest weights | field hidden until explicitly enabled per device | available |
| Plant count in exports | never | included |
| Telemetry retention | 90 days raw, 2 years hourly rollup, then deleted | 5 years hourly (§ 26 KCanG horizon) |
| Participation log (§ 17 Abs. 2 "eigenhändig") | off | on: `PLANNED → PENDING → COMPLETED → REJECTED` on tasks |
| Export default | anonymised | pseudonymised, with a `NONE\|PSEUDONYMIZED\|ANONYMIZED` toggle |
| One-tap wipe | yes | yes, but warns about the club's retention duty |

Rationale, stated plainly: harvest weights and plant counts are the exact facts separating a lawful hobby
from an offence in many jurisdictions, and § 28 Abs. 2 KCanG lets German authorities seize digital data
with **no prior judicial approval**. Today InfluxDB is created with no retention policy and zero downsample
tasks — raw 5-second samples are kept forever. That is both a cost curve and an evidentiary liability.

**Hard refusals.** No per-member consumption or dispensing records. Not the club's § 26 system of record —
that market has ~20 products at €0.50–1.00/member/month, Cannanas claims >250 licensed clubs, and winning
it means owning 23 differing authority export formats. No identity requirement, no location collection or
inference, no analytics or ad SDKs, no undeletable audit log by default, **no `KCanG-konform` badge or any
compliance warranty ever**. EU hosting, stated on the page.

Where Terp Control *can* win: be the **device-attested environmental layer** those platforms consume.
Cannanas' `CreateDiaryRequest.measurements` has 40 numeric fields that read like a controller telemetry
dump, and its zones carry `streams.video[].url` — which the webcam fills directly. It has no sensor
ingestion endpoint; that path is private and unclaimed. That is business development (`integration@
cannanas.club`), not a sprint, and it stores zero personal data.

### 8.4 Publishing

Not sync. **No grow-diary platform can be two-way synced** — GrowDiaries has no public API and is
Cloudflare-hardened, Grow with Jane's ToS forbids automation and its backend is Firebase, SeedFinder's API
has been dead since 2024-07. The feature is named `Woche exportieren` / `Export week` and
`Tagebuch teilen` / `Share diary`, and it consists of:

1. A **public diary URL** `/d/{slug}-{5char}` — pseudonymous, no real names, no location, no plant count in
   private mode, revocable. This is the universal integration: it works in every forum, Discord and
   Telegram with nobody's permission. It is **not** a feed — there is no index, no browse, no comments,
   nothing to moderate.
2. A **BBCode / Markdown post generator** with hot-linked image URLs, built from a `WeekSummary`. XenForo
   and Discourse both render remote images, which collapses "integrate with four forums that have no usable
   API between them" into one copy button.
3. **Update destinations**: Discord webhooks, Telegram bots, generic webhook. Telegram is already the Terp
   community channel. `server/src/utils/webhookTemplate.ts` already does `{{placeholder}}` substitution in
   JSON and URL modes and `alarm.service.ts` already does GET/POST/PUT with custom headers and separate
   payloads — what is missing is a **non-alarm trigger** (there is no generic event bus), image attachment,
   and a preset library. Plan events are the perfect first triggers. The existing code has no timeout, no
   retry, no queue and no SSRF guard; that is the real cost.
4. **Never** Instagram, Threads, TikTok or Reddit auto-post. A failed Discord post means a revoked webhook;
   a failed Instagram post means the *user* loses their account with no appeal and Terp Control was the
   proximate cause. X is tier 3 at best ($0.200/post with a URL: 5,000 users × 8 posts = $8,000/month).
5. **No "Powered by Terp Control" footer on user posts.** § 6 KCanG bans cannabis advertising and
   sponsoring with fines to €30,000, read broadly. It is the single riskiest string in the feature and it
   buys nothing.

## 9. Old devices — type 7 (air, light, socket, fridge)

The rule: **the Plan degrades to whatever the device can express, and the shell is identical.** No
`ngSwitch` on `device_type` in the new chrome.

**What each type sees, concretely:**

## 10. Onboarding

The existing `setup-wizard` (289 lines, `name → [connections] → stage → plan → done`) is already
hardware-adaptive and already supports joining a grow mid-plan via `planStartIndex`. C07 keeps its
structure and asks **one** real question.

**Capability is established without asking anything:**

| Fact | Source | Asked? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which sockets, which roles | `hardwareInfo.sockets` CSV → `parseSocketRoles()` | no |
| CO₂ sensor present | `hardwareInfo.co2 !== 'off'` | no |
| Camera paired | `hardwareInfo.webcam_did` | no |
| Leaf-temp / lux sensor | `hardwareInfo` | no |
| Timezone | browser, **confirmed once** (it is a required plan field and DST matters) | shown, one tap to accept |

**Critical fix:** `deviceControlCapability()` returns `'full'` when `hardwareInfo.sockets` is *absent*.
It must **fail closed** to `'monitor'` with the banner `Ich kenne deine Steckdosen noch nicht.` /
`I don't know your sockets yet.` and a link to pairing. Failing open is why a heater-only tent is shown
humidity targets, a humidity-deviation warning and a dehumidifier alarm preset it can never act on.

**The one question, step 3:**

```
Was hast du vor?  /  What are you doing?

┌───────────────┐ ┌───────────────┐ ┌───────────────┐
│      🌱       │ │      ⚡       │ │      👁       │
│ Photoperiode  │ │  Automatik    │ │ Nur beobachten│
│  12 Wochen    │ │   10 Wochen   │ │               │
│ Keim→Veg→Blüte│ │ ohne 12/12    │ │ kein Plan,    │
│ →Trocknen     │ │               │ │ keine Hinweise│
└───────────────┘ └───────────────┘ └───────────────┘
```

Three cards, three finger-sized targets. `Nur beobachten` is a **legitimate, permanent answer** that
disables every task, every nag and the plan band for that device — it is the entire onboarding for Type 1.

**Step 4, only if a plan was chosen:** `Wo bist du gerade?` / `Where are you now?` — the stage picker,
pre-selected on `Keimung`, so a grower with plants already in flower joins the plan at the right step
instead of being told to start over. Durations are editable inline (the wizard already does this).

**Step 5, done:** `Fertig. Ich schreibe dein Tagebuch ab jetzt mit.` /
`Done. I'll keep your journal from here.`

The wizard gets its 30 MB of already-shot videos back (`assets/wizard/`: `connect.mp4`, `teachin.mp4`,
`sockettype_*.mp4`, `overview.mp4` — 23 files, referenced nowhere, copied into every production build), and
a `Einrichtung erneut starten` / `Re-run setup` entry in the device menu, which does not exist today.

## 11. Per-user-type walkthrough — the first week

**Type 1 — monitoring only.** Unboxes, claims, wizard, picks `Nur beobachten`. Three taps total. His home
screen is: liveness chip, three climate tiles with `nur gemessen` captions, no plan band, no task card, no
nagging, ever. Day 3 he opens Verlauf, taps `7 T`, sees four stacked panels with a day/night-shaded
background and — the thing that actually lands — the scorecard: `Im Ziel 71 % · Tag 88 % · Nacht 44 % ·
längste Abweichung 4 Std. 20 Min.` He learns his nights are the problem, which he could not have learned
from fifteen overlapping translucent areas with no axis. Day 6 the app tells him the device went offline
for 40 minutes at 03:00. He never learns the word "Plan". **Honest cost:** the Plan tab still sits in his
tab bar saying nothing useful. See §15.4.

**Type 2 — the same, with a camera.** Identical, plus: the film strip under the chart, so scrubbing to the
temperature spike shows him the tent at that moment, and a weekly summary card in the Tagebuch with an
auto-picked lights-on hero image. End of week 1 he gets the `1w` timelapse, and — because he is on
`Nur beobachten` and has no run — it is filed by date rather than by stage. He taps `Woche exportieren`,
gets Markdown with hot-linked images, pastes it in the Terp Telegram group. Correlation of image to value
is the whole product for him and it is one shared x-axis.

**Type 3 — controls CO₂ and heating only.** Sockets report `heater,co2`. Because capability is per-role,
his home screen has a temperature tile with a target and a CO₂ tile with a target, and a humidity tile
**without** one, captioned `nur gemessen`. No dehumidifier alarm preset is offered. He picks the
photoperiod plan and edits only the temperature rows in the stage editor; the humidity rows in the stage
editor are shown greyed with `Kein Entfeuchter gekoppelt` / `No dehumidifier paired` and a link to add one.
Day 4 he nudges night temperature down 2 °C on the Jetzt screen; the override sheet appears and he picks
`Für diese Phase`. The journal records it. Week 1 ends with a chart on which the setpoint line steps at
21:00 exactly where the plan says, and the deviation fill shows his heater is losing 1.5 °C every night —
a fact the current chart is structurally incapable of showing.

**Type 4 — the whole tent.** The type the marketing promise is written for and the type the firmware
cannot express. He binds light, secondary_light, heater, dehumidifier, co2 — the only five roles that
exist. C07 is honest with him: the Plan's stage editor shows exactly those five, and the sockets page says
in words that `dehumidifier` doubles as the cooler in `temp`/`breed` workmodes, that humidity can only go
down, and that there is no humidifier or exhaust-fan role. His first week is the best case for this
concept: he picks `Photoperiode`, sets 3 pots as plants, and by day 7 he has a plan band, a task cadence,
a chart with setpoint + band + output lane, and a week summary he did not write. **He is also the type most
likely to hit the plan's ceiling** — see §15.1 and §15.2.

**Type 5 — DIY closed loop (a fridge, a mushroom chamber, a cheese cave).** He does *not* want stages. He
creates a plan with **one** stage, `Dauerbetrieb`, no gate, and target 12 °C / 85 % — which the fridge
hardware can actually hold. He then goes straight to `Plan als JSON`, sees the whole plan as a document,
edits it in a text field, and pastes it back. He wires the generic webhook to his own ntfy instance on plan
events. **What he does not get in v1:** a write API for outputs (`POST /device/test/:device_id` is the
admin harness, requires `workmode === 'off'` and auto-stops), custom measures (`VALID_SENSORS` is a
hard-coded allowlist), or Home Assistant. He gets the honest sentence in the docs instead. See §15.5 —
this is the type C07 serves worst.

**Type 6 — a club with shared controllers.** Two people, `owner` and `grower`, on the same device. The
grower opens Jetzt, sees `Heute: Gießen — 2,0 L pro Topf`, taps `Erledigt`. The owner opens the Tagebuch
and sees `19:14 Gegossen 2,0 L · EC 1,8 — Marek`. **This is the feature.** Not compliance software — a
shared to-do list with attribution and a plant record, which is exactly what "who did what? how did the
plants react?" means. Watering and fertiliser are the club's most important topic, and this concept makes
them the *primary* scheduled object in the product rather than an entry type that does not exist today.
Privacy mode is `private` by default; the owner flips it to `club` and gets attribution, the participation
log for § 17 Abs. 2, and longer retention. No badge, no grams ledger, no member identities. Week 1
realistically ends with them discovering that one plan per device means one climate for the whole room —
which is physically true and which they will still complain about.

**Type 7 — old device owner (a fan, a light, a plug).** Nothing about his device changes. He opens the app
and the chart is better, the offline state is finally visible, and there is a Tagebuch with photos he
previously had nowhere to put. His Plan tab says `Dauerbetrieb · seit 14. Mai` and one button that opens
the settings form he already knows. He can add a watering task and a plant even though his plug has no
sensors, because tasks and plants are cloud-side. He is not the focus group and the design does not pretend
he is — but nothing he had was taken away, and four things he did not have showed up.

## 12. Stoner / normal / techie

One design, two persisted modes (`localStorage['app-settings-expert']`, the existing precedent, documented
in German at `docs/einfach-modus.md`), and progressive disclosure inside them. **Three modes would be a
maintenance disaster in a codebase with 38 tests and four already-duplicated workmode truth tables.**

**Stoner** — served by *reduction of decisions*, not by a mode:
- The home screen answers three questions above the fold and has **exactly one primary button** (`Erledigt`).
- Onboarding is one question with three cards.
- `Erledigt` is a one-tap complete action; the correction path is opt-in via `⋯`.
- Nothing on Jetzt requires reading a chart. The verdict is a word: `im Ziel`.
- `Später` snoozes to the light change, not to a time he would have to think about.
- `Nur beobachten` is a first-class permanent answer that turns the whole plan machinery off.
- No modal ever asks a question he cannot answer from the tent.

**Normal** — served by *the plan band + the chart + the journal*, and by the override sheet, which is the
one place C07 asks for a decision. It exists because the alternative (silently reverting his change at the
next plan step, which is what a naive implementation does) is worse:

```
Ziel geändert: Nacht 20,0 → 18,0 °C
( ) Nur jetzt — bis zum nächsten Planschritt
(•) Für diese Phase — Blüte
( ) Für den ganzen Plan ab hier
                                [Übernehmen]
```
EN: `Just now — until the next plan step` / `For this stage — Flowering` / `For the whole plan from here`.
Default is the middle option. An active temporary override shows as a pill on Jetzt:
`Übersteuert bis 06:00` / `Overridden until 06:00`.

**Techie** — served by *the plan being a document*:
- `Plan als JSON` — read, edit, paste back. Import/export. The plan is portable between devices and
  shareable as a file.
- **The compiler is inspectable**: for any stage, `Was der Controller bekommt` / `What the controller gets`
  shows the exact 15 firmware-read config keys that stage renders, and marks the keys the firmware
  **ignores** (`lights.maintenanceOn`, `co2.sunsetOff`, `daynight.linearChange`, and the entire
  `daynight.floating` beta, which no firmware anywhere reads — zero grep hits). Today those are offered as
  working controls. C07 removes them from the UI and lists them here as a documented nothing.
- Per-stage linear ramps, gate grace periods, task editor, custom feed programmes with all nine unit
  systems and `ecBasis`.
- Webhooks on plan events (stage change, gate, task due, task missed, week summary, harvest).
- A read/write API keyed per plan, shaped like Pulse Grow's (`x-api-key`, quotas in datapoints, OpenAPI +
  Redoc) — with the difference that Terp Control owns the hardware and can offer **writes**, which no
  monitoring vendor can. v1.1.
- Published cloud-outage behaviour, in writing. Every experienced grower has watched Grobo, Seedo,
  Cloudponics and Leaf brick themselves.

## 13. Scope

**The ship date is October 2026 and today is 24 August 2026. That is about six weeks.** Any concept that
plans a year of work for "v1" is not a plan, it is a wish. C07's v1 is therefore deliberately smaller than
C07.

### v1 — ships with the hardware (~6 weeks)

**Tier 0 — non-negotiable, roughly the first half.** The product is not credible at launch without these
and none of them are C07-specific:

1. Controller naming pass + `devices.controller` i18n block in `en.json` **and** `de.json`. (The dashboard
   currently says "Terp Control FRIDGE GROW".)
2. `POST /device/claimcode` authentication — knowing a `device_id`, which appears in every webapp URL, is
   currently enough to steal a device.
3. `deviceControlCapability()` fails **closed**; per-role capability drives every target row, alarm preset
   and tile.
4. Remove the dead controls (`daynight.floating`/`float_start`/`day_duration`/`light_duration`,
   `lights.maintenanceOn`, `co2.sunsetOff`, `daynight.linearChange`).
5. **Liveness: live / delayed / offline, everywhere.** One component, the highest ratio of value to effort
   in the document.
6. Fix `deleted` semantics so "Clear logs" means something; fix the server's stage-transition entries being
   written without the `'diary'` prefix.
7. Flux parameterisation + interval clamping on `/data/series`; server-side log pagination; an InfluxDB
   retention policy and an hourly downsample task.
8. The six live i18n bugs.

**Tier 1 — C07's core.**

9. `GrowPlan` collection + **the compiler to `Recipe`** + the executor changes in §14a. Plan tab with stage
   list and stage editor (reusing `stage-preset-picker` and `value-edit-row`).
10. The **Jetzt** screen: plan band, next-action card, climate tiles with targets, outputs lane, journal
    peek — with every stale/offline/absent/no-plan state built.
11. The **override sheet** with its three scopes.
12. **Tasks**: water / feed / measure, one-tap done, auto-written journal rows. *No* feed-programme engine
    yet — tasks carry free numbers plus one ownerless EC ladder per medium.
13. `Plant` + `Run` entities, the one-time backfill from `convertEventsToGrowCycles`, optional plant chips.
14. The **tab-bar shell** + device switcher + language switcher.
15. de + en for all of the above, same commit.

**Tier 2 — first to be cut, in this order.**

16. `WeekSummary` generation + `Woche exportieren` (Markdown/BBCode/CSV).
17. **The chart overhaul.** ECharts migration, small multiples, stepped setpoint + band + deviation fill,
    day/night shading, output lane, annotation rail, scorecard, scrub header, Okabe-Ito, the future half,
    `sampling:'minmax'`. Chart.js deleted.

Being honest about item 17: it is the largest single piece of work in the list and the one whose value is
most visible in a demo. If Tier 0 slips — and Tier 0 is a P0 list written by someone reading the code, so
it will — **v1 is Tier 0 + Tier 1 and the chart ships in November**. Saying that now is better than
discovering it in October. The scorecard specifically must **not** ship until the raw-sample endpoint
exists: a time-in-range number computed on averaged data is wrong-but-plausible, which is worse than absent.

### v1.1 — Q1 2027

Feed-programme engine with `ecBasis`, ranges, mixing order, run-length adaptation, autoflower multiplier,
seeded with ownerless programmes; the nutrient panel on the chart; flip auto-detection from `out_light`;

Telegram / generic webhook destinations on plan events; the film-strip sprite endpoint; stage and run
timelapses; `Plan als JSON` + import/export; the VPD matrix; run-over-run comparison.

### v2 and beyond

Brand schedule content layer (only after German IP counsel on UWG § 4 Nr. 3 and UrhG §§ 87a–87e, with a
takedown policy live first, and ideally after written permission from three brands — which converts the
biggest risk into the biggest moat). Club mode with the participation log. The public read/**write** API.
Cannanas / 420cloud integration — and note the window is closing: 420cloud already sells "unlimitierte
Sensor-Einbindung" and Cannanas already lists GrowControl and TrolMaster as live integrations.

## 14. Non-goals

1. **No two-way sync with any grow-diary platform.** Not GrowDiaries, not Grow with Jane. The feature is
   named `Export` and `Teilen` and it will stay named that.
2. **No auto-posting to Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Reddit or Facebook.** Ever, regardless of demand.
3. **No community feed.** Zero of ~200 App Store reviews across TrolMaster and VIVOSUN praised theirs; it is
   permanent moderation cost. A link you can paste anywhere is not a feed you must moderate.
4. **Not the club's § 26 system of record.** No grams, no member identities, no dispensing, no THC content.
5. **No irrigation or dosing automation.** There is no hardware for it. Watering and feeding are capture and
   recall, and this concept says so on the screen.
6. **No per-plant climate.** One tent, one climate, one plan. Plants get their own feed lane and their own
   stage offset; they do not get their own targets. This is physics, not a limitation.
7. **No new socket roles, no new sensor fields, no new MQTT topics in v1.** Everything above is cloud-side.
   The one place a firmware change would pay for itself — a `humidifier` and an `exhaust_fan` role for
   Type 4 — is named as firmware work for the next hardware revision and not smuggled into a cloud concept.
8. **No offline mode, no native app.** There is no service worker and `capacitor.config.ts` is untouched
   scaffold (`io.ionic.starter`). Adding either is a new dependency and a build change that six weeks
   cannot absorb.
9. **No three-tier capability mode.** Simple/Expert stays two.
10. **No compliance badge, no "Powered by Terp Control" footer, no branded string on user-generated posts.**
11. **No brand feed charts in v1**, and never baked into the bundle.
12. **No rewrite of `fan-settings` / `light-settings` / `plug-settings`.** They get new chrome and are
    otherwise left alone.

### 14a. Confronting the recipe engine — what breaks and what I do about it

This is the part of the concept most likely to fail, so it is spelled out.

| Real limit (`device.service.ts:396-550`) | C07's answer | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One recipe per device**, embedded in `Device` | Keep it. One plan per device is *correct* for a tent: one climate. Multi-plant is expressed as plants with `stageOffsetDays` and their own feed lane, not as multiple plans. | none — but see §15.2 |
| **`RecipeStep.settings` is applied as the whole device configuration**, blowing away hardware tuning | The plan owns a named key set (`planOwnedKeys`: workmode, day.*, night.*, daynight.day/night, lights.*, co2.target). The compiler emits **only** those; `configureDevice` is changed to merge a patch onto the last known config. | ~1 server function + an allowlist. No firmware change. This also makes the config-diff log meaningful. |
| **Push at most hourly, and only to a device seen in the last 60 s** | Push immediately on step change and on plan edit; push on device reconnect (the fetch path already exists); keep the hourly heartbeat. When the guard blocks, **show it**: `Planschritt wartet auf den Controller`. | small; the honesty is the feature |
| **`waitForConfirmation` halts the entire plan indefinitely** | Becomes a **Gate** with `graceDays` (default 3, editable, `0` = wait forever) held on `GrowPlan`, not on `RecipeStep`. The plan tick auto-confirms when grace expires — mechanically identical to what the confirm endpoint already does. **A gate never halts climate**: the current stage's setpoints stay applied; only advancement waits. Reminders at `remindEveryHours`. | ~30 lines in the tick + a field |
| **Unknown `durationUnit` silently means minutes** | The plan stores `plannedDays: number` and the compiler only ever emits `durationUnit: 'days'`. | none |
| **The engine cannot ramp a setpoint** | A `ramp: 'linear'` stage is compiled into sub-steps (one per 3 days by default). Honest consequence: the compiled recipe array gets long; capped at 60 steps with `Rampe zu fein für diese Phase` if exceeded. Rejected alternative: per-tick interpolation, which needs a new executor **and** more frequent config pushes than the device accepts. | compile-time only |
| **`RecipeTemplate.name` is globally unique across all users** | Compound index `{owner_id, name}`. Two growers can both have "Autoflower". | index change + a fix to the already-broken `PUT /users/:id` CRUD surface |
| **The device has a local display and rotary knob** — a user can change the config out from under the plan | **Drift detection**: compare the echoed config to the compiled step; on divergence show `Adopt into plan` / `Restore plan` on the Jetzt screen. This is a strength of having a plan at all — today divergence is silent. | a comparison + a banner |

Everything in this table is cloud-side. **No firmware change is requested for v1.**

## 15. Honest weaknesses

**15.1 The plan is a straitjacket, and the override sheet is the tax.** The single most common objection
will be "I just want to turn the temperature down". C07 makes that three taps and a decision instead of one
tap. The mitigation (default-selected middle option, a big `Übernehmen`) reduces the cost but does not
remove it. A grower who fiddles daily will find this concept nagging. The counter-argument is that today's
alternative is worse — you change a value, the recipe engine silently overwrites it within the hour, and
nothing tells you why — but "less bad than a bug" is not the same as "good".

**15.2 One plan per device is honest about climate and dishonest about plants.** A tent with a plant in
week 2 of veg and a plant in week 6 of flower gets one stage ribbon, one setpoint line, one day/night band.
`stageOffsetDays` gives the lagging plant a correct **feed** lane, but the chart's stage ribbon is
tent-truth and will read as wrong to that user. The owner explicitly asked for "maybe even at different
stages". C07 supports different stages *per plant for feeding and journalling* and refuses them *for
climate*. That is a real, arguable refusal and a critic will land on it.

**15.3 Ramps compiled into sub-steps are ugly under the hood.** A 42-day flowering stage with a linear
ramp becomes 14 recipe steps. Anyone who opens the Expert recipe editor sees a list that does not resemble
the plan they built. The plan screen hides this, which means there are now two representations of the same
thing and one of them is generated — a classic source of drift bugs, in a codebase with no meaningful test
suite.

**15.4 Type 1 — the largest and laziest segment — carries dead weight.** `Nur beobachten` turns off the
nagging, but the tab bar still has a Plan tab that says nothing, and the entire concept's centre of gravity
is a feature this user has declined. A concept built around *the chart* or around *the journal* serves him
better. C07's defence is that he still gets the best chart and the honest offline state, and that his cost
is one tab he never taps. That is a defence, not a win.

**15.5 Type 5 is served worst.** The plan's vocabulary — stages, gates, photoperiod, flip — is cannabis
photoperiod vocabulary. A user building a fridge-based closed loop for mushrooms or curing wants
"hold 12 °C / 85 % forever, and let me script it". A one-stage plan works mechanically and reads as
ceremony. He is also the segment with the highest advocacy and the deepest scepticism (report 04: these
people write their own Go apps rather than use anyone's), and v1 gives him no write API, no custom
measures, and no Home Assistant. `Plan als JSON` is a v1.1 consolation prize.

**15.6 The auto-written diary generates volume in a collection with no pagination.** A chatty plan can
produce eight to twelve `DeviceLog` rows a day. Today `getDeviceLogs` has no `.limit()`, no cursor and no
count, and the Entries report fetches the device's **entire** history on every open. Server-side pagination
therefore moves from "nice cleanup" onto C07's critical path. If it slips, the feature that is supposed to
be the product's selling point makes the diary page slower every week it runs.

**15.7 Six weeks.** §13 is written honestly and it is still optimistic. Tier 0 alone is eleven P0 items
including an auth vulnerability, a Flux injection, an Influx retention policy and an i18n pass. The
realistic bad outcome is that October ships Tier 0 + the Jetzt screen + the plan compiler, and the chart —
the thing everyone will judge the redesign by, and the thing the owner explicitly asked for ("The Graph
needs some real overhaul to look good") — lands after the hardware. C07 should be chosen with that outcome
priced in, not discovered.

**15.8 The compiler introduces a second source of truth against a device that can be changed locally.**
Drift detection (§14a) makes divergence visible, but every drift dialog is a moment where the user has to
understand that the app and the box disagree — a concept where the device config *is* the truth never has
that conversation. This is the price of the plan being a timeline.

**15.9 Time-in-range is easy to get quietly wrong.** It must be computed server-side on raw samples;
computing it on `aggregateWindow(fn:mean)` output destroys the excursions the metric exists to count and
the error is **invisible by eye**. There is no test baseline to catch it. This is the single most likely
place for C07 to ship a confident lie.

**15.10 Feed-programme correctness is physical.** Nine unit systems including drops/10 L and 1:5000
dilution ratios, three PPM scales, and `EC +` vs absolute EC. A conversion bug here burns a crop.
Deferring the engine to v1.1 is partly scope and partly cowardice about shipping arithmetic that can
damage plants with six weeks of testing behind it.

**15.11 Nothing here fixes Type 4's actual gap.** "Ein Controller für dein ganzes Zelt" needs a humidifier
role, an exhaust-fan role and a cooler that is not the same socket as the dehumidifier. Five socket roles
exist. C07 is honest about it on screen, which is better than today's silence, but a concept cannot design
its way out of missing firmware — and being honest about a missing feature is not the same as having it.
