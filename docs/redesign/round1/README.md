# Round one — ten concepts, ten critics

Ten UI/UX concepts for the controller-era redesign, and the scoring that followed.

Read [`00-ranking.md`](00-ranking.md) first. Its §4 — the cross-cutting findings — is the part worth
keeping: needs that *no* concept met, found independently by several critics. Those outlived the
concepts that prompted them.

`C01`–`C10` are the concepts. Each ends with a section where its own author attacks it, written
before the critics saw it, so a concept's known weaknesses are not buried in someone else's review.

`critique-T1`…`critique-T7` are the seven user types the product must serve; `critique-CAP-*` are the
three capability lenses (stoner, normal, techie). Each critic scored all ten from one perspective
only and was told not to balance — balancing is what the ranking does, and it says which weighting
it used.

Two caveats before quoting any of it:

- The scores are one panel's opinion, not measurement. The useful signal is where critics *disagreed*
  sharply (§1.4), because that marks a real product tension rather than a flaw to patch.
- C04 proposed separate simple and expert applications. That approach has since been ruled out, so
  its score is kept for the record but its design is not a live option.
