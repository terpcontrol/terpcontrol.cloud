# The app rewrite

The rewrite replaced the Angular web app with the React app in `webapp/`, the server's app-facing routes with the
`/v1` API whose contract is `shared-types/src/v1/`, and the old database shapes with the model of ADR 0001; the
device-facing routes stayed as they were. It was merged on 2026-10-04 as #104 and is complete. The navigation
redesign of November 2026 was built on top of it and is described below. Internal notes on the product side of
both exist outside this repository.

## Where its knowledge lives

- [ADR 0001](adr/0001-app-rewrite-data-model.md): the data model, the `/v1` API and the migration.
  [ADR 0002](adr/0002-app-rewrite-frontend-stack.md): the web app's libraries and its look.
- [device-protocol.md](device-protocol.md): the frozen contract with the firmware.
- [`server/src/migrations/README.md`](../server/src/migrations/README.md) and the runbook
  [Upgrading an install from before the app rewrite](runbooks/upgrade-a-pre-rewrite-install.md): upgrading an install
  from before the rewrite. The server migrates the database at boot and no command undoes a run, so the way back is a
  backup that has been restored once before the upgrade.

  [data](knowledge/data.md), [webapp](knowledge/webapp.md), [app-ux](knowledge/app-ux.md),

## The navigation redesign

Chris asked for it on 2026-11-11 - the diary was hard to find, places and rooms were inconsistent, the charts less
precise and less intuitive than the old app's -, chose the direction the same day and accepted a click prototype
on 2026-11-12, which is the specification. New routes need no redirects from addresses that never reached
production, and a change to the data model comes as a migration step (Chris, 2026-11-11). It was built in rounds
by one session, each round walked by a critic who had built nothing
([development-workflow.md](knowledge/development-workflow.md#long-runs-and-rounds)); the walks end when one finds no
major in any part, and the minors left then go to Chris's list and the pull request (Chris, 2026-11-15). After the fix
round of the eleventh walk the run winds down (Chris, 2026-11-16: "Komme langsam zum Ende und nutze nicht mehr mehrere
Kritiker nach jeder Runde"): one focused critic a round rather than three whole-app walks, then the final checks and
the pull request. Before the pull request a short round reads the German catalogue for strings the rewrite translated
word for word from English - its sentence structure, noun chains, literal idioms - and rewrites them into natural
German (Chris, 2026-11-16; the lasting rule is in [app-wording.md](knowledge/app-wording.md#german-slips-to-check-for));
built (below, "the German catalogue read for calques"). The briefs, the list of every
function with its new place and the decisions on each critic's findings are internal notes. **Everything in the
second list below is that session's decision, not Chris's word, until he confirms it with the pull request.**

With the pull request open, Chris looked at it beside master (2026-11-25): he likes the new structure, but master
looks better to him - its type and its colours, and an overview that draws less as graphics. The look is
therefore drafted again, by a designer agent on Fable with mockups to choose from, settled with him and then
built by Opus agents (his instruction). From three directions he chose (2026-11-26) **"Marke fein"**: master's
Inter and master's palette with a lighter hierarchy - only a page's title and a name heavy, figures, chips, buttons
and tags at 500, section labels in sentence case -, the **desktop rail navy as on master** rather than the light rail
the direction proposed, and a **Start between master's calm Home and the fuller drafts: a small camera picture and
small curves**, the readings as figures. It is built and replaces "Nebel" in
[ADR 0002](adr/0002-app-rewrite-frontend-stack.md#the-look-marke-fein). Two Opus agents built it on disjoint files (the look, and
Start); the designer then walked the built app as its one critic, majors and minors as in the run before, and an
Opus round fixed the majors, and a short re-walk found none left and no new one. The walk found four: the desktop
Start's cards in one narrow column, an offline place red on Start but amber on its own page, the small curve of a
reading out of its band drawn on the plot's edge, and a focus ring lost on the navy rail; the minors that need
Chris's word go to "Open for Chris".

The same day Chris passed on a colleague's view: the redesign's onboarding and places are better, but the page of
one place - and how it shows the values - does not feel as powerful as master's cockpit. The designer agent (Fable,
his instruction for graphical work) drew it again from master's cockpit, and Chris chose (2026-11-26): **master's
tiles back on the place page** - one per reading with the big figure, Ø 1 Std, the target and whether it is met, a
24 h curve with the band, and what the hardware does -, **VPD inside the humidity tile** as on master (five tiles),
**two tile columns on the phone**, the **big camera picture with the grow under it**, and on the Start cards for
several places **master's Ø 1 Std and hardware line**. He added: leaf temperature is only very rarely measured, so
the page is designed for the usual case without the leaf tile - four tiles (temperature, humidity, CO₂, light) or
three without CO₂ - and must look full there, not like a page with a gap. The designer's grid for it, one rule for
five to two tiles: three tiles a row from a 630 px tile column, two from 330 px, one below; rows fill evenly, the
fuller first, and every row fills the width; a tile 300 px or wider takes master's wide layout (text left, curve
right). Four tiles are thus 2 + 2 wide on a desktop and 2 + 2 on a phone. The rest of the drawing (the right column's order, an alarms
card on the place page, sentence-case headings, what of master's cockpit stays out) is the designer's and goes to
"Open for Chris". It is built ([app-ux.md §2](knowledge/app-ux.md#2-navigation-and-the-place-page), "A place's
Start is master's cockpit"); what the build decided where the drawing was silent is there too (questions 100 to 109).
The designer then walked the build: two majors - a press on the curve did nothing, and the tiles re-formed as the
page's reads landed - and the plain minors were fixed; two minors wait for Chris (questions 110 and 111).

  germination is ended; Start says a work mode the grow's phase contradicts instead of "Alles im Ziel"; a phase

  unknown `space=` says so; a page that could not be read keeps its title, with one retry wording and one

  ([ADR 0002, "The look"](adr/0002-app-rewrite-frontend-stack.md#the-look-marke-fein), app-ux §9).

## Left open

Checked against the code on 2026-10-08. Take an item out once it is done.

- **Unit preferences are saved, not applied.** Ich › Darstellung stores °C/°F, g/oz and l/gal
  (`preferences.units`), but every figure is still drawn in °C, g and l, as the help beside "Einheiten" tells the
  reader (`help.units`); only the row's line on Ich reads the choice (`webapp/src/screens/me/doors.ts`).
- **Plan templates cannot be renamed or deleted in the app.** `PATCH` and `DELETE /v1/plan-templates/{id}` exist;
  `webapp/src/api/plans.ts` only lists and creates.
- **Two task sources are not derived.** The contract's `taskSource` names `scheme` and `plan_suggestion`, but tasks
  come only from reminders and from plan steps waiting to be confirmed; `server/src/modules/v1/diary/tasks.service.ts`
  says why.
- **A standalone Terp Cam cannot be added.** The app does not offer it any more (Chris, 2026-10-08, for now) and
  `POST /v1/cameras` refuses `terpcam_standalone` with `not_yet`: a Terp Cam is reached over a relay its device
  opens, and one paired at no device has nothing to open it. The kind stays in the model in case it comes back.
- **The firmware since 2026-09-23 has not been checked on hardware.** #109, #110, #111 (fridge heat pulses,
  controller heater cut, fridge SCD4x fallback), #128 (Terp Cam relay on AIR and plug) and #141 (settings reported
  on connect) say in their descriptions that they were not tried on a device, and no `/firmware-check` is recorded
  for them or for the rewrite's own firmware changes: the smart-socket roles, overrides and protections
  (`firmware/src/wifi.cpp`, `firmware/src_hwtype/plug/plug.cpp`) and the controller's germination cooling with the
  exhaust (`firmware/src_hwtype/controller/controller.cpp`). A passing check comments "Skill check: ..." on its pull
  request. Two server behaviours also want a real device to confirm them: the 24-hour light encoding
  (`ALWAYS_LIT_FROM` in `shared-types/src/v1/day-night.ts`) and the schedule re-sent when the clocks change
  (`server/src/modules/device-protocol/schedule-clock.service.ts`).
